## Statement

Use the normalized systems of accepted fact `b046e3066136e781` and the reduced word automaton and counts of accepted fact `46afc7eb815ffc5b`. For every \(\eta\in\{0,1,2\}\) and every allowed interior/interior label vector
\[
\sigma_P\in\mathcal A,\qquad
\sigma_Q,\sigma_R\in\mathcal C,\qquad
\sigma_Q\in\mathcal B\ \text{or}\ \sigma_R\in\mathcal D,
\]
the system
\[
\mathcal S_{\eta,\mathrm I,\mathrm I,\sigma}
\]
is infeasible whenever \(\sigma_R\notin\mathcal D\).

Equivalently, every feasible interior/interior system has
\[
\ell_R<0
\quad\text{and}\quad
\sigma_R\in\mathcal D.
\]
Among the mixed-label, lexicographically symmetry-reduced systems, this removes exactly
\[
3\cdot17464=52392
\]
systems. The surviving interior/interior row contains exactly
\[
3(69968-17464)=157512
\]
systems.

## Proof

Put \(s=m+k\). First note the row-independent residual consequence
\[
s>1. \tag{1}
\]
This consequence is also used in accepted fact `b23633f36a9edcd1`, and follows directly from the residual branches of accepted fact `b046e3066136e781`. If \(\eta=0\), then \(m=q^2\) and \(k>1-q^2\), so \(s>1\). If \(\eta=1\) or \(2\), then
\[
5q>3,\qquad 2k>3q^2+2q-1.
\]
Consequently,
\[
2s=2q^2+2k
>5q^2+2q-1
=2+(5q-3)(q+1)>2,
\]
which again gives (1).

Suppose now that an interior/interior system is feasible and that
\[
\ell_R\ge0. \tag{2}
\]
Horizontal projection of midpoint inclusion, as recorded in accepted fact `b046e3066136e781`, gives
\[
\ell_R\le\frac{\ell_P+\ell_Q}{2}
=\frac{-\delta+\ell_Q}{2}.
\]
Together with \(\delta>0\) and (2), this yields
\[
\ell_Q\ge\delta+2\ell_R>0. \tag{3}
\]

For a body \(E\), write \(h_E=U_E-L_E\) and
\[
g_E(x)
=[-L_E(x)]_+-[-U_E(x)]_+
+[U_E(x)+x]_+-[L_E(x)+x]_+.
\]
At a fixed \(x\), \(g_E(x)\) is the sum of the lengths of
\[
[L_E(x),U_E(x)]\cap(-\infty,0]
\quad\text{and}\quad
[L_E(x),U_E(x)]\cap[-x,\infty).
\]
If \(x\ge0\), these two half-lines cover \(\mathbb R\), with overlap \([-x,0]\). Hence
\[
g_E(x)
=h_E(x)+
\left|[L_E(x),U_E(x)]\cap[-x,0]\right|
\ge h_E(x). \tag{4}
\]
For every \(x\), one also has \(g_E(x)\ge0\).

Accepted fact `b046e3066136e781` identifies the overlap remainder as
\[
\Omega=\frac1\Lambda\left[
\int g_P+
\int\bigl(g_Q-\mathbf 1_{\{x\ge D\}}h_Q\bigr)+
\int\bigl(g_R-\mathbf 1_{\{x\ge1\}}h_R\bigr)
\right]. \tag{5}
\]
Because the \(Q\)-tail is interior,
\[
b=\frac1\Lambda\int_D^{u_Q}h_Q(x)\,dx.
\]
By (3), every point of the \(Q\)-support has nonnegative horizontal coordinate. On \([\ell_Q,D)\), (4) gives \(g_Q\ge h_Q\); on \([D,u_Q]\), it gives \(g_Q-h_Q\ge0\). Therefore the \(Q\)-contribution in (5) is at least
\[
\frac1\Lambda\int_{\ell_Q}^{D}h_Q(x)\,dx=m-b. \tag{6}
\]

Likewise, (2) makes every point of the \(R\)-support nonnegative. Since the \(R\)-tail is interior,
\[
d=\frac1\Lambda\int_1^{u_R}h_R(x)\,dx.
\]
Using (4) on \([\ell_R,1)\) and \(g_R-h_R\ge0\) on \([1,u_R]\), the \(R\)-contribution in (5) is at least
\[
k-d. \tag{7}
\]
The \(P\)-contribution is nonnegative because \(g_P\ge0\). Thus (5), (6), and (7) give
\[
\Omega\ge(m-b)+(k-d)=s-b-d. \tag{8}
\]

The exact crossing and tail identities in accepted fact `b046e3066136e781` also give
\[
\Omega=2t-b-d,\qquad
9t=2(1+s). \tag{9}
\]
Combining (8) and (9) and cancelling \(b+d\) yields
\[
s\le2t=\frac{4(1+s)}9,
\]
hence
\[
s\le\frac45.
\]
This contradicts (1). Therefore every feasible interior/interior system satisfies
\[
\ell_R<0. \tag{10}
\]

Only now use the sign words. An interior \(R\)-tail has \(u_R>1>0\). Thus (10) says that the signs of the two support endpoints of \(R\) are \((-,+)\). By the complete automaton of accepted fact `46afc7eb815ffc5b`, its word must therefore belong to
\[
\mathcal T_{-+}=\mathcal D.
\]
Hence \(\sigma_R\notin\mathcal D\) is impossible.

It remains to count the eliminated reduced systems. In the original interior/interior row, if \(\sigma_R\notin\mathcal D\), the exact retained condition
\[
\sigma_Q\in\mathcal B
\quad\text{or}\quad
\sigma_R\in\mathcal D
\]
forces \(\sigma_Q\in\mathcal B\). Thus the eliminated word triples are exactly
\[
\sigma_P\in\mathcal A,\qquad
\sigma_Q\in\mathcal B,\qquad
\sigma_R\in\mathcal C\setminus\mathcal D. \tag{11}
\]
Since \(\mathcal D\subseteq\mathcal C\), the accepted table counts give
\[
|\mathcal C\setminus\mathcal D|=54-36=18.
\]
Its number of \(\{\mathrm N,\mathrm P\}\)-only words is \(6-3=3\), its number of \(\pi\)-fixed words is \(6-4=2\), and its number of fixed \(\{\mathrm N,\mathrm P\}\)-only words is \(2-1=1\).

Before imposing the mixed-label condition, (11) contains
\[
54\cdot36\cdot18=34992
\]
vectors. Exactly
\[
6\cdot6\cdot3=108
\]
are \(\{\mathrm N,\mathrm P\}\)-only, so there are \(34884\) mixed vectors. The number of fixed mixed vectors is
\[
6\cdot4\cdot2-2\cdot2\cdot1
=48-4=44.
\]
The family (11) is \(\pi\)-invariant. Burnside's formula therefore gives
\[
\frac{34884+44}{2}=17464
\]
lexicographically selected mixed-label systems for each \(\eta\). There are three residual indices, so the exact number removed is
\[
3\cdot17464=52392.
\]
The original condition is retained throughout: it forces \(\sigma_Q\in\mathcal B\) on the eliminated subfamily, while every survivor has \(\sigma_R\in\mathcal D\).
