## Statement

Assume the full transformed triangular system and the below-half singleton-singleton branch supplied by accepted fact 63975845ca0e2926 and the restrictions of accepted fact a5bbbf5d61041b23. Thus \(P_0,P_1,P_2\) are nondegenerate triangles of normalized areas \(A,1,B\), respectively,
\[
q>1,\qquad 0<A<B,\qquad A+B>1,\qquad B<1+A,
\]
\[
t=\frac{2(1+A+B)}9\leq\frac12,\qquad
\frac{P_0+P_2}{2}\subseteq P_1,
\]
and, with
\[
u_i=\frac{|P_0\cap\{x_i\geq0\}|}{A},\qquad
c_i=|P_1\cap\{x_i\geq0\}|,\qquad
v_i=\frac{|P_2\cap\{x_i\geq0\}|}{B},
\]
one has
\[
A u_i+c_i+Bv_i<t\qquad(i=1,2,3).
\tag{1}
\]
The centroid-incidence sets are
\[
I_0=\{1\},\qquad I_1=\{2\},\qquad I_2=\varnothing.
\]
Let \(g_2\) be the centroid of \(P_2\), put
\[
\rho_i:=-(g_2)_i>0,\qquad
W_i:=\operatorname{width}_{x_i}(P_2)\qquad(i=1,2,3),
\]
and retain the notation
\[
d_1=|P_1\cap\{x_1\geq1\}|
\]
from accepted fact a5bbbf5d61041b23.

Then the three large-endpoint cap masses satisfy the exact strict bounds
\[
\boxed{
Bv_1<\frac{2-2A+B}{9}<\frac{4B}{9},
}
\tag{2}
\]
\[
\boxed{
Bv_2<\frac{2(A+B-1)}9<\frac{4B}{9},
}
\tag{3}
\]
\[
\boxed{
Bv_3<\frac{2A+6B-3}{9}<\frac{4B}{9}.
}
\tag{4}
\]
Consequently
\[
\boxed{
W_1<\frac{18B\rho_1}{3B+2A-2},\qquad
W_2<\frac{9B\rho_2}{1+B-A},\qquad
W_3<\frac{18B\rho_3}{3-2(A+B)}.
}
\tag{5}
\]
All denominators in (5) are strictly positive. In particular, for \(f_j(x)=x_1-x_j\),
\[
\boxed{
\operatorname{width}_{f_2}(P_2)
<
\frac{18B\rho_1}{3B+2A-2}
+\frac{9B\rho_2}{1+B-A},
}
\tag{6}
\]
\[
\boxed{
\operatorname{width}_{f_3}(P_2)
<
\frac{18B\rho_1}{3B+2A-2}
+\frac{18B\rho_3}{3-2(A+B)}.
}
\tag{7}
\]
Moreover,
\[
\rho_1+\rho_2+\rho_3=q-1.
\tag{8}
\]
The conclusions include empty caps, support at a vertex or along an edge, and repeated coordinate values.

## Proof

Accepted fact a5bbbf5d61041b23 gives, in the stated orbit,
\[
u_1>\frac49,\qquad
\frac B9<d_1<c_1,\qquad
c_2>\frac49,\qquad
c_2+c_3\geq1-d_1.
\tag{9}
\]
The first strict row budget in (1) and (9) give
\[
\begin{aligned}
Bv_1
&<t-Au_1-c_1\\
&<t-\frac{4A}{9}-\frac B9
=\frac{2-2A+B}{9}.
\end{aligned}
\tag{10}
\]
The gap between \(4B/9\) and the last expression is
\[
\frac{3B+2A-2}{9}
=\frac{2(A+B-1)+B}{9}>0,
\tag{11}
\]
which proves (2) and also proves positivity of the first denominator in (5).

The second strict row budget and \(c_2>4/9\) give
\[
Bv_2<t-c_2< t-\frac49
=\frac{2(A+B-1)}9.
\tag{12}
\]
Here the gap to \(4B/9\) is
\[
\frac{2(1+B-A)}9>0
\tag{13}
\]
because \(B>A\). This proves (3), and the second denominator in (5) is positive.

It remains to retain the information in both of the first two rows when treating the third cap. The last inequality in (9), together with \(d_1<c_1\), gives
\[
c_1+c_2+c_3>1.
\tag{14}
\]
Adding the first two strict row budgets and using \(u_1>4/9\) gives
\[
c_1+c_2<2t-\frac{4A}{9}.
\tag{15}
\]
Therefore
\[
c_3>1-2t+\frac{4A}{9}
=\frac{5-4B}{9}.
\tag{16}
\]
The third strict row budget now yields
\[
Bv_3<t-c_3
<\frac{2A+6B-3}{9}.
\tag{17}
\]
Accepted fact 63975845ca0e2926 gives \(t\leq1/2\), equivalently
\[
A+B\leq\frac54.
\tag{18}
\]
Thus the gap between \(4B/9\) and the right side of (17) is
\[
\frac{3-2(A+B)}9\geq\frac1{18}>0.
\tag{19}
\]
This proves (4) and positivity of the last denominator in (5).

We next prove the support-width conversion used for each coordinate. Let \(T\) be any nondegenerate triangle, let \(\varphi\) be a nonconstant affine functional on \(T\), let \(m\) be its value at the centroid of \(T\), and suppose \(m<0\). Put
\[
w=\operatorname{width}_{\varphi}(T),\qquad
\nu=\frac{|T\cap\{\varphi\geq0\}|}{|T|}.
\]
If \(\nu<4/9\), then
\[
\boxed{
w\leq\frac{2(-m)}{4/9-\nu}.
}
\tag{20}
\]

To prove (20), let \(a\leq b\leq c\) be the three vertex values of \(\varphi\), so \(w=c-a>0\). For thresholds \(r\in[a,b]\), the normalized lower-cap distribution is
\[
\frac{|T\cap\{\varphi\leq r\}|}{|T|}
=\frac{(r-a)^2}{(b-a)(c-a)},
\tag{21}
\]
and for \(r\in[b,c]\), the normalized upper cap is
\[
\frac{|T\cap\{\varphi\geq r\}|}{|T|}
=\frac{(c-r)^2}{(c-a)(c-b)}.
\tag{22}
\]
Consequently the density of \(\varphi\) is everywhere at most \(2/w\). The same conclusion holds when \(a=b\) or \(b=c\), either from the surviving formula or by direct cancellation; points and support edges have zero planar area.

At the centroid threshold \(m=(a+b+c)/3\), the upper cap has normalized area at least \(4/9\). For completeness, translate the values so that \(m=0\). If \(b\leq0<c\), write \(a=-x\), \(b=-y\), where \(x\geq y\geq0\), so \(c=x+y\). Formula (22) gives
\[
\frac{(x+y)^2}{(2x+y)(x+2y)}\geq\frac49,
\tag{23}
\]
because the difference after clearing denominators is \((x-y)^2\geq0\). If \(a<0\leq b\), write \(a=-(b+c)\). Formula (21) gives the upper-cap fraction
\[
1-\frac{(b+c)^2}{(2b+c)(b+2c)}\geq\frac12>\frac49,
\tag{24}
\]
where the weak inequality is equivalent to \(bc\geq0\). These cases include \(b=0\).

Raising the threshold from \(m<0\) to \(0\) removes an interval of length \(-m\) from this one-dimensional distribution. By the density bound, it removes at most \(2(-m)/w\) of the normalized area. Hence
\[
\nu\geq\frac49-\frac{2(-m)}w.
\tag{25}
\]
Since \(4/9-\nu>0\), rearranging (25) proves (20). This argument also covers the case in which the zero threshold lies outside the support, because the density is zero there.

Apply (20) to \(T=P_2\) and \(\varphi=x_i\). Each coordinate is nonconstant on the nondegenerate triangle \(P_2\subset\{\sum_i x_i=1-q\}\), since otherwise \(P_2\) would lie in the intersection of two independent affine planes and would be one-dimensional. Also \(I_2=\varnothing\) gives \((g_2)_i=-\rho_i<0\). Equations (2)-(4) give
\[
\frac49-v_1>
\frac{3B+2A-2}{9B},
\qquad
\frac49-v_2>
\frac{2(1+B-A)}{9B},
\tag{26}
\]
\[
\frac49-v_3>
\frac{3-2(A+B)}{9B}.
\tag{27}
\]
Substitution in (20) proves all three inequalities in (5).

For any two affine functionals, the range of their difference is at most the sum of their ranges. Therefore
\[
\operatorname{width}_{x_1-x_j}(P_2)\leq W_1+W_j
\qquad(j=2,3).
\tag{28}
\]
Equations (5) and (28) prove (6)-(7). Finally, the centroid of \(P_2\) lies in the plane with coordinate sum \(1-q\), so
\[
\rho_1+\rho_2+\rho_3
=-\sum_i(g_2)_i=q-1,
\]
which is (8). No cap-free, active-cell, independent vertex-labeling, or edge-order hypothesis was used.
