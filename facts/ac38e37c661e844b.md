## Statement

Adopt the positive all-double normal-fan and clipping-stratum hypotheses of accepted fact `42e17c270e11ad3e`. Thus
\[
x=(A_0,A_2,K)\in\mathbb R^{2(n_0+n_2+n_K)},\qquad
b_j=\frac{a_{\iota_0(j)}+q_{\iota_2(j)}}2,
\]
and the stratum fixes all individual \(P,Q,R\) chamber codes, persistent cut incidences, and persistent shared normal-fan rays. Use exactly the reduced support families, the sets \(Z,W\), and the bounded codes of accepted fact `7e23b6c690af212a`. Each of \(Z,W\) may have a two-dimensional, rank-one interval, rank-two singleton, or empty code. All strict validity predicates of the chosen codes hold. In particular, every chosen boundedness margin is positive. Put
\[
\mathscr C(x)=|Z(x)|-|W(x)|.
\]
For a two-dimensional code, use its counterclockwise shoelace area and differential. For a rank-one, rank-two, or empty code, use area and differential zero. These conventions give the fixed-code \(C^1\) capacity differential \(D\mathscr C(x)\) in every bounded case.

Let \(\mathcal F_{\rm can}\) be the following finite list of scalar equality functions:

1. every individual \(P,Q,R\) chamber area minus its fixed stratum value;
2. every persistent vertex-cut or marked-wall incidence, every persistent shared-ray determinant, and every other persistent repeated-vertex, collinearity, or positive coincident-support identity declared by the geometric stratum;
3. for each of the fixed \(Z,W\) codes, every scalar identity declared structural in accepted fact `7e23b6c690af212a`. These are the selected support incidences, fixed shared or parallel rays, marked wall incidences, coefficientwise carrier identities \(\sum_{i\in A}\mu_i g_i\equiv0\), the normalization and normal-balance identities of an empty certificate, and every retained triple, endpoint, or singleton incidence.

Auxiliary intersections, carrier points, endpoints, and certificate multipliers are expressed through the fixed rational pivots of their codes before differentiation. Identically zero defining identities contribute zero rows and may be suppressed. The three crossing equalities are not included because accepted fact `89563f2f27529ab0` makes each of them a fixed linear combination of the individual chamber-area equalities. Define
\[
E_{\rm can}(x)=D\mathcal F_{\rm can}(x).
\]

For a supporting edge \(e_s=k_{s+1}-k_s\) of \(K\), orient the common-plane containment slack by
\[
\eta_{sj}(x)=[e_s,b_j-k_s]\geq0,
\qquad I(x)=\{(s,j):\eta_{sj}(x)=0\}.
\]
For a two-dimensional capacity code \(P\in\{Z,W\}\), let
\(h_{\nu i}=-g_i(p_\nu)\). For a rank-one code, let
\(h_i^\pm=-(\alpha_i t_\pm+\kappa_i)\). For a rank-two code, let
\(h_i^0=-g_i(X_0)\). Let \(\mathcal A_P(x)\) contain exactly those
slacks of the applicable type which vanish at \(x\) and are not declared
structural. The empty code has \(\mathcal A_P(x)=\varnothing\). Define
\[
G_{\rm can}(x)=
\begin{pmatrix}
\bigl(D\eta_{sj}(x)\bigr)_{(s,j)\in I(x)}\\
\bigl(Dh(x)\bigr)_{h\in\mathcal A_Z(x)\cup\mathcal A_W(x)}\\
D\mathscr C(x)
\end{pmatrix}.
\tag{1}
\]
Empty blocks are omitted. There are no other rows in \(G_{\rm can}\).
In particular, no strict code-validity predicate, boundedness witness,
or arbitrary valid inequality is differentiated into an inequality row.

Fix an unmarked genuine endpoint-slice vertex \(a_r\in A_0\) and put
\[
\Delta_r(x)=[a_r-a_{r-1},a_{r+1}-a_r]>0,\qquad
\mathcal R(x)=-D\Delta_r(x).
\]
The canonical first-order release system toward deletion of \(a_r\) is
\[
E_{\rm can}(x)\xi=0,\qquad
G_{\rm can}(x)\xi\geq0,\qquad
\mathcal R(x)\xi\geq1.
\tag{2}
\]
Exactly one of the following alternatives holds:
\[
\begin{array}{ll}
\mathrm{(P)}&
\text{system \((2)\) has a solution},\\[1mm]
\mathrm{(D)}&
\text{there are \(y\) and \(\lambda\geq0\) such that}\\
&\displaystyle
\mathcal R(x)=y^{\mathsf T}E_{\rm can}(x)
-\lambda^{\mathsf T}G_{\rm can}(x).
\end{array}
\tag{3}
\]
If the instantiated matrices have rational entries, the feasible
primal or dual certificate may be chosen rational.

The zero predicates have the following exhaustive classification.

* A zero is structural exactly when the fixed stratum declares it
persistent. This includes all equalities in \(\mathcal F_{\rm can}\),
the adjacent input-support incidences, selected capacity-code
incidences, marked wall incidences, fixed shared or parallel rays,
positive coincident supports, carrier identities, empty-certificate
identities, and retained triple, endpoint, or singleton incidences.
Its differential is an equality row of \(E_{\rm can}\), unless it is
an identically zero defining identity.

* A nonstructural zero is an active intrinsic inequality exactly when
it is a common-plane containment slack or one of
\(h_{\nu i},h_i^\pm,h_i^0\). Its differential, with the sign
conventions above, is a row of \(G_{\rm can}\) and is required to be
nonnegative.

* Every other zero is an immediate deletion, boundary, recoding, or
stopping event. For the geometric clipping stratum these are the first
zeros of an undeclared polygon turn, vertex-cut sign, clipping
denominator, edge-through-cut-intersection determinant, cross-fan
determinant, or strict noncontact. The zero \(\Delta_r=0\) is the
deletion event. For a capacity code these are exactly an unmarked input
vertex reaching a coordinate wall, an input support slack or edge
length vanishing, a selected intersection denominator vanishing,
selected vertices colliding, a strict skeleton slack or
\(\Omega_P\) vanishing, a carrier multiplier or fixed rank minor
vanishing, a selected one-dimensional bound slope vanishing, an
interval collapsing, an empty-code pivot, coefficient, or strict
Farkas margin vanishing, or a chosen boundedness margin vanishing.
One recodes before taking another capacity differential. In particular,
a previously strict noncontact reaching zero ends the old stratum. In
the recoded stratum its zero containment slack is an active row of
\(G_{\rm can}\), so the active and event cases do not overlap for one
fixed code.

If a boundedness margin vanishes but another complete positive witness
family exists, this is a bounded-code recoding event. If no such family
exists, the bounded release stops. In particular, a nonempty
two-dimensional unbounded \(Z\) has \(\mathscr C=+\infty\), while an
unbounded lower-dimensional \(Z\) has no fixed capacity formula under
the accepted lemma. Neither case has a finite row
\(D\mathscr C\), and system \((2)\) is not asserted there.

Let the columns of \(N(x)\) be any basis of
\(\ker E_{\rm can}(x)\), and set
\[
\overline{\mathcal R}(x)=\mathcal R(x)N(x),\qquad
\overline G_{\rm can}(x)=G_{\rm can}(x)N(x).
\]
Then the exact remaining first-order condition is
\[
\boxed{\quad
\overline{\mathcal R}(x)\notin
-\operatorname{cone}\{\text{rows of }\overline G_{\rm can}(x)\}.
\quad}
\tag{4}
\]
Condition \((4)\) is equivalent to (P), and its failure is exactly
(D). This assertion is independent of the chosen basis \(N\).

The system and condition \((4)\) are also invariant under positive
\(C^1\) rescaling of a support equation, cyclic relabeling, canonical
orientation reversal, insertion or deletion of repeated boundary
points, subdivision or merging of a collinear side, and replacement
by an equivalent bounded code. For the last assertion, codes are
compared on the common refinement of their declared structural
equalities. On that common structural tangent space they describe the
same moving \(Z,W\), their area differentials agree, and their active
intrinsic slacks define the same feasible tangent cone. A change of
carrier rank, dimension, or structural incidence is instead a
recoding event. No claim is made that \((4)\) holds, that a solution
of \((2)\) integrates to a motion, or that deletion can be iterated.

## Proof

Accepted fact `7e23b6c690af212a` gives
\(\mathscr C=|Z|-|W|\) for bounded \(Z\), together with an exact
rational fixed-code area formula. Its shoelace differentiation gives
the two-dimensional area row. On a persistent rank-one or rank-two
carrier, and on an empty code, the area is identically zero and its
differential is zero. Thus the displayed \(D\mathscr C\) is the
fixed-code \(C^1\) capacity row required by accepted fact
`42e17c270e11ad3e`, including every bounded lower-dimensional case.

The same accepted capacity fact proves the complete code
classification. Every declared incidence or carrier relation is
structural. Apart from those identities, the only weak validity
predicates are the facet-vertex slacks in a two-dimensional code, the
endpoint slacks in a rank-one code, and the point slacks in a rank-two
code. At a zero their exact one-sided feasibility condition is,
respectively, \(Dh_{\nu i}\geq0\), \(Dh_i^\pm\geq0\), or
\(Dh_i^0\geq0\). The singleton row includes the velocity of the
moving carrier point. An empty code has no weak feasibility predicate.
Every remaining validity predicate is strict or nonzero and is listed
as an immediate event in that fact. The boundedness witnesses are
strict validity predicates, not retained feasibility inequalities.
This proves the capacity-code part of the trichotomy and shows that
the middle block of \((1)\) is complete.

Accepted fact `42e17c270e11ad3e` supplies the other rows. Its equality
list is precisely the individual chamber areas and the persistent
incidence, shared-ray, and other stratum identities. Adding the
persistent capacity-code identities only records the fixed
capacity-code stratum and therefore gives \(E_{\rm can}\).
Accepted fact `89563f2f27529ab0` expresses each crossing functional as
a fixed linear combination of individual chamber areas, so no
crossing row is needed. The active containment slacks have the
orientation \(\eta_{sj}\geq0\). The accepted release lemma states
that strict turns, cut signs, denominators, and noncontacts are events,
not tangent inequalities. Combining this with the capacity
classification leaves exactly the rows in \((1)\). Hence its general
release system, with its formerly arbitrary feasibility family
specialized to the intrinsic active slacks, is exactly \((2)\).

For completeness, put
\[
\mathcal K=\{\xi:E_{\rm can}\xi=0,\ G_{\rm can}\xi\geq0\}.
\]
If (D) holds, then every \(\xi\in\mathcal K\) satisfies
\(\mathcal R\xi=-\lambda^{\mathsf T}G_{\rm can}\xi\leq0\), so (P)
is impossible. Conversely, if (D) fails, separation from the closed
polyhedral cone
\[
\{y^{\mathsf T}E_{\rm can}-\lambda^{\mathsf T}G_{\rm can}:
y\ \text{free},\ \lambda\geq0\}
\]
gives \(\xi\) with \(E_{\rm can}\xi=0\),
\(G_{\rm can}\xi\geq0\), and \(\mathcal R\xi>0\).
Scaling \(\xi\) proves (P). This proves the exclusive exact
alternative \((3)\), with the displayed signs.

Writing \(\xi=Nz\), the unnormalized primal condition is
\(\overline G_{\rm can}z\geq0\) and
\(\overline{\mathcal R}z>0\). Applying the same alternative in the
reduced coordinates shows that it is feasible exactly when there is
no \(\lambda\geq0\) with
\(\overline{\mathcal R}=-\lambda^{\mathsf T}
\overline G_{\rm can}\), which is \((4)\). Replacing \(N\) by
\(NQ\), with \(Q\) invertible, right-multiplies both reduced row
families by \(Q\) and leaves this equality unchanged.

Finally, at a zero of a rescaled support,
\[
D(\alpha g)=\alpha\,Dg\qquad(\alpha>0),
\]
so equality rows change by nonzero scaling and active inequality rows
by positive scaling. The halfspaces, intersections, areas, and
capacity are unchanged. The code-equivalence assertions of accepted
fact `7e23b6c690af212a` give equality of the area differentials on
the common structural tangent space, and its weak-slack
classification gives the same intrinsic feasible tangent cone.
Indeed, two-dimensional codes have the same exposed vertices and
facets up to cyclic order, canonical reversal, and choices at
persistent multiple incidences. Rank-one codes parameterize the same
two endpoints, rank-two codes use the same point, and empty codes have
no intrinsic rows. After passing to the common structural refinement,
their weak rows are therefore the same geometric support slacks up to
relabeling and positive scaling.
Consequently (P), (D), and \((4)\) are unchanged by every listed code
operation. The accepted boundedness classification gives the stated
recode-or-stop rule and completes the proof without asserting cone
exclusion.
