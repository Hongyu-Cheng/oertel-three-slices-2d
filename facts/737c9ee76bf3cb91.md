## Statement

Let \(f_1,f_2,f_3:\mathbb R^2\to\mathbb R\) be nonconstant affine
functions with pairwise nonparallel linear parts and
\[
f_1+f_2+f_3=-1.
\tag{1}
\]
Let \(K\subset\mathbb R^2\) be a compact two-dimensional convex polygon.
For \(j\in\{0,2\}\), choose real numbers \(b_{1j},b_{2j},b_{3j}\), put
\[
r_j=b_{1j}+b_{2j}+b_{3j}-1>0,
\tag{2}
\]
and assume that the endpoint bodies have the common-row facet form
\[
A_j=\bigcap_{i=1}^3\{z:f_i(z)\ge-b_{ij}\}.
\tag{3}
\]
Assume also the exact compatibility condition
\[
\frac{A_0+A_2}{2}\subseteq K.
\tag{4}
\]
Write
\[
M=|A_0|\ge m=|A_2|>0,\qquad k=|K|,
\qquad t=\frac{2(M+m+k)}9,
\tag{5}
\]
and suppose \(0<t<M\) and \(M<m+k\).  Put
\[
c_i=|K\cap\{f_i\le0\}|\qquad(i=1,2,3).
\tag{6}
\]
Take the support-contact level
\[
L_1=\min_{A_0}f_1=-b_{10},
\qquad
v_1=|A_2\cap\{f_1\le-L_1\}|,
\tag{7}
\]
and assume the one-tail equality and strict contact failure
\[
a:=t-c_1=m,\qquad c_1+v_1<t.
\tag{8}
\]
For \(i=2,3\), assume that
\[
L_i=\inf\left\{\ell:
c_i+|A_0\cap\{f_i\le\ell\}|
+|A_2\cap\{f_i\le-\ell\}|<t\right\}
\tag{9}
\]
is a genuine exact lower crossing.  Thus, with
\[
u_i=|A_0\cap\{f_i\le L_i\}|,\qquad
v_i=|A_2\cap\{f_i\le-L_i\}|,\qquad
d_i=t-c_i,
\tag{10}
\]
one has
\[
u_i+v_i=d_i,\qquad v_i>0,\qquad
d_2\le d_3<m.
\tag{11}
\]
Then no such data exist.  In particular, there is no rational
calibration in this facet-triangle family, whether either lower crossing
is empty-proper or both are proper-proper.

## Proof

Because the linear parts of \(f_1,f_2\) are nonparallel, the affine map
\[
z\longmapsto(f_1(z),f_2(z),f_3(z))
\tag{12}
\]
is an affine bijection from \(\mathbb R^2\) to the plane
\(\{w_1+w_2+w_3=-1\}\).  For fixed \(j\), adding
\((b_{1j},b_{2j},b_{3j})\) maps \(A_j\) to
\[
\Delta_{r_j}=\{g\in\mathbb R_{\ge0}^3:g_1+g_2+g_3=r_j\}.
\tag{13}
\]
Consequently there is a constant \(\kappa>0\), depending only on the
linear parts in (1), such that
\[
M=\kappa r_0^2,\qquad m=\kappa r_2^2.
\tag{14}
\]
Set
\[
\rho=\frac{r_0}{r_2}=\sqrt{\frac Mm}\ge1.
\tag{15}
\]

For \(0\le z\le1\), a coordinate cap
\(\{g_i\le zr\}\) of \(\Delta_r\) has relative area
\[
\Phi(z)=1-(1-z)^2=2z-z^2,
\qquad \Phi(z)\ge z.
\tag{16}
\]
For \(i=2,3\), define
\[
\alpha_i=\frac{L_i+b_{i0}}{r_0},
\qquad
\beta_i=\frac{-L_i+b_{i2}}{r_2}.
\tag{17}
\]
Since \(u_i\le d_i<m\le M\), the \(A_0\)-cap is not full, so
\(\alpha_i<1\).  Since \(0<v_i\le d_i<m\), one has
\(0<\beta_i<1\).  Therefore, with
\(x_i=\max\{\alpha_i,0\}\),
\[
\frac{u_i}{M}=\Phi(x_i),
\qquad
\frac{v_i}{m}=\Phi(\beta_i).
\tag{18}
\]
The crossing level cancels in the common support sum:
\[
P_i:=b_{i0}+b_{i2}
=r_0\alpha_i+r_2\beta_i.
\tag{19}
\]
Equations (15), (16), and (18) imply
\[
\begin{aligned}
\frac{d_i}{m}
&=\rho^2\Phi(x_i)+\Phi(\beta_i)\\
&\ge \rho^2x_i+\beta_i
\ge \frac{P_i}{r_2}.
\end{aligned}
\tag{20}
\]
The last inequality is valid also when \(\alpha_i<0\), because then
\(x_i=0\) and
\(\beta_i>\rho\alpha_i+\beta_i=P_i/r_2\).  Since \(d_i\ge0\), (20)
therefore gives the uniform bound
\[
\frac{d_i}{m}\ge
\max\left\{0,\frac{P_i}{r_2}\right\}.
\tag{21}
\]
This is the common-support constraint missed by a scalar cap table.

The maximum of \(f_1\) on \(A_2\) is \(r_2-b_{12}\).  Equations
(7)-(8) give \(v_1<m\), so the threshold \(b_{10}=-L_1\) lies strictly
below that maximum.  Hence
\[
P_1=b_{10}+b_{12}<r_2.
\tag{22}
\]
On the other hand, (2) gives the exact conservation identity
\[
P_1+P_2+P_3
=(1+r_0)+(1+r_2)=2+r_0+r_2.
\tag{23}
\]
Combining (22), (23), and \(r_0\ge r_2\) yields
\[
P_2+P_3>2+r_0>r_2.
\tag{24}
\]

In the layer \(a=t-c_1=m\) and \(d_2\le d_3<m\), the nondecreasing
rearrangement in accepted fact `4e176b68a1636efc` is
\((d_2,d_3,m)\).  Since \(M<m+k\), that fact gives the strict survivor
gate
\[
4d_2+5d_3<4m.
\tag{25}
\]
But (21) and (24) give the opposite strict inequality:
\[
\begin{aligned}
4d_2+5d_3
&\ge \frac{m}{r_2}
\left(4\max\{P_2,0\}+5\max\{P_3,0\}\right)\\
&\ge \frac{4m}{r_2}
\left(\max\{P_2,0\}+\max\{P_3,0\}\right)\\
&\ge \frac{4m}{r_2}(P_2+P_3)
>4m.
\end{aligned}
\tag{26}
\]
This contradicts (25).  The argument allowed \(x_i=0\), so it covers
empty-proper crossings, and it also allows \(x_i>0\), so it covers the
proper-proper calibration stratum.  Compatibility (4) was retained
throughout; the contradiction is stronger and occurs before any facet
of \(K\) can repair the endpoint support-sum mismatch.
