## Proof

At the four \(A_0\) vertices the \(p\)-coordinates are
\[
a_0=(-2,150),\qquad a_1=(0,1),\qquad
a_2=(u,r),\qquad a_3=(0,149).
\]
The fixed stratum has precisely the two persistent cut incidences
\(p_1(a_1)=p_1(a_3)=0\).  Thus \(a_1,a_3\) are marked, while
\(a_0,a_2\) are genuine unmarked vertices.  Removing the already tested
\(a_0\) leaves the singleton eligible set \(\{a_2\}\).

The line \(p_1=0\) divides \(A_0\) into the two triangles
\[
P_{\varnothing}=\operatorname{conv}\{a_1,a_2,a_3\},
\qquad
P_{\{1\}}=\operatorname{conv}\{a_3,a_0,a_1\}.
\]
Their signed fixed-code extensions are
\[
|P_{\varnothing}|=\frac12[a_2-a_1,a_3-a_1],
\qquad
|P_{\{1\}}|=\frac12[a_0-a_3,a_1-a_3].
\]
Differentiating these two formulas and the two incidence functions
gives the displayed block \(E_P\).

For \(Q\), the only positive cells are
\(Q_{12},Q_{13},Q_{23},Q_{123}\).  For \(R\), all seven nonempty sign
sets occur.  Substitution of the exact rational vertices into the
intersection and shoelace formulas in the statement gives exactly the
displayed blocks \(E_Q,E_R\).  The other chamber codes are separated
from the polygons by strict sign predicates, so their area functions
are identically zero near the realization on this stratum.

The strict audit in accepted fact `ac12ad62775a1d5f` excludes every
other vertex-cut incidence, shared normal-fan ray, repeated vertex,
collinearity, and containment contact.  Its capacity code is the same
transverse triangle for \(Z\) and \(W\).  After its pairwise support
intersections are eliminated by the displayed rational pivots, its
selected support incidences are defining identities and contribute
zero rows.  There is no retained triple incidence or marked input-wall
incidence.  Thus the matrix in the statement contains every nonzero
canonical equality row.  Crossing rows are fixed linear combinations
of its chamber rows by the conventions of accepted facts
`42e17c270e11ad3e` and `ac38e37c661e844b`, so they add no row-space
condition.

For the remaining eligible vertex,
\[
\begin{aligned}
\Delta_2
&=[a_2-a_1,a_3-a_2]\\
&=[a_2-a_1,a_3-a_1]
=2|P_{\varnothing}|.
\end{aligned}
\]
This is an identity of the signed fixed-code extensions, not merely a
numerical equality at the realization.  Differentiation therefore gives
\[
\mathcal R_2=-D\Delta_2=-2D|P_{\varnothing}|,
\]
and direct substitution of \(a_1,a_2,a_3\) gives the rational row
displayed in the statement.

Finally, accepted fact `ac12ad62775a1d5f` gives no nonzero canonical
inequality row: there is no containment contact, no active intrinsic
capacity slack, and \(D(|Z|-|W|)=0\).  Hence for every
\(\xi\in\ker E_{\rm can}\),
\[
\mathcal R_2\xi=-2D|P_{\varnothing}|\xi=0.
\]
In particular no equality-preserving velocity can have positive
release.  The multiplier vector with entry \(-2\) on
\(D|P_{\varnothing}|\) and zero elsewhere is the required exact
rational equality stress for the only eligible alternative ear.
