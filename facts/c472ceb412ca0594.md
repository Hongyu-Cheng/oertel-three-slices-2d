## Statement

Let \(N=\{1,2,3\}\). Let \(f_1,f_2,f_3\) be nonconstant affine functions on \(\mathbb R^2\), with pairwise nonparallel linear parts, satisfying
\[
f_1+f_2+f_3=-1.
\]
Let \(L_1+L_2+L_3<-1\), put
\[
\delta=-1-(L_1+L_2+L_3)>0,
\qquad
p_i=f_i-L_i,
\qquad
q_i=f_i+L_i,
\]
and let \(A_0,A_2,K\) be nonempty two-dimensional convex polytopes with
\[
(A_0+A_2)/2\subseteq K.
\]
Define
\[
P_0=A_0\cap\{p_i>0\text{ for every }i\},
\]
\[
Q_J=A_2\cap\{q_i\le0\ (i\in J),\ q_i>0\ (i\notin J)\},
\]
and
\[
R_G=K\cap\{f_i\le0\ (i\in G),\ f_i>0\ (i\notin G)\}.
\]
Fix \(j\in N\), write \(N\setminus\{j\}=\{k,\ell\}\), and assume
\[
|P_0|>0,\qquad |Q_{\{j\}}|>0,\qquad |Q_{\{k,\ell\}}|>0.
\]
Set
\[
E=\overline{Q_{\{j\}}},
\qquad
F=\overline{Q_{\{k,\ell\}}},
\qquad
H=\operatorname{conv}(E\cup F).
\]
Then
\[
\frac{P_0+E}{2}\subseteq\{f_j<-1\},
\qquad
\frac{P_0+F}{2}\subseteq\{f_j>0\},
\]
while the slab subset
\[
D:=\frac{P_0+H}{2}\cap\{-1<f_j<0\}
\]
satisfies
\[
|D|>0.
\]
Consequently,
\[
\frac{P_0+H}{2}
\ne
\frac{P_0+E}{2}\cup\frac{P_0+F}{2}
\quad\text{even up to a null set}.
\]
The proved positive-area slab subset satisfies
\[
D\subseteq R_{\{j,k\}}\cup R_{\{j,\ell\}}\cup R_N.
\]

## Proof

The canonical identities from accepted fact `0e51a47a9add571f` give
\[
\sum_i p_i=\delta,
\qquad
\sum_i q_i=-\delta-2,
\qquad
2f_i\!\left(\frac{x+y}{2}\right)=p_i(x)+q_i(y).
\tag{1}
\]
If \(x\in P_0\), then all \(p_i(x)>0\), so
\[
0<p_j(x)<\delta.
\tag{2}
\]
If \(e\in E\), closure of the \(\{j\}\)-cell gives
\[
q_k(e)\ge0,\qquad q_\ell(e)\ge0.
\]
Hence
\[
q_j(e)=-\delta-2-q_k(e)-q_\ell(e)\le-\delta-2.
\]
By (1) and (2),
\[
2f_j\!\left(\frac{x+e}{2}\right)
=p_j(x)+q_j(e)<\delta-(\delta+2)=-2.
\]
Thus
\[
(P_0+E)/2\subseteq\{f_j<-1\}.
\tag{3}
\]

If \(v\in F\), closure of the \(\{k,\ell\}\)-cell gives \(q_j(v)\ge0\). Therefore
\[
2f_j\!\left(\frac{x+v}{2}\right)
=p_j(x)+q_j(v)>0,
\]
and hence
\[
(P_0+F)/2\subseteq\{f_j>0\}.
\tag{4}
\]

Because \(P_0\) is convex and has positive area, choose \(x_0\in P_0\) and \(r>0\) such that
\[
B(x_0,r)\subseteq P_0.
\]
Choose \(e\in E\) and \(v\in F\), and put
\[
h(t)=(1-t)e+tv\in H.
\]
The affine function
\[
\phi(t)=p_j(x_0)+q_j(h(t))
\]
satisfies \(\phi(0)<-2\) by (3) and \(\phi(1)>0\) by (4). Hence some \(t_0\in(0,1)\) satisfies \(\phi(t_0)=-1\). For
\[
z_0=\frac{x_0+h(t_0)}2
\]
identity (1) gives \(f_j(z_0)=-1/2\). Moreover,
\[
B(z_0,r/2)
=
\frac{B(x_0,r)+h(t_0)}2
\subseteq
\frac{P_0+H}{2}.
\]
After shrinking the radius, continuity of \(f_j\) gives an open ball about \(z_0\) contained in \(\{-1<f_j<0\}\). Therefore
\[
|D|
=
\left|\frac{P_0+H}{2}\cap\{-1<f_j<0\}\right|
>0.
\tag{5}
\]
By (3) and (4), \(D\) is disjoint from both endpoint images. Those endpoint images are also mutually disjoint, so
\[
\left|\frac{P_0+H}{2}\right|
\ge
\left|\frac{P_0+E}{2}\right|
+
\left|\frac{P_0+F}{2}\right|
+
|D|,
\]
which proves the strict area inequality and rules out saturation.

Finally, \(H\subseteq A_2\), so compatibility places \(D\) inside \(K\). At every point of \(D\),
\[
-1<f_j<0,
\qquad
f_k+f_\ell=-1-f_j<0.
\]
Thus \(j\) and at least one of \(k,\ell\) belong to the exact nonpositive-coordinate set. Under the exact convention of accepted fact `0e51a47a9add571f`,
\[
D\subseteq R_{\{j,k\}}\cup R_{\{j,\ell\}}\cup R_N.
\]
Since \(|D|>0\), at least one of
\[
D\cap R_{\{j,k\}},
\qquad
D\cap R_{\{j,\ell\}},
\qquad
D\cap R_N
\]
has positive area. This positive portion is disjoint from both endpoint images, and the corresponding coefficients \(3|G|-2\) are \(4,4,7\). Accepted fact `78d1d57e3961c4e4` separately ensures that the assumed opposite positive-area pair has genuine positive-area outer bridge cells; the conclusion proved here concerns only the displayed slab subset \(D\).
