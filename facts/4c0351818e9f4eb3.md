## Statement

Let \(K_0'\subset\mathbb R^2\) be a compact convex body in area-preserving affine coordinates \((s,t)\). Suppose that \(O=(0,0)\) is a boundary vertex, its two adjacent exposed edges have other endpoints
\[
F=(-A,B),\qquad E=(C,-D),
\qquad A,B,C,D>0,
\]
the positive \(t\)-axis is on the exterior side of the edge \([O,F]\), the positive \(s\)-axis is on the exterior side of the edge \([E,O]\), and
\[
K_0'\cap\{s>0,\ t>0\}=\varnothing.
\]
For any compact convex \(K'\supseteq K_0'\), put
\[
\Delta=|K'\setminus K_0'|,\qquad
e_2=|(K'\setminus K_0')\cap\{s\leq0\}|,\qquad
e_3=|(K'\setminus K_0')\cap\{t\leq0\}|.
\]
If
\[
e_2<\frac{AB}{2},\qquad e_3<\frac{CD}{2},
\]
then necessarily
\[
\boxed{\quad AD(\Delta-e_2-e_3)\leq4e_2e_3.\quad} \tag{1}
\]

For the explicit \(K_0\), \(\Delta=320\), and halfplanes \(H_i\) of accepted fact `6cedf2fe59ea9b71`, take
\[
(s,t)=(f_2,f_3)=(y,-1-x-y).
\]
Then \(A=4064/75\), \(B=10659/200\), \(C=10351/200\), and \(D=7897/150\). Hence every vector
\[
(e_1,e_2,e_3)\in\mathcal E_{K_0}(\Delta)
\]
whose last two coordinates satisfy the displayed threshold conditions must satisfy
\[
\boxed{\quad
\frac{16046704}{5625}(\Delta-e_2-e_3)\leq4e_2e_3.
\quad} \tag{2}
\]
The vector
\[
\left(
\frac{79790321}{259200},
\frac{64660331}{8424000},
\frac{250146401}{23976000}
\right)
\]
violates (2), so it is not in \(\mathcal E_{K_0}(320)\).

## Proof

First prove the general inequality. Define the two positive-axis reaches
\[
T=\max\{t\geq0:(0,t)\in K'\},\qquad
S=\max\{s\geq0:(s,0)\in K'\}.
\]
They exist because \(K'\) is compact and both defining sets contain \(0\). Convexity gives
\[
\operatorname{conv}\{O,F,(0,T)\}\subseteq K'.
\]
The positive \(t\)-axis is exterior to the exposed edge \([O,F]\), so this triangle is disjoint from \(K_0'\), apart from an area-zero subset of the supporting line. It lies in \(\{s\leq0\}\) and has area \(AT/2\). Therefore
\[
e_2\geq\frac{AT}{2}. \tag{3}
\]
Likewise, the triangle \(\operatorname{conv}\{O,E,(S,0)\}\) is an added triangle in \(\{t\leq0\}\), of area \(DS/2\), and hence
\[
e_3\geq\frac{DS}{2}. \tag{4}
\]
The threshold hypotheses and (3)--(4) imply
\[
T<B,\qquad S<C. \tag{5}
\]

Let \(P=(s,t)\in K'\) have \(s,t>0\). The segment \([F,P]\subseteq K'\) meets the positive \(t\)-axis at height
\[
t_P=\frac{At+Bs}{A+s}.
\]
Thus \(t_P\leq T\). If \(t>T\), then both \(t\) and \(B\) would be strictly larger than \(T\), so their displayed positive weighted average would also be larger than \(T\), a contradiction. Hence \(t\leq T\). Similarly, the segment \([E,P]\) meets the positive \(s\)-axis at
\[
s_P=\frac{Ds+Ct}{D+t}\leq S.
\]
Since \(C>S\), the same weighted-average argument gives \(s\leq S\). Consequently,
\[
|K'\cap\{s>0,\ t>0\}|\leq ST. \tag{6}
\]
Because \(K_0'\) misses the open positive quadrant, and because the complement of that quadrant is \(\{s\leq0\}\cup\{t\leq0\}\), subadditivity gives
\[
\Delta-e_2-e_3
\leq |(K'\setminus K_0')\cap\{s>0,\ t>0\}|
=|K'\cap\{s>0,\ t>0\}|. \tag{7}
\]
Combining (3), (4), (6), and (7) yields
\[
\Delta-e_2-e_3
\leq ST
\leq \frac{4e_2e_3}{AD},
\]
which proves (1).

Now specialize. The map
\[
(x,y)\longmapsto(s,t)=(y,-1-x-y)
\]
has Jacobian determinant \(1\), so it preserves area, and it sends \(H_2,H_3\) to \(\{s\leq0\},\{t\leq0\}\), respectively. Using the counterclockwise vertex list in accepted fact `6cedf2fe59ea9b71`, the transformed vertices of \(K_0\) are
\[
\begin{aligned}
O&=(0,0),\\
F&=\left(-\frac{4064}{75},\frac{10659}{200}\right),\\
G&=\left(-\frac{16607}{300},\frac{9993}{200}\right),\\
H&=\left(\frac{10117}{200},-\frac{16793}{300}\right),\\
E&=\left(\frac{10351}{200},-\frac{7897}{150}\right).
\end{aligned}
\]
Thus \([O,F]\) and \([E,O]\) are the adjacent boundary edges required above. For \(T,S>0\),
\[
\det(F,(0,T))=-AT<0,
\]
and
\[
\det(O-E,(S,0)-E)=-DS<0,
\]
so the corresponding positive coordinate rays are on the exterior sides of those two counterclockwise boundary edges. Also \(s+t=-1-x\), while every vertex of \(K_0\) has \(x\geq-1\). Convexity therefore gives \(s+t\leq0\) throughout \(K_0\), proving that \(K_0\) misses the open positive quadrant. All hypotheses of (1) hold, and
\[
AD=\frac{4064}{75}\frac{7897}{150}
=\frac{16046704}{5625},
\]
which gives (2).

For the proposed last two marginals, write
\[
X=\frac{64660331}{8424000},\qquad
Y=\frac{250146401}{23976000}.
\]
Their exact threshold gaps are
\[
\frac{AB}{2}-X
=\frac{60495417449}{42120000}>0,
\qquad
\frac{CD}{2}-Y
=\frac{162069478301}{119880000}>0.
\]
Thus (2) is necessary. But direct rational reduction gives
\[
\frac{16046704}{5625}(320-X-Y)-4XY
=\frac{43469747358957186677}{50493456000000}>0,
\]
which is the strict reverse of (2). Therefore no compact convex extension of \(K_0\) with added area \(320\) has the prescribed crossing increments, proving the asserted nonmembership.
