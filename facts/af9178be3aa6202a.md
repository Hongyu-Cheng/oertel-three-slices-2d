## Statement

Let \(\mathcal F\) be the class of all full common-plane realizations
satisfying the complete canonical finite-interior hypotheses, exact-cell
convention, \(M\geq m\), the three crossings, the canonical tails,
\(W=0\), the all-double positive \(Q\)-support
\[
\{123,12,13,23\},
\]
and \(r_1r_2r_3r_{123}>0\).  Write
\[
q=q_{123},\qquad
x_i=q_{\{1,2,3\}\setminus\{i\}},\qquad
\eta=s_P+4d_P+3d_R+6r_{123}.
\]
If \(\mathcal F\) is nonempty, then
\[
c_*:=
\inf_{\mathcal R\in\mathcal F}\frac{\eta(\mathcal R)}{q(\mathcal R)}
>0.
\tag{1}
\]
Thus every \(\mathcal R\in\mathcal F\) satisfies
\[
\frac{\eta}{q}\geq c_*,
\qquad
\frac{r_{123}}q+\frac{\eta}{q}\geq c_*.
\tag{2}
\]
The constant \(c_*\) is the greatest universal lower bound on
\(\eta/q\), although accepted fact `444fb905d0cc5122` does not make its
numerical value effective.

Assume henceforth that \(\mathcal F\) is nonempty and use the constant
\(c_*\) from (1).  If \(\mathcal F\) is empty, there is no \(W=0\)
realization to exclude.

For a positive cap-ratio triple put
\[
\lambda_i=\frac{x_i}{q},\qquad
R=\lambda_1+\lambda_2+\lambda_3,
\]
\[
g(\lambda_1,\lambda_2,\lambda_3)
=
\min_{S\subseteq\{1,2,3\}}
\left(
2\sum_{i\notin S}\lambda_i+
\sum_{\{i,j\}\subseteq S}\sqrt{\lambda_i\lambda_j}
\right),
\]
\[
\epsilon(\lambda_1,\lambda_2,\lambda_3)
=
\max\left\{
0,\
\min\left\{
\frac32,\
\frac{1-R}{2}+g(\lambda_1,\lambda_2,\lambda_3)
\right\}
\right\}.
\tag{3}
\]
For \(\{i,j,k\}=\{1,2,3\}\), define
\[
A_k=\frac{1+R}{\lambda_k},\qquad
b_k=2\sqrt{\lambda_i\lambda_j},
\qquad
d_{1k}=\frac{4A_kb_k^2}{(A_k+b_k^2)^2},
\]
\[
d_{2k}
=1-\operatorname{clip}\left(
\frac{\sqrt{A_k}-b_k}{1+b_k}
\right)^2,
\qquad
\operatorname{clip}(s)=\min\{1,\max\{0,s\}\},
\]
\[
d_0=
\min\left\{
1,\
\frac3{\lambda_1^{-1}+\lambda_2^{-1}+\lambda_3^{-1}}
\right\},
\qquad
\delta=
\min\{d_0,d_{11},d_{12},d_{13},d_{21},d_{22},d_{23}\}.
\tag{4}
\]
Define
\[
\tau(R)=
\frac{49}{
\left(\sqrt{1+R}+\sqrt{204+120R}\right)^2},
\tag{5}
\]
\[
\tau_c(R)=
\frac{49}{
\left(
\sqrt{1+R}+\sqrt{204+120R+28c_*}
\right)^2},
\tag{6}
\]
\[
\tau_\epsilon(\lambda)=
\frac{49}{
\left(
\sqrt{1+R}+\sqrt{204+120R+28\epsilon(\lambda)}
\right)^2},
\tag{7}
\]
and
\[
\tau_*(\lambda)
=
\frac{49}{
\left(
\sqrt{1+R}
+
\sqrt{204+120R+
28\max\{c_*,\epsilon(\lambda)\}}
\right)^2}.
\tag{8}
\]
Then
\[
\tau_*(\lambda)
=\min\{\tau_c(R),\tau_\epsilon(\lambda)\}
\leq\tau_c(R)<\tau(R).
\tag{9}
\]
Every realization in \(\mathcal F\) necessarily satisfies
\[
\boxed{\ \delta(\lambda)<\tau_*(\lambda).\ }
\tag{10}
\]
Equivalently, the whole closed region
\[
\delta(\lambda)\geq\tau_*(\lambda)
\tag{11}
\]
is excluded.  Inside the old open survivor
\(\delta<\tau\) from accepted fact `c44e67089f10e853`, the exact strip
\[
\boxed{\quad
\tau_*(\lambda)\leq\delta(\lambda)<\tau(R)
\quad}
\tag{12}
\]
is therefore excluded, including equality at
\(\delta=\tau_*\).  On the region \(\epsilon>0\),
\(\tau_\epsilon\) is exactly the threshold
\(\tau_{\mathrm{def}}\) of accepted fact `0009896ebff12f31`; hence (8)
is the exact combination of the global geometric separation and that
pointwise defect threshold.

No compactness hypothesis on \((\lambda_1,\lambda_2,\lambda_3)\) is
needed.  The uniform bound (1) includes unbounded ratio sequences,
ratios approaching the boundary of the positive orthant, every
coincident-endpoint stratum, and every proper-cell boundary covered by
accepted fact `444fb905d0cc5122`.  The only ratio region not excluded by
this argument is the open set \(\delta<\tau_*\).

## Proof

If \(\mathcal F\) is empty, all exclusion conclusions are immediate.
Assume it is nonempty.  Every member has \(q>0\), and all cell areas
scale by the same factor, so the ratios in (1) are scale invariant.

Suppose that the infimum in (1) were zero.  For every positive integer
\(n\), choose \(\mathcal R_n\in\mathcal F\) such that
\[
\frac{\eta_n}{q_n}<\frac1n.
\]
Since
\[
\eta_n=s_{P,n}+4d_{P,n}+3d_{R,n}+6r_{123,n}
\geq6r_{123,n},
\]
we would also have
\[
0<
\frac{r_{123,n}}{q_n}
\leq
\frac1{6}\frac{\eta_n}{q_n}
\longrightarrow0.
\]
Thus both \(r_{123,n}/q_n\) and \(\eta_n/q_n\) would tend to zero,
contradicting accepted fact `444fb905d0cc5122`.  This proves (1).
Equation (2) follows immediately.  Since \(c_*\) is an infimum, it is
the largest constant for which the first inequality in (2) holds for
every member of \(\mathcal F\).

Fix a realization in \(\mathcal F\), and abbreviate
\[
\rho=\frac{r_{123}}q,\qquad
\mu=\frac{\eta}{q}.
\]
Accepted fact `0009896ebff12f31` gives
\[
\mu>\epsilon(\lambda),
\]
while (1) gives \(\mu\geq c_*\).  Consequently
\[
\mu\geq\max\{c_*,\epsilon(\lambda)\}.
\tag{13}
\]

Put
\[
D=\frac q{|\mathcal T_Q|}.
\]
Apply the exact scalar bridge of accepted fact
`084bf2f472bf29c7` with
\(|\mathcal T_Q|=q/D\), and divide by \(q\).  Since
\(m/q=1+R\), it gives
\[
\frac1D
\geq
\left(
2\sqrt\rho+
\frac{
\sqrt{1+R}+
\sqrt{204+120R+28\mu}
}{7}
\right)^2.
\tag{14}
\]
Here \(\rho>0\).  Dropping its strictly positive term and then using
(13) yields
\[
\frac1D
>
\frac1{49}
\left(
\sqrt{1+R}
+
\sqrt{
204+120R+
28\max\{c_*,\epsilon(\lambda)\}
}
\right)^2.
\]
All quantities are positive, so this is exactly
\[
D<\tau_*(\lambda).
\tag{15}
\]

Accepted fact `6aa12d1b76699246` applies to the central \(Q\)-cell and
the three pairwise interior-disjoint positive \(Q\)-caps on every
endpoint stratum.  With the definitions (4), it gives
\[
D\geq\delta(\lambda).
\tag{16}
\]
Combining (15) and (16) proves (10) and excludes (11), including its
equality boundary.

The denominator in (8) uses the maximum of \(c_*\) and \(\epsilon\).
Since the displayed threshold is strictly decreasing in that last
argument,
\[
\tau_*=\min\{\tau_c,\tau_\epsilon\}.
\]
Also \(c_*>0\), so \(\tau_c<\tau\).  This proves (9).
Accepted fact `c44e67089f10e853` excluded
\(\delta\geq\tau\), while `0009896ebff12f31` excluded
\(\delta\geq\tau_\epsilon=\tau_{\mathrm{def}}\) where
\(\epsilon>0\).  Thus (12) and the claimed exact combination follow.

No ratio compactness was used in the infimum argument.  The contradiction
from `444fb905d0cc5122` quantifies over arbitrary sequences of full
realizations and already includes all ratio and geometric boundary
escapes.  Hence \(\delta<\tau_*\) is the full remaining ratio region for
this combination of accepted bounds.
