## Statement

Use the common-plane coordinates and physical Jacobian of accepted facts `2b42506f5de09be1` and `989c9b585d2b42cc`, the full \(A_2\) chord coordinates of accepted fact `fa0e57b225178b3b`, and the complete opposite hypotheses and notation of accepted fact `18e8f69f5a782c16`.  Thus
\[
p_j=X,\qquad p_\ell=Y,\qquad p_k=\delta-X-Y,
\]
coordinate area in \((p_j,p_\ell)\) is \(\Delta>0\) times physical area,
\[
B=\beta+H=\delta-\tau,\qquad G=\gamma+K,
\]
and the relevant full chords are
\[
A_0\cap\{p_\ell=0\}
=\{(X,Y):Y=0,\ \beta\le X\le B\},
\]
\[
A_0\cap\{p_k=0\}
=\{(\delta+t,-t):\gamma\le t\le G\},
\]
where \(H,K>0\) and \(\tau,\gamma\ge0\).  On \(A_2\), with
\[
U=-q_k,\qquad V=q_\ell,\qquad q_j=U-V-(\delta+2),
\]
the full \(q_j=0\) chord is
\[
(U,V)=(\delta+2-t,-t),
\qquad w_0\le t\le w_0+K_2,
\]
where \(w_0\ge0\) and \(K_2>0\).

For arbitrary actual compact convex endpoint polygons realizing these chords, put
\[
\mathcal B=\frac{A_0+A_2}{2}.
\]
Then
\[
|\mathcal B\cap R_\ell|>0
\]
or
\[
|\mathcal B\cap R_{k\ell}|
\ge
\frac{\tau K+2K_2(\tau+G)}{8\Delta}.
\]
In particular, if the prescribed \(R_\ell\)-budget is zero, then the second alternative holds.  If moreover the prescribed \(R_{k\ell}\)-budget is \(w\), the endpoint data are impossible whenever
\[
\tau K+2K_2(\tau+G)>8\Delta w.
\]
The conclusion includes the legal branch \(\gamma=\tau=0\) and every endpoint or cutting-line tie.

## Proof

The three fixed \(A_0\) chord endpoints
\[
C=(B,0),\qquad D_\gamma=(\delta+\gamma,-\gamma),
\qquad D_G=(\delta+G,-G)
\]
belong to \(A_0\).  Convexity therefore gives
\[
T:=\operatorname{conv}\{C,D_\gamma,D_G\}\subseteq A_0.
\]
Since \(B=\delta-\tau\) and \(G-\gamma=K\),
\[
D_\gamma-C=(\tau+\gamma,-\gamma),
\qquad D_G-D_\gamma=(K,-K).
\]
Consequently the exact coordinate area of \(T\) is
\[
|T|_{\rm coord}
=\frac12\left|
\det\begin{pmatrix}\tau+\gamma&K\\-\gamma&-K\end{pmatrix}
\right|
=\frac{\tau K}{2}.
\tag{1}
\]
Also
\[
\operatorname{proj}_X T=[B,\delta+G],
\qquad
|\operatorname{proj}_X T|=\delta+G-B=\tau+G.
\tag{2}
\]

In the coordinate pair \((q_j,q_\ell)\), the full \(q_j=0\) chord of \(A_2\) is the vertical segment
\[
\Sigma=\{(0,-t):w_0\le t\le w_0+K_2\}.
\]
For each \(X\in[B,\delta+G]\), the vertical section \(T_X\) is an interval, possibly a singleton.  The corresponding section of \(T+\Sigma\) is \(T_X+[-w_0-K_2,-w_0]\), whose one-dimensional length is the length of \(T_X\) plus \(K_2\).  Cavalieri's formula, (1), and (2) therefore give the exact identity
\[
|T+\Sigma|_{\rm coord}
=|T|_{\rm coord}+K_2|\operatorname{proj}_X T|
=\frac{\tau K}{2}+K_2(\tau+G).
\tag{3}
\]
This calculation includes a degenerate \(T\), because singleton vertical sections still acquire length \(K_2\).

For a midpoint of a point \((X,Y)\in T\) and the \(A_2\)-chord point indexed by \(t\),
\[
2r_j=p_j+q_j=X,\qquad
2r_\ell=p_\ell+q_\ell=Y-t.
\tag{4}
\]
Every point of \(T\) has
\[
X\ge B=\beta+H>0,\qquad Y\le0,
\]
and every chord parameter has \(t\ge0\).  Thus (4) gives
\[
r_j>0,\qquad r_\ell\le0.
\tag{5}
\]
For chord parameters in the relative interior, \(t>0\), so \(r_\ell<0\).  On the full closed chord, equality \(r_\ell=0\) can occur only when \(t=0\) and \(Y=0\), hence only on an affine boundary set of planar area zero.  The remaining sign is exactly the sign of \(r_k\).  If \(r_k>0\), (5) places the midpoint in \(R_\ell\); if \(r_k\le0\), it places the midpoint in \(R_{k\ell}\).  Under the accepted exact-cell convention the equality \(r_k=0\) is assigned to \(R_{k\ell}\).  Equivalently, using strict cells, that cutting line has area zero.  Hence the entire generated midpoint hull, up to no area loss, lies in
\[
R_\ell\cup R_{k\ell}.
\tag{6}
\]

The coordinate image of this midpoint hull is \((T+\Sigma)/2\).  Midpoint scaling contributes the two-dimensional factor \(1/4\), and coordinate area is \(\Delta\) times physical area.  Therefore (3) gives its exact physical area
\[
\frac1{4\Delta}
\left(\frac{\tau K}{2}+K_2(\tau+G)\right)
=
\frac{\tau K+2K_2(\tau+G)}{8\Delta}.
\tag{7}
\]
This audits both the segment-addition term and the \(1/(4\Delta)\) factor.

If \(|\mathcal B\cap R_\ell|>0\), the first alternative holds.  Otherwise the generated hull has zero area in \(R_\ell\).  By (6), its full area (7) is then in \(R_{k\ell}\), and since it is contained in \(\mathcal B\),
\[
|\mathcal B\cap R_{k\ell}|
\ge
\frac{\tau K+2K_2(\tau+G)}{8\Delta}.
\]
This proves the dichotomy and the zero-budget charge.

Finally, accepted fact `18e8f69f5a782c16` includes the literal incidence
\(\tau=0\Longleftrightarrow\gamma=0\).  On the legal zero branch,
\[
\tau=\gamma=0,\qquad B=\delta,\qquad G=K,
\]
so \(C=D_\gamma\) and \(T=[(\delta,0),(\delta+K,-K)]\).  Adding \(\Sigma\) produces a parallelogram of coordinate area \(KK_2\), exactly the value of (3).  Equation (7) becomes \(KK_2/(4\Delta)\), also exactly the registered expression.  The ties \(w_0=0\), \(r_k=0\), and \(\beta=0\) do not change the area: respectively they affect only a chord endpoint, an affine cutting line, and the still-strict inequality \(B=H>0\).  Thus all legal zero and tie strata are covered, and the registered coefficient requires no correction.
