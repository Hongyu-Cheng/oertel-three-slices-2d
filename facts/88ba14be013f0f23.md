## Statement

Take the rational polygons \(A_0\) and \(A_2\) of accepted facts `eae946d6c38eda2c` and `bde54892be142ae1`, with the datum and middle-cell budgets of accepted fact `73a2cf54a856ca90`.  For an \(A_0\) point use
\[
(p_j,p_\ell)=(X,Y),\qquad p_k=100-X-Y.
\]
For an \(A_2\) point whose listed coordinates are \((U,V)\), use
\[
(q_j,q_\ell)=(U-V-102,V),\qquad q_k=-U.
\]
At a midpoint put
\[
(r_j,r_\ell)=\frac12\bigl((p_j,p_\ell)+(q_j,q_\ell)\bigr),
\qquad r_k=-1-r_j-r_\ell.
\]
Then, for \(B=(A_0+A_2)/2\), the exact areas of its sign cells, with signs ordered as \((r_j,r_k,r_\ell)\), are
\[
\begin{array}{c|c|c|c}
\text{cell}&\text{signs}&|B\text{-cell}|&\text{prescribed budget}\\ \hline
R_j&-++&253819861517920999/39409999800000000&4\\
R_k&+-+&0&0\\
R_\ell&++-&0&0\\
R_{jk}&--+&24532357499854029799/3960804000000000000&1/10000000\\
R_{j\ell}&-+-&541671399/3980000000000&0\\
R_{k\ell}&+--&3{,}193{,}133{,}704{,}929{,}249/203980000000000&4\\
R_N&---&14995800355780599/40796000000000000&1/10000\\
\text{all positive}&+++&0&0.
\end{array}
\]
In particular,
\[
|B\cap R_{j\ell}|=\frac{541671399}{3980000000000}>0,
\]
whereas the prescribed \(R_{j\ell}\)-budget is zero.  Hence \(B\) cannot be contained in any middle polygon having the prescribed cell budgets, so these two realized endpoints cannot occur in a convex three-slice body with that middle datum.

## Proof

\[
