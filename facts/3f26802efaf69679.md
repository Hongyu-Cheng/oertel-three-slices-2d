## Statement

Assume the actual dominant all-double common-plane hypotheses, exact-cell convention, and rank-two normalization of accepted facts 174b54e9843ef8fc, 29dfdfc94477e532, and e4694936037f7d3f. Assume total corner collapse:
\[
\operatorname{int}_{\mathbb R} I_i=\varnothing
\qquad(i=1,2,3).
\]
Write
\[
r=r_{123}=|\mathcal T|,\qquad
\lambda_1=\frac{r\eta_1}{2},\qquad
a=\min_{S_1}f_1,\qquad b=\max_{S_1}f_1,
\]
and define the full-midpoint packing defect
\[
\varepsilon
=|B_0|-r-\lambda_1-|S_1|.
\]
Then \(0<\eta_1/2\leq a<b\), and
\[
\boxed{\;
\varepsilon
\geq
r\left(2a-\frac{a^2}{b}\right)-\lambda_1
=r\left(a-\frac{\eta_1}{2}\right)
+ra\,\frac{b-a}{b}
>0.
\;}
\tag{1}
\]
Consequently,
\[
a-\frac{\eta_1}{2}\leq\frac{\varepsilon}{r},
\qquad
\frac{b-a}{b}\leq\frac{\varepsilon}{\lambda_1}.
\tag{2}
\]
If \(0\leq\varepsilon<\lambda_1\), the second estimate also gives
\[
b-a
\leq\frac{a\varepsilon}{\lambda_1-\varepsilon}
\leq
\frac{\varepsilon(\lambda_1+\varepsilon)}
{r(\lambda_1-\varepsilon)}.
\tag{3}
\]
In particular, every total-collapse realization satisfies
\[
|B_0|
>
r+\lambda_1+|S_1|
\geq
r+\lambda_1+\frac{(\sqrt p+\sqrt d)^2}{4}.
\tag{4}
\]
Thus the non-strict region
\[
|B_0|
\leq
r_{123}+\lambda_1+\frac{(\sqrt p+\sqrt d)^2}{4}
\tag{5}
\]
rules out total corner collapse. In particular, the zero-defect, positively homothetic, three-piece packing boundary described in accepted fact e4694936037f7d3f has no realization, regardless of whether three singleton tangent normals occur.

## Proof

Accepted fact 29dfdfc94477e532 defines \(D_0=M-p\) and proves \(D_0\leq U\), while accepted fact 174b54e9843ef8fc gives \(U<M/3\). Hence
\[
p=M-D_0\geq M-U>\frac{2M}{3}>0.
\]
Also, accepted fact 29dfdfc94477e532 gives
\[
d\geq\frac{4x_1}{9}>0.
\]
Thus \(P,C\), and \(S_1=(P+C)/2\) are two-dimensional. Rank two and
\(\nabla f_1+\nabla f_2+\nabla f_3=0\) imply
\(\nabla f_1\neq0\): otherwise \(\nabla f_2=-\nabla f_3\), contradicting rank two. Therefore the nonconstant affine function \(f_1\) has distinct minimum and maximum on the two-dimensional body \(S_1\). Accepted fact e4694936037f7d3f gives \(a\geq\eta_1/2>0\), so
\[
0<\frac{\eta_1}{2}\leq a<b.
\tag{6}
\]

Under total corner collapse, accepted fact e4694936037f7d3f gives
\[
\mathcal T=\operatorname{conv}\{O_1,O_2,O_3\}\subseteq B_0,
\qquad
|\mathcal T|=r,
\qquad
S_1\subseteq B_0.
\tag{7}
\]
Put \(E=[O_2,O_3]\). Then \(f_1=0\) on \(E\), whereas
\(f_1(O_1)=-1\). Choose \(x\in S_1\) with \(f_1(x)=b\). Convexity of
\(B_0\), together with \(E\cup\{x\}\subseteq B_0\), gives
\[
\Delta_b:=\operatorname{conv}(E\cup\{x\})\subseteq B_0.
\tag{8}
\]
The triangles \(\mathcal T=\operatorname{conv}(E\cup\{O_1\})\) and
\(\Delta_b\) have the same base \(E\). Their altitudes, measured in the
nonconstant affine coordinate \(f_1\), are respectively \(1\) and \(b\).
Therefore
\[
|\Delta_b|=br.
\tag{9}
\]
The cap of \(\Delta_b\) cut out by \(a\leq f_1\leq b\) is similar to
\(\Delta_b\), with linear ratio \((b-a)/b\). Hence, for
\[
Q=\Delta_b\cap\{0\leq f_1\leq a\},
\]
\[
|Q|
=br-br\left(\frac{b-a}{b}\right)^2
=r\left(2a-\frac{a^2}{b}\right).
\tag{10}
\]

The sets \(\mathcal T,Q,S_1\) are pairwise area-disjoint. Indeed,
\(\mathcal T\subseteq\{f_1\leq0\}\), \(Q\subseteq\{0\leq f_1\leq a\}\),
and \(S_1\subseteq\{f_1\geq a\}\); their possible intersections lie on
the two affine lines \(f_1=0\) and \(f_1=a\), which have planar area
zero. All three sets lie in \(B_0\), so (7), (8), and (10) imply
\[
|B_0|
\geq r+|S_1|+r\left(2a-\frac{a^2}{b}\right).
\tag{11}
\]
Subtracting \(r+\lambda_1+|S_1|\), using
\(\lambda_1=r\eta_1/2\), and rewriting
\[
2a-\frac{a^2}{b}
=a+a\,\frac{b-a}{b}
\]
proves the first equality and inequality in (1). Both summands on the
right of
\[
\varepsilon
\geq
r\left(a-\frac{\eta_1}{2}\right)
+ra\,\frac{b-a}{b}
\tag{12}
\]
are nonnegative, and the second is strictly positive by (6). This proves
the strict conclusion in (1).

Dropping one nonnegative summand at a time in (12) gives
\[
a-\frac{\eta_1}{2}\leq\frac{\varepsilon}{r},
\qquad
\frac{b-a}{b}\leq\frac{\varepsilon}{ra}
\leq\frac{\varepsilon}{\lambda_1},
\]
which is (2). If \(\varepsilon<\lambda_1\), writing \(b=a+(b-a)\) and
rearranging the second inequality in (2) gives
\[
b-a\leq\frac{a\varepsilon}{\lambda_1-\varepsilon}.
\]
The first inequality in (2) gives
\[
a\leq\frac{\eta_1}{2}+\frac{\varepsilon}{r}
=\frac{\lambda_1+\varepsilon}{r},
\]
which proves (3).

Finally, accepted fact 29dfdfc94477e532 gives
\[
|S_1|=\frac{p+d+2\mathcal V(P,C)}4,
\qquad
\mathcal V(P,C)\geq\sqrt{pd}.
\]
Thus
\[
|S_1|\geq\frac{(\sqrt p+\sqrt d)^2}{4}.
\]
Combining this with \(\varepsilon>0\) proves (4), whose contrapositive is
(5). Equality in the boundary condition of accepted fact
e4694936037f7d3f is therefore impossible before any tangent-normal
classification is needed.
