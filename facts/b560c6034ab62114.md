## Statement

Assume the full transformed triangular system of accepted facts 09393c6edf1fc4e2, 13c0df55864b79eb, 64760c76cadff481, 987dc770ddaf5b3b, and a61bda3f7065e3e1 in the proper-small-cap double-singleton branch. Thus the nondegenerate triangles \(P_0,P_1,P_2\), of normalized areas \(A,1,B\), satisfy
\[
\frac{P_0+P_2}{2}\subseteq P_1,
\qquad
A+B>1,
\qquad
t=\frac{2(1+A+B)}9\leq\frac12,
\qquad
\frac3{10}\leq\frac AB<\frac38,
\]
and, with
\[
u_i=\frac{|P_0\cap\{x_i\geq0\}|}{A},
\qquad
c_i=|P_1\cap\{x_i\geq0\}|,
\qquad
v_i=\frac{|P_2\cap\{x_i\geq0\}|}{B},
\]
all three literal row masses obey
\[
A u_i+c_i+Bv_i<t
\qquad(i=1,2,3).
\tag{1}
\]
The centroid incidence set of \(P_1\) is \(\{1,2\}\). In the common row and column labeling, the first two rows of its vertex matrix are
\[
(p,-a,-b),
\qquad
(-c,r,-d),
\tag{2}
\]
where
\[
p,r,a,b,c,d>0,
\qquad
p\geq a+b,
\qquad
r\geq c+d,
\qquad
c_1,c_2\geq\frac49.
\tag{3}
\]
Then the system is infeasible. In particular, for \(s=(2-(g_2)_3)/4\) and
\[
G=
c_1\left(1-\frac{s}{p}\right)_+^2
+c_2\left(1-\frac{s}{r}\right)_+^2
-\frac{A(1-u_3)}4,
\]
there is no full exact cap-order and endpoint-compatibility stratum, rational or real, on which all the hypotheses hold and \(G\geq0\).

## Proof

First extract the endpoint budget left by rows \(1\) and \(2\). Put
\[
\delta_0=A(u_1+u_2),
\qquad
\delta_2=B(v_1+v_2),
\qquad
\delta=\delta_0+\delta_2.
\]
Summing (1) for \(i=1,2\) and using (3) gives
\[
\delta
<2t-c_1-c_2
\leq \frac{4(1+A+B)}9-\frac89
=\frac{4(A+B-1)}9.
\tag{4}
\]
Since \(t\leq1/2\), one has \(A+B\leq5/4\), so
\[
0\leq\delta<\frac19.
\tag{5}
\]
The ratio interval and \(A+B>1\) also give the strict bounds
\[
A>\frac3{13},
\qquad
B>\frac8{11}.
\tag{6}
\]
Indeed, \(A/B\geq3/10\) implies \(B\leq10A/3\), while \(A/B<3/8\) implies \(A<3B/8\).

We next prove the exact common-middle upper bound. Let
\[
Q=P_1\cap\{x_1\leq0,\ x_2\leq0\}.
\]
Write a point of \(P_1=L\Delta\) as \(L\lambda\), where
\(\lambda=(\lambda_1,\lambda_2,\lambda_3)\in\Delta\). The two coordinate functions from (2) are
\[
x_1=p\lambda_1-a\lambda_2-b\lambda_3,
\qquad
x_2=-c\lambda_1+r\lambda_2-d\lambda_3.
\tag{7}
\]
Their values at the barycenter
\(\bar\lambda=(1/3,1/3,1/3)\) are
\[
\mu_1=\frac{p-a-b}{3}\geq0,
\qquad
\mu_2=\frac{r-c-d}{3}\geq0.
\tag{8}
\]
Consequently the preimage of \(Q\) is contained in
\[
\widehat Q
=\{\lambda\in\Delta:x_1\leq\mu_1,\ x_2\leq\mu_2\}.
\tag{9}
\]

Both boundary lines in (9) pass through \(\bar\lambda\). They are nonparallel because \(\det L\ne0\), as supplied by 09393c6edf1fc4e2. The first line separates \(e_1\) from \(e_2,e_3\), while the second separates \(e_2\) from \(e_1,e_3\). Their relevant intersections with the two edges incident to \(e_3\) are
\[
z_1=(\theta,0,1-\theta),
\qquad
\theta=\frac{p-a+2b}{3(p+b)}<\frac12,
\tag{10}
\]
and
\[
z_2=(0,\eta,1-\eta),
\qquad
\eta=\frac{r-c+2d}{3(r+d)}<\frac12.
\tag{11}
\]
The strict inequalities follow from \(a,b<p\) and \(c,d<r\), which follow from (3). For completeness, on the remaining edge \([e_1,e_2]\), the first line meets at first barycentric coordinate
\[
\xi=\frac{p+2a-b}{3(p+a)}<\frac12,
\]
whereas the second meets at first barycentric coordinate
\[
\zeta=\frac{2r+c+d}{3(r+c)}>\frac12.
\]
Thus the two halfplanes in (9) have no common segment on \([e_1,e_2]\), and
\(\widehat Q\) is exactly the quadrilateral with consecutive vertices
\[
e_3,\ z_1,\ \bar\lambda,\ z_2.
\]
Because \(|\Delta|=1\), the shoelace formula in the
\((\lambda_1,\lambda_2)\)-coordinates gives
\[
|\widehat Q|
=\frac{\theta}{3}+\frac{\eta}{3}
<\frac13.
\tag{12}
\]
The affine map \(L\) preserves normalized area fractions between
\(\Delta\) and \(P_1\), so (9)-(12) prove
\[
|Q|<\frac13.
\tag{13}
\]

It remains to use the endpoints without compressing their common geometry. Define
\[
E_0=P_0\cap\{x_1\leq0,\ x_2\leq0\},
\qquad
E_2=P_2\cap\{x_1\leq0,\ x_2\leq0\}.
\]
Every coordinate restriction is nonconstant on each nondegenerate endpoint triangle, so its zero line has area zero. The union bound for the two coordinate caps therefore gives
\[
|E_0|\geq A-\delta_0,
\qquad
|E_2|\geq B-\delta_2.
\tag{14}
\]
Equations (5)-(6) make both right sides positive. Exact endpoint compatibility gives
\[
\frac{E_0+E_2}{2}\subseteq Q.
\tag{15}
\]
Planar Brunn--Minkowski, applied in the common translation plane, now yields
\[
\sqrt{|Q|}
\geq
\frac{\sqrt{|E_0|}+\sqrt{|E_2|}}2
\geq
\frac{\sqrt{A-\delta_0}+\sqrt{B-\delta_2}}2.
\tag{16}
\]

For fixed \(\delta=\delta_0+\delta_2\), the expression
\[
\sqrt{A-x}+\sqrt{B-(\delta-x)}
\]
is concave for \(0\leq x\leq\delta\), so its minimum occurs at an endpoint. By (5)-(6), one endpoint is bounded as
\[
\sqrt{A-\delta}+\sqrt B
>
\sqrt{\frac{14}{117}}+\sqrt{\frac8{11}}
>
\frac13+\frac56
=\frac76,
\tag{17}
\]
where \(14/117>1/9\) and \(8/11>25/36\). The other endpoint satisfies
\[
\sqrt A+\sqrt{B-\delta}
>
\sqrt{\frac3{13}}+\sqrt{\frac{61}{99}}
>
\frac{11}{24}+\frac{17}{24}
=\frac76.
\tag{18}
\]
Here \(3/13>121/576\) and \(61/99>289/576\). Therefore (16)-(18) give
\[
|Q|>\left(\frac7{12}\right)^2
=\frac{49}{144}
>\frac13,
\tag{19}
\]
contradicting (13).

The contradiction used the common middle matrix, both endpoint triangles, exact midpoint compatibility, the determinant nonzero condition, the branch ratio and residual bounds, and the original strict literal row inequalities. It is independent of cap-order and compatibility min-column strata. Hence the full proper-small-cap double-singleton system is infeasible, proving the statement.

Planar Brunn--Minkowski theorem: for nonempty compact convex sets \(K,L\subset\mathbb R^2\),
\[
\left|\frac{K+L}{2}\right|^{1/2}
\geq
\frac{|K|^{1/2}+|L|^{1/2}}2.
\]
Primary source: Hermann Brunn, *Ueber Ovale und Eiflaechen: Inaugural-Dissertation*, Akademische Buchdruckerei von F. Straub, Munich, 1887, https://www.deutsche-digitale-bibliothek.de/item/6J63DGQIMCO3LHKRNUPGC47B3OQEZI7N. In (16), the closures of \(E_0,E_2\) are nonempty compact convex subsets of parallel planes identified with their common two-dimensional translation space; taking closures does not change their areas, and (15) supplies the required Minkowski containment.
