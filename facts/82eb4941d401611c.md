## Statement

For every \(0<\delta\leq 1/100\), there is a unit-area triangle \(\Delta\), a minimal cover of \(\mathbb R^2\) by the interiors of three closed halfplanes \(L_1,L_2,L_3\), and bonuses
\[
(e_1,e_2,e_3)=\left(0,\frac{8\delta-10\delta^2}{9},0\right)
\]
such that, writing \(p_i=|\Delta\cap L_i|\),
\[
\max_i(p_i+e_i)
<
\frac49+\frac{e_1+e_2+e_3}{3}.
\]
Moreover, for every \(\eta>0\), taking
\[
\delta=\min\left\{\eta,\frac1{100}\right\}
\]
makes \(0\leq e_i\leq\eta\) for every \(i\).

## Proof

Let
\[
\Delta=\operatorname{conv}\{v_1,v_2,v_3\},
\qquad
v_1=(0,0),\quad v_2=(2,0),\quad v_3=(0,1).
\]
Thus \(|\Delta|=1\). For \(0<\delta\leq1/100\), put
\[
a=\frac12-2\delta+\frac72\delta^2,
\qquad
b=\frac12+\delta.
\]
Both \(a\) and \(b\) are positive. Let \(g_1,g_2,g_3\) be the affine functions determined by
\[
\begin{array}{c|ccc}
 &v_1&v_2&v_3\\ \hline
g_1&1&-a&-b\\
g_2&-b&1&-b\\
g_3&-b&-a&1
\end{array}
\]
and set \(L_i=\{x:g_i(x)\geq0\}\).

These halfplanes form a strict minimal cover. Indeed, set
\[
u=1+b=\frac{3+2\delta}{2},
\qquad
v=1-b+2a=\frac32-5\delta+7\delta^2.
\]
The weights satisfy \(u>0\) and \(v\geq3/2-1/20>0\). At each of the three vertices, the affine function \(ug_1+vg_2+ug_3\) has the value
\[
u(1-b)-bv=v-2au
=1-b-2ab
=\frac{\delta^2(1-14\delta)}2>0.
\]
Since the vertices are affinely independent,
\[
ug_1(x)+vg_2(x)+ug_3(x)
=\frac{\delta^2(1-14\delta)}2
\qquad(x\in\mathbb R^2).
\]
The positive weights and positive right-hand side imply that at every point at least one \(g_i\) is strictly positive. Hence the interiors of \(L_1,L_2,L_3\) cover \(\mathbb R^2\). No two interiors cover: \(v_3\) is strictly outside \(L_1,L_2\), \(v_2\) is strictly outside \(L_1,L_3\), and \(v_1\) is strictly outside \(L_2,L_3\). Thus every member is indispensable.

Each \(L_i\cap\Delta\) is the corner triangle at the unique vertex where \(g_i\) is positive. For \(L_1\), its two edge fractions from \(v_1\) are \(1/(1+a)\) and \(1/(1+b)\). For \(L_2\), both fractions from \(v_2\) are \(1/(1+b)\), and \(L_3\) has the same two fractions as \(L_1\). Since \(|\Delta|=1\),
\[
p_1=p_3
=\frac1{(1+a)(1+b)}
=\frac4{(3+2\delta)(3-4\delta+7\delta^2)},
\qquad
p_2=\frac1{(1+b)^2}
=\frac4{(3+2\delta)^2}.
\]

Put
\[
E=\frac{8\delta-10\delta^2}{9},
\qquad
R=\frac49+\frac E3.
\]
The bonus triple is \((0,E,0)\). Since \(0<\delta\leq1/100\), one has \(0<E<8\delta/9\). Direct exact subtraction and factorization give
\[
R-p_1
=
\frac{2\delta^2(9+166\delta-9\delta^2-70\delta^3)}
{27(3+2\delta)(3-4\delta+7\delta^2)}
\]
and
\[
R-(p_2+E)
=
\frac{4\delta^2(20\delta^2+44\delta+9)}
{27(3+2\delta)^2}.
\]
All denominators are positive. The second numerator is positive, while
\[
9+166\delta-9\delta^2-70\delta^3
\geq
9-\frac9{10^4}-\frac{70}{10^6}>0.
\]
Therefore \(p_1=p_3<R\) and \(p_2+E<R\), proving
\[
\max_i(p_i+e_i)<R
=\frac49+\frac{e_1+e_2+e_3}{3}.
\]

Finally, given any \(\eta>0\), choose
\(\delta=\min\{\eta,1/100\}\). Then the construction applies and
\[
0<E<\frac{8\delta}{9}\leq\frac{8\eta}{9}<\eta.
\]
Thus all three bonuses lie in \([0,\eta]\), completing the construction for every positive \(\eta\).
