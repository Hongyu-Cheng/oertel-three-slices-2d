## Statement

Use the normalized systems of accepted fact `b046e3066136e781`, the word automaton of accepted fact `46afc7eb815ffc5b`, and the 106446 surviving interior/interior systems of accepted fact `93b455550ddc0cd0`.

Thus
\[
\sigma_P\in\mathcal A^\circ,\qquad
\sigma_Q\in\mathcal C,
\]
where
\[
\mathcal A^\circ
=\{\sigma_P\in\mathcal A:
\sigma_{P2}\ne\mathrm P,\ \sigma_{P4}\ne\mathrm P\},
\]
and the current \(R\)-words are
\[
\mathcal R_{01}
=\{\sigma_R\in\mathcal D:
\sigma_{R2},\sigma_{R4}\ne\mathrm P,\
\sigma_{R1},\sigma_{R3}\ne\mathrm N\}
\]
for \(\eta=0,1\), while
\[
\mathcal R_2
=\{\sigma_R\in\mathcal D:
\sigma_{R2},\sigma_{R4}\ne\mathrm P\}
\]
for \(\eta=2\).

Every current system is infeasible if
\[
\left[
\sigma_{Q2}=\mathrm P
\ \text{and}\
(\sigma_{P1},\sigma_{P2})
\notin\{(\mathrm N,\mathrm N),(\mathrm P,\mathrm N)\}
\right]
\]
or
\[
\left[
\sigma_{Q4}=\mathrm P
\ \text{and}\
(\sigma_{P3},\sigma_{P4})
\notin\{(\mathrm N,\mathrm N),(\mathrm P,\mathrm N)\}
\right].
\]
Among the mixed-label, lexicographically symmetry-reduced current systems, this condition deletes exactly
\[
24132
\]
systems.

## Proof

Accepted fact `b046e3066136e781` gives
\[
\alpha_P+\alpha_Q+\alpha_R=t,
\qquad
\alpha_R+m\ge t.
\]
Consequently,
\[
\alpha_P+\alpha_Q=t-\alpha_R\le m. \tag{1}
\]
If \(\sigma_{Q2}=\mathrm P\), both endpoint values of \(-U_Q\) are nonnegative. Since \(U_Q\) is affine,
\[
U_Q(x)\le0
\]
throughout the support of \(Q\). Hence
\[
\alpha_Q=m.
\]
Equation (1), together with \(\alpha_P\ge0\), forces
\[
\alpha_P=0. \tag{2}
\]

We determine the necessary first-two-letter pattern of a positive-area affine strip satisfying (2). If \(L_P\) were negative anywhere, its affinity would make it negative on a nonempty interval. The nonnegative affine height \(U_P-L_P\), having positive integral, is strictly positive in the interior of the support. The portion of \(P\) below \(y=0\) would therefore have positive area, contrary to (2). Thus
\[
L_P\ge0.
\]
The position-1 endpoint pair \((-\lambda_{P0},-\lambda_{P1})\) is consequently nonpositive. Its label is \(\mathrm N\) unless both entries vanish, in which case the boundary convention of accepted fact `b046e3066136e781` gives label \(\mathrm P\).

Moreover,
\[
U_P\ge L_P\ge0.
\]
The function \(U_P\) cannot vanish identically, since that would force \(L_P=U_P=0\) and give zero area. Hence its position-2 endpoint pair is nonpositive and not identically zero, so
\[
\sigma_{P2}=\mathrm N.
\]
Therefore (2) necessarily implies
\[
(\sigma_{P1},\sigma_{P2})
\in\{(\mathrm N,\mathrm N),(\mathrm P,\mathrm N)\}. \tag{3}
\]
This proves infeasibility under the first displayed filter condition.

The symmetric crossing relations are
\[
\beta_P+\beta_Q+\beta_R=t,
\qquad
\beta_R+m\ge t,
\]
and hence
\[
\beta_P+\beta_Q\le m. \tag{4}
\]
If \(\sigma_{Q4}=\mathrm P\), the affine function \(L_Q(x)+x\) is nonnegative throughout the support of \(Q\). Thus
\[
Q\subseteq\{x+y\ge0\},\qquad \beta_Q=m,
\]
so (4) forces
\[
\beta_P=0. \tag{5}
\]

Condition (5) implies
\[
U_P(x)+x\le0
\]
throughout the support. Its position-3 endpoint pair is therefore nonpositive, with label \(\mathrm N\) unless it is identically zero, when its label is \(\mathrm P\). Also
\[
L_P(x)+x\le U_P(x)+x\le0.
\]
The function \(L_P(x)+x\) cannot vanish identically: otherwise \(U_P(x)+x\ge L_P(x)+x=0\), so (5) would force \(U_P=L_P\), contradicting positive area. Its position-4 pair is therefore nonpositive and not identically zero, giving
\[
\sigma_{P4}=\mathrm N.
\]
Thus
\[
(\sigma_{P3},\sigma_{P4})
\in\{(\mathrm N,\mathrm N),(\mathrm P,\mathrm N)\}, \tag{6}
\]
which proves the second filter condition.

It remains to count the new deletions. Put
\[
X=\mathrm C_+,\qquad Y=\mathrm C_-.
\]
Reading the \(\mathcal A\)-table of accepted fact `46afc7eb815ffc5b` after the six-word deletion defining \(\mathcal A^\circ\), there are \(48\) current \(P\)-words. Exactly \(14\) have first-two-letter prefix \(\mathrm{NN}\) or \(\mathrm{PN}\), so
\[
34
\]
have a forbidden prefix. By \(\pi\)-symmetry, exactly \(34\) have a forbidden suffix. Exactly \(24\) have both a forbidden prefix and a forbidden suffix: their contributions by prefix are
\[
4+2+6+6+4+2=24
\]
for the prefixes
\[
\mathrm{PX},\mathrm{PY},\mathrm{XN},
\mathrm{XX},\mathrm{YN},\mathrm{YY}.
\]

In \(\mathcal C\), exactly \(9\) words have \(\sigma_{Q2}=\mathrm P\), exactly \(9\) have \(\sigma_{Q4}=\mathrm P\), and their intersection consists only of
\[
\mathrm{PPPP}.
\]
Inclusion-exclusion therefore gives
\[
34\cdot9+34\cdot9-24\cdot1=588 \tag{7}
\]
filtered \(P,Q\)-word pairs. Every such pair already contains \(X\) or \(Y\), because all \(\mathrm{PP}\)-prefix and \(\mathrm{PP}\)-suffix words were removed in forming \(\mathcal A^\circ\). Hence no non-mixed triple must be subtracted.

The filter is invariant under
\[
\pi(\sigma_1,\sigma_2,\sigma_3,\sigma_4)
=(\sigma_3,\sigma_4,\sigma_1,\sigma_2).
\]
The fixed filtered \(P\)-words are exactly
\[
\mathrm{PXPX},\quad
\mathrm{XNXN},\quad
\mathrm{XXXX},\quad
\mathrm{YNYN},
\]
and the only fixed compatible \(Q\)-word is \(\mathrm{PPPP}\). Thus there are \(4\) fixed filtered \(P,Q\)-pairs.

From the exact deletion lists of accepted fact `93b455550ddc0cd0`,
\[
|\mathcal R_{01}|=26,\qquad |\mathcal R_2|=30,
\]
and both sets retain all \(4\) \(\pi\)-fixed words of \(\mathcal D\). Burnside's formula gives, for each of \(\eta=0,1\),
\[
\frac{588\cdot26+4\cdot4}{2}
=\frac{15288+16}{2}
=7652
\]
newly deleted systems. For \(\eta=2\), it gives
\[
\frac{588\cdot30+4\cdot4}{2}
=\frac{17640+16}{2}
=8828.
\]
The exact total deletion count is therefore
\[
2\cdot7652+8828=24132.
\]
Consequently the surviving interior/interior list has
\[
106446-24132=82314
\]
systems.
