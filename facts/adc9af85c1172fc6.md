## Statement

Assume the complete canonical finite-interior common-plane hypotheses, exact-cell convention, \(M\geq m\), the three crossing equalities, the canonical left and right tails, the all-double positive \(Q\)-support
\[
\{123,12,13,23\},
\]
and \(r_1r_2r_3r_{123}>0\).  Put
\[
q=q_{123}>0,\qquad
\lambda_i=\frac{q_{\{1,2,3\}\setminus\{i\}}}{q}>0,\qquad
R=\lambda_1+\lambda_2+\lambda_3.
\]
For \(\{i,j,k\}=\{1,2,3\}\), define the components of accepted fact `6aa12d1b76699246` by
\[
A_k=\frac{1+R}{\lambda_k},\qquad
C_k=2\sqrt{\lambda_i\lambda_j},\qquad
d_{1k}=\frac{4A_kC_k^2}{(A_k+C_k^2)^2},
\]
\[
d_{2k}
=1-\operatorname{clip}\!\left(
\frac{\sqrt{A_k}-C_k}{1+C_k}
\right)^2,
\qquad
\operatorname{clip}(t)=\min\{1,\max\{0,t\}\},
\]
\[
d_0=\min\!\left\{
1,\frac3{\lambda_1^{-1}+\lambda_2^{-1}+\lambda_3^{-1}}
\right\},
\qquad
\delta=\min\{d_0,d_{11},d_{12},d_{13},d_{21},d_{22},d_{23}\},
\]
and define the accepted scalar threshold
\[
\tau(R)=
\frac{49}{
\left(\sqrt{1+R}+\sqrt{204+120R}\right)^2}.
\]
If, after a permutation of indices,
\[
\frac13\leq\lambda_1,\lambda_2\leq\frac38,
\qquad
\lambda_3\geq\frac23,
\tag{1}
\]
then
\[
\delta>\frac{17}{81}>\tau(R).
\tag{2}
\]
Consequently no full common-plane realization satisfying (1) can have \(W=0\).  The conclusion includes equality on every boundary in (1), all eight endpoint-coincidence strata, and all collinear, redundant-vertex, and weak/strict cutting-line boundaries allowed by the exact-cell convention.

## Proof

Write
\[
a=\lambda_1,\qquad b=\lambda_2,\qquad c=\lambda_3.
\]
We first audit both source cap constraints from accepted fact `8234d1112ec2f031`.  Its product inequality becomes
\[
4abc\leq1+a+b+c.
\tag{3}
\]
In fact, throughout (1),
\[
4ab\leq4\left(\frac38\right)^2=\frac9{16}<1,
\]
so (3) holds strictly for every \(c>0\).

The square-root cap inequality becomes
\[
4(\sqrt a+\sqrt b+\sqrt c)^2
\leq9(1+a+b+c).
\tag{4}
\]
Put \(s=\sqrt a+\sqrt b\) and \(t=\sqrt c\).  The exact identity
\[
\begin{aligned}
&9(1+a+b+c)-4(\sqrt a+\sqrt b+\sqrt c)^2\\
&\qquad
=5\left(t-\frac{4s}{5}\right)^2
+\frac95\left(5+a+b-8\sqrt{ab}\right)
\end{aligned}
\]
shows that (4) is strict on the whole region: since \(a,b\leq3/8\),
\[
5+a+b-8\sqrt{ab}\geq5+a+b-3>0.
\]
Thus neither accepted source cap constraint silently removes a part of the closed region (1).

We now bound every component of \(\delta\).  From (1),
\[
\lambda_1^{-1}+\lambda_2^{-1}+\lambda_3^{-1}
\leq3+3+\frac32=\frac{15}{2}.
\]
Therefore
\[
d_0\geq\frac25. \tag{5}
\]

For every \(k\), the ratio \(C_k^2/A_k\) is the same number
\[
y=\frac{4abc}{1+a+b+c}.
\]
It is strictly increasing in each of \(a,b,c\), and hence
\[
y\geq
\frac{4(1/3)(1/3)(2/3)}{1+1/3+1/3+2/3}
=\frac8{63}>\frac18.
\tag{6}
\]
Equation (3) gives \(y\leq1\).  Since \(4y/(1+y)^2\) is nondecreasing on \(0<y\leq1\), equations (3) and (6) yield, for all \(k\),
\[
d_{1k}
=\frac{4y}{(1+y)^2}
>
\frac{4(1/8)}{(1+1/8)^2}
=\frac{32}{81}.
\tag{7}
\]

It remains to bound the three clipped components.  We prove
\[
9\sqrt{A_k}<8+17C_k
\qquad(k=1,2,3).
\tag{8}
\]
For \(k=1\), inequality (8), after squaring its positive sides and multiplying by \(a\), is equivalent to
\[
F_1(a,b,c)
:=
a(8+34\sqrt{bc})^2-81(1+a+b+c)>0.
\]
Expanding \(F_1\), its three partial derivatives satisfy
\[
\frac{\partial F_1}{\partial a}
=64+544\sqrt{bc}+1156bc-81>0,
\]
\[
\frac{\partial F_1}{\partial b}
=a\left(272\sqrt{\frac cb}+1156c\right)-81
\geq\frac{1583}{9}>0,
\]
\[
\frac{\partial F_1}{\partial c}
=a\left(272\sqrt{\frac bc}+1156b\right)-81
\geq\frac{427}{9}>0.
\]
Hence \(F_1\) is minimized at
\[
(a,b,c)=\left(\frac13,\frac13,\frac23\right).
\]
At that point \(A_1=7\), \(C_1=2\sqrt2/3\), and
\[
8+17C_1
=8+\frac{34\sqrt2}{3}
>
\frac{358}{15},
\]
where \(\sqrt2>7/5\).  Since
\[
\left(\frac{358}{15}\right)^2
=\frac{128164}{225}
>567
=81A_1,
\]
inequality (8) follows for \(k=1\).  The case \(k=2\) is symmetric.

For \(k=3\), after squaring and multiplying by \(c\), define
\[
F_3(a,b,c)
:=
c(8+34\sqrt{ab})^2-81(1+a+b+c).
\]
Its partial derivatives obey
\[
\frac{\partial F_3}{\partial c}
=64+544\sqrt{ab}+1156ab-81>0,
\]
\[
\frac{\partial F_3}{\partial a}
=c\left(272\sqrt{\frac ba}+1156b\right)-81
\geq\frac{1583}{9}>0,
\]
and the same bound holds for \(\partial F_3/\partial b\).  Thus its minimum is again at \((1/3,1/3,2/3)\).  There
\[
A_3=\frac72,\qquad C_3=\frac23,
\]
and
\[
(8+17C_3)^2-81A_3
=\left(\frac{58}{3}\right)^2-\frac{567}{2}
=\frac{1625}{18}>0.
\]
This completes the proof of (8).

Rearranging (8) gives
\[
\frac{\sqrt{A_k}-C_k}{1+C_k}<\frac89.
\]
If the left side is negative, its clipped value is zero; otherwise clipping does not increase it.  Therefore
\[
d_{2k}>1-\left(\frac89\right)^2=\frac{17}{81}
\qquad(k=1,2,3).
\tag{9}
\]
Equations (5), (7), and (9) prove
\[
\delta>\frac{17}{81}. \tag{10}
\]

Finally, (1) gives \(R\geq4/3\).  Hence
\[
\sqrt{1+R}>\frac32,\qquad
\sqrt{204+120R}>19,
\]
and consequently
\[
\tau(R)
<
\frac{49}{(41/2)^2}
=\frac{196}{1681}
<\frac7{60}
<\frac{17}{81}.
\tag{11}
\]
This proves (2).  Accepted fact `c44e67089f10e853` now excludes \(W=0\) whenever \(\delta\geq\tau(R)\), so (10)-(11) give the claimed contradiction.

The region (1) is closed, and the union of its three index permutations is a finite symmetric closed region.  Accepted facts `6aa12d1b76699246` and `c44e67089f10e853` retain coincident chord endpoints, collinear or redundant vertices, and exact-cell cutting-line boundaries.  Thus no endpoint or proper-cell boundary stratum is lost.
