## Statement

Use the normalized systems and word notation of accepted facts `b046e3066136e781` and `46afc7eb815ffc5b`, restricted to the 56,538 systems surviving accepted fact `ebcc3143a486e8cd`.

For every feasible surviving system with \(\eta\in\{1,2\}\), all three conditions hold:
\[
\sigma_Q\ne\mathrm{PPPP},
\]
\[
\sigma_Q=\mathrm{NNPP}
\quad\Longrightarrow\quad
(\sigma_{P1},\sigma_{P2})\ne\mathrm{NN},
\]
\[
\sigma_Q=\mathrm{PPNN}
\quad\Longrightarrow\quad
(\sigma_{P3},\sigma_{P4})\ne\mathrm{NN}.
\]
Deleting the systems violating at least one of these conditions preserves the feasibility union. It deletes exactly \(132\) reduced systems for each of \(\eta=1,2\), hence exactly \(264\) systems in total.

## Proof

Write
\[
K=\frac{P+Q}{2},
\qquad
A=\frac{(1+q)^2}{4}.
\]
Accepted fact `ebcc3143a486e8cd` proves
\[
K\subseteq R\cap\{x\ge1\},
\qquad
d\ge A,
\]
and also
\[
m=q^2,\qquad
t=\frac{2(1+m+k)}9.
\]

We first record bounds valid in the two relevant residual branches. If \(\eta=1\), then
\[
q>\frac35,\qquad k<1+m,
\]
so
\[
t-m=\frac{2+2k-7m}{9}
<\frac{4-5m}{9}
<\frac{11}{45}.
\]
If \(\eta=2\), then
\[
q>\frac35,\qquad m>\frac12,\qquad k<2-m,
\]
so
\[
t-m<\frac{6-9m}{9}<\frac16.
\]
In both branches,
\[
A=\frac{(1+q)^2}{4}>\frac{16}{25},
\]
and consequently
\[
t-m<A,\qquad 2(t-m)<A. \tag{1}
\]

Let
\[
\alpha_E=\frac{|E\cap\{y\le0\}|}{\Lambda},
\qquad
\beta_E=\frac{|E\cap\{x+y\ge0\}|}{\Lambda}.
\]
The systems of accepted fact `b046e3066136e781` contain
\[
\alpha_P+\alpha_Q+\alpha_R=t,\qquad
\beta_P+\beta_Q+\beta_R=t,
\]
\[
\alpha_R\ge t-m,\qquad \beta_R\ge t-m. \tag{2}
\]
Accepted fact `ebcc3143a486e8cd` proves that an \(\mathrm{NN}\) half has zero corresponding cap and a \(\mathrm{PP}\) half has full corresponding cap.

Suppose first that \(\sigma_Q=\mathrm{PPPP}\). Then
\[
\alpha_Q=\beta_Q=m.
\]
By (2) and nonnegativity,
\[
\alpha_P=\beta_P=0,\qquad
\alpha_R=\beta_R=t-m.
\]
Every point with \(x\ge1\) belongs to
\[
\{y\le0\}\cup\{x+y\ge0\}.
\]
Therefore
\[
d\le\alpha_R+\beta_R=2(t-m),
\]
contradicting \(d\ge A\) and (1).

Next suppose
\[
\sigma_Q=\mathrm{NNPP},
\qquad
(\sigma_{P1},\sigma_{P2})=\mathrm{NN}.
\]
The two first halves imply
\[
P,Q\subseteq\{y\ge0\}.
\]
The second half of \(\sigma_Q\) gives \(\beta_Q=m\). Equations (2) then force
\[
\beta_P=0,\qquad \beta_R=t-m.
\]
Every point of \(K\) has \(x\ge1\) and \(y\ge0\), hence
\[
K\subseteq R\cap\{x+y\ge0\}.
\]
Thus
\[
\beta_R\ge\frac{|K|}{\Lambda}\ge A,
\]
contradicting \(\beta_R=t-m<A\).

Finally suppose
\[
\sigma_Q=\mathrm{PPNN},
\qquad
(\sigma_{P3},\sigma_{P4})=\mathrm{NN}.
\]
The two second halves imply
\[
P,Q\subseteq\{x+y\le0\}.
\]
The first half of \(\sigma_Q\) gives \(\alpha_Q=m\), so (2) forces
\[
\alpha_P=0,\qquad \alpha_R=t-m.
\]
Every point of \(K\) satisfies \(x\ge1\) and \(x+y\le0\), hence \(y<0\). Therefore
\[
K\subseteq R\cap\{y\le0\},
\]
and
\[
\alpha_R\ge\frac{|K|}{\Lambda}\ge A,
\]
again contradicting \(\alpha_R=t-m<A\). This proves the filter, including all boundary cases.

It remains to count the deleted reduced systems. Under the first filter of `ebcc3143a486e8cd`, the violating \((\sigma_P,\sigma_Q)\) pairs are exactly
\[
\begin{array}{c|c}
\sigma_Q&\sigma_P\\ \hline
\mathrm{PPPP}
&\mathrm{NNNN},\mathrm{NNPN},\mathrm{PNNN},\mathrm{PNPN},\\
\mathrm{NNPP}
&\mathrm{NNNN},\mathrm{NNPN},\\
\mathrm{PPNN}
&\mathrm{NNNN},\mathrm{PNNN}.
\end{array}
\]
Hence there are eight ordered \(P,Q\) pairs. All eight use only \(\mathrm N,\mathrm P\). Their set is invariant under the half-swap involution \(\pi\), and exactly two pairs are fixed:
\[
(\mathrm{NNNN},\mathrm{PPPP}),
\qquad
(\mathrm{PNPN},\mathrm{PPPP}).
\]

Accepted fact `ebcc3143a486e8cd` counts \(33\) surviving \(R\)-words, of which exactly one is \(\{\mathrm N,\mathrm P\}\)-only. It also counts five fixed surviving \(R\)-words, exactly one of which is \(\{\mathrm N,\mathrm P\}\)-only. Thus there are \(32\) mixed \(R\)-words and four fixed mixed \(R\)-words. Burnside’s formula gives
\[
\frac{8\cdot32+2\cdot4}{2}=132
\]
deleted reduced systems for each fixed \(\eta\). The branches \(\eta=1,2\) therefore contribute
\[
2\cdot132=264
\]
deleted systems. Hence the surviving full/interior list has
\[
56538-264=56274
\]
systems.
