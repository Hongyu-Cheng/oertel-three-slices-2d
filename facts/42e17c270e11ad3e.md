## Statement

Write \([u,v]=u_1v_2-u_2v_1\) and \(Ju=(-u_2,u_1)\). Fix a positive all-double normal-fan stratum with counterclockwise vertex lists
\[
A_0=(a_0,\ldots,a_{n_0-1}),\qquad
A_2=(q_0,\ldots,q_{n_2-1}),\qquad
K=(k_0,\ldots,k_{n_K-1}).
\]
The stratum fixes the clipping order of every \(P,Q,R\) chamber cut out by the three fixed affine cutting lines, the merged-fan map
\[
b_j=\frac{a_{\iota_0(j)}+q_{\iota_2(j)}}2
\]
for the vertices of \(B=(A_0+A_2)/2\), every persistent zero-coordinate vertex-cut incidence, and every persistent shared normal-fan ray.

Let \(x\in\mathbb R^{2(n_0+n_2+n_K)}\) be the vector of all vertex coordinates. On this stratum, let \(F(x)\) consist of:

1. every individual \(P,Q,R\) chamber area minus its fixed value;
2. every retained zero-coordinate incidence \(\ell(v)=0\);
3. every persistent shared-ray equation
\[
[a_{i+1}-a_i,q_{j+1}-q_j]=0;
\]
4. every other persistent vertex-cut, repeated-vertex, or collinearity equality declared by the fixed stratum code.

Put \(E(x)=DF(x)\). The three crossing equalities may be appended to \(F\), but they add no condition once all individual chamber areas are fixed.

For every supporting edge \(e_s=k_{s+1}-k_s\) of \(K\) and every merged-fan vertex \(b_j\), orient the containment slack as
\[
\eta_{sj}(x)=[e_s,b_j-k_s]\geq0,
\qquad
I(x)=\{(s,j):\eta_{sj}(x)=0\}.
\]
Assume that the zero-charge capacity has an explicitly supplied fixed-code \(C^1\) formula \(\mathscr C(x)\) in a neighborhood of the realization under consideration. This is an input hypothesis, not a consequence of the polygonal clipping code. Let \(h_\beta(x)\geq0\), \(\beta\in J(x)\), be every other active feasibility slack which the motion must retain rather than treating as an immediate stopping event. Define \(G(x)\) to have all of the rows
\[
D\eta_{sj}(x)\quad((s,j)\in I(x)),\qquad
D\mathscr C(x),\qquad
Dh_\beta(x)\quad(\beta\in J(x)).
\tag{1}
\]
Strict convexity, strict cut signs, nonzero clipping denominators, and strict noncontacts need no row in \(G\) when their first zero is declared to be a boundary event.

For an unmarked genuine vertex \(a_r\), put
\[
\Delta_r(x)=[a_r-a_{r-1},a_{r+1}-a_r]>0,\qquad
\mathcal R(x)=-D\Delta_r(x).
\]
Then the exact first-order contact-release system is
\[
E(x)\xi=0,\qquad G(x)\xi\geq0,\qquad
\mathcal R(x)\xi\geq1,
\tag{2}
\]
where the middle inequality applies to every row in (1), including every appended \(Dh_\beta\) row. Exactly one of the following alternatives holds:
\[
\begin{array}{ll}
\mathrm{(P)}&\text{system (2) has a solution};\\[1mm]
\mathrm{(D)}&\text{there are }y\text{ and }\lambda\geq0\text{ such that}\\
&\displaystyle
\mathcal R(x)=y^{\mathsf T}E(x)-\lambda^{\mathsf T}G(x).
\end{array}
\tag{3}
\]
If every entry of the already instantiated matrices \(E(x),G(x),\mathcal R(x)\) is rational, a certificate for whichever alternative holds may be chosen rational. No rationality of \(D\mathscr C(x)\) is asserted merely from rational polygon vertices.

Write the components of \(\lambda\) as \(\lambda_{sj}\) for active contacts, \(\lambda_{\mathscr C}\) for capacity, and \(\lambda_\beta\) for appended feasibility rows. The \(a_r\)-coordinate block of (3) is the exact common-plane stress identity
\[
\begin{aligned}
J(a_{r+1}-a_{r-1})
={}&
\sum_\alpha y_\alpha\nabla_{a_r}F_\alpha
-\frac12
\sum_{\substack{(s,j)\in I(x)\\\iota_0(j)=r}}
\lambda_{sj}J(k_{s+1}-k_s)\\
&-\lambda_{\mathscr C}\nabla_{a_r}\mathscr C
-\sum_{\beta\in J(x)}
\lambda_\beta\nabla_{a_r}h_\beta .
\end{aligned}
\tag{4}
\]
There are analogous exact coordinate-block balances at every \(a_i,q_j,k_s\). For every velocity satisfying the equality and every row inequality in (2), a dual stress gives
\[
-D\Delta_r(x)[\xi]
=-\lambda^{\mathsf T}G(x)\xi\leq0.
\tag{5}
\]

Let the columns of \(N(x)\) be any basis of \(\ker E(x)\), and set
\[
\overline{\mathcal R}(x)=\mathcal R(x)N(x),\qquad
\overline G(x)=G(x)N(x).
\]
The exact remaining first-order geometric assertion needed for release at \(x\) is
\[
\overline{\mathcal R}(x)\notin
-\operatorname{cone}\{\text{rows of }\overline G(x)\}.
\tag{6}
\]
It is equivalent to (P), and its failure is exactly (D). If (D) holds at every realization in a stratum component, with all active rows of (1) taken at that realization, then \(\Delta_r\) is nondecreasing along every feasible piecewise \(C^1\), hence every feasible piecewise-rational, motion in that component for which \(\mathscr C\) is nondecreasing. Such a component admits no motion deleting \(a_r\).

There is also the following restricted exact first-event rule. Suppose an explicitly constructed rational motion \(x(t)\), \(0\leq t\leq T<\infty\), satisfies \(F(x(t))=F(x(0))\), all original support-contact and appended feasibility inequalities, and nondecreasing capacity. Assume also that each appended \(h_\beta(x(t))\) included in the event list is rational in \(t\). For each non-capacity code function which vanishes at \(t=0\), factor its exact power of \(t\); its first nonzero coefficient determines the one-sided sign on \(0<t\ll1\), while an identically zero function is a persistent equality. If a positive geometric boundary event occurs, it is the least positive zero or pole among:

1. polygon turn determinants;
2. vertex-cut signs, edge-cut denominators, and edge-through-cut-intersection determinants;
3. cross-fan determinants;
4. support-contact slacks;
5. the explicitly supplied appended feasibility slacks \(h_\beta\); and
6. \(\Delta_r\).

The one-sided geometric code is unchanged before that value. At it, either \(\Delta_r=0\), so the collinear vertex may be deleted without changing the polygon, or one or more listed functions identify the declared boundary event. A capacity formula or capacity-code change may be included in this rule only under the additional hypothesis that a finite list of rational functions defining that capacity formula and its validity predicates has been supplied. Without that additional input, the rule makes no claim about capacity rationality, exhaustiveness of capacity events, or persistence of a capacity formula. It also does not assert (6), rational integrability of (2), or iteration to a three-side terminal polygon.

## Proof

We first derive every unconditional geometric row. If an edge with endpoints \(u,v\) meets a fixed affine cut \(\ell=0\) and \(\ell(v)-\ell(u)\neq0\), its intersection is
\[
\mathsf i_\ell(u,v)
=\frac{\ell(v)u-\ell(u)v}{\ell(v)-\ell(u)}.
\tag{7}
\]
Thus every vertex of every clipped chamber polygon is either an original polygon vertex, a fixed cut-cut intersection, or a rational function (7) of two original vertices. If \(w_0(x),\ldots,w_{m-1}(x)\) are the cyclic vertices of one clipped cell on the fixed code, its signed area is
\[
\mathcal A(x)=\frac12\sum_{\nu=0}^{m-1}
[w_\nu(x),w_{\nu+1}(x)]
\tag{8}
\]
and
\[
D\mathcal A(x)[\xi]
=\frac12\sum_{\nu=0}^{m-1}
\left(
[Dw_\nu(x)[\xi],w_{\nu+1}(x)]
+[w_\nu(x),Dw_{\nu+1}(x)[\xi]]
\right).
\tag{9}
\]
Equations (7)-(9), with the quotient rule, give every chamber-area row of \(E\) exactly.

For a persistent cut incidence, the row is \(D(\ell(v))\). For a persistent shared ray,
\[
\begin{aligned}
D[a_{i+1}-a_i,q_{j+1}-q_j][\xi]
={}&[\dot a_{i+1}-\dot a_i,q_{j+1}-q_j]\\
&+[a_{i+1}-a_i,\dot q_{j+1}-\dot q_j].
\end{aligned}
\tag{10}
\]
For containment,
\[
\begin{aligned}
D\eta_{sj}(x)[\xi]
={}&[\dot k_{s+1}-\dot k_s,b_j-k_s]\\
&+[k_{s+1}-k_s,\dot b_j-\dot k_s],
\end{aligned}
\qquad
\dot b_j=\frac{\dot a_{\iota_0(j)}+\dot q_{\iota_2(j)}}2.
\tag{11}
\]
Finally,
\[
\begin{aligned}
D\Delta_r(x)[\xi]
={}&[\dot a_r-\dot a_{r-1},a_{r+1}-a_r]\\
&+[a_r-a_{r-1},\dot a_{r+1}-\dot a_r].
\end{aligned}
\tag{12}
\]
Equations (9)-(12), together with the supplied differentials \(D\mathscr C\) and \(Dh_\beta\), generate all rows of \(E,G,\mathcal R\). Nothing in this derivation supplies a formula for \(\mathscr C\); the capacity row in (1) exists only under the fixed-code \(C^1\) hypothesis in the statement.

To check the crossings, write every endpoint cap and middle cap as the sum of its individual sign-cell areas. In the convention of accepted fact `89563f2f27529ab0`, the \(i\)-th crossing functional is
\[
\sum_{J\ni i}P_J+\sum_{G\ni i}Q_G+\sum_{G\ni i}R_G
-\frac29\left(\sum_JP_J+\sum_GQ_G+\sum_GR_G\right).
\tag{13}
\]
It is a fixed linear combination of the individual chamber-area coordinates, so every vector in \(\ker E\) preserves all three crossings. Appending their rows does not change \(\ker E\).

We prove the exact alternative. Let
\[
\mathcal K=\{\xi:E\xi=0,\ G\xi\geq0\},
\]
where \(G\xi\geq0\) means componentwise nonnegativity for every row in (1). If (D) holds, then every \(\xi\in\mathcal K\) satisfies
\[
\mathcal R\xi
=y^{\mathsf T}E\xi-\lambda^{\mathsf T}G\xi
=-\lambda^{\mathsf T}G\xi\leq0.
\]
Thus (P) is impossible.

Conversely, suppose (D) fails. The set
\[
\mathcal D=\{y^{\mathsf T}E-\lambda^{\mathsf T}G:
y\in\mathbb R^{\operatorname{rows}(E)},\
\lambda\geq0\}
\]
is a closed polyhedral cone. Since \(\mathcal R\notin\mathcal D\), finite-dimensional strong separation gives \(\xi\) such that
\[
d\xi\leq0\quad(d\in\mathcal D),\qquad
\mathcal R\xi>0.
\]
Both signs of every row-space vector belong to \(\mathcal D\), hence \(E\xi=0\). Since \(-\lambda^{\mathsf T}G\in\mathcal D\) for every \(\lambda\geq0\), every component of \(G\xi\) is nonnegative. Scaling by \(1/(\mathcal R\xi)\) proves (P). The alternatives are therefore exclusive and exhaustive.

If \(E,G,\mathcal R\) have rational entries, (P) and (D) are rational polyhedra. A nonempty rational polyhedron contains a rational point, proving the conditional exact-certificate assertion. This argument does not claim that the capacity row is rational.

From (12),
\[
\nabla_{a_r}(-\Delta_r)=J(a_{r+1}-a_{r-1}).
\]
From (11),
\[
\nabla_{a_r}\eta_{sj}
=\frac12J(k_{s+1}-k_s)
\quad\text{if }\iota_0(j)=r,
\]
and this gradient is zero otherwise. Taking the \(a_r\)-block of (3), including every appended feasibility-row block, gives (4). Multiplying (3) by a velocity satisfying every condition in (2) gives (5).

Substitute \(\xi=Nz\). Before normalization, (2) becomes
\[
\overline Gz\geq0,\qquad
\overline{\mathcal R}z>0.
\]
Applying the proved alternative in the reduced coordinates shows that this is feasible exactly when no \(\lambda\geq0\) satisfies
\[
\overline{\mathcal R}=-\lambda^{\mathsf T}\overline G,
\]
which is (6).

Let \(x(t)\) be a feasible piecewise \(C^1\) motion and assume a dual certificate exists at each point with every currently active row. At a differentiability point, \(DF(x(t))x'(t)=0\). Any active nonnegative slack has nonnegative one-sided derivative at an endpoint and derivative zero at an interior contact time. The assumed monotonicity of the supplied \(C^1\) capacity gives \(D\mathscr C(x(t))x'(t)\geq0\). Hence every row of \(G\) has nonnegative pairing with the feasible one-sided velocity, and (5) yields
\[
-\frac{d}{dt}\Delta_r(x(t))\leq0.
\]
Thus \(\Delta_r\) is nondecreasing and cannot reach zero from a positive value. This proves the uniform path obstruction.

For the restricted event assertion, (7)-(12) show that each listed non-capacity geometric predicate is rational along a rational motion. Factoring its exact order at \(t=0\) determines the one-sided sign or persistent equality. After this initialization, each remaining numerator and denominator is a nonzero univariate polynomial and has finitely many roots on \([0,T]\). If the finite family has a positive root or pole, its least one is exact and no listed sign changes beforehand. A zero of \(\Delta_r\) makes \(a_r\) collinear with its neighbors; convexity and unchanged cyclic edge order place it on their segment, so deletion leaves the convex set and therefore every chamber area, crossing, and contact unchanged. Another first zero or pole is the corresponding declared geometric event.

The same reasoning applies to capacity only if its exact rational formula and a finite exhaustive rational predicate list for the validity of that formula are separately supplied. The accepted predecessor used here gives no such list, so no unconditional capacity-event claim follows. The proof therefore stops at the exact cone condition (6). A feasible direction at one realization does not establish (6) uniformly, and a tangent direction does not by itself give a piecewise-rational curve or an iteration to a three-side terminal polygon.
