## Statement

Write planar coordinates as \((u,v)\), and define the rational rectangles
\[
\begin{aligned}
A_0&=[-1,1]\times[-1/4,1/4],\\
A_1&=[-5/8,103/40]\times[-5/32,5/32],\\
A_2&=[-1/4,1/4]\times[-1/16,1/16].
\end{aligned}
\]
These polygons form a compatible balanced triple: there is a compact polytope \(C\subseteq\mathbb R^3\) whose integer-height sections are exactly \(\{i\}\times A_i\), and their areas satisfy \(a_i<T/2\) for every \(i\). Nevertheless, for
\[
y=\left(1,\frac{g_0+g_2}{2}\right),
\]
the single closed halfspace
\[
H=\{(x,u,v)\in\mathbb R^3:u\le x-1\}
\]
satisfies
\[
\mathcal H_2(S\cap H)=\frac{33}{128}<\frac{2T}{9}.
\]
Consequently \(h_S(y)<2T/9\).

## Proof

Coordinatewise Minkowski addition gives
\[
\frac{A_0+A_2}{2}
=
[-5/8,5/8]\times[-5/32,5/32]
\subseteq A_1.
\]
Thus accepted fact `8ad0785e5b4ae9ef` shows that
\[
C=\operatorname{conv}\left(
(\{0\}\times A_0)\cup(\{1\}\times A_1)\cup(\{2\}\times A_2)
\right)
\]
is a compact polytope whose integer-height sections are exactly the three prescribed slices.

The areas are
\[
a_0=2\cdot\frac12=1,\qquad
a_1=\left(\frac{103}{40}+\frac58\right)\frac5{16}
=\frac{16}{5}\frac5{16}=1,
\qquad
a_2=\frac12\cdot\frac18=\frac1{16}.
\]
Hence
\[
T=\frac{33}{16},
\qquad
\frac T2=\frac{33}{32},
\]
so \(a_0,a_1,a_2<T/2\).

Both \(A_0\) and \(A_2\) are centrally symmetric about the origin, hence
\[
g_0=g_2=(0,0).
\]
Therefore \(y=(1,0,0)\), which belongs to \(\{1\}\times A_1\). The boundary of
\[
H=\{(x,u,v):u\le x-1\}
\]
contains \(y\). Its three slice sections have areas
\[
\left|A_0\cap\{u\le-1\}\right|=0,
\]
because this intersection is only the edge
\(\{-1\}\times[-1/4,1/4]\),
\[
\left|A_1\cap\{u\le0\}\right|
=
\frac58\cdot\frac5{16}
=
\frac{25}{128},
\]
and
\[
\left|A_2\cap\{u\le1\}\right|
=
|A_2|
=
\frac1{16}
=
\frac8{128}.
\]
Thus
\[
\mathcal H_2(S\cap H)
=
\frac{25}{128}+\frac8{128}
=
\frac{33}{128}.
\]
On the other hand,
\[
\frac{2T}{9}
=
\frac{2}{9}\frac{33}{16}
=
\frac{11}{24},
\qquad
\frac{11}{24}-\frac{33}{128}
=
\frac{77}{384}>0.
\]
Since \(H\) is a closed halfspace containing \(y\),
\[
h_S(y)\le\mathcal H_2(S\cap H)
=\frac{33}{128}
<\frac{2T}{9}.
\]
