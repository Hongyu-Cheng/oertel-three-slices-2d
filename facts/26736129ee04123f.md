## Statement

Fix \(x,\ell,b>0\) with \(x+\ell+b=1\), put
\(\lambda=x+\ell\), and assume
\[
\lambda\leq t_1<t_2<1.
\]
Define
\[
D=\frac{16\ell-9(b+\ell)^2}{2},\qquad
K_0=\frac{5b^2-6b\ell+5\ell^2}{2},
\]
\[
a_*(t)=\frac{-t^2+2t+9x^2-2x}{2},
\]
and, on this chamber,
\[
k_*(t)=\frac{8\ell^2+3t^2-6t-3x^2+6x}{2}.
\]
There do not exist \(h\geq0\) and \(d_1,d_2>0\) satisfying
\[
h+d_1+d_2<\ell/x,
\]
\[
\ell+(1-x)h+(t_1-x)d_1+(t_2-x)d_2\geq b,
\]
\[
a_*(1)h+a_*(t_1)d_1+a_*(t_2)d_2<D,
\]
\[
K_0+k_*(1)h+k_*(t_1)d_1+k_*(t_2)d_2<0.
\]

## Proof

Put
\[
a_1=a_*(1)=\frac{1+9x^2-2x}{2},
\qquad
k_1=k_*(1)=\frac{8\ell^2-3(1-x)^2}{2}.
\]
Direct expansion, with \(y=1-t\), gives for every \(t\in[\lambda,1]\)
\[
a_*(t)=a_1-\frac{y^2}{2},
\qquad
k_*(t)=k_1+\frac{3y^2}{2}.
\tag{1}
\]
In particular,
\[
k_1+3a_1=4\ell^2+12x^2>0.
\tag{2}
\]
Also \(a_1>0\), since
\[
2a_1=9\left(x-\frac19\right)^2+\frac89,
\]
and \(K_0>0\), since
\[
2K_0=5(b-\ell)^2+4b\ell.
\tag{3}
\]

If \(k_1\geq0\), then (1) gives \(k_*(t)\geq0\) throughout the
chamber.  Hence
\[
K_0+k_1h+k_*(t_1)d_1+k_*(t_2)d_2\geq K_0>0,
\]
contrary to the last inequality.

It remains to consider \(k_1<0\).  Define the positive multiplier
\[
\mu=-\frac{k_1}{a_1}.
\tag{4}
\]
Equation (2) gives \(0<\mu<3\).  Equations (1) and (4) then give the
coefficientwise dual inequality
\[
k_*(t)+\mu a_*(t)
=\frac{3-\mu}{2}(1-t)^2\geq0
\qquad(\lambda\leq t\leq1).
\tag{5}
\]
Thus the multiplier of the \(A\)-budget is \(\mu\), while the
multipliers of the total-positivity budget and the full-right constraint
are both \(0\).

Applying (5) to \(t=1,t_1,t_2\), using the nonnegative weights
\(h,d_1,d_2\), and then using the strict \(A\)-budget gives
\[
\begin{aligned}
K_0+k_1h+k_*(t_1)d_1+k_*(t_2)d_2
&\geq
K_0-\mu\bigl(a_1h+a_*(t_1)d_1+a_*(t_2)d_2\bigr)\\
&>K_0-\mu D
=\frac{K_0a_1+k_1D}{a_1}.
\end{aligned}
\tag{6}
\]
It remains only to check that the last numerator is nonnegative.

Set
\[
r=b+\ell=1-x,\qquad p=\frac{\ell}{r}.
\]
Because \(x,\ell,b>0\), one has \(0<r<1\) and \(0<p<1\).  Substitution
into the definitions gives the exact identity
\[
K_0a_1+k_1D=2r^2F(p,r),
\tag{7}
\]
where
\[
\begin{aligned}
F(p,r)
={}&9(1-p)^2r^2
-2(1-p)(8p^2-8p+5)r\\
&\quad+16p^2-16p+5.
\end{aligned}
\]
Completing the square yields
\[
\begin{aligned}
F(p,r)
={}&\left(
3(1-p)r-\frac{8p^2-8p+5}{3}
\right)^2\\
&\quad+
\frac{4(2p-1)^2(5+4p-4p^2)}{9}\geq0,
\end{aligned}
\tag{8}
\]
because \(5+4p-4p^2=5+4p(1-p)>0\).  Equations (7)--(8) show
\[
K_0a_1+k_1D\geq0.
\]
Since \(a_1>0\), (6) is therefore strictly positive, again
contradicting the last inequality of the system.

The proof used only the right-chamber formulas (1), which remain valid at
\(t=\lambda\).  Hence the weak boundary \(t_1=\lambda\) is included.
