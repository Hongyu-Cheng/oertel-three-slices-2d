## Statement

Assume the common affine-plane, compatibility, exact-cell, and positive-area hypotheses of accepted fact `d3afd91c459bcce8`, relabeled as
\[
f_1+f_2+f_3=-1,\qquad
L_1+L_2+L_3<-1,
\]
\[
\frac{A_0+A_2}{2}\subseteq K,\qquad
p_\varnothing>0,\qquad
|Q_{\{1\}}|>0,\qquad |Q_{\{2\}}|>0.
\]
Put
\[
\Delta=-1-(L_1+L_2+L_3)>0,
\]
\[
a_i(x)=f_i(x)-L_i,\qquad
q_i(y)=f_i(y)+L_i,
\]
and let
\[
P=\overline{P_\varnothing},\qquad
Q_1=\overline{Q_{\{1\}}},\qquad
Q_2=\overline{Q_{\{2\}}}.
\]
Define
\[
\Gamma_{12}
:=
\left|
\operatorname{conv}\!\left(
\frac{P_\varnothing+\overline{Q_{\{1\}}}}2
\mathbin{\cup}
\frac{P_\varnothing+\overline{Q_{\{2\}}}}2
\right)
\cap R_{\{1,2\}}
\right|.
\tag{0}
\]
Define four compact convex endpoint lifts in
\(\mathbb R^2\times\mathbb R\) by
\[
\mathscr P_1
=\{(x,t):x\in P,\ 0\leq t\leq a_2(x)+a_3(x)\},
\]
\[
\mathscr Q_1
=\{(y,t):y\in Q_1,\ 0\leq t\leq2+q_2(y)+q_3(y)\},
\]
\[
\mathscr P_2
=\{(x,t):x\in P,\ 0\leq t\leq a_1(x)+a_3(x)\},
\]
\[
\mathscr Q_2
=\{(y,t):y\in Q_2,\ 0\leq t\leq2+q_1(y)+q_3(y)\}.
\]
Set
\[
\mathscr U_i=\frac{\mathscr P_i+\mathscr Q_i}{2}
\quad(i=1,2),
\qquad
\mathscr H=\operatorname{conv}(\mathscr U_1\cup\mathscr U_2),
\]
\[
U_i=\frac{P+Q_i}{2},
\qquad
H=\operatorname{conv}(U_1\cup U_2).
\]
Then
\[
\mathscr U_1
=\{(z,t):z\in U_1,\ 0\leq t\leq-f_1(z)\},
\tag{1}
\]
\[
\mathscr U_2
=\{(z,t):z\in U_2,\ 0\leq t\leq-f_2(z)\}.
\tag{2}
\]
Moreover the joint bridge lift
\[
\mathscr B
=
\left\{
(z,t):
z\in H\cap R_{\{1,2\}},\
0\leq t\leq-f_1(z)-f_2(z)
\right\}
\tag{3}
\]
is contained in \(\mathscr H\). Since
\[
-f_1-f_2=1+f_3\geq1
\quad\hbox{on }R_{\{1,2\}},
\tag{4}
\]
the joint lifted-volume excess
\[
\mathcal J_{12}
:=
\lambda_3(\mathscr H)
-\lambda_3(\mathscr U_1)
-\lambda_3(\mathscr U_2)
\tag{5}
\]
satisfies the exact physical-area charge
\[
\mathcal J_{12}
\geq
\int_{H\cap R_{\{1,2\}}}(1+f_3(z))\,dz
\geq
\Gamma_{12}.
\tag{6}
\]
Finally, the compact-convex Brunn--Minkowski step used in accepted
fact `c33057d413e5306c` applies to the actual endpoint pairings and gives
\[
\lambda_3(\mathscr U_i)^{1/3}
\geq
\frac{
\lambda_3(\mathscr P_i)^{1/3}
+\lambda_3(\mathscr Q_i)^{1/3}
}{2},
\qquad i=1,2.
\tag{7}
\]

## Proof

The constant-sum equations are
\[
a_1+a_2+a_3=\Delta,
\qquad
q_1+q_2+q_3=-(\Delta+2).
\tag{8}
\]
On \(P\), all \(a_i\) are nonnegative. On \(Q_1\),
\[
q_1\leq0,\qquad q_2,q_3\geq0,
\]
and on \(Q_2\),
\[
q_2\leq0,\qquad q_1,q_3\geq0.
\]
Therefore every displayed endpoint height is nonnegative. Each lift is
the intersection of a compact convex cylinder with the hypograph of
an affine function and with \(\{t\geq0\}\), so all four endpoint lifts
are nonempty compact convex sets.

Fix \(x\in P\), \(y\in Q_1\), and put \(z=(x+y)/2\). Affinity gives
\[
2f_i(z)=a_i(x)+q_i(y)
\qquad(i=1,2,3).
\tag{9}
\]
The sum of the two endpoint heights in the first pairing is
\[
\begin{aligned}
&a_2(x)+a_3(x)+2+q_2(y)+q_3(y)\\
&\qquad
=2+2f_2(z)+2f_3(z)
=-2f_1(z).
\end{aligned}
\tag{10}
\]
For fixed \(x,y\), the midpoint sum of the two vertical intervals is
exactly
\[
\left[0,\frac{-2f_1(z)}2\right]
=[0,-f_1(z)].
\]
The upper endpoint in (10) depends only on \(z\), not on its
representation as a midpoint. Taking all \(x,y\) proves (1). The same
argument, with indices \(1,2\) interchanged, proves (2).

Equation (9) also shows that on \(U_1\),
\[
f_2\geq0,\qquad f_3\geq0,\qquad
f_1=-1-f_2-f_3\leq-1,
\tag{11}
\]
whereas on \(U_2\),
\[
f_1\geq0,\qquad f_3\geq0,\qquad
f_2=-1-f_1-f_3\leq-1.
\tag{12}
\]
In particular, the interiors of \(U_1,U_2\) lie in the two distinct
middle sign cells \(R_{\{1\}}\) and \(R_{\{2\}}\), respectively.

Because \(U_1,U_2\) are convex, every \(z\in H\) has a representation
\[
z=(1-\theta)z_1+\theta z_2
\tag{13}
\]
with \(z_i\in U_i\) and \(0\leq\theta\leq1\). The two roof points
\[
(z_1,-f_1(z_1))\in\mathscr U_1,
\qquad
(z_2,-f_2(z_2))\in\mathscr U_2
\]
give, by convexity of \(\mathscr H\), the point \((z,T)\in\mathscr H\),
where
\[
T=(1-\theta)(-f_1(z_1))
+\theta(-f_2(z_2)).
\tag{14}
\]
Using (13), the difference between this height and the desired
two-row height is
\[
\begin{aligned}
T-\bigl(-f_1(z)-f_2(z)\bigr)
&=(1-\theta)f_2(z_1)+\theta f_1(z_2)\\
&\geq0
\end{aligned}
\tag{15}
\]
by (11)-(12). The two bottom points \((z_1,0)\), \((z_2,0)\) similarly
give \((z,0)\in\mathscr H\). Hence the full vertical interval
\[
\{z\}\times[0,-f_1(z)-f_2(z)]
\]
lies in \(\mathscr H\) whenever the upper endpoint is nonnegative.
For \(z\in H\cap R_{\{1,2\}}\), (4) makes it at least \(1\), proving
\(\mathscr B\subseteq\mathscr H\).

The base of \(\mathscr U_1\) lies in
\(\{f_2\geq0,f_3\geq0\}\), the base of \(\mathscr U_2\) lies in
\(\{f_1\geq0,f_3\geq0\}\), and the base of \(\mathscr B\) lies in
\(\{f_1\leq0,f_2\leq0,f_3>0\}\). Their pairwise base intersections
are contained in cutting lines and therefore have planar measure zero.
Thus the three lifted sets have pairwise three-dimensional
measure-zero intersections. Since all three lie in \(\mathscr H\),
\[
\lambda_3(\mathscr H)
\geq
\lambda_3(\mathscr U_1)
+\lambda_3(\mathscr U_2)
+\lambda_3(\mathscr B).
\tag{16}
\]
Tonelli's theorem and (4) give
\[
\lambda_3(\mathscr B)
=
\int_{H\cap R_{\{1,2\}}}(1+f_3(z))\,dz
\geq
|H\cap R_{\{1,2\}}|.
\tag{17}
\]
By the literal definition (0),
\[
\operatorname{conv}\!\left(
\frac{P_\varnothing+\overline{Q_{\{1\}}}}2
\mathbin{\cup}
\frac{P_\varnothing+\overline{Q_{\{2\}}}}2
\right)
\subseteq H,
\]
because \(P_\varnothing\subseteq P\) and
\(\overline{Q_{\{i\}}}=Q_i\). Hence
\[
|H\cap R_{\{1,2\}}|\geq\Gamma_{12}.
\tag{18}
\]
Equations (16)-(18) prove (6).

Finally, (1)-(2) are exact midpoint Minkowski sums of nonempty compact
convex sets. Applying the three-dimensional Brunn--Minkowski inequality
in the zero-volume-safe form verified in accepted fact
`c33057d413e5306c` proves (7). No closure changes a planar area or a
cell support: every added base point lies in a finite union of cutting
lines. The original hull defining \(\Gamma_{12}\) was used only through
the literal containment in (18). Thus all uses of the exact
\(\leq\)/\(>\) cell convention are unchanged.
