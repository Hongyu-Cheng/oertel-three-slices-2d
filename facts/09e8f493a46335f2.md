## Statement

Assume the hypotheses and notation of accepted fact
`05153059dfd604c8`, restated as follows.  Let \(N=\{1,2,3\}\).  The
nonconstant affine functions \(f_1,f_2,f_3\) have pairwise nonparallel
linear parts and satisfy
\[
f_1+f_2+f_3=-1,\qquad L_1+L_2+L_3<-1.
\]
The sets \(A_0,A_2,K\subseteq\mathbb R^2\) are nonempty
two-dimensional convex polytopes satisfying
\[
\frac{A_0+A_2}{2}\subseteq K.
\]
For \(J\subseteq N\), define
\[
P_J=A_0\cap
\{f_i\leq L_i\ (i\in J),\ f_i>L_i\ (i\notin J)\},
\]
\[
Q_J=A_2\cap
\{f_i\leq-L_i\ (i\in J),\ f_i>-L_i\ (i\notin J)\},
\]
\[
R_J=K\cap
\{f_i\leq0\ (i\in J),\ f_i>0\ (i\notin J)\},
\]
and write
\[
p_J=|P_J|,\qquad q_J=|Q_J|,\qquad r_J=|R_J|.
\]
Put
\[
M=|A_0|,\qquad m=|A_2|,\qquad \kappa=|K|,
\]
\[
u_i=\sum_{J\ni i}p_J,\qquad
v_i=\sum_{J\ni i}q_J,\qquad
c_i=\sum_{J\ni i}r_J,\qquad
D_i=u_i+v_i+c_i,
\]
\[
h=\frac{2(M+m+\kappa)}9.
\]
Assume
\[
0<u_i<M,\qquad 0<v_i<m,\qquad 0<c_i<\kappa
\qquad(i\in N),
\]
\[
D_i=h,\qquad c_i+m\geq h,\qquad c_i+M\geq h
\qquad(i\in N),
\]
and
\[
W:=
\sum_{J\subseteq N}(3|J|-2)p_J+
\sum_{J\subseteq N}(3|J|-2)q_J+
\sum_{J\subseteq N}(3|J|-2)r_J=0.
\]
Assume that
\[
\{J:q_J>0\}=\{123,1,2,12\}.
\]
Write
\[
q=q_{123},\qquad a=q_1,\qquad b=q_2,\qquad
\gamma=q_{12},\qquad p=p_\varnothing,
\]
so that
\[
q,a,b,\gamma>0,\qquad m=q+a+b+\gamma.
\]
Put
\[
\Delta=-1-(L_1+L_2+L_3),\qquad
\mathcal T_P=\{x:f_i(x)>L_i\ (i=1,2,3)\},
\]
\[
\Theta=|\mathcal T_P|,\qquad \alpha=\frac p\Theta.
\]
Let \(y_1,y_2\) be the area centroids of \(Q_1,Q_2\), set
\(\eta_i=f_i+L_i\), and define
\[
A_*=\frac{\eta_1(y_2)-\eta_1(y_1)}{\Delta},\qquad
B_*=\frac{\eta_2(y_1)-\eta_2(y_2)}{\Delta},
\]
\[
\Lambda=\max\{A_*,B_*\},\qquad
\lambda=\min\{A_*,B_*\}.
\]
Then
\[
r_1+r_2+r_{12}\geq
\frac14\left(\sqrt p+\sqrt{m-q}\right)^2,
\]
and
\[
\begin{aligned}
2p>{}&\frac32(a+b)+5\gamma+7q+7r_{123}\\
&+\frac38\left(\sqrt p+\sqrt{m-q}\right)^2
+\frac{7p\Lambda}
{2\Delta\lambda(1+\sqrt{1-\alpha})}.
\end{aligned}
\]

## Proof

First audit the impossible cells and the positivity needed below.  If a
point belonged to \(P_{123}\), summing its three weak inequalities would
give
\[
-1=\sum_i f_i\leq\sum_iL_i<-1,
\]
which is impossible.  Similarly, a point of \(Q_\varnothing\) would
satisfy
\[
-1=\sum_i f_i>-\sum_iL_i>1,
\]
and a point of \(R_\varnothing\) would satisfy
\(-1=\sum_i f_i>0\).  Hence
\[
P_{123}=Q_\varnothing=R_\varnothing=\varnothing.
\tag{1}
\]
The coefficient of an empty-index cell in \(W\) is \(-2\), whereas the
coefficients of singleton, doubleton, and triple cells are \(1,4,7\).
Thus (1), the prescribed positive \(Q\)-support, and \(W=0\) give
\[
2p\geq a+b+4\gamma+7q>0.
\tag{2}
\]
In particular \(p>0\).  Also \(m-q=a+b+\gamma>0\).

Consider the literal endpoint upper half
\[
U=A_2\cap\{f_3>-L_3\}.
\]
The exact weak/strict cell convention partitions this set as
\[
U=Q_\varnothing\mathbin{\dot\cup}Q_1
\mathbin{\dot\cup}Q_2\mathbin{\dot\cup}Q_{12}
=Q_1\mathbin{\dot\cup}Q_2\mathbin{\dot\cup}Q_{12},
\tag{3}
\]
where the last equality uses (1).  Consequently
\[
|U|=a+b+\gamma=m-q.
\tag{4}
\]
Because \(A_0,A_2\) are closed,
\[
\overline{P_\varnothing}\setminus P_\varnothing
\subseteq\bigcup_{i=1}^3\{f_i=L_i\},
\qquad
\overline U\setminus U\subseteq\{f_3=-L_3\}.
\tag{5}
\]
Every set on the right of (5) is a line and hence has planar area zero.
Thus
\[
|\overline{P_\varnothing}|=p,\qquad
|\overline U|=m-q.
\tag{6}
\]
Both closures are compact convex sets of positive area.

For \(x\in\overline{P_\varnothing}\) and \(y\in\overline U\), one has
\[
f_3(x)\geq L_3,\qquad f_3(y)\geq-L_3.
\]
Compatibility and affinity therefore give the exact closed inclusion
\[
\frac{\overline{P_\varnothing}+\overline U}{2}
\subseteq K\cap\{f_3\geq0\}.
\tag{7}
\]
On the strict upper half \(f_3>0\), the only possible middle cells are
\(R_\varnothing,R_1,R_2,R_{12}\), and \(R_\varnothing\) is empty by
(1).  The remaining boundary \(K\cap\{f_3=0\}\) lies in a line.
Therefore the exact boundary convention gives
\[
|K\cap\{f_3\geq0\}|=r_1+r_2+r_{12}.
\tag{8}
\]

The planar Brunn--Minkowski inequality, applied to the two closures in
(6), states
\[
\left|
\frac{\overline{P_\varnothing}+\overline U}{2}
\right|^{1/2}
\geq
\frac12\left(
|\overline{P_\varnothing}|^{1/2}+|\overline U|^{1/2}
\right).
\tag{9}
\]
Squaring (9) and using (6)--(8) proves
\[
r_1+r_2+r_{12}\geq
\frac14\left(\sqrt p+\sqrt{m-q}\right)^2.
\tag{10}
\]
This calculation also shows why the closures and the line
\(f_3=0\) change no coefficient or area.

It remains to audit the weighted crossing ledger.  Since
\(D_1=D_2=D_3=h\),
\[
\frac12D_1+\frac12D_2-D_3=0.
\tag{11}
\]
For any one of the \(P,Q,R\) cells indexed by \(J\), its coefficient in
\(W+\frac12D_1+\frac12D_2-D_3\) is
\[
\omega(J)=3|J|-2
+\frac12\mathbf 1_{\{1\in J\}}
+\frac12\mathbf 1_{\{2\in J\}}
-\mathbf 1_{\{3\in J\}}.
\tag{12}
\]
Every exact-cell coefficient is
\[
\begin{array}{c|rrrrrrrr}
J&\varnothing&1&2&3&12&13&23&123\\ \hline
\omega(J)&-2&\frac32&\frac32&0&5&\frac72&\frac72&7.
\end{array}
\tag{13}
\]
The same row applies separately to the \(P,Q,R\) families.  Using
(1), the exact \(Q\)-support, and (11), the identity represented by
(13) is
\[
\begin{aligned}
0={}&-2p+\frac32(a+b)+5\gamma+7q\\
&+\frac32(r_1+r_2)+5r_{12}+7r_{123}+E,
\end{aligned}
\tag{14}
\]
where the complete positive-coefficient remainder is
\[
\begin{aligned}
E={}&\frac32(p_1+p_2)+5p_{12}
+\frac72(p_{13}+p_{23})+7p_{123}\\
&+\frac72(q_{13}+q_{23})
+\frac72(r_{13}+r_{23})\geq0.
\end{aligned}
\tag{15}
\]
The cells \(P_3,Q_3,R_3\) have coefficient zero.  The only negative
cells omitted from (14) are \(Q_\varnothing,R_\varnothing\), which are
empty by (1).  Thus (13)--(15) audit every omitted cell and show that
none has an unaccounted negative coefficient.

Rewrite the middle contribution in (14) as
\[
\frac32(r_1+r_2)+5r_{12}
=\frac32(r_1+r_2+r_{12})+\frac72r_{12}.
\tag{16}
\]
The positivity in (2), together with \(a=|Q_1|>0\) and
\(b=|Q_2|>0\), verifies the hypotheses of accepted fact
`f1fcc678305bbd1c`.  Its closed-cell quantitative bridge gives
\[
r_{12}>
\frac{p\Lambda}
{\Delta\lambda(1+\sqrt{1-\alpha})}.
\tag{17}
\]
The inequality is strict because that fact constructs a positive-area
subset of \(R_{12}\) whose area is strictly larger than the right-hand
side; its weak \(f_1,f_2\) boundaries and strict \(f_3\) boundary agree
with the present definition of \(R_{12}\).

Finally, solve (14) for \(2p\), use \(E\geq0\), substitute (10) into
the first term on the right of (16), and substitute the strict bound
(17) into the second.  This yields
\[
\begin{aligned}
2p>{}&\frac32(a+b)+5\gamma+7q+7r_{123}\\
&+\frac38\left(\sqrt p+\sqrt{m-q}\right)^2
+\frac{7p\Lambda}
{2\Delta\lambda(1+\sqrt{1-\alpha})},
\end{aligned}
\]
as required.  The final strict sign comes solely from (17), multiplied
by its exact positive ledger coefficient \(7/2\).
