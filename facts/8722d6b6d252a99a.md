## Statement

Assume the canonical finite-interior hypotheses, exact-cell convention, all-double positive \(Q\)-support
\[
\{123,12,13,23\},
\]
positive \(p_\varnothing,r_{123},r_1,r_2,r_3\), the three crossings, and the canonical tails.  Put
\[
q=q_{123},\qquad x_i=q_{N\setminus\{i\}},\qquad
m=q+x_1+x_2+x_3,\qquad
D=\frac{q}{|\mathcal T_Q|}.
\]
If
\[
\frac{x_i}{q}\ge\frac25\qquad(i=1,2,3),
\]
then
\[
D\ge\frac{15}{64}>
\frac{q}{\Phi(m,q)},\qquad
\Phi(m,q)=
\left(\frac{\sqrt m+\sqrt{120m+84q}}7\right)^2.
\]
Consequently
\[
|\mathcal T_Q|<\Phi(m,q),
\]
and no full common-plane realization in this ratio region can have \(W=0\).
This includes all endpoint-coincidence, collinear-label, cap-feasibility-equality, and ratio-boundary strata allowed by the stated positive-cell hypotheses.

## Proof

Write
\[
t_i=\frac{x_i}{q},\qquad R=t_1+t_2+t_3,\qquad
P=t_1t_2t_3.
\]
Accepted fact `c49613ac26c0c4a8` identifies the central \(Q\)-cell and the three doubleton \(Q\)-cells as, respectively, the central cell and three pairwise interior-disjoint caps of \(A_2\) relative to \(\mathcal T_Q\), with \(D=q/|\mathcal T_Q|\).  Hence accepted fact `6aa12d1b76699246` applies, including on every endpoint-coincidence stratum, and gives
\[
D\ge\delta(t_1,t_2,t_3)
=\min\{d_0,d_{11},d_{12},d_{13},d_{21},d_{22},d_{23}\}.
\tag{1}
\]

Accepted fact `8234d1112ec2f031`, applied to these same three caps in \(A_2\), gives both exact necessary cap constraints
\[
4P\le1+R,\qquad
\sum_{i=1}^3\sqrt{\frac{t_i}{1+R}}\le\frac32.
\tag{2}
\]
Only the product constraint is needed below; thus the proof covers, in particular, the full subset also satisfying the square-root constraint, including equality in either constraint.

First,
\[
d_0=\min\left\{1,\frac3{t_1^{-1}+t_2^{-1}+t_3^{-1}}\right\}
\ge\frac25.
\tag{3}
\]
For any \(k\), with \(\{i,j,k\}=\{1,2,3\}\), the quantities of accepted fact `6aa12d1b76699246` satisfy
\[
\frac{c_k^2}{A_k}
=\frac{4t_it_jt_k}{1+R}
=:y.
\]
The first inequality in (2) gives \(0<y\le1\).  Moreover, \(y\) is strictly increasing in each ratio on the positive orthant, since, for example,
\[
\frac{\partial y}{\partial t_k}
=\frac{4t_it_j(1+t_i+t_j)}{(1+R)^2}>0.
\]
Therefore \(t_i\ge2/5\) gives
\[
y\ge
\frac{4(2/5)^3}{1+3(2/5)}
=\frac{32}{275}.
\]
The function \(4y/(1+y)^2\) is nondecreasing on \(0<y\le1\).  Consequently, for every \(k\),
\[
d_{1k}
=\frac{4y}{(1+y)^2}
\ge\frac{35200}{94249}
>\frac{15}{64}.
\tag{4}
\]

It remains to bound \(d_{2k}\).  Fix \(k\), and abbreviate
\[
u=t_i,\qquad v=t_j,\qquad w=t_k,\qquad
A=1+\frac{1+u+v}{w},\qquad c=2\sqrt{uv}.
\]
Since \(u,v,w\ge2/5\),
\[
A\le\frac72+\frac52(u+v).
\tag{5}
\]
Define
\[
H(u,v)=
\frac{(7+30\sqrt{uv})^2}{64}
-\left(\frac72+\frac52(u+v)\right).
\]
On \(u,v\ge2/5\),
\[
\frac{\partial H}{\partial u}
=\frac{900v+210\sqrt{v/u}-160}{64}>0,
\qquad
\frac{\partial H}{\partial v}
=\frac{900u+210\sqrt{u/v}-160}{64}>0.
\]
Thus
\[
H(u,v)\ge H(2/5,2/5)=\frac9{64}>0.
\]
Combining this with (5) yields
\[
\sqrt A\le\frac{7+30\sqrt{uv}}8
=\frac{7+15c}{8}.
\]
It follows that
\[
\frac{\sqrt A-c}{1+c}\le\frac78.
\]
Since the clipping function in accepted fact `6aa12d1b76699246` takes values in \([0,1]\), the last inequality, including the case in which its left side is negative, gives
\[
d_{2k}
=1-\operatorname{clip}\left(\frac{\sqrt A-c}{1+c}\right)^2
\ge1-\left(\frac78\right)^2
=\frac{15}{64}.
\tag{6}
\]
Equations (1), (3), (4), and (6) prove
\[
D\ge\frac{15}{64}.
\tag{7}
\]

Now \(m=q(1+R)\) and \(R\ge6/5\).  Hence
\[
\frac{q}{\Phi(m,q)}
=
\frac{49}{
\left(\sqrt{1+R}+\sqrt{120(1+R)+84}\right)^2}
<
\frac{49}{120(1+R)+84}
\le\frac{49}{348}
<\frac{15}{64}.
\tag{8}
\]
The first inequality in (8) is strict even when \(R=6/5\), because \(\sqrt{1+R}>0\).  Thus (7)-(8) imply
\[
|\mathcal T_Q|=\frac qD<\Phi(m,q).
\]
Taking \(U(q,x_1,x_2,x_3)=\Phi(m,q)\) in accepted fact `084bf2f472bf29c7` rules out \(W=0\).

All inequalities defining the ratio region are non-strict, and the proof explicitly allows equality in (2), \(t_i=2/5\), \(\min_i t_i=9/20\), and \(\max_i t_i=11/20\).  Endpoint coincidences, collinear or redundant boundary labels, and cutting-line boundary assignments are already included in accepted facts `6aa12d1b76699246` and `c49613ac26c0c4a8`; no limiting genericity assumption is added here.  This proves the stated exhaustive closed-region exclusion.
