## Statement

Fix the \(A_0\) decagon \(v_1,\ldots,v_{10}\) of accepted fact `18e8f69f5a782c16` and the \(A_2\) decagon \(z_1,\ldots,z_{10}\) of accepted fact `f8cf62cef1390fac`, without changing any vertex, cutting line, or scalar datum.  Put
\[
(r,s)=(f_j,f_\ell),\qquad f_k=-1-r-s.
\]
In these common middle coordinates, their exact Minkowski half-sum \(B=(A_0+A_2)/2\) has area
\[
|B|=\frac{10082813221}{384000000}.
\]
Its only nonempty strict middle cells and their exact areas are
\[
\begin{array}{c|c|c|c}

|B\cap R_I|-\text{prescribed }|R_I|\\ \hline

Thus an \(A_0\) point with displayed coordinates \((X,Y)\) and an \(A_2\) point with displayed coordinates \((U,V)\) have common-coordinate midpoint
