## Statement

Let \(A_0,A_2,K\subset\mathbb R^2\) be nonempty two-dimensional convex polytopes in the canonical finite-boundary setting, relabeled so that
\[
M=|A_0|\ge m=|A_2|>0,\qquad k=|K|,\qquad
t=\frac{2(M+m+k)}9,
\]
and so that the support-contact direction is row \(1\). Let \(f_1,f_2,f_3\) be the canonical affine rows, with
\[
f_1+f_2+f_3=-1,
\]
and put
\[
c_i=|K\cap\{f_i\le0\}|.
\]
For the endpoint thresholds \(L_i\), put
\[
u_i=|A_0\cap\{f_i\le L_i\}|,\qquad
v_i=|A_2\cap\{f_i\le-L_i\}|.
\]
Assume rows \(2\) and \(3\) are both empty-full lower crossings. Thus, in the terminology and classification of accepted fact `624b129b68e0fdee`,
\[
u_2=u_3=0,\qquad
v_2=v_3=m,\qquad
c_2=c_3=t-m.
\]
Assume also the residual area hypotheses of accepted fact `15bb2c1680a0254e`; in particular,
\[
k>M-m.
\]
If
\[
c_1+v_1<t,
\]
then
\[
\frac mM<\frac13.
\]

## Proof

For every \(x\in K\), at least one of \(f_1(x),f_2(x),f_3(x)\) is nonpositive. Indeed, if all three were positive, then
\[
f_1(x)+f_2(x)+f_3(x)>0,
\]
contrary to \(f_1+f_2+f_3=-1\). Therefore the three closed middle caps cover \(K\), including all points on their boundary lines, and hence
\[
k\le c_1+c_2+c_3.
\tag{1}
\]

The assumed failure \(c_1+v_1<t\), together with \(v_1\ge0\), gives the strict inequality
\[
c_1<t.
\tag{2}
\]
By the empty-full lower-crossing classification in accepted fact `624b129b68e0fdee`,
\[
c_2=c_3=t-m.
\tag{3}
\]
Combining (1)-(3) yields
\[
k<t+2(t-m)=3t-2m.
\tag{4}
\]
Using \(t=2(M+m+k)/9\), inequality (4) becomes
\[
\begin{aligned}
k
&<\frac{2(M+m+k)}3-2m\\
&=\frac{2M-4m+2k}{3}.
\end{aligned}
\]
Multiplying by \(3\) and subtracting \(2k\) gives
\[
k<2M-4m.
\tag{5}
\]

The residual strip in accepted fact `15bb2c1680a0254e` gives the strict lower bound
\[
k>M-m.
\tag{6}
\]
Equations (5) and (6) imply
\[
M-m<2M-4m.
\]
Thus
\[
3m<M,
\]
and division by \(M>0\) proves
\[
\frac mM<\frac13.
\]
Every inequality used to obtain the final threshold is strict exactly where required: strictness enters through the failed support-contact inequality in (2) and through the residual lower bound in (6).
