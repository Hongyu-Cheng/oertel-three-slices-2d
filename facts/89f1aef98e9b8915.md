## Statement

Assume the surviving transformed triangular \(N=3\) system, incidence orbit, and canonical notation of accepted fact `82b7c8c40e88aaf1`. Thus the middle vertex matrix is \(L=(\ell_{ij})_{i,j=1}^3\), \(J_i=\{j:\ell_{ij}>0\}\), all nine entries \(\ell_{ij}\) are nonzero, and the three strict counterexample inequalities
\[
A u_i+c_i+B v_i<t\qquad(i=1,2,3)
\]
hold. Then, up to only the allowed interchange of rows \(1,2\) and the allowed interchange of columns \(2,3\),
\[
J_1=\{2\},\qquad J_2=\{2,3\},\qquad J_3=\{1\}.
\]
No conclusion is asserted for any feasible stratum on which at least one entry \(\ell_{ij}\) is zero.

## Proof

Let \(s\) interchange rows \(1,2\), and let \(\tau=(2\ 3)\) interchange columns \(2,3\). On an ordered pair of first-two-row supports these allowed interchanges act by
\[
s(X,Y)=(Y,X),\qquad \tau(X,Y)=(\tau X,\tau Y).
\]
Accepted fact `82b7c8c40e88aaf1` states that \(J_3=\{1\}\) and that, modulo exactly the group \(\{1,s,\tau,s\tau\}\), every strict counterexample with all nine entries nonzero has one of the three representatives
\[
(\{2\},\{2,3\}),\qquad
(\{1,2\},\{2,3\}),\qquad
(\{2,3\},\{2,3\}).
\]
For completeness, their exact support orbits are
\[
\begin{aligned}
\mathcal O_{\mathrm{cont}}={}&\{(\{2\},\{2,3\}),(\{2,3\},\{2\}),
 (\{3\},\{2,3\}),(\{2,3\},\{3\})\},\\
\mathcal O_{\mathrm{cross}}={}&\{(\{1,2\},\{2,3\}),(\{2,3\},\{1,2\}),
 (\{1,3\},\{2,3\}),(\{2,3\},\{1,3\})\},\\
\mathcal O_{\mathrm{equal}}={}&\{(\{2,3\},\{2,3\})\}.
\end{aligned}
\]
These are distinct: in \(\mathcal O_{\mathrm{cont}}\) a singleton support is contained in a two-element support, in \(\mathcal O_{\mathrm{cross}}\) the two supports are unequal two-element sets, and in \(\mathcal O_{\mathrm{equal}}\) they are equal. The interchange \(\tau\) fixes column \(1\), and \(s\) fixes row \(3\), so every displayed member retains \(J_3=\{1\}\).

The representative \((J_1,J_2)=(\{1,2\},\{2,3\})\) of \(\mathcal O_{\mathrm{cross}}\) matches exactly the open chamber excluded by accepted fact `37f97f30533ded8d`. Every other member of \(\mathcal O_{\mathrm{cross}}\) is carried to that representative by one of the same allowed lossless interchanges \(s,\tau,s\tau\). Hence no strict counterexample lies in \(\mathcal O_{\mathrm{cross}}\).

The orbit \(\mathcal O_{\mathrm{equal}}\) is a singleton, and its support pattern \(J_1=J_2=\{2,3\}\), \(J_3=\{1\}\) matches exactly the open chamber excluded by accepted fact `20ed70c898723fda`. Hence no strict counterexample lies in \(\mathcal O_{\mathrm{equal}}\).

The exhaustive three-orbit list from accepted fact `82b7c8c40e88aaf1` therefore leaves only \(\mathcal O_{\mathrm{cont}}\). Its stated representative is
\[
J_1=\{2\},\qquad J_2=\{2,3\},\qquad J_3=\{1\},
\]
as required. This is only a composition of the three accepted facts and does not exclude that contained-support chamber.

Finally, the argument assumed \(\ell_{ij}\ne0\) for every \((i,j)\). Accepted fact `82b7c8c40e88aaf1` explicitly makes no conclusion when any middle entry is zero, and the two chamber exclusions just used are open-chamber statements with all entries nonzero. Therefore, for every nonempty \(Z\subseteq\{1,2,3\}\times\{1,2,3\}\), every feasible boundary stratum satisfying \(\ell_{ij}=0\) for all \((i,j)\in Z\), including strata with additional zero entries, remains unresolved. In particular, this composition does not prove the full \(N=3\) theorem.
