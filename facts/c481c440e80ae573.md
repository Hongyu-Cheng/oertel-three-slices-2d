## Proof

First we prove exact preservation of all equality functions.  The moving
ear remains in the \(R_{\{1,2\}}\) chamber on the interval proved below.
Its signed area is
\[
\frac12[k_1(t)-k_0,k_2-k_0]
=\frac12[k_1-k_0,\alpha v]
 +\frac t2[v,\alpha v]
=z.
\tag{13}
\]
Thus the \(R_{\{1,2\}}\)-cell area is constant.  No other middle-cell area
depends on \(k_1\).  All \(P\)- and \(Q\)-data are fixed, so their chamber
areas and the two persistent incidences \(p_1(a_1)=p_1(a_3)=0\) are
constant.  The three crossing equalities are fixed linear combinations of
these individual chamber areas.  Finally, \(B\), its support code, and all
structural capacity identities depend only on \(A_0,A_2\), which are
stationary.  Hence every canonical equality function, including the zero
capacity function, is exactly constant along the path.

We next calculate the turns.  From (1),
\[
k_2-k_1(t)
=\left(\frac{\alpha}{2}-t\right)v-\beta\mathsf R(v).
\tag{14}
\]
Consequently
\[
T_2(t)
=\left(\frac{\alpha}{2}-t\right)[v,w]
 -\beta[\mathsf R(v),w].
\tag{15}
\]
Equations (3)--(5) show that this affine function is positive for
\(0\leq t<t_*\) and vanishes at \(t_*\).  The other two moving turns satisfy
\[
T_1(t)=2z>0
\tag{16}
\]
and
\[

=T_0(0)
+t[k_0-k_{13},v].
\tag{17}
\]
Accepted facts `ac12ad62775a1d5f` and `eb58997cea9b3136` give
\(T_0(0)>1/1000\) and
\[

=\frac{16208345651254381711}{100000000000000000}>0.

Every other turn is fixed and positive.  This proves weak convexity through
\(t_*\), with only \(T_2\) zero at the endpoint among the turn functions.

At the event, (15) makes \(k_2-k_1(t_*)\) parallel to \(w\).  Taking its
determinant with \(v\) and using
