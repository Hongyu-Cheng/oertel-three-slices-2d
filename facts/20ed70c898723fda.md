## Statement

Normalize \(\Delta=\operatorname{conv}\{e_1,e_2,e_3\}\) to have area one and write
\[
\mathcal C(z;\tau)
=\left|\{\lambda\in\Delta:z\cdot\lambda\ge\tau\}\right|.
\]
Let \(U,V,L\in\mathbb R^{3\times3}\) and \(\sigma=(\sigma_1,\sigma_2,\sigma_3)^\top\in\mathbb R^3\) satisfy
\[
\mathbf1^\top U=\mathbf1^\top V=\mathbf1^\top L=\mathbf1^\top,
\qquad \det U\det V\det L\ne0,
\tag{S1}
\]
and
\[
\min_jU_{kj}+\min_jV_{kj}\ge0\qquad(k=1,2,3).
\tag{S2}
\]
These are the exact matrix and compatibility conditions of accepted fact `64760c76cadff481`, after the surviving invertible normal form of accepted fact `09393c6edf1fc4e2`.  Put
\[
A=|\det U|,\qquad B=|\det V|,\qquad
t=\frac{2(1+A+B)}9,
\]
and
\[
u_i=\mathcal C((LU)_{i*};-\sigma_i),\qquad
c_i=\mathcal C(L_{i*};0),\qquad
v_i=\mathcal C((LV)_{i*};\sigma_i).
\]
Let
\[
P_0=LU\Delta+\sigma,\qquad P_1=L\Delta,\qquad
P_2=LV\Delta-\sigma,
\]
let \(g_j\) be the area centroid of \(P_j\), and set
\[
I_j=\{i:(g_j)_i\ge0\}\qquad(j=0,1).
\]
Assume the incidence orbit and numerical branch of accepted fact `f8e871d10dac1791`, namely
\[
I_0=\{1,2\},\qquad I_1=\{3\},\qquad
0<A<B,\qquad B<1+A,\qquad t\le\frac12,
\]
so, in particular, that predecessor's exact cross-cap conclusion holds:
\[
0<c_i<\kappa:=\frac{2(1+B-A)}9<\frac49
\qquad(i=1,2).
\tag{S3}
\]
and assume, as in the open chamber retained by accepted fact `82b7c8c40e88aaf1`, that all entries of \(L\) are nonzero and
\[
J_1=J_2=\{2,3\},\qquad J_3=\{1\}.
\]
Then the three inequalities
\[
Au_i+c_i+Bv_i<t\qquad(i=1,2,3)
\]
cannot hold simultaneously.  Hence no compatible \((U,V,L,\sigma)\) completion exists in this entire open chamber.

## Proof

The column-sum identity in accepted fact `64760c76cadff481` is
\[
\sum_{i=1}^3L_{ij}=1\qquad(j=1,2,3).
\tag{1}
\]
The asserted open signs therefore let us write the first two rows, without assuming that they are proportional or imposing any order on their two positive entries, as
\[
L_{1*}=(-d,x,y),\qquad L_{2*}=(-e,z,w),
\tag{2}
\]
where
\[
d,e,x,y,z,w>0.
\tag{3}
\]
Equation (1) then fixes the third row exactly:
\[
L_{3*}=(1+d+e,\ 1-x-z,\ 1-y-w).
\tag{4}
\]
Because \(J_3=\{1\}\), the numbers
\[
p:=1+d+e,\qquad a:=x+z-1,\qquad b:=y+w-1
\tag{5}
\]
satisfy \(p,a,b>0\), and (4) is \((p,-a,-b)\).

Accepted fact `f8e871d10dac1791` gives, for the first two rows in this incidence orbit,
\[
0<c_i<\kappa:=\frac{2(1+B-A)}9<\frac49
\qquad(i=1,2).
\tag{6}
\]
The exact cap formulas of accepted fact `64760c76cadff481` apply symmetrically to the entries in (2), including equality between the two positive values, and give
\[
c_1=1-\frac{d^2}{(d+x)(d+y)},\qquad
c_2=1-\frac{e^2}{(e+z)(e+w)}.
\tag{7}
\]
From \(c_1<4/9\) and (7),
\[
(d+x)(d+y)<\frac95d^2,
\]
so
\[
d(x+y)+xy<\frac45d^2.
\]
Since \(xy>0\), this implies
\[
x+y<\frac45d.
\tag{8}
\]
The second formula in (7) gives identically
\[
z+w<\frac45e.
\tag{9}
\]

Set
\[
s:=\frac{a+b}{p}.
\]
Equations (3) and (5) give \(s>0\), while (8)-(9) give
\[
a+b=x+y+z+w-2
<\frac45(d+e)-2
<\frac45(1+d+e)=\frac45p.
\]
Consequently
\[
0<s<\frac45.
\tag{10}
\]
The exact one-positive cap formula of accepted fact `64760c76cadff481`, again symmetric in the two negative entries and valid when they are equal, gives
\[
c_3=\frac{p^2}{(p+a)(p+b)}.
\tag{11}
\]
By \(ab\le(a+b)^2/4\) and (10),
\[
\frac{(p+a)(p+b)}{p^2}
=1+s+\frac{ab}{p^2}
\le 1+s+\frac{s^2}{4}
<1+\frac45+\frac4{25}
=\frac{49}{25}.
\tag{12}
\]
Equations (11)-(12) yield the strict chamber-wide lower bound
\[
c_3>\frac{25}{49}>\frac12.
\tag{13}
\]

On the other hand, every cap fraction supplied by accepted fact `64760c76cadff481` is nonnegative, including an empty cap, a full cap, a repeated projected value, or a threshold at a support endpoint.  Thus the third purported strict row gives
\[
c_3\le Au_3+c_3+Bv_3<t\le\frac12,
\tag{14}
\]
contradicting (13).

The contradiction used no endpoint ordering, no sign of \(\det U\), \(\det V\), or \(\det L\), no strengthening of midpoint compatibility, and no relation among the independent \(\sigma_i\).  Endpoint degeneracies of the cap formulas and strict-boundary case \(t=1/2\) are already included in (14).  Therefore it excludes every full compatible endpoint completion and independent-shift choice in the stated open chamber, rather than only a middle-only relaxation.
