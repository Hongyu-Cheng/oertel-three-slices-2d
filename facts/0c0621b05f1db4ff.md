## Statement

Work in
\[
\Pi=\{(y_1,y_2,y_3):y_1+y_2+y_3=1\},
\qquad y_3=1-y_1-y_2,
\]
and measure area in the \((y_1,y_2)\)-coordinates.  Put
\[
f_i=-200y_i+\frac{199}{3},
\qquad L_i=-\frac{199}{3},
\qquad
p=\frac{199}{300},
\qquad
t=\frac{199}{600}.
\tag{1}
\]
Then \(f_1+f_2+f_3=-1\), \(\sum_iL_i=-199<-1\), and
\[
f_i\le L_i\Longleftrightarrow y_i\ge p,\qquad
f_i\le-L_i\Longleftrightarrow y_i\ge0,\qquad
f_i\le0\Longleftrightarrow y_i\ge t.
\tag{2}
\]
For every subset of \(\{1,2,3\}\), use the exact convention that an
index belongs to the cell when the corresponding weak inequality in
(2) holds, while every absent index satisfies the strict reverse
inequality.  Thus, for example, a middle cell \(G\) is
\[
\mathcal C_G=\{y:y_i\ge t\ (i\in G),\ y_i<t\ (i\notin G)\},
\]
and the endpoint cells use the same convention with cuts \(p\) and
\(0\).  All cutting lines have area zero.
