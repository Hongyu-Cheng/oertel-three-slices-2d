## Statement

Under the locked three-slice hypotheses, put
\[
M=\max\{|A_0|,|A_2|\},\qquad
m=\min\{|A_0|,|A_2|\}.
\]
If
\[
\frac mM\geq\frac12,
\]
then
\[
\max_{y\in S}h_S(y)\geq\frac{2T}{9}.
\]

## Proof

Write
\[
r=\frac mM,\qquad s=\frac{|A_1|}{M}.
\]
The locked hypotheses imply
\[
\frac{A_0+A_2}{2}\subseteq A_1:
\]
indeed, the midpoint of any point of \(\{0\}\times A_0\) and any point of
\(\{2\}\times A_2\) lies in the convex polytope \(C\), has height \(1\),
and therefore belongs to \(\{1\}\times A_1\).

Assume for contradiction that the locked conclusion fails and \(r\geq1/2\).
Accepted fact `6f36d5017fd780b5` says that every counterexample belongs to
exactly one of the two parameter regions
\[
0<r\leq\frac15
\]
or
\[
\frac15<r<r_0,\qquad S_c(r)<s<U(r),
\tag{1}
\]
where
\[
r_0=\frac{163-12\sqrt{51}}{125},
\qquad
S_c(r)=
\left(\frac{2\sqrt5+\sqrt{14r-2}}6\right)^2.
\tag{2}
\]
In particular, the first region is incompatible with \(r\geq1/2\).
The same accepted fact proves
\[
\frac12<r_0<\rho<1.
\tag{3}
\]
Thus the endpoint \(r=r_0\) and the whole range \(r>r_0\) are already
excluded: neither region above contains them.  It remains only to exclude
\[
\frac12\leq r<r_0.
\tag{4}
\]

We prove on (4) the exact comparison
\[
S_c(r)\geq1+\frac r2.
\tag{5}
\]
First audit the radicals and the left endpoint.  For \(r\geq1/2\),
\[
14r-2\geq5>0,\qquad 70r-10\geq25>0.
\]
At \(r=1/2\),
\[
S_c(1/2)
=\left(\frac{2\sqrt5+\sqrt5}{6}\right)^2
=\frac54
=1+\frac{1/2}{2}.
\tag{6}
\]
Expanding (2), using only positive radicands, gives
\[
S_c(r)
=\frac12+\frac{7r}{18}+\frac{\sqrt{70r-10}}9.
\]
Consequently (5) is equivalent to
\[
\sqrt{70r-10}\geq r+\frac92.
\tag{7}
\]
On (4), the left side of (7) is at least \(5\), and the right side is at
least \(5\).  Both sides are therefore nonnegative before squaring.
Squaring is reversible, and
\[
\begin{aligned}
70r-10-\left(r+\frac92\right)^2
&=-r^2+61r-\frac{121}{4}\\
&=-\left(r-\frac12\right)
       \left(r-\frac{121}{2}\right).
\end{aligned}
\tag{8}
\]
By (3)-(4),
\[
r-\frac12\geq0,\qquad
r-\frac{121}{2}<0.
\]
Thus the right side of (8) is nonnegative, proving (7) and hence (5).
It vanishes at \(r=1/2\) by (6), and is positive for
\(1/2<r<r_0\).

Now (1) and (5) give
\[
s>S_c(r)\geq1+\frac r2.
\]
After undoing the normalization, this is
\[
|A_1|>M+\frac m2.
\]
Accepted fact `776f5d2aa96794fd`, which applies to arbitrary compact
two-dimensional convex bodies satisfying the midpoint containment, then
yields
\[
\max_{y\in S}h_S(y)\geq\frac{2T}{9},
\]
contrary to the assumed counterexample.  Hence every counterexample has
\(m/M<1/2\), as claimed.
