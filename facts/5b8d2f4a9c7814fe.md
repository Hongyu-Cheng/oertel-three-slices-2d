## Statement

Put
\[
f_1(x,y)=x,\qquad f_2(x,y)=y,\qquad
f_3(x,y)=-1-x-y,
\qquad L_1=L_2=L_3=-100.
\tag{1}
\]
Use the rational affine coordinates
\[
S=\frac{x+y+200}{299},\qquad
T=\frac{x-y}{299}.
\tag{2}
\]
For every rational
\[
\frac65\leq\lambda<360,
\tag{3}
\]
define two quadrilaterals by their rectangles in the \((S,T)\)-plane:
\[
A_0=
\left\{(S,T):
\frac1{50}\leq S\leq\frac{49}{50},\
|T|\leq\frac1{100}\right\},
\tag{4}
\]
\[
A_2(\lambda)=
\left\{(S,T):
\frac{99}{299}-\frac1{1000}
\leq S\leq
\frac{99}{299}-\frac1{1000}+\lambda,\
|T|\leq\frac1{100000}\right\}.
\tag{5}
\]
Let
\[
B(\lambda)=\frac{A_0+A_2(\lambda)}2.
\tag{6}
\]
There is no compact two-dimensional convex body
\(K\supseteq B(\lambda)\) which simultaneously:

1. satisfies the three crossing equalities at the levels in (1), and
2. can be the middle slice of a counterexample to the locked problem.

This conclusion already holds after imposing only the scalar completion
conditions of accepted fact `6f6869df3d151764`, the prospective-\(K\)
three-cap filter of accepted fact `a7747c32a219e2c9`, and the strict
slice-balance consequence of accepted fact `2f9be517307232c7`.

The whole family has
\[
\frac{|A_2(\lambda)|}{|A_0|}
=\frac{\lambda}{960}<\frac38,
\tag{7}
\]
positive opposite cells \(Q_{12}\) and \(Q_3\), and genuine lower
orientation in all three rows.  Moreover, the separate nonconcurrent
three-cap inequalities on \(A_0\) and \(A_2(\lambda)\) both hold
strictly; the obstruction is not an endpoint-gate failure.

## Proof

The Jacobian of (2) is
\[
\left|\frac{\partial(x,y)}{\partial(S,T)}\right|
=\frac{299^2}{2}.
\]
It is convenient to divide every physical area by \(299^2\).  Thus a
rectangle
\([a,b]\times[-w,w]\) in \((S,T)\)-coordinates has scaled area
\((b-a)w\).  All crossing, scalar, three-cap, and slice-balance
inequalities are homogeneous in area, so this common scaling changes
none of them.  All areas below are scaled in this way.

The six relevant fan halfplanes become
\[
\begin{array}{c|ccc}
&1&2&3\\ \hline
A_0\text{ cap at }L_i
&S+T\leq0&S-T\leq0&S\geq1\\
A_2\text{ cap at }-L_i
&S+T\leq\frac{400}{299}
&S-T\leq\frac{400}{299}
&S\geq\frac{99}{299}\\
\text{middle zero cap}
&S+T\leq\frac{200}{299}
&S-T\leq\frac{200}{299}
&S\geq\frac{199}{299}.
\end{array}
\tag{8}
\]
Introduce the abbreviations
\[
\epsilon=\frac1{1000},\qquad
w=\frac1{100000},\qquad
\alpha=
\frac{400}{299}
-\left(\frac{99}{299}-\epsilon\right)
=\frac{301299}{299000}.
\tag{9}
\]
The endpoint and core areas are
\[
M=\frac6{625},\qquad
m=\frac{\lambda}{100000},
\tag{10}
\]
\[
k_0=|B(\lambda)|
=\frac{1001}{400000}\lambda
+\frac{3003}{1250000}.
\tag{11}
\]
Equation (7) follows immediately from (10).

We next verify the opposite cells and lower orientation directly.
Write
\[
q_i=f_i+L_i=f_i-100
\]
and, up to null boundaries, define
\[
Q_J=A_2(\lambda)\cap
\{q_i\leq0:i\in J\}\cap
\{q_i>0:i\notin J\}.
\tag{12}
\]
The strip
\[
\frac{99}{299}-\epsilon<S<\frac{99}{299},
\qquad |T|<w
\]
lies in \(Q_{12}\), so
\[
|Q_{12}|\geq\epsilon w=\frac1{100000000}>0.
\tag{13}
\]
The rectangle
\[
\frac{400}{299}+w<S<
\frac{99}{299}-\epsilon+\lambda,
\qquad |T|<w
\]
lies in \(Q_3\).  At the smallest allowed \(\lambda=6/5\), its scaled
area is already
\[
\left(
\frac65-\frac{301}{299}
-\frac1{1000}-\frac1{100000}
\right)w
=\frac{5749801}{2990000000000}>0.
\tag{14}
\]

The three \(A_0\) caps in the first row of (8) vanish with strict support
clearance:
\[
\min_{A_0}(S+T)
=\min_{A_0}(S-T)=\frac1{100}>0,
\qquad
\max_{A_0}S=\frac{49}{50}<1.
\tag{15}
\]
Each of the three \(A_2\) cut lines in the second row of (8) crosses the
interior of (5).  Indeed,
\[
\frac{99}{299}-\epsilon+w
<\frac{400}{299}
<\frac{99}{299}-\epsilon+\lambda-w
\tag{16}
\]
and
\[
\frac{99}{299}-\epsilon
<\frac{99}{299}
<\frac{99}{299}-\epsilon+\lambda.
\tag{17}
\]
Consequently, as an endpoint level moves immediately to the right of
\(-100\), its \(A_0\) cap stays empty while its \(A_2\) cap shrinks with
positive chord density.  Hence every row has the genuine lower
orientation.

We now apply the nonconcurrent three-cap inequality separately to both
endpoints.  The three \(A_0\) cap masses are
\[
u_1=u_2=u_3=0.
\tag{18}
\]
The functions \(p_i=f_i-L_i=f_i+100\) sum to \(299\).  Applying accepted
fact `5b75ecd9ec86dbcf` to \(-p_i/299\) uses the complementary cap
masses \(M-u_i=M\).  Its exact residual is
\[
5(3M)-6M-4M=5M=\frac6{125}>0.
\tag{19}
\]

The three \(A_2\) cap masses obtained by exact clipping of (5) are
\[
v_1=v_2=w\alpha
=\frac{301299}{29900000000},
\qquad
v_3=w(\lambda-\epsilon).
\tag{20}
\]
For (3), \(v_3>v_1\).  The functions \(q_i\) sum to \(-301\), so
accepted fact `5b75ecd9ec86dbcf` applies to \(q_i/301\) with cap masses
\(v_i\).  Its exact residual is
\[
\begin{aligned}
5(v_1+v_2+v_3)-6\min_i v_i-4m
&=4v_1+5v_3-4m\\
&=w(\lambda+4\alpha-5\epsilon)>0.
\end{aligned}
\tag{21}
\]
Thus both endpoint gates pass strictly throughout the family.

Suppose now that a convex completion \(K\supseteq B(\lambda)\) exists,
put
\[
A=|K\setminus B(\lambda)|,
\]
and assume the three crossings.  Since (18) holds and \(v_3\) is the
largest endpoint total, accepted fact `a7747c32a219e2c9`, which is the
nonconcurrent three-cap inequality applied to the prospective \(K\),
gives
\[
A\leq
A_K:=
M+m-k_0-5v_1+\frac12v_3.
\tag{22}
\]
On the other hand, an actual counterexample cannot have
\(M\geq(M+m+|K|)/2\), by accepted fact `2f9be517307232c7`.  Therefore
\[
|K|>M-m,
\qquad
A>M-m-k_0.
\tag{23}
\]
Subtracting the lower endpoint in (23) from (22) gives
\[
A_K-(M-m-k_0)
=w\left(
\frac52\lambda-5\alpha-\frac{\epsilon}{2}
\right).
\tag{24}
\]
Define the exact critical length
\[
\lambda_c
=2\alpha+\frac{\epsilon}{5}
=\frac{3013289}{1495000}.
\tag{25}
\]
For \(6/5\leq\lambda\leq\lambda_c\), (24) is nonpositive.
Equations (22)-(23) are then incompatible, including at equality
because (23) is strict.

It remains to exclude \(\lambda>\lambda_c\).  The midpoint rectangle has
\[
B_L=\frac{104681}{598000},\qquad
B_H=\frac12\left(
\frac{49}{50}+\frac{99}{299}-\epsilon+\lambda
\right),
\qquad
B_W=\frac{1001}{200000}.
\tag{26}
\]
The three zero cut lines in the last row of (8) cross the indicated
interior portions of this rectangle for every \(\lambda\geq6/5\).
Exact rectangle clipping therefore gives
\[
b_1=b_2
=B_W\left(\frac{200}{299}-B_L\right)
=\frac{22739563}{9200000000},
\tag{27}
\]
\[
b_3
=B_W\left(B_H-\frac{199}{299}\right)
=\frac{1001}{400000}\lambda
-\frac{21021}{400000000}.
\tag{28}
\]
Also
\[
h_0=\frac{2(M+m+k_0)}9
=\frac{67}{120000}\lambda+\frac{1667}{625000}.
\tag{29}
\]
Thus the base defects of accepted fact `6f6869df3d151764` are
\[
F_1=F_2
=-\frac{67}{120000}\lambda
-\frac{4435521}{23920000000},
\tag{30}
\]
\[
F_3
=\frac{469}{240000}\lambda
-\frac{217581}{80000000}.
\tag{31}
\]
At \(\lambda=\lambda_c\),
\[
F_3=\frac{109345439}{89700000000}>0,
\tag{32}
\]
\[
F_3-F_1
=\frac{302565099}{119600000000}>0.
\tag{33}
\]
Both quantities have positive derivative in \(\lambda\).  Hence for
\(\lambda>\lambda_c\), \(F_3>0\) and \(F_3=\max_iF_i\).

The scalar lower and upper bounds from accepted fact
`6f6869df3d151764` would then require
\[
\frac92F_3\leq A\leq-3(2F_1+F_3).
\tag{34}
\]
But
\[
5F_3+4F_1
=\frac{603}{80000}\lambda
-\frac{343025679}{23920000000},
\tag{35}
\]
and at \(\lambda=\lambda_c\) the right side is
\[
\frac{12735609}{14950000000}>0.
\tag{36}
\]
Its derivative is \(603/80000>0\).  Therefore, for every
\(\lambda>\lambda_c\),
\[
\frac92F_3
-\bigl[-3(2F_1+F_3)\bigr]
=\frac32(5F_3+4F_1)>0,
\tag{37}
\]
contradicting (34).

The two cases \(\lambda\leq\lambda_c\) and
\(\lambda>\lambda_c\) exhaust (3).  SageMath 10.8 independently
constructed (4)-(6) as `Polyhedron` objects over \(\mathbb Q\), clipped
them by every halfplane in (8), and returned exactly (10)-(11),
(18)-(21), and (27)-(36).  No floating-point comparison enters the
certificate.
