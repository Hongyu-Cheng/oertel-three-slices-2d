## Statement

Use the normalized witnesses supplied by accepted fact `fdfc3e7459f0e194`, writing their original shifts as \(\sigma_i\):
\[
H_i(\sigma_i)=\{(h,w):f_i(w)+\sigma_i(h-1)\leq 0\},
\qquad
\mu(H_i(\sigma_i))<t,
\]
\[
f_1+f_2+f_3=-1,\qquad \sum_i\sigma_i<-1.
\]
For
\[
c_i=\left|A_1\cap\{f_i\leq0\}\right|,
\qquad
\mu_i(a)=c_i+\left|A_0\cap\{f_i\leq a\}\right|
                 +\left|A_2\cap\{f_i\leq-a\}\right|,
\]
let
\[
I_i=\{a:\mu_i(a)<t\},
\qquad L_i=\inf I_i,
\]
whenever accepted fact `624b129b68e0fdee` is applied to give the genuine lower crossing \(L_i\).  Then
\[
L_i<\sigma_i\quad(i=1,2,3),
\qquad
0<\sum_i(\sigma_i-L_i)<-1-\sum_iL_i.
\tag{1}
\]
Moreover, the retained original gap
\[
B=A_0\cap\bigcap_{i=1}^3\{f_i>\sigma_i\}
\tag{2}
\]
satisfies
\[
|B|>\frac{T}{3},
\qquad
B\subseteq A_0\cap\bigcap_{i=1}^3\{f_i>L_i\}.
\tag{3}
\]

More generally, suppose near a crossing \(L_i\) that
\[
A_0=[r_0^-,r_0^+]\times[-h_0/2,h_0/2],
\qquad
A_2=[r_2^-,r_2^+]\times[-h_2/2,h_2/2],
\]
and
\[
f_i-L_i=\lambda_i r+\nu_i\omega+\rho_{0i},
\qquad
f_i+L_i=\lambda_i r+\nu_i\omega+\rho_{2i},
\qquad \lambda_i\neq0.
\]
If, for \(|\eta|\leq\eta_0\), both cutting lines in
\[
A_0\cap\{f_i-L_i\leq\eta\},
\qquad
A_2\cap\{f_i+L_i\leq-\eta\}
\]
cross the full transverse sides of their rectangles, then
\[
\mu_i(L_i+\eta)-\mu_i(L_i)
=\frac{h_0-h_2}{|\lambda_i|}\eta.
\tag{4}
\]
Consequently, if \(h_0>h_2\) and \(\mu_i(L_i)=t\), then \(L_i\) is not a lower crossing: points immediately to its left, rather than its right, belong to \(I_i\).

For the exact rational realization of accepted fact `fbf19a78233b5ef1`, (4) holds for all three indices whenever \(|\eta|\leq10^{-5}\), and
\[
\mu_k(L_k+\eta)=\mu_\ell(L_\ell+\eta)
=t+\frac{99}{10000}\eta,
\qquad
\mu_j(L_j+\eta)=t+\frac{99}{20000}\eta.
\tag{5}
\]
Thus none of its displayed \(L_i\) is the lower crossing required above.  Nevertheless the shifts
\[
\widehat\sigma_i=L_i-10^{-5}
\tag{6}
\]
give three proper strict-low-mass halfspaces whose interiors cover the whole middle plane and the whole height-\(2\) plane.  They do not extend to a strict-low-mass cover of all three slices.  If \(y_0\) is the center of the \(A_0\)-rectangle in that realization, then
\[
h_S(y_0)\geq\frac{M}{2}
=t+\frac{5220227}{49500000}>t.
\tag{7}
\]

## Proof

The original halfspace \(H_i(\sigma_i)\) has mass \(\mu_i(\sigma_i)\), so \(\sigma_i\in I_i\).  Accepted fact `624b129b68e0fdee` gives \(\mu_i(L_i)=t\).  Hence \(\sigma_i\neq L_i\), and the definition \(L_i=\inf I_i\) gives \(L_i<\sigma_i\).  Summing these strict inequalities and using \(\sum_i\sigma_i<-1\), which is supplied by accepted fact `fdfc3e7459f0e194`, proves (1).

At height \(1\), the three witness interiors cover the whole plane because \(\sum_i f_i=-1<0\).  At height \(2\), their defining functions have sum
\[
\sum_i(f_i+\sigma_i)=-1+\sum_i\sigma_i<0,
\]
so their interiors also cover the whole plane.  Therefore, on \(S\), the complement of \(\bigcup_iH_i(\sigma_i)\) is exactly \(\{0\}\times B\).  Subadditivity and the three strict mass bounds give
\[
T-|B|
=\mathcal H_2\left(S\cap\bigcup_iH_i(\sigma_i)\right)
\leq\sum_i\mu(H_i(\sigma_i))
<3t=\frac{2T}{3}.
\]
This proves \(|B|>T/3\).  Since \(L_i<\sigma_i\), each inequality \(f_i>\sigma_i\) implies \(f_i>L_i\), proving the inclusion in (3).

For the rectangular calculation, increasing the threshold in
\(\lambda_i r+\nu_i\omega+\rho_{0i}\leq\eta\) by \(\eta\) translates its cutting graph in the \(r\)-direction by \(\eta/\lambda_i\).  As long as the graph crosses the full transverse sides, the \(A_0\)-cap area therefore changes by \(h_0\eta/|\lambda_i|\).  In the \(A_2\)-cap the threshold is \(-\eta\), so its area changes by \(-h_2\eta/|\lambda_i|\).  The middle cap is fixed.  Adding the changes proves (4).  If \(h_0>h_2\), (4) is negative for \(\eta<0\) and positive for \(\eta>0\).  Thus a crossing with value \(t\) has strict-low-mass points immediately to its left and cannot equal \(\inf I_i\).

We now apply this calculation to accepted fact `fbf19a78233b5ef1`.  Its transverse widths are
\[
h_0=\frac1{100},\qquad h_2=\frac1{10000},
\]
and, with \(p_i=f_i-L_i\) and \(q_i=f_i+L_i\), its common \(r\)-coefficients are
\[
\lambda_k=\lambda_\ell=-1,\qquad \lambda_j=2.
\]
For \(|\eta|\leq10^{-5}\), the six cutting graphs remain strictly between the relevant \(r\)-endpoints.  Indeed, on \(A_0\) they are
\[
r=\alpha_k-\eta,\qquad
r=100+\omega-\eta,\qquad
r=\frac{\omega+\eta}{2},
\]
inside
\[
\left[-\frac1{200},\,100+\frac{31}{495}+\frac1{1000}\right]
\times\left[-\frac1{200},\frac1{200}\right],
\]
and on \(A_2\) they are
\[
r=\eta,\qquad
r=\frac{26}{99}+\omega+\eta,\qquad
r=\beta_j+\frac{\omega}{2}-\frac{\eta}{2},
\]
inside
\[
\left[-\frac{61}{10},\,\beta_j+\frac12\right]
\times\left[-\frac1{20000},\frac1{20000}\right].
\]
Equation (4), together with the three crossing equalities from `fbf19a78233b5ef1`, now gives (5).  In particular,
\[
\mu_k(L_k-10^{-5})=\mu_\ell(L_\ell-10^{-5})
=t-\frac{99}{1000000000},
\]
\[
\mu_j(L_j-10^{-5})
=t-\frac{99}{2000000000}.
\tag{8}
\]
This both proves that the displayed \(L_i\) are upper crossings and verifies the strict masses asserted for (6).  Since \(\sum_iL_i<-1\), the shifts in (6) still have sum less than \(-1\).  The same sum arguments used above show that the corresponding interiors cover the middle and height-\(2\) planes.  Hence the rational realization retains an original strict witness triple only on the side opposite to the genuine-lower-crossing orientation.

It remains to decide whether further strict-low-mass halfspaces could cover the omitted part of \(A_0\).  Let \(y_0=(0,\bar w)\), where \(\bar w\) is the center of the \(A_0\)-rectangle.  For any closed halfspace \(H\subset\mathbb R^3\) containing \(y_0\), its height-\(0\) section is either the whole plane or a planar closed halfplane containing \(\bar w\).  Every planar halfplane containing the center of a centrally symmetric planar body contains at least half its area: move its boundary parallel toward the center, and then use central reflection to pair the two halfplanes through the center.  Therefore
\[
\mathcal H_2(S\cap H)\geq\frac{|A_0|}{2}=\frac M2.
\]
The exact values in `fbf19a78233b5ef1` are
\[
M=\frac{4953397}{4950000},
\qquad
t=\frac{98721}{250000},
\]
and direct subtraction gives
\[
\frac M2-t
=\frac{4953397}{9900000}-\frac{98721}{250000}
=\frac{5220227}{49500000}>0.
\]
Taking the infimum over all \(H\ni y_0\) proves (7).  Thus no strict-low-mass halfspace contains \(y_0\), so no family of such halfspaces can cover all three slices and this rational realization is not a counterexample to the locked theorem.
