## Statement

Let \(A_0,A_1,A_2\subset\mathbb R^2\) be the two-dimensional slice bodies in the locked three-slice problem, and write \(a_0=|A_0|\) and \(a_2=|A_2|\).  For \(j\in\{0,2\}\) and \(u\in\mathbb S^1\), put
\[
m_j(u)=\min_{x\in A_j}\langle u,x\rangle,\qquad
w_j(u)=\max_{x\in A_j}\langle u,x\rangle-m_j(u),
\]
and
\[
\gamma_j^u(r)=
\left|A_j\cap\{x:\langle u,x\rangle\le m_j(u)+r\}\right|
\qquad(r\in\mathbb R).
\]
Fix \(z\in A_1\).  Let \(u_1,u_2,u_3\in\mathbb S^1\) be pairwise nonparallel, and let \(\alpha_1,\alpha_2,\alpha_3>0\) satisfy
\(\sum_{i=1}^3\alpha_i u_i=0\).  Define
\[
v_i=\alpha_i u_i,\qquad K=-(A_0+A_2),\qquad
m=\min\{a_0,a_2\},
\]
\[
d_i=2\langle u_i,z\rangle-m_0(u_i)-m_2(u_i),\qquad
D_i=\frac{w_0(u_i)^2}{a_0}+\frac{w_2(u_i)^2}{a_2},
\]
and
\[
E_i=\inf_{r\in\mathbb R}
\bigl(\gamma_0^{u_i}(r)+\gamma_2^{u_i}(d_i-r)\bigr).
\]
Then either \(E_i\ge m\) for some \(i\), or
\[
\sum_{i=1}^3\alpha_i\sqrt{D_iE_i}
\ge
\sqrt{2|\det(v_1,v_2)|\,|K|}.
\]
In the second case,
\[
\sum_{i=1}^3E_i
\ge
\frac{2|\det(v_1,v_2)|\,|K|}
{\sum_{i=1}^3\alpha_i^2D_i}.
\]

## Proof

Write \(p=-2z\) and \(b_i=\alpha_i d_i\).  Accepted fact `508b5d8f1a3d6fca` gives
\[
d_i=h_K(u_i)-\langle u_i,p\rangle.
\]
Positive homogeneity of the support function therefore gives
\[
b_i=h_K(v_i)-\langle v_i,p\rangle.
\tag{1}
\]
Also \(v_1+v_2+v_3=0\).  For every \(x\in K\), (1) implies
\(\langle v_i,x-p\rangle\le b_i\).  Under the affine coordinate map
\[
\Phi(x)=\bigl(\langle v_1,x-p\rangle,\langle v_2,x-p\rangle\bigr),
\]
whose area Jacobian is
\(\Delta:=|\det(v_1,v_2)|>0\), the set \(K\) is consequently mapped into
\[
R=\{(s_1,s_2):s_1\le b_1,\ s_2\le b_2,
s_1+s_2\ge-b_3\}.
\tag{2}
\]
Put \(S=b_1+b_2+b_3\).  The substitution
\(q_1=b_1-s_1,\ q_2=b_2-s_2\) identifies \(R\) with
\[
\{(q_1,q_2):q_1,q_2\ge0,\ q_1+q_2\le S\}.
\]
Because \(K\) has positive area and \(\Delta>0\), the containment
\(\Phi(K)\subseteq R\) forces \(S>0\).  Thus \(R\) is a right triangle of
area \(S^2/2\), and (2) yields
\[
\Delta|K|=|\Phi(K)|\le\frac{S^2}{2}.
\]
It follows that
\[
\sum_{i=1}^3\alpha_i(d_i)_+
=\sum_{i=1}^3(b_i)_+
\ge\sum_{i=1}^3b_i=S
\ge\sqrt{2\Delta|K|}.
\tag{3}
\]
This proves the needed support-area estimate while allowing arbitrary
signs of the individual deficits.

Suppose now that the first alternative fails, so \(E_i<m\) for every
\(i\).  Since the endpoint bodies are two-dimensional,
\(w_j(u_i)>0\), hence \(D_i>0\).  If \(d_i\le0\), then
\[
\sqrt{D_iE_i}\ge0=(d_i)_+.
\tag{4}
\]
If \(d_i>0\), the width-sensitive cap estimate (9) proved in accepted
fact `7aecfa43fb452e02` gives
\[
E_i\ge\min\left\{m,\frac{d_i^2}{D_i}\right\}.
\tag{5}
\]
Were \(d_i^2/D_i\ge m\), (5) would give \(E_i\ge m\), contrary to the
current branch.  Therefore \(d_i^2/D_i<m\), so (5) has the correct
active term and gives
\[
\sqrt{D_iE_i}\ge d_i=(d_i)_+.
\tag{6}
\]
Multiplying (4) or (6) by \(\alpha_i\), summing, and using (3) proves
\[
\sum_{i=1}^3\alpha_i\sqrt{D_iE_i}
\ge\sqrt{2\Delta|K|}.
\]
This establishes the dichotomy.

Finally, Cauchy--Schwarz in the upper-bound direction gives
\[
\left(\sum_{i=1}^3\alpha_i\sqrt{D_iE_i}\right)^2
\le
\left(\sum_{i=1}^3\alpha_i^2D_i\right)
\left(\sum_{i=1}^3E_i\right).
\]
The first factor on the right is positive.  Combining this inequality
with the lower bound just proved and dividing by that factor yields
\[
\sum_{i=1}^3E_i
\ge
\frac{2\Delta|K|}{\sum_{i=1}^3\alpha_i^2D_i},
\]
as claimed.
