## Statement

Assume the complete Alternative 1 hypotheses of accepted facts `bc026780453747b8` and `92a1b537d7e7876c`, and all notation, branch conditions, and scalar inequalities of accepted fact `8b69ff3587679629`.  Thus
\[
p=\frac{97}{100},\qquad
\delta=\frac3{100},\qquad
k=\frac{65+29\sqrt{65}}{400},
\]
\[
A=a+1,\qquad B=b+1,\qquad
0\le X\le A\le W,\qquad 0\le Y\le B\le H,
\]
and the applicable cap, defect, anchor, support-polygon, zero-level, chord-order, and maximum-chord inequalities of that accepted fact all hold.

First, for arbitrary real \(\alpha,\beta\), define
\[
\ell_1(u,v)=u-A-\alpha v,\qquad
\ell_2(u,v)=v-B-\beta u.
\]
For a counterclockwise cyclic vertex list \(V=(v_0,\ldots,v_{m-1})\) and an affine function \(\ell\), define \(\operatorname{Clip}_{\ell}(V)\) edge by edge.  For the directed edge \(p\to q\), concatenate the following list:
\[
\begin{array}{c|c}
(\ell(p),\ell(q))&\text{output}\\ \hline
\ell(p)\le0,\ \ell(q)\le0&(q)\\
\ell(p)\le0<\ell(q)&
\left(\dfrac{\ell(q)p-\ell(p)q}{\ell(q)-\ell(p)}\right)\\[2mm]
\ell(p)>0\ge\ell(q)&
\left(\dfrac{\ell(p)q-\ell(q)p}{\ell(p)-\ell(q)},q\right)\\[2mm]
\ell(p)>0,\ \ell(q)>0&()
\end{array}                                                    \tag{27}
\]
and delete consecutive repeated vertices.  Every denominator used in (27) is strictly positive in the branch in which it occurs.

Put
\[
V^{(0)}=((0,0),(W,0),(W,H),(0,H)),\qquad
V^{(1)}=\operatorname{Clip}_{\ell_1}(V^{(0)}),\qquad
V^{(2)}=\operatorname{Clip}_{\ell_2}(V^{(1)}).             \tag{28}
\]
If \(V^{(2)}=(z_0,\ldots,z_{m-1})\), cyclically, then the exact envelope formula is
\[
|\mathcal E|=
\begin{cases}
\displaystyle\frac12\sum_{i=0}^{m-1}\det(z_i,z_{i+1}),
&m\ge3,\quad z_m=z_0,\\[2mm]
0,&m<3.
\end{cases}                                                \tag{29}
\]
Equations (27)-(29) are a finite piecewise rational formula: the first clipping has at most five vertices and the second has at most six.  They cover every sign of \(\alpha,\beta\), all box-edge and box-corner incidences, redundant inequalities, collinearities, and all ties.

The possible non-box intersection coordinates generated by (27) are
\[
(A,0),\quad (A+\alpha H,H),\quad
\left(0,-\frac A\alpha\right),\quad
\left(W,\frac{W-A}{\alpha}\right),                         \tag{30}
\]
for the first line, with the last two used only after the sign of
\(\alpha\ne0\) and segment incidence have been fixed, and
\[
(0,B),\quad (W,B+\beta W),\quad
\left(-\frac B\beta,0\right),\quad
\left(\frac{H-B}{\beta},H\right),                          \tag{31}
\]
for the second line, with the last two treated analogously.  If the two outer lines cross, their intersection is
\[
\left(
\frac{A+\alpha B}{1-\alpha\beta},
\frac{B+\beta A}{1-\alpha\beta}
\right),                                                   \tag{32}
\]
and (32) is used only in a crossing branch, where the sign of
\(1-\alpha\beta\) has already been fixed.  If
\(\alpha\beta=1\), no division by \(1-\alpha\beta\) occurs.  In that
case
\[
\ell_2=-\beta\ell_1-(B+\beta A).                           \tag{33}
\]
For \(\beta<0\), the two halfplanes in (33) are coincident when
\(B+\beta A=0\), and otherwise one is redundant or is the stricter
parallel halfplane.  For \(\beta>0\), they cut out the parallel strip
\[
-A-\frac B\beta\le\ell_1\le0.
\]
Formula (29) applies unchanged in all of these parallel branches.

Adjoin to the complete scalar system of accepted fact
`8b69ff3587679629` the necessary inequality
\[
\frac pD+E\le|\mathcal E|.                                \tag{34}
\]
Then every scalar tuple in every one of its cap, defect, outer-slope,
support-polygon, zero-level, and chord-order branches satisfies
\[
\frac W{M_x}+\frac H{M_y}
<
\frac{5000}{291}.                                        \tag{35}
\]
In particular, the weak bound requested in the subgoal holds.

## Proof

Formula (27) is the exact intersection of a directed segment with one
closed affine halfplane.  In an inside-to-outside crossing its
intersection parameter has denominator
\(\ell(q)-\ell(p)>0\), while in an outside-to-inside crossing the
displayed denominator is \(\ell(p)-\ell(q)>0\).  Thus no division is
performed before its denominator sign is fixed.  Sequential clipping
of a convex counterclockwise polygon preserves its boundary order, so
(28) is exactly the intersection of the box and the two outer
halfplanes.  Formula (29) is its signed-triangle, equivalently
triangle/trapezoid-subtraction, area formula.  A boundary endpoint is
always treated as inside.  Consequently an edge or corner tie merely
creates a repeated or collinear vertex with zero signed contribution.
If all vertices satisfy an inequality, that clipping is unchanged,
which covers redundancy.  Direct solution of the relevant edge-line
and line-line equations gives (30)-(33).  This proves the claimed
boundary-complete formula, including \(\alpha=0\), \(\beta=0\), and
\(\alpha\beta=1\).

We now eliminate the augmented scalar system.  The case \(a=b=0\) is
immediate: equations (18) and (20) of accepted fact
`8b69ff3587679629` give \(M_x=W\) and \(M_y=H\), so the aspect sum is
\(2\).  Exactly one zero offset is excluded by the same accepted
fact.  Hence assume \(a,b>0\).

Set
\[
\xi=\frac1\sigma,\qquad
\eta=\frac1\rho,\qquad
d=\frac D\delta=\frac{100D}{3}.                           \tag{36}
\]
The inner-support conditions give
\[
\xi\ge\frac ab,\qquad
\eta\ge\frac ba,\qquad
\xi\eta\ge1.                                             \tag{37}
\]
Define the exact support-defect lower bound
\[
\Psi=
\begin{cases}
\displaystyle
\frac{a^2\eta+b^2\xi-2ab}{2(\xi\eta-1)},&\xi\eta>1,\\[2mm]
\displaystyle\frac{ab}{2},&\xi\eta=1.
\end{cases}                                               \tag{38}
\]
Equations (8)-(10) of accepted fact `8b69ff3587679629` say
\(E\ge\Psi\).  Its anchor, derived there from accepted facts
`92a1b537d7e7876c` and `bc026780453747b8`, therefore gives
\[
z:=D\Psi\le DE\le k.                                     \tag{39}
\]

The cap inequalities imply, without a sign assumption,
\[
\alpha\le d-\xi,\qquad \beta\le d-\eta.                  \tag{40}
\]
Indeed, if \(q_x=\alpha+\xi>0\), then
\(D\ge\delta q_x\); if \(q_x\le0\), then
\(\alpha\le-\xi<d-\xi\).  The proof for \(\beta\) is identical.

If \(Y>0\), the point \(R=(W,Y)\in K\) gives
\[
\alpha_K\ge\frac{W-A}{Y}.
\]
Together with \(\alpha\ge\alpha_K\), (40), and \(Y\le B\), this gives
\[
W\le A+B\max\{d-\xi,0\}.
\]
If \(Y=0\), the zero-level implication in accepted fact
`8b69ff3587679629` gives \(W=A\), so the same bound holds.  Coordinate
exchange gives
\[
H\le B+A\max\{d-\eta,0\}.                                \tag{41}
\]

Increasing either outer coefficient enlarges its halfplane on the
nonnegative quadrant.  Equations (40)-(41) therefore put
\(\mathcal E\) inside
\[
\begin{aligned}
\overline{\mathcal E}
={}&[0,A+B\max\{d-\xi,0\}]
\times[0,B+A\max\{d-\eta,0\}]\\
&{}\cap
\{(u,v):u-A\le(d-\xi)v,\quad
v-B\le(d-\eta)u\}.                                      \tag{42}
\end{aligned}
\]
Normalize \(u=AU\), \(v=BV\), and put
\[
r=(d-\xi)\frac BA,\qquad
s=(d-\eta)\frac AB.                                     \tag{43}
\]
Then
\[
|\overline{\mathcal E}|=AB\,F(r,s),                     \tag{44}
\]
where the complete exact branch formula is
\[
F(r,s)=
\begin{cases}
\displaystyle 1+\frac{r+s}{2}+rs,
&r\ge0,\ s\ge0,\\[2mm]
\displaystyle\frac{2+r+s}{2(1-rs)},
&
\begin{array}{l}
r\ge0,\ -1\le s\le0,\ \text{or}\\
s\ge0,\ -1\le r\le0,\ \text{or}\\
-1\le r\le0,\ -1\le s\le0,\\
(r,s)\ne(-1,-1),
\end{array}\\[5mm]
\displaystyle-\frac1{2s},
&s\le-1,\ r\ge-1,\\[2mm]
\displaystyle-\frac1{2r},
&r\le-1,\ s\ge-1,\\[2mm]
\displaystyle\frac{2rs+r+s}{2rs(rs-1)},
&r\le-1,\ s\le-1,\ (r,s)\ne(-1,-1),\\[2mm]
\displaystyle\frac12,&r=s=-1.
\end{cases}                                               \tag{45}
\]
On overlaps along branch boundaries the displayed values agree.
For \(r,s\ge0\), (45) is the area of the enlarged box after
subtracting the two disjoint corner triangles of areas \(r/2\) and
\(s/2\).  The second branch is the signed trapezoid with the two-line
intersection
\[
\left(\frac{1+r}{1-rs},\frac{1+s}{1-rs}\right).
\]
In the third and fourth branches one descending outer line cuts out
the contained triangle.  In the fifth branch the two descending
triangles meet in a four-vertex polygon.  The last branch is the
parallel/coincident line \(U+V=1\).  Thus (45) also follows directly
from (27)-(29), with no omitted edge or equality case.

We need one exact inequality for (45).  Put \(t=A/B>0\) and
\[
L=\xi+\eta+2-2d=2-rt-\frac{s}{t}.                        \tag{46}
\]
If \(L>0\), then every branch of (45) satisfies
\[
ABF(r,s)\le\frac{(A+B)^2}{2L}.                           \tag{47}
\]
Here are division-free certificates for all branches.  After dividing
the difference in (47) by \(AB\), it is enough to prove
\[
t+\frac1t+2-2F(r,s)
\left(2-rt-\frac{s}{t}\right)\ge0.                       \tag{48}
\]

For \(r,s\ge0\), set
\[
c=2+r+s+2rs.
\]
Multiplication of (48) by \(t\) gives the quadratic
\[
(1+cr)t^2+2(1-c)t+(1+cs),
\]
whose discriminant is
\[
-4rs(r+s+2rs)c\le0.
\]
For the second branch, the left side of (48) is exactly
\[
\frac{(1+s-t-rt)^2}{(1-rs)t}\ge0.                        \tag{49}
\]
For \(s\le-1,\ r\ge-1\), it is
\[
\frac{2(1+s)+t(s-r)}{s}\ge0,                             \tag{50}
\]
because both terms in the numerator are nonpositive and \(s<0\).
The coordinate-swapped branch is identical; explicitly its value is
\[
\frac{r-s+2t(1+r)}{rt}\ge0.                              \tag{51}
\]
For \(r=-R\le-1\), \(s=-S'\le-1\), multiplication by the positive
denominator \(RS'(RS'-1)t\) leaves
\[
\begin{aligned}
&S'^2(R-1)^2\\
&\quad+2t\bigl(R+S'-3RS'+R^2S'^2\bigr)
+R^2t^2(S'-1)^2.                                        \tag{52}
\end{aligned}
\]
Writing \(R=1+u\), \(S'=1+v\), the middle parenthesis in
(52) is
\[
u^2+uv+2u^2v+v^2+2uv^2+u^2v^2\ge0.
\]
Finally, \(r=s=-1\) makes (48) zero.  This proves (47) on all open
branches and all their boundaries.

The support defect supplies the matching lower denominator.  If
\(\xi\eta>1\), let
\[
G=\begin{pmatrix}\xi&1\\1&\eta\end{pmatrix}.
\]
This matrix is positive definite and
\[
2\Psi=(a,b)G^{-1}(a,b)^{\mathsf T}.
\]
Cauchy-Schwarz in the \(G\)-inner product gives
\[
(a+b)^2
\le 2\Psi(\xi+\eta+2).                                  \tag{53}
\]
If \(\xi\eta=1\), (37) forces
\(\xi=a/b\), \(\eta=b/a\), and (53) is equality because
\(\Psi=ab/2\).  Thus (53) is valid in every support branch.

Put \(S=a+b\).  If \(L\le0\), (53) gives
\[
S^2\le4d\Psi=\frac{4z}{\delta}\le\frac{4k}{\delta}.       \tag{54}
\]
The same conclusion holds whenever \(S^2\le4d\Psi\), regardless of
the sign of \(L\).  Otherwise \(L>0\) and (53) gives
\[
L\ge\frac{S^2-4d\Psi}{2\Psi}>0.
\]
Combining this with (42), (44), and (47) yields
\[
|\mathcal E|
\le ABF(r,s)
\le
\frac{\Psi(S+2)^2}{S^2-4d\Psi}.                          \tag{55}
\]

The augmented envelope inequality (34) and \(E\ge\Psi\) give
\[
\frac pD+\Psi\le|\mathcal E|.
\]
Multiply this and (55) by \(D=\delta d\), and use
\(z=D\Psi\):
\[
p+z
\le
\frac{z(S+2)^2}{S^2-4z/\delta}.
\]
After multiplying by the positive denominator and cancelling
\(zS^2\), this is
\[
pS^2
\le
4z\left(S+1+\frac{p+z}{\delta}\right)
\le
4k\left(S+1+\frac{p+k}{\delta}\right),                   \tag{56}
\]
where the last step uses \(0<z\le k\).

Let
\[
S_0=\frac{4418}{291}
=\frac{5000}{291}-2
\]
and
\[
P(S)=pS^2-4k\left(S+1+\frac{p+k}{\delta}\right).
\]
Exact substitution gives
\[
P(S_0)
=
\frac{24{,}962{,}933-3{,}005{,}067\sqrt{65}}
     {174{,}600}
>0,                                                       \tag{57}
\]
because
\[
24{,}962{,}933^2
-65\cdot3{,}005{,}067^2
=36{,}170{,}225{,}120{,}704>0.
\]
Also
\[
P'(S_0)=\frac{8{,}641-87\sqrt{65}}{300}>0,               \tag{58}
\]
because
\[
8{,}641^2-65\cdot87^2=74{,}174{,}896>0.
\]
Since \(P'\) is increasing, (57)-(58) show that \(P(S)>0\)
for every \(S\ge S_0\).  Inequality (56), which says
\(P(S)\le0\), therefore implies \(S<S_0\).

The alternative estimate (54) gives the same conclusion, because
\[
S_0^2-\frac{4k}{\delta}
=
\frac{17{,}683{,}969-818{,}583\sqrt{65}}{84{,}681}
>0,                                                       \tag{59}
\]
and
\[
17{,}683{,}969^2
-65\cdot818{,}583^2
=269{,}167{,}681{,}280{,}176>0.
\]
Thus every augmented scalar branch has
\[
a+b+2<S_0+2=\frac{5000}{291}.                            \tag{60}
\]

It remains only to pass from offsets to the requested chord aspect.
If \(b\le Y\), equation (18) of accepted fact
`8b69ff3587679629` gives \(W/M_x=1\le A\).  If \(Y<b\), equation
(19) gives
\[
M_x\ge G_x
=W-\frac{a(b-Y)}b
\ge W-a,
\]
and hence, using \(W\ge A=a+1\),
\[
\frac W{M_x}\le\frac W{W-a}\le a+1=A.                   \tag{61}
\]
The coordinate-swapped chord formulas give
\[
\frac H{M_y}\le b+1=B.                                  \tag{62}
\]
Equations (60)-(62) prove (35).  All inherited cap, defect, anchor,
support-polygon, chord-order, redundancy, and tie branches were kept:
the proof used necessary consequences common to all of them and did
not assert actual-body realizability.
