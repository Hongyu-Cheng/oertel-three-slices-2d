## Statement

Let \(P,Q,K\subset\mathbb R^2\) be compact two-dimensional convex polygons,
write
\[
|P|=M\geq m=|Q|>0,\qquad |K|=k,\qquad
\frac{P+Q}{2}\subseteq K,
\]
and put
\[
t=\frac{2(M+m+k)}9.
\]
Define
\[
c_i=|K\cap\{x\geq0\}|,\qquad
c_\ell=|K\cap\{y\geq0\}|,\qquad
c_q=|K\cap\{x+y\leq1\}|,
\]
\[
p_x=|P\cap\{x\geq0\}|+|Q\cap\{x\geq0\}|,
\qquad
p_y=|P\cap\{y\geq0\}|+|Q\cap\{y\geq0\}|.
\]
If \(p_x+p_y<m\), then
\[
M+m-p_x-p_y\leq4c_q.
\tag{1}
\]
Consequently, assume the strict \(q\)-tail
\[
c_q+m<t.
\tag{2}
\]
If
\[
2M-4m\leq k\leq8m-M,
\tag{3}
\]
then, with
\[
\mathcal E_K=c_i+c_\ell+c_q-k,\qquad
D_0=3t-k-m,
\]
one has
\[
\mathcal E_K\geq D_0-p_x-p_y.
\tag{4}
\]

The following exact rational \(K\) shows that the requested tapered
common-middle gates themselves are feasible at the fixed scalar data.  Set
\[
M=1,\qquad m=\frac13,\qquad k=\frac{17}{25},\qquad
t=\frac{302}{675},\qquad p_x=p_y=\frac1{10},
\tag{5}
\]
and use
\[
u=x-y,\qquad v=x+y.
\]
Let \(K\) be the triangle
\[
K=\left\{(u,v):
0\leq\tau:=v+3\leq12,\quad
-1703+201\tau\leq u
\leq-1703+\left(201+\frac{17}{900}\right)\tau
\right\}.
\tag{6}
\]
Its vertices are
\[
(-1703,-3),\qquad (709,9),\qquad
\left(\frac{53192}{75},9\right).
\tag{7}
\]
For the cuts
\[
H_i=\{u+v\geq0\},\qquad
H_\ell=\{v-u\geq0\},\qquad
H_q=\{v\leq1\},
\]
let \(K_J\) be the cell which lies in exactly the halfplanes indexed by
the nonempty set \(J\subseteq\{i,\ell,q\}\), up to null cutting-line
boundaries.  The complete seven-cell table is
\[
\begin{array}{c|c}
J&|K_J|\\ \hline
\{i\}&\dfrac{3049953}{9000850}\\[3pt]
\{\ell\}&\dfrac{2158744847}{8263582650}\\[3pt]
\{q\}&0\\[3pt]
\{i,\ell\}&\dfrac{14402578262}{3305745239789}\\[3pt]
\{i,q\}&0\\[3pt]
\{\ell,q\}&\dfrac{17}{225}\\[3pt]
\{i,\ell,q\}&0
\end{array}
\tag{8}
\]
and these seven entries sum to \(17/25\).  Moreover
\[
\inf_{a\in\mathbb R}
\left(\max_K(u-av)-\min_K(u-av)\right)=\frac{17}{75},
\qquad
-\min_Kv=3.
\tag{9}
\]
Thus (6) passes the requested width-depth prefilter.  It also satisfies
all three strict tail budgets and the strict overlap-surplus violation at
the common-middle level.  However, no polygons \(P,Q\) with the areas and
profile values in (5) can satisfy \(P+Q\subseteq2K\).  More generally, by
(4), no endpoint pair at the fixed areas in (5), with any \(p_x,p_y\),
can violate the registered overlap-surplus conclusion.

## Proof

We first prove (1) without using a width estimate.  Put
\[
P_-=P\cap\{x+y\leq0\},\qquad
Q_-=Q\cap\{x+y\leq0\}.
\]
The cutting lines have planar area zero.  Since
\[
\{x+y\geq0\}\subseteq\{x\geq0\}\cup\{y\geq0\},
\]
the union bound, summed over \(P\) and \(Q\), gives
\[
|P_-|+|Q_-|\geq M+m-p_x-p_y.
\tag{10}
\]
The hypothesis \(p_x+p_y<m\leq M\) also shows that both \(P_-\) and
\(Q_-\) have positive area.

We use the following elementary planar Minkowski-sum inequality:
\[
|A+B|\geq|A|+|B|
\tag{11}
\]
for nonempty compact planar sets \(A,B\).  To verify it directly, fix a
nonzero linear functional \(e\), choose \(a^+\in A\) maximizing \(e\)
and \(b^-\in B\) minimizing \(e\), and consider the two translates
\[
A+b^-\subseteq A+B,\qquad a^++B\subseteq A+B.
\]
The first lies in
\[
\{z:e(z)\leq e(a^++b^-)\},
\]
and the second lies in the opposite closed halfplane.  Their intersection
is contained in the separating line, which has area zero.  Their union
therefore has area \(|A|+|B|\), proving (11).

Apply (11) to \(P_-,Q_-\).  Every sum of one point from each set has
\(x+y\leq0\), so
\[
P_-+Q_-
\subseteq (P+Q)\cap\{x+y\leq0\}
\subseteq 2K\cap\{x+y\leq0\}.
\]
Dilation by \(2\) preserves the sign of \(x+y\), hence
\[
2K\cap\{x+y\leq0\}
=2\bigl(K\cap\{x+y\leq0\}\bigr).
\]
Combining this identity with (10) and (11) yields
\[
M+m-p_x-p_y
\leq |P_-|+|Q_-|
\leq |P_-+Q_-|
\leq4|K\cap\{x+y\leq0\}|
\leq4c_q,
\]
which is (1).

We next prove (4).  The three halfplanes
\[
\{x\geq0\},\qquad\{y\geq0\},\qquad\{x+y\leq1\}
\]
cover the plane: a point in all three open complements would have
\(x<0\), \(y<0\), and \(x+y>1\), which is impossible.  Integrating the
three indicator functions over \(K\) gives
\[
\mathcal E_K=c_i+c_\ell+c_q-k\geq0.
\tag{12}
\]
Suppose, contrary to (4), that
\[
\mathcal E_K<D_0-p_x-p_y.
\tag{13}
\]
By (12), (13) forces
\[
p_x+p_y<D_0.
\tag{14}
\]
Since
\[
D_0=3t-k-m=\frac{2M-m-k}{3},
\]
the left inequality in (3) is exactly \(D_0\leq m\).  Thus (14) lets
us apply (1).  Using (2), we obtain the strict chain
\[
M+m-D_0
<M+m-p_x-p_y
\leq4c_q
<4(t-m).
\tag{15}
\]
On the other hand,
\[
\begin{aligned}
M+m-D_0-4(t-m)
&=M+6m+k-7t\\
&=\frac{5(8m-M-k)}9
\geq0
\end{aligned}
\tag{16}
\]
by the right inequality in (3).  Equations (15) and (16) contradict
each other, proving (4).

We now audit the fixed rational calibration.  Here
\[
\frac mM=\frac13<\frac9{25},\qquad
\frac23=1-\frac13<\frac{17}{25}<1+\frac13=\frac43.
\]
Also
\[
\frac13<\frac{27-2\sqrt{26}}{25},
\]
because this is equivalent to \(6\sqrt{26}<56\), and
\(936<3136\).  Hence (5) lies in the residual strip of accepted fact
`15bb2c1680a0254e`.  It also satisfies
\[
2M-4m=\frac23<\frac{17}{25}<\frac53=8m-M,
\tag{17}
\]
so the conclusion (4) already applies to every compatible endpoint
realization at these fixed areas.

For completeness, we verify every common-middle gate of (6) exactly.
Write
\[
\beta=\frac{17}{900}
\]
and parameterize the triangle by
\[
u=-1703+201\tau+\beta\tau r,\qquad
v=-3+\tau,\qquad
0\leq\tau\leq12,\quad0\leq r\leq1.
\tag{18}
\]
The \(uv\)-Jacobian is \(\beta\tau\), while
\(dx\,dy=\frac12\,du\,dv\).  Therefore
\[
|K|=\frac{\beta}{2}\int_0^{12}\tau\,d\tau
=36\beta=\frac{17}{25}.
\tag{19}
\]
For fixed \(r\), the three cuts in (18) are
\[
H_q\Longleftrightarrow \tau\leq4,
\tag{20}
\]
\[
H_i\Longleftrightarrow
\tau\geq\tau_i(r):=\frac{1706}{202+\beta r},
\tag{21}
\]
\[
H_\ell\Longleftrightarrow
\tau\leq\tau_\ell(r):=\frac{1700}{200+\beta r}.
\tag{22}
\]
For every \(0\leq r\leq1\),
\[
4<\tau_i(r)<\tau_\ell(r)<12.
\tag{23}
\]
The middle comparison follows after positive cross multiplication from
\[
1700(202+\beta r)-1706(200+\beta r)
=2200-6\beta r>0,
\]
and the two outside comparisons are immediate from the adverse
endpoints.

The two exact squared-threshold integrals are
\[
\int_0^1\tau_i(r)^2\,dr
=\frac{1706^2}{202(202+\beta)}
=\frac{1309696200}{18363517},
\tag{24}
\]
\[
\int_0^1\tau_\ell(r)^2\,dr
=\frac{1700^2}{200(200+\beta)}
=\frac{13005000}{180017}.
\tag{25}
\]
Since the \(xy\)-area element is
\(\frac12\beta\tau\,d\tau\,dr\), integrating the four successive
\(\tau\)-intervals in (23) gives
\[
|K_{\{\ell,q\}}|=\frac{\beta}{4}\,4^2,
\]
\[
|K_{\{\ell\}}|
=\frac{\beta}{4}
\left(\int_0^1\tau_i(r)^2\,dr-4^2\right),
\]
\[
|K_{\{i,\ell\}}|
=\frac{\beta}{4}
\left(\int_0^1\tau_\ell(r)^2\,dr
-\int_0^1\tau_i(r)^2\,dr\right),
\]
\[
|K_{\{i\}}|
=\frac{\beta}{4}
\left(12^2-\int_0^1\tau_\ell(r)^2\,dr\right).
\]
These formulas give exactly the four positive entries in (8); (23)
gives the three zero entries.  In particular,
\[
c_i=\frac{315125753}{918175850},\qquad
c_\ell=\frac{122825}{360034},\qquad
c_q=\frac{17}{225},
\tag{26}
\]
\[
\mathcal E_K
=\frac{59438249185363}{743792678952525}.
\tag{27}
\]
At the values (5),
\[
t-\frac1{10}=\frac{469}{1350},\qquad
t-m=\frac{77}{675},\qquad
D_0-\frac15=\frac{29}{225}.
\]
All four strict margins are
\[
\frac{469}{1350}-c_i
=\frac{52047071}{12395373975}>0,
\]
\[
\frac{469}{1350}-c_\ell
=\frac{760549}{121511475}>0,
\]
\[
\frac{77}{675}-c_q=\frac{26}{675}>0,
\]
\[
\frac{29}{225}-\mathcal E_K
=\frac{12142787589506}{247930892984175}>0.
\tag{28}
\]

Finally, the two top vertices in (7) have the same \(v\)-coordinate and
their \(u\)-coordinates differ by \(17/75\).  Hence every functional
\(u-av\) has width at least \(17/75\) on \(K\).  For \(a=201\),
\[
u-201v
\]
ranges exactly from \(-1100\) to \(-1100+17/75\), proving (9).  Thus
\[
(-\min_Kv)\inf_a\operatorname{width}_K(u-av)
=3\cdot\frac{17}{75}
=\frac{17}{25}
>\frac{17}{30}.
\tag{29}
\]
This triangle passes every requested common-middle and width-depth gate.
The first failed interface is instead forced by (1): if
\(p_x=p_y=1/10\), then
\[
M+m-p_x-p_y=\frac{17}{15},
\]
whereas the strict \(q\)-tail would give
\[
4c_q<\frac{308}{675}<\frac{17}{15}.
\]
Therefore no endpoint containment and cap realization exists, so the
exposed-face, section, support, and full profile-minimum tests are not
reached.
