## Statement

Let \(A_0,A_1,A_2\subseteq\mathbb R^2\) be nonempty compact two-dimensional convex bodies, put
\[
a_i=|A_i|,\qquad T=a_0+a_1+a_2,\qquad
M=\max\{a_0,a_2\},\qquad m=\min\{a_0,a_2\},
\]
let
\[
S=\bigcup_{i=0}^2(\{i\}\times A_i),
\]
and set
\[
r=\frac mM,\qquad s=\frac{a_1}{M},\qquad
t=\frac{2T}{9},\qquad
\delta=t-\frac{4a_1}{9}
=\frac{2M(1+r-s)}9.
\]
Assume the strict residual area strip of accepted fact `15bb2c1680a0254e`. Only its strict inequalities
\[
s>1-r,\qquad s<1+r
\tag{1}
\]
are needed below.

For \(i\in\{0,1,2\}\), \(q\in\mathbb S^1\), and \(u\in\mathbb R\), define
\[
F_{i,q}(u)=\left|A_i\cap\{w:q\cdot w\le u\}\right|,
\qquad
R_i(q)=\max_{w\in A_i}q\cdot w.
\tag{2}
\]
Choose \(j\in\{0,2\}\) with \(a_j=M\), put \(k=2-j\), so \(a_k=m\), and put
\(\sigma=1-j\in\{1,-1\}\). Consider a normalized row
\[
H=\{(h,w):q\cdot w+\lambda(h-1)\le c\}.
\tag{3}
\]
Its large, middle, and small slice thresholds are respectively
\[
c+\sigma\lambda,\qquad c,\qquad c-\sigma\lambda.
\tag{4}
\]
Suppose its closed middle cap contains \(z\in A_1\), and the planar depth of \(z\) satisfies
\[
d_{A_1}(z):=
\inf_{\substack{Q\text{ a closed planar halfplane}\\z\in Q}}
|A_1\cap Q|
\ge\frac{4a_1}{9}.
\tag{5}
\]
Then exactly one of the following two branches occurs:

\[
\begin{array}{ll}
\text{\rm full capture:}&
c-\sigma\lambda\ge R_k(q),\\[1mm]
&
\mathcal H_2(S\cap H)\ge \dfrac{4a_1}{9}+m>t;
\\[3mm]
\text{\rm reflected cap:}&
c-\sigma\lambda<R_k(q),\\[1mm]
&
\mathcal H_2(S\cap H)\ge
\dfrac{4a_1}{9}
+F_{j,q}\!\left(2c-R_k(q)\right).
\end{array}
\tag{6}
\]
Consequently,
\[
\mathcal H_2(S\cap H)\ge
\frac{4a_1}{9}
+\min\left\{m,F_{j,q}\!\left(2c-R_k(q)\right)\right\}.
\tag{7}
\]
In the labeling \(a_0=M\ge a_2=m\), formula (7) is exactly
\[
\mathcal H_2(S\cap H)\ge \frac{4a_1}{9}
+\min\!\left\{m,\,
F_{0,q}\!\left(2c-\max_{x\in A_2}q\cdot x\right)\right\}.
\tag{8}
\]

If the row is strict-low-mass, \(\mathcal H_2(S\cap H)<t\), then it necessarily satisfies
\[
F_{j,q}(c+\sigma\lambda)+F_{k,q}(c-\sigma\lambda)<\delta,
\tag{9}
\]
\[
c-\sigma\lambda<R_k(q),
\tag{10}
\]
and the precise shallow-reflection condition
\[
F_{j,q}\!\left(2c-R_k(q)\right)<\delta
=\frac{2M(1+r-s)}9.
\tag{11}
\]
Thus, if \(a_0\ge a_2\), (10)-(11) read
\[
c-\lambda<R_2(q),\qquad
F_{0,q}\!\left(2c-R_2(q)\right)
<\frac{2M(1+r-s)}9,
\tag{12}
\]
whereas if \(a_2\ge a_0\), they read
\[
c+\lambda<R_0(q),\qquad
F_{2,q}\!\left(2c-R_0(q)\right)
<\frac{2M(1+r-s)}9.
\tag{13}
\]
When \(a_0=a_2\), both labelings are valid, so both (12) and (13) hold for every strict-low-mass row satisfying (5).

Finally, in the normalized strict three-row certificate supplied by accepted fact `d2d117bcc58a1ba0` under a global counterexample, the open middle caps cover \(\mathbb R^2\). The planar centroid \(g_1\) of \(A_1\) has \(d_{A_1}(g_1)\ge4a_1/9\) by the planar Grünbaum inequality recorded in accepted fact `2f9be517307232c7`. Hence at least one strict-low-mass row contains \(g_1\) in its open middle cap and must satisfy (10)-(11).

## Proof

The middle section of (3) is
\[
A_1\cap P,\qquad P=\{w:q\cdot w\le c\}.
\]
Here \(P\) is a closed planar halfplane, and \(q\cdot z\le c\) says that it contains \(z\). Definition (5) therefore gives
\[
F_{1,q}(c)\ge d_{A_1}(z)\ge\frac{4a_1}{9}.
\tag{14}
\]
The exact row mass is
\[
\mathcal H_2(S\cap H)
=F_{1,q}(c)
+F_{j,q}(c+\sigma\lambda)
+F_{k,q}(c-\sigma\lambda).
\tag{15}
\]

The cap functions in (2) are nondecreasing. Compactness makes \(R_k(q)\) finite, and, because the caps are closed,
\[
F_{k,q}(u)=m\qquad\text{for every }u\ge R_k(q),
\tag{16}
\]
including \(u=R_k(q)\). This is the pointwise row version of the saturation split in accepted fact `996c3218c6534822`.

If \(c-\sigma\lambda\ge R_k(q)\), equations (14)-(16) give
\[
\mathcal H_2(S\cap H)\ge\frac{4a_1}{9}+m.
\tag{17}
\]
If \(c-\sigma\lambda<R_k(q)\), then
\[
2c-R_k(q)<c+\sigma\lambda.
\tag{18}
\]
Monotonicity, nonnegativity of the small-slice cap, and (14) give
\[
\begin{aligned}
\mathcal H_2(S\cap H)
&\ge \frac{4a_1}{9}
+F_{j,q}(c+\sigma\lambda)\\
&\ge \frac{4a_1}{9}
+F_{j,q}\!\left(2c-R_k(q)\right).
\end{aligned}
\tag{19}
\]
The alternatives in (6) are complementary and exhaustive. In particular, equality \(c-\sigma\lambda=R_k(q)\) belongs to the full-capture branch because both the halfspace and its slice cap are closed. Taking the weaker of (17) and (19) proves (7), and \(j=0,k=2,\sigma=1\) gives (8).

It remains to use the strict residual endpoints. The upper inequality in (1) gives
\[
\delta=\frac{2M(1+r-s)}9>0.
\tag{20}
\]
The lower inequality in (1) gives
\[
m-\delta
=\frac{M(7r+2s-2)}9
>\frac{5Mr}{9}>0.
\tag{21}
\]
Since \(t=4a_1/9+\delta\), equation (21) is equivalent to
\[
\frac{4a_1}{9}+m>t.
\tag{22}
\]
This proves the strict inequality in the full-capture line of (6).

Now suppose \(\mathcal H_2(S\cap H)<t\). Subtracting (14) from (15) yields the strict outer-mass bound
\[
F_{j,q}(c+\sigma\lambda)+F_{k,q}(c-\sigma\lambda)
<t-\frac{4a_1}{9}=\delta,
\]
which is (9). Full capture is impossible by (22), so the strict alternative (10) holds. Equation (18) and cap monotonicity then imply
\[
F_{j,q}\!\left(2c-R_k(q)\right)
\le F_{j,q}(c+\sigma\lambda)
<\delta,
\]
proving (11). Substituting \(j=0\) or \(j=2\) proves (12) or (13). If the outer areas are equal, the proof can be run with either choice of \(j\), proving both exchanged conclusions.

For the final certificate consequence, accepted fact `d2d117bcc58a1ba0` gives finite normalized rows of the form (3), each of mass below \(t\), whose open middle caps cover the middle plane. Accepted fact `2f9be517307232c7` records the planar Grünbaum bound \(d_{A_1}(g_1)\ge4a_1/9\) for the centroid \(g_1\). The open cover places \(g_1\) in at least one row's middle cap. That row satisfies (5), so the already proved strict-low-mass conclusion applies.
