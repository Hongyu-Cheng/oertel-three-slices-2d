## Statement

Let \(N=\{1,2,3\}\). Let \(f_1,f_2,f_3\) be nonconstant affine functions on \(\mathbb R^2\) with pairwise nonparallel linear parts and
\[
f_1+f_2+f_3=-1.
\]
Let \(L_1+L_2+L_3<-1\), and let \(A_0,A_2,K\) be nonempty two-dimensional convex polytopes satisfying
\[
\frac{A_0+A_2}{2}\subseteq K.
\]
Use the exact weak/strict cell convention of accepted fact
0e51a47a9add571f:
\[
P_J=A_0\cap
\{f_j\leq L_j\ (j\in J),\ f_j>L_j\ (j\notin J)\},
\]
\[
Q_H=A_2\cap
\{f_j\leq-L_j\ (j\in H),\ f_j>-L_j\ (j\notin H)\},
\]
\[
R_G=K\cap
\{f_j\leq0\ (j\in G),\ f_j>0\ (j\notin G)\}.
\]
Write
\[
M=|A_0|,\qquad m=|A_2|,\qquad k=|K|,
\qquad p_\varnothing=|P_\varnothing|,
\qquad r_G=|R_G|.
\]

Assume
\[
p_\varnothing>0,\qquad
r_1r_2r_3r_{123}>0,
\]
and that the positive-area \(Q\)-support is exactly
\[
\{123,12,13,23\}.
\]
Put
\[
q=|Q_{123}|>0,\qquad
x_i=|Q_{N\setminus\{i\}}|>0,
\qquad
m=q+x_1+x_2+x_3.
\]
Assume that index \(1\) is the dominant index furnished in the canonical
actual-counterexample branch by accepted fact 2f637c4da2df28f1. Explicitly,
\[
x_1>\frac{5m}{9},
\tag{1}
\]
and, with the exact convention above,
\[
C_1:=Q_{23}
=A_2\cap\{f_1>-L_1\},
\qquad |C_1|=x_1,
\tag{2}
\]
\[
C_1^{\rm co}:=
A_2\cap\{f_1\leq-L_1\},
\qquad |C_1^{\rm co}|=m-x_1.
\tag{3}
\]
Define
\[
h=\frac{2(M+m+k)}9,\qquad
u_1=\sum_{J\ni1}|P_J|,\qquad
v_1=\sum_{H\ni1}|Q_H|,\qquad
c_1=\sum_{G\ni1}r_G,

and assume the canonical crossing
\[
u_1+v_1+c_1=h.
\tag{3a}
\]
This crossing and the explicit geometric conclusions (1)--(3) are all that
is required below from the branch-specific hypotheses of
2f637c4da2df28f1.

Set
\[
\phi=f_1+L_1,\qquad
\omega_1=\max_{A_2}\phi-\min_{A_2}\phi.
\tag{4}
\]
Let \(g_{C_1}\) be the centroid of the compact closure
\[
\overline C_1=A_2\cap\{\phi\geq0\},
\]
and put
\[
\eta_1=\phi(g_{C_1}).
\tag{5}
\]
Then
\[
\boxed{\quad
\eta_1\geq
\frac{9x_1-5m}{12x_1}\,\omega_1>0.
\quad}
\tag{6}
\]

Define the deep centroid subcap
\[
C_1^\sharp
=\overline C_1\cap\{\phi\geq\eta_1\}.
\tag{7}
\]
Then \(C_1^\sharp\) is a two-dimensional compact convex body and
\[
\boxed{\quad
|C_1^\sharp|\geq\frac{4x_1}{9}.
\quad}
\tag{8}
\]

Let
\[
P=\overline{P_\varnothing}
\quad\text{and}\quad
S_1=\frac{P+C_1^\sharp}{2}.
\tag{9}
\]
Then
\[
S_1\subseteq K\cap\left\{f_1\geq\frac{\eta_1}{2}\right\},
\tag{10}
\]
and planar Brunn--Minkowski gives
\[
\boxed{\quad
|S_1|\geq
\sigma_1:=
\frac14\left(
\sqrt{p_\varnothing}+\frac23\sqrt{x_1}

\quad}
\tag{11}

Accepted fact e40abbed940a40ab gives the nondegenerate central triangle

\qquad

\tag{12}

Let

E_1=\mathcal T_1\cap\{f_1=0\},

\tag{13}

G_1=\operatorname{conv}(E_1\cup\{z_1\}).

d_1\geq\frac{\eta_1}{2},

\tag{20}

Equivalently, the endpoint slack satisfies

## Proof

By (2)--(3), both sides of the nonconstant affine cut \(\phi=0\) have
positive area in \(A_2\). Hence
\[
\min_{A_2}\phi<0<\max_{A_2}\phi,
\qquad
\omega_1>0.
\tag{25}
\]
Let \(g_2\) be the centroid of \(A_2\), and let \(g_{\rm co}\) be the
centroid of \(C_1^{\rm co}\). The sharp dominant-cut displacement estimate
of accepted fact f1096f0628017701, using the dominant conclusion of
2f637c4da2df28f1, is
\[
\phi(g_2)\geq
\frac{9x_1-5m}{12m}\,\omega_1.
\tag{26}
\]
The cutting line has area zero, so centroid decomposition across the cut is
exact:
\[
m\phi(g_2)
=x_1\phi(g_{C_1})+(m-x_1)\phi(g_{\rm co}).
\tag{27}
\]
Since \(\phi\leq0\) on \(C_1^{\rm co}\),
\[
\phi(g_{\rm co})\leq0.
\]
Thus (27) gives
\[
x_1\eta_1\geq m\phi(g_2).
\]
Combining this with (26) proves (6).

The closure \(\overline C_1\) is a two-dimensional compact convex body of
area \(x_1\), and its centroid is \(g_{C_1}\). The closed halfplane
\(\{\phi\geq\eta_1\}\) contains that centroid on its boundary. The planar
centroid-halfplane inequality used in accepted facts 2f637c4da2df28f1 and
f1096f0628017701 therefore gives
\[
|C_1^\sharp|\geq\frac49|\overline C_1|
=\frac{4x_1}{9}.
\]
This proves (8). In particular \(C_1^\sharp\) has positive area, so it is
two-dimensional.

Because \(p_\varnothing>0\), \(P=\overline{P_\varnothing}\) is also a
two-dimensional compact convex body and \(|P|=p_\varnothing\). Every
\(p\in P\) satisfies \(f_1(p)\geq L_1\), and every
\(c\in C_1^\sharp\) satisfies
\[
f_1(c)\geq-L_1+\eta_1.
\]
Hence every \(z=(p+c)/2\in S_1\) satisfies
\[
f_1(z)\geq
\frac{L_1+(-L_1+\eta_1)}2
=\frac{\eta_1}{2}.
\tag{28}
\]
Also \(P\subseteq A_0\), \(C_1^\sharp\subseteq A_2\), and
\((A_0+A_2)/2\subseteq K\), so \(S_1\subseteq K\). This proves (10).
Planar Brunn--Minkowski gives
\[
|S_1|^{1/2}
\geq
\frac{\sqrt{|P|}+\sqrt{|C_1^\sharp|}}2
\geq
\frac{\sqrt{p_\varnothing}+(2/3)\sqrt{x_1}}2,
\]
which proves (11).

The hypotheses \(p_\varnothing>0\), \(r_{123}>0\), \(r_1r_2r_3>0\), and
the all-double \(Q\)-support allow application of accepted fact
e40abbed940a40ab, proving (12). In the affine sign-plane coordinates of
accepted fact 0e51a47a9add571f, the edge \(E_1\) has \(f_1=0\), while the
opposite vertex of \(\mathcal T_1\) has
\[
(f_1,f_2,f_3)=(-1,0,0).
\tag{29}
\]
Thus \(E_1\) is nondegenerate and the affine \(f_1\)-height of
\(\mathcal T_1\) over \(E_1\) is exactly \(1\).

The compactness of \(S_1\) gives the minimizer \(z_1\), and (28) gives
\[
d_1\geq\eta_1/2>0.
\]
Since \(E_1\subseteq K\), \(z_1\in K\), and \(K\) is convex,
\[
G_1\subseteq K.
\]
The affine function \(f_1\) ranges from \(0\) on the base \(E_1\) to \(d_1\)
at the apex \(z_1\), proving the containment in (15). The triangles
\(\mathcal T_1\) and \(G_1\) share the same base and have affine
\(f_1\)-heights \(1\) and \(d_1\). Therefore
\[
|G_1|=d_1|\mathcal T_1|=r_{123}d_1.
\]
Together with \(d_1\geq\eta_1/2\) and (6), this proves (16).

Every point of \(S_1\) has \(f_1\geq d_1\). Every point of \(G_1\) other
than its apex has \(f_1<d_1\), and
\[
G_1\cap\{f_1=d_1\}=\{z_1\}.
\]
Hence \(G_1\cap S_1\) has planar area zero, proving (17). Both bodies lie in
\(K\cap\{f_1\geq0\}\), so their areas add as a lower bound and give (19).

Under the exact convention of 0e51a47a9add571f, the positive open
\(f_1\)-halfplane in \(K\) is the disjoint union of
\[
R_2,\qquad R_3,\qquad R_{23}.
\]
The line \(f_1=0\) has area zero. This proves (18). The crossing identity
(3a), supplied in the target branch by 2f637c4da2df28f1, has
\[
v_1=m-x_1,\qquad A_1=k-c_1.
\]
Substitution proves (20).

Finally, \(P\subseteq A_0\), \(\overline C_1\subseteq A_2\), and the
midpoint containment give (21). The weak endpoint inequalities give
\(f_1\geq0\) on \(B_1\). Applying planar Brunn--Minkowski to \(P\) and
\(\overline C_1\) proves (22). Therefore
\[
A_1\geq\max\{\beta_1,\sigma_1+\lambda_1\}.
\]
A direct subtraction gives
\[
\beta_1-\sigma_1
=\frac{5x_1}{36}
+\frac{\sqrt{p_\varnothing x_1}}6.
\]
Using
\[
\max\{\beta,\sigma+\lambda\}
=\beta+[\lambda-(\beta-\sigma)]_+
\]
proves (23)--(24). The zero-area cutting-line audit in
0e51a47a9add571f justifies every closure and exact-cell area identity used
above.
