## Statement

Let \(C\) satisfy the locked problem, let \(A_0,A_1,A_2\) be its nonempty
two-dimensional integer-height sections, write \(a_i=|A_i|\),
\(T=a_0+a_1+a_2\), and assume
\[
a_i<a_j+a_k
\qquad(\{i,j,k\}=\{0,1,2\}).
\tag{1}
\]
Let \(g_i\) be the centroid of \(A_i\), and put
\[
t=\frac{2T}{9},\qquad m=\min\{a_0,a_2\}.
\tag{2}
\]

For \(q\in\mathbb S^1\), use the directional cap functions, quantiles, and
zero-demand thresholds of accepted fact `17ca54faa05ad878`:
\[
F_{i,q}(r)=|A_i\cap\{x:q\cdot x\le r\}|,
\]
\[
Q_{i,q}(0)=\min_{x\in A_i}q\cdot x,\qquad
Q_{i,q}(u)=\inf\{r:F_{i,q}(r)\ge u\}\quad(0<u\le a_i),
\]
and
\[
D_{i,q}(0)=-\infty,\qquad
D_{i,q}(u)=Q_{i,q}(u)\quad(0<u\le a_i).
\tag{3}
\]
For \(0<b\le m\), also put
\[
R_q(b)=
\max_{0\le u\le b}
\bigl(Q_{0,q}(u)+Q_{2,q}(b-u)\bigr),
\tag{4}
\]
and use the scalar zero-demand convention
\[
E_q(0)=-\infty,\qquad E_q(b)=R_q(b)\quad(0<b\le m).
\tag{5}
\]

Define, for \(0\le a\le a_1\),
\[
\mathcal M(a)=
A_1\cap\bigcap_{q\in\mathbb S^1}
\{z:q\cdot z\ge D_{1,q}(a)\},
\tag{6}
\]
with \(\mathcal M(0)=A_1\), and, for \(0\le b\le m\), define
\[
\mathcal E(b)=
\bigcap_{\substack{q\in\mathbb S^1\\0\le u\le b}}
\left\{z:
2q\cdot z\ge Q_{0,q}(u)+Q_{2,q}(b-u)
\right\},
\tag{7}
\]
with the distinct zero-demand convention
\(\mathcal E(0)=\mathbb R^2\).

Then
\[
J=
\left[
\max\left\{0,t-\frac{4m}{9}\right\},
\min\left\{t,\frac{4a_1}{9}\right\}
\right]
\tag{8}
\]
is nonempty. In fact it has positive length, and every \(a\in J\), with
\(b=t-a\), satisfies
\[
0<a<a_1,\qquad 0<b<m,\qquad
a\le\frac{4a_1}{9},\qquad b\le\frac{4m}{9}.
\tag{9}
\]
For every such \(a\),
\[
g_1\in\mathcal M(a),\qquad
\frac{g_0+g_2}{2}\in\mathcal E(b)\cap A_1.
\tag{10}
\]

On their full stated domains, \(\mathcal M(a)\) and \(\mathcal E(b)\) are
closed convex sets, possibly empty. Their endpoint conventions are literal:
\[
\mathcal M(0)=A_1,\qquad
\mathcal M(a_1)=\varnothing,\qquad
\mathcal E(0)=\mathbb R^2,
\tag{11}
\]
while \(\mathcal E(m)\) is the intersection in (7) with all finite
support-endpoint and capacity quantiles included. Their nesting is
\[
0\le a\le a'\le a_1
\quad\Longrightarrow\quad
\mathcal M(a')\subseteq\mathcal M(a),
\tag{12}
\]
\[
0\le b\le b'\le m
\quad\Longrightarrow\quad
\mathcal E(b')\subseteq\mathcal E(b).
\tag{13}
\]
Consequently, for \(a\le a'\) in \(J\),
\[
\mathcal M(a')\subseteq\mathcal M(a),
\qquad
\mathcal E(t-a)\subseteq\mathcal E(t-a').
\tag{14}
\]

Finally, if \(D_t\) denotes the true middle depth region in accepted fact
`d2d117bcc58a1ba0`, then for every \(a\in J\),
\[
\mathcal M(a)\cap\mathcal E(t-a)\ne\varnothing
\quad\Longrightarrow\quad
D_t\ne\varnothing.
\tag{15}
\]

## Proof

Let
\[
M=\max\{a_0,a_2\}.
\]
The strict inequalities (1) imply \(a_i<T/2\) for every \(i\), and the
inequality belonging to the larger endpoint is
\[
M<a_1+m.
\tag{16}
\]
Thus
\[
\frac{4m}{9}<t,\qquad \frac{4a_1}{9}<t.
\tag{17}
\]
The two endpoints in (8) therefore simplify to
\[
t-\frac{4m}{9}
\quad\hbox{and}\quad
\frac{4a_1}{9}.
\]
Moreover, (16) is exactly
\[
t-\frac{4m}{9}<\frac{4a_1}{9}.
\tag{18}
\]
Hence \(J\) has positive length. If \(a\in J\) and \(b=t-a\), then
\[
t-\frac{4m}{9}\le a\le\frac{4a_1}{9},
\qquad
t-\frac{4a_1}{9}\le b\le\frac{4m}{9}.
\]
Equations (17), together with \(a_1,m>0\), prove all assertions in (9).
In particular, every quantile used for \(a\in J\) is strictly below its
capacity, although the definitions (3), (6), and (7) remain valid at the
full capacity endpoints.

We next check closedness, convexity, endpoints, and nesting. Accepted fact
`17ca54faa05ad878` proves that \(Q_{i,q}\) is finite, continuous, and
strictly increasing on \([0,a_i]\), with
\[
Q_{i,q}(0)=\min_{A_i}q\cdot x,\qquad
Q_{i,q}(a_i)=\max_{A_i}q\cdot x.
\tag{19}
\]
For \(a>0\), formula (6) is an intersection of the compact convex set
\(A_1\) and closed halfplanes. For \(a=0\), it is \(A_1\) by convention.
Thus every \(\mathcal M(a)\) is closed and convex. Monotonicity of the
quantiles, together with \(D_{1,q}(0)=-\infty\), proves (12).

At \(a=a_1\), suppose that \(z\in\mathcal M(a_1)\). Fix any
\(q\in\mathbb S^1\). Applying (6) to \(q\) and \(-q\), and using (19),
gives
\[
q\cdot z\ge\max_{x\in A_1}q\cdot x,
\qquad
q\cdot z\le\min_{x\in A_1}q\cdot x.
\]
Because \(A_1\) has nonempty interior, its width in direction \(q\) is
positive, a contradiction. Hence \(\mathcal M(a_1)=\varnothing\). This
also verifies the capacity endpoint rather than silently identifying
\(D_{1,q}(0)\) with the finite support value \(Q_{1,q}(0)\).

For \(0<b\le m\), every parameter in (7) lies in its quantile domain:
\[
0\le u\le b\le m\le a_0,\qquad
0\le b-u\le b\le m\le a_2.
\]
Thus all thresholds in (7), including those at \(u=0,b\) and \(b=m\),
are finite. Each constraint is a closed halfplane, so \(\mathcal E(b)\)
is closed and convex. At \(b=m\), if \(m=a_0\), the constraint \(u=m\)
uses \(Q_{0,q}(a_0)=\max_{A_0}q\cdot x\); if \(m=a_2\), the constraint
\(u=0\) uses \(Q_{2,q}(a_2)=\max_{A_2}q\cdot x\). The other zero-demand
summand in either endpoint constraint is its finite support value
\(Q_{i,q}(0)\), as required by (4), not \(-\infty\). Accepted fact
`17ca54faa05ad878` shows that (4) is an attained maximum and that, for
\(b>0\),
\[
\mathcal E(b)=
\bigcap_{q\in\mathbb S^1}
\{z:2q\cdot z\ge R_q(b)\}.
\tag{20}
\]
By contrast, at total demand \(b=0\), the correct convention is
\(\mathcal E(0)=\mathbb R^2\), corresponding to \(E_q(0)=-\infty\);
one must not substitute \(b=0\) into the support-endpoint maximum (4).

Now let \(0<b\le b'\le m\) and \(z\in\mathcal E(b')\). For every
\(q\in\mathbb S^1\) and \(u\in[0,b]\), membership in
\(\mathcal E(b')\) and monotonicity of \(Q_{2,q}\) give
\[
\begin{aligned}
2q\cdot z
&\ge Q_{0,q}(u)+Q_{2,q}(b'-u)\\
&\ge Q_{0,q}(u)+Q_{2,q}(b-u).
\end{aligned}
\]
Hence \(z\in\mathcal E(b)\). The case \(b=0\) follows from
\(\mathcal E(0)=\mathbb R^2\), proving (13). Substituting
\(b=t-a\) gives the second inclusion in (14), while (12) gives the
first. The directions are opposite: increasing \(a\) shrinks
\(\mathcal M(a)\) and enlarges \(\mathcal E(t-a)\).

We now prove the centroid containments. Accepted fact
`2f9be517307232c7` supplies the planar \(4/9\) centroid-cap bound.
Consequently, for every \(i\), \(q\), and
\(0\le v\le4a_i/9\),
\[
F_{i,q}(q\cdot g_i)\ge\frac{4a_i}{9}\ge v,
\qquad
Q_{i,q}(v)\le q\cdot g_i.
\tag{21}
\]
For \(v=0\), the second inequality follows directly from the support
endpoint in (19); for \(v>0\), it follows from the quantile definition.

If \(a\in J\), then \(0<a\le4a_1/9\). Equation (21) gives
\[
q\cdot g_1\ge D_{1,q}(a)
\qquad(q\in\mathbb S^1),
\]
and \(g_1\in A_1\), so \(g_1\in\mathcal M(a)\).

Put \(b=t-a\) and \(z_0=(g_0+g_2)/2\). For every \(u\in[0,b]\),
\[
u\le b\le\frac{4m}{9}\le\frac{4a_0}{9},
\qquad
b-u\le b\le\frac{4m}{9}\le\frac{4a_2}{9}.
\]
Applying (21) to both endpoint slices and adding yields
\[
2q\cdot z_0
=q\cdot g_0+q\cdot g_2
\ge Q_{0,q}(u)+Q_{2,q}(b-u).
\]
This holds for every \(q\) and \(u\), including \(u=0,b\), and proves
\(z_0\in\mathcal E(b)\). The locked polytope and accepted fact
`8ad0785e5b4ae9ef` give
\[
\frac{A_0+A_2}{2}\subseteq A_1.
\]
Since each centroid belongs to its convex body,
\[
z_0\in\frac{A_0+A_2}{2}\subseteq A_1.
\]
This proves (10). The zero-demand cases outside \(J\) are also literal:
\(g_1\in\mathcal M(0)=A_1\) and
\(z_0\in\mathcal E(0)=\mathbb R^2\). No centroid containment is claimed
at a positive full-capacity demand.

It remains to prove the reduction. From (16),
\[
T=M+(a_1+m)<2(a_1+m),
\]
so
\[
0<t<a_1+m=\min\{a_0+a_1,a_1+a_2\}.
\tag{22}
\]
Thus both the exact profile formula of accepted fact
`17ca54faa05ad878` and the true-region identity of accepted fact
`d2d117bcc58a1ba0` apply at \(t\).

Fix \(a\in J\), put \(b=t-a\), and take
\(z\in\mathcal M(a)\cap\mathcal E(b)\). For every
\(q\in\mathbb S^1\), membership in \(\mathcal M(a)\) gives
\[
q\cdot z\ge D_{1,q}(a).
\tag{23}
\]
Membership in \(\mathcal E(b)\), equation (20), and (5) give
\[
q\cdot z\ge\frac12E_q(b).
\tag{24}
\]
Equation (24) remains automatic at \(b=0\) because then
\(E_q(0)=-\infty\). Likewise, (23) is automatic at \(a=0\) because
\(D_{1,q}(0)=-\infty\).

The attained fixed-direction formula in accepted fact
`17ca54faa05ad878` is
\[
\rho_q(t)=
\min_{0\le c\le t}
\max\left\{D_{1,q}(c),\frac12E_q(t-c)\right\}.
\tag{25}
\]
Using the admissible choice \(c=a\) in (25), followed by (23)-(24),
gives
\[
\rho_q(t)
\le
\max\left\{D_{1,q}(a),\frac12E_q(b)\right\}
\le q\cdot z.
\tag{26}
\]
Since \(z\in\mathcal M(a)\subseteq A_1\), equation (26) for every
\(q\) and the exact identity in accepted fact `d2d117bcc58a1ba0`
imply
\[
z\in
A_1\cap\bigcap_{q\in\mathbb S^1}
\{w:q\cdot w\ge\rho_q(t)\}
=D_t.
\]
This proves (15). The argument is conditional on a common point
\(z\); the separate centroid containments above do not provide such a
point and do not imply that \(\mathcal M(a)\) and \(\mathcal E(t-a)\)
intersect.
