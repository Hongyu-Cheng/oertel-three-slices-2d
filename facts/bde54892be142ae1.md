Direct line-side checks give the three asserted full intersections.  In particular, the closed clipped cells, in cyclic vertex order, are
\[

Their areas can be checked without any approximation.  The first is a triangle with vertical base \(1/100\) and horizontal altitude \(1/5000\), so its area is \(1/10^6\).  The hull of the \(U=0\) and \(V=0\) chords has \(Q_{jk}\)-area

Likewise, the hull of the \(V=0\) and \(102-U+V=0\) chords has \(Q_N\)-area
