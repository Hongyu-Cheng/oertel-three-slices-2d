## Statement

Assume the complete common-plane hypotheses of `2b42506f5de09be1`, the full-section conventions of `989c9b585d2b42cc`, and the \(A_2\) coordinates and parameters of `fa0e57b225178b3b`.  Thus coordinate area is \(\Delta>0\) times physical area,
\[
 U=-q_k,\qquad V=q_\ell,\qquad -q_j=S-U+V,
\]
the only positive-area strict \(A_2\)-cells are
\[
\begin{array}{c|ccc}
Q_j&U<0&V>0&-q_j>0\\
Q_{jk}&U>0&V>0&-q_j>0\\
Q_N&U>0&V<0&-q_j>0\\
Q_{k\ell}&U>0&V<0&-q_j<0,
\end{array}
\]
and
\[
 |Q_j|=e>0,\qquad |Q_{jk}|=g_0>0.
\]
Write the three full chords as
\[
 U=0,\quad V\in[v,v+J],\qquad
 V=0,\quad U\in[u,u+H],
\]
\[
 -q_j=0,\quad (U,V)=(S-t,-t),
 \quad t\in[w_0,w_0+K],
\]
where \(J,H,K>0\), \(u,v,w_0\ge0\), and put
\[
 L=u+H,\qquad q=v+J,\qquad
 \theta=S-L,\qquad \psi=S-w_0-K.
\]
Retain the literal accepted strata
\[
 u=0\Longleftrightarrow v=0,\qquad
 \theta=0\Longleftrightarrow w_0=0,\qquad
 \psi>0.
\]
Define the polynomial quantities
\[
 P=vH-uJ,
\qquad
 X=2\Delta g_0-uJ-vH-JH.
\]
Then \(X\ge0\), and, with \(r_+=\max\{r,0\}\), exactly one of the
following endpoint-order cases applies.

1. If \(\theta\ge w_0>0\), then
\[
 2\Delta e\,(LP+uX)_+
 \le J^2uL^2
 \tag{A+}
\]
when \(u>0\), while on the literal stratum \(u=v=0\),
\[
 2\Delta e\,(X-JL)_+
 \le J^2L^2.
 \tag{A0}
\]

2. If \(w_0>\theta\), put
\[
 k=w_0-\theta,\qquad
 R=w_0L+qk,\qquad
 Z=Xk+LR.
\]
Here \(k,R,Z>0\).  If \(u>0\), then
\[
 2\Delta e\,(PZ+uXR)_+
 \le J^2uLZ,
 \tag{B+}
\]
and if \(u=v=0\), then
\[
 2\Delta e\,(-JZ+XR)_+
 \le J^2LZ.
 \tag{B0}
\]

3. If \(\theta=w_0=0\), put
\[
 R=L+q,\qquad Z=X+LR.
\]
If \(u>0\), then
\[
 2\Delta e\,(PZ+uXR)_+
 \le J^2uLZ,
 \tag{C+}
\]
and if \(u=v=0\), then
\[
 2\Delta e\,(-JZ+XR)_+
 \le J^2LZ.
 \tag{C0}
\]

The equivalences above make (A), (B), and (C) exhaustive: the only
case with \(w_0=0\) is \(\theta=w_0=0\), and equality
\(\theta=w_0>0\) belongs to (A).  No displayed inequality divides by
\(u\), \(v\), \(w_0-\theta\), or any other parameter.  Each
positive-part inequality is the finite polynomial disjunction in
which its argument is nonpositive, or its argument is positive and
the displayed ordinary polynomial inequality holds.  Hence these
relations may be added literally to the inherited finite
semialgebraic region \(\mathcal S_4^+\) of `cc1a8f11f870255e`.

## Proof

We prove the inequalities directly from global convexity.  Let \(I\)
be the \(U\)-projection of \(A_2\), and for \(x\in I\) write
\[
 h(x)=\max\{V:(x,V)\in A_2\},\qquad
 \ell(x)=\min\{V:(x,V)\in A_2\},
\qquad W(x)=h(x)-\ell(x).
\]
The function \(h\) is concave, \(\ell\) is convex, and \(W\) is
nonnegative and concave.  Positive area in \(Q_j,Q_{jk}\) puts \(0\)
in the interior of \(I\), so all one-sided polygonal slopes below are
finite.  Put
\[
 a=h'_+(0),\qquad b=\ell'_+(0),\qquad d=W'_-(0).
\]
Monotonicity of one-sided slopes gives
\[
 d\ge a-b.
 \tag{1}
\]

First suppose \(u>0\), and hence \(v>0\).  Exactness of the full
\(V=0\) chord gives
\[
 \ell(u)=0,\qquad
 \ell(x)>0\quad(0\le x<u),\qquad
 \ell(x)\le0\le h(x)\quad(u\le x\le L).
 \tag{2}
\]
For example, a point below \(V=0\) with \(0<x<u\), joined to
\((0,v)\), would create a point of the full horizontal section with
\(U<u\).  The endpoint assertions follow by the same argument and
closure.  Convexity of \(\ell\) and (2) give
\[
 b\le-\frac vu.
 \tag{3}
\]
Concavity of \(W\), (1), and \(W(0)=J\) give
\[
 W(x)\le J+dx\qquad(0\le x\le u).
 \tag{4}
\]
Also (1) and (3) imply \(a\le d-v/u\), so concavity of \(h\) gives
\[
 h(x)\le q+\left(d-\frac vu\right)x
 \qquad(u\le x\le L).
 \tag{5}
\]
Let \(G_{\mathrm{main}}\) be the coordinate area of \(Q_{jk}\) in
\(0\le U\le L\).  Equations (2), (4), and (5) yield
\[
\begin{aligned}
G_{\mathrm{main}}
&=\int_0^uW(x)\,dx+\int_u^Lh(x)\,dx\\
&\le
\frac{uJ+vH+JH}{2}
+\frac{L^2}{2}
\left(d-\frac{P}{uL}\right).
\end{aligned}
\tag{6}
\]
The hull inequality in `fa0e57b225178b3b` says
\[
 X=2\Delta g_0-uJ-vH-JH\ge0.
\tag{7}
\]
Since \(h(L)\ge0\), (5) also shows
\[
 z:=d-\frac{P}{uL}\ge0.
\tag{8}
\]

On the stratum \(u=v=0\), the full horizontal chord is
\([0,L]\times\{0\}\), \(q=J\), and the \(Q_{jk}\)-chord length at
\(x\in[0,L]\) is \(h(x)\).  Here \(\ell(0)=0\) and
\(\ell(x)\le0\) on \([0,L]\), so \(b\le0\).  Equations (1) and
concavity of \(h\) give
\[
 h(x)\le J+dx.
\]
Consequently (6)-(8) remain valid after replacing
\[
 \frac{P}{uL}\quad\hbox{by}\quad-\frac JL,
\qquad
 z\quad\hbox{by}\quad d+\frac JL.
\tag{9}
\]
This is the reason the zero-offset formulas must be stated
separately rather than obtained by division by \(u\).

We next control all possible \(Q_{jk}\)-area with \(U>L\).  A point
of the full diagonal chord has the form
\[
 C_t=(S-t,-t)=(L+\theta-t,-t).
\tag{10}
\]
If \(\theta\ge w_0>0\), then \(C_{w_0}\) has negative
\(V\)-coordinate and \(U\)-coordinate at least \(L\).  Were
\((x,y)\in A_2\) to have \(x>L,y>0\), its segment to
\(C_{w_0}\) would meet \(V=0\) at \(U>L\), contradicting the exact
full horizontal chord.  Thus there is no positive-\(V\) tail beyond
\(L\) in case (A).  Equations (6)-(8) then give
\[
 z\ge\frac{X}{L^2}.
\tag{11}
\]
For \(u>0\), (11) is
\[
 d\ge\frac{LP+uX}{uL^2};
\tag{12}
\]
for \(u=v=0\), it is
\[
 d\ge\frac{X-JL}{L^2}.
\tag{13}
\]

Now suppose \(w_0>\theta\), and put \(k=w_0-\theta>0\).  For any
\((x,y)\in A_2\) with \(x>L,y>0\), the segment to
\[
 C_{w_0}=(L-k,-w_0)
\]
meets \(V=0\) inside the full horizontal chord.  Its crossing
coordinate is at most \(L\), which after cross multiplication is
\[
 y\ge\mu(x-L),\qquad
 \mu=\frac{w_0}{k}>0.
\tag{14}
\]
If instead \(\theta=w_0=0\), take any
\(t\in(0,K]\) in the full diagonal chord.  Then
\(C_t=(L-t,-t)\), and the identical crossing argument gives (14)
with
\[
 \mu=1.
\tag{15}
\]
Thus the whole diagonal chord, including its zero-offset endpoint,
has been accounted for.  When \(w_0>\theta\), the bound from
\(t=w_0\) is the strongest one because
\(t/(t-\theta)\) decreases with \(t\); when \(\theta=0\), every
\(t>0\) gives the same slope \(1\).

For the moment use either finite value of \(\mu\) from (14)-(15), and
put
\[
 A=\mu+\frac qL.
\]
In the \(u>0\) stratum, (5), (8), and (14) show that the vertical
length of the \(Q_{jk}\)-tail at \(x=L+s\) is at most
\[
 \bigl(Lz-(A-z)s\bigr)_+.
\tag{16}
\]
The same formula follows from (9) when \(u=v=0\).  If \(z<A\),
integration of (16) and addition to (6) give
\[
 \Delta g_0-\frac{uJ+vH+JH}{2}
\le
\frac{L^2z}{2}
+\frac{L^2z^2}{2(A-z)}
=\frac{L^2Az}{2(A-z)}.
\tag{17}
\]
If \(z\ge A\), the lower bound obtained below is automatic.  Since
the left side of (17) is \(X/2\), both cases give
\[
 z\ge
\frac{XA}{X+L^2A}.
\tag{18}
\]
Writing
\[
 M=\mu L+q,\qquad Z'=X+LM,
\]
equation (18) is
\[
 z\ge\frac{XM}{LZ'}.
\tag{19}
\]

In case (B), \(M=R/k\) and \(Z'=Z/k\), with the polynomial \(R,Z\)
defined in the statement.  Hence (8) and (19) give, without any
division by \(k\) in the resulting numerator,
\[
 d\ge\frac{PZ+uXR}{uLZ}
 \quad(u>0),
\tag{20}
\]
whereas (9) and (19) give
\[
 d\ge\frac{-JZ+XR}{LZ}
 \quad(u=v=0).
\tag{21}
\]
In case (C), \(M=R=L+q\) and \(Z'=Z=X+LR\), so (20)-(21) hold
with the case-(C) definitions.  All denominators in (20)-(21) are
strictly positive on the strata in which they were used.

It remains to turn the slope bounds into cap-area bounds.  There is no
point of \(A_2\) with \(U<0,V<0\): a point in that unsupported strict
chamber can be perturbed toward an interior point while retaining its
strict signs, creating an unsupported positive-area cell.  For
\(U<0,V\ge0\), one automatically has
\(-q_j=S-U+V>0\).  Therefore, up to cutting-line boundaries, the
entire negative-\(U\) part of \(A_2\) is \(Q_j\), and its coordinate
area is \(\Delta e\).

The left derivative \(d=W'_-(0)\) is a supergradient of the concave
width \(W\).  If \(d>0\), then for \(x\le0\),
\[
 0\le W(x)\le J+dx,
\]
and hence
\[
 \Delta e\le\frac{J^2}{2d}.
\tag{22}
\]
If a displayed lower bound for \(d\) is nonpositive, the
corresponding positive-part inequality is automatic.  If it is
positive, substitute it into (22).  Clearing only the strictly
positive denominators in (12), (13), (20), and (21) gives,
respectively, (A+), (A0), (B+)/(C+), and (B0)/(C0).  Thus every
zero-offset, tangent, and endpoint-order stratum is covered without
unsafe division.

Finally specialize to the rational point of `cc1a8f11f870255e`:
\[
 \Delta=1,\quad e=\frac15,\quad g_0=\frac1{10},\quad
 u=v=\frac3{10},\quad J=\frac15,\quad H=\frac14,\quad
 w_0=50,\quad \theta=\frac{2029}{20}.
\]
This is case (A+) and
\[
 L=\frac{11}{20},\qquad
 P=X=\frac3{200},\qquad
 LP+uX=\frac{51}{4000}.
\]
The two sides of (A+) are
\[
 2\Delta e(LP+uX)=\frac{51}{10000},
\qquad
 J^2uL^2=\frac{363}{100000},
\]
and \(510>363\).  Hence the rational relative-interior survivor is
eliminated.  The slope lower bound (12) is
\[
 d\ge
\frac{51/4000}{(3/10)(11/20)^2}
=\frac{17}{121},
\]
so (22) gives
\[
 e\le\frac{121}{850}.
\]
This recovers exactly the special global-convexity contradiction of
`094a0f7aa5ab4f6e`, while the formulas above provide its symbolic and
boundary-complete extension.
