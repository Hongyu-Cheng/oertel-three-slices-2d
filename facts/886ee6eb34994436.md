## Statement

There is no full common-plane \(A_0,A_2,K\) realization of the strict rational
table in the polytopal setting of accepted fact `eef70ce3abe7606e`.

## Proof

Suppose such a realization exists. Accepted fact `eef70ce3abe7606e` supplies
its fifteen-variable normal-form tuple. In particular, the tuple satisfies
\[
D\le\frac4{169},
\tag{1}
\]
and, for each of the three cyclic endpoint pairs
\[
(x_{12},x_{21}),\qquad
(x_{23},x_{32}),\qquad
(x_{31},x_{13}),
\tag{2}
\]
condition (2a) of that fact says that either both entries vanish or both are
positive.

Let \(m\) be the number of pairs in (2) whose two entries vanish. Then
\[
m\in\{0,1,2,3\},
\tag{3}
\]
and these four cases are exhaustive.

If \(m=0\), all six endpoint parameters are positive, so accepted fact
`4cdcb422d6f20e28` gives
\[
D>\frac4{169}.
\]
If \(m=1\), accepted fact `064c82c25ed3dde1` gives the same strict
inequality. If \(m=2\) or \(m=3\), accepted fact `be4033c5411ca728`
again gives
\[
D>\frac4{169}.
\]
Every possible value of \(m\) therefore contradicts (1). Hence the assumed
full common-plane \(A_0,A_2,K\) realization does not exist.
