## Statement

Let \(E,L,K,\sigma=(-1,0,0)\), and \(A=25\sqrt3\) be exactly those of accepted fact `df56c5e5c2427a69`. There is no nonempty set \(P\subset E\) such that
\[
\frac{P+K}{2}\subseteq L.
\]
In particular, there is no rational nondegenerate triangle \(P\subset E\) of area \(A\) satisfying that containment and the three strict cap inequalities
\[
|L\cap\{s_j>0\}|+|P\cap\{s_j>-\sigma_j\}|+|K\cap\{s_j>\sigma_j\}|<\frac{4A}{9}
\qquad(j=1,2,3).
\]
For this seed, the cap inequalities would require exactly
\[
|P\cap\{s_1>1\}|<\frac{55}{9}\sqrt3,
\qquad
|P\cap\{s_2>0\}|<\frac{13}{36}\sqrt3,
\qquad
|P\cap\{s_3>0\}|<\frac{13}{36}\sqrt3,
\]
but the containment has no nonempty feasible \(P\) even before these cap bounds are imposed.

## Proof

Accepted fact `df56c5e5c2427a69` gives
\[
L=\operatorname{conv}\{(-1,-9,11),(0,-19/2,21/2),(0,21/2,-19/2),(-1,11,-9)\}.
\]
Thus every \(\ell\in L\) satisfies
\[
-1\le s_1(\ell)\le0.
\tag{1}
\]
The same accepted fact gives the two vertices
\[
k^-=(3,-1,-1)\in K,
\qquad
k^+=(7,-5,-1)\in K.
\tag{2}
\]

Suppose that \(P\subset E\) is nonempty and \((P+K)/2\subseteq L\), and fix any \(p\in P\). Applying the containment to \((p+k^-)/2\) and using (1) yields
\[
-1\le\frac{s_1(p)+3}{2}\le0,
\qquad\text{hence}\qquad
-5\le s_1(p)\le-3.
\tag{3}
\]
Applying it instead to \((p+k^+)/2\) yields
\[
-1\le\frac{s_1(p)+7}{2}\le0,
\qquad\text{hence}\qquad
-9\le s_1(p)\le-7.
\tag{4}
\]
The intervals in (3) and (4) are disjoint, a contradiction. Since \(p\) was an arbitrary point of an assumed nonempty \(P\), no such \(P\) exists. Equivalently, \(K\) has exact \(s_1\)-width \(7-3=4\), so
\[
\operatorname{width}_{s_1}\!\left(\frac{P+K}{2}\right)
=\frac{\operatorname{width}_{s_1}(P)+4}{2}\ge2,
\]
whereas (1) gives \(\operatorname{width}_{s_1}(L)=1\).

It remains only to record the exact cap geometry requested in the subgoal. Accepted fact `df56c5e5c2427a69` gives the fixed contributions
\[
\bigl(|L\cap\{s_j>0\}|\bigr)_{j=1}^3
=\left(0,\frac{43}{4}\sqrt3,\frac{43}{4}\sqrt3\right)
\]
and
\[
\bigl(|K\cap\{s_j>\sigma_j\}|\bigr)_{j=1}^3
=(5\sqrt3,0,0).
\]
Since \(4A/9=100\sqrt3/9\) and \(-\sigma=(1,0,0)\), subtracting these fixed contributions gives
\[
\frac{100}{9}\sqrt3-5\sqrt3=\frac{55}{9}\sqrt3,
\qquad
\frac{100}{9}\sqrt3-\frac{43}{4}\sqrt3=\frac{13}{36}\sqrt3.
\]
These are precisely the three residual strict cap budgets stated above. The containment contradiction (3)-(4) applies to every nonempty \(P\subset E\), independent of its area or cap profile, and therefore proves the required global infeasibility for every candidate triangle.
