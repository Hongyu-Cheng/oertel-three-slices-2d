## Statement

Let \(q_1,q_2,q_3\in\mathbb S^1\) be noncollinear, and let
\(\alpha_i>0\) satisfy
\[
\sum_{i=1}^3\alpha_iq_i=0.
\tag{1}
\]
After a cyclic relabeling, let \(B\) be the triangle whose three outer unit
normals are \(q_i\) and whose corresponding edge lengths are \(\alpha_i\).
Write
\[
\tau=|B|>0,\qquad d(c)=\sum_{i=1}^3\alpha_i c_i,
\tag{2}
\]
\[
P^-(c)=\bigcap_{i=1}^3\{x:q_i\cdot x\leq c_i\},\qquad
P^+(c)=\bigcap_{i=1}^3\{x:q_i\cdot x\geq c_i\}.
\tag{3}
\]
Use area zero for an empty set or a point.  Then
\[
\boxed{\quad
d(c)=2\sqrt{\tau}\left(\sqrt{|P^-(c)|}-\sqrt{|P^+(c)|}\right).
\quad}
\tag{4}
\]
More precisely, if \(d(c)>0\), then \(P^-(c)\) is a translate of
\(d(c)B/(2\tau)\), \(P^+(c)=\varnothing\), and
\[
d(c)=2V(P^-(c),B),\qquad |P^-(c)|=\frac{d(c)^2}{4\tau}.
\tag{5}
\]
If \(d(c)<0\), then \(P^+(c)\) is a positive homothet of \(-B\),
\(P^-(c)=\varnothing\), and
\[
-d(c)=2V(P^+(c),-B),\qquad |P^+(c)|=\frac{d(c)^2}{4\tau}.
\tag{6}
\]
If \(d(c)=0\), the three cutting lines are concurrent and both sets in
(3) are their common point.  Here \(V\) is planar mixed area normalized by
\[
|K+tL|=|K|+2tV(K,L)+t^2|L|.
\tag{7}
\]

For a two-dimensional compact convex body \(K\), put
\[
K_i=K\cap\{q_i\cdot x\leq c_i\},\qquad
\omega_K=|K_1\cap K_2\cap K_3|,
\tag{8}
\]
\[
u_K=\left|K\setminus\bigcup_{i=1}^3K_i\right|,
\qquad
e_K^-=\left|P^-(c)\setminus K\right|,
\qquad
e_K^+=\left|P^+(c)\setminus K\right|.
\tag{9}
\]
All cutting-line boundaries are planar null, so inclusion-exclusion gives
\[
u_K
=|K|-\sum_i|K_i|+\sum_{i<j}|K_i\cap K_j|-\omega_K.
\tag{10}
\]
Equation (4) therefore becomes the exact cap-valuation identity
\[
\boxed{\quad
d(c)=2\sqrt{\tau}
\left(
\sqrt{\omega_K+e_K^-}
-
\sqrt{u_K+e_K^+}
\right).
\quad}
\tag{11}
\]
The quantities \(\omega_K\) and \(u_K\) are determined by all individual,
pairwise, and triple cap areas.  The two exterior remainders are not.
Indeed, for fixed \(K\),
\[
c_i(R)=h_K(q_i)+R\qquad(R\geq0)
\tag{12}
\]
makes every \(K_i\) and every nonempty intersection of the \(K_i\)'s equal
to \(K\), while
\[
d(c(R))=d(c(0))+R\sum_i\alpha_i\longrightarrow+\infty.
\tag{13}
\]
Thus even the complete cap intersection lattice, with arbitrary intrinsic,
boundary, or mixed-area valuations applied to its members, admits no finite
upper control of \(d(c)\).  The missing information is exactly \(e_K^-\).
The symmetric construction below the three lower supports makes every cap
empty and sends \(d(c)\) to \(-\infty\), with \(e_K^+\) as the missing term.
The optimizer classification in accepted fact `65512ded3a06d971`
explicitly includes zero-demand and capacity-active endpoints, so profile
optimization does not justify deleting these saturation remainders by
assuming that every endpoint cut is active inside its body.

Now let compatible bodies satisfy
\[
\frac{A_0+A_2}{2}\subseteq A_1,
\tag{14}
\]
and use paired finite thresholds
\[
c_{0i}=r_i+\lambda_i,\qquad
c_{1i}=r_i,\qquad
c_{2i}=r_i-\lambda_i.
\tag{15}
\]
The three Farkas sums therefore satisfy
\[
d(c_0)+d(c_2)=2d(c_1).
\]
For every compact convex reference body \(L\), compatibility and the
monotonicity and Minkowski linearity of mixed area give
\[
2V(A_1,L)\geq V(A_0,L)+V(A_2,L).
\]
This is the whole-body second-difference information available from any
single mixed-area valuation; it contains no absolute sign for \(d(c_1)\).
Let \(P_h^\pm\) be (3) with thresholds \(c_{hi}\), and define
\[
\Omega_h=A_h\cap P_h^-,\qquad G_h=A_h\cap P_h^+.
\tag{16}
\]
Affinity and (14) give the two exact aggregate containments
\[
\frac{\Omega_0+\Omega_2}{2}\subseteq\Omega_1,\qquad
\frac{G_0+G_2}{2}\subseteq G_1.
\tag{17}
\]
When the two endpoint sets in the first inclusion are nonempty, the exact
area polynomial and monotonicity give the genuine cross-slice term
\[
\boxed{\quad
4|\Omega_1|
\geq
|\Omega_0|+2V(\Omega_0,\Omega_2)+|\Omega_2|.
\quad}
\tag{18}
\]
There is an identical inequality for \(G_0,G_2,G_1\).  If one endpoint set
is empty, the corresponding same-state mixed-area term gives no information.
There is no analogous halfplane localization for
\((G_0+\Omega_2)/2\) or \((\Omega_0+G_2)/2\): the two endpoint inequalities
have opposite signs.  This opposite-state localization is the first missing
cross-slice term.

Finally, the failure is realized exactly.  Let
\[
\Delta=\operatorname{conv}\{(0,0),(1,0),(0,1)\},
\qquad
A_0=\frac32\Delta,\quad A_1=\Delta,\quad A_2=\frac12\Delta.
\tag{19}
\]
Then
\[
\frac{A_0+A_2}{2}=A_1.
\tag{20}
\]
Accepted fact `8ad0785e5b4ae9ef` therefore realizes these three rational
polygons as the integer-height sections of a compact polytope.
Let
\[
\beta_1(x,y)=1-x-y,\qquad \beta_2(x,y)=x,\qquad\beta_3(x,y)=y,
\tag{21}
\]
\[
b=\frac{31}{96},\qquad
f_i=32(b-\beta_i),\qquad
\sigma=-\frac{161}{3}.
\tag{22}
\]
The three affine functions satisfy
\[
f_1+f_2+f_3=-1.
\tag{23}
\]
Set
\[
q_1=\frac{(1,1)}{\sqrt2},\qquad
q_2=(-1,0),\qquad q_3=(0,-1),
\tag{24}
\]
\[
\alpha_1=32\sqrt2,\qquad \alpha_2=\alpha_3=32,
\tag{25}
\]
\[
r_1=\frac{65}{96\sqrt2},\qquad
r_2=r_3=-\frac{31}{96},
\tag{26}
\]
\[
\lambda_1=-\frac{161}{96\sqrt2},\qquad
\lambda_2=\lambda_3=-\frac{161}{96}.
\tag{27}
\]
Then
\[
f_i(w)=\alpha_i(q_i\cdot w-r_i),\qquad
\sigma=\alpha_i\lambda_i,
\tag{28}
\]
\[
\sum_i\alpha_iq_i=0,\qquad
\sum_i\alpha_i r_i=1.
\tag{29}
\]
For
\[
H_i=\{(h,w):f_i(w)+\sigma(h-1)\leq0\},
\tag{30}
\]
the three section conditions at \(h=0,1,2\) are respectively
\[
\beta_i(w)\geq2,\qquad
\beta_i(w)\geq\frac{31}{96},\qquad
\beta_i(w)\geq-\frac{65}{48}.
\tag{31}
\]
Because \(\sum_i\beta_i=1>3(31/96)\), the three open middle caps
\(\{\beta_i>31/96\}\) cover the whole plane.
Thus every bottom cap is empty, every top cap is all of \(A_2\), and the
middle caps have equal area.  Writing
\[
T=|A_0|+|A_1|+|A_2|=\frac74,\qquad t=\frac{2T}{9}=\frac7{18},
\tag{32}
\]
one has, for every row,
\[
\mathcal H_2(S\cap H_i)
=\frac12\left(\frac{65}{96}\right)^2+\frac18
=\frac{6529}{18432}
=t-\frac{71}{2048}
<t.
\tag{33}
\]
Consequently, for the profiles of accepted fact `d2d117bcc58a1ba0`,
\[
\Phi_{q_i}(r_i)<t,\qquad r_i<\rho_{q_i}(t),
\tag{34}
\]
and hence
\[
\boxed{\quad
\sum_i\alpha_i\rho_{q_i}(t)
>
\sum_i\alpha_i r_i
=1>0.
\quad}
\tag{35}
\]

In this example the reference triangle in (2) is \(B=32\Delta\), so
\(\tau=512\).  The middle arrangement triangle is
\[
P_1^-=
\left\{x\geq\frac{31}{96},\
y\geq\frac{31}{96},\
x+y\leq\frac{65}{96}\right\},
\qquad |P_1^-|=\frac1{2048},
\tag{36}
\]
and (4) reads exactly
\[
1=2\sqrt{512}\sqrt{\frac1{2048}}.
\tag{37}
\]
Thus the positive Farkas sum is already visible as a genuine middle
triple-overlap valuation.  Midpoint compatibility does not reverse its sign:
\[
\Omega_0=\varnothing,\qquad \Omega_2=A_2,\qquad
G_0=A_0,\qquad G_2=\varnothing,
\tag{38}
\]
so both same-state terms in (17)-(18) vanish.  The opposite-state midpoint
body is instead
\[
\frac{G_0+\Omega_2}{2}
=\frac{A_0+A_2}{2}
=A_1,
\tag{39}
\]
but compatibility alone does not specify which of the three middle cap
labels receives each of its points.  Since all three bodies in (19) are
homothetic and (20) is equality, the whole-body mixed-area inequality after
(15) is also equality for every reference body \(L\).

## Proof

Relabel the normals in their cyclic order and rotate every \(q_i\) by
\(+\pi/2\).  The three vectors
\[
\alpha_iR_{\pi/2}q_i
\]
sum to zero by (1), and, because the normals are noncollinear and positively
span the plane, they form the oriented edge vectors of a nondegenerate
triangle \(B\).  Its \(i\)-th edge has length \(\alpha_i\) and outer normal
\(q_i\).

Put \(b_i=h_B(q_i)\).  The polygonal divergence identity gives
\[
2\tau
=\int_{\partial B}x\cdot n(x)\,d\mathcal H^1(x)
=\sum_i\alpha_i b_i.
\tag{40}
\]
This is also the polygonal mixed-area formula
\[
2V(K,B)=\sum_i\alpha_i h_K(q_i)
\tag{41}
\]
when \(K\) is any compact convex body.  Formula (41) follows directly by
moving the three supporting edges of \(B\) by the support distances of \(K\)
in the area polynomial (7); polygonal approximation gives the same statement
for nonsmooth \(K\).  In the present application the arrangement bodies are
triangles and the slice bodies are polytopes, so no regularity assumption is
being used.

Suppose \(d(c)>0\), and put
\[
s=\frac{d(c)}{2\tau}>0.
\tag{42}
\]
The vector \(c-sb\in\mathbb R^3\) is orthogonal to \(\alpha\), by (2) and
(40).  If \(Q\) is the \(3\times2\) matrix with rows \(q_i^{\mathsf T}\),
then \(Q\) has rank two and its left kernel is spanned by \(\alpha\).
Therefore \(\operatorname{range}Q=\alpha^\perp\), and there is \(z\) such
that
\[
Qz=c-sb.
\tag{43}
\]
The three inequalities in (3) now show exactly that
\[
P^-(c)=z+sB.
\tag{44}
\]
The opposite intersection is empty, since a point in it would give
\[
0=\sum_i\alpha_iq_i\cdot x\geq\sum_i\alpha_i c_i=d(c)>0.
\tag{45}
\]
Equations (40)-(44), translation invariance and homogeneity of mixed area,
give (5).  If \(d(c)<0\), apply the same argument to the normals \(-q_i\),
thresholds \(-c_i\), and reference triangle \(-B\), proving (6).  If
\(d(c)=0\), (43) holds with \(s=0\); positive spanning makes the common
polar cone trivial, so both intersections in (3) equal the resulting point
\(z\).  This proves (4)-(6), including every boundary case.

The identities
\[
|P^-(c)|=\omega_K+e_K^-,
\qquad
|P^+(c)|=u_K+e_K^+
\tag{46}
\]
hold up to subsets of the three cutting lines, which have planar area zero.
Ordinary inclusion-exclusion proves (10).  Substitution in (4) proves (11).
Equations (12)-(13) prove the asserted non-identifiability without any
continuity or valuation classification theorem: all cap sets themselves are
constant, so every functional of those sets is constant.  Only the exterior
arrangement triangle changes.

For (17), take \(x\in\Omega_0,z\in\Omega_2\).  Compatibility puts
\((x+z)/2\) in \(A_1\), while (15) gives, for every \(i\),
\[
q_i\cdot\frac{x+z}{2}
\leq\frac{(r_i+\lambda_i)+(r_i-\lambda_i)}2
=r_i.
\tag{47}
\]
Thus the midpoint lies in \(\Omega_1\).  Reversing all three inequalities
proves the assertion for \(G_h\).  The exact planar area polynomial gives
\[
\left|\frac{\Omega_0+\Omega_2}{2}\right|
=\frac14\left(
|\Omega_0|+2V(\Omega_0,\Omega_2)+|\Omega_2|
\right),
\tag{48}
\]
and containment proves (18).  If the endpoint signs are opposite, the two
terms in (47) point in opposite directions and no middle halfplane conclusion
follows.  This proves the claimed location of the first missing cross-slice
term.  The displayed whole-body mixed-area inequality follows similarly from
\[
\frac{A_0+A_2}{2}\subseteq A_1,\qquad
V\left(\frac{A_0+A_2}{2},L\right)
=\frac{V(A_0,L)+V(A_2,L)}2.
\]

It remains to audit the calibration.  Since
\(\beta_1+\beta_2+\beta_3=1\), (22) gives (23).  Direct substitution of
(24)-(27) proves (28)-(29).  Substituting \(h=0,1,2\) in (30) gives (31).
On \(A_0=(3/2)\Delta\), the maxima of \(\beta_1,\beta_2,\beta_3\) are
respectively \(1,3/2,3/2\), so all bottom caps are empty.  On
\(A_2=(1/2)\Delta\), one has
\[
\beta_2,\beta_3\geq0,\qquad \beta_1\geq\frac12,
\]
so all top caps are full.  In \(A_1=\Delta\), each set
\(\{\beta_i\geq31/96\}\) is a corner triangle with linear ratio \(65/96\).
This proves (33).

The profile range condition holds because
\[
0<t=\frac7{18}<|A_1|+|A_2|=\frac58
\leq |A_0|+|A_1|.
\tag{49}
\]
The halfspace in (30) is a finite-slope witness at middle threshold \(r_i\),
so (33) gives
\[
\Phi_{q_i}(r_i)\leq\mathcal H_2(S\cap H_i)<t.
\]
The exact superlevel identity in accepted fact `d2d117bcc58a1ba0` yields
(34), and positivity of all \(\alpha_i\), together with (29), proves (35).
Equations (36)-(39) follow by direct substitution.

Finally,
\[
|A_0|=\frac98,\qquad |A_1|=\frac12,\qquad |A_2|=\frac18,
\qquad \frac T2=\frac78.
\tag{50}
\]
Thus the example violates the necessary global-counterexample condition
\(a_i<T/2\) used in accepted fact `d2d117bcc58a1ba0`; it does not disprove
the locked theorem.  It proves that compatibility, exact profile
optimization, and any collection of separate single-body cap valuations do
not imply the global Farkas balance.  To use the residual area condition, a
new argument must control the opposite-state midpoint body in (39) relative
to the middle three-label arrangement.  The endpoint payment of
`0b5d84877f51b7c3` and the nonnegative surplus decompositions of
`3081dc9f6b1f023f` do not contain this label-resolved cross-slice placement;
adding or renaming their scalar terms cannot recover the exterior remainders
in (11).  The equality-case label rigidity of `ea6c0c97668fdba3` starts only
after three separate zero-surplus identities hold; it does not provide the
missing opposite-state transport away from that equality stratum.
