## Statement

Let \(\delta\ge1\). Assign every unlisted cell mass zero and set
\[
p_\varnothing=\frac{58+187\delta}{20},
\qquad
p_{\{1\}}=p_{\{2\}}=p_{\{3\}}=\frac15,
\tag{1}
\]
\[
q_{123}=\frac25,
\qquad
q_{12}=q_{13}=q_{23}=\frac15,
\tag{2}
\]
\[
r_1=r_2=r_3=2\delta,
\qquad
r_{12}=r_{13}=r_{23}=\delta,
\qquad
r_{123}=\frac{\delta}{10}.
\tag{3}
\]
Then
\[
M=\frac{70+187\delta}{20},
\qquad
m=1,
\qquad
k=\frac{91\delta}{10}.
\tag{4}
\]
This scalar-only all-double table satisfies every condition supplied by accepted facts `91cb5bfc71da49ac`, `624b129b68e0fdee`, `8234d1112ec2f031`, `2bb72698f0248a65`, `93f1cefaa4795a25`, `ce343e749df349fe`, `0819231d0af83197`, and `e1361027c1dda6fa`. In particular,
\[
\sum_i u_i+m+q_{123}
+r_{12}+r_{13}+r_{23}+2r_{123}
=
\frac{2M-m-k}{3},
\tag{5}
\]
\[
\left(
\sum_i\sqrt{r_i+\frac{2r_{123}}9}
\right)^2
=2k,
\tag{6}
\]
while
\[
\left(\sum_i\sqrt{r_i}\right)^2<2k.
\tag{7}
\]

## Proof

The totals in (4) follow directly:
\[
\frac{58+187\delta}{20}+3\cdot\frac15
=
\frac{70+187\delta}{20},
\]
\[
\frac25+3\cdot\frac15=1,
\]
\[
3(2\delta)+3\delta+\frac{\delta}{10}
=\frac{91\delta}{10}.
\]
Every listed mass is positive for \(\delta\ge1\). Thus (2) has exactly the all-double support of accepted fact `91cb5bfc71da49ac`, and (3) lies in the required branch
\[
r_1r_2r_3r_{123}>0.
\tag{8}
\]

For every \(i\),
\[
u_i=\frac15,
\qquad
v_i=\frac45,
\qquad
c_i=2\delta+2\delta+\frac{\delta}{10}
=\frac{41\delta}{10}.
\tag{9}
\]
The common crossing level is
\[
h
=\frac{2(M+m+k)}9
=1+\frac{41\delta}{10}.
\tag{10}
\]
Equations (9)-(10) give all three crossings:
\[
u_i+v_i+c_i
=1+\frac{41\delta}{10}
=h.
\]
The canonical left tails of accepted fact `624b129b68e0fdee` hold at equality:
\[
c_i+m
=\frac{41\delta}{10}+1
=h.
\tag{11}
\]
The right tails are strict because
\[
c_i+M-h=M-1>0.
\]
Moreover,
\[
0<u_i<M,\qquad
0<v_i<m,\qquad
0<h<M,
\]
the last inequality following from
\[
M-h=\frac{50+105\delta}{20}>0.
\]
Thus all three crossings are in the allowed proper-proper equality-tail state.

The crossings already force the \(W=0\) excess identity. Explicitly, put
\[
U=\sum_i u_i=\frac35,
\qquad
E_R=r_{12}+r_{13}+r_{23}+2r_{123}
=\frac{16\delta}{5}.
\tag{12}
\]
Then
\[
U+m+q_{123}+E_R
=
\frac35+1+\frac25+\frac{16\delta}{5}
=2+\frac{16\delta}{5}.
\]
On the other hand,
\[
\frac{2M-m-k}{3}
=
\frac13\left(
\frac{70+187\delta}{10}
-1-\frac{91\delta}{10}
\right)
=2+\frac{16\delta}{5}.
\]
This proves (5), hence exact equality in the all-double excess of accepted fact `91cb5bfc71da49ac`.

The equivalent defect identity of accepted fact `0819231d0af83197` also holds term by term. Since
\[
\sum_i(u_i+v_i)=3,
\]
one has
\[
2M+2m-3\sum_i(u_i+v_i)-k
=\frac{48\delta}{5}
=3E_R.
\tag{13}
\]

For the source \(Q\)-caps, put
\[
x_i=q_{N\setminus\{i\}}=\frac15.
\]
The product inequality of accepted fact `8234d1112ec2f031` is strict:
\[
q_{123}^2m-4x_1x_2x_3
=\frac4{25}-\frac4{125}
=\frac{16}{125}>0.
\tag{14}
\]
Its square-root inequality is also strict:
\[
\sum_i\sqrt{\frac{x_i}{m}}
=\frac3{\sqrt5}
<\frac32.
\tag{15}
\]

We next verify the overlap-corrected inequality of accepted fact `2bb72698f0248a65`. Its right side is
\[
k-r_{123}+\sum_i r_i
=
\frac{91\delta}{10}-\frac{\delta}{10}+6\delta
=15\delta.
\tag{16}
\]
Its left side is
\[
\frac14\sum_i(\sqrt{p_\varnothing}+\sqrt{x_i})^2
=
\frac34\left(
\sqrt{\frac{58+187\delta}{20}}+\frac1{\sqrt5}
\right)^2
\]
\[
=
\frac{3(62+187\delta)}{80}
+\frac{3\sqrt{58+187\delta}}{20}.
\tag{17}
\]
The strict comparison between (17) and (16) is equivalent to
\[
12\sqrt{58+187\delta}
<
639\delta-186.
\tag{18}
\]
The right side is positive for \(\delta\ge1\). Squaring (18) is therefore legitimate, and after division by \(9\) it becomes
\[
16(58+187\delta)
<
(213\delta-62)^2.
\tag{19}
\]
The difference between the right and left sides of (19) is
\[
45369\delta^2-29404\delta+2916.
\]
At \(\delta=1\) this equals \(18881>0\), and its derivative
\[
90738\delta-29404
\]
is positive for every \(\delta\ge1\). Thus (19), hence the overlap inequality, holds strictly throughout the family.

All three pair masses in (3) are positive, and
\[
r_{123}=\frac{\delta}{10}>0.
\]
Therefore the middle-support implication of accepted fact `93f1cefaa4795a25` holds.

For the corrected quantities of accepted fact `ce343e749df349fe`, every positive part vanishes:
\[
e_i
=
\left(
2\delta-\frac{\delta^2}{\delta/10}
\right)_+
=(-8\delta)_+
=0.
\tag{20}
\]
Consequently the corrected product inequality reads
\[
k^3>0=4e_1e_2e_3,
\]
and the corrected square-root inequality reads
\[
0<\frac32\sqrt{k}.
\]

Finally, the quantitative inequality of accepted fact `e1361027c1dda6fa` is saturated. Indeed,
\[
r_i+\frac{2r_{123}}9
=
2\delta+\frac{\delta}{45}
=\frac{91\delta}{45},
\]
so
\[
\left(
\sum_i\sqrt{r_i+\frac{2r_{123}}9}
\right)^2
=9\cdot\frac{91\delta}{45}
=\frac{91\delta}{5}
=2k.
\]
This proves (6). The strict cyclic inequality is
\[
\left(\sum_i\sqrt{r_i}\right)^2
=9(2\delta)
=18\delta
<
\frac{91\delta}{5}
=2k,
\]
which proves (7).

Thus every listed scalar condition is satisfied for every \(\delta\ge1\), despite \(W=0\). This is an exact scalar residual family only; no affine functions, common-plane polygons, or midpoint inclusion realizing these masses are asserted.
