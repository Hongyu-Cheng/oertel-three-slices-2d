## Statement

Let
\[
\Delta=\operatorname{conv}\{e_1,e_2,e_3\},\qquad |\Delta|=1,
\]
and define
\[
\mathcal C(z;\alpha)
=\left|\{\lambda\in\Delta:z\cdot\lambda\ge\alpha\}\right|.
\]
Assume the exact \(N=3\) triangular barycentric system of accepted fact
`64760c76cadff481` in the surviving orientation of accepted fact
`09393c6edf1fc4e2`.  Thus
\[
\mathbf{1}^{\top}U=\mathbf{1}^{\top},\qquad
\mathbf{1}^{\top}V=\mathbf{1}^{\top},\qquad
\mathbf{1}^{\top}L=\mathbf{1}^{\top},
\]
\[
A=|\det U|\le B=|\det V|,\qquad
q:=\sum_{i=1}^3\sigma_i>1,
\]
and the three exact row masses are
\[
M_i=
\mathcal C(L_{i*};0)
+A\mathcal C((LU)_{i*};-\sigma_i)
+B\mathcal C((LV)_{i*};\sigma_i).
\tag{1}
\]
Put
\[
r=\frac AB,\qquad S=\frac1B,\qquad
0<r<\frac38,
\qquad
t=\frac{2(1+A+B)}9.
\tag{2}
\]

For any \(\delta>0\), define
\[
R=\delta L,\qquad \tau=\delta\sigma.
\tag{3}
\]
Suppose that for some \(p,a,b>0\) with \(p>a+b\), and some permutation
matrices \(\Pi,\Gamma\),
\[
\Pi R\Gamma=
R(p,a,b):=
\begin{pmatrix}
p&-a&-b\\
-b&p&-a\\
-a&-b&p
\end{pmatrix}.
\tag{4}
\]
Then
\[
\boxed{\quad
\max_{1\le i\le3}(M_i-t)
>
\frac{2S+r-2}{9S}.
\quad}
\tag{5}
\]
In particular, no real or rational strict feasible point of the full iff
system occurs in the chamber (4) whenever
\[
\boxed{\qquad S\ge1-\frac r2.\qquad}
\tag{6}
\]
This exclusion includes equality in (6).

## Proof

Positive scaling does not change a cap fraction:
\[
\mathcal C(\delta z;\delta\alpha)=\mathcal C(z;\alpha)
\qquad(\delta>0).
\]
Therefore (1) can be written exactly as
\[
M_i=
\mathcal C(R_{i*};0)
+A\mathcal C((RU)_{i*};-\tau_i)
+B\mathcal C((RV)_{i*};\tau_i).
\tag{7}
\]
The column sums and allocation sum in the scale-free variables are
\[
\mathbf{1}^{\top}R=\delta\mathbf{1}^{\top},
\qquad
\sum_{i=1}^3\tau_i=\delta q>\delta.
\tag{8}
\]

Row permutations only reorder the middle caps, while column permutations
only permute the three vertex values in each middle cap.  Hence the sum
of the middle caps is unchanged by the two permutations in (4).

Put
\[
d=p-a-b>0.
\]
Every row of \(R(p,a,b)\) is a permutation of \((p,-a,-b)\).  The
complete cap formula in accepted fact `64760c76cadff481` gives
\[
\mathcal C(p,-a,-b;0)
=\frac{p^2}{(p+a)(p+b)}.
\tag{9}
\]
Moreover,
\[
\begin{aligned}
9p^2-4(p+a)(p+b)
&=(a-b)^2+6d(a+b)+5d^2\\
&>0.
\end{aligned}
\tag{10}
\]
Thus every middle cap is strictly larger than \(4/9\), and
\[
\sum_{i=1}^3\mathcal C(R_{i*};0)>\frac43.
\tag{11}
\]
The chamber is nonsingular, since
\[
\det R(p,a,b)
=d\left(3a^2+3ab+3b^2+3ad+3bd+d^2\right)>0.
\tag{12}
\]
Also, comparing column sums in (4) and (8) gives \(d=\delta\), although
only positivity of these quantities is needed below.

The three small-end caps in (7) cover \(\Delta\).  Indeed, if some
\(\lambda\in\Delta\) lay outside all three closed caps, then
\[
(RU)_{i*}\lambda<-\tau_i
\qquad(i=1,2,3).
\]
Using (8) and \(\mathbf{1}^{\top}U=\mathbf{1}^{\top}\), summation would
give
\[
\delta
=\mathbf{1}^{\top}RU\lambda
<-\sum_i\tau_i
<-\delta,
\]
which is impossible.  Therefore
\[
\sum_{i=1}^3
\mathcal C((RU)_{i*};-\tau_i)\ge1.
\tag{13}
\]
The three large-end cap fractions in (7) are nonnegative.  Combining
(11) and (13) yields
\[
\sum_{i=1}^3M_i>\frac43+A.
\tag{14}
\]
Consequently
\[
\max_iM_i>\frac49+\frac A3.
\tag{15}
\]
Subtracting the target in (2) gives
\[
\begin{aligned}
\max_i(M_i-t)
&>
\frac49+\frac A3-\frac{2(1+A+B)}9\\
&=\frac{2+A-2B}{9}.
\end{aligned}
\tag{16}
\]
Since \(A=r/S\) and \(B=1/S\), the last expression is exactly
\[
\frac{2+A-2B}{9}
=\frac{2S+r-2}{9S}.
\tag{17}
\]
Equations (16)-(17) prove (5).  The proof used the endpoint matrices
only through the exact column-sum identity for \(U\); the endpoint
shapes, all three allocations, both determinant signs, and all
compatibility slacks remain arbitrary.

Finally, \(S>0\), and (6) is equivalent to
\[
2S+r-2\ge0.
\]
Equation (5) therefore gives
\[
\max_i(M_i-t)>0.
\]
At the boundary \(S=1-r/2\), the right side of (5) is exactly zero,
but the left side is still strictly positive because (10)-(11) are
strict.  Hence at least one row violates the required strict inequality
\(M_i<t\), both on the boundary and above it.  This proves the claimed
cyclic-chamber exclusion.

As an exact algebra audit, SageMath 10.8 verified
\[
\frac49+\frac{r}{3S}
-\frac{2(1+r/S+1/S)}9
=\frac{2S+r-2}{9S}
\]
in \(\mathbb Q(r,S)\), and returned zero after substituting
\(S=1-r/2\) into the right side.  No numerical approximation is used.
