## Statement

Assume the canonical finite-interior common-plane hypotheses and exact-cell
convention of accepted fact 0e51a47a9add571f and the actual dominant
all-double counterexample hypotheses of accepted facts
2f637c4da2df28f1 and 174b54e9843ef8fc. Thus \(A_0,A_2,K\) are nonempty
two-dimensional convex polytopes,
\[
\frac{A_0+A_2}{2}\subseteq K,
\]
the nonconstant affine functions \(f_1,f_2,f_3\) have pairwise nonparallel
linear parts and satisfy
\[
f_1+f_2+f_3=-1,
\]
and \(L_1+L_2+L_3<-1\). The exact cells are
\[
P_J=A_0\cap
\{f_j\leq L_j\ (j\in J),\ f_j>L_j\ (j\notin J)\},
\]
\[
Q_H=A_2\cap
\{f_j\leq-L_j\ (j\in H),\ f_j>-L_j\ (j\notin H)\},
\]
\[
R_G=K\cap
\{f_j\leq0\ (j\in G),\ f_j>0\ (j\notin G)\}.
\]
Write
\[
M=|A_0|,\qquad m=|A_2|,\qquad k=|K|,
\qquad p_\varnothing=|P_\varnothing|,
\]
\[
q=|Q_{123}|,\qquad
x_i=|Q_{N\setminus\{i\}}|,
\qquad
m=q+x_1+x_2+x_3.
\]
The positive \(Q\)-support is exactly
\[
\{123,12,13,23\},
\]
and the dominant index is labeled so that
\[
x_1>\frac{5m}{9}.
\tag{1}
\]
Accepted facts 2f637c4da2df28f1 and 174b54e9843ef8fc give
\[
C_1:=Q_{23}
=A_2\cap\{f_1>-L_1\},
\qquad |C_1|=x_1,
\tag{2}
\]
and \(p_\varnothing>0\). Put
\[
\phi=f_1+L_1,\qquad
\omega_1=\max_{A_2}\phi-\min_{A_2}\phi.
\]
Let \(g_{C_1}\) be the centroid of
\[
\overline C_1=A_2\cap\{\phi\geq0\},
\]
and define
\[
\eta_1=\phi(g_{C_1}),\qquad
C_1^\sharp=\overline C_1\cap\{\phi\geq\eta_1\}.
\tag{3}
\]
Then
\[
\eta_1\geq
\frac{9x_1-5m}{12x_1}\,\omega_1>0,
\qquad
|C_1^\sharp|\geq\frac{4x_1}{9}.
\tag{4}
\]

Let
\[
P=\overline{P_\varnothing},
\qquad
S_1=\frac{P+C_1^\sharp}{2}.
\tag{5}
\]
Then
\[
S_1\subseteq
K\cap\left\{f_1\geq\frac{\eta_1}{2}\right\},
\qquad
|S_1|\geq
\sigma_1:=
\frac14\left(
\sqrt{p_\varnothing}+\frac23\sqrt{x_1}
\right)^2.
\tag{6}
\]

For \(i=2,3\), record the shifted transverse support intervals
\[
a_i^-=\min_P(f_i-L_i),\qquad
a_i^+=\max_P(f_i-L_i),
\tag{7}
\]
\[
b_i^-=\min_{C_1^\sharp}(-f_i-L_i),\qquad
b_i^+=\max_{C_1^\sharp}(-f_i-L_i).
\tag{8}
\]
They satisfy
\[
0\leq a_i^-<a_i^+,\qquad
0\leq b_i^-<b_i^+.
\tag{9}
\]
Define
\[
\Omega_i
=(a_i^+-a_i^-)+(b_i^+-b_i^-)>0,
\tag{10}
\]
\[
\theta_i
=
\min\left\{
1,\,
\max\left\{
0,\,
\frac{b_i^+-a_i^-}{\Omega_i}
\right\}
\right\},
\qquad i=2,3,
\tag{11}
\]
and
\[
\chi_{23}=
\left[\theta_2^2+\theta_3^2-1\right]_+.
\tag{12}
\]
Then the exact \(R_{23}\)-part of the deep midpoint body obeys
\[
\boxed{\quad
\left|
S_1\cap\{f_2\leq0,\ f_3\leq0\}
\right|
\geq
\chi_{23}|S_1|
\geq
\chi_{23}\sigma_1.
\quad}
\tag{13}
\]
Since \(f_1>0\) throughout \(S_1\), this gives the exact-cell charge
\[
\boxed{\quad
r_{23}\geq\chi_{23}|S_1|
\geq\chi_{23}\sigma_1.
\quad}
\tag{14}
\]
In particular, the explicit support condition
\[
\boxed{\quad
\theta_2^2+\theta_3^2>1
\quad}
\tag{15}
\]
forces a positive fraction of \(S_1\) into \(R_{23}\). A stronger
endpoint-separation condition is
\[
a_2^+\leq b_2^-,
\qquad
a_3^+\leq b_3^-.
\tag{16}
\]
Under (16), \(\theta_2=\theta_3=1\), the whole body \(S_1\) lies in
\(R_{23}\) up to null cutting-line boundaries, and
\[
r_{23}\geq|S_1|\geq\sigma_1.
\tag{17}
\]

Use the canonical notation
\[
h=\frac{2(M+m+k)}9,\qquad
u_i=\sum_{J\ni i}|P_J|,\qquad
v_i=\sum_{H\ni i}|Q_H|,\qquad
c_i=\sum_{G\ni i}r_G,
\]
\[
U=u_1+u_2+u_3,\qquad
E_R=r_{12}+r_{13}+r_{23}+2r_{123}.
\]
The three crossings are \(u_i+v_i+c_i=h\). Therefore (14) has the exact
crossing and budget consequences
\[
\boxed{\quad
h-u_i-m+x_i
=c_i
\geq r_{23}
\geq\chi_{23}\sigma_1,
\qquad i=2,3,
\quad}
\tag{18}
\]
\[
\boxed{\quad
E_R\geq\chi_{23}\sigma_1.
\quad}
\tag{19}
\]
The strict inequalities in accepted facts 174b54e9843ef8fc and
2f637c4da2df28f1 consequently sharpen to
\[
\boxed{\quad
M>
3m+3(U+q)+3\chi_{23}\sigma_1,
\quad}
\tag{20}
\]
\[
\boxed{\quad
k>
2m+3q+3U+3\chi_{23}\sigma_1.
\quad}
\tag{21}
\]
No claim is made that the accepted one-sided centroid-margin bounds alone
force (15), and no branch closure is asserted.

All minima and maxima above are taken on compact closures. Replacing an
exact open cell by its closure changes only subsets of the cutting lines
\(f_i=L_i\), \(f_i=-L_i\), or \(f_i=0\). Those lines have planar area zero
by 0e51a47a9add571f, so every area and exact-cell charge above respects the
canonical weak/strict convention.

## Proof

Accepted fact 174b54e9843ef8fc places the centroid of \(A_0\) in the strict
cell \(P_\varnothing\). Since that centroid is an interior point of the
two-dimensional body \(A_0\), it follows that \(p_\varnothing>0\), and
\(P=\overline{P_\varnothing}\) is a two-dimensional compact convex body.
Accepted fact 2f637c4da2df28f1 gives (1)--(2), with complementary closed cap
\[
A_2\cap\{\phi\leq0\}
\quad\text{of area}\quad m-x_1<\frac{4m}{9}.
\tag{22}
\]

Let \(g_2\) be the centroid of \(A_2\), and let \(g_{\rm co}\) be the
centroid of the complementary cap in (22). Accepted fact
f1096f0628017701 gives
\[
\phi(g_2)\geq
\frac{9x_1-5m}{12m}\,\omega_1.
\tag{23}
\]
Decomposing the \(\phi\)-first moment across the cut gives
\[
m\phi(g_2)
=x_1\phi(g_{C_1})+(m-x_1)\phi(g_{\rm co})
\leq x_1\eta_1,
\tag{24}
\]
because \(\phi(g_{\rm co})\leq0\). Equations (23)--(24) prove the first
inequality in (4).

The closed halfplane \(\{\phi\geq\eta_1\}\) contains the centroid of the
two-dimensional convex body \(\overline C_1\). The planar centroid-halfplane
bound used in 2f637c4da2df28f1 and f1096f0628017701 therefore gives
\[
|C_1^\sharp|\geq\frac49|\overline C_1|
=\frac{4x_1}{9},
\]
proving the second assertion in (4).

Every \(p\in P\) has \(f_1(p)\geq L_1\), and every
\(c\in C_1^\sharp\) has \(f_1(c)\geq-L_1+\eta_1\). Hence
\[
f_1\left(\frac{p+c}{2}\right)\geq\frac{\eta_1}{2}>0.
\tag{25}
\]
The common-plane midpoint inclusion gives \(S_1\subseteq K\). Planar
Brunn--Minkowski gives
\[
|S_1|^{1/2}
\geq
\frac{\sqrt{|P|}+\sqrt{|C_1^\sharp|}}2
\geq
\frac{\sqrt{p_\varnothing}+(2/3)\sqrt{x_1}}2.
\]
This proves (6).

For \(i=2,3\), the weak endpoint inequalities on the closures give
\[
f_i-L_i\geq0\quad\text{on }P,
\qquad
-f_i-L_i\geq0\quad\text{on }C_1^\sharp.
\]
Both bodies are two-dimensional, and \(f_i\) is nonconstant, so the support
intervals have positive lengths. This proves (9)--(10).

Support extrema are additive under Minkowski addition. Therefore
\[
\min_{S_1}f_i
=\frac{a_i^- - b_i^+}{2},
\qquad
\max_{S_1}f_i
=\frac{a_i^+ - b_i^-}{2},
\tag{26}
\]
and the \(f_i\)-support width of \(S_1\) is \(\Omega_i/2\). If both open
sides of \(f_i=0\) meet \(S_1\), the normalized fraction of the support
interval lying on the side \(f_i\leq0\) is
\[
\frac{-\min_{S_1}f_i}
{\max_{S_1}f_i-\min_{S_1}f_i}
=\frac{b_i^+-a_i^-}{\Omega_i}
=\theta_i.
\tag{27}
\]
If \(\max_{S_1}f_i\leq0\), the corresponding fraction is \(1\); if
\(\min_{S_1}f_i\geq0\), it is \(0\). Thus the clipping in (11) covers all
support positions, including endpoint contact.

Apply the exact feasibility lower bound
\[
s^2\leq\rho
\]
from accepted fact f1096f0628017701 to the convex body \(S_1\), the affine
cut \(f_i=0\), and the support fraction \(s=\theta_i\). The two exterior
support cases just audited give the same inequality directly. Hence, for
\[
N_i=S_1\cap\{f_i\leq0\},
\qquad i=2,3,
\]
one has
\[
|N_i|\geq\theta_i^2|S_1|.
\tag{28}
\]
Bonferroni's inequality inside the common ambient set \(S_1\) gives
\[
\begin{aligned}
|N_2\cap N_3|
&\geq |N_2|+|N_3|-|S_1|\\
&\geq
(\theta_2^2+\theta_3^2-1)|S_1|.
\end{aligned}
\]
Taking the positive part and using (6) proves (13).

By (25), every point in \(N_2\cap N_3\) has
\[
f_1>0,\qquad f_2\leq0,\qquad f_3\leq0.
\]
It therefore belongs to the exact middle cell \(R_{23}\) under
0e51a47a9add571f. This proves (14). If (16) holds, then (26) gives
\(\max_{S_1}f_2,\max_{S_1}f_3\leq0\), proving (17).

Under all-double \(Q\)-support,
\[
v_i=m-x_i,\qquad i=2,3.
\]
Also \(c_2\geq r_{23}\), \(c_3\geq r_{23}\), and
\(E_R\geq r_{23}\). Substitution in the crossings proves (18), while
(14) proves (19). Accepted fact 174b54e9843ef8fc gives
\[
M>3m+3(U+q+E_R),
\]
and accepted fact 2f637c4da2df28f1 gives
\[
k>2m+3q+3U+3E_R.
\]
Using (19) in these two strict inequalities proves (20)--(21).
