## Statement

Let \(K,A_2\subset\mathbb R^2\) be compact convex bodies with nonempty interiors, and let \(\widehat P\subset\mathbb R^2\) be a compact convex body. Put
\[
p=|\widehat P|,\qquad r=|A_2|.
\]
Let \(f_i,f_\ell,f_j\) be affine functions such that
\[
f_i+f_\ell+f_j=-1,\qquad \nabla f_j\ne0,
\]
and let \(a_i,a_\ell\in\mathbb R\). Assume
\[
\widehat P\subseteq\{f_i\ge a_i,\ f_\ell\ge a_\ell\},
\qquad
\frac{\widehat P+A_2}{2}\subseteq K.
\]
Use the closed endpoint cells
\[
\begin{aligned}
Q&=A_2\cap\{f_i\ge-a_i,\ f_\ell\ge-a_\ell\},\\
B_i&=A_2\cap\{f_i\le-a_i,\ f_\ell\ge-a_\ell\},\\
B_\ell&=A_2\cap\{f_i\ge-a_i,\ f_\ell\le-a_\ell\},\\
R&=A_2\cap\{f_i\le-a_i,\ f_\ell\le-a_\ell\},
\end{aligned}
\]
and define
\[
D_Q=\frac{\widehat P+Q}{2},\qquad
D_i=\frac{\widehat P+B_i}{2},\qquad
D_\ell=\frac{\widehat P+B_\ell}{2},\qquad
D_R=\frac{\widehat P+R}{2},
\qquad
c_j=|K\cap\{f_j\le0\}|.
\]
Empty cells have empty midpoint bodies. Put
\[
E=|D_i\cap\{f_j>0\}|+
|D_\ell\cap\{f_j>0\}|.
\]
Write
\[
n=\frac{\nabla f_j}{\|\nabla f_j\|}
\]
and choose a unit vector \(u\perp n\). For a nonempty compact set \(X\), write
\[
\operatorname{wid}_u(X)
=\max_{x\in X}u\mathbin{\cdot}x-\min_{x\in X}u\mathbin{\cdot}x.
\]
If \(R\ne\varnothing\), define
\[
d_R=
\frac{
\left[
\frac12\left(
\max_{\widehat P}f_j+\max_R f_j
\right)
\right]_+
}{\|\nabla f_j\|},
\qquad
W_R=
\frac{\operatorname{wid}_u(\widehat P)+
\operatorname{wid}_u(R)}2,
\]
where \([x]_+=\max\{x,0\}\). If \(R=\varnothing\), set
\[
d_R=W_R=0.
\]
Then
\[
\boxed{
E\ge
\left[
\frac14(\sqrt p+\sqrt r)^2-c_j-d_RW_R
\right]_+.
}
\]

The Alternative 1 objects of accepted fact `879a3c45b5f50670`, with \(\widehat P=\overline P\), satisfy these hypotheses because affine cutting lines have area zero and \((A_0+A_2)/2\subseteq K\).

At
\[
p=\frac{97}{100},\qquad
r=\frac1{16},\qquad
c_j=\frac{29}{80},
\]
the conclusion is
\[
E\ge
\left[
\frac{20\sqrt{97}-167}{1600}-d_RW_R
\right]_+.
\]
Consequently this lemma forces \(E>0\) precisely under the additional strict estimate
\[
d_RW_R<
\frac{20\sqrt{97}-167}{1600}.
\]
More generally, it forces \(E\ge\eta>0\) from
\[
d_RW_R\le
\frac{20\sqrt{97}-167}{1600}-\eta.
\]

## Proof

The four closed cells cover \(A_2\). Their overlaps lie on the two cutting lines, which have planar area zero. Hence
\[
U:=\frac{\widehat P+A_2}{2}
=D_Q\cup D_i\cup D_\ell\cup D_R.
\]
If \(z=(x+y)/2\in D_Q\), then
\[
f_i(z)=\frac{f_i(x)+f_i(y)}2\ge0,
\qquad
f_\ell(z)=\frac{f_\ell(x)+f_\ell(y)}2\ge0.
\]
Therefore
\[
f_j(z)=-1-f_i(z)-f_\ell(z)\le-1,
\]
so
\[
D_Q\cap\{f_j>0\}=\varnothing.
\]
It follows, without any disjointness assumption on the remaining midpoint bodies, that
\[
U\cap\{f_j>0\}
\subseteq
\bigl(D_i\cap\{f_j>0\}\bigr)
\cup
\bigl(D_\ell\cap\{f_j>0\}\bigr)
\cup
\bigl(D_R\cap\{f_j>0\}\bigr).
\]
Subadditivity of area gives
\[
|U\cap\{f_j>0\}|
\le E+|D_R\cap\{f_j>0\}|.
\tag{1}
\]

Compatibility gives \(U\subseteq K\), and therefore
\[
|U\cap\{f_j\le0\}|\le c_j.
\]
The two halfplanes \(\{f_j\le0\}\) and \(\{f_j>0\}\) partition \(U\), so
\[
|U\cap\{f_j>0\}|\ge |U|-c_j.
\tag{2}
\]
Planar Brunn--Minkowski applied to \(\widehat P\) and \(A_2\) yields
\[
|U|
\ge\frac14(\sqrt p+\sqrt r)^2.
\tag{3}
\]

It remains to bound the last term in (1). If \(R=\varnothing\), then \(D_R=\varnothing\), and the asserted bound is immediate. Suppose \(R\ne\varnothing\). Write
\[
f_j(x)=\nabla f_j\mathbin{\cdot}x+\beta.
\]
Minkowski additivity of an affine function gives
\[
\max_{z\in D_R}f_j(z)
=
\frac12\left(
\max_{x\in\widehat P}f_j(x)+
\max_{y\in R}f_j(y)
\right).
\tag{4}
\]
If the value in (4) is nonpositive, then
\[
D_R\cap\{f_j>0\}=\varnothing
\]
and \(d_R=0\). If it is positive, the orthonormal normal coordinate \(n\mathbin{\cdot}x\) ranges over an interval of length at most \(d_R\) on
\[
D_R\cap\{f_j>0\}.
\]
Its tangential coordinate \(u\mathbin{\cdot}x\) ranges over an interval of length at most
\[
\operatorname{wid}_u(D_R)
=
\frac{\operatorname{wid}_u(\widehat P)+
\operatorname{wid}_u(R)}2
=W_R.
\tag{5}
\]
The map \(x\mapsto(n\mathbin{\cdot}x,u\mathbin{\cdot}x)\) preserves area. Equations (4)-(5) place the cap inside a rectangle of side lengths \(d_R,W_R\), including the cases \(d_R=0\) or \(W_R=0\). Thus
\[
|D_R\cap\{f_j>0\}|\le d_RW_R.
\tag{6}
\]

Combining (1), (2), (3), and (6) gives
\[
E\ge
\frac14(\sqrt p+\sqrt r)^2-c_j-d_RW_R.
\]
Since \(E\ge0\), taking the positive part proves the boxed inequality.

For the exact calibration,
\[
\begin{aligned}
\frac14\left(
\sqrt{\frac{97}{100}}+\sqrt{\frac1{16}}
\right)^2-\frac{29}{80}
&=
\frac{97}{400}+\frac1{64}+\frac{\sqrt{97}}{80}
-\frac{29}{80}\\
&=\frac{20\sqrt{97}-167}{1600}.
\end{aligned}
\]
This number is positive because
\[
(20\sqrt{97})^2=38800>27889=167^2.
\]
The stated threshold consequences follow directly.
