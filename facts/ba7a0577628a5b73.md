## Statement

\[
p_j=X,\qquad p_\ell=Y,\qquad p_k=1-X-Y,
\]
with coordinate-area factor \(\Delta=1\).  The complete \(A_0\) data of accepted fact `d1a8755c0cc50d14`,
\[

\]
\[
\alpha=\frac1{100},\quad J=\frac1{1000},\quad
\beta=\frac{199}{1000},\quad H=\frac45,\quad
\gamma=\tau=\frac1{1000},\quad K=\frac1{1000},
\]
are realized by one compact two-dimensional rational convex polygon.

In counterclockwise cyclic order, its vertices are
\[
\begin{aligned}
v_1&=\left(0,\frac1{100}\right),&

v_3&=\left(\frac35,-\frac{13401}{803000}\right),&

For \(A=\operatorname{conv}\{v_1,\ldots,v_{10}\}\), the three full sections are exactly

## Proof

First certify the complete convex hull.  For the oriented edge \(v_i v_{i+1}\), with indices modulo \(10\), let
\[
\ell_i(X,Y)=A_iX+B_iY+C_i.
\]
The following table gives a primitive integer triple \((A_i,B_i,C_i)\) whose zero set is the line through that edge.  The last column is the exact minimum of \(\ell_i(v_r)\) over all \(r\notin\{i,i+1\}\).
\[
\begin{array}{c|r r r|c|c}
i&A_i&B_i&C_i&\min\ell_i(v_r)&\text{attained at}\\ \hline
1&1000&19900&-199&993/100&v_{10}\\
2&13401000&322003000&-2666799&553231&v_1\\
3&-5897500&161403000&6232101&17041061/200&v_5\\
4&-12750&-12250&12751&1/2&v_6\\
5&-24500&-25500&24499&1&v_4\\
6&-1000&-2000&999&47/100&v_5\\
7&-517267000&-3323340000&516749733&2288806&v_6\\
8&444007&-3330000&36630&82805993/50000&v_{10}\\
9&25000&-1000&11&1&v_1\\
10&2500&100&-1&1/10&v_9
\end{array}
\]
Direct substitution gives
\[
\ell_i(v_i)=\ell_i(v_{i+1})=0,
\qquad
\ell_i(v_r)\ge\min\ell_i(v_r)>0
\quad(r\notin\{i,i+1\}).
\]
Thus every listed edge is globally supporting, with all other vertices strictly in its left half-plane.  Hence the displayed cyclic list is the complete boundary of a two-dimensional convex rational decagon.

The values of the three cutting forms at the vertices are
\[
\begin{array}{c|c|c|c}
r&X(v_r)&Y(v_r)&p_k(v_r)\\ \hline
1&0&1/100&99/100\\
2&199/1000&0&801/1000\\

9&0&11/1000&989/1000\\
10&-1/50000&21/2000&12369/12500
\end{array}
\]
Together with the certified boundary order, the sign changes in this table show
\[
A\cap\{X=0\}=[v_1,v_9],\qquad
A\cap\{Y=0\}=[v_2,v_7],\qquad
A\cap\{p_k=0\}=[v_4,v_6].
\]
These are exactly the three stated full sections.  Their offsets and lengths are
\[

\]
\[

\[

\]

The same sign table gives the four closed cell polygons
\[
\overline{P_j}=\operatorname{conv}(v_9,v_{10},v_1),
\]
\[
\overline{P_0}=\operatorname{conv}(v_1,v_2,v_7,v_8,v_9),
\]
\[
\overline{P_\ell}=\operatorname{conv}(v_2,v_3,v_4,v_6,v_7),
\]
\[
\overline{P_{k\ell}}=\operatorname{conv}(v_4,v_5,v_6).

All points in the relative interiors of these four pieces have exactly the required strict sign patterns.  The pieces cover \(A\), meet only on cutting-line segments, and lie in no unsupported chamber.

It remains to check their areas.  The two small triangles have
\[
\left|\operatorname{conv}(v_9,v_{10},v_1)\right|
=\frac12\left(\frac1{1000}\right)\left(\frac1{50000}\right)

For the \(P_0\) polygon, split off the triangle

\[
\left|\operatorname{conv}(v_1,v_2,v_7,v_9)\right|
=\frac{8999}{2000000},
\]

\left|\operatorname{conv}(v_7,v_8,v_9)\right|

\]
Therefore
\[
|P_0|
=\frac{8999}{2000000}
+ \frac{1442021}{40000000}
=\frac{1622001}{40000000}.
\]
For the \(P_\ell\) polygon, split off
\(\operatorname{conv}(v_2,v_3,v_4)\).  Again by exact shoelace evaluation,
\[
\left|\operatorname{conv}(v_2,v_4,v_6,v_7)\right|

\]
\[

=\frac{12599}{2000000},
\]
and hence
\[

\]
As a final partition check,
\[
|A|
=\frac{9530009}{200000000}
=a+b+c+d.
\]
Since cutting lines have area zero, every unsupported exact cell has area zero.  This completes the simultaneous full-section, convex-hull, and exact-area realization.
