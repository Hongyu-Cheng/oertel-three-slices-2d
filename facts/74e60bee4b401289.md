## Statement

Let
\[
A_0=\operatorname{conv}\left\{
\left(\frac23,-\frac13\right),
\left(-\frac13,\frac23\right),
\left(-\frac13,-\frac13\right)\right\},
\]
\[
A_1=\operatorname{conv}\left\{
\left(\frac34,-\frac7{24}\right),
\left(-\frac14,\frac7{12}\right),
\left(-\frac12,-\frac7{24}\right)\right\},
\]
and
\[
A_2=\operatorname{conv}\left\{
\left(-\frac16,\frac12\right),
\left(-\frac8{21},-\frac14\right),
\left(\frac13,-\frac14\right)\right\}.
\]
These are compatible rational nondegenerate triangles, \(A_0\) is the larger outer slice, and their normalized areas lie in the strict residual strip of accepted fact `15bb2c1680a0254e`.

For \(q\ne0\), put
\[
F_{i,q}(c)=\left|A_i\cap\{x:q\cdot x\le c\}\right|,
\qquad
h_i(q)=\max_{x\in A_i}q\cdot x .
\]
There is no \(z\in A_1\), indeed no \(z\in\mathbb R^2\), such that for every \(q\ne0\),
\[
F_{1,q}(q\cdot z)+
\max\left\{
\min\left(a_2,F_{0,q}(2q\cdot z-h_2(q))\right),
\min\left(a_0,F_{2,q}(2q\cdot z-h_0(q))\right)
\right\}
\ge \frac{2T}{9}.
\]
Nevertheless,
\[
h_S((1,0))\ge\frac{217}{720}
>\frac{589}{2016}=\frac{2T}{9},
\]
so the true mixed depth region at level \(2T/9\) is nonempty.

## Proof

The matrix
\[
\begin{pmatrix}5/4&1/4\\0&7/8\end{pmatrix}
\]
maps \(A_0\) onto \(A_1\). Hence \(A_1\) is a genuinely sheared, non-homothetic triangle with centroid \(0\). Direct determinants give
\[
a_0=\frac12,\qquad a_1=\frac{35}{64},\qquad a_2=\frac{15}{56},
\qquad
T=\frac{589}{448},\qquad \frac{2T}{9}=\frac{589}{2016}.
\tag{1}
\]
The centroid of \(A_2\) is \((-1/14,0)\), so the small slice is off-center.

We first check compatibility exactly. The three facet inequalities of \(A_1\) are
\[
\frac78x+y\le\frac{35}{96},\qquad
-\frac78x+\frac14y\le\frac{35}{96},\qquad
-\frac54y\le\frac{35}{96}.
\tag{2}
\]
Subtracting the support values of \(A_0\) from twice the right sides in (2) shows that
\[
K:=2A_1\ominus A_0
=\left\{v:A_0+v\subseteq2A_1\right\}
\]
is cut out by
\[
\frac78x+y\le\frac{17}{48},\qquad
-\frac78x+\frac14y\le\frac{13}{48},\qquad
-\frac54y\le\frac5{16}.
\tag{3}
\]
The three pairwise intersections in (3) give
\[
K=\operatorname{conv}\left\{
\left(-\frac8{21},-\frac14\right),
\left(\frac{29}{42},-\frac14\right),
\left(-\frac16,\frac12\right)\right\}.
\tag{4}
\]
The first and second vertices of \(A_2\) are vertices of \(K\), while
\[
\left(\frac13,-\frac14\right)
=\frac13\left(-\frac8{21},-\frac14\right)
+\frac23\left(\frac{29}{42},-\frac14\right).
\]
Thus \(A_2\subseteq K\), equivalently \((A_0+A_2)/2\subseteq A_1\). Accepted fact `8ad0785e5b4ae9ef` therefore supplies the required compact three-slice polytope.

For the residual-strip check, \(M=a_0\), \(m=a_2\), and
\[
r=\frac mM=\frac{15}{28},\qquad s=\frac{a_1}{M}=\frac{35}{32}.
\tag{5}
\]
Here
\[
r-\frac9{25}=\frac{123}{700}>0,
\qquad
(2-r)-s=\frac{83}{224}>0.
\tag{6}
\]
Moreover,
\[
2s-3r+1=\frac{177}{112}>0,
\qquad
\left(\frac{177}{112}\right)^2-4r=\frac{4449}{12544}>0,
\tag{7}
\]
so \(s>(3r+2\sqrt r-1)/2\). Finally,
\[
5-5r=\frac{65}{28}>0,
\qquad
\left(\frac{65}{28}\right)^2-4r=\frac{2545}{784}>0.
\tag{8}
\]
Thus \(5r+2\sqrt r-5<0\), which is exactly \(r<(27-2\sqrt{26})/25\). Equations (5)-(8), together with the piecewise formulas in accepted fact `15bb2c1680a0254e`, place this area pair strictly inside its residual strip.

We now refute the two-routing witness. For fixed \(q\), define the nondecreasing function
\[
V_q(c)=F_{1,q}(c)+
\max\left\{
\min\left(a_2,F_{0,q}(2c-h_2(q))\right),
\min\left(a_0,F_{2,q}(2c-h_0(q))\right)
\right\}.
\tag{9}
\]
Take
\[
q_1=(3,5),\qquad q_2=(-3,-1),\qquad q_3=(-1,-3).
\tag{10}
\]
Positive rescaling of \(q\) rescales its support and cap threshold by the
same factor and therefore leaves every cap set in (9) unchanged. Thus
testing \(q_i\) is exactly equivalent to testing the unit direction
\(q_i/\lVert q_i\rVert\) required in the registered statement.
The following table is an exact cap calculation at \(c=0\). The columns headed \(R_0,R_2\) are the two quantities inside the maximum in (9).
\[
\begin{array}{c|c|c|c|c|c|c|c}

(3,5)&2&7/3&
35287/141696&2/135&25/247968&
186931/708480&140423/4959360\\[1mm]
(-3,-1)&39/28&4/3&

\tag{11}
\]
Every last-column entry is positive.

For a fully reproducible audit of (11), clip each triangle by the indicated rational halfplane and apply the shoelace formula. For \(q_1\), the \(A_1\)-cap at \(0\), the \(A_0\)-cap at \(-h_2(q_1)=-2\), and the \(A_2\)-cap at \(-h_0(q_1)=-7/3\) have respective vertex lists

Their areas are \(35287/141696\), \(2/135\), and \(25/247968\).

For \(q_2\), the \(A_1\)-cap at \(0\) and the \(A_0\)-cap at \(-h_2(q_2)=-39/28\) have vertex lists

Their areas are \(15463/58752\) and \(529/84672\). The other threshold is \(-h_0(q_2)=-4/3<\min_{A_2}q_2\cdot x=-3/4\), so its \(A_2\)-cap has area zero.

For \(q_3\), the \(A_1\)-cap at \(0\) and the \(A_0\)-cap at \(-h_2(q_3)=-95/84\) have vertex lists

If a point \(z\) satisfied the proposed witness in all directions, then (9)-(11) and monotonicity would force
\[
q_1\cdot z>0,\qquad q_2\cdot z>0,\qquad q_3\cdot z>0.
\tag{12}
\]
But
\[
2q_1+q_2+3q_3=(0,0).
\tag{13}
\]
Multiplying (12) by \(2,1,3\) and adding contradicts (13). Thus no common center exists, even though the routing choice in (9) may depend on the direction.

It remains to identify the true mixed depth region. The origin is the centroid of both \(A_0\) and \(A_1\). Accepted fact `2b809ac6ae61991f` gives the exact centroid depth of a triangle and its barycentric lower bound, hence
\[
d_{A_0}(0)=\frac{2}{9},
\qquad
d_{A_1}(0)=\frac{35}{144}.
\tag{14}
\]
The barycentric coordinates of \(0\) in the displayed order of the vertices of \(A_2\) are
\[
\left(\frac13,\frac7{30},\frac{13}{30}\right).
\tag{15}
\]
Applying the same triangle estimate with \(r=7/30<1/3\) gives
\[
d_{A_2}(0)\ge
4\left(\frac7{30}\right)^2\frac{15}{56}
=\frac7{120}.
\tag{16}
\]

For every \(q\) and every \(c\in\mathbb R\),
\[
F_{0,q}(c)+F_{2,q}(-c)\ge\frac7{120}.
\tag{17}
\]
Indeed, if \(c\ge0\), the first term is at least \(F_{0,q}(0)\ge d_{A_0}(0)\ge7/120\); if \(c\le0\), the second is at least \(F_{2,q}(0)\ge d_{A_2}(0)\ge7/120\). Therefore the endpoint infimal convolution at \(0\) is at least \(7/120\). The exact middle-slice depth formula in accepted fact `996c3218c6534822` now yields
\[
h_S((1,0))
\ge d_{A_1}(0)+\frac7{120}
=\frac{217}{720}.
\tag{18}
\]
The two horizontal masses are
\[
a_0+a_1=\frac{67}{64},
\qquad
a_1+a_2=\frac{365}{448},
\]
so neither lowers (18). Finally,
\[
\frac{217}{720}-\frac{589}{2016}
=\frac{31}{3360}>0.
\tag{19}
\]
Thus \(0\) belongs to the true mixed depth region, completing both the counterexample verification and the requested calibration.
