## Statement

Assume the affine sign-plane hypotheses, exact-cell convention, and
midpoint compatibility of accepted fact `0e51a47a9add571f`.  Normalize
\[
m=|A_2|=1,\qquad M=|A_0|\geq1.
\tag{1}
\]
Assume \(p_\varnothing>0\), and assume that the positive \(Q\)-support
is exactly
\[
\{123,1,2,12\}.
\tag{2}
\]
Write
\[
p_J=|P_J|,\qquad q_J=|Q_J|,\qquad r_J=|R_J|,
\]
\[
u_i=\sum_{J\ni i}p_J,\qquad
v_i=\sum_{J\ni i}q_J,\qquad
c_i=\sum_{J\ni i}r_J,
\]
\[
k=|K|,\qquad
h=\frac{2(M+1+k)}9.
\tag{3}
\]
Assume the three crossings and the canonical left tails:
\[
u_i+v_i+c_i=h,\qquad
c_i+1\geq h
\quad(i=1,2,3).
\tag{4}
\]

Fix \(d>0\), and define normalized affine functions
\[
\psi_i=3d\left(f_i+\frac13\right)
\quad(i=1,2,3).
\tag{5}
\]
Then
\[
\psi_1+\psi_2+\psi_3=0.
\tag{6}
\]
Assume their linear parts are pairwise nonparallel.  Put
\[
J_\psi=
\left|\det(\nabla\psi_1,\nabla\psi_2)\right|>0,
\]
\[
g=\min\{\|\nabla\psi_1\|,\|\nabla\psi_2\|\}>0,
\qquad
H=\|\nabla(\psi_1-\psi_2)\|>0,
\tag{7}
\]
and suppose
\[
\operatorname{diam}(A_2)\leq D.
\tag{8}
\]

Let \(x_\varnothing\) be the area centroid of
\(P_\varnothing\), and let \(y_i\) be the area centroid of \(Q_i\)
for \(i=1,2\).  Define
\[
z_i=\frac{x_\varnothing+y_i}{2}
\in
\frac{P_\varnothing+\overline{Q_i}}2
\subseteq K,
\tag{9}
\]
and define
\[
B_U=
\frac{(\psi_1-\psi_2)(z_2)
      -(\psi_1-\psi_2)(z_1)}{H}.
\tag{10}
\]
Then \(B_U>0\).  With
\[
A=\frac{2DH}{g},
\qquad
C=\frac{H^2}{2J_\psi},
\tag{11}
\]
one has
\[
\boxed{\qquad
C B_U^2+A B_U>\frac15.
\qquad}
\tag{12}
\]
Equivalently,
\[
\boxed{\qquad
B_U>
\frac{\sqrt{A^2+4C/5}-A}{2C}>0.
\qquad}
\tag{13}
\]

For a family of such configurations, additionally assume the uniform
bounds
\[
D\leq D_0,\qquad
H\leq H_0,\qquad
J_\psi^{-1}\leq J_0,\qquad
g^{-1}\leq g_0.
\tag{14}
\]
Put
\[
A_*=2D_0H_0g_0,\qquad
C_*=\frac{H_0^2J_0}{2}.
\tag{15}
\]
Then every member of the family satisfies
\[
\boxed{\qquad
B_U>
\frac{\sqrt{A_*^2+4C_*/5}-A_*}{2C_*}>0.
\qquad}
\tag{16}
\]

## Proof

Set
\[
a_i=3d\left(L_i+\frac13\right),
\qquad
b_i=3d\left(\frac13-L_i\right)=2d-a_i.
\tag{17}
\]
Then
\[
P_J
=A_0\cap
\{\psi_i\leq a_i\ (i\in J),\
  \psi_i>a_i\ (i\notin J)\},
\]
\[
Q_J
=A_2\cap
\{\psi_i\leq b_i\ (i\in J),\
  \psi_i>b_i\ (i\notin J)\}.
\tag{18}
\]
The offset hypothesis in accepted fact `0e51a47a9add571f` gives
\[
\sum_i a_i=3d\left(\sum_iL_i+1\right)<0.
\tag{19}
\]
Consequently, if
\[
\delta=-\sum_i a_i,\qquad
\eta=\sum_i b_i,
\]
then
\[
\delta>0,\qquad
\eta=6d+\delta>0.
\tag{20}
\]

We first estimate the transverse first moments of \(Q_1\) and \(Q_2\).
For \(y\in Q_1\), write
\[
\alpha=b_1-\psi_1(y)\geq0,\qquad
\sigma=\psi_2(y)-b_2>0.
\]
Using (6),
\[
\psi_3(y)-b_3
=\alpha-\sigma-\eta>0.
\]
Hence
\[
(\psi_1-\psi_2)(y)
<b_1-b_2-\eta-2\sigma.
\tag{21}
\]
Similarly, for \(y\in Q_2\), write
\[
\rho=\psi_1(y)-b_1>0,\qquad
\gamma=b_2-\psi_2(y)\geq0.
\]
The inequality \(\psi_3(y)>b_3\) gives
\[
(\psi_1-\psi_2)(y)
>b_1-b_2+\eta+2\rho.
\tag{22}
\]

Let
\[
\overline\sigma
=\frac1{q_1}\int_{Q_1}(\psi_2-b_2),
\qquad
\overline\rho
=\frac1{q_2}\int_{Q_2}(\psi_1-b_1).
\tag{23}
\]
Averaging (21)-(22), using (9), and cancelling the common
\(x_\varnothing\) term gives
\[
B_U>
\frac{\eta+\overline\sigma+\overline\rho}{H}.
\tag{24}
\]

Slice \(Q_1\) by the signed-distance coordinate
\[
r=\frac{\psi_2-b_2}{\|\nabla\psi_2\|}>0.
\]
Every section has length at most \(D\).  Among all nonnegative section
length functions bounded by \(D\) and having integral \(q_1\), the
first moment is minimized by filling
\([0,q_1/D]\) with density \(D\).  Therefore
\[
\overline\sigma
\geq
\frac{\|\nabla\psi_2\|q_1}{2D}.
\tag{25}
\]
The same argument gives
\[
\overline\rho
\geq
\frac{\|\nabla\psi_1\|q_2}{2D}.
\tag{26}
\]
Combining (24)-(26) yields
\[
B_U>
\frac{\eta+g(q_1+q_2)/(2D)}H.
\tag{27}
\]
In particular,
\[
\eta<H B_U,
\qquad
q_1+q_2<\frac{2DH}{g}B_U=A B_U.
\tag{28}
\]
This also proves \(B_U>0\).

We next bound the empty endpoint cell.  The affine map
\[
y\longmapsto(\psi_1(y),\psi_2(y))
\]
has Jacobian magnitude \(J_\psi\).  By (6), the three strict
inequalities defining \(P_\varnothing\) place it inside
\[
\{\psi_1>a_1,\ \psi_2>a_2,\
  \psi_1+\psi_2<-a_3\}.
\]
In the \((\psi_1,\psi_2)\)-plane this is a right triangle with both
legs equal to
\(\delta=-a_1-a_2-a_3\).  Therefore
\[
p_\varnothing
\leq\frac{\delta^2}{2J_\psi}
<\frac{\eta^2}{2J_\psi}
<\frac{H^2}{2J_\psi}B_U^2
=C B_U^2.
\tag{29}
\]

It remains to use the crossings and tails.  From the support (2),
\[
v_1=1-q_2,\qquad
v_2=1-q_1,\qquad
v_3=q_{123}.
\tag{30}
\]
Subtracting each crossing in (4) from its left-tail inequality gives
\[
u_i+v_i\leq1.
\tag{31}
\]
Equations (30)-(31) imply
\[
u_1\leq q_2,\qquad
u_2\leq q_1.
\tag{32}
\]

Every \(P\)-cell other than
\(P_\varnothing\) and \(P_3\) has index \(1\) or \(2\).  Hence
\[
M-p_\varnothing-p_3\leq u_1+u_2.
\tag{33}
\]
Also
\[
p_3\leq u_3\leq1-v_3\leq1
\tag{34}
\]
by (31).  Put
\[
\varepsilon=p_\varnothing+q_1+q_2.
\tag{35}
\]
Equations (32)-(34) give
\[
M\leq1+\varepsilon.
\tag{36}
\]
The third crossing and (33) give
\[
h=u_3+v_3+c_3
\geq u_3
\geq p_3
\geq M-\varepsilon
\geq1-\varepsilon.
\tag{37}
\]

Accepted fact `0e51a47a9add571f` forces
\[
Q_\varnothing=R_\varnothing=\varnothing.
\]
Since the exact cells partition \(A_2\) and \(K\),
\[
\sum_{i=1}^3v_i
=\sum_{J\ne\varnothing}|J|q_J\geq1,
\qquad
\sum_{i=1}^3c_i
=\sum_{J\ne\varnothing}|J|r_J\geq k.
\tag{38}
\]
Summing the three crossings and using
\(\sum_i u_i\geq0\) gives
\[
\frac{2(M+1+k)}3=3h
\geq1+k.
\]
Thus
\[
k\leq2M-1.
\tag{39}
\]
Using (36),
\[
h=\frac{2(M+1+k)}9
\leq\frac{2M}{3}
\leq\frac23+\frac{2\varepsilon}{3}.
\tag{40}
\]
Combining (37) and (40) gives
\[
\varepsilon\geq\frac15.
\tag{41}
\]

Finally, (28), (29), and (35) give
\[
\varepsilon
<C B_U^2+A B_U.
\tag{42}
\]
Equations (41)-(42) prove (12), and solving the positive quadratic
inequality proves (13).

Under the uniform assumptions (14), one has
\[
A\leq A_*,\qquad C\leq C_*.
\]
Therefore (12) implies
\[
C_*B_U^2+A_*B_U>\frac15,
\]
and solving this quadratic proves (16).
