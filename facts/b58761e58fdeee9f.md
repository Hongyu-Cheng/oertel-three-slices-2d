## Proof

Fix \(i\in\{1,2\}\) and \(x\in X_i\).  Since
\(P\) lies in the standard simplex,
\[
0\leq x\leq1.
\tag{28}
\]
Every strict point of \(Q_i\) has \(\tau_i>1\), while the opposite
singleton \(Q_{3-i}\) has a strict point with \(\tau_i<0\).  Both
cells lie in \(D_\tau\).  Its projection \(\widehat T_i\) is a compact
interval, so its maximum satisfies
\(\widehat\beta_i>1\) and
\([0,\widehat\beta_i]\subseteq\widehat T_i\).  Thus equations
(6) and (28) give
\[
\left[\frac xs,\widehat\beta_i\right]
\subseteq\widehat T_i.
\tag{29}
\]
For
\[
x-s\widehat\beta_i\leq u\leq0,
\qquad
t_-(u)=t_+(u)=\frac{x-u}{s},
\tag{30}
\]
one has \(t_\pm(u)\in\widehat T_i\) and
\[
u+st_\pm(u)=x\in X_i.
\]
Thus (30) is an admissible continuous selector pair in the exact
two-rail formula of accepted fact `cb3cba490b10885c`.  Its integrand
is
\[
w_i^P(x)+s\,w_i^D\!\left(\frac{x-u}{s}\right)\geq0.
\]
Consequently,
\[
\begin{aligned}
\left|H_D\cap\{f_i\leq0\}\right|
&\geq\frac14\left[
(s\widehat\beta_i-x)w_i^P(x)
+s^2\int_{x/s}^{\widehat\beta_i}w_i^D(t)\,dt
\right].
\end{aligned}
\tag{31}
\]
Up to the null coordinate lines, the exact support and
\(\tau_3\leq0\) partition the last tail into the whole singleton
\(Q_i^\tau\) and the portion of \(Q_{12}^\tau\) with
\(\tau_i\geq x/s\).  Every strict point of \(Q_i\) has
\(\tau_i>1\geq x/s\).  Hence
\[
s^2\int_{x/s}^{\widehat\beta_i}w_i^D(t)\,dt
=q_i+q_{12,i}(x).
\tag{32}
\]
Taking the supremum in (31) proves (8).

Suppose \(\alpha_i>0\), and write
\[
X_i=[\alpha_i,\omega_i].
\]
Because \(P\) has positive area,
\[
0<\alpha_i<\omega_i\leq1.
\tag{33}
\]
The nonnegative function
\[
g_i(x)=(s\widehat\beta_i-x)w_i^P(x)
\]
satisfies
\[
\begin{aligned}
\max_{x\in X_i}g_i(x)
&\geq
\frac1{\omega_i-\alpha_i}
\int_{X_i}g_i(x)\,dx\\
&\geq
\frac{s\widehat\beta_i-1}
{\omega_i-\alpha_i}
\int_{X_i}w_i^P(x)\,dx\\
&\geq
\frac{(s\widehat\beta_i-1)p}{1-\alpha_i}.
\end{aligned}
\tag{34}
\]
Use (31)-(32) at a maximizer in (34) and discard only the
nonnegative \(q_{12,i}\)-term.  This proves (10).
Accepted facts `3c17eb0b21eeb63c` and
`d288193baa0edc6b` give
\[
|H_D\cap\{f_i\leq0\}|\leq c_i<U_i.
\tag{35}
\]
Equations (10)-(11) contradict (35), proving the exclusion gate.
Finally, \(\widehat\beta_i>1\), \(s>1\), and
\(\alpha_i>0\) prove (12).

For (15), apply the planar maximal-section inequality proved in
accepted fact `3c17eb0b21eeb63c` to \(P,D_r\) with weights
\(1/2,1/2\):
\[
|H_D|
\geq
\frac14(\pi_L+\delta_L)
\left(\frac p{\pi_L}+\frac d{\delta_L}\right).
\tag{36}
\]
Accepted fact `d288193baa0edc6b` gives
\[

\frac{5(M+m)}9+u_3+q_{123}.
\tag{37}

Combining (36)-(37) proves (15).
