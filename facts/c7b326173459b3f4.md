## Statement

Normalize the area of
\(\Delta=\operatorname{conv}\{e_1,e_2,e_3\}\) to one, and write
\[
\mathcal C(h;z)
=
\left|\{\lambda\in\Delta:h\lambda\geq z\}\right|
\tag{1}
\]
for a row vector \(h\in\mathbb R^3\).

Let \(0<A\leq B\), let \(L\in\mathbb R^{3\times3}\) have column
sums one and three nonconstant rows, and put
\[
c_i=\mathcal C(L_{i*};0),\qquad
\alpha_i=\frac{t-c_i}{A}.
\tag{2}
\]
Let
\(\alpha_{[1]}\leq\alpha_{[2]}\leq\alpha_{[3]}\) be the sorted
allowances.  Each of the following regions admits no real endpoint
completion:
\[
\mathcal R_0=
\left\{
0<\alpha_{[1]}\leq\frac3{10},\
\alpha_{[2]}\leq\frac9{20},\
\alpha_{[3]}\leq\frac12
\right\},
\tag{3}
\]
\[
\mathcal R_1=
\left\{
0<\alpha_{[1]}\leq\frac38,\
\alpha_{[2]}\leq\frac{23}{50},\
\alpha_{[3]}\leq\frac{12}{25}
\right\}.
\tag{4}
\]
More precisely, in either region there are no
\(U,V\in\mathbb R^{3\times3}\) and \(\sigma\in\mathbb R^3\)
satisfying
\[
\mathbf1^\top U=\mathbf1^\top,\qquad
\mathbf1^\top V=\mathbf1^\top,\qquad
|\det U|=A,\qquad |\det V|=B,
\tag{5}
\]
and all three strict rows
\[
c_i+
A\mathcal C((LU)_{i*};-\sigma_i)
+B\mathcal C((LV)_{i*};\sigma_i)
<t
\qquad(i=1,2,3).
\tag{6}
\]
Thus the conclusion remains true after adding the compatibility
inequalities on \(U,V\), and it is independent of both determinant
signs.

For the exact calibration in accepted fact
`998fb0c4cc064e88`, the point
\((A,B,\alpha_1,\alpha_2,\alpha_3)\) has an explicit open
\(\ell_\infty\) neighborhood contained in the fixed-index version
of \(\mathcal R_0\):
\[
\varepsilon_0=\frac{977}{871500},
\qquad
\max\left\{
|\widetilde A-A|,\ |\widetilde B-B|,\
\max_i|\widetilde\alpha_i-\alpha_i|
\right\}<\varepsilon_0.
\tag{7}
\]

The gate is stronger on the whole chamber slice used there.  Fix
\[
A=\frac1{10},\quad
B=\frac{10999}{10000},\quad
t=\frac{7333}{15000},
\tag{8}
\]
\[
L(x,y,z)=
\begin{pmatrix}
x&y&z\\
-999-x&751-y&246-z\\
1000&-750&-245
\end{pmatrix}.
\tag{9}
\]
Every nondegenerate point satisfying
\[
-999<x<0,\qquad y>751,\qquad z<0,\qquad
-2<x+y+z<0,
\tag{10}
\]
and \(c_1,c_2<t\) belongs to \(\mathcal R_0\).  In particular,
\(\mathcal R_0\) covers the full chamber-1 uncovered
semialgebraic cell \(\mathscr E_1\) of accepted fact
`998fb0c4cc064e88`, not only its recorded rational point.

Finally, the exact asymmetric theorem underlying (4) is
\[
\boxed{
\mathcal C(f;0)\leq\frac{23}{50},\qquad
\mathcal C(g;0)\leq\frac38
\quad\Longrightarrow\quad
\left|\{f+g\leq0\}\right|>\frac{12}{25}}
\tag{11}
\]
for all nonconstant affine \(f,g\) on \(\Delta\), including all
support boundaries.

## Proof

We first record the general transfer from a two-cap theorem to an
endpoint gate.  Suppose that constants \(P,Q,R\in(0,1)\) satisfy
\[
\mathcal C(f;0)\leq P,\qquad
\mathcal C(g;0)\leq Q
\quad\Longrightarrow\quad
\left|\{f+g\leq0\}\right|>R
\tag{12}
\]
for all nonconstant affine \(f,g\).  Suppose, after a row
permutation, that
\[
0<\alpha_1\leq P,\qquad
0<\alpha_2\leq Q,\qquad
0<\alpha_3\leq R.
\tag{13}
\]

If nonconstant affine functions \(w_1,w_2,w_3\) satisfy
\[
w_1+w_2+w_3=\kappa,
\]
let \(\psi_i\) be their unique upper-cap thresholds at levels
\[
\mathcal C(w_1;\psi_1)=P,\qquad
\mathcal C(w_2;\psi_2)=Q,\qquad
\mathcal C(w_3;\psi_3)=\alpha_3.
\tag{14}
\]
Applying (12) to \(w_1-\psi_1,w_2-\psi_2\) gives
\[
\left|\{w_3\geq\kappa-\psi_1-\psi_2\}\right|
>R\geq\alpha_3.
\]
Strict cap monotonicity and (14) imply
\[
\psi_1+\psi_2+\psi_3>\kappa.
\tag{15}
\]

Assume, for contradiction, that (5)-(6) hold.  For
\[
\nu_k=\min_jV_{kj},\quad
s_V=\sum_k\nu_k,\quad
\eta=1-s_V,\quad
Y=\frac{V-\nu\mathbf1^\top}{\eta},
\tag{16}
\]
one has \(\eta>0\), and \(Y\) is nonnegative, invertible, and
column-stochastic.  These facts follow because every column of
\(V-\nu\mathbf1^\top\) is nonnegative with sum \(\eta\), while
\(\det V\ne0\).  The three functions
\[
y_i(\lambda)=\eta(LY)_{i*}\lambda
\tag{17}
\]
are nonconstant and sum to \(\eta\).  Apply (15) to their
thresholds at \(P,Q,\alpha_3\).

Put \(e=L\nu-\sigma\).  Then
\[
\mathcal C((LV)_{i*};\sigma_i)
=\mathcal C(y_i;-e_i).
\tag{18}
\]
Dropping the nonnegative \(U\)-cap from (6) gives
\[
\mathcal C(y_i;-e_i)
<\frac AB\alpha_i\leq\alpha_i.
\tag{19}
\]
Equations (13)-(15), (19), and strict cap monotonicity give
\[
\sum_i e_i<-\eta=s_V-1.
\]
Since \(\sum_i e_i=s_V-\sum_i\sigma_i\), this proves
\[
\sum_i\sigma_i>1.
\tag{20}
\]

Apply the same normalization to \(U\):
\[
\ell_k=\min_jU_{kj},\quad
s_U=\sum_k\ell_k,\quad
\rho=1-s_U,\quad
X=\frac{U-\ell\mathbf1^\top}{\rho}.
\tag{21}
\]
The nonconstant functions
\[
z_i(\lambda)=\rho(LX)_{i*}\lambda
\]
sum to \(\rho\).  Put \(d=L\ell+\sigma\).  Dropping the
nonnegative \(V\)-cap from (6) gives
\[
\mathcal C(z_i;-d_i)<\alpha_i.
\]
Equations (13)-(15) now imply
\[
\sum_i d_i<-\rho=s_U-1.
\tag{22}
\]
But the column sums of \(L\) and (20) give
\[
\sum_i d_i=s_U+\sum_i\sigma_i>s_U+1,
\]
contradicting (22).  This proves the transfer criterion.  Notice
that endpoint compatibility was not used.

Accepted fact `be5af7e42cc954e7` proves (12) with
\[
(P,Q,R)=\left(\frac9{20},\frac3{10},\frac12\right).
\tag{23}
\]
The transfer criterion gives (3), after assigning the smallest
allowance to \(Q\), the middle allowance to \(P\), and the largest
allowance to \(R\).

For the point in accepted fact `998fb0c4cc064e88`, the exact
allowances are
\[
(\alpha_1,\alpha_2,\alpha_3)
=
\left(
\frac{2054650333}{4599001500},
\frac{418459}{934500},
\frac{260473}{871500}
\right).
\tag{24}
\]
Their margins are
\[
\frac9{20}-\alpha_1
=\frac{7450171}{2299500750},\qquad
\frac12-\alpha_2
=\frac{48791}{934500},\qquad
\frac3{10}-\alpha_3
=\varepsilon_0.
\tag{25}
\]
The last margin is the smallest.  Moreover,
\[
A-\varepsilon_0=\frac{86173}{871500}>0,
\qquad
B-A-2\varepsilon_0
=\frac{17389177}{17430000}>0.
\tag{26}
\]
Equations (24)-(26) prove the open-neighborhood claim (7).

We next prove the chamber-slice claim.  Under (10), put
\[
m=-x,\qquad n=-z,\qquad
r=-(x+y+z).
\tag{27}
\]
Then
\[
m>0,\quad n>0,\quad 0<r<2,\quad
m<999,\quad p:=m+n-r=y>751.
\tag{28}
\]
The first two rows in (9) have one positive entry.  Their exact caps are
\[
c_1=
\frac{(m+n-r)^2}
{(2m+n-r)(m+2n-r)},
\tag{29}
\]
\[
c_2=
\frac{(n+246)^2}
{(n+1245-m)(m+2n-r-505)}.
\tag{30}
\]

For any one-positive row \((-a,p,-b)\) whose negative row sum is
\(-\delta\), one has \(a+b=p+\delta\) and
\[
\mathcal C((-a,p,-b);0)
=\frac{p^2}{(p+a)(p+b)}
\geq
\frac{4p^2}{(3p+\delta)^2}.
\tag{31}
\]
Indeed,
\[
ab\leq\frac{(a+b)^2}{4}
=\frac{(p+\delta)^2}{4},
\]
which gives (31) after expanding the denominator.  The right side
of (31) increases with \(p\) and decreases with \(\delta\).

For the first row, \(p>751\) and \(0<\delta=r<2\).  For the second
row, its positive entry is \(n+246>246\), and its deficit is
\(2-r\in(0,2)\).  Hence
\[
c_1>
\frac{4\cdot751^2}{(3\cdot751+2)^2}
=\frac{2256004}{5085025}
>\frac{6583}{15000},
\tag{32}
\]
\[
c_2>
\frac{4\cdot246^2}{(3\cdot246+2)^2}
=\frac{15129}{34225}
>\frac{6583}{15000}.
\tag{33}
\]
The two final exact margins are
\[
\frac{14613617}{3051015000}>0,
\qquad
\frac{65273}{20535000}>0.
\tag{34}
\]

It remains to show that at least one cap reaches the \(9/20\)
allowance boundary.  Since
\[
t-\frac A2=\frac{6583}{15000},
\qquad
t-\frac{9A}{20}=\frac{3329}{7500},
\tag{35}
\]
the required assertion is
\[
\max\{c_1,c_2\}\geq\frac{3329}{7500}.
\tag{36}
\]
All four factors in the denominators of (29)-(30) are positive
under (28).  Therefore a strict failure of (36) is exactly the
rational polynomial system
\[
\begin{gathered}
m>0,\ n>0,\ 0<r<2,\ m<999,\ m+n-r>751,\\
7500(m+n-r)^2
<3329(2m+n-r)(m+2n-r),\\
7500(n+246)^2
<3329(n+1245-m)(m+2n-r-505).
\end{gathered}
\tag{37}
\]
Exact real cylindrical algebraic decomposition evaluated
\[

\exists(m,n,r)\in\mathbb R^3:\text{system (37)},\mathbb R
\right]
\tag{38}
\]
as `False`.  The query contained no inexact real.  WolframScript
1.13.0 with the local Wolfram Language 14.3.0 kernel returned the

We finally prove (11).  Use the complete eight-orbit
\(SS,SD,DS,DD\) parametrization and all support boundaries recorded

\tag{40}
\]

\[
\left|\{h\leq0\}\right|\leq\frac{12}{25}
\tag{41}
\]

\[
h_i<0,\quad h_j,h_k\geq0,\quad
25h_i^2\leq

\tag{42}
\]
\[
h_i,h_j\leq0<h_k,\quad
25h_k^2\geq
13(h_k-h_i)(h_k-h_j),
\tag{43}
\]
for the three choices of the distinguished index.  These are the
exact one-negative cap formula and the exact one-positive
complement formula.

Substitute (40), the two closed parameter intervals, and
\(\lambda>0\) into each of the seven predicates in every one of the

\[
\operatorname{Resolve}\!\left[

\[

\left(\frac{23}{50},\frac38,\frac{12}{25}\right)
