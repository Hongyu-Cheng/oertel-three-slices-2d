## Statement

For \(b,c,t\geq0\), define
\[
J(b,c,t)=\int_0^t
\min\{c,(2\sqrt b-\sqrt p)_+^2\}\,dp,
\]
\[
D_r(b;x,y)=J(b,1,x)+J(b,y,r)-J(b,y,x),
\]
and
\[
F(q)=\max_{0\leq t\leq q}J(q-t,1,t).
\]
Then
\[
D_r\left(q-z;\frac z2,\frac z2\right)<F(q)
\tag{1}
\]
whenever
\[
\frac{11}{18}\leq q\leq\frac23,\qquad
\frac38\leq r\leq\frac12,\qquad
r\leq\frac{16q}{17},\qquad
0\leq z\leq q.
\tag{2}
\]
In particular, the requested weak inequality holds.

## Proof

Put
\[
h_b(p)=(2\sqrt b-\sqrt p)_+^2
\]
and
\[
H_b(t)=4bt-\frac83\sqrt b\,t^{3/2}+\frac{t^2}{2}.
\tag{3}
\]
Thus \(H_b\) is an antiderivative of
\((2\sqrt b-\sqrt p)^2\) on \(0\leq p\leq4b\), and
\[
H_b(4b)=\frac83b^2.
\tag{4}
\]

First we record all clipping chambers of \(J\). If \(c=0\), then
\(J(b,c,t)=0\). Suppose \(c>0\). If \(4b\leq c\), then
\[
J(b,c,t)=
\begin{cases}
H_b(t),&0\leq t\leq4b,\\
H_b(4b),&t\geq4b.
\end{cases}
\tag{5}
\]
If \(4b\geq c\), put
\[
\alpha_c=(2\sqrt b-\sqrt c)^2.
\]
Then
\[
J(b,c,t)=
\begin{cases}
ct,&0\leq t\leq\alpha_c,\\
c\alpha_c+H_b(t)-H_b(\alpha_c),
   &\alpha_c\leq t\leq4b,\\
c\alpha_c+H_b(4b)-H_b(\alpha_c),
   &t\geq4b.
\end{cases}
\tag{6}
\]
These formulas follow by splitting the integral where \(h_b=c\) and
where \(h_b=0\). They agree at \(t=\alpha_c\), at \(t=4b\), and, when
\(4b=c\), between (5) and (6). Hence (5)-(6) also cover every degenerate
and equality case.

For the balanced split, put
\[
a=\frac z2,\qquad b=q-z.
\]
The bounds in (2) give
\[
0\leq a\leq\frac13<\frac38\leq r,
\]
so the definition of \(D_r\) becomes the exact integral identity
\[
D_r(b;a,a)
=\int_0^a\min\{1,h_b(p)\}\,dp
+ \int_a^r\min\{a,h_b(p)\}\,dp.
\tag{7}
\]
For fixed \(a\), the right side is nondecreasing in both \(b\) and \(r\).
Since \(b=q-z\leq2/3-z\) and \(r\leq1/2\), it follows that
\[
D_r(q-z;a,a)\leq B(z):=
D_{1/2}\left(\frac23-z;a,a\right),
\qquad 0\leq z\leq\frac23.
\tag{8}
\]
Notice also that the condition \(r\leq16q/17\) is redundant here, since
\[
\frac{16q}{17}\geq\frac{88}{153}>\frac12.
\]

For completeness, we now list every clipping chamber of \(B\). In this
list,
\[
b=\frac23-z,\qquad a=\frac z2,\qquad
\alpha=(2\sqrt b-1)^2,\qquad
\beta=(2\sqrt b-\sqrt a)^2,
\]
and
\[
z_*=\frac{41-4\sqrt{10}}{81}.
\tag{9}
\]
Equations (5)-(7) give
\[
B(z)=
\begin{cases}
a+a(1/2-a),
  &0\leq z\leq2/9,\\
\alpha+H_b(a)-H_b(\alpha)+a(1/2-a),
  &2/9\leq z\leq z_*,\\
\alpha+H_b(a)-H_b(\alpha)
+ a(\beta-a)+H_b(1/2)-H_b(\beta),
  &z_*\leq z\leq5/12,\\
H_b(a)+a(\beta-a)+H_b(1/2)-H_b(\beta),
  &5/12\leq z\leq4/9,\\
H_b(1/2),
  &4/9\leq z\leq13/24,\\
H_b(4b)=8b^2/3,
  &13/24\leq z\leq16/27,\\
H_b(4b)=8b^2/3,
  &16/27\leq z\leq2/3.
\end{cases}
\tag{10}
\]
The repeated last expression represents two different clipping
geometries. On the penultimate interval the support endpoint \(4b\)
lies between \(a\) and \(1/2\); on the last interval it lies at or below
\(a\).

Here are the exact transition checks for (10):
\[
\begin{array}{c|c}
z=2/9&\alpha=a\\
z=z_*&\beta=1/2\\
z=5/12&\alpha=0\\
z=4/9&\beta=a\\
z=13/24&4b=1/2\\
z=16/27&4b=a.
\end{array}
\tag{11}
\]
Moreover,
\[
\frac29<z_*<\frac5{12}<\frac49<\frac{13}{24}
<\frac{16}{27}<\frac23.
\]
The first two comparisons follow respectively from
\(23^2>16\cdot10\) and \(87^2<48^2\cdot10\).
Thus adjacent expressions in (10) agree at every transition by
(4)-(6), including all equality cases.

We next prove the uniform upper bound
\[
B(z)<\frac{37}{200}
\qquad\left(0\leq z\leq\frac23\right).
\tag{12}
\]
The union of the two strips in (7), inside
\([0,1/2]\times[0,1]\), has area
\[
a+a(1/2-a)=\frac{3z}{4}-\frac{z^2}{4}.
\]
This polynomial is increasing on \([0,2/3]\). Hence, for
\(0\leq z\leq27/100\),
\[
B(z)\leq
\frac{3z}{4}-\frac{z^2}{4}
\leq\frac{7371}{40000}
=\frac{37}{200}-\frac{29}{40000}.
\tag{13}
\]

For every \(z\), discarding the curved constraint only in the second
integral of (7) gives
\[
B(z)\leq
G(z):=J\left(\frac23-z,1,\frac z2\right)
+\frac z2\left(\frac12-\frac z2\right).
\tag{14}
\]
On \(27/100\leq z\leq5/12\), formula (6) gives
\[
G(z)=
\frac{
428-192\sqrt{6-9z}+3(82-345z)z
-48\sqrt6\sqrt{(2-3z)z^3}
}{216}.
\tag{15}
\]
Direct differentiation, using only positive square roots on this
interval, gives
\[
G''(z)=
-\frac{115}{12}
+\frac{18}{(6-9z)^{3/2}}
+\frac{2\sqrt{2/3}\,(6z-6z^2-1)}
{\sqrt z\,(2-3z)^{3/2}}.
\tag{16}
\]
On the interval under consideration,
\[
6-9z\geq\frac94,\qquad
\sqrt z\geq\frac12,\qquad
2-3z\geq\frac34,\qquad
0<6z-6z^2-1\leq\frac{11}{24}.
\]
Therefore the two positive terms after \(-115/12\) in (16) are at most
\[
\frac{16}{3}
\quad\hbox{and}\quad
\frac{44\sqrt2}{27}<3,
\]
respectively. The last strict inequality follows by squaring:
\(3872<6561\). Consequently,
\[
G''(z)<-\frac54<0
\tag{17}
\]
throughout this interval.

Put \(z_0=8/25\). Exact substitution in (15) gives
\[
G(z_0)=
\frac{7(8945-912\sqrt{78})}{33750},
\tag{18}
\]
\[
G'(z_0)=
-\frac{347}{180}
+\frac{214}{25}\sqrt{\frac2{39}}
=:s_0>0.
\tag{19}
\]
The sign in (19) is exact, because both compared terms are positive and
\[
\left(\frac{214}{25}\right)^2\frac2{39}
-\left(\frac{347}{180}\right)^2
=\frac{434819}{10530000}>0.
\]
Also
\[
\sqrt{78}>\frac{8831}{1000},
\qquad
78-\left(\frac{8831}{1000}\right)^2
=\frac{13439}{10^6},
\]
and hence
\[
\frac{37}{200}-G(z_0)
>\frac{2927}{16875000}>0.
\tag{20}
\]
By concavity, the tangent at \(z_0\) majorizes \(G\). Since \(s_0>0\),
(20) proves \(G(z)<37/200\) for
\[
\frac{27}{100}\leq z\leq z_0.
\tag{21}
\]

For the rest of the interval, put \(z_1=29/90\). Exact substitution
gives
\[
G'(z_1)=
-\frac{421}{216}
+4\sqrt{\frac{10}{31}}
-\frac{32}{135}\sqrt{\frac{58}{31}}
=:s_1.
\tag{22}
\]
The rational bounds
\[
\sqrt{\frac{10}{31}}<\frac{71}{125},
\qquad
\left(\frac{71}{125}\right)^2-\frac{10}{31}
=\frac{21}{484375},
\]
\[
\sqrt{\frac{58}{31}}>\frac{1367}{1000},
\qquad
\frac{58}{31}-\left(\frac{1367}{1000}\right)^2
=\frac{70641}{31000000}
\]
give
\[
s_1<-\frac{149}{135000}<0.
\tag{23}
\]
The tangent at \(z_1\) majorizes \(G\), and its negative slope makes it
largest on \([z_0,5/12]\) at \(z=z_0\). Its value there is
\[
U:=G(z_1)+s_1(z_0-z_1)
=\frac{
55903385-2687040\sqrt{310}-71408\sqrt{1798}
}{30132000}.
\tag{24}
\]
Finally,
\[
\sqrt{310}>\frac{8803}{500},
\qquad
310-\left(\frac{8803}{500}\right)^2
=\frac{7191}{250000},
\]
\[
\sqrt{1798}>\frac{21201}{500},
\qquad
1798-\left(\frac{21201}{500}\right)^2
=\frac{17599}{250000}.
\]
Substitution in (24) yields the exact margin
\[
\frac{37}{200}-U
>\frac{862907}{3766500000}>0.
\tag{25}
\]
Equations (17), (23), and (25) prove
\[
G(z)<\frac{37}{200}
\qquad\left(z_0\leq z\leq\frac5{12}\right).
\tag{26}
\]

It remains to check \(5/12\leq z\leq2/3\). On
\(5/12\leq z\leq16/27\), formula (5) gives
\[
G(z)=G_2(z):=
\frac{z\left(114-153z-16\sqrt6\sqrt{(2-3z)z}\right)}{72},
\tag{27}
\]
\[
G_2'(z)=
\frac{19-51z}{12}
+\frac{2\sqrt6\,\sqrt z\,(2z-1)}
{3\sqrt{2-3z}}.
\tag{28}
\]
For \(5/12\leq z\leq1/2\), both terms in (28) are negative. For
\(1/2\leq z\leq16/27\),
\[
\sqrt{\frac z{2-3z}}\leq\sqrt{\frac83},
\qquad
2z-1\leq\frac5{27},
\]
so the second term in (28) is at most \(40/81\), while the first is at
most \(-13/24\). Thus
\[
G_2'(z)\leq-\frac{13}{24}+\frac{40}{81}
=-\frac{31}{648}<0.
\tag{29}
\]

On \(16/27\leq z\leq2/3\), the support ends before \(a\), and (4) gives
\[
G(z)=G_3(z):=
\frac{32}{27}+\frac{z(-119+87z)}{36},
\tag{30}
\]
\[
G_3'(z)=\frac{174z-119}{36}\leq-\frac1{12}<0.
\tag{31}
\]
At \(z=16/27\), one has \(4b=a\), so (27) and (30) agree. Hence \(G\)
is decreasing throughout \([5/12,2/3]\). At its left endpoint,
\[
\frac{37}{200}-G\left(\frac5{12}\right)
=-\frac{3047}{28800}
+\frac5{36}\sqrt{\frac56}.
\]
Since
\[
\sqrt{\frac56}>\frac9{10},
\qquad
\frac56-\left(\frac9{10}\right)^2=\frac7{300},
\]
we obtain
\[
\frac{37}{200}-G\left(\frac5{12}\right)
>\frac{553}{28800}>0.
\tag{32}
\]
Together, (13), (14), (20)-(21), (25)-(26), and (29)-(32) prove
(12).

It remains to lower-bound \(F(q)\). The fixed choice \(t=1/4\) is
feasible for every \(q\) in (2). Since \(J(b,1,1/4)\) is nondecreasing
in \(b\), formulas (5)-(6) give
\[
\begin{aligned}
F(q)
&\geq J\left(q-\frac14,1,\frac14\right)\\
&\geq J\left(\frac{13}{36},1,\frac14\right)\\
&=\frac{15467}{7776}-\frac{\sqrt{13}}2.
\end{aligned}
\tag{33}
\]
The exact upper bound
\[
\sqrt{13}<\frac{1803}{500},
\qquad
\left(\frac{1803}{500}\right)^2-13
=\frac{809}{250000}
\]
implies
\[
\frac{15467}{7776}-\frac{\sqrt{13}}2
-\frac{37}{200}
>\frac{1039}{972000}>0.
\tag{34}
\]
Combining (8), (12), (33), and (34) gives
\[
D_r\left(q-z;\frac z2,\frac z2\right)
\leq B(z)
<\frac{37}{200}
<F(q).
\]
This proves (1). The two strict rational margins also show that equality
never occurs anywhere on the closed parameter box, including every
transition and boundary case.
