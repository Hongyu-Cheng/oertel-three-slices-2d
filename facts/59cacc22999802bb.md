## Statement

Fix
\[
A=\frac{17}{100},\qquad B=\frac{29}{25},\qquad
L=\begin{pmatrix}
-279&457&-206\\
-279&-206&457\\
559&-250&-250
\end{pmatrix}.
\]
There are no real column-stochastic matrices \(U,V\) and no real \(\sigma\in\mathbb R^3\) satisfying
\[
|\det U|=A,\qquad |\det V|=B,
\qquad
\min_jU_{kj}+\min_jV_{kj}\ge0
\quad(k=1,2,3),
\]
together with
\[
\mathcal C((LU)_{1*};-\sigma_1)
=\mathcal C((LU)_{2*};-\sigma_2)=\frac12,
\qquad
\mathcal C((LU)_{3*};-\sigma_3)=\frac{401}{1700},
\]
and
\[
\mathcal C((LV)_{i*};\sigma_i)=\frac1{11600}
\qquad(i=1,2,3).
\]
Thus, a fortiori, there is no requested rational lift with \(\sum_i\sigma_i>1\), endpoint-centroid incidences \(I_0=\{1,2\}\) and \(I_2=\varnothing\), and the three strict original row masses.

## Proof

Let \(\Delta\) be the normalized barycentric triangle of accepted fact `64760c76cadff481`. The fixed matrix has column sums one and
\[
\det L=536367\ne0.
\]
Suppose for contradiction that \(U,V,\sigma\) satisfy the displayed conditions. Put
\[
z=L^{-1}\sigma
\]
and define three triangles
\[
P_0=L(U\Delta+z),\qquad
P_1=L\Delta,\qquad
P_2=L(V\Delta-z).
\tag{1}
\]
Normalize their common planar area scale by \(|P_1|=1\). Translation does not change area, and the determinant-area identity in `64760c76cadff481` gives
\[
|P_0|=A,\qquad |P_2|=B.
\tag{2}
\]
The exact compatibility equivalence in that fact gives
\[
\frac{P_0+P_2}{2}
=L\left(\frac{U\Delta+V\Delta}{2}\right)
\subseteq L\Delta=P_1.
\tag{3}
\]

For \(i=1,2,3\), the \(i\)-th vertex values of \(P_0\) are the entries of
\[
LU+\sigma\mathbf1^{\mathsf T},
\]
while those of \(P_2\) are the entries of
\[
LV-\sigma\mathbf1^{\mathsf T}.
\]
Consequently the prescribed cap equations are exactly
\[
\frac{|P_0\cap\{x_i\ge0\}|}{A}=u_i,
\qquad
\frac{|P_2\cap\{x_i\ge0\}|}{B}=v_i,
\tag{4}
\]
where
\[
(u_1,u_2,u_3)=\left(\frac12,\frac12,\frac{401}{1700}\right),
\qquad
(v_1,v_2,v_3)=\left(\frac1{11600},\frac1{11600},\frac1{11600}\right).
\tag{5}
\]
This identification uses the complete closed-cap convention of `64760c76cadff481` and makes no cap-order assumption.

For \(j=0,1,2\), set
\[
K_j:=P_j\cap\{x_1\le0,\ x_3\le0\}.
\tag{6}
\]
The complement of \(K_0\) in \(P_0\) is contained, up to the two zero lines, in the union of the coordinate-\(1\) and coordinate-\(3\) nonnegative caps. The union bound and (2), (4), and (5) therefore give the exact positive lower bound
\[
|K_0|
\ge A(1-u_1-u_3)
=\frac{17}{100}\left(1-\frac12-\frac{401}{1700}\right)
=\frac{449}{10000}>0.
\tag{7}
\]
The same argument on the large endpoint gives
\[
|K_2|
\ge B(1-v_1-v_3)
=\frac{29}{25}\left(1-\frac2{11600}\right)
=\frac{5799}{5000}.
\tag{8}
\]
There is no boundary convention hidden in (7)-(8): the strict-positive complement is contained in the union of the corresponding closed caps, which is all the union bound requires.

Both constrained coordinates remain nonpositive under averaging. Hence (3) implies
\[
\frac{K_0+K_2}{2}\subseteq K_1.
\tag{9}
\]
Planar Brunn-Minkowski, in the common area normalization, now yields
\[
2\sqrt{|K_1|}
\ge \sqrt{|K_0|}+\sqrt{|K_2|}
>\sqrt{|K_2|}
\ge\sqrt{\frac{5799}{5000}}.
\tag{10}
\]
The strict inequality uses the positive area in (7). Thus compatibility would force
\[
4|K_1|>\frac{5799}{5000}.
\tag{11}
\]

It remains to compute the fixed middle wedge exactly. Pull \(K_1\) back through \(L\) and write
\[
\lambda=(x,y,1-x-y)\in\Delta.
\]
The two nonpositive-coordinate inequalities in (6) become
\[
(L\lambda)_1=-73x+663y-206\le0,
\qquad
(L\lambda)_3=809x-250\le0.
\tag{12}
\]
Therefore
\[
0\le x\le\frac{250}{809},
\qquad
0\le y\le\frac{206+73x}{663}.
\tag{13}
\]
The simplex bound \(y\le1-x\) is looser throughout this interval, because
\[
\frac{250}{809}<\frac{457}{736}
\]
and \((206+73x)/663\le1-x\) is equivalent to \(x\le457/736\). Since the ordinary \((x,y)\)-area of \(\Delta\) is \(1/2\), its normalized area is twice ordinary area. Equations (12)-(13) give
\[
|K_1|
=2\int_0^{250/809}\frac{206+73x}{663}\,dx
=\frac{29296500}{144640301}.
\tag{14}
\]
But
\[
\frac{5799}{5000}-4|K_1|
=\frac{5799}{5000}
-\frac{117186000}{144640301}
=\frac{252839105499}{723201505000}>0,
\tag{15}
\]
which contradicts (11).

The contradiction used only exact cap areas, the fixed rational middle triangle, and full midpoint compatibility. It therefore excludes every real cap-order cell at once, including all repeated projection values and compatibility boundaries. The stronger requested rational system with its incidence, shift-sum, and strict-row conditions cannot exist.
