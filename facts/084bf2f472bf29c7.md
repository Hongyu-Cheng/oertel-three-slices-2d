## Statement

Let \(N=\{1,2,3\}\), and use the common-plane exact cells of accepted fact `e40abbed940a40ab`.  Assume the all-double support of accepted fact `91cb5bfc71da49ac` and write
\[
q=q_{123}>0,\qquad x_i=q_{N\setminus\{i\}}>0,\qquad
m=q+x_1+x_2+x_3.
\]
Write
\[
p_0=p_\varnothing,\quad
s_P=\sum_i p_{\{i\}},\quad d_P=\sum_{i<j}p_{\{i,j\}},
\]
\[
s_R=\sum_i r_{\{i\}},\quad d_R=\sum_{i<j}r_{\{i,j\}},
\quad r=r_{123},
\]
so that
\[
M=p_0+s_P+d_P,\qquad k=s_R+d_R+r.
\]
Assume \(p_0,r,r_1,r_2,r_3>0\), the three crossings
\[
u_i+v_i+c_i=h,\qquad h=\frac{2(M+m+k)}9,
\]
the canonical left and right tails, \(W=0\), and the accepted exact-cell inequalities.  In particular, use the complete partial-union family of accepted fact `2186f01c16b1f4e3`, the overlap inequality of accepted fact `2bb72698f0248a65`, the source cap inequalities of accepted fact `8234d1112ec2f031`, and the middle cap inequalities of accepted fact `ce343e749df349fe`.

Define the nonnegative \(P/R\)-defect
\[
\eta=s_P+4d_P+3d_R+6r.
\]
If
\[
|\mathcal T_Q|\le U(q,x_1,x_2,x_3),
\]
then every such \(W=0\) table satisfies the exact scalar bridge
\[
U(q,x_1,x_2,x_3)
\ge
\left(
2\sqrt r+
\frac{\sqrt m+\sqrt{120m+84q+28\eta}}7
\right)^2. \tag{1}
\]
Consequently
\[
U(q,x_1,x_2,x_3)>
\Phi(m,q):=
\left(
\frac{\sqrt m+\sqrt{120m+84q}}7
\right)^2. \tag{2}
\]
Thus the explicit \(Q\)-only sufficient condition
\[
U(q,x_1,x_2,x_3)\le\Phi(m,q) \tag{3}
\]
rules out every \(W=0\) all-double common-plane table.

More generally, if one additionally knows \(r_{123}\ge\rho>0\), then
\[
U(q,x_1,x_2,x_3)\ge
\Psi(m,q,\rho):=
\left(
2\sqrt\rho+
\frac{\sqrt m+\sqrt{120m+84q+168\rho}}7
\right)^2, \tag{4}
\]
so the strict condition \(U<\Psi(m,q,\rho)\) also rules out \(W=0\).

The \(Q\)-only threshold in (2) is sharp for the accepted scalar system.  For
\[
q=\frac7{12},\qquad x_1=x_2=x_3=\frac5{36},\qquad m=1,
\]
one has \(\Phi(m,q)=4\).  For every \(0<\varepsilon\le1/100\), assign every unlisted mass zero and set
\[
p_\varnothing=4+3\sqrt3\,\varepsilon+5\varepsilon^2,
\qquad p_{\{i\}}=\varepsilon^2,
\]
\[
r_{\{i\}}=\frac34,\qquad
r_{\{i,j\}}=\frac{\sqrt3}{2}\varepsilon,\qquad
r_{123}=\varepsilon^2.
\tag{5}
\]
These exact scalar tables have positive proper marginals, satisfy the crossings, both tails, \(W=0\), the complete partial-union and overlap inequalities, and both source and middle cap inequalities.  Moreover,
\[
\left(\sqrt{p_\varnothing}+2\sqrt{r_{123}}\right)^2
=
\left(
\sqrt{4+3\sqrt3\,\varepsilon+5\varepsilon^2}
+2\varepsilon
\right)^2
\longrightarrow4. \tag{6}
\]
The family is a scalar calibration, not a claimed common-plane realization.  It proves that no larger \(Q\)-only threshold follows from the accepted scalar inequalities and the scale lower bound.  To use a \(Q\)-only upper bound strictly above \(\Phi\), some quantitative \(P/R\) nondegeneracy must enter.  A lower bound for the single cell \(r_{123}\) is sufficient by (4); the fully retained defect is \((\eta,r_{123})\) in (1).

## Proof

By the exact weights in accepted fact `91cb5bfc71da49ac`, the all-double \(Q\)-contribution to \(W\) is
\[
4(x_1+x_2+x_3)+7q=4m+3q.
\]
The \(P\)- and \(R\)-contributions, using the forced absence of \(P_N\) and \(R_\varnothing\), are respectively
\[
-2p_0+s_P+4d_P,\qquad s_R+4d_R+7r.
\]
Therefore \(W=0\) is exactly
\[
2p_0
=4m+3q+s_P+4d_P+s_R+4d_R+7r
=4m+3q+k+\eta. \tag{7}
\]

Row \(\mathrm{F0}\) of accepted fact `2186f01c16b1f4e3` gives
\[
(\sqrt M+\sqrt m)^2\le4k.
\]
Since \(M\ge p_0\), equation (7) implies
\[
2p_0
\ge
4m+3q+\frac{(\sqrt{p_0}+\sqrt m)^2}{4}+\eta.
\]
Putting \(z=\sqrt{p_0}\) and clearing the positive denominator gives
\[
7z^2-2\sqrt m\,z-(17m+12q+4\eta)\ge0.
\]
The other root is negative, so
\[
\sqrt{p_0}\ge
\frac{\sqrt m+\sqrt{120m+84q+28\eta}}7. \tag{8}
\]
Accepted fact `e40abbed940a40ab` gives
\[
|\mathcal T_Q|\ge(\sqrt{p_0}+2\sqrt r)^2.
\]
Combining this with (8) and \(|\mathcal T_Q|\le U\) proves (1).  Since \(r>0\), (1) is strictly larger than its value at \(\eta=r=0\), proving (2) and hence (3).  Finally, \(\eta\ge6r\), and the right side of (1) is increasing in both \(\eta\) and \(r\).  If \(r\ge\rho\), replacing \(\eta\) by \(6\rho\) and \(r\) by \(\rho\) proves (4).

It remains to verify sharpness.  For (5),
\[
M=4+3\sqrt3\,\varepsilon+8\varepsilon^2,\qquad
k=\frac94+\frac{3\sqrt3}{2}\varepsilon+\varepsilon^2,
\]
\[
u_i=\varepsilon^2,\qquad
v_i=\frac{31}{36},\qquad
c_i=\frac34+\sqrt3\,\varepsilon+\varepsilon^2.
\]
The common crossing value is
\[
h=\frac{29}{18}+\sqrt3\,\varepsilon+2\varepsilon^2,
\]
so all three crossings hold.  The left-tail slack is
\[
c_i+m-h=\frac5{36}-\varepsilon^2>0,
\]
and the right-tail slack is
\[
c_i+M-h=M-u_i-v_i>0.
\]
Also \(0<u_i<M\), \(0<v_i<m\), \(0<h<M\), and \(M\ge m\).

The three contributions to \(W\) are
\[
-2p_\varnothing+3\varepsilon^2,\qquad
\frac{23}{4},\qquad
\frac94+6\sqrt3\,\varepsilon+7\varepsilon^2.
\]
Their sum is zero.

We next verify the complete partial-union list from accepted fact `2186f01c16b1f4e3`.  Only rows \(\mathrm{F0}\), \(\mathrm{F1}\), \(\mathrm{F2}\), and \(\mathrm{F3}\) have two positive sources; rows \(\mathrm{F4}\) and \(\mathrm{F5}\) are not imposed because \(p_{\{i,j\}}=0\).

For \(\mathrm{F0}\), put
\[
A=4k-M-1=4+3\sqrt3\,\varepsilon-4\varepsilon^2.
\]
Then \(A>0\) and
\[
A^2-4M
=\varepsilon\left(
12\sqrt3-37\varepsilon-24\sqrt3\,\varepsilon^2+16\varepsilon^3
\right)>0
\]
for \(0<\varepsilon\le1/100\): the bracket is larger than
\[
20-\frac{37}{100}-\frac{48}{10000}>0.
\]
Thus \(A>2\sqrt M\), which is exactly
\[
(\sqrt M+1)^2<4k.
\]

For \(\mathrm{F1}\),
\[
M-u_i=4+3\sqrt3\,\varepsilon+7\varepsilon^2
<\left(\frac{203}{100}\right)^2,
\qquad
\frac{\sqrt5}{6}<\frac38.
\]
Hence
\[
\left(\sqrt{M-u_i}+\sqrt{\frac5{36}}\right)^2
<
\left(\frac{203}{100}+\frac38\right)^2
<6
<
4(k-c_i).
\]
For \(\mathrm{F2}\),
\[
\left(\sqrt{u_i}+\sqrt{v_i}\right)^2
=\left(\varepsilon+\frac{\sqrt{31}}6\right)^2
<\left(\frac{101}{100}\right)^2
<3
<4c_i.
\]
For every \(\mathrm{F3}\) instance,
\[
\left(\sqrt{p_{\{i\}}+p_{\{i,j\}}}+\sqrt{q_{\{i,j\}}}\right)^2
=\left(\varepsilon+\frac{\sqrt5}{6}\right)^2
<\left(\frac{77}{200}\right)^2
<1
<4(r_{\{i\}}+r_{\{i,j\}}).
\]
This exhausts the complete \(19\)-inequality family.

The overlap inequality of accepted fact `2bb72698f0248a65` follows by summing the three \(\mathrm{F1}\) inequalities: \(p_\varnothing<M-u_i\), and
\[
\sum_i(k-c_i)
=k-r_{123}+\sum_i r_{\{i\}}.
\]
For the source caps of accepted fact `8234d1112ec2f031`,
\[
q^2m=\frac{49}{144}>
\frac{125}{11664}=4x_1x_2x_3,
\qquad
\sum_i\sqrt{\frac{x_i}{m}}=\frac{\sqrt5}{2}<\frac32.
\]
For the corrected middle quantities of accepted fact `ce343e749df349fe`,
\[
\frac{r_{\{i,j\}}r_{\{i,\ell\}}}{r_{123}}
=\frac34=r_{\{i\}},
\]
so all three corrected cap areas are zero and both middle inequalities hold.  This verifies every declared predecessor condition exactly.

Finally, (6) follows directly from (5).  Since its left side is continuous for \(\varepsilon\ge0\), for every \(\delta>0\) a sufficiently small positive \(\varepsilon\le1/100\) makes it less than \(4+\delta\).  At the purely scalar level, introduce an auxiliary scale \(\tau\) equal to the left side of (6).  Then
\[
\left(\sqrt{p_\varnothing}+2\sqrt{r_{123}}\right)^2
\le\tau<4+\delta,
\]
so the accepted scalar system and the numerical conclusion of the scale lower bound are consistent below every proposed \(Q\)-only threshold \(4+\delta\).  No common-plane realization of (5) is asserted.  This proves the claimed scalar sharpness and identifies the extra \(r_{123}\)-input in (4).
