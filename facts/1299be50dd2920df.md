## Statement

Let \(A_0,A_1,A_2\subseteq\mathbb R^2\) be nonempty compact
two-dimensional convex bodies with
\[
\frac{A_0+A_2}{2}\subseteq A_1,
\]
and let
\[
\mathcal K=\operatorname{conv}\bigl(
(\{0\}\times A_0)\cup(\{1\}\times A_1)
\cup(\{2\}\times A_2)\bigr),
\qquad
\mathcal K_v=\{x:(v,x)\in\mathcal K\}.
\]
Write
\[
M=|A_0|,
\qquad
k=|A_1|,
\qquad
m=|A_2|.
\]
For \(i=2,3\), let \(\Lambda_i:\mathbb R^3\to\mathbb R\) be affine and
define
\[
c_i=\bigl|A_1\cap\{x:\Lambda_i(1,x)\geq0\}\bigr|,
\qquad
s_i(v)=\max_{x\in\mathcal K_v}\Lambda_i(v,x),
\]
\[
E_i(v)=
\{x\in\mathcal K_v:\Lambda_i(v,x)+s_i(2-v)\geq0\},
\qquad
I_i=\int_0^2|E_i(v)|\,dv.
\]
The reflected paired-support inequality in accepted fact
`d1c7a9b4c142230a` gives
\[
I_i\leq8c_i\qquad(i=2,3).                       \tag{1}
\]
Define
\[
\mathcal V=\int_0^2|\mathcal K_v|\,dv,
\quad
B_1=\int_0^2
\left|\mathcal K_v\setminus(E_2(v)\cup E_3(v))\right|\,dv,
\quad
I_{23}=\int_0^2|E_2(v)\cap E_3(v)|\,dv.          \tag{2}
\]
Suppose real numbers \(t,d_2,d_3\) satisfy
\[
9t=2(M+m+k),
\qquad
c_i=t-d_i\quad(i=2,3).                          \tag{3}
\]
Put
\[
R=4m-4d_2-5d_3,
\qquad
\varepsilon_i=8c_i-I_i\geq0,
\]
\[
P=4\mathcal V-4B_1+I_3+4I_{23}.                \tag{4}
\]
Then
\[
P=4I_2+5I_3\leq32c_2+40c_3,                    \tag{5}
\]
\[
\bigl[32k+8R\bigr]-\bigl[32c_2+40c_3\bigr]
=16(k+m-M),                                     \tag{6}
\]
and, more precisely,
\[
\bigl[32k+8R\bigr]-P
=16(k+m-M)+4\varepsilon_2+5\varepsilon_3.       \tag{7}
\]
Consequently, if \(M<m+k\), then
\[
P<32k+8R,                                       \tag{8}
\]
so the raw support-order payment \(P\geq32k+8R\) is impossible.

Moreover,
\[
P+4\varepsilon_2+5\varepsilon_3
=32c_2+40c_3,                                   \tag{9}
\]
and
\[
\begin{aligned}
&P+4\varepsilon_2+5\varepsilon_3-[32k+8R]\\
&\qquad=16(M-m-k)\\
&\qquad=8\bigl[(4c_2+5c_3-4k)-R\bigr].
\end{aligned}                                   \tag{10}
\]
Thus the augmented ledger inequality is exactly equivalent to
\[
4c_2+5c_3-4k\geq4m-4d_2-5d_3,                  \tag{11}
\]
and both are equivalent, under (3), to \(M\geq m+k\).

## Proof

For every \(v\), the pointwise indicator identity
\[
4\mathbf 1_{E_2(v)}+5\mathbf 1_{E_3(v)}
=4\mathbf 1_{E_2(v)\cup E_3(v)}
+\mathbf 1_{E_3(v)}
+4\mathbf 1_{E_2(v)\cap E_3(v)}
\]
holds, including on all ties.  Integrating over \(\mathcal K_v\) and
then over \(v\), and using
\[
\int_0^2|E_2(v)\cup E_3(v)|\,dv=\mathcal V-B_1,
\]
gives the equality in (5).  Accepted fact `d1c7a9b4c142230a`,
applied separately to \(\Lambda_2\) and \(\Lambda_3\), gives (1), so
the inequality in (5) follows.

Using (3),
\[
\begin{aligned}
&32k+8(4m-4d_2-5d_3)
 -32(t-d_2)-40(t-d_3)\\
&\qquad=32(k+m)-72t\\
&\qquad=16(k+m-M),
\end{aligned}
\]
which proves (6).  Also,
\[
(32c_2+40c_3)-P
=4(8c_2-I_2)+5(8c_3-I_3)
=4\varepsilon_2+5\varepsilon_3.
\]
Adding this identity to (6) proves (7), hence (8) in the residual
case.

The same identity proves (9).  Subtracting \(32k+8R\), using (6),
and expanding the factor \(8\) gives (10).  This proves the exact
equivalence with (11).

No step uses whether either crossing is empty-proper or
proper-proper, whether \(d_2=d_3\), or whether a support face has a
unique maximizer.  These cases therefore cannot repair the raw
payment.
