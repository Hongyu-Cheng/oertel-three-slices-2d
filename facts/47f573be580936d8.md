## Statement

Fix \(x,\ell,b>0\) with
\[
x+\ell+b=1,
\qquad
\lambda=x+\ell,
\]
and assume
\[
x<t_1\leq\lambda\leq t_2<1,
\qquad
t_1<t_2.
\tag{1}
\]
Define
\[
D=\frac{16\ell-9(b+\ell)^2}{2},
\qquad
K_0=\frac{5b^2-6b\ell+5\ell^2}{2},
\tag{2}
\]
\[
a_*(t)=\frac{-t^2+2t+9x^2-2x}{2},
\tag{3}
\]
and
\[
k_*(t)=
\begin{cases}
\dfrac{(t-x)(16\ell-5t+11x-6)}2,
&t\leq\lambda,\\[6pt]
\dfrac{8\ell^2+3t^2-6t-3x^2+6x}{2},
&t\geq\lambda.
\end{cases}
\tag{4}
\]
There do not exist \(h\geq0\) and \(d_1,d_2>0\) satisfying
\[
h+d_1+d_2<\frac{\ell}{x},
\tag{5}
\]
\[
\ell+(1-x)h+(t_1-x)d_1+(t_2-x)d_2\geq b,
\tag{6}
\]
\[
a_*(1)h+a_*(t_1)d_1+a_*(t_2)d_2<D,
\tag{7}
\]
\[
K_0+k_*(1)h+k_*(t_1)d_1+k_*(t_2)d_2<0.
\tag{8}
\]

## Proof

Put
\[
s=b+\ell=1-x,
\qquad
p=\frac{\ell}{s}.
\tag{9}
\]
Thus \(0<s<1\) and \(0<p<1\).  For \(t=x+qs\), direct substitution
into (2)--(4) gives
\[
2D=s(16p-9s),
\qquad
2K_0=s^2(16p^2-16p+5),
\tag{10}
\]
\[
2a_*(x+qs)=G(q):=8x^2+s^2q(2-q),
\tag{11}
\]
and
\[
2k_*(x+qs)=
\begin{cases}
s^2\kappa_L(q),&0\leq q\leq p,\\
s^2\kappa_R(q),&p\leq q\leq1,
\end{cases}
\tag{12}
\]
where
\[
\kappa_L(q)=q(16p-6-5q),
\qquad
\kappa_R(q)=8p^2-3q(2-q).
\tag{13}
\]
The two expressions in (12) agree at \(q=p\), since both equal
\(s^2p(11p-6)\).  The affine \(h\)-ray is the right ray at \(q=1\):
\[
2a_*(1)=G(1)=8x^2+s^2,
\qquad
2k_*(1)=s^2\kappa_h,
\qquad
\kappa_h:=8p^2-3.
\tag{14}
\]
Also \(G(q)>0\) on \(0\leq q\leq1\).  Therefore the left side of
(7) is positive, so (7) already forces \(D>0\).

We first dispose of the case \(\kappa_h\geq0\).  For
\(p\leq q\leq1\),
\[
\kappa_R(q)=\kappa_h+3(1-q)^2\geq0.
\tag{15}
\]
Moreover \(\kappa_h\geq0\) implies
\[
p\geq\sqrt{\frac38}>\frac6{11}.
\]
Thus, for \(0<q\leq p\),
\[
16p-6-5q\geq11p-6>0,
\]
so \(\kappa_L(q)>0\).  Finally,
\[
16p^2-16p+5=16\left(p-\frac12\right)^2+1>0.
\tag{16}
\]
Equations (10), (12), and (14)--(16) make every variable coefficient
in (8) nonnegative and \(K_0>0\), contradicting (8).

It remains to treat \(\kappa_h<0\).  Define the positive \(A\)-budget
multiplier
\[
\eta
=-\frac{k_*(1)}{a_*(1)}
=\frac{s^2(3-8p^2)}{G(1)}>0.
\tag{17}
\]
We prove the pointwise dual inequalities
\[
k_*(x+qs)+\eta a_*(x+qs)\geq0
\tag{18}
\]
on both sides of \(q=p\).

For the right side, the exact identity
\[
\kappa_R(q)G(1)-\kappa_hG(q)
=8(1-q)^2(s^2p^2+3x^2)
\tag{19}
\]
is nonnegative for \(p\leq q\leq1\).  For the left side, put
\[
F(q)=\kappa_L(q)G(1)-\kappa_hG(q).
\]
This is a quadratic in \(q\).  Its leading coefficient is
\[
8\bigl(s^2(p^2-1)-5x^2\bigr)<0,
\tag{20}
\]
so \(F\) is strictly concave.  Its endpoint values on \([0,p]\) are
\[
F(0)=8x^2(3-8p^2)>0
\tag{21}
\]
and, because the two ray formulas agree at \(p\), equation (19) gives
\[
F(p)=8(1-p)^2(s^2p^2+3x^2)>0.
\tag{22}
\]
Concavity and (21)--(22) imply \(F(q)>0\) throughout \(0\leq q\leq p\).
After multiplying (18) by the positive factor
\[
\frac{2G(1)}{s^2},
\]
inequality (18) is exactly (19) on the right and exactly \(F(q)\geq0\)
on the left.  Hence (18) holds for every required atom ray, and it
holds with equality for the affine ray \(q=1\).

It remains to check the constant term of this dual certificate.
Define
\[
\begin{aligned}
P(s,p)={}&9(1-p)^2s^2\\
&+(16p^3-32p^2+26p-10)s\\
&+16p^2-16p+5.
\end{aligned}
\tag{23}
\]
Direct expansion of (2), (10), (14), and (17) gives the exact identity
\[
K_0-\eta D=\frac{4s^2}{G(1)}P(s,p).
\tag{24}
\]
As a quadratic in \(s\), the polynomial in (23) has positive leading
coefficient \(9(1-p)^2\) and discriminant
\[
\operatorname{disc}_sP
=16(p-1)^2(2p-1)^2(4p^2-4p-5).
\tag{25}
\]
For \(0<p<1\),
\[
4p^2-4p-5=-5-4p(1-p)<0.
\]
Thus the discriminant in (25) is negative unless \(p=1/2\).  At that
remaining value,
\[
P\left(s,\frac12\right)=\frac{(3s-2)^2}{4}\geq0.
\tag{26}
\]
Consequently \(P(s,p)\geq0\) throughout the geometric domain, and
(24) gives
\[
K_0-\eta D\geq0.
\tag{27}
\]

Apply (18) to \(t_1,t_2\) and use its equality case \(q=1\) for the
\(h\)-ray.  Since \(h,d_1,d_2\geq0\), equation (7), the strict
positivity of \(\eta\), and (27) yield
\[
\begin{aligned}
&K_0+k_*(1)h+k_*(t_1)d_1+k_*(t_2)d_2\\
&\quad\geq
K_0-\eta
\bigl(a_*(1)h+a_*(t_1)d_1+a_*(t_2)d_2\bigr)\\
&\quad>
K_0-\eta D
\geq0,
\end{aligned}
\tag{28}
\]
contradicting (8).

The positivity-budget and full-right constraints (5)--(6) were not
needed: their exact LP-dual multipliers are both zero, while (17) is
the \(A\)-budget multiplier.  At \(t_i=\lambda\), one has \(q_i=p\),
and (19), (22), and the equality of the two formulas in (12) verify
the boundary from both sides.  This includes both weak
\(\lambda\)-boundaries and completes chamber (22b).
