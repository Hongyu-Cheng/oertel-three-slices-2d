## Statement

Put
\[
A=\frac1{10},\qquad
B=\frac{10999}{10000},\qquad
t=\frac{2(1+A+B)}9=\frac{7333}{15000},
\tag{1}
\]
and take
\[
L=
\begin{pmatrix}
367&-265&-113\\
-260&810&-525\\
-106&-544&639
\end{pmatrix}.
\tag{2}
\]
Let
\[
c_i=\mathcal C(L_{i*};0)
\qquad(i=1,2,3)
\]
in the exact cap notation of accepted fact `64760c76cadff481`.
Then
\[
c_1=\frac{134689}{303360},\qquad
c_2=\frac{4374}{9523},\qquad
c_3=\frac{408321}{881335}.
\tag{3}
\]
The row means have singleton-singleton signs
\[
\frac13\sum_jL_{1j}<0,\qquad
\frac13\sum_jL_{2j}>1,\qquad
\frac13\sum_jL_{3j}<0.
\tag{4}
\]

There is a strict rational scalar endpoint allocation
\[
(u_1,u_2,u_3)
=
\left(
\frac{359}{800},
\frac{29557}{100000},
\frac{799}{3125}
\right),
\qquad
(v_1,v_2,v_3)=(0,0,0),
\tag{5}
\]
such that
\[
u_1+u_2+u_3=1
\tag{6}
\]
and
\[
Au_i+c_i+Bv_i<t
\qquad(i=1,2,3).
\tag{7}
\]
The data (1)-(5) satisfy the residual strip and every scalar inequality
from accepted facts `a5bbbf5d61041b23`,
`0c27d2a820afec95`, and the cap bound in accepted fact
`53099f38de81eac5`.

Nevertheless, no accepted endpoint gate removes this calibration:

1. With
\[
\alpha_i=\frac{t-c_i}{A},
\tag{8}
\]
one has
\[
\alpha_i>\frac14
\qquad(i=1,2,3).
\tag{9}
\]
Thus the hypothesis of accepted fact `37652924248e8019` fails under
every row permutation.

2. Every middle cap lies strictly above \(43/100\):
\[
c_i>\frac{43}{100}
\qquad(i=1,2,3).
\tag{10}
\]
Hence no ordered pair of rows lies in the closed asymmetric cap
rectangle
\[
c_i\leq\frac{225}{529},\qquad
c_k\leq\frac{43}{100}.
\tag{11}
\]
This conclusion is only an arithmetic separation from that proposed
rectangle; it assumes no unaccepted two-cap theorem.

3. For each row, the radical function \(G\) defined in accepted fact
`86e9bf31e8f5df7a` takes a strictly negative value at an exact rational
scale and budget split.  Thus even the rowwise radical certificate
\(\min G\geq0\) does not hold.

The statement is only a strict survivor of the displayed scalar
relaxation.  It does not assert that the cap vector (5) is jointly
realizable by an endpoint triangle, nor that compatible
\(U,V,\sigma\) exist.

## Proof

The columns of \(L\) sum to one,
\[
\det L=681080,
\]
and its row sums are
\[
-11,\qquad25,\qquad-11.
\tag{12}
\]
This proves nondegeneracy and (4).  Each row has one positive vertex
value.  The complete one-positive formula of accepted fact
`64760c76cadff481` gives
\[
c_1=\frac{367^2}{(367+265)(367+113)}
=\frac{134689}{303360},
\]
\[
c_2=\frac{810^2}{(810+525)(810+260)}
=\frac{4374}{9523},
\]
\[
c_3=\frac{639^2}{(639+544)(639+106)}
=\frac{408321}{881335},
\]
proving (3).

We first audit the strict scalar rows.  Direct exact subtraction gives
\[
t-c_1-Au_1=\frac{13}{12640000}>0,
\tag{13}
\]
\[
t-c_2-Au_2=\frac{17867}{28569000000}>0,
\tag{14}
\]
\[
t-c_3-Au_3=\frac{24779}{66100125000}>0.
\tag{15}
\]
The fractions in (5) sum to one, so (6)-(7) follow.

The exact residual variables are
\[
r=\frac AB=\frac{1000}{10999},
\qquad
S=\frac1B=\frac{10000}{10999}.
\]
They lie in the small-\(r\) residual branch because
\[
S-(1-r)=\frac1{10999}>0,
\qquad
(1+r)-S=\frac{1999}{10999}>0,
\tag{16}
\]
\[
\frac38-r=\frac{24997}{87992}>0.
\tag{17}
\]
Moreover
\[
1+A-B=\frac1{10000}>0,
\qquad
\frac12-t=\frac{167}{15000}>0.
\tag{18}
\]

We next check the singleton-singleton scalar restrictions in accepted
fact `a5bbbf5d61041b23`.  Put
\[
d_1=|P_1\cap\{x_1\geq1\}|
=\frac{366^2}{(367+265)(367+113)}
=\frac{11163}{25280}.
\tag{19}
\]
Then
\[
d_1-\frac B9=\frac{9082691}{28440000}>0,
\tag{20}
\]
\[
\frac{2(1+B-A)}9-c_1
=\frac{49097}{113760000}>0,
\tag{21}
\]
\[
c_2-\frac49=\frac{1274}{85707}>0.
\tag{22}
\]
The two upper middle-cap margins are
\[
1-\frac B9-c_2
=\frac{358666523}{857070000}>0,
\]
\[
1-\frac B9-c_3
=\frac{6575491267}{15864030000}>0,
\tag{23}
\]
and the column-sum cover margin is
\[
c_2+c_3-(1-d_1)
=\frac{15453967195691}{42434771404480}>0.
\tag{24}
\]
For the scalar endpoint fractions,
\[
u_1-\frac49=\frac{31}{7200}>0,
\]
\[
\frac59-u_2=\frac{233987}{900000}>0,
\qquad
\frac59-u_3=\frac{8434}{28125}>0,
\tag{25}
\]
and

\frac{2(A+B-1)}9-Au_2
=\frac{133787}{9000000}>0.
\tag{26}
\]
Thus the cap consequence of accepted fact `53099f38de81eac5` is also
respected.  No sign-cell allocation is asserted.

The full budget of accepted fact `0c27d2a820afec95` is strict as well.
Indeed, the endpoint excess terms vanish because
\(\sum_i u_i=1\) and \(v_i=0\), while
\[

-\left(c_1+c_2+c_3-1\right)
=\frac{129134305427}{6365215710672\cdot 10^4}>0.

\]
Also
\[

<

Equations (12)-(28) prove the claimed strict scalar admissibility.

We now audit separation from the accepted endpoint criteria.  From
(3) and (8),
\[

=
\left(

Accepted fact `37652924248e8019` requires, after a row permutation, a

The margins in (10) are

It remains to check the full radical condition from accepted fact

Every entry in (37) is positive.  Hence both inverse-capacity

The exact square comparisons

`86e9bf31e8f5df7a` does not cover this calibration.
