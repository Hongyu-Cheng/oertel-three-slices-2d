## Statement

Assume the surviving transformed \(N=3\) triangle system of accepted facts `64760c76cadff481` and `a5bbbf5d61041b23` in the orbit
\[
I_0=\{1\},\qquad I_1=\{2\},\qquad I_2=\varnothing .
\]
Thus \(0<A\leq B\), \(1+A<2B\), the three exact row masses
\[
M_i=A u_i+c_i+Bv_i
\]
satisfy
\[
M_i<t:=\frac{2(1+A+B)}9\qquad(i=1,2,3),
\]
and, with
\[
d_1=|P_1\cap\{x_1\geq1\}|,
\]
one has
\[
\frac B9<d_1<c_1,\qquad c_2+c_3\geq1-d_1.
\]
Put
\[
\delta:=\frac{2B-A-1}{3}>0.
\]
Then every such system satisfies the exact strict budget
\[
\boxed{
A\left(\sum_{i=1}^3u_i-1\right)
+B\sum_{i=1}^3v_i
+(c_1-d_1)
+\bigl(c_2+c_3-1+d_1\bigr)
<\delta .}
\tag{1}
\]
In particular,
\[
\boxed{\ c_1+c_2+c_3<\frac{2+2B-A}{3}\ },
\tag{2}
\]
and
\[
A\left(\sum_i u_i-1\right)+B\sum_i v_i
+\bigl(c_2+c_3-1+d_1\bigr)
<\delta-(c_1-d_1).
\tag{3}
\]

Let \(a\leq b\leq c\) be the sorted first-coordinate values at the vertices of \(P_1\).  Then \(a<0<c\), \(c>1\), and the three threshold chambers \(b\leq0\), \(0\leq b\leq1\), and \(b\geq1\) exhaust all possibilities.  In them respectively,
\[
\Gamma(a,b,c):=c_1-d_1
=
\begin{cases}
\displaystyle\frac{2c-1}{(c-a)(c-b)},&b\leq0,\\[3mm]
\displaystyle
1-\frac{a^2}{(b-a)(c-a)}
-\frac{(c-1)^2}{(c-a)(c-b)},&0\leq b\leq1,\\[4mm]
\displaystyle\frac{1-2a}{(b-a)(c-a)},&b\geq1.
\end{cases}
\tag{4}
\]
The adjacent formulas agree at \(b=0\) and \(b=1\), include the repeated lower or upper vertex cases, and are strictly positive.  Consequently, in each chamber the full parameter region
\[
\boxed{\ \Gamma(a,b,c)\geq\delta\ }
\tag{5}
\]
is infeasible for the exact matrix system.

## Proof

Accepted fact `a5bbbf5d61041b23` places \(P_0\) in the plane
\[
x_1+x_2+x_3=1+q
\]
with \(q>1\).  Hence every point of \(P_0\) has at least one positive coordinate.  Its three closed coordinate caps cover \(P_0\), so
\[
\sum_{i=1}^3u_i\geq1.
\tag{6}
\]
The same accepted fact gives
\[
c_2+c_3-1+d_1\geq0
\tag{7}
\]
and \(c_1-d_1>0\).  Trivially, \(v_i\geq0\) for every \(i\).

Summing the three strict row inequalities gives
\[
A\sum_i u_i+\sum_i c_i+B\sum_i v_i<3t.
\tag{8}
\]
The left side has the exact decomposition
\[
\begin{aligned}
A\sum_i u_i+\sum_i c_i+B\sum_i v_i
={}&A+1
+A\left(\sum_i u_i-1\right)
+B\sum_i v_i\\
&+(c_1-d_1)
+\bigl(c_2+c_3-1+d_1\bigr).
\end{aligned}
\tag{9}
\]
Moreover,
\[
3t-(A+1)
=\frac{2(1+A+B)}3-A-1
=\frac{2B-A-1}{3}
=\delta.
\tag{10}
\]
Equations (8)--(10) prove (1).  The positivity of \(\delta\) is exactly \(1+A<2B\).  Dropping the first two nonnegative terms of (1), and observing that
\[
(c_1-d_1)+(c_2+c_3-1+d_1)=c_1+c_2+c_3-1,
\]
proves (2).  Moving \(c_1-d_1\) to the right side of (1) proves (3).  Notice that (1) retains the literal independently allocated endpoint caps \(u_i,v_i\); they have not been replaced by free scalar variables.

It remains to compute the first-coordinate strip exactly.  Accepted fact `64760c76cadff481` supplies the complete closed triangle-cap formula, while accepted fact `a5bbbf5d61041b23` supplies \(a<0\), \(c>1\), and the threshold-\(0\) and threshold-\(1\) branches.

If \(b\leq0\), both caps use their unique-upper-vertex branch:
\[
c_1=\frac{c^2}{(c-a)(c-b)},\qquad
d_1=\frac{(c-1)^2}{(c-a)(c-b)}.
\]
Their difference is the first line of (4).

If \(0\leq b\leq1\), the threshold-\(0\) cap uses the two-upper-vertex branch and the threshold-\(1\) cap uses the unique-upper-vertex branch:
\[
c_1=1-\frac{a^2}{(b-a)(c-a)},\qquad
d_1=\frac{(c-1)^2}{(c-a)(c-b)}.
\]
This is the second line of (4).

If \(b\geq1\), both caps use their two-upper-vertex branch:
\[
c_1=1-\frac{a^2}{(b-a)(c-a)},\qquad
d_1=1-\frac{(1-a)^2}{(b-a)(c-a)}.
\]
Their difference is the third line of (4).  Direct substitution shows agreement at \(b=0\) and \(b=1\).  The repeated-value formulas in accepted fact `64760c76cadff481` give the same expressions when \(a=b\) in the first chamber or \(b=c\) in the third chamber.

Finally, \(P_1\) has vertices on both sides of each of the lines \(x_1=0\) and \(x_1=1\), because \(a<0<c\) and \(c>1\).  Therefore the relatively open strip
\[
P_1\cap\{0<x_1<1\}
\]
has positive area.  Its area is exactly \(c_1-d_1=\Gamma(a,b,c)\), so every expression in (4) is strictly positive.  If (5) held, the single term \(c_1-d_1\) would already be at least the right side of (1), while every other term on the left of (1) is nonnegative.  This contradicts the strict inequality (1), proving the chamber exclusions.
