## Statement

Let \(A_0,A_1,A_2\subseteq\mathbb R^2\) be nonempty two-dimensional convex polytopes, let
\[
B=\frac{A_0+A_2}{2}\subseteq A_1,
\qquad
S_B=(\{0\}\times A_0)\cup(\{1\}\times B)\cup(\{2\}\times A_2),
\]
and put
\[
T_B=|A_0|+|B|+|A_2|,
\qquad
E=A_1\setminus B,
\qquad
e=|E|.
\]
Fix \(z\in B\), set \(y=(1,z)\), and assume
\[
h_{S_B}(y)\ge\frac{2T_B}{9}.
\]
For every closed halfspace \(H\subseteq\mathbb R^3\) whose boundary contains \(y\), define
\[
K_H=\{w\in\mathbb R^2:(1,w)\in H\},
\qquad
\sigma(H)=\mathcal H_2(S_B\cap H)-\frac{2T_B}{9}.
\]
Then the same point \(y\) satisfies
\[
h_{(\{0\}\times A_0)\cup(\{1\}\times A_1)\cup(\{2\}\times A_2)}(y)
\ge\frac{2(T_B+e)}9
\]
if and only if, for every such \(H\),
\[
|E\cap K_H|
\ge
\left(\frac{2e}{9}-\sigma(H)\right)_+.
\tag{1}
\]
Thus (1) is the weakest exact local condition on the added middle measure.

In particular, if
\[
\delta=h_{S_B}(y)-\frac{2T_B}{9},
\]
then the scalar condition
\[
e\le\frac92\delta
\tag{2}
\]
transfers the witness without any geometric assumption on \(E\). Another sufficient condition is
\[
|E\cap K|\ge\frac{2e}{9}
\tag{3}
\]
for every closed planar halfplane \(K\) whose boundary contains \(z\).

Beyond (2), total added area alone does not determine whether the same witness transfers. More precisely, let \(s>0\), \(Q_s=[-s,s]^2\), and \(A_0=A_2=B=Q_s\). For every \(e>15s^2\), the two convex enlargements
\[
A_1^-=\operatorname{conv}\left(Q_s\cup
\left\{\left(s+\frac es,0\right)\right\}\right)
\]
and
\[
A_1^+=
\left[-s-\frac{e}{4s},\,s+\frac{e}{4s}\right]\times[-s,s]
\]
both satisfy \(|A_1^\pm\setminus B|=e\), but \(y=(1,0,0)\) fails the enlarged \(2/9\) bound for \(A_1^-\) and satisfies it for \(A_1^+\). By accepted fact `8ad0785e5b4ae9ef`, the minimal triple and both enlargements are compatible three-slice triples.

## Proof

Every halfspace containing \(y\) contains a parallel halfspace whose boundary passes through \(y\). The latter is a subset of the former, so it is enough to test halfspaces whose boundary contains \(y\).

For every such \(H\), additivity of planar area and \(A_1=B\sqcup E\) give the exact identity
\[
\mathcal H_2\!\left(
\left((\{0\}\times A_0)\cup(\{1\}\times A_1)\cup(\{2\}\times A_2)\right)
\cap H\right)
=
\mathcal H_2(S_B\cap H)+|E\cap K_H|.
\]
Since the enlarged total mass is \(T_B+e\), its mass in \(H\) is at least \(2(T_B+e)/9\) exactly when
\[
|E\cap K_H|
\ge
\frac{2e}{9}
-\left(\mathcal H_2(S_B\cap H)-\frac{2T_B}{9}\right)
=
\frac{2e}{9}-\sigma(H).
\]
Because the left side is nonnegative, this is equivalent to (1). Quantifying over all \(H\) proves the equivalence.

Moreover,
\[
\sigma(H)\ge
h_{S_B}(y)-\frac{2T_B}{9}
=\delta.
\]
Hence (2) makes the right side of (1) zero for every \(H\), proving the scalar transfer. If \(H\) is nonhorizontal, \(K_H\) is a planar halfplane whose boundary contains \(z\), so (3) implies (1). If \(H\) is horizontal, then \(K_H=\mathbb R^2\) and \(|E\cap K_H|=e\ge2e/9\). Thus (3) is also sufficient.

It remains to verify the obstruction. The minimal square triple is centrally symmetric about \(y=(1,0,0)\). Every halfspace containing its center has mass at least half its total mass, while the vertical halfspace
\[
H=\{(x,u,v):u\le0\}
\]
has exactly half. Therefore
\[
h_{S_B}(y)=6s^2,
\qquad
T_B=12s^2,
\qquad
\delta=6s^2-\frac{24s^2}{9}=\frac{10s^2}{3},
\]
so \((9/2)\delta=15s^2\).

For \(A_1^-\), the added region is the triangle with base
\(\{s\}\times[-s,s]\) and apex \((s+e/s,0)\), hence it has area \(e\) and lies entirely in \(\{u>0\}\). Consequently the displayed vertical halfspace still has mass \(6s^2\). Since \(e>15s^2\),
\[
6s^2<\frac{2(12s^2+e)}9,
\]
so \(y\) fails the enlarged target.

The rectangle \(A_1^+\) also adds exactly \(e\) area. The corresponding three-slice measure is centrally symmetric about \(y\), so every halfspace containing \(y\) has at least half the total mass:
\[
h(y)=\frac{12s^2+e}{2}
>
\frac{2(12s^2+e)}9.
\]
Thus two compatible convex enlargements with the same minimal triple, witness, and added area have opposite transfer outcomes. The first obstruction is exactly a halfspace with base slack below \(2e/9\) whose middle section misses enough added mass, as quantified by (1).
