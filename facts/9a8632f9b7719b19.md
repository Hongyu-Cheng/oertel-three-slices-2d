## Statement

Let
\[
f_1(x,y)=x,\qquad f_2(x,y)=y,\qquad
f_3(x,y)=-1-x-y,\qquad L_1=L_2=L_3=-100,
\tag{1}
\]
\[
A_0=\operatorname{conv}\left\{
\left(-\frac{999}{10},-\frac{981}{10}\right),
\left(197,-\frac{981}{10}\right),
\left(\frac{49}{2},-50\right)
\right\},
\tag{2}
\]
and
\[
A_2=
\left\{
\left(-\frac{99}{2},\frac{991}{10}\right)
+s\left(302,\frac{19}{10}\right)
+t\left(-\frac1{10},\frac1{20}\right):
-\frac12\le s,t\le\frac12
\right\}.
\tag{3}
\]
Put \(B=(A_0+A_2)/2\).  Suppose that \(K\supseteq B\) is a compact
convex body and
\[
A=|K\setminus B|.
\tag{4}
\]
If all three crossing equalities
\[
\left|A_0\cap\{f_i\le L_i\}\right|
+\left|A_2\cap\{f_i\le-L_i\}\right|
+\left|K\cap\{f_i\le0\}\right|
=\frac{2(|A_0|+|A_2|+|K|)}9
\qquad(i=1,2,3)
\tag{5}
\]
hold, then necessarily
\[
A\le
A_\star:=\frac{3678114899083}{2325037600}.
\tag{6}
\]
Consequently no compact convex \(K\) satisfies (4)--(5) for any
\[
A\in
\left[
\frac{23930259}{6650},
\frac{17881582275919}{2325037600}
\right],
\tag{7}
\]
the scalar-completion interval in accepted fact
`6f6869df3d151764`.  In particular, no such \(K\) exists for any
\(A>3600\) in that interval.

## Proof

The three functions in (1) are nonconstant, have pairwise nonparallel
linear parts, and satisfy
\[
f_1+f_2+f_3=-1.
\tag{8}
\]
Exact determinant, Minkowski-hull, halfplane-clipping, and shoelace
computations give
\[
M:=|A_0|=\frac{1428089}{200},
\qquad
m:=|A_2|=\frac{1529}{100},
\qquad
k_0:=|B|=\frac{2202453}{400}.
\tag{9}
\]
All three functions \(p_i=f_i-L_i=f_i+100\) are strictly positive on
\(A_0\).  Their respective minimum margins are
\[
\frac1{10},\qquad \frac{19}{10},\qquad \frac1{10}.
\tag{10}
\]
Thus
\[
u_i:=\left|A_0\cap\{f_i\le L_i\}\right|=0
\qquad(i=1,2,3).
\tag{11}
\]
The three opposite endpoint caps have exact areas
\[
\begin{aligned}
v_1&:=\left|A_2\cap\{f_1\le100\}\right|
=\frac{918929}{60400},\\
v_2&:=\left|A_2\cap\{f_2\le100\}\right|
=\frac{56573}{3800},\\
v_3&:=\left|A_2\cap\{f_3\le100\}\right|
=\frac{3083993}{202600}.
\end{aligned}
\tag{12}
\]
Their order is strict:
\[
v_3-v_1=\frac{490809}{61185200}>0,
\qquad
v_3-v_2=\frac{643709}{1924700}>0.
\tag{13}
\]

The endpoint datum also has the required positive opposite pair.
Writing \(q_i=f_i+L_i\) and
\[
Q_J=A_2\cap
\{q_j\le0\ (j\in J),\ q_j>0\ (j\notin J)\},
\]
exact clipping gives
\[
|Q_{\{3\}}|=\frac{4587}{60400}>0,
\qquad
|Q_{\{1,2\}}|=\frac{13761}{202600}>0.
\tag{14}
\]
Moreover
\[
\frac mM=\frac{3058}{1428089}<\frac38,
\tag{15}
\]
and \(A_0\) is a triangle, so the endpoint family has not lost the
intended asymmetric opposite-pair features.

Now assume that a compact convex \(K\) satisfies (4)--(5), and set
\[
c_i=|K\cap\{f_i\le0\}|.
\]
Since \(|K|=k_0+A\), equations (5), (9), (11), and (12) give
\[
\begin{aligned}
c_1&=\frac{2A}{9}+\frac{507094411}{181200},\\
c_2&=\frac{2A}{9}+\frac{7976753}{2850},\\
c_3&=\frac{2A}{9}+\frac{850472129}{303900}.
\end{aligned}
\tag{16}
\]
Equation (13) shows that
\[
\min\{c_1,c_2,c_3\}=c_3.
\tag{17}
\]

Accepted fact `5b75ecd9ec86dbcf` applies to the compact convex body
\(K\) and the three nonconcurrent halfplanes
\(\{f_i\le0\}\).  It gives the genuinely coupled necessary inequality
\[
5(c_1+c_2+c_3)-6\min_i c_i\ge4|K|.
\tag{18}
\]
Substituting (9), (16), and (17) into the left side of (18), and moving
the right side over, gives the exact affine residual
\[
\begin{aligned}
\Delta(A)
&:=5(c_1+c_2+c_3)-6c_3-4(k_0+A)\\
&=-2A+\frac{3678114899083}{1162518800}.
\end{aligned}
\tag{19}
\]
Therefore (18) forces
\[
A\le
\frac{3678114899083}{2325037600}
=A_\star,
\]
which proves (6).  For calibration, already at \(A=3600\),
\[
\Delta(3600)
=-\frac{4692020460917}{1162518800}<0.
\tag{20}
\]

It remains to compare the coupled cutoff with the scalar interval.
The exact gap between its lower endpoint and \(A_\star\) is
\[
\frac{23930259}{6650}-A_\star
=\frac{32820303025091}{16275263200}>0.
\tag{21}
\]
Hence every \(A\) in (7) is strictly larger than the maximum allowed by
(18).  This proves that the full scalar-completion interval, and a
fortiori its high-extension part \(A>3600\), contains no convex middle
completion.

All areas, cap orders, affine residuals, cutoffs, and endpoint
comparisons above were independently audited with SageMath \(10.8\)
over \(\mathbb Q\).  No floating-point or numerical-root decision is
used.
