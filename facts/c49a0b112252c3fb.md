## Statement

Work in the common endpoint coordinates
\[
X=p_j,\qquad Y=p_\ell,\qquad p_k=1-X-Y,
\]
corresponding to
\[
L_j=L_k=L_\ell=-\frac23
\]
and coordinate Jacobian
\[
\Delta=\frac{3001}{40000}.
\]
Use the exact-cell convention in which the only positive \(A_0\) cells are
\[
P_j,\qquad P_0,\qquad P_\ell,\qquad P_{k\ell}.
\]
There is no nonempty two-dimensional compact convex polygon \(A_0\) whose
physical cell areas are
\[
|P_j|=|P_\ell|=|P_{k\ell}|=\frac1{100},
\qquad
|P_0|=\frac{64}{25},
\tag{1}
\]
and whose one-sided \(\ell\)-trace at \(Y=0\) is
\[
\lambda_{0\ell}=\frac1{4000}.
\tag{2}
\]
This conclusion allows arbitrary piecewise-linear polygonal chord profiles.
Consequently no pair \(A_0,A_2\) can realize all the proposed endpoint cell
areas, strict lower crossings, retained traces, and the positive support-gap
direction, and no compatible middle polygon \(K\) can realize the proposed
middle cells and \(W=0\).

## Proof

Coordinate area is \(\Delta\) times physical area.  Thus (1) requires
\[
|P_0|_{XY}
=\Delta\frac{64}{25}
=\frac{3001}{15625}
\tag{3}
\]
and
\[
|P_\ell\cup P_{k\ell}|_{XY}
=\Delta\left(\frac1{100}+\frac1{100}\right)
=\frac{3001}{2000000}.
\tag{4}
\]

For \(y\) in the vertical projection of \(A_0\), let
\[
w(y)=
\mathcal H^1\{X:(X,y)\in A_0\}
\]
be its horizontal chord length in the \((X,Y)\)-plane.  Horizontal sections
of a convex planar body are intervals, and Minkowski interpolation of those
intervals shows that \(w\) is concave on its support interval.

The positive areas of \(P_0\) and \(P_\ell\) put interior points of \(A_0\)
on both sides of \(Y=0\).  Hence \(Y=0\) meets
\(\operatorname{int}A_0\), and the chord length is continuous there.
Along a \(Y\)-level line, the absolute rate of change of \(X\) with
respect to Euclidean arclength is
\(\Delta/\|\nabla f_\ell\|\).  Therefore the definition
\(\lambda_{0\ell}=\Delta\rho_{0\ell}^+\) identifies (2) with the
one-sided limit of the coordinate \(X\)-length.  Continuity now gives
\[
w(0)=\frac1{4000}.
\tag{5}
\]
Let
\[
g=w'_+(0).
\]
For a concave polygonal function, \(g\) exists and is a supergradient at
zero, so
\[
w(y)\le w(0)+gy
\tag{6}
\]
for every \(y\) in the support interval.

Every point of \(P_0\) has
\[
Y>0,\qquad X>0,\qquad X+Y<1,
\]
and therefore \(0<Y<1\).  It follows from (3) that
\[
\frac{3001}{15625}
=|P_0|_{XY}
\le\int_0^1w(y)\,dy.
\tag{7}
\]
If \(g\le0\), then (6) gives \(w(y)\le w(0)\) for \(y\ge0\), and (7)
would give
\[
\frac{3001}{15625}\le\frac1{4000},
\]
which is false.  Thus \(g>0\).  The tangent in (6) is then positive on
\([0,1]\), and extending \(w\) by zero outside its projection gives
\[
\frac{3001}{15625}
\le\int_0^1w(y)\,dy
\le\int_0^1\left(\frac1{4000}+gy\right)dy
=\frac1{4000}+\frac g2.
\]
Consequently
\[
g\ge
2\left(\frac{3001}{15625}-\frac1{4000}\right).
\tag{8}
\]

For \(y\le0\), inequalities (5)-(6) and nonnegativity of \(w\) imply
that the lower endpoint of the vertical projection is at least
\(-1/(4000g)\).  Hence the whole coordinate area of \(A_0\) below
\(Y=0\) is at most the area under that tangent triangle:
\[
\begin{aligned}
|A_0\cap\{Y<0\}|_{XY}
&=\int_{-\infty}^0w(y)\,dy\\
&\le
\int_{-1/(4000g)}^0
\left(\frac1{4000}+gy\right)dy\\
&=\frac{1}{2g\,4000^2}\\
&\le
\frac{(1/4000)^2}
{4\left(3001/15625-1/4000\right)}
=\frac1{12276096},
\end{aligned}
\tag{9}
\]
where the last inequality uses (8).  The exact simplification follows from
\[
\frac{3001}{15625}-\frac1{4000}
=\frac{95907}{500000}.
\]

Under the stated exact-cell support, the only positive-area cells below
\(Y=0\) are \(P_\ell\) and \(P_{k\ell}\).  Thus (4) instead requires
\[
|A_0\cap\{Y<0\}|_{XY}
=\frac{3001}{2000000}
>\frac1{12276096},
\tag{10}
\]
contradicting (9).  Therefore no such \(A_0\) exists.

This contradiction uses neither the clamped-affine profiles excluded by
accepted fact `fb8b8546366584e7` nor any other global specification of the
cap functions.  It applies to every rational polygonal choice with the exact
cell areas and trace above.  Accepted fact `331355cde8383f52` can turn a
positive support-gap witness into absence of the forbidden midpoint chamber
only after actual endpoint bodies exist; (9)-(10) show that these endpoint
data never reach that stage.  Hence no construction of \(A_2\) or \(K\) can
complete the proposed common-plane realization.
