## Statement

Let \(N=\{1,2,3\}\). Assign every unlisted cell mass zero and, for distinct \(i,j\in N\), set
\[
p_\varnothing=56,\qquad p_{\{i\}}=1,
\]
\[
q_N=1,\qquad q_{N\setminus\{i\}}=1,
\]
\[
r_{\{i\}}=7,\qquad r_{\{i,j\}}=4,\qquad r_N=3.
\]
Then
\[
M=59,\qquad m=4,\qquad k=36.
\]
With the notation of accepted facts `0819231d0af83197`, `2bb72698f0248a65`, `624b129b68e0fdee`, `8234d1112ec2f031`, `91cb5bfc71da49ac`, `93f1cefaa4795a25`, `ce343e749df349fe`, and `e1361027c1dda6fa`, this nonnegative all-double scalar table satisfies every listed old condition and \(W=0\). All three singleton masses \(r_{\{i\}}\) are positive, and
\[
\left(\sum_{i=1}^3\sqrt{r_{\{i\}}+\frac{2r_N}{9}}\right)^2
<2k,
\qquad
\left(\sum_{i=1}^3\sqrt{r_{\{i\}}}\right)^2<2k.
\]
No common-plane geometric realization is asserted.

## Proof

The totals are
\[
56+3=59,\qquad 1+3=4,
\]
and
\[
3\cdot7+3\cdot4+3=36.
\]
All four prescribed \(Q\)-cells are positive, so this is exactly the all-double support of accepted fact `91cb5bfc71da49ac`.

For every \(i\),
\[
u_i=1,\qquad v_i=1+2\cdot1=3,
\]
and
\[
c_i=7+2\cdot4+3=18.
\]
Moreover,
\[
h=\frac{2(M+m+k)}9
=\frac{2(59+4+36)}9
=22.
\]
Hence all three crossings hold:
\[
u_i+v_i+c_i=1+3+18=22=h.
\]
The canonical tails from accepted fact `624b129b68e0fdee` hold at equality:
\[
c_i+m=18+4=22=h.
\]
Also
\[
M>m>0,\qquad
0<u_i<M,\qquad
0<v_i<m,\qquad
0<c_i<k,\qquad
0<h<M.
\]
Thus this is the proper-proper equality-tail scalar state.

Put
\[
U=\sum_i u_i=3,\qquad
E_R=r_{12}+r_{13}+r_{23}+2r_N=12+6=18.
\]
The defect identity of accepted fact `0819231d0af83197` holds exactly:
\[
2M+2m-3\sum_i(u_i+v_i)-k
=118+8-36-36
=54
=3E_R.
\]
Its all-double excess specialization is
\[
U+m+q_N+E_R
=3+4+1+18
=26
=\frac{2M-m-k}{3}.
\]
Therefore the identity for \(W\) in accepted fact `91cb5bfc71da49ac` gives
\[
W
=3\left(U+m+q_N+E_R\right)-(2M-m-k)
=3\cdot26-78
=0.
\]

For the source \(Q\)-caps, put \(x_i=q_{N\setminus\{i\}}=1\). Both inequalities of accepted fact `8234d1112ec2f031` hold at equality:
\[
q_N^2m=4=4x_1x_2x_3,
\]
and
\[
\sum_i\sqrt{\frac{x_i}{m}}
=3\sqrt{\frac14}
=\frac32.
\]

For the overlap inequality of accepted fact `2bb72698f0248a65`,
\[
\frac14\sum_i(\sqrt{p_\varnothing}+\sqrt{x_i})^2
=\frac34(\sqrt{56}+1)^2
=\frac{171}{4}+\frac32\sqrt{56}.
\]
Its right-hand side is
\[
k-r_N+\sum_i r_{\{i\}}
=36-3+21
=54.
\]
Since
\[
56<\left(\frac{15}{2}\right)^2,
\]
one has
\[
\frac{171}{4}+\frac32\sqrt{56}
<
\frac{171}{4}+\frac{45}{4}
=54.
\]
Thus the overlap inequality is strict.

All three pair masses equal \(4>0\), and
\[
t=r_N=3>0.
\]
Thus the middle-support implication of accepted fact `93f1cefaa4795a25` holds.

For every \(i\), the corrected quantity of accepted fact `ce343e749df349fe` lies in its positive branch:
\[
e_i
=\left(7-\frac{4^2}{3}\right)_+
=\frac53>0.
\]
Consequently
\[
k-\sum_i e_i=36-5=31,
\]
and the corrected product inequality is strict:
\[
\left(k-\sum_i e_i\right)^2k
=31^2\cdot36
=34596
>
\frac{500}{27}
=4e_1e_2e_3.
\]
The corrected square-root inequality is also strict:
\[
\sum_i\sqrt{e_i}
=3\sqrt{\frac53}
<9
=\frac32\sqrt{k},
\]
where squaring the positive sides reduces the comparison to \(15<81\).

Finally, all three singleton masses are \(7>0\). With \(t=r_N=3\),
\[
r_{\{i\}}+\frac{2t}{9}
=7+\frac23
=\frac{23}{3}.
\]
Hence the strengthened inequality of accepted fact `e1361027c1dda6fa` is strict:
\[
\left(\sum_i\sqrt{r_{\{i\}}+\frac{2t}{9}}\right)^2
=\left(3\sqrt{\frac{23}{3}}\right)^2
=69
<72
=2k.
\]
The ordinary cyclic inequality from the same accepted fact is also strict:
\[
\left(\sum_i\sqrt{r_{\{i\}}}\right)^2
=(3\sqrt7)^2
=63
<72
=2k.
\]
This completes the exact scalar verification.
