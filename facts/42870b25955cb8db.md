## Statement

Let \(N=\{1,2,3\}\). Assume the full actual dominant rank-two all-double common-plane hypotheses and exact-cell convention of accepted facts 29dfdfc94477e532, e4694936037f7d3f, and 3f26802efaf69679. Thus
\[
B_0=\frac{A_0+A_2}{2}\subseteq K,\qquad
f_1+f_2+f_3=-1,
\]
where \(A_0,A_2,K\) are compact two-dimensional convex polytopes and the linear parts of the affine functions \(f_i\) span \(\mathbb R^2\). Write
\[
M=|A_0|,\qquad m=|A_2|,\qquad k=|K|,\qquad
h=\frac{2(M+m+k)}9,
\]
\[
p_J=|P_J|,\qquad q_H=|Q_H|,\qquad r_G=|R_G|,
\]
\[
u_i=\sum_{J\ni i}p_J,\qquad
v_i=\sum_{H\ni i}q_H,\qquad
c_i=\sum_{G\ni i}r_G,\qquad U=u_1+u_2+u_3.
\]
Assume \(M\geq m\), the three crossings and canonical tails,
\[
u_i+v_i+c_i=h,\qquad c_i+m\geq h,\qquad c_i+M\geq h,
\]
and let the positive \(Q\)-support be \(\{123,12,13,23\}\), with dominant index \(1\), so
\[
q=q_{123}>0,\qquad x_i=q_{N\setminus\{i\}}>0,\qquad
m=q+x_1+x_2+x_3,\qquad x_1>\frac{5m}{9}.
\]
Put
\[
E_R=r_{12}+r_{13}+r_{23}+2r_{123},
\]
and assume the all-double \(W=0\) identity
\[
3(U+m+q+E_R)=2M-m-k.
\]
The middle cells satisfy
\[
r_1r_2r_3r_{123}>0,
\]
For cyclic \((i,j,k)\), let \(O_i\) be the point where \(f_j=f_k=0\), write
\[
h_X(g)=\max_{x\in X}g(x),\qquad
I_i=\{r>0:h_{B_0}(f_k+r f_j)\leq0\},
\]
and assume total corner collapse:
\[
\operatorname{int}_{\mathbb R}I_i=\varnothing
\qquad(i=1,2,3).
\]
Use the dominant bodies and notation of accepted fact 29dfdfc94477e532:
\[
P=\overline{P_\varnothing},\qquad
C=C_1^\sharp,\qquad
p=|P|,\qquad d=|C|,\qquad
S_1=\frac{P+C}{2},
\]
\[
\lambda_1=\frac{r_{123}\eta_1}{2},\qquad
\left|K\cap\{f_1\geq0\}\right|=A_1,
\]
and normalize planar mixed area by
\[
|P+tC|=p+2t\mathcal V(P,C)+t^2d.
\]
Put
\[
a=\min_{S_1}f_1,\qquad b=\max_{S_1}f_1,
\qquad
\Delta_P=p_{12}+p_{13}+p_{23}.
\]
Define
\[
\mathscr B
=
\frac{13p}{6}
-\frac{14}{3}(m+q+E_R)
-2x_1-2(u_2+u_3)
-\frac d2-2\lambda_1.
\]

Let
\[
\mathcal T=\operatorname{conv}\{O_1,O_2,O_3\},
\qquad E=[O_2,O_3].
\]
Choose \(x\in S_1\) with \(f_1(x)=b\), and define
\[
\Delta_b=\operatorname{conv}(E\cup\{x\}),\qquad
Z_1=\Delta_b\cap\{0\leq f_1\leq a\},
\]
\[
\Theta=|Z_1|
=r_{123}\left(2a-\frac{a^2}{b}\right).
\]
Finally define the uncovered positive-cap area and full noncentral remainder by
\[
\rho_1
=\left|
\bigl(K\cap\{f_1\geq0\}\bigr)\setminus(S_1\cup Z_1)
\right|,
\]
\[
\Gamma_1=k-r_{123}-|S_1|-\Theta.
\]
Then
\[
\rho_1\geq0,
\]
\[
\boxed{
\mathcal V(P,C)+\frac83\Delta_P
=
\mathscr B-2\Theta+2\lambda_1-2\rho_1,
}
\tag{1}
\]
and
\[
\boxed{
\Gamma_1=\rho_1+r_1+r_{12}+r_{13}>\rho_1.
}
\tag{2}
\]
Consequently,
\[
\boxed{
\mathcal V(P,C)+\frac83\Delta_P
>
\mathscr B-2\Theta+2\lambda_1-2\Gamma_1.
}
\tag{3}
\]
In contrast, the formerly proposed stronger direction
\[
\mathcal V(P,C)+\frac83\Delta_P
>
\mathscr B-2\Theta+2\lambda_1
\tag{4}
\]
is equivalent by (1) to \(\rho_1<0\), and is therefore impossible in every realization satisfying the hypotheses. Equivalently, every such realization satisfies the opposite non-strict inequality obtained from (4) by replacing \(>\) with \(\leq\).

All conclusions hold uniformly for all eight choices in which each collapsed interval \(I_i\) is either empty or a singleton tangent, including every endpoint-coincidence and proper-cell boundary allowed by the exact-cell convention.

## Proof

Accepted fact e4694936037f7d3f proves, uniformly over all eight empty/singleton collapse strata,
\[
\mathcal T\subseteq B_0.
\tag{5}
\]
Accepted fact 3f26802efaf69679 proves
\[
0<\frac{\eta_1}{2}\leq a<b
\tag{6}
\]
and \(S_1\subseteq B_0\). In particular, the maximizer \(x\) in the statement exists. The edge \(E\) lies in \(\mathcal T\), so (5), convexity of \(B_0\), and \(x\in S_1\subseteq B_0\) give
\[
\Delta_b\subseteq B_0\subseteq K.
\tag{7}
\]
The affine function \(f_1\) is zero on \(E\), equals \(-1\) at the opposite vertex \(O_1\) of \(\mathcal T\), and equals \(b\) at \(x\). Hence \(\mathcal T\) and \(\Delta_b\) have the same base \(E\), with affine \(f_1\)-heights \(1\) and \(b\), respectively. Since \(|\mathcal T|=r_{123}\),
\[
|\Delta_b|=br_{123}.
\tag{8}
\]
The cap of \(\Delta_b\) on which \(a\leq f_1\leq b\) is similar to \(\Delta_b\), with linear ratio \((b-a)/b\). Therefore
\[
\begin{aligned}
|Z_1|
&=br_{123}
-br_{123}\left(\frac{b-a}{b}\right)^2\\
&=r_{123}\left(2a-\frac{a^2}{b}\right)
=\Theta.
\end{aligned}
\tag{9}
\]

Every point of \(Z_1\) has \(0\leq f_1\leq a\), while every point of \(S_1\) has \(f_1\geq a\). Their possible intersection is contained in the affine line \(f_1=a\), which has planar area zero. Both sets lie in \(K\cap\{f_1\geq0\}\) by (7) and the definition of \(S_1\). The definition of \(\rho_1\) therefore gives the exact area decomposition
\[
A_1=|S_1|+\Theta+\rho_1,
\qquad \rho_1\geq0.
\tag{10}
\]
This argument uses only (5), so it does not distinguish the eight collapse strata.

We next derive the exact ledger identity. Accepted fact 29dfdfc94477e532 gives
\[
|S_1|=\frac{p+d+2\mathcal V(P,C)}4.
\tag{11}
\]
The same fact defines
\[
D_0=M-p,\qquad U=u_1+u_2+u_3
\]
and gives the exact crossing formula
\[
A_1
=\frac{4M-7m-7(U+q+E_R)}3+u_1-x_1.
\]
Since \(P_{123}\) is empty, exact-cell multiplicities give
\[
U-D_0=p_{12}+p_{13}+p_{23}=\Delta_P.
\]
Substituting \(M=p+D_0\) and \(D_0=U-\Delta_P\) into the preceding crossing formula gives
\[
A_1
=
\frac{4p-7m-7q-7E_R}{3}
-x_1-u_2-u_3-\frac43\Delta_P.
\tag{12}
\]
Subtracting (11) and \(\lambda_1\) from (12), and using the definition of \(\mathscr B\), gives
\[
A_1-|S_1|-\lambda_1
=
\frac{\mathscr B-\mathcal V(P,C)}2-\frac43\Delta_P.
\tag{13}
\]
On the other hand, (10) gives
\[
A_1-|S_1|-\lambda_1
=\Theta+\rho_1-\lambda_1.
\tag{14}
\]
Equating (13) and (14), multiplying by \(2\), and rearranging proves (1).

Let
\[
c_1=\left|K\cap\{f_1\leq0\}\right|.
\]
The cutting line \(f_1=0\) has area zero, so
\[
k=A_1+c_1.
\tag{15}
\]
The exact middle-cell partition gives
\[
c_1=r_1+r_{12}+r_{13}+r_{123}.
\tag{16}
\]
Substituting (10), (15), and (16) into the definition of \(\Gamma_1\) yields
\[
\begin{aligned}
\Gamma_1
&=k-r_{123}-|S_1|-\Theta\\
&=\rho_1+c_1-r_{123}\\
&=\rho_1+r_1+r_{12}+r_{13}.
\end{aligned}
\]
Exact-cell positivity gives \(r_1>0\), while \(r_{12},r_{13}\geq0\). This proves (2). Subtracting the right side of (3) from the left side and using (1)-(2) gives
\[
2(\Gamma_1-\rho_1)
=2(r_1+r_{12}+r_{13})>0,
\]
which proves (3).

Finally, (1) turns (4) into
\[
-2\rho_1>0,
\]
equivalently \(\rho_1<0\), contradicting (10). Thus the stronger strict direction is impossible in any actual realization, while its non-strict reverse follows directly from \(\rho_1\geq0\). Every boundary and endpoint coincidence used above lies on an affine cutting or support line of area zero, so the exact weak/strict cell convention preserves all area identities. This completes the uniform proof.
