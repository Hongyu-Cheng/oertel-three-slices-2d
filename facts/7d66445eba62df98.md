## Statement

Assume the surviving transformed triangular system of accepted facts `09393c6edf1fc4e2`, `13c0df55864b79eb`, `64760c76cadff481`, and `f8e871d10dac1791` in the orbit
\[
I_0=\{1,2\},\qquad I_1=\{3\},\qquad t=\frac{2(1+A+B)}9\leq\frac12.
\]
Thus \(0<A<B\), \(A+B>1\), \(B<1+A\), and \(P_0,P_1,P_2\) have normalized areas \(A,1,B\), satisfy
\[
\frac{P_0+P_2}{2}\subseteq P_1,
\]
and have coordinate-cap fractions \(u_i,c_i,v_i\) with
\[
Au_i+c_i+Bv_i<t\qquad(i=1,2,3).
\]
Then
\[
A+\sum_{i=1}^3c_i<3t,
\qquad
A+3\sum_{i=1}^3c_i<2+2B,
\qquad
\boxed{\sum_{i=1}^3c_i<\frac{11}{8}}.
\tag{1}
\]
Moreover the exact third-row cap satisfies the strictly stronger complement-allocation inequality
\[
\boxed{\sqrt{A-t+c_3}+\sqrt B<2\sqrt{1-c_3}}.
\tag{2}
\]

## Proof

Every point of \(P_0\) has coordinate sum \(1+q>0\), so its three closed coordinate-nonnegative caps cover \(P_0\).  Hence
\[
\sum_i u_i\geq1.
\]
Summing the three strict row budgets and discarding the nonnegative \(B\sum_i v_i\) gives
\[
A+\sum_i c_i
\leq A\sum_i u_i+\sum_i c_i
<3t.
\]
Substitution of \(3t=2(1+A+B)/3\) gives the second inequality in (1).

It remains to obtain the numerical constant \(11/8\).  The second inequality in (1) is
\[
\sum_i c_i<\frac{2+2B-A}{3}.
\tag{7}
\]
If \(A\leq1/8\), then \(B<1+A\) gives
\[
2B-A<2+A\leq\frac{17}{8}.
\]
If \(A\geq1/8\), then \(t\leq1/2\), equivalently \(A+B\leq5/4\), gives
\[
2B-A\leq\frac52-3A\leq\frac{17}{8}.
\]
Combining either case with (7) proves the strict bound in (1).

We next prove (2).  Put
\[
X=Au_3,\qquad Y=Bv_3,\qquad b=t-c_3.
\]
Accepted fact `f8e871d10dac1791`, using the exact cap formulas of accepted fact `64760c76cadff481`, gives \(c_3\geq4/9\).  The third strict row budget gives \(X+Y<b\), while accepted fact `13c0df55864b79eb` gives
\[
0<b\leq t-\frac49=\frac{2(A+B-1)}9<\frac{4A}{9}<A\leq B.
\tag{8}
\]
Let
\[
D_j=P_j\cap\{x_3\leq0\}\qquad(j=0,1,2).
\]
All three coordinate restrictions are nonconstant on the nondegenerate triangles, so zero-coordinate lines have area zero, including when a vertex entry is zero.  Thus
\[
|D_0|=A-X,\qquad |D_1|=1-c_3,\qquad |D_2|=B-Y.
\]
Compatibility gives \((D_0+D_2)/2\subseteq D_1\), and planar Brunn--Minkowski gives
\[
\sqrt{A-X}+\sqrt{B-Y}\leq2\sqrt{1-c_3}.
\tag{9}
\]
Set \(s=X+Y<b\).  For fixed \(s\), the function
\[
\xi\longmapsto\sqrt{A-\xi}+\sqrt{B-s+\xi},
\qquad0\leq\xi\leq s,
\]
is concave, so its minimum is at an endpoint.  Because \(A\leq B\) and \(s<A\),
\[
\sqrt A-\sqrt{A-s}\geq\sqrt B-\sqrt{B-s},
\]
so the smaller endpoint is \(\xi=s\).  Therefore
\[
\sqrt{A-X}+\sqrt{B-Y}
\geq\sqrt{A-s}+\sqrt B
>\sqrt{A-b}+\sqrt B.
\]
Combining this strict lower bound with (9) proves (2).  This proof uses only closed caps and therefore also proves the asserted boundary statement.
