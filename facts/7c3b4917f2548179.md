## Statement

Let \(A_0,A_2,K\subset\mathbb R^2\) be nonempty two-dimensional convex polytopes with
\[
\frac{A_0+A_2}{2}\subseteq K,
\qquad
M=|A_0|\ge m=|A_2|>0,
\qquad
k=|K|,
\qquad
t=\frac{2(M+m+k)}9<M.
\]
Let \(f_1,f_2,f_3\) be nonconstant affine functions with pairwise nonparallel linear parts and
\[
f_1+f_2+f_3=-1.
\]
For \(j=1,2,3\), set
\[
c_j=|K\cap\{f_j\le0\}|,
\qquad
\mu_j(a)=c_j+|A_0\cap\{f_j\le a\}|
                 +|A_2\cap\{f_j\le-a\}|,
\]
\[
I_j=\{a:\mu_j(a)<t\}.
\]
Assume
\[
c_j+m\ge t,\qquad I_j\ne\varnothing,
\qquad L_j=\inf I_j
\quad(j=1,2,3).
\tag{1}
\]
Suppose that, for one index \(q\),
\[
L_q<\alpha_q:=\min_{A_0}f_q,
\tag{2}
\]
and suppose, toward the strict-low alternative, that
\[
S:=L_1+L_2+L_3<-1.
\tag{3}
\]
Write \(\{i,\ell,q\}=\{1,2,3\}\), and define
\[
\Delta_{i\ell}
=\left|\det(\nabla f_i,\nabla f_\ell)\right|>0,
\qquad
D=-1-\alpha_q-L_i-L_\ell,
\qquad
D_+=\max\{D,0\}.
\tag{4}
\]
Then
\[
\boxed{\quad
M-\frac{D_+^2}{2\Delta_{i\ell}}<2m.
\quad}
\tag{5}
\]
Equivalently,
\[
D_+^2>2\Delta_{i\ell}(M-2m).
\tag{6}
\]
In particular, if \(m\le M/2\), then \(D>0\), and
\[
D^2>2\Delta_{i\ell}(M-2m).
\tag{7}
\]
Consequently, no rational triangle or quadrilateral \(A_0\), with rational affine rows and rational levels, can occur in a strict-empty counterexample when
\[
\bigl(\max\{-1-\min_{A_0}f_q-L_i-L_\ell,0\}\bigr)^2
\le
2\left|\det(\nabla f_i,\nabla f_\ell)\right|(M-2m).
\tag{8}
\]

The excluded class is nonvacuous inside the exact residual area band. For an explicit rational calibration, take
\[
f_1(x,y)=x,\qquad f_2(x,y)=y,\qquad
f_3(x,y)=-1-x-y,
\tag{9}
\]
\[
A_0=
\left[-\frac{185}{6},\frac{181}{6}\right]
\times
\left[-\frac{65}{12},\frac{301}{12}\right],
\tag{10}
\]
\[
A_2=
\left[-\frac{21}{2},\frac{59}{6}\right]
\times
\left[\frac{19}{4},\frac{179}{12}\right],
\tag{11}
\]
\[
K=\operatorname{conv}\{(-41,20),(20,-41),(20,20)\}.
\tag{12}
\]
Put
\[
L_2=-\frac{41767}{6588},
\qquad
L_1=L_3=\frac{41}{24}.
\tag{13}
\]
Then the slices are compatible, their area ratios lie strictly in the raw residual band, all three middle-cap tails are strict, row \(2\) has a genuine finite strict-empty lower crossing at \(L_2\), and
\[
L_1+L_2+L_3=-\frac{9629}{3294}<-1.
\tag{14}
\]
However, for \(q=2\), the value in (4) is \(D=1\), while
\[
2\Delta_{13}(M-2m)=\frac{26047}{9}>1.
\tag{15}
\]
Therefore rows \(1\) and \(3\) cannot both have finite lower crossings at the proposed levels. More generally, (8) excludes every rational triangular or quadrilateral replacement of \(A_0\) with the same \(M,m\), row-\(2\) support, and two-direction wedge budget, independently of the detailed shapes of \(A_2\) and \(K\).

## Proof

Put
\[
\delta=-1-S>0,
\qquad
g=\alpha_q-L_q>0.
\tag{16}
\]
For \(x\in A_0\), define
\[
p_j(x)=f_j(x)-L_j.
\]
The affine-plane identity and (3) give
\[
p_1+p_2+p_3=\delta.
\tag{17}
\]
Condition (2) gives \(p_q(x)\ge g\) throughout \(A_0\). Hence
\[
p_i(x)+p_\ell(x)=\delta-p_q(x)\le\delta-g.
\tag{18}
\]
The right side is exactly
\[
\delta-g
=-1-S-\alpha_q+L_q
=-1-\alpha_q-L_i-L_\ell
=D.
\tag{19}
\]

Let
\[
C_i=A_0\cap\{f_i\le L_i\},
\qquad
C_\ell=A_0\cap\{f_\ell\le L_\ell\},
\tag{20}
\]
and write \(u_i=|C_i|\), \(u_\ell=|C_\ell|\). Up to the two null cutting lines,
\[
A_0\setminus(C_i\cup C_\ell)
=A_0\cap\{p_i>0,\ p_\ell>0\}.
\tag{21}
\]
The affine map
\[
x\longmapsto(p_i(x),p_\ell(x))
\tag{22}
\]
has absolute Jacobian \(\Delta_{i\ell}\). By (18), the image of the set in (21) is contained in
\[
\{(a,b):a>0,\ b>0,\ a+b\le D\}.
\tag{23}
\]
This set is empty for \(D\le0\), and for \(D>0\) it is a right triangle of area \(D^2/2\). Therefore
\[
\left|A_0\setminus(C_i\cup C_\ell)\right|
\le\frac{D_+^2}{2\Delta_{i\ell}}.
\tag{24}
\]
Since the area of a union is at most the sum of the two cap areas,
\[
u_i+u_\ell
\ge |C_i\cup C_\ell|
\ge M-\frac{D_+^2}{2\Delta_{i\ell}}.
\tag{25}
\]
This is the two-direction support-clearance estimate.

Accepted fact `624b129b68e0fdee` applies separately to rows \(i\) and \(\ell\) under (1). It gives the two crossing equalities
\[
c_j+u_j+v_j=t,
\qquad
v_j:=|A_2\cap\{f_j\le-L_j\}|>0
\quad(j=i,\ell),
\tag{26}
\]
and it includes every allowed empty, proper, and full endpoint-cap state. Combining (26) with the tail inequality \(c_j+m\ge t\) gives
\[
u_j=t-c_j-v_j\le m-v_j<m
\quad(j=i,\ell).
\tag{27}
\]
This remains strict in every boundary state: if \(c_j+m=t\) and the row is empty-full, then \(u_j=0\) and \(v_j=m\); in the proper-proper state \(v_j>0\); and if \(c_j+m>t\), the small-end cap is proper and again has positive area. Thus
\[
u_i+u_\ell<2m.
\tag{28}
\]
Equations (25) and (28) prove (5), and rearrangement gives (6). If \(m\le M/2\), the right side of (6) is nonnegative while the inequality is strict. Hence \(D_+>0\), so \(D>0\), and (7) follows.

The factor \(1/(2\Delta_{i\ell})\) in (24) is sharp before imposing the crossings. Indeed, in the affine coordinates (22), take \(A_0\) to be the inverse image of
\[
\{(a,b):a\ge0,\ b\ge0,\ a+b\le D\}.
\tag{29}
\]
Then \(p_q=\delta-a-b\ge\delta-D=g\), with equality on the hypotenuse, and the missed upper-upper region has area exactly \(D^2/(2\Delta_{i\ell})\). For rational affine data and rational \(D>0\), this is a rational triangle. Thus improving (5) requires further crossing or compatibility input; the support-wedge area itself has no slack.

It remains to audit the rational calibration. The three areas are
\[
M=|A_0|=\frac{3721}{2},
\qquad
m=|A_2|=\frac{3721}{18},
\qquad
k=|K|=\frac{3721}{2},
\tag{30}
\]
and hence
\[
t=\frac{70699}{81}.
\tag{31}
\]
Moreover,
\[
\frac{A_0+A_2}{2}
=\left[-\frac{62}{3},20\right]
\times\left[-\frac13,20\right].
\tag{32}
\]
The triangle \(K\) is
\[
\{(x,y):x\le20,\ y\le20,\ x+y\ge-21\},
\]
so (32) is contained in \(K\), with its lower-left vertex on \(x+y=-21\). This proves compatibility.

The normalized area ratios are
\[
r=\frac mM=\frac19,\qquad s=\frac kM=1.
\]
They obey the exact raw residual inequalities
\[
\frac{(1+\sqrt r)^2}{4}=\frac49\le1,
\qquad
1-r=\frac89<1<\frac{10}{9}=1+r,
\]
\[
1<2-r=\frac{17}{9},
\qquad
1>\frac{3r+2\sqrt r-1}{2}=0.
\tag{33}
\]

Each of the three central caps of \(K\) is a right triangle with legs \(41\), so
\[
c_1=c_2=c_3=\frac{1681}{2}.
\tag{34}
\]
Consequently,
\[
t-c_j=\frac{5237}{162},
\qquad
c_j+m-t=\frac{14126}{81}>0
\quad(j=1,2,3).
\tag{35}
\]
Thus every middle-cap tail is strict.

For row \(2\), the lower \(A_0\)-support and upper \(A_2\)-support are
\[
\alpha_2=-\frac{65}{12},
\qquad
\beta_2=\frac{179}{12}.
\tag{36}
\]
The proposed level satisfies
\[
-\beta_2<L_2<\alpha_2,
\qquad
\alpha_2-L_2=\frac{3041}{3294}>0.
\tag{37}
\]
Hence the \(A_0\)-cap is literally empty. The threshold \(-L_2\) cuts from the bottom of the rectangle \(A_2\) a strip of height
\[
\frac{41767}{6588}-\frac{19}{4}
=\frac{5237}{3294}.
\]
Since the horizontal width of \(A_2\) is \(61/3\), the cap area is
\[
v_2
=\frac{61}{3}\frac{5237}{3294}
=\frac{5237}{162}
=t-c_2.
\tag{38}
\]
Thus \(\mu_2(L_2)=t\). On the interval
\[
(-\beta_2,\alpha_2)
\]
the \(A_0\)-cap remains empty while this rectangular \(A_2\)-cap decreases strictly as the level increases. To the left, the eventual tail has value \(c_2+m>t\) by (35); immediately to the right of \(L_2\), the value is below \(t\). Therefore \(L_2=\inf I_2\), so this is a genuine finite lower crossing, not merely a mass equality.

Finally, direct arithmetic gives
\[
L_1+L_2+L_3=-\frac{9629}{3294}<-1,
\qquad
\delta=\frac{6335}{3294},
\qquad
g=\frac{3041}{3294},
\qquad
D=\delta-g=1.
\tag{39}
\]
Here \(\Delta_{13}=1\), while
\[
2(M-2m)
=2\left(\frac{3721}{2}-\frac{3721}{9}\right)
=\frac{26047}{9}>1=D^2.
\tag{40}
\]
This violates the necessary inequality (7). Equivalently, the part of \(A_0\) missed by both proposed row-\(1\) and row-\(3\) lower caps has area at most \(1/2\), so those two caps have union area at least \(M-1/2\), whereas two genuine crossings and their tails would force their summed area to be strictly below \(2m\). This completes both the general filter and the exact rational audit, including all possible empty/full states of the two remaining crossings.
