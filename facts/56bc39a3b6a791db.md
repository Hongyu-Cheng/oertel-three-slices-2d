## Statement

Let \(\Delta\) be a nondegenerate triangle and let \((a\Delta,\Delta,c\Delta)\) be residual in the sense of accepted fact `8be4b2011a05cfd2`, with \(a\ge c>0\). Put \(x=c/a\), and define
\[
q=\frac{\sqrt{10}-1}{3},
\qquad
\theta=\frac{\sqrt{26}-1}{5}.
\]
The covered subset
\[
\{(a,c)\text{ residual}:a\ge1,\ c\le 1/(3\sqrt2)\}
\]
is exactly the disjoint union of
\[
0<x\le\frac1{\sqrt{19}},
\qquad
1\le a<\frac1{\sqrt{1-x^2}},
\tag{C1}
\]
and
\[
\frac1{\sqrt{19}}<x\le\frac{\sqrt2}{6},
\qquad
1\le a\le\frac{\sqrt2}{6x}.
\tag{C2}
\]
Thus, within the residual \(a\ge1\) sector, the uncovered subset is exactly
\[
\frac1{\sqrt{19}}<x\le\frac{\sqrt2}{6},
\qquad
\frac{\sqrt2}{6x}<a<\frac1{\sqrt{1-x^2}},
\tag{U1}
\]
\[
\frac{\sqrt2}{6}<x\le\frac35,
\qquad
1\le a<\frac1{\sqrt{1-x^2}},
\tag{U2}
\]
or
\[
\frac35<x<q,
\qquad
1\le a<\sqrt{\frac2{3x^2+2x-1}}.
\tag{U3}
\]
The complement of the covered subset in the entire residual domain additionally contains, exactly, the \(a<1\) pieces
\[
0<x\le\frac1{\sqrt2},
\qquad
\frac1{\sqrt{1+x^2}}<a<1,
\tag{U4}
\]
\[
\frac1{\sqrt2}<x\le q,
\qquad
\frac1{\sqrt{2-x^2}}<a<1,
\tag{U5}
\]
and
\[
q<x<\theta,
\qquad
\frac1{\sqrt{2-x^2}}<a<
\sqrt{\frac2{3x^2+2x-1}}.
\tag{U6}
\]

## Proof

Accepted fact `8be4b2011a05cfd2` gives the exact \(a\ge1\) residual sector as
\[
0<x\le\frac35,
\qquad
1\le a<\frac1{\sqrt{1-x^2}},
\tag{1}
\]
or
\[
\frac35<x<q,
\qquad
1\le a<\sqrt{\frac2{3x^2+2x-1}}.
\tag{2}
\]
Since \(c=ax\), the small-\(c\) condition is
\[
a\le\frac1{3\sqrt2\,x}=\frac{\sqrt2}{6x}.
\tag{3}
\]
Because \(a\ge1\), condition (3) forces \(x\le\sqrt2/6<3/5\). Hence every covered pair lies in (1), and no pair in (2) is covered.

On (1), compare the cap in (3) with the residual upper endpoint. Since both are positive,
\[
\frac{\sqrt2}{6x}\ge\frac1{\sqrt{1-x^2}}
\quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad
\frac1{18x^2}\ge\frac1{1-x^2}
\quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad
x\le\frac1{\sqrt{19}}.
\tag{4}
\]
Also,
\[
\frac{\sqrt2}{6x}\ge1
\quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad
x\le\frac{\sqrt2}{6}.
\tag{5}
\]
The exact ordering
\[
\frac1{\sqrt{19}}<\frac{\sqrt2}{6}<\frac35
\]
follows by squaring, since \(1/19<1/18<9/25\).

For \(0<x\le1/\sqrt{19}\), (4) says that the cap is at least the strict residual upper endpoint, so every pair in (1) is covered, giving (C1). For \(1/\sqrt{19}<x\le\sqrt2/6\), (4)-(5) place the cap between the two endpoints of (1), inclusively at the lower endpoint and strictly below the upper endpoint except at the excluded transition value. This gives (C2). Taking the complement inside (1) gives (U1) and (U2), while all of (2) gives (U3).

Finally, accepted fact `8be4b2011a05cfd2` states that the complete residual \(a<1\) sector is exactly (U4)-(U6). These pairs fail \(a\ge1\), so none is covered. Consequently (U1)-(U6) are exactly the complement of (C1)-(C2) in the full residual domain.
