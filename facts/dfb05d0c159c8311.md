## Statement

Use the exact-cell convention of accepted fact `0e51a47a9add571f` and the central common-order support of accepted fact `b431dbefa8a4aec7`.  In common affine coordinates \((X,Y)\), let
\[
L_j=L_k=L_\ell=-\frac56,
\qquad
f_j=X-\frac56,\qquad
f_\ell=Y-\frac56,\qquad
f_k=\frac23-X-Y.
\]
Define
\[
A_0=\operatorname{conv}\left\{
\left(-\frac{21}{71},\frac{43}{142}\right),
\left(\frac3{10},\frac7{10}\right),
\left(\frac45,\frac35\right),
\left(\frac{29}{19},-\frac3{133}\right)
\right\}
\]
and
\[
A_2=\operatorname{conv}\left\{
\left(\frac{683}{345},-\frac{73}{138}\right),
\left(\frac{41}{30},-\frac{11}{15}\right),
\left(\frac16,\frac16\right),
\left(-\frac{187}{75},\frac{34}{15}\right)
\right\}.
\]
These are the exact two endpoint polygons of the rational \(j\ell\)-row model, with the second polygon written in the same \((X,Y)\)-coordinates as the first.

For every convex set \(K\) satisfying
\[
\frac{A_0+A_2}{2}\subseteq K,
\]
one has the exact lower bound
\[
|R_{j\ell}|\ge\frac{52143}{1136000}>0.
\]
Consequently there is no convex polygon \(K\) for which the positive middle cells are precisely
\[
R_j,\qquad R_{jk},\qquad R_N,\qquad R_{k\ell}.
\]
In particular, the displayed \(A_0,A_2\) admit no completion having all twelve central common-order cells positive and no other positive cell, regardless of the scalar choices required by the three crossings, genuine lower crossings, canonical tails, finite interior, and \(W=0\).

## Proof

The second endpoint polygon was originally specified in
\[
u=\frac53-X,\qquad v=\frac53-Y
\]
coordinates by the four vertices
\[
\left(-\frac{36}{115},\frac{101}{46}\right),\quad
\left(\frac3{10},\frac{12}{5}\right),\quad
\left(\frac32,\frac32\right),\quad
\left(\frac{104}{25},-\frac35\right).
\]
Applying \(X=5/3-u\) and \(Y=5/3-v\) gives exactly the four displayed vertices of \(A_2\), so both endpoint polygons above lie in one common plane.

Let \(a_1,a_3\) denote respectively the first and third displayed vertices of \(A_0\), and let \(b_2,b_3,b_4\) denote respectively the second, third, and fourth displayed vertices of \(A_2\).  The midpoint polygon
\[
S=\frac{A_0+A_2}{2}
\]
contains
\[
z_1=\frac{a_1+b_3}{2}
=\left(-\frac{55}{852},\frac{50}{213}\right),
\]
\[
z_2=\frac{a_1+b_2}{2}
=\left(\frac{2281}{4260},-\frac{917}{4260}\right),
\]
and
\[
z_0=\frac{a_3+b_4}{2}
=\left(-\frac{127}{150},\frac{43}{30}\right).
\]
Since \(S\) is convex, it also contains
\[
z_3=\frac34z_1+\frac14z_0
=\left(-\frac{22159}{85200},\frac{4553}{8520}\right).
\]

For these three points the exact strict-chamber margins are
\[
\begin{array}{c|ccc}
&\frac56-X&\frac56-Y&\frac23-X-Y\\ \hline
z_1&\frac{255}{284}&\frac{85}{142}&\frac{141}{284}\\[1mm]
z_2&\frac{423}{1420}&\frac{1489}{1420}&\frac{123}{355}\\[1mm]
z_3&\frac{31053}{28400}&\frac{849}{2840}&\frac{11143}{28400}.
\end{array}
\]
Every entry is positive.  Thus at every \(z_i\), \(i=1,2,3\),
\[
f_j<0,\qquad f_\ell<0,\qquad f_k>0.
\]
The strict chamber defined by these three inequalities is convex, so
\[
T:=\operatorname{conv}\{z_1,z_2,z_3\}
\]
is contained in that chamber and in \(S\).  Moreover,
\[
\det(z_2-z_1,z_3-z_1)=\frac{52143}{568000},
\]
and hence
\[
|T|=\frac12\left|\det(z_2-z_1,z_3-z_1)\right|
=\frac{52143}{1136000}>0.
\]

By the convention of accepted fact `0e51a47a9add571f`, the strict chamber
\[
f_j<0,\qquad f_\ell<0,\qquad f_k>0
\]
belongs to the exact middle cell \(R_{j\ell}\).  Therefore every
\(K\supseteq S\) satisfies
\[
T\subseteq R_{j\ell},
\qquad
|R_{j\ell}|\ge |T|
=\frac{52143}{1136000}>0.
\]
Accepted fact `b431dbefa8a4aec7` requires the full central common-order middle support to contain only
\[
R_j,R_{jk},R_N,R_{k\ell}.
\]
The forced positive \(R_{j\ell}\) area contradicts that support.  This is the first failed condition: common-plane midpoint incidence fails before the scalar crossing system is reached.
