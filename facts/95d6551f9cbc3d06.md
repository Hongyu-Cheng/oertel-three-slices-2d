## Statement

Use the exact-cell convention of accepted fact `0e51a47a9add571f` and the one-sided chord-density convention of accepted fact `eddda25c734d34bc`. Set
\[
f_k(x,y)=x,\qquad f_\ell(x,y)=y,\qquad
f_j(x,y)=-1-x-y
\]
and
\[
L_k=L_\ell=0,\qquad L_j=-\frac{1591}{25}.
\]
Then \(f_j+f_k+f_\ell=-1\), the three linear parts are pairwise nonparallel, and
\[
L_j+L_k+L_\ell=-\frac{1591}{25}<-1.
\]
In particular,
\[
p_j=\frac{1566}{25}-x-y,\quad p_k=x,\quad p_\ell=y,
\]
\[
q_j=-\frac{1616}{25}-x-y,\quad q_k=x,\quad q_\ell=y.
\]

Let \(A_0\) be the convex polygon with the following cyclically ordered vertices:
\[
\left(-\frac1{10},-\frac{49}{100}\right),\quad
\left(\frac{81}{100},-\frac{133}{100}\right),\quad
\left(\frac{6267}{100},\frac3{100}\right),\quad
\left(\frac{1059}{50},\frac{3361}{100}\right),\quad
\left(\frac1{100},-\frac3{25}\right).
\tag{1}
\]
Let \(A_2\) be the convex polygon with the following cyclically ordered vertices:
\[

\left(-\frac{3037}{100},-\frac{41}{50}\right),\quad

The endpoint areas are

Both cut intersections are the origin, and

The complete positive endpoint supports are exactly

Exactly one of the six endpoint levels contains a polygon vertex:

The other five levels \(p_j=0,p_k=0,p_\ell=0,q_j=0,q_\ell=0\)

## Proof

Write the vertices in (1) as \(a_1,\ldots,a_5\) and those in (2) as
\(b_1,\ldots,b_6\), with cyclic subscripts. For an ordered polygonal
list \(z_1,\ldots,z_r\), define its successive oriented turns by
\[
\Delta_i(z)=
\det(z_{i+1}-z_i,z_{i+2}-z_{i+1}).
\]
Direct exact evaluation for (1) gives
\[
\bigl(\Delta_1(a),\ldots,\Delta_5(a)\bigr)
=
\left(
\frac{266}{5},
\frac{5334213}{2500},
\frac{21103463}{10000},
\frac{20613}{5000},
\frac{4291}{10000}
\right),
\tag{18a}
\]
and for (2) it gives
\[
\bigl(\Delta_1(b),\ldots,\Delta_6(b)\bigr)
=
\left(
\frac{241061}{5000},
\frac{8013}{500},
\frac{10023}{5000},
\frac{53009}{1000},
\frac{329}{50},
\frac{4838}{625}
\right).
\tag{18b}
\]
Every displayed turn is strictly positive.

There is also a global facet certificate for the asserted cyclic orders.
For \(A_0\), the five affine forms
\[
\begin{gathered}
757+1200x+1300y,\qquad
416877-6800x+309300y,\\
21057033-335800x-414900y,\qquad
-28777+337300x-211700y,\\
-169+3700x-1100y
\end{gathered}
\tag{18c}
\]
are nonnegative at all five vertices. In the displayed order, they
vanish exactly on the consecutive pairs
\[
(a_1,a_2),\ (a_2,a_3),\ (a_3,a_4),\ (a_4,a_5),\ (a_5,a_1),
\]
respectively, and are strictly positive at every other listed vertex.
For \(A_2\), the six affine forms
\[
\begin{gathered}
121607+1400x+96450y,\qquad
2381-8100x+302900y,\\
-101-1900x+5100y,\qquad
3857-1540x-2900y,\\
175959+2860x-132300y,\qquad

\end{gathered}
\tag{18d}
\]
are nonnegative at all six vertices. In the displayed order, they
vanish exactly on
\[
(b_1,b_2),\ (b_2,b_3),\ (b_3,b_4),\ (b_4,b_5),\
(b_5,b_6),\ (b_6,b_1),
\]
respectively, and are strictly positive at every other listed vertex.
Thus every consecutive pair is a supporting facet, every listed point
is an extreme point, and (1)-(2) are the boundary vertex lists of
convex polygons in the asserted counterclockwise cyclic orders.

All remaining calculations below are over \(\mathbb Q\). The shoelace
formula applied to (1) and (2) gives
\[

m=|A_2|=\frac{745313}{10000},

\[

\tag{18}

The convex hull of each finite vertex list is compact. The facet audit
(18a)-(18d) proves convexity and the stated boundary order, while the
positive areas in (18) prove full dimension. Hence \(A_0\) and \(A_2\)
are compact two-dimensional convex polygons.

The inequalities
\[

\tag{19}
\]
and
\[
-1900x+5100y\ge101\qquad(x,y\in A_2)
\tag{20}
\]
hold at every listed vertex and hence throughout the respective polygons.
They are equalities on an edge in each case. Since the origin violates both
(19) and (20), the intersection \(p_k=p_\ell=0\) lies outside \(A_0\), and
the intersection \(q_k=q_\ell=0\) lies outside \(A_2\).
