## Statement

Use the exact-cell convention of accepted fact `0e51a47a9add571f`, the scalar notation and hypotheses of accepted fact `a2a9387275a2c97c`, and the affine normalization of accepted fact `8375b9cde7d74d1a`.  Fix \(j=3,k=1,\ell=2\).  In the central common-order regime
\[
x_{\alpha,j}<x_{\alpha,\ell}<x_{\alpha,k},\qquad
x_{\beta,k}<x_{\beta,\ell}<x_{\beta,j},\qquad
x_{\gamma,k}<x_{\gamma,\ell}<x_{\gamma,j},
\]
the only positive cells may be denoted, in section order, by
\[
P_{\{j\}},P_\varnothing,P_{\{\ell\}},P_{\{k,\ell\}}
        \quad\hbox{with areas }a,b,c,d,
\]
\[
Q_{\{j\}},Q_{\{j,k\}},Q_N,Q_{\{k,\ell\}}
        \quad\hbox{with areas }e,f,g,s,
\]
\[
R_{\{j\}},R_{\{j,k\}},R_N,R_{\{k,\ell\}}
        \quad\hbox{with areas }x,y,z,w.
\]
The three crossings imply the exact central identities
\[
c=f+y,\qquad a+e+x=d+s+w,
\tag{1}
\]
and
\[
2b=5f+5y+5d+5s+5w+7g+7z.
\tag{2}
\]
Thus the coefficients of \(Q_N\) and \(R_N\) are \(7\), not the outer-path coefficients in accepted fact `115550229db5db1a`.

There exist nonconstant affine \(f_1,f_2,f_3\) with pairwise nonparallel linear parts, constants \(L_i\) with
\[
f_1+f_2+f_3=-1,\qquad L_1+L_2+L_3<-1,
\]
and two-dimensional rectangles \(A_0,A_2,K\) such that
\[
\frac{A_0+A_2}{2}\subseteq K,
\]
all three displayed orders and sectionwise transversality hold, every one of the twelve displayed cells has positive area, \(b=p_\varnothing=1\), all three crossings, canonical tails, and finite-interior inequalities hold, and \(W=0\).

## Proof

First derive (1)--(2), without borrowing the outer coefficients from accepted fact `115550229db5db1a`.  In the displayed support,
\[
\begin{array}{c|ccc}
 &j&k&\ell\\ \hline
u_i&a&d&c+d\\
v_i&e+f+g&f+g+s&g+s\\
c_i&x+y+z&y+z+w&z+w .
\end{array}
\]
Subtracting the \(\ell\)-crossing from the \(k\)-crossing gives
\[
0=-c+f+y,
\]
and subtracting the \(k\)-crossing from the \(j\)-crossing gives
\[
0=a-d+e-s+x-w.
\]
These are (1).  The crossings and \(t=2(M+m+\kappa)/9\) give
\[
W=3\sum_i(u_i+v_i+c_i)-2(M+m+\kappa)=0.
\]
The exact central support therefore gives
\[
\begin{aligned}
0={}&-2b+a+c+4d+e+4f+7g+4s\\
   &+x+4y+7z+4w.
\end{aligned}
\]
Substitution of (1) yields (2).

We now construct the realization.  Work in coordinates \((r,\omega)\), and put
\[
h_0=\frac1{100},\qquad h_2=\frac1{10000},\qquad h=\frac3{500}.
\]
Choose
\[
\alpha_j=0,\qquad
\alpha_\ell=100,\qquad
\alpha_k=100+\frac{31}{495},
\]
\[
\beta_k=0,\qquad
\beta_\ell=\frac{26}{99},\qquad
\beta_j=\frac{26}{99}+\frac{1009}{10}.
\]
Define
\[
\begin{array}{lll}
p_k=\alpha_k-r,&p_\ell=\alpha_\ell+\omega-r,
&p_j=2r-\omega-2\alpha_j,\\
q_k=\beta_k-r,&q_\ell=\beta_\ell+\omega-r,
&q_j=2r-\omega-2\beta_j,
\end{array}
\]
and then define
\[
f_i=\frac{p_i+q_i}{2},\qquad L_i=\frac{q_i-p_i}{2}.
\]
The three linear parts are \(-r,\omega-r,2r-\omega\), hence are pairwise nonparallel.  Moreover,
\[
\sum_i p_i=\delta=\frac{99031}{495},\qquad
\sum_i q_i=-D=-\frac{100021}{495}=-(\delta+2).
\]
Consequently \(\sum_i f_i=-1\) and
\[
\sum_iL_i=-(\delta+1)<-1.
\]
Put \(\gamma_i=(\alpha_i+\beta_i)/2\).

Set
\[
\begin{array}{c|cccc}
 &a&b&c&d\\ \hline
P&\dfrac1{20000}&1&\dfrac{31}{49500}&\dfrac1{100000}
\end{array}
\]
\[
\begin{array}{c|cccc}
 &e&f&g&s\\ \hline
Q&\dfrac{61}{100000}&\dfrac{13}{495000}
 &\dfrac{1009}{100000}&\dfrac1{20000}
\end{array}
\]
and
\[
\begin{array}{c|cccc}
 &x&y&z&w\\ \hline
R&\dfrac{18849983}{49500000}&\dfrac3{5000}
 &\dfrac{27}{10000}&\dfrac{18879683}{49500000}.
\end{array}
\tag{3}
\]
Define the rectangles
\[
A_0=
\left[\alpha_j-\frac a{h_0},\,
      \alpha_k+\frac d{h_0}\right]
\times\left[-\frac{h_0}{2},\frac{h_0}{2}\right],
\]
\[
A_2=
\left[\beta_k-\frac e{h_2},\,
      \beta_j+\frac s{h_2}\right]
\times\left[-\frac{h_2}{2},\frac{h_2}{2}\right],
\]
\[
K=
\left[\gamma_k-\frac xh,\,
      \gamma_j+\frac wh\right]
\times\left[-\frac h2,\frac h2\right].
\tag{4}
\]

On \(A_0\), the consecutive threshold gaps are
\[
(\alpha_\ell+\omega)-(\alpha_j+\omega/2)
  =100+\frac{\omega}{2}>0,
\]
\[
\alpha_k-(\alpha_\ell+\omega)
  =\frac{31}{495}-\omega>0.
\]
On \(A_2\), they are
\[
(\beta_\ell+\omega)-\beta_k
  =\frac{26}{99}+\omega>0,
\]
\[
(\beta_j+\omega/2)-(\beta_\ell+\omega)
  =\frac{1009}{10}-\frac{\omega}{2}>0.
\]
Finally,
\[
\gamma_\ell-\gamma_k=\frac1{10},\qquad
\gamma_j-\gamma_\ell=\frac9{20},
\]
so on \(K\) the consecutive gaps are
\[
\frac1{10}+\omega>0,\qquad
\frac9{20}-\frac{\omega}{2}>0.
\]
These inequalities hold throughout the respective projection intervals.  The endpoint margins in (4) also put all three threshold graphs strictly inside every section.  Thus sectionwise transversality and the three claimed orders hold.

Integration over the symmetric projection intervals now gives exactly the cell areas (3).  For example,
\[
p_\varnothing=h_0(\alpha_\ell-\alpha_j)=1,
\qquad
p_{\{\ell\}}=h_0(\alpha_k-\alpha_\ell)
=\frac{31}{49500},
\]
\[
q_{\{j,k\}}=h_2(\beta_\ell-\beta_k)
=\frac{13}{495000},
\qquad
q_N=h_2(\beta_j-\beta_\ell)
=\frac{1009}{100000},
\]
\[
r_{\{j,k\}}=h(\gamma_\ell-\gamma_k)=\frac3{5000},
\qquad
r_N=h(\gamma_j-\gamma_\ell)=\frac{27}{10000}.
\]
The endpoint cells have the prescribed areas by the four endpoint margins in (4).  Hence every unlisted cell has area zero and all twelve listed cells have positive area.  Directly,
\[
c=f+y,
\]
\[
a+e+x=d+s+w
=\frac{18882653}{49500000},
\]
and (2) holds.

It remains to verify compatibility.  The midpoint of the two rectangles \(A_0,A_2\) is a rectangle.  Its \(\omega\)-projection is contained in that of \(K\) because
\[
h-\frac{h_0+h_2}{2}=\frac{19}{20000}>0.
\]
The lower and upper \(r\)-endpoint containments are respectively equivalent to
\[
x\ge
\frac h2\left(\frac{a+b+c}{h_0}+\frac e{h_2}\right),
\qquad
w\ge
\frac h2\left(\frac{b+c+d}{h_0}+\frac s{h_2}\right).
\]
Both hold strictly, since the two slacks are
\[
\frac{6168181}{99000000}>0,\qquad
\frac{7891969}{99000000}>0.
\]
Therefore \((A_0+A_2)/2\subseteq K\) exactly.  In particular, all single-cell and convex-union midpoint Brunn--Minkowski consequences of the compatibility table in accepted fact `0e51a47a9add571f` hold for this realization.

The total areas and common crossing value are
\[
M=\frac{4953397}{4950000},\qquad
m=\frac{21337}{1980000},\qquad
\kappa=\frac{4736627}{6187500},\qquad
t=\frac{98721}{250000}
=\frac{2(M+m+\kappa)}9.
\]
For completeness, the crossings and tail slacks are
\[
\begin{array}{c|ccc|c|c|c}
i&u_i&v_i&c_i&u_i+v_i+c_i&c_i+m-t&c_i+M-t\\ \hline
k&\dfrac1{100000}&\dfrac{50323}{4950000}
 &\dfrac{19043033}{49500000}
 &t&\dfrac3{5000}&\dfrac{99051}{100000}\\[2mm]
\ell&\dfrac{6299}{9900000}&\dfrac{507}{50000}
 &\dfrac{19013333}{49500000}
 &t&0&\dfrac{98991}{100000}\\[2mm]
j&\dfrac1{20000}&\dfrac{10619}{990000}
 &\dfrac{19013333}{49500000}
 &t&0&\dfrac{98991}{100000}.
\end{array}
\]
Thus all left tails are nonnegative and all right tails are strict.  Every \(u_i,v_i,c_i\) is positive, and its complement in \(M,m,\kappa\), respectively, contains a listed positive cell, proving finite interior.  Equation (2), equivalently the direct weighted sum of (3), gives \(W=0\).

This exact compatible realization disproves the attempted assertion that the entire positive-\(p_\varnothing\) central common-order branch is empty under the accepted hypotheses.  Since the realization already satisfies compatibility itself, no additional inequality valid for every compatible triple, including any single-cell or convex-union Brunn--Minkowski inequality, can provide the missing contradiction.  The next necessary input must instead encode an extra property of the original depth-failure witnesses, such as the central retained-gap halfspace condition that is not among the hypotheses of accepted facts `8375b9cde7d74d1a` and `a2a9387275a2c97c`.
