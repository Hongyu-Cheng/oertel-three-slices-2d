## Statement

Let \(\mathscr R\) be the class of full common-plane residual-counterexample realizations satisfying all hypotheses of accepted fact `d1177b21864bbcc6`, including the complete canonical finite-interior and exact-cell conventions, \(M\ge m\), the three crossings, the canonical tails, \(W=0\), all-double positive \(Q\)-support
\[
\{123,12,13,23\},
\qquad
r_1r_2r_3r_{123}>0,
\]
and normalized data
\[
q=1,\qquad x_1=x_2=x_3=\frac5{21},\qquad m=\frac{12}{7},
\]
together with the residual condition that no slice has at least half the total area. There is a universal constant \(D_0>0\) such that every \(\mathcal R\in\mathscr R\) satisfies \(D(\mathcal R)\ge D_0\).

## Proof

If \(\mathscr R=\varnothing\), take \(D_0=1\). Assume henceforth that \(\mathscr R\ne\varnothing\). If
\[
\inf_{\mathcal R\in\mathscr R}D(\mathcal R)=0,
\]
then, since every full realization has \(D>0\), there is a sequence \(\mathcal R_n\in\mathscr R\) with \(0<D_n<1/n\).

Apply a common planar affine map putting \(A_{2,n}\) in the John position used in `d1177b21864bbcc6`; normalized cell-area ratios and \(D=q/|\mathcal T_Q|\) are unchanged. Retain the same notation for the transformed bodies. Choose \(z_n\in A_{0,n}\), put \(v_n=z_n/2\), and apply
\[
G_n(h,z)=\bigl(h,z-(2-h)v_n\bigr).
\tag{1}
\]
Thus
\[
\widehat A_{0,n}=A_{0,n}-2v_n,\qquad
\widehat K_n=K_n-v_n,\qquad
\widehat A_{2,n}=A_{2,n}.
\tag{2}
\]
Accepted fact `3ff81f067c99b5ad` says that \(\widehat A_{0,n}\) and \(\widehat K_n\) lie in fixed compact sets. In particular, \(A_{2,n}\) remains in the same John position.

We audit the hypotheses before reapplying the dichotomy. The map \(G_n\) is an invertible affine map preserving the height coordinate and every integer slice; on each slice it is a translation. It maps the original polytope, all cutting halfspaces, and all exact cells bijectively to their gauged versions and preserves slice area. Moreover,
\[
\frac{\widehat A_{0,n}+\widehat A_{2,n}}2
=\frac{A_{0,n}+A_{2,n}}2-v_n
\subseteq K_n-v_n=\widehat K_n,
\]
so full common-plane compatibility is preserved. Consequently the finite-interior and exact-cell conventions, all cell masses and support positivities, \(M\ge m\), the crossings, tails, \(W=0\), \(r_1r_2r_3r_{123}>0\), the fixed values of \(q,x_i,m\), and the no-half-slice inequalities are unchanged. The \(Q\)-plane is fixed pointwise by (1), so \(D_n\) is unchanged as well. Finally, (1) is the height shear of `ecee03aff61db31e` composed with a common planar translation; that fact preserves halfspace depth and therefore preserves the residual-counterexample property. Hence the gauged sequence satisfies every hypothesis of `d1177b21864bbcc6` and `6b69e5c4a97d4e32`, still with \(D_n\to0\).

Reapply `d1177b21864bbcc6` to the gauged sequence, using the identity as its John map. Its escape alternative (E) is impossible because the \(\widehat K_n\) lie in one fixed compact set. After passage to the classified subsequence, the sequence must therefore satisfy the bounded alternative (T). Accepted fact `6b69e5c4a97d4e32` excludes alternative (T) for precisely such a residual sequence, a contradiction.

Thus no sequence of values \(D(\mathcal R)\) can tend to \(0\). Equivalently, by the sequential characterization of closure in \(\mathbb R\),
\[
D_0:=\inf_{\mathcal R\in\mathscr R}D(\mathcal R)>0.
\]
By definition of the infimum, every \(\mathcal R\in\mathscr R\) satisfies \(D(\mathcal R)\ge D_0\).
