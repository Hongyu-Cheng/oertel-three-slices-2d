## Statement

Assume the surviving transformed \(N=3\) coordinate-cap model of accepted facts `09393c6edf1fc4e2` and `64760c76cadff481`. Thus \(q>1\), the nondegenerate triangles \(P_0,P_1,P_2\) lie respectively in
\[
\left\{\sum_i x_i=1+q\right\},\qquad
\left\{\sum_i x_i=1\right\},\qquad
\left\{\sum_i x_i=1-q\right\},
\]
have normalized areas \(A,1,B\), satisfy
\[
\frac{P_0+P_2}{2}\subseteq P_1,
\]
and have coordinate-cap fractions
\[
u_i=\frac{|P_0\cap\{x_i\geq0\}|}{A},\qquad
c_i=|P_1\cap\{x_i\geq0\}|,\qquad
v_i=\frac{|P_2\cap\{x_i\geq0\}|}{B}.
\]
Suppose
\[
Au_i+c_i+Bv_i<\frac{2(1+A+B)}9\qquad(i=1,2,3),
\]
the residual inequalities \(A+B>1\) and \(B<1+A\) hold, and \(A/B\geq3/10\). Let \(g_j\) be the area centroid of \(P_j\), and assume the assigned incidence pattern
\[
\{i:(g_1)_i\geq0\}=\{1,2\},\qquad
\{i:(g_0)_i\geq0\}=\{3\},
\]
with the row-\(3\) small-endpoint cap proper:
\[
0<u_3<1.
\]
Then the strict system reconstructs a counterexample by accepted fact `64760c76cadff481`, so accepted fact `291c24bb45bd4946` gives
\[
\frac3{10}\leq\frac AB<\frac38.
\]
Moreover,
\[
v_1,v_2<\frac{4A}{9B}<\frac16,\qquad
v_3<\frac{2(1+B-A)}{9B}<\frac49,
\]
and
\[
(g_2)_1<0,\qquad (g_2)_2<0,\qquad (g_2)_3<0.
\]
Consequently, for
\[
h=\frac{g_0+g_2}{2},
\]
one has
\[
h\in P_1,\qquad h_1<0,\qquad h_2<0,\qquad h_3>1.
\]
In particular, no point of the stated incidence chamber satisfying \(h_1\geq0\) or \(h_2\geq0\) can satisfy the strict cap system.

## Proof

We first prove an elementary triangle-cap implication using the exact formula of accepted fact `64760c76cadff481`. Let \(a\leq b\leq c\) be the three vertex values of an affine functional on a nondegenerate triangle. If
\[
a+b+c\geq0,
\]
then the closed cap on which the functional is nonnegative has area fraction at least \(4/9\).

If \(a\geq0\), the cap is full. If \(a<0\leq b\), put \(u=-a>0\). The nonnegative mean gives \(b+c\geq u\), and the exact two-positive branch gives
\[
\mathcal C(a,b,c;0)
=1-\frac{u^2}{(u+b)(u+c)}
\geq\frac12.
\]
If \(b<0<c\), put \(u=-a\geq v=-b>0\). Then \(c\geq u+v\), and the unique-positive cap fraction is increasing in \(c\), so
\[
\mathcal C(a,b,c;0)
\geq
\frac{(u+v)^2}{(2u+v)(u+2v)}
\geq\frac49.
\]
The last inequality is equivalent to
\[
9(u+v)^2-4(2u+v)(u+2v)=(u-v)^2\geq0.
\]
If \(c\leq0\), the nonnegative-mean assumption forces \(a=b=c=0\), and the closed cap is full. Thus, contrapositively, a cap fraction strictly below \(4/9\) forces the corresponding centroid coordinate to be strictly negative.

Accepted fact `13c0df55864b79eb` gives, for \(i=1,2\),
\[
Au_i+Bv_i<
\frac{2(A+B-1)}9
<\frac{4A}{9}.
\]
Hence
\[
v_i<\frac{4A}{9B}.
\]
The strict system reconstructs a counterexample by `64760c76cadff481`; accepted fact `291c24bb45bd4946` therefore gives \(A/B<3/8\), and consequently
\[
v_1,v_2<\frac{4A}{9B}<\frac16<\frac49.
\]
For \(i=3\), the other cross-cap bound in `13c0df55864b79eb` gives
\[
c_3+Bv_3<\frac{2(1+B-A)}9.
\]
Since \(c_3\geq0\),
\[
v_3<\frac{2(1+B-A)}{9B}.
\]
The residual inequality \(A+B>1\) makes \(1+B-A<2B\), so \(v_3<4/9\). Applying the triangle-cap implication to each coordinate functional on \(P_2\) proves
\[
(g_2)_i<0\qquad(i=1,2,3).
\]

Because the small-endpoint incidence set is exactly \(\{3\}\),
\[
(g_0)_1<0,\qquad (g_0)_2<0.
\]
Both centroids are points of their respective triangles, so compatibility from accepted fact `09393c6edf1fc4e2` gives
\[
h=\frac{g_0+g_2}{2}\in\frac{P_0+P_2}{2}\subseteq P_1.
\]
The first two coordinates of \(h\) are strict averages of negative numbers, hence \(h_1,h_2<0\). Its coordinate sum is
\[
\sum_i h_i=\frac{(1+q)+(1-q)}2=1,
\]
so
\[
h_3=1-h_1-h_2>1.
\]
This proves all conclusions. The properness assumption \(0<u_3<1\) is retained exactly, and the exclusion does not require emptying or filling that cap.
