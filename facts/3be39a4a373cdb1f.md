## Statement

In coordinates \((z,y)\), let
\[
f_1(z,y)=z,\qquad
f_2(z,y)=\frac{-1-z+y}{2},\qquad
f_3(z,y)=\frac{-1-z-y}{2},
\]
\[
L_1=0,\qquad L_2=L_3=-\frac{3181}{300},
\]
and take the rational convex bodies
\[
A_0=\left[0,\frac{953}{75}\right]
       \times\left[-\frac{15}{2},\frac{15}{2}\right],
\]
\[
A_2=\left[-\frac1{10},\frac9{10}\right]
       \times\left[-\frac12,\frac12\right],
\qquad
K=\left[-\frac{729}{80},8\right]\times[-4,4].
\tag{1}
\]
Put
\[
M=|A_0|,\qquad m=|A_2|,\qquad k=|K|,
\qquad
\tau=\frac{2(M+m+k)}9,
\]
\[
u_i=\left|A_0\cap\{f_i\le L_i\}\right|,\quad
v_i=\left|A_2\cap\{f_i\le-L_i\}\right|,\quad
c_i=\left|K\cap\{f_i\le0\}\right|.
\]
Then
\[
M=\frac{953}{5},\qquad m=1,\qquad
k=\frac{1369}{10},\qquad \tau=73,
\tag{2}
\]
\[
(u_1,u_2,u_3)=(0,0,0),\quad
(v_1,v_2,v_3)=\left(\frac1{10},1,1\right),\quad
(c_1,c_2,c_3)=\left(\frac{729}{10},72,72\right).
\tag{3}
\]
Thus both crossing equalities and the contact combined-cap equality are
exact:
\[
c_2+u_2+v_2=c_3+u_3+v_3
=c_1+u_1+v_1=\tau.
\tag{4}
\]
Moreover,
\[
\frac{A_0+A_2}{2}
=\left[-\frac1{20},\frac{2041}{300}\right]\times[-4,4]
\subseteq K.
\tag{5}
\]

Recenter the middle first coordinate by \(x=z+1\), and denote the
resulting vertical section by \(H_x\).  The actual middle sections and the
midpoint projection are
\[
H_x=[-4,4]\quad
\left(-\frac{649}{80}\le x\le9\right),
\tag{6}
\]
\[
P=\left[\frac{19}{20},\frac{2341}{300}\right].
\tag{7}
\]
On \(P=[L,B]\), (6) has symmetric affine half-width
\[
w(x)=A+\frac{H-A}{B-L}(x-L)=4
\quad\text{with}\quad
L=\frac{19}{20},\ B=\frac{2341}{300},\ A=H=4.
\tag{8}
\]
There is no convex replacement \(\widetilde K\) that preserves the actual
sections (6) outside \(P\) and changes the end section at \(L\) to
\(\widetilde H_L=\{0\}\).  Hence no deformation satisfying the longer list
of requirements in the finite move can reduce \(A\) to \(0\).

## Proof

The rational configuration, the full two-parameter midpoint inclusion, and
the exact values (2)--(5) are certified by accepted fact
`5b0e805e0321d4b0`.  Equation (5) also directly gives (7) after adding
\(1\) to the middle first coordinate, while (1) gives (6).  Therefore the
sections on \(P\) have the affine form (8) with nonzero end half-width
\(A=4\).  In the recentered coordinates the three middle cuts are
\[
x\le1,\qquad y\le x,\qquad y\ge-x,
\]
so (3) and (4) are exactly the contact and crossing data relevant to the
move.

The rational points \(0\) and \(8\) lie in the two components of the
exterior set:
\[
0<\frac{19}{20},\qquad
8>\frac{2341}{300}.
\]
Literal exterior-section preservation therefore forces
\[
\widetilde H_0=\widetilde H_8=[-4,4].
\]
For any convex \(\widetilde K\), the one-line invariant is
\[
[0,8]\times[-4,4]
=\operatorname{conv}\!\left(
 \{0\}\times[-4,4]\ \cup\ \{8\}\times[-4,4]\right)
\subseteq\widetilde K.
\tag{9}
\]
Since \(L=19/20\in[0,8]\), (9) implies
\(\widetilde H_L\supseteq[-4,4]\).  Thus a symmetric end section at \(L\)
has half-width at least \(4\), contradicting the required half-width
\(0\).  This obstruction uses only convexity and the two fixed exterior
sections; preserving the full midpoint inclusion, \(M,m,k\), the two exact
crossing equalities, and the contact combined cap cannot remove it.
