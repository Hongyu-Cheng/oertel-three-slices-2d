## Proof

The new table is obtained from the \(Q\)-table in accepted fact
`9aa5e8e4ddcd1c62` by the exact four-cell move
\[
q_3\longmapsto q_3-\eta,\qquad
q_{13}\longmapsto q_{13}+\eta,
\tag{13}
\]
\[
q_{23}\longmapsto q_{23}+\eta,\qquad
q_{123}\longmapsto q_{123}-\eta,
\qquad
\eta=\frac{49}{100000}.
\tag{14}
\]
All four resulting masses are positive.  The changes sum to zero.
For cap \(1\), the changes in \(q_{13}\) and \(q_{123}\) cancel; for
cap \(2\), those in \(q_{23}\) and \(q_{123}\) cancel; for cap \(3\),
all four changed cells occur and the signed changes again sum to
zero.  This proves (4)--(5).

Every assertion in the accepted scalar system that depends only on
the endpoint row totals or cap sums is therefore unchanged.  This
includes the crossings, tails, \(W\), balance, all four three-cap
tests, and the completion equations and filters.  The common-fan
positivity hypotheses also remain strict.

It remains to check the scalar conditions that see individual
\(Q\)-cells.  The values of \(q_1,q_2,q_{12}\) are unchanged, while
\(q_3\) decreases.  Hence all singleton forced-cell capacities and
all opposite conditions become weaker or stay unchanged.  Only the
\(13\)- and \(23\)-doubleton capacities require a new check.  With
\[
p_{13}=p_{23}=\frac{18}{D},\qquad
\rho_{13}=\frac{19}{18000},\qquad
\rho_{23}=\frac{607}{18000},
\tag{15}
\]
put
\[
Z_{13}=4\rho_{13}-p_{13}-q_{13},\qquad
Z_{23}=4\rho_{23}-p_{23}-q_{23}.
\]
Exact reduction gives
\[
Z_{13}=\frac{46737483629}{14464872900000}>0,
\tag{16}
\]
\[
Z_{13}^2-4p_{13}q_{13}
=
\frac{2183464425444762209641}
{209232548013154410000000000}>0,
\tag{17}
\]
\[
Z_{23}=\frac{1922349336329}{14464872900000}>0,
\tag{18}
\]
\[
Z_{23}^2-4p_{23}q_{23}
=
\frac{3695425105610256558396241}
{209232548013154410000000000}>0.
\tag{19}
\]
For nonnegative \(a,c\), the conditions
\[
4U-a-c>0,\qquad
(4U-a-c)^2>4ac
\]
imply \(U>(\sqrt a+\sqrt c)^2/4\).  Thus (16)--(19)
prove both new doubleton capacities.  Every previously accepted
scalar/cell gate has now been checked.

We next write the new consequences of accepted fact
`9fdba5fffd591cda` in symbolic form.  If \(C_i=\overline{Q_i}\),
\[
r_i=\max_{C_i}(x+y)-\min_{C_i}x-\min_{C_i}y,
\qquad
h_i=\max_{C_i}y
\quad(i=1,3),
\tag{20}
\]
then its support-triangle and common-section arguments give
\[
q_i\le
\gamma\left(r_ih_i-\frac{h_i^2}{2}\right)
\quad(i=1,3),
\tag{21}
\]
\[
q_{13}\ge
\gamma\left(12\min\{h_1,h_3\}
+\frac{\min\{h_1,h_3\}^2}{2}\right),
\tag{22}
\]
and its exact Minkowski support identity gives
\[
\rho_i\ge
\frac{\gamma}{4}
\left(50+\frac{q_i}{\gamma}+10r_i\right)
\quad(i=1,3).
\tag{23}
\]
Moreover \(0<h_i<r_i\) for the parameters in (6), so (21) also
implies (10).

Substitution of (1)--(3) and (6) into the left-minus-right sides of
(21), (22), and (23) gives exactly the five positive rational
numbers in (11)--(12).  Hence all inequalities are strict.  The
calculation uses only rational arithmetic.  SageMath \(10.8\)
independently reduced the five margins to (11)--(12) and checked
their signs; no numerical feasibility decision is used.

The variables in (20) are only extrema of two cells.  Satisfying
(21)--(23) does not produce the single concave horizontal section
profile required of an actual convex \(Q\), nor does it determine
the remaining five exact cells.  Therefore the exact realization of
(3) by one convex \(Q\), rather than another inequality among
\((r_1,h_1,r_3,h_3)\), is the first unproved planar condition.
