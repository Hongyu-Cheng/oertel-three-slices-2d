## Proof

First identify what generated the positive \(R_{j\ell}\) polygon in accepted fact `88ba14be013f0f23`.  In its notation that clip is
\[
(\xi_1,w_4,w_5,\xi_5,\xi_2).
\]

\[

w_6:(v_7,z_9).
\]

Their determinant is not a function of the full-chord parameters alone.
