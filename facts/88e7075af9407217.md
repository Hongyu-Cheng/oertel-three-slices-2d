## Statement

Let \(A_0,A_1,A_2\subset\mathbb R^2\) be nonempty two-dimensional convex bodies, and set
\[
S=\bigcup_{i=0}^2(\{i\}\times A_i),
\qquad
T=|A_0|+|A_1|+|A_2|.
\]
For \(y\in S\), define
\[
h_S(y)=
\inf\left\{
\sum_{i=0}^2
\left|\{z\in A_i:(i,z)\in H\}\right|:
H\subset\mathbb R^3\text{ is a closed halfspace and }y\in H
\right\}.
\]
Assume that \(A_0\) and \(A_2\) are centrally symmetric about \(0\), that
\[
|A_0|\le |A_2|,
\]
and that \(A_1\) contains a nonempty two-dimensional convex body \(K\) centrally symmetric about \(0\). Then
\[
h_S((1,0))\ge \frac{|A_0|+|K|}{2}.
\]
Consequently, if
\[
\frac{|A_0|+|K|}{2}\ge\frac{2T}{9},
\]
then
\[
\max_{y\in S}h_S(y)\ge\frac{2T}{9}.
\]

## Proof

Because \(K\) is centrally symmetric about \(0\), one has \(0\in K\subseteq A_1\), so \((1,0)\in S\).

Let \(H\) be an arbitrary closed halfspace containing \((1,0)\). If \(H=\mathbb R^3\), the claimed bound is immediate. Otherwise write
\[
H=\{(h,z):\alpha h+\ell(z)\le\beta\},
\]
where \(\ell:\mathbb R^2\to\mathbb R\) is linear and \((\alpha,\ell)\ne(0,0)\). Put
\[
\tau_i=\alpha i-\beta
\qquad(i=0,1,2).
\]
Since \((1,0)\in H\), one has \(\tau_1\le0\). Moreover,
\[
\tau_0+\tau_2=2\tau_1\le0.
\]
Hence \(\tau_i\le0\) for at least one \(i\in\{0,2\}\). The height-\(i\) section of \(H\) therefore contains the symmetry center \(0\) of \(A_i\).

Every planar halfplane containing the center of a two-dimensional centrally symmetric convex body contains at least half of its area. Indeed, the parallel halfplane whose boundary passes through the center and lies on the same side is contained in the original halfplane, and central reflection exchanges the two open halfplanes bounded by that line. Thus the selected endpoint section contributes at least
\[
\frac{|A_i|}{2}\ge\frac{|A_0|}{2}.
\]
This conclusion also holds when \(\ell=0\), because a nonempty slice section is then the whole slice.

The height-\(1\) section of \(H\) contains \(0\). Applying the same symmetry argument to \(K\subseteq A_1\), its contribution is at least
\[
\frac{|K|}{2}.
\]
All remaining slice contributions are nonnegative, so
\[
\sum_{j=0}^2
\left|\{z\in A_j:(j,z)\in H\}\right|
\ge\frac{|A_0|+|K|}{2}.
\]
Taking the infimum over all closed halfspaces \(H\) containing \((1,0)\) proves
\[
h_S((1,0))\ge\frac{|A_0|+|K|}{2}.
\]
The stated consequence follows immediately when this lower bound is at least \(2T/9\).
