## Statement

Assume the complete canonical finite-interior common-plane hypotheses, exact-cell convention, \(M\geq m\), the three crossing equalities, the canonical left and right tails, and the all-double positive \(Q\)-support
\[
\{123,12,13,23\}.
\]
Write
\[
q=q_{123}>0,\qquad x_i=q_{\{1,2,3\}\setminus\{i\}}>0,\qquad
D=\frac{q}{|\mathcal T_Q|},
\]
and assume the positive-middle condition \(r_1r_2r_3r_{123}>0\).  Put
\[
\lambda_i=\frac{x_i}{q}>0,\qquad R=\lambda_1+\lambda_2+\lambda_3.
\]
For \(k\in\{1,2,3\}\), with \(\{i,j,k\}=\{1,2,3\}\), define
\[
A_k=\frac{1+R}{\lambda_k},\qquad
c_k=2\sqrt{\lambda_i\lambda_j},\qquad
d_{1k}=\frac{4A_kc_k^2}{(A_k+c_k^2)^2}.
\]
For real \(t\), let
\[
\operatorname{clip}(t)=\min\{1,\max\{0,t\}\},
\]
and define
\[
d_{2k}
=1-\operatorname{clip}\!\left(
\frac{\sqrt{A_k}-c_k}{1+c_k}
\right)^2,
\]
\[
d_0=\min\!\left\{1,\frac{3}{\lambda_1^{-1}+\lambda_2^{-1}+\lambda_3^{-1}}\right\},
\qquad
\delta=\min\{d_0,d_{11},d_{12},d_{13},d_{21},d_{22},d_{23}\},
\]
\[
\tau(R)=
\frac{49}{
\left(\sqrt{1+R}+\sqrt{204+120R}\right)^2}.
\]
If
\[
\delta\geq\tau(R),
\]
then no such full common-plane realization can have \(W=0\).  In particular, no such realization with \(W=0\) exists whenever
\[
\frac9{20}\leq\frac{x_i}{q}\leq\frac{11}{20}
\qquad(i=1,2,3).
\]
Both conclusions include equality on every displayed ratio boundary, coincident chord endpoints, collinear or redundant polygon vertices, and every weak/strict cutting-line boundary allowed by the exact-cell convention.

## Proof

Assume for contradiction that \(W=0\).  Apply accepted fact `084bf2f472bf29c7` with the exact upper bound
\[
U(q,x_1,x_2,x_3)=|\mathcal T_Q|=\frac qD.
\]
Its strict scalar bridge gives
\[
\frac qD>
\Phi(m,q)
=\left(
\frac{\sqrt m+\sqrt{120m+84q}}7
\right)^2.
\]
Because
\[
m=q+x_1+x_2+x_3=q(1+R),
\]
we have the exact identity
\[
\Phi(m,q)
=\frac q{49}
\left(\sqrt{1+R}+\sqrt{204+120R}\right)^2
=\frac q{\tau(R)}.
\]
All quantities are positive, so the strict inequality above is equivalent to
\[
D<\tau(R). \tag{1}
\]

Apply accepted fact `6aa12d1b76699246` to \(A_2\), the three \(Q\)-cutting lines, the central cell \(Q_{123}\), and the three outer cap cells \(Q_{\{1,2,3\}\setminus\{i\}}\).  The all-double support makes the cap interiors pairwise disjoint, and the four displayed \(Q\)-areas are positive.  That fact therefore applies with its cap ratios equal to the present \(\lambda_i=x_i/q\), and it gives
\[
D\geq\delta. \tag{2}
\]
If \(\delta\geq\tau(R)\), then (2) contradicts (1).  This proves the first conclusion, including the boundary \(\delta=\tau(R)\).

For the explicit rational box, accepted fact `6aa12d1b76699246` gives directly
\[
D\geq\frac25. \tag{3}
\]
Since \(R>0\),
\[
\sqrt{1+R}>1,\qquad
\sqrt{204+120R}>\sqrt{204}>14.
\]
Consequently
\[
\tau(R)<\frac{49}{225}<\frac25. \tag{4}
\]
Equations (1), (3), and (4) are contradictory.  Hence the whole closed box is excluded.

Accepted fact `6aa12d1b76699246` proves (2) for every coincident-endpoint stratum by an area-convergent truncation argument and proves (3) on the entire closed ratio box.  Accepted fact `084bf2f472bf29c7` uses the exact-cell convention, so cutting lines have zero area and its strict bridge is unchanged on collinear, redundant-vertex, and weak/strict boundary strata.  Thus no geometric or cell boundary was removed in either contradiction.  Finally, positivity of the \(\lambda_i\) makes \(\delta\) and \(\tau\) defined throughout the positive ratio domain, which is the disjoint exhaustive union of the closed region \(\delta\geq\tau\) proved above and the remaining open region \(\delta<\tau\).
