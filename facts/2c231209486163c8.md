## Statement

Let \(f_1,f_2,f_3:\mathbb R^2\to\mathbb R\) be nonconstant affine
functions whose linear parts are pairwise nonparallel and which satisfy
\[
f_1+f_2+f_3=-1.
\]
Let \(A_0,A_2\subset\mathbb R^2\) be compact two-dimensional convex
polygons, let \(L_1,L_2,L_3\in\mathbb R\), and put
\[
B=\frac{A_0+A_2}{2},\qquad k_0=|B|,
\]
\[
u_i=|A_0\cap\{f_i\le L_i\}|,\qquad
v_i=|A_2\cap\{f_i\le-L_i\}|,
\]
\[
b_i=|B\cap\{f_i\le0\}|,
\qquad
F_i=u_i+v_i+b_i-\frac{2(|A_0|+|A_2|+k_0)}9.
\]
If a compact two-dimensional convex body \(K\supseteq B\) satisfies all
three crossing equalities and
\[
A=|K\setminus B|,
\]
then, with
\[
d_i=b_i-F_i,
\]
one necessarily has the exact core-cap cutoff
\[
\boxed{\quad
2A\le5(d_1+d_2+d_3)-6\min_i d_i-4k_0.
\quad}
\tag{1}
\]
Consequently the following defect-only relaxation is also necessary:
\[
\boxed{\quad
2A\le
6k_0-5(F_1+F_2+F_3)+6\max_iF_i.
\quad}
\tag{2}
\]

Now fix
\[
f_1(x,y)=x,\qquad f_2(x,y)=y,\qquad
f_3(x,y)=-1-x-y,
\qquad
L_1=L_2=L_3=-100.
\tag{3}
\]
For a rational parameter \(s\in[0,100]\), put
\[
A_0(s)=\operatorname{conv}\left\{
\left(-\frac{999}{10},-\frac{981}{10}\right),
\left(197,-\frac{981}{10}\right),
(s,-50)
\right\}.
\tag{4}
\]
Let \(A_2\) be the parallelogram with center
\[
c=\left(-\frac{99}{2},\frac{991}{10}\right)
\]
and full edge generators
\[
g=\left(302,\frac{19}{10}\right),
\qquad
h=\left(-\frac1{10},\frac1{20}\right),
\tag{5}
\]
that is,
\[
A_2=c+\left[-\frac g2,\frac g2\right]
       +\left[-\frac h2,\frac h2\right].
\]
Put \(B(s)=(A_0(s)+A_2)/2\).

There is no compact convex body \(K\supseteq B(s)\) for which the three
crossing equalities hold and whose extension area \(A=|K\setminus B(s)|\)
belongs to the scalar-completion interval of accepted fact
`6f6869df3d151764`.  Equivalently, for every rational
\(s\in[0,100]\), the scalar interval and the exact three-cap feasible
interval have empty intersection.

## Proof

First derive the general filter.  Accepted fact
`6f6869df3d151764` rewrites the three crossing equalities as
\[
e_i:=|(K\setminus B)\cap\{f_i\le0\}|
=\frac{2A}{9}-F_i.
\tag{6}
\]
Therefore the three full cap areas of \(K\) are
\[
c_i:=|K\cap\{f_i\le0\}|
=b_i+e_i
=\frac{2A}{9}+d_i.
\tag{7}
\]
The additional hypotheses in the first paragraph of the statement,
namely that the \(f_i\) are nonconstant and have pairwise nonparallel
linear parts, allow accepted fact `5b75ecd9ec86dbcf` to be applied to
\(K\).  It gives
\[
5(c_1+c_2+c_3)-6\min_i c_i\ge4(k_0+A).
\tag{8}
\]
Substitution of (7) into (8) gives
\[
\frac{10A}{3}+5\sum_i d_i
-\frac{4A}{3}-6\min_i d_i
\ge4k_0+4A.
\]
Rearranging proves (1).

To eliminate the individual core caps, choose an index \(j\) attaining
\(\min_i(b_i-F_i)\).  Since \(0\le b_i\le k_0\),
\[
\begin{aligned}
5\sum_i(b_i-F_i)-6(b_j-F_j)
&=
5\sum_{i\ne j}b_i-b_j
-5\sum_iF_i+6F_j\\
&\le
10k_0-5\sum_iF_i+6\max_iF_i.
\end{aligned}
\tag{9}
\]
Combining (1) and (9) proves (2).  The coefficient
\(10k_0\) in (9) is the exact maximum over the cube
\(0\le b_i\le k_0\): it is attained algebraically by taking
\(b_j=0\), the other two \(b_i=k_0\), and \(F_j=\max_iF_i\).
Thus no stronger relaxation follows from the three box constraints
alone.

We next audit the parameter family.  The directed edge vectors of
\(A_0(s)\), in cyclic order, are
\[
e_1=\left(\frac{2969}{10},0\right),\qquad
e_2=\left(s-197,\frac{481}{10}\right),\qquad
e_3=\left(-s-\frac{999}{10},-\frac{481}{10}\right).
\tag{10}
\]
Their determinants with the two generators in (5) are
\[
\begin{array}{c|cc}
&g&h\\ \hline
e_1&
\dfrac{56411}{100}&\dfrac{2969}{200}\\[2mm]
e_2&
\dfrac{19s-149005}{10}&\dfrac{5s-504}{100}\\[2mm]
e_3&
\dfrac{1433639-190s}{100}&-\dfrac{10s+1961}{200}.
\end{array}
\tag{11}
\]
Every entry in (11) is nonzero for \(0\le s\le100\).  Also
\[
\det(g,h)=\frac{1529}{100}>0.
\]
Thus both endpoints are genuine, their normal fans are transverse, and
\(B(s)\) is a genuine seven-vertex polygon throughout the declared
interval.

The endpoint areas are independent of \(s\):
\[
M:=|A_0(s)|=\frac{1428089}{200},
\qquad
m:=|A_2|=\frac{1529}{100},
\qquad
\frac mM=\frac{3058}{1428089}<\frac38.
\tag{12}
\]
For \(p_i=f_i-L_i=f_i+100\), the minimum values on \(A_0(s)\)
are respectively
\[
\frac1{10},\qquad\frac{19}{10},\qquad\frac1{10}.
\tag{13}
\]
Indeed, at the moving vertex the three values are
\[
s+100,\qquad 50,\qquad149-s,
\]
which are all larger than the displayed minima on
\(0\le s\le100\).  Hence
\[
u_1=u_2=u_3=0
\tag{14}
\]
with strict support margins.

For \(q_i=f_i+L_i=f_i-100\), define, up to null boundaries,
\[
Q_J=
A_2\cap\{q_i\le0:i\in J\}
\cap\{q_i>0:i\notin J\}
\qquad
(\varnothing\ne J\subseteq\{1,2,3\}).
\tag{15}
\]
Exact rational clipping of the fixed parallelogram gives
\[
|Q_{\{3\}}|=\frac{4587}{60400}>0,
\qquad
|Q_{\{1,2\}}|=\frac{13761}{202600}>0.
\tag{16}
\]
The three opposite endpoint cap areas are
\[
(v_1,v_2,v_3)=
\left(
\frac{918929}{60400},
\frac{56573}{3800},
\frac{3083993}{202600}
\right),
\tag{17}
\]
and they are all proper:
\[
(m-v_1,m-v_2,m-v_3)
=
\left(
\frac{4587}{60400},
\frac{1529}{3800},
\frac{13761}{202600}
\right)>0.
\tag{18}
\]
Thus \(0<v_i<m\) for every \(i\), so each line \(f_i=100\) cuts the
interior of \(A_2\).  Moreover,
\[
v_3-v_1=\frac{490809}{61185200}>0,
\qquad
v_3-v_2=\frac{643709}{1924700}>0.
\tag{19}
\]

These facts verify the genuine lower-crossing predicate precisely
whenever a completion equality is imposed.  For such a \(K\), put
\[
c_i=|K\cap\{f_i\le0\}|,
\quad
\mu_i(a)=c_i+|A_0(s)\cap\{f_i\le a\}|
+|A_2\cap\{f_i\le-a\}|,
\]
\[
t=\frac{2(M+m+|K|)}9,
\qquad
I_i=\{a\in\mathbb R:\mu_i(a)<t\}.
\tag{20}
\]
The crossing equality is \(\mu_i(-100)=t\).  For every \(a\le-100\),
all three \(A_0(s)\)-caps remain empty, while the corresponding
\(A_2\)-cap at threshold \(-a\) contains its cap at threshold \(100\).
Hence
\[
I_i\cap(-\infty,-100]=\varnothing.
\tag{21}
\]
Immediately to the right of \(-100\), the \(A_0(s)\)-cap is still empty
by (13), whereas the \(A_2\)-cap shrinks strictly because
\(0<v_i<m\).  Thus some \(\delta_i>0\) satisfies
\[
(-100,-100+\delta_i)\subset I_i.
\tag{22}
\]
Consequently \(I_i\ne\varnothing\) and
\(\inf I_i=-100\), which is the claimed genuine lower crossing with no
earlier strict-low component.

It remains to compare the two area intervals.  For a polygon \(P\) and
a centered full segment with vector \(z\), segment addition increases
area by
\[
\max_{x\in P}\det(z,x)-\min_{x\in P}\det(z,x).
\tag{23}
\]
On the three vertices in (4), the values for \(z=g\) are
\[
-\frac{2943639}{100},\qquad
-\frac{60001}{2},\qquad
-15100-\frac{19s}{10}.
\]
For \(0\le s\le100\), the second is the minimum and the third is the
maximum.  The corresponding values for \(z=h\) are
\[
\frac{2961}{200},\qquad
-\frac1{25},\qquad
5-\frac{s}{20};
\]
the second and first are respectively the minimum and maximum.
Adding the two segments successively, including their mutual
parallelogram area, and then dilating by \(1/2\), gives
\[
k_0(s):=|B(s)|
=\frac14\left(
M+
\frac{29801}{2}-\frac{19s}{10}
+\frac{2969}{200}
+m
\right)
=\frac{551777}{100}-\frac{19s}{40}.
\tag{24}
\]

Put
\[
h_0(s)=\frac{2(M+m+k_0(s))}{9}
=\frac{2534701}{900}-\frac{19s}{180}.
\tag{25}
\]
The core \(B(s)\) lies in \(\{y\ge1/80\}\): the minimum \(y\)-values
of \(A_0(s)\) and \(A_2\) are \(-981/10\) and \(785/8\),
respectively.  Hence its second core cap is zero, and
\[
F_2=v_2-h_0(s)<0.
\tag{26}
\]
The scalar lower endpoint in accepted fact
`6f6869df3d151764` is therefore at least
\[
A_{\mathrm{sc}}(s)
:=\frac97(-F_2)
=\frac{95809481}{26600}-\frac{19s}{140}.
\tag{27}
\]

For any convex crossing completion, equations (7), (14), and (25)
give
\[
c_i=\frac{2A}{9}+h_0(s)-v_i.
\]
By (19), \(c_3=\min_i c_i\).  Substitution in the exact cutoff (1),
or directly in (8), gives
\[
A\le A_{\mathrm{3cap}}(s)
:=
M+m-k_0(s)-\frac52(v_1+v_2+v_3)+3v_3
\]
\[
=
\frac{3651057274013}{2325037600}
+\frac{19s}{40}.
\tag{28}
\]
The exact separation between the scalar lower bound and the coupled
convexity upper bound is
\[
A_{\mathrm{sc}}(s)-A_{\mathrm{3cap}}(s)
=
\frac{33063821650721}{16275263200}
-\frac{171s}{280}.
\tag{29}
\]
This affine function decreases on \([0,100]\), but at its right
endpoint it still equals
\[
\frac{32069868076721}{16275263200}>0.
\tag{30}
\]
Thus
\[
A_{\mathrm{sc}}(s)>A_{\mathrm{3cap}}(s)
\qquad(0\le s\le100),
\]
so no scalar-completable \(A\) survives the exact three-cap filter.

At \(s=49/2\), the scalar interval is genuinely nonempty and equals
\[
\frac{23930259}{6650}
\le A\le
\frac{17881582275919}{2325037600},
\]
whereas (28) gives
\[
A\le
\frac{3678114899083}{2325037600}.
\]
Their exact separating gap is
\[
\frac{23930259}{6650}
-\frac{3678114899083}{2325037600}
=\frac{32820303025091}{16275263200}>0.
\]
Hence the parameterized exclusion strictly adds information beyond
the scalar gate.
