## Statement

Assume all hypotheses and notation of accepted fact `a2a9387275a2c97c`.  Fix \(j\in N=\{1,2,3\}\), write
\[
N\setminus\{j\}=\{k,\ell\},
\]
and retain the notation of accepted fact `72662f57a0efe8fb`:
\[
P=\overline{P_\varnothing},\qquad p=|P|=p_\varnothing,
\]
\[
E=\overline{Q_{\{j\}}},\qquad
F=\overline{Q_{\{k,\ell\}}},\qquad
H=\operatorname{conv}(E\cup F),
\]
\[
Z=\frac{P+H}{2},\qquad
D=Z\cap\{-1<f_j<0\},\qquad s_j=|D|.
\]
For every nonempty \(I\subseteq N\), put
\[
B_I=\frac{P+\overline{Q_I}}2,
\qquad w_I=3|I|-2,
\]
and put
\[
\eta_j
=
|Z\cap R_N|
+
\sum_{\substack{G\subseteq N\\|G|=2}}
\left|(Z\cap R_G)\setminus D\right|.
\tag{1}
\]
All equalities below are understood up to the null cutting-line boundaries.

The exact midpoint incidences and their extra coefficients above the unit
\(R\)-coefficient are
\[
\begin{array}{c|c|c}
\text{\(Q\)-cell}&\text{possible target cells of \(B_I\)}
&\text{extra target coefficients}\\ \hline
\{j\}&R_{\{j\}}&0\\
\{j,k\}&R_{\{j\}},R_{\{k\}},R_{\{j,k\}}&0,0,3\\
\{k\}&R_{\{k\}}&0\\
\{k,\ell\}&R_{\{k\}},R_{\{\ell\}},R_{\{k,\ell\}}&0,0,3\\
N&\{R_G:\varnothing\ne G\subseteq N\}
&0\text{ on singletons},\ 3\text{ on doubletons},\ 6\text{ on }N.
\end{array}
\tag{2}
\]
Moreover, the complete weighted contribution of \(Z\) is exactly
\[
\sum_{\varnothing\ne G\subseteq N}
w_G|Z\cap R_G|
=|Z|+3s_j+3\eta_j.
\tag{3}
\]

Suppose first that the positive \(Q\)-support contains the outer path
\[
\{j\},\{j,k\},\{k\},\{k,\ell\}.
\tag{4}
\]
Put
\[
\mathcal B_{\rm o}=B_{\{j,k\}}\cup B_{\{k\}},
\qquad
e_G^{\rm o}
=
\left|(\mathcal B_{\rm o}\setminus Z)\cap R_G\right|.
\tag{5}
\]
Then \(e_G^{\rm o}=0\) unless
\[
G\in\{\{j\},\{k\},\{j,k\}\},
\tag{6}
\]
and every canonical realization obeys
\[
\boxed{
\begin{aligned}
2p\ge{}&
q_{\{j\}}+4q_{\{j,k\}}+q_{\{k\}}+4q_{\{k,\ell\}}\\
&+|Z|+3s_j+3\eta_j
+e_{\{j\}}^{\rm o}+e_{\{k\}}^{\rm o}
+4e_{\{j,k\}}^{\rm o}.
\end{aligned}}
\tag{7}
\]

Suppose instead that the positive \(Q\)-support contains the central path
\[
\{j\},\{j,k\},N,\{k,\ell\}.
\tag{8}
\]
Put
\[
\mathcal B_{\rm c}=B_{\{j,k\}}\cup B_N,
\qquad
e_G^{\rm c}
=
\left|(\mathcal B_{\rm c}\setminus Z)\cap R_G\right|.
\tag{9}
\]
Then every canonical realization obeys
\[
\boxed{
\begin{aligned}
2p\ge{}&
q_{\{j\}}+4q_{\{j,k\}}+7q_N+4q_{\{k,\ell\}}\\
&+|Z|+3s_j+3\eta_j
+\sum_{\varnothing\ne G\subseteq N}w_Ge_G^{\rm c}.
\end{aligned}}
\tag{10}
\]
Thus (7) and (10) strictly strengthen the budget in accepted fact
`72662f57a0efe8fb`: in particular they use the positive areas of every
intermediate path cell rather than only \(q_{\{j\}}\) and
\(q_{\{k,\ell\}}\).

The strengthened budgets still do not close either path without the full
crossing geometry.  More precisely, take
\[
(j,k,\ell)=(3,1,2)
\tag{11}
\]
and coordinates \((r,w)\).  There are two exact rational common-plane
configurations with
\[
P_\varnothing=A_0
=[0,200]\times\left[0,\frac1{200}\right],
\qquad p_\varnothing=1,
\tag{12}
\]
\[
\frac{A_0+A_2}{2}=K=Z,\qquad H=A_2,
\tag{13}
\]
one having exact \(Q\)-support
\[
\{3,13,1,12\},
\tag{14a}
\]
and the other exact \(Q\)-support
\[
\{3,13,123,12\}.
\tag{14b}
\]
For both configurations all intermediate midpoint images are contained in
\(Z\), so every \(e_G^{\rm o}\) or \(e_G^{\rm c}\) is zero.  Nevertheless,
the entire right side of (7) in the outer case is
\[
\frac{7628364961}{4096000000}
<2,
\tag{15a}
\]
and the entire right side of (10) in the central case is
\[
\frac{37986304161}{20480000000}
<2.
\tag{15b}
\]
These are actual common-plane convex configurations, not scalar
calibrations.

They are not canonical counterexamples.  If
\[
D_i=u_i+v_i+c_i-\frac{2(M+m+|K|)}9
\tag{16}
\]
is the \(i\)-th crossing residual, then in the outer configuration
\[
D_1=\frac{14434753127}{92160000000}>0,
\qquad
c_2+m-\frac{2(M+m+|K|)}9
=-\frac{81278880787}{230400000000}<0,
\tag{17a}
\]
whereas in the central configuration
\[
D_1=\frac{28325185127}{184320000000}>0,
\qquad
c_2+m-\frac{2(M+m+|K|)}9
=-\frac{323217608783}{921600000000}<0.
\tag{17b}
\]
Thus the index-\(2\) left tail fails, and the three crossings are not
equalities.  In fact, keeping \(A_0,A_2\) and the affine arrangement fixed,
no convex enlargement \(K'\supseteq K\) can make all three crossings hold.
Consequently, the missing statement cannot be an additional path-only
midpoint charge or a post hoc enlargement of \(K\); it must couple the
endpoint geometry to the three crossing equations.

## Proof

Accepted fact `0e51a47a9add571f` gives, for \(P=P_\varnothing\),
\[
\frac{P+\overline{Q_I}}2
\subseteq
\bigcup_{\varnothing\ne G\subseteq I}R_G.
\tag{18}
\]
Taking \(I=\{j\},\{j,k\},\{k\},\{k,\ell\},N\) gives exactly the incidence
table (2).  The listed extra coefficients are
\[
w_G-1=0,3,6
\]
for \(|G|=1,2,3\), respectively.

We next prove (3).  Since \(R_\varnothing=\varnothing\), the exact
\(R\)-weight on \(Z\) is
\[
|Z|
+3\sum_{|G|=2}|Z\cap R_G|
+6|Z\cap R_N|.
\tag{19}
\]
Every point of \(D\) has \(j\) and at least one of \(k,\ell\) nonpositive,
because
\[
-1<f_j<0,\qquad f_k+f_\ell=-1-f_j<0.
\tag{20}
\]
Conversely, a point of \(R_N\) satisfies
\[
-1\le f_j\le0,
\]
and equality can occur only on cutting-line subsets of area zero.  Hence
\[
|D|
=
\sum_{|G|=2}|D\cap R_G|+|Z\cap R_N|.
\tag{21}
\]
Splitting the doubleton areas in (19) into their portions inside and
outside \(D\), and using (21), gives (3).

For the outer path, let
\[
\mathcal Y_{\rm o}=Z\cup\mathcal B_{\rm o}.
\]
The endpoint bodies \(B_{\{j\}}\) and \(B_{\{k,\ell\}}\) are already
contained in \(Z\), since their second summands lie in \(E,F\subseteq H\).
The exact \(R\)-weight on \(\mathcal Y_{\rm o}\) is
\[
\sum_Gw_G|\mathcal Y_{\rm o}\cap R_G|
=
\sum_Gw_G|Z\cap R_G|
+\sum_Gw_Ge_G^{\rm o}.
\tag{22}
\]
The incidence table gives (6), so (3) turns (22) into the second line of
(7).  Under the hypotheses of accepted fact `a2a9387275a2c97c`, the only
negative term in \(W\) is \(-2p\).  The four path cells contribute
\[
q_{\{j\}}+4q_{\{j,k\}}+q_{\{k\}}+4q_{\{k,\ell\}}
\tag{23}
\]
to its \(Q\)-part, and (22) is a contribution from a subset of \(K\).
Every uncounted term is nonnegative.  Since \(W=0\), (7) follows.
The same argument with
\[
\mathcal Y_{\rm c}=Z\cup B_{\{j,k\}}\cup B_N
\]
and \(Q\)-weight
\[
q_{\{j\}}+4q_{\{j,k\}}+7q_N+4q_{\{k,\ell\}}
\tag{24}
\]
proves (10).

Finally, let \(K'\supseteq K\) be any convex enlargement with the same
endpoints and affine arrangement, and put
\[
\mathscr R_G
=
\{f_i\le0\ (i\in G),\ f_i>0\ (i\notin G)\},

\]

\[
g_{\rm o}
=2-W_{Q,\rm o}-W_{R,\rm o}
=\frac{563635039}{4096000000},
\tag{52}

and for the central example define
\[
g_{\rm c}
=2-W_{Q,\rm c}-W_{R,\rm c}
=\frac{2973695839}{20480000000}.

If all three crossings held for \(K'\), then their sum would give
\(W(K')=0\).  Since \(W(K)=-g_\tau\), this would require
\[
\sum_Gw_Gx_G=g_\tau.
\tag{54}
\]

the first crossing residual changes from \(D_1\) to
\[
D_1+\sum_{G\ni1}x_G-\frac{2x}{9}.
\tag{55}
\]
Since \(D_1>0\), making (55) zero requires
\[
x\ge\frac92D_1.
\tag{56}
\]
The exact gaps are
\[
\frac92D_1-g_{\rm o}
=\frac{2904144483}{5120000000}>0
\tag{57a}
\]
and
\[
\frac92D_1-g_{\rm c}
=\frac{22377793449}{40960000000}>0.
\tag{57b}
\]
Equations (54), (56), and (57) are incompatible.  Therefore no
enlargement \(K'\) with the fixed endpoints can repair the crossings,
which completes the proof.
