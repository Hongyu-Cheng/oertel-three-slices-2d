## Statement

Assume Alternative 3 of accepted fact `879a3c45b5f50670` and the Regime A parametrization of accepted fact `ab2620c83e116de2`. Thus \(A_0,K,A_2\subset\mathbb R^2\) are nonempty two-dimensional convex polytopes satisfying
\[
\frac{A_0+A_2}{2}\subseteq K,
\qquad
|A_0|=1,\quad |A_2|=r,\quad |K|=s,
\]
\[
t=\frac{2(1+r+s)}9,
\qquad
f_i+f_j+f_\ell=-1,
\qquad
c_h=|K\cap\{f_h\leq0\}|.
\]
The affine functions have nonparallel nonzero linear parts. There are shifts \(a_i,a_\ell\) for which
\[
u_h=|A_0\cap\{f_h\leq a_h\}|,
\qquad
v_h=|A_2\cap\{f_h\leq-a_h\}|,
\qquad
c_h+u_h+v_h<t
\quad(h=i,\ell).
\]
Put
\[
P=A_0\cap\{f_i>a_i,\ f_\ell>a_\ell\},
\quad
Q=A_2\cap\{f_i>-a_i,\ f_\ell>-a_\ell\},
\]
\[
R=A_2\cap\{f_i\leq-a_i,\ f_\ell\leq-a_\ell\},
\qquad
p=|P|,\quad q=|Q|,\quad w=|R|.
\]
Assume
\[
q=0<w
\]
and stay outside the second-tail branch:
\[
c_i+r\geq t,\qquad c_\ell+r\geq t.
\tag{1}
\]
Define
\[
\alpha=\frac{5r+5s-4}{9},
\qquad
\beta=\frac{2+2s-7r}{9},
\qquad
d=c_j-\alpha=\delta-r,
\qquad
D=\beta-\alpha=\frac{2-s-4r}{3}.
\tag{2}
\]
Then
\[
\frac12+\frac{5r}{4}+\frac{3w}{4}
+\frac12\sqrt{r-w}
<2(c_j+t).
\tag{3}
\]
Equivalently,
\[
d>
\theta(r,s,w):=
\frac{34-11r-56s+27w}{72}
+\frac14\sqrt{r-w}.
\tag{4}
\]

More explicitly, let
\[
\phi(r,s,w)
:=
1-12t+12w+4\sqrt{3w(t-w)}.
\tag{5}
\]
The exact Regime A window, Alternative 3 inequality (18), aggregate inequality (21), and the new directional inequality (3) together say precisely
\[
\begin{cases}
0<r\leq1/5,\quad 1-r<s<1+r,\\
\text{or}\\
1/5<r<1/3,\quad 1-r<s<2-4r,
\end{cases}
\tag{6}
\]
and
\[
0<w<r,
\qquad
\max\{w,\phi(r,s,w),\theta(r,s,w)\}<d<D.
\tag{7}
\]
Every actual no-second-tail Alternative 3 configuration therefore lies in (6)-(7). In particular, every such configuration satisfies
\[
r<r_*=
\left(\frac{\sqrt{1855}-3}{71}\right)^2.
\tag{8}
\]
The containment in (6)-(7) is strict relative to the scalar region given only by (18)-(21).

## Proof

Accepted fact `ab2620c83e116de2` gives (6), \(c_j=\alpha+d\), and
\[
0\leq d<D.
\tag{9}
\]
Accepted fact `879a3c45b5f50670`, in Alternative 3, gives
\[
0<w<d
\tag{10}
\]
and
\[
p>1-\delta+r+w=1-d+w>\frac23.
\tag{11}
\]

Resolve the two mixed cells of \(A_2\) by putting
\[
B_i=A_2\cap\{f_i\leq-a_i,\ f_\ell\geq-a_\ell\},
\qquad
B_\ell=A_2\cap\{f_i\geq-a_i,\ f_\ell\leq-a_\ell\},
\]
\[
b_i=|B_i|,\qquad b_\ell=|B_\ell|.
\]
All cutting lines have area zero. Since \(q=0\), the four sign cells give
\[
b_i+b_\ell=r-w,
\qquad
v_i=b_i+w,
\qquad
v_\ell=b_\ell+w.
\tag{12}
\]
If \(b_i=0\), then \(v_\ell=r\), and the \(\ell\)-th strict low-mass inequality gives \(c_\ell+r<t\), contrary to (1). Similarly \(b_\ell=0\) contradicts (1). Hence
\[
b_i>0,\qquad b_\ell>0,\qquad 0<w<r.
\tag{13}
\]
Thus the closures of \(P,B_i,B_\ell\) are two-dimensional compact convex bodies of areas \(p,b_i,b_\ell\).

Compatibility puts
\[
D_i:=\frac{\overline P+B_i}{2}\subseteq K.
\]
For \(x_0\in\overline P\) and \(x_2\in B_i\), one has
\[
f_\ell\left(\frac{x_0+x_2}{2}\right)\geq0.
\]
At that midpoint,
\[
f_i+f_j=-1-f_\ell<0.
\]
Consequently it belongs to \(\{f_i\leq0\}\cup\{f_j\leq0\}\), and hence
\[
D_i\subseteq
\bigl(K\cap\{f_i\leq0\}\bigr)
\cup
\bigl(K\cap\{f_j\leq0\}\bigr).
\tag{14}
\]
The analogous crossing statement holds with \(i\) and \(\ell\) interchanged. Planar Brunn--Minkowski and (14) therefore give
\[
\frac14(\sqrt p+\sqrt{b_i})^2\leq c_i+c_j,
\qquad
\frac14(\sqrt p+\sqrt{b_\ell})^2\leq c_\ell+c_j.
\tag{15}
\]
Adding \(u_i+v_i\) and \(u_\ell+v_\ell\), respectively, and using the strict low-mass inequalities yields
\[
\frac14(\sqrt p+\sqrt{b_i})^2+u_i+b_i+w<c_j+t,
\]
\[
\frac14(\sqrt p+\sqrt{b_\ell})^2+u_\ell+b_\ell+w<c_j+t.
\]
After summing and using (12),
\[
\frac p2+\frac{r-w}{4}
+\frac{\sqrt p}{2}(\sqrt{b_i}+\sqrt{b_\ell})
+u_i+u_\ell+r+w
<2(c_j+t).
\tag{16}
\]

Inclusion-exclusion on \(A_0\) gives
\[
u_i+u_\ell\geq1-p.
\tag{17}
\]
Because both numbers in (13) are positive and sum to \(r-w\),
\[
\sqrt{b_i}+\sqrt{b_\ell}>\sqrt{r-w}.
\tag{18}
\]
The left side of (16) is therefore strictly larger than
\[
g(p):=
1-\frac p2+\frac{5r}{4}+\frac{3w}{4}
+\frac12\sqrt{p(r-w)}.
\tag{19}
\]
For \(2/3<p\leq1\) and \(0<r-w<1/3\),
\[
g'(p)
=-\frac12+\frac14\sqrt{\frac{r-w}{p}}
<0.
\]
Thus \(g(p)\geq g(1)\). Combining this with (16) proves (3). Substituting \(c_j=\alpha+d\) and the value of \(t\) into (3), then collecting rational terms, gives exactly (4).

It remains to audit the advertised exact scalar form. By (6), \(s>1-r\), so \(t>4/9\). Equations (10) and (13) give \(w<r<1/3\), and therefore
\[
12(t-w)>w.
\]
The aggregate consequence (21) of accepted fact `879a3c45b5f50670` is
\[
\frac14\left(\sqrt{1-d+w}+\sqrt w\right)^2<3(t-w).
\tag{20}
\]
Both sides used below are positive, so taking square roots and squaring back is reversible. Equation (20) is equivalent to
\[
\sqrt{1-d+w}<\sqrt{12(t-w)}-\sqrt w,
\]
and then to
\[
d>1-12t+12w+4\sqrt{3w(t-w)}=\phi(r,s,w).
\tag{21}
\]
Now (9)-(10), (13), (4), and (21) are exactly (7). This proves the claimed exact description of the combined scalar inequalities.

For (8), first note that \(r_*>2/9\): indeed \(\sqrt{1855}>43\), so
\[
r_*>\left(\frac{40}{71}\right)^2>\frac29.
\]
Hence there is nothing to prove when \(r\leq2/9\). Suppose \(r>2/9\). From (6), (9), and \(w<d\),
\[
w<D<\frac13-r,
\]
so
\[
r-w>\frac19.
\]
The function
\[
h(w)=\frac{3w}{4}+\frac12\sqrt{r-w}
\]
therefore has
\[
h'(w)=\frac34-\frac1{4\sqrt{r-w}}>0.
\]
In particular, \(h(w)>h(0)=\sqrt r/2\). On the other hand, \(c_j<\beta\) and \(s<2-4r\). Applying these two strict bounds to (3) gives
\[
\frac12+\frac{5r}{4}+\frac12\sqrt r
<
2(\beta+t)
<
\frac83-\frac{14r}{3}.
\]
Equivalently,
\[
71r+6\sqrt r<26.
\tag{22}
\]
The left side is strictly increasing for \(r>0\), and equality in (22) occurs at
\[
\sqrt r=\frac{\sqrt{1855}-3}{71}.
\]
This proves (8).

Finally, the new restriction is not algebraically redundant. Take
\[
r=\frac8{25},\qquad
s=\frac7{10},\qquad
t=\frac{101}{225},
\]
\[
\alpha=\frac{11}{90},\qquad
d=\frac1{200},\qquad
D=\frac1{150},\qquad
c_j=\frac{229}{1800},\qquad
w=\frac1{1000}.
\tag{23}
\]
These values lie strictly in the old Regime A window and satisfy \(0<w<d<D\). To see that the old scalar inequalities (19)-(21) leave them feasible, set provisionally
\[
c_i=c_\ell=\frac25.
\]
Since
\[
\frac14\left(
\sqrt{\frac{249}{250}}+\sqrt{\frac1{1000}}
\right)^2
<
\frac{961}{3600}
<
\frac{1669}{1800}
=c_i+c_j+c_\ell
\]
and
\[
c_i=c_\ell<t-w,\qquad c_j<t-w,
\]
the scalar inequalities (19)-(21) all hold, as does (18). Also \(c_i+r,c_\ell+r>t\), so these provisional scalar cap values are outside the second-tail branch. But
\[
\frac8{25}>r_*,
\]
because
\[
71\left(\frac8{25}\right)+6\sqrt{\frac8{25}}>26
\quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad
30\sqrt2>41.
\]
Thus (3) excludes (23). This proves that (6)-(7) is a strict reduction of the earlier scalar Alternative 3 region. The obstruction is the directional allocation (14): the two mixed small-endpoint cells must charge the two crossing unions \(C_i\cup C_j\) and \(C_\ell\cup C_j\) separately, not merely charge their aggregate union.
