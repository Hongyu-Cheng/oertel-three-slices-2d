## Proof

For the three \(A_0\) vertices, the triples
\((f_1,f_2,f_3)\) are
\[
\left(-\frac{49}{100},-\frac{199}{200},\frac{97}{200}\right),
\quad
\left(\frac1{100},-\frac{199}{200},-\frac3{200}\right),
\quad
\left(-\frac{49}{100},-\frac{99}{200},-\frac3{200}\right).
\tag{21}
\]
Every coordinate is strictly greater than \(-1=L_i\), which proves the
\(P_\varnothing\) code and shows that every vertex is unmarked.
Shoelace gives \(|A_0|=1/8\).

For \(q_i=f_i+L_i=f_i-1\), the \(A_2\) vertex triples are
\[
\left(\frac1{100},-\frac1{100},-4\right),\quad
\left(-\frac1{100},\frac1{100},-4\right),\quad
\left(-1,-\frac{301}{100},\frac1{100}\right).
\tag{22}
\]
They lie strictly in \(Q_{23},Q_{13},Q_{12}\), respectively, and the
interior meets \(Q_{123}\).  Exact half-plane clipping and shoelace give
(4), so (3) is the complete positive \(Q\)-support.  In addition,
\[
q_0-(1,1)=\left(\frac1{100},-\frac1{100}\right),\qquad
q_1-(1,1)=\left(-\frac1{100},\frac1{100}\right),
\]
which proves \(H=0\), and direct differentiation gives the nonzero row
\[
DH=(0,\ldots,0,1/100,1/100,1/100,1/100,0,\ldots,0),
\]
with the four displayed entries in the \(q_0,q_1\) blocks.  Every other
edge-through-cut-intersection determinant is nonzero, so declaring this
single equality persistent supplies the corrected fixed clipping stratum.

The polygon \(K\) is the triangle
\[
x\le2,\qquad y\le2,\qquad x+y\ge-3.
\tag{23}
\]
Clipping it by \(f_i=0\) gives the seven values in (5).  Direct
substitution of the five vertices in (6) into (23) gives strict
inequalities, so every containment slack is positive.

In the \((S,T)=(f_1,f_2)\) coordinates, the five midpoint vertices are
exactly the five points in (6).  Each consecutive edge has a negative
\(S\)- or \(T\)-coordinate at an endpoint, so all five supports are
charging.  Intersecting those five support halfplanes with \(S,T\ge0\)
gives the triangle (9).  The active support pairs are exactly (10);
all other support evaluations are strict.  This proves the complete
two-dimensional \(Z,W\) codes, their equality, the absence of active
weak rows, and the zero capacity row in (11).

The only shared \(A_0,A_2\) ray is
\[
a_2-a_1=\left(-\frac12,\frac12\right),\qquad
q_1-q_0=\left(-\frac1{50},\frac1{50}\right).
\tag{24}
\]
Differentiating the fixed-code chamber shoelace formulas, the persistent
equality \(H=0\), and (24) gives the complete rational row space in
(14).  Direct comparison of the two clipping-branch extensions for
\(Q_{13}\) and \(Q_{123}\) gives (14a); adding a multiple of \(DH\)
changes neither differential on the fixed-stratum tangent space.  The
absent chamber rows and the fixed cut-defined \(R_{123}\) row are
identically zero.

Finally, a counterclockwise triangle satisfies
\[

\]
as an identity, not merely at the displayed point.  Differentiation

nondeletable triangular case omitted from the wording of the registered
