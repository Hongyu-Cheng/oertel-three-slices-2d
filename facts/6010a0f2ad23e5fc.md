## Statement

Define affine functions on \(\mathbb R^2\) by
\[
g_i(x,y)=x,\qquad
g_\ell(x,y)=y,\qquad
g_q(x,y)=1-x-y.
\tag{1}
\]
For a compact two-dimensional convex polygon \(K\subset\mathbb R^2\), define
three clips of this same polygon by
\[
C_i=K\cap\{g_i\geq0\},\qquad
C_\ell=K\cap\{g_\ell\geq0\},\qquad
C_q=K\cap\{g_q\geq0\},
\tag{2}
\]
and put
\[
c_i=|C_i|,\qquad c_\ell=|C_\ell|,\qquad c_q=|C_q|.
\tag{3}
\]
If
\[
c_i>0,\qquad c_\ell>0,\qquad c_q>0,
\tag{4}
\]
then
\[
c_i+c_\ell+c_q>|K|.
\tag{5}
\]

Consequently, there do not exist rational two-dimensional convex polygons
\(A_0,A_2,K\subset\mathbb R^2\) and rational shifts \(a_i,a_\ell\) satisfying
all of the following calibration requirements:
\[
|A_0|=1,\qquad |A_2|=\frac18,\qquad |K|=\frac{147}{160},
\qquad
\frac{A_0+A_2}{2}\subseteq K,
\tag{6}
\]
\[
c_i=\left|K\cap\{g_i\geq0\}\right|=\frac{107}{320},
\qquad
c_\ell=\left|K\cap\{g_\ell\geq0\}\right|=\frac{107}{320},
\qquad
c_q=\left|K\cap\{g_q\geq0\}\right|=\frac14,
\tag{7}
\]
\[
\left|A_0\cap\{x\geq-a_i\}\right|
=
\left|A_0\cap\{y\geq-a_\ell\}\right|
=\frac1{20},
\tag{8}
\]
\[
\left|A_2\cap\{x\geq a_i\}\right|
=
\left|A_2\cap\{y\geq a_\ell\}\right|
=\frac1{16},
\tag{9}
\]
and
\[
d_0=d_2=2,
\qquad
d_u:=\max_{(x,y)\in A_u}(x+y)
      -\min_{(x,y)\in A_u}x
      -\min_{(x,y)\in A_u}y
\quad(u\in\{0,2\}).
\tag{10}
\]

## Proof

We first prove (5).  Equation (1) gives the pointwise identity
\[
g_i+g_\ell+g_q=1.
\tag{11}
\]
Thus at least one of the three values is strictly positive at every point of
\(\mathbb R^2\).  In particular, the three closed halfplanes in (2) cover the
plane and
\[
C_i\cup C_\ell\cup C_q=K.
\tag{12}
\]
For \(z\in K\), let
\[
N(z)=\mathbf 1_{C_i}(z)+\mathbf 1_{C_\ell}(z)+\mathbf 1_{C_q}(z).
\]
Then \(N(z)\geq1\) by (12), and exact clipping multiplicity gives
\[
c_i+c_\ell+c_q-|K|
=\int_K\bigl(N(z)-1\bigr)\,dz
\geq0.
\tag{13}
\]

Suppose for contradiction that equality holds in (13).  The nonnegative
integrand must then vanish almost everywhere.  Since \(N\geq2\) on every
pairwise intersection, this implies
\[
|C_i\cap C_\ell|
=|C_i\cap C_q|
=|C_\ell\cap C_q|
=0.
\tag{14}
\]

The boundary of the two-dimensional polygon \(K\) and each of the three
cutting lines \(\{g_j=0\}\) have planar area zero.  By \(c_i>0\) and (14),
there is therefore a point
\[
p_i=(x_i,y_i)\in\operatorname{int}K
\]
which belongs to \(C_i\) and to neither of the other two clips, and which
lies on no cutting line.  By the definitions in (1)-(2),
\[
x_i>0,\qquad y_i<0,\qquad 1-x_i-y_i<0,
\tag{15}
\]
so \(x_i+y_i>1\).  Similarly, \(c_\ell>0\) supplies
\[
p_\ell=(x_\ell,y_\ell)\in\operatorname{int}K
\]
such that
\[
x_\ell<0,\qquad y_\ell>0,\qquad 1-x_\ell-y_\ell<0,
\tag{16}
\]
and hence \(x_\ell+y_\ell>1\).

Consider
\[
z(t)=(1-t)p_i+tp_\ell,\qquad 0\leq t\leq1.
\tag{17}
\]
The interior of a convex set is convex, so \(z(t)\in\operatorname{int}K\)
for every \(t\).  Affinity and (15)-(16) also give
\[
g_q(z(t))
=(1-t)g_q(p_i)+t g_q(p_\ell)<0
\qquad(0\leq t\leq1).
\tag{18}
\]
Equivalently,
\[
x(z(t))+y(z(t))>1
\qquad(0\leq t\leq1).
\tag{19}
\]

The coordinate \(y(z(t))\) changes from negative to positive, while
\(x(z(t))\) changes from positive to negative.  Let \(\tau_y\) and
\(\tau_x\) be their respective unique zero parameters.  At \(t=\tau_y\),
equation (19) gives \(x(z(\tau_y))>1\), so the \(x\)-coordinate has not yet
reached zero.  Hence
\[
\tau_y<\tau_x.
\tag{20}
\]
For any \(t\in(\tau_y,\tau_x)\),
\[
x(z(t))>0,\qquad y(z(t))>0.
\tag{21}
\]
Such a point lies in \(\operatorname{int}K\) and satisfies both inequalities
strictly.  A sufficiently small open disk around it is therefore contained
in
\[
K\cap\{g_i>0\}\cap\{g_\ell>0\}
\subseteq C_i\cap C_\ell.
\]
This makes \(|C_i\cap C_\ell|>0\), contradicting (14).  Equality in (13) is
impossible, which proves the strict inequality (5).

Now apply (5) to the single polygon \(K\) required in (6)-(7).  All three
clip areas in (7) are positive, but exact arithmetic gives
\[
c_i+c_\ell+c_q
=\frac{107}{320}+\frac{107}{320}+\frac14
=\frac{294}{320}
=\frac{147}{160}
=|K|.
\tag{22}
\]
This contradicts (5).  Therefore no such \(K\) exists, and consequently no
choice of \(A_0,A_2,a_i,a_\ell\) can satisfy the complete simultaneous
calibration (6)-(10).
