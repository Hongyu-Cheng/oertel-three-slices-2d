## Statement

Use the common-plane cells and compatibility separator of accepted fact
`2b42506f5de09be1`, with the full-section conventions of accepted fact
`989c9b585d2b42cc`.  In the \(k\ell\)-row coordinates, put
\[
B=\{(p_k,-p_\ell):\overline{P_\ell}\}
\subseteq\mathbb R_{\ge0}^2,
\tag{1}
\]
\[
D=\{(-q_k,q_\ell):\overline{Q_{jk}}\}
\subseteq\mathbb R_{\ge0}^2.
\tag{2}
\]
Suppose the full axis sections are
\[
B\cap\{Y=0\}
=[\tau,\tau+H]\times\{0\},
\tag{3}
\]
\[
B\cap\{X=0\}
=\{0\}\times[\gamma,\gamma+K],
\tag{4}
\]
\[
D\cap\{V=0\}
=[u,u+H_2]\times\{0\},
\tag{5}
\]
\[
D\cap\{U=0\}
=\{0\}\times[v,v+J_2],
\tag{6}
\]
where all four lengths are positive and all offsets are nonnegative.
Assume
\[
|D|_{\rm coord}=\Delta g_0
\tag{7}
\]
and that compatibility supplies \(r,s>0\) with
\[
\sup_{(X,Y)\in B}(rX+sY)
\le
\inf_{(U,V)\in D}(rU+sV).
\tag{8}
\]

Then necessarily
\[
u\ge\tau+H,\qquad
v\ge\gamma+K,
\tag{9}
\]
and hence
\[
2\Delta g_0
\ge
(\tau+H)J_2
+(\gamma+K)H_2
+H_2J_2.
\tag{10}
\]
Equivalently, in the trace notation of the accepted facts,
\[
2\Delta g_0
\ge
(\tau+\lambda_{0\ell})\lambda_{2k}
+(\gamma+\lambda_{0k})\lambda_{2\ell}
+\lambda_{2k}\lambda_{2\ell}.
\tag{11}
\]
This is a four-section separator-hull inequality.  It couples both
polygons and both coordinate axes.

For the exact survivor in accepted fact `fa0e57b225178b3b`,
\[
\Delta=1,\qquad g_0=\frac1{10},
\tag{12}
\]
\[
\tau=\frac32,\qquad
H=\lambda_{0\ell}=\frac15,
\tag{13}
\]
\[
\gamma=\frac45,\qquad
K=\lambda_{0k}=\frac1{10},
\tag{14}
\]
\[
J_2=\lambda_{2k}=\frac15,\qquad
H_2=\lambda_{2\ell}=\frac14.
\tag{15}
\]
Substitution in the right side of (10) gives
\[
\frac{17}{10}\cdot\frac15
+\frac9{10}\cdot\frac14
+\frac14\cdot\frac15
=\frac{123}{200}.
\tag{16}
\]
The left side is
\[
2\Delta g_0=\frac15=\frac{40}{200},
\tag{17}
\]
contradicting (10).

The survivor also fixes
\[
u=v=\frac3{10}.
\tag{18}
\]
Thus it already violates both coordinatewise consequences in (9):
\[
\frac3{10}<\frac{17}{10},
\qquad
\frac3{10}<\frac9{10}.
\tag{19}
\]
The area contradiction (16)-(17) is stronger in scope: it excludes
every possible choice of \(u,v\), not only the offsets in (18).

No endpoint-cap datum appears in (10): the outer cap areas
\(a,d,e,s\), the chord-profile derivatives, and the positive-part
branches of accepted fact `32a80f2a555ef83b` are absent.  Therefore
(10) is not a reformulation of any pairwise endpoint-cap inequality.
It is the required multi-section compatibility obstruction.

## Proof

The four far axis endpoints
\[
b_x=(\tau+H,0),\qquad
b_y=(0,\gamma+K),
\tag{20}
\]
\[
d_x=(u,0),\qquad
d_y=(0,v)
\tag{21}
\]
belong to \(B,B,D,D\), respectively, because (3)-(6) are full closed
sections.

Apply (8) first to \(b_x\in B\) and \(d_x\in D\).  It gives
\[
r(\tau+H)\le ru.
\]
Since \(r>0\),
\[
u\ge\tau+H.
\tag{22}
\]
Apply (8) to \(b_y\in B\) and \(d_y\in D\).  Since \(s>0\),
\[
s(\gamma+K)\le sv
\quad\Longrightarrow\quad
v\ge\gamma+K.
\tag{23}
\]
This proves (9).  Positivity of both separator coefficients is
essential; accepted fact `2b42506f5de09be1` proves it from the full
positive support.

The convex hull of the two \(D\)-axis intervals in (5)-(6) lies in
\(D\).  Its coordinate area is
\[
\frac12\bigl((u+H_2)(v+J_2)-uv\bigr)
=\frac12\bigl(uJ_2+vH_2+H_2J_2\bigr).
\tag{24}
\]
Using (7), (22), and (23) gives
\[
\begin{aligned}
\Delta g_0
&\ge
\frac12\bigl(
uJ_2+vH_2+H_2J_2
\bigr)\\
&\ge
\frac12\bigl(
(\tau+H)J_2
+(\gamma+K)H_2
+H_2J_2
\bigr),
\end{aligned}
\]
which is (10).

Equations (12)-(17) now give the strict contradiction
\[
\frac{40}{200}<\frac{123}{200}.
\]
Hence the exact rational survivor cannot have a positive compatibility
separator and therefore cannot extend to full common-plane polygons.

This argument uses all four full sections in (3)-(6): two locate the
far support values of \(B\), two charge area inside \(D\), and the same
positive separator orders both coordinate pairs.  Dropping either
axis loses one of the two terms in (10), and dropping the separator
removes (9).  This establishes the claimed genuinely multi-section
obstruction.
