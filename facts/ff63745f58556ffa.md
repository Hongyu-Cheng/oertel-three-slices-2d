## Statement

Let \(A_0,A_2\subset\mathbb R^2\) be nonempty two-dimensional convex polytopes, let
\[
B=\frac{A_0+A_2}{2},
\qquad
M=\max\{|A_0|,|A_2|\},
\qquad
m=\min\{|A_0|,|A_2|\},
\qquad
b=|B|,
\]
and define
\[
S_B=(\{0\}\times A_0)\cup(\{1\}\times B)\cup(\{2\}\times A_2),
\qquad
T_B=M+m+b.
\]
Then
\[
\max_{z\in B}h_{S_B}((1,z))-\frac{2T_B}{9}
\le
\frac{7(b+m)-2M}{9}.
\tag{1}
\]
Consequently \(7(b+m)\ge2M\) is necessary for the middle-only surplus at \(B\) to be nonnegative.

Moreover, let
\[
Q=[-1/2,1/2]^2,\qquad
A_0=K=Q,\qquad
A_2=\frac1{40}Q.
\]
Then
\[
B=\frac{A_0+A_2}{2}=\frac{41}{80}Q\subsetneq K.
\]
The enlarged triple with middle slice \(K\) lies in the exact residual area regime of accepted fact `15bb2c1680a0254e`, but
\[
\max_{z\in B}h_{S_B}((1,z))-\frac{2T_B}{9}
\le-\frac{67}{3840}<0.
\tag{2}
\]
At the same time, accepted theorem `96f5513b8ab88a8f` certifies the minimal triple \(S_B\) using an outer-slice witness. Thus (2) does not contradict the locked theorem; it disproves only the registered inference from that theorem to a nonnegative middle-only endpoint.

## Proof

For any \(z\in B\), choose the horizontal closed halfspace containing the middle slice and the smaller outer slice. If \(|A_2|=m\), take
\[
H=\{(x,w):x\ge1\};
\]
if \(|A_0|=m\), take
\[
H=\{(x,w):x\le1\}.
\]
In either case \((1,z)\in H\) and
\[
\mathcal H_2(S_B\cap H)=b+m.
\]
Therefore
\[
h_{S_B}((1,z))\le b+m
\]
for every \(z\in B\). Taking the maximum and subtracting \(2T_B/9\) gives
\[
\max_{z\in B}h_{S_B}((1,z))-\frac{2T_B}{9}
\le
b+m-\frac{2(M+m+b)}9
=
\frac{7(b+m)-2M}{9},
\]
proving (1).

For the explicit construction, \(|Q|=1\), so
\[
|A_0|=|K|=1,\qquad |A_2|=\frac1{1600}.
\]
Since \(Q+(1/40)Q=(41/40)Q\),
\[
B=\frac{41}{80}Q,
\qquad
b=|B|=\frac{1681}{6400}.
\]
For the enlarged triple,
\[
r=\frac{m}{M}=\frac1{1600},
\qquad
s=\frac{|K|}{M}=1.
\]
Here \(r<9/25\), and
\[
1-r=\frac{1599}{1600}<1<\frac{1601}{1600}=1+r.
\]
Also \(r<(27-2\sqrt{26})/25\). Thus accepted fact `15bb2c1680a0254e` places \((r,s)\) in its exact residual strip.

For the minimal triple,
\[
b+m
=
\frac{1681}{6400}+\frac1{1600}
=
\frac{337}{1280},
\qquad
T_B
=
1+\frac{337}{1280}
=
\frac{1617}{1280}.
\]
The horizontal halfspace \(x\ge1\) therefore gives, for every \(z\in B\),
\[
h_{S_B}((1,z))
\le\frac{337}{1280}.
\]
Since
\[
\frac{2T_B}{9}=\frac{539}{1920},
\]
one obtains
\[
\max_{z\in B}h_{S_B}((1,z))-\frac{2T_B}{9}
\le
\frac{337}{1280}-\frac{539}{1920}
=
-\frac{67}{3840}<0.
\]

Nevertheless,
\[
|A_0|=1>\frac{T_B}{2}=\frac{1617}{2560}.
\]
Hence the first alternative of accepted theorem `96f5513b8ab88a8f` supplies the outer witness \((0,(0,0))\) for \(S_B\). That theorem guarantees
\[
\max_{y\in S_B}h_{S_B}(y)-\frac{2T_B}{9}\ge0,
\]
but it does not guarantee the corresponding maximum restricted to \(y=(1,z)\).

Accordingly, define the all-slice surplus
\[
\Psi(L)=
\max_{y\in S_L}h_{S_L}(y)
-\frac{2(|A_0|+|L|+|A_2|)}9.
\]
Accepted theorem `96f5513b8ab88a8f` gives \(\Psi(B)\ge0\). Therefore an inclusion-monotonicity or finite cap-shaving lemma for \(\Psi\), rather than for the middle-only surplus, would have the required nonnegative endpoint. Alternatively, any middle-only route must first establish a separate endpoint lemma and cannot apply when \(7(b+m)<2M\).
