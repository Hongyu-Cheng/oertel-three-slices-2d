## Statement

Assume the affine sign-plane hypotheses and exact-cell convention of accepted fact `0e51a47a9add571f`. Thus \(f_1,f_2,f_3\) are nonconstant affine functions on \(\mathbb R^2\) with pairwise nonparallel linear parts,
\[
f_1+f_2+f_3=-1,
\qquad
s:=L_1+L_2+L_3<-1,
\]
and the source and middle exact cells are cut from two-dimensional convex polytopes \(A_0,A_2,K\) by the thresholds \(L_i,-L_i,0\), respectively.

Suppose these polytopes realize the strict rational table of accepted fact `c5aad5c9408fcf74`, in particular
\[
p_\varnothing=\frac{121}{10},\qquad
r_1=r_2=r_3=\frac{19}{10},\qquad
r_{123}=\frac1{10}.
\]
Define
\[
\delta=-1-s>0,\qquad
\gamma=1-s=\delta+2,
\]
and the three global ambient triangles
\[
\mathcal T_1=\{x:f_i(x)\leq0\text{ for }i=1,2,3\},
\]
\[
\mathcal T_P=\{x:f_i(x)\geq L_i\text{ for }i=1,2,3\},
\]
\[
\mathcal T_Q=\{x:f_i(x)\leq-L_i\text{ for }i=1,2,3\}.
\]
Then
\[
R_{123}=\mathcal T_1
\quad\text{up to cutting-line boundaries},\qquad
|\mathcal T_1|=\frac1{10},
\]
\[
|\mathcal T_P|=\delta^2|\mathcal T_1|=\frac{\delta^2}{10},
\qquad
\delta\geq11,
\]
and
\[
|\mathcal T_Q|=\gamma^2|\mathcal T_1|
=\frac{\gamma^2}{10},
\qquad
\gamma\geq13,\qquad
|\mathcal T_Q|\geq\frac{169}{10}.
\]

## Proof

By the affine-plane conclusion of accepted fact `0e51a47a9add571f`, the map
\[
F:\mathbb R^2\longrightarrow
\{z\in\mathbb R^3:z_1+z_2+z_3=-1\},
\qquad
F(x)=(f_1(x),f_2(x),f_3(x)),
\]
is an affine isomorphism.

All three middle singleton areas are positive. Accepted fact `ce343e749df349fe` therefore applies and gives
\[
\mathcal T_1\subseteq K,\qquad
R_{123}=\mathcal T_1
\]
up to the zero-area cutting lines. The realized table gives
\[
|\mathcal T_1|=r_{123}=\frac1{10}.
\tag{1}
\]

Let
\[
\Delta=\{u\in\mathbb R^3:u_i\geq0,\ u_1+u_2+u_3=1\}.
\]
The image of the middle triangle is
\[
F(\mathcal T_1)=-\Delta.
\tag{2}
\]

For the \(P\)-arrangement, put
\[
p_i(x)=f_i(x)-L_i.
\]
Their sum is
\[
\sum_i p_i=-1-s=\delta.
\]
The closure of the ambient chamber for the empty exact cell is \(\mathcal T_P\), and
\[
F(\mathcal T_P)
=(L_1,L_2,L_3)+\delta\Delta.
\tag{3}
\]
Equations (2)-(3) show that \(\mathcal T_P\) is a translated reflected dilation of \(\mathcal T_1\) by linear factor \(\delta\). The reflection has determinant of absolute value one on the two-dimensional affine plane, so
\[
|\mathcal T_P|=\delta^2|\mathcal T_1|
=\frac{\delta^2}{10}.
\tag{4}
\]

Under the exact convention, \(P_\varnothing\) uses the strict inequalities
\[
f_i>L_i\qquad(i=1,2,3).
\]
Hence \(P_\varnothing\) is contained in the interior of \(\mathcal T_P\). The three boundary lines have planar area zero, so (4) and the realized value of \(p_\varnothing\) give
\[
\frac{121}{10}=|P_\varnothing|
\leq|\mathcal T_P|
=\frac{\delta^2}{10}.
\]
Since \(\delta>0\), this yields
\[
\delta\geq11.
\tag{5}
\]

For the \(Q\)-arrangement, put
\[
q_i(x)=f_i(x)+L_i.
\]
Then
\[
\sum_iq_i=s-1=-\gamma.
\]
The global ambient chamber for the central exact cell is \(\mathcal T_Q\), and
\[
F(\mathcal T_Q)
=-(L_1,L_2,L_3)-\gamma\Delta.
\tag{6}
\]
Comparing (6) with (2), \(\mathcal T_Q\) is a translated dilation of
\(\mathcal T_1\) by linear factor \(\gamma\). Therefore
\[
|\mathcal T_Q|
=\gamma^2|\mathcal T_1|
=\frac{\gamma^2}{10}.
\tag{7}
\]
Finally,
\[
\gamma=\delta+2\geq13
\]
by (5), and (7) gives
\[
|\mathcal T_Q|\geq\frac{13^2}{10}
=\frac{169}{10}.
\]

All uses of strict versus weak inequalities differ only on the six source cutting lines and the three middle cutting lines, each of which has planar area zero by accepted fact `0e51a47a9add571f`. No inclusion of \(\mathcal T_Q\) in \(A_2\) has been used or claimed.
