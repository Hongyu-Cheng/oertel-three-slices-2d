## Statement

A triple \((a_0,a_1,a_2)\) is realized as the planar areas of three
two-dimensional convex-polytope sections at integer heights \(0,1,2\) of a
compact polytope \(C\subseteq\mathbb R^3\), with no other nonempty
integer-height section, if and only if
\[
a_0,a_1,a_2>0
\quad\text{and}\quad
\sqrt{a_0}+\sqrt{a_2}\le 2\sqrt{a_1}.
\]

## Proof

The planar Brunn-Minkowski theorem states that for nonempty planar convex
bodies \(B,D\subseteq\mathbb R^2\) and every \(\lambda\in[0,1]\),
\[
\bigl|(1-\lambda)B+\lambda D\bigr|^{1/2}
\ge (1-\lambda)|B|^{1/2}+\lambda|D|^{1/2}.
\]

For necessity, let \(A_i\) be the height-\(i\) section of a realizing
polytope. By hypothesis, each \(A_i\) is two-dimensional, so
\(a_i=|A_i|>0\). By accepted fact `8ad0785e5b4ae9ef`,
\[
\frac{A_0+A_2}{2}\subseteq A_1.
\]
Area monotonicity and the planar Brunn-Minkowski theorem with \(B=A_0\),
\(D=A_2\), and \(\lambda=1/2\) give
\[
\sqrt{a_1}
\ge \left|\frac{A_0+A_2}{2}\right|^{1/2}
\ge \frac{\sqrt{a_0}+\sqrt{a_2}}{2}.
\]

For sufficiency, suppose \(a_0,a_1,a_2>0\) and
\(\sqrt{a_0}+\sqrt{a_2}\le2\sqrt{a_1}\). Let \(K=[0,1]^2\) and define
\(A_i=\sqrt{a_i}\,K\). Then each \(A_i\) is a two-dimensional convex
polytope with area \(a_i\), and
\[
\frac{A_0+A_2}{2}
=\frac{\sqrt{a_0}+\sqrt{a_2}}{2}K
\subseteq\sqrt{a_1}K=A_1.
\]
Accepted fact `8ad0785e5b4ae9ef` therefore supplies a compact polytope whose
integer-height sections are exactly these \(A_0,A_1,A_2\), with no other
nonempty integer-height section.
