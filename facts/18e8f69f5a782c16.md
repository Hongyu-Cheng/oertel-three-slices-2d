## Proof

For \(\gamma>0\), the full diagonal and horizontal chords give, for
every \((X,Y)\in P_0\),
\[
 \gamma X+(\gamma+\tau)Y\le\gamma B,
\qquad
 GX+(G+H+\tau)Y\ge G\beta.
\tag{4}
\]
Let \(U\) be the positive-quadrant triangle cut out by the first row,
and let \(L\) be the positive-quadrant triangle cut out by reversing
the second row.  Their \(X\)-axis intercepts are \(B,\beta\), and their
\(Y\)-axis intercepts are \(u_b,\ell_b\).  The two perspective rows of
`fa0e57b225178b3b` are exactly
\[
 \ell_b\le\alpha,\qquad A\le u_b.
\]
Since \(J=A-\alpha>0\), one has \(\ell_b<u_b\); hence \(L\subset U\).
This proves at once that the \(D_b=0\) and \(D_b<0\) lens strata in
`d1a8755c0cc50d14` cannot occur for a genuine positive vertical
chord.

Let
\[
 D=\{X,Y\ge0:w_0X+\theta Y\le\theta w_0\}.
\]
The coordinatewise incidences give
\(\theta\ge B>\beta\) and
\(w_0\ge A>\alpha\ge\ell_b\), so \(L\subset D\).  Therefore the
largest allowed coordinate region is
\[
 (U\cap D)\setminus\operatorname{int}L.
\tag{5}
\]
If \(w_0\ge u_b\), then \(U\subset D\).  If \(w_0<u_b\), the two upper
lines meet in the positive quadrant.  The triangle removed from \(U\)
has \(Y\)-axis base \(u_b-w_0\) and horizontal altitude
\[
 x_*=\frac{B\theta(u_b-w_0)}
 {\theta u_b-Bw_0}.
\]
Its area is exactly \({\cal C}\).  Subtracting the area
\[
 |L|=\frac{G\beta^2}{2(G+H+\tau)}
\]
from \(|U|-{\cal C}\) proves (1).  It also proves all tie statements,
because no factor was divided out on a zero denominator.

The literal incidence
\(\gamma=0\Longleftrightarrow\tau=0\) removes the
\(\gamma=0<\tau\) stratum.  At \(\gamma=\tau=0\), the positive
endpoint \(K\) gives
\[

\]
while
\[
 |L|=\frac{K(\delta-H)^2}{2(K+H)}.
\]
The same clipping calculation, now with \(B=u_b=\delta\), proves
(2).  Cutting two wedges of width tending to zero at \(X=0\) gives
convex regions with the prescribed subchord and areas tending to
\({\cal M}_b\); the uncut envelope attains it precisely on the stated
endpoint saturation.  This establishes sharpness and boundary
completeness.
