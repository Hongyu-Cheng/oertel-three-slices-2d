## Statement

## Proof

Accepted fact `2b42506f5de09be1` uses
\[
p_i=f_i-L_i,\qquad q_i=f_i+L_i .
\]
For the survivor, accepted facts `989c9b585d2b42cc` and `d1a8755c0cc50d14` give
\[
p_j=X,\qquad p_\ell=Y,\qquad p_k=1-X-Y,
\]
while accepted facts `fa0e57b225178b3b` and `79e06fd7932f4121` give
\[
U=-q_k,\qquad V=q_\ell,\qquad -q_j=3-U+V .
\]
Thus an \(A_0\) point with displayed coordinates \((X,Y)\) has common coordinates
\[
(f_j,f_\ell)=(X+L_j,Y+L_\ell),
\]
whereas an \(A_2\) point with displayed coordinates \((U,V)\) has common coordinates
\[
(f_j,f_\ell)=(U-V-3-L_j,V-L_\ell).
\]
The constants cancel at the midpoint, so the exact common-coordinate midpoint map is
\[
(X,Y;U,V)\longmapsto
\left(\frac{X+U-V-3}{2},\frac{Y+V}{2}\right).
\tag{1}
\]
Also \(p_j+p_k+p_\ell=1\) and \(q_j+q_k+q_\ell=-3\), hence
\[
f_j+f_k+f_\ell=-1,
\]
which proves the asserted formula for \(f_k\).

Let \(a_1,\ldots,a_{10}\) be the cyclic \(A_0\) vertices listed in accepted fact `ba7a0577628a5b73`.  Let \((U_i,V_i)\), \(0\le i\le9\), be the cyclic \(A_2\) vertices listed in accepted fact `79e06fd7932f4121`, and put
\[
c_i=(U_i-V_i-3,V_i).
\]
Applying (1), the displayed \(b_1,\ldots,b_{20}\) arise from the following endpoint-index pairs:
\[
\begin{gathered}

\end{gathered}
\tag{2}
\]
where a pair \((i,j)\) denotes \((a_i+c_j)/2\).

Both endpoint facts certify their displayed lists as complete strictly convex counterclockwise boundary cycles.  For completeness, the following table audits the exact edge merge in (2).  Each edge \(b_{r+1}-b_r\), with \(b_{21}=b_1\), is a positive rational multiple of the primitive integer vector \(e_r\), and the last column is \(\det(e_r,e_{r+1})\), with \(e_{21}=e_1\).

\[

This contradicts (9), so no middle polygon \(K\) with the survivor's cell, section, crossing, and zero-cell data can contain the endpoint half-sum.
