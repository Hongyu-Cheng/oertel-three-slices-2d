## Statement

Let \(N=\{1,2,3\}\). Assume the affine sign-plane hypotheses and exact-cell convention of accepted fact `0e51a47a9add571f`: the nonconstant affine functions \(f_i\) have pairwise nonparallel linear parts,
\[
f_1+f_2+f_3=-1,
\]
the offsets satisfy \(\sum_iL_i<-1\), and the nonempty two-dimensional convex polytopes \(A_0,A_2,K\subset\mathbb R^2\) satisfy
\[
\frac{A_0+A_2}{2}\subseteq K.
\]
For \(J,H,G\subseteq N\), let \(P_J,Q_H,R_G\) be the exact cells
\[
P_J=A_0\cap\{f_\ell\le L_\ell\ (\ell\in J),\ f_\ell>L_\ell\ (\ell\notin J)\},
\]
\[
Q_H=A_2\cap\{f_\ell\le-L_\ell\ (\ell\in H),\ f_\ell>-L_\ell\ (\ell\notin H)\},
\]
\[
R_G=K\cap\{f_\ell\le0\ (\ell\in G),\ f_\ell>0\ (\ell\notin G)\},
\]
and write \(p_J=|P_J|\), \(q_H=|Q_H|\), and \(r_G=|R_G|\).

For \(k\in N\), suppose
\[
\sum_{J\subseteq N\setminus\{k\}}p_J>0
\quad\text{and}\quad
\sum_{H\subseteq N\setminus\{k\}}q_H>0.
\]
Then
\[
\frac14\left(
\sqrt{\sum_{J\subseteq N\setminus\{k\}}p_J}
+
\sqrt{\sum_{H\subseteq N\setminus\{k\}}q_H}
\right)^2
\le
\sum_{G\subseteq N\setminus\{k\}}r_G.
\tag{1}
\]

For the integer table of accepted fact `0835a8b11411ade7`, all three source partial-union areas are positive and each of the three inequalities (1) is violated. Consequently no common-plane convex polytopes can realize that table together with \((A_0+A_2)/2\subseteq K\).

## Proof

Fix \(k\in N\), and define the exact strict partial unions
\[
D_k=A_0\cap\{f_k>L_k\},\qquad
E_k=A_2\cap\{f_k>-L_k\},\qquad
T_k=K\cap\{f_k>0\}.
\]
The exact-cell definitions give the disjoint unions
\[
D_k=\bigcup_{J\subseteq N\setminus\{k\}}P_J,\qquad
E_k=\bigcup_{H\subseteq N\setminus\{k\}}Q_H,\qquad
T_k=\bigcup_{G\subseteq N\setminus\{k\}}R_G.
\tag{2}
\]
Therefore
\[
|D_k|=\sum_{J\subseteq N\setminus\{k\}}p_J,\quad
|E_k|=\sum_{H\subseteq N\setminus\{k\}}q_H,\quad
|T_k|=\sum_{G\subseteq N\setminus\{k\}}r_G.
\tag{3}
\]

Take \(x\in D_k\), \(y\in E_k\), and \(z=(x+y)/2\). The compatibility hypothesis puts \(z\in K\), while affinity gives
\[
2f_k(z)
=\bigl(f_k(x)-L_k\bigr)+\bigl(f_k(y)+L_k\bigr)>0.
\]
Thus the exact partial-union inclusion is
\[
\frac{D_k+E_k}{2}\subseteq T_k.
\tag{4}
\]
This is also the one-coordinate partial-union form of the incidence rule in accepted fact `0e51a47a9add571f`.

To apply the exact planar theorem with no openness issue, put
\[
\overline D_k=A_0\cap\{f_k\ge L_k\},\qquad
\overline E_k=A_2\cap\{f_k\ge-L_k\},\qquad
\overline T_k=K\cap\{f_k\ge0\}.
\]
The cutting lines have planar area zero, so
\[
|\overline D_k|=|D_k|,\qquad
|\overline E_k|=|E_k|,\qquad
|\overline T_k|=|T_k|.
\tag{5}
\]
The assumed positivity in the statement makes \(\overline D_k\) and \(\overline E_k\) nonempty planar convex bodies. The same affine calculation with weak inequalities gives
\[
\frac{\overline D_k+\overline E_k}{2}\subseteq\overline T_k.
\tag{6}
\]
The planar Brunn--Minkowski theorem stated in accepted fact `d7bdfc054d5093e8`, applied to \(\overline D_k,\overline E_k\) with \(\lambda=1/2\), and area monotonicity in (6) give
\[
\frac14\bigl(\sqrt{|\overline D_k|}+\sqrt{|\overline E_k|}\bigr)^2
\le
\left|\frac{\overline D_k+\overline E_k}{2}\right|
\le|\overline T_k|.
\]
Substitution of (3) and (5) proves (1).

Now use the exact scalar table in accepted fact `0835a8b11411ade7`. By symmetry it suffices to take \(k=3\), so \(N\setminus\{k\}=\{1,2\}\). The three areas in (3) are
\[
|D_3|=p_0+p_1+p_2+p_{12}=56+1+1+0=58,
\]
\[
|E_3|=q_0+q_1+q_2+q_{12}=0+0+0+1=1,
\]
and
\[
|T_3|=r_0+r_1+r_2+r_{12}=0+7+7+4=18.
\]
Therefore (1) would require
\[
\frac{(\sqrt{58}+1)^2}{4}
=\frac{59+2\sqrt{58}}4
\le18.
\]
But \(2\sqrt{58}>13\), because \(232>169\), so the left side is strictly larger than \(18\). This contradiction proves universal nonrealizability of the calibration.
