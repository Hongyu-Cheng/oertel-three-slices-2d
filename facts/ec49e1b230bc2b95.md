## Statement

Let \(K\subset\mathbb R^2\) be a compact convex body with nonempty interior, area
\[
D=|K|,
\]
and centroid \(g\). Let \(p\in K\), and let
\[
a\ge c>0,\qquad
1<a^2+c^2,\qquad
a+c<2,\qquad
a^2<1+c^2.
\]
Set
\[
A_0=p+a(K-p),\qquad
A_1=K,\qquad
A_2=p+c(K-p),
\]
\[
S=\bigcup_{i=0}^2(\{i\}\times A_i),\qquad
T=D(a^2+1+c^2).
\]
Suppose there is a compact polytope \(C\subset\mathbb R^3\) such that
\[
C\cap(\mathbb Z\times\mathbb R^2)=S.
\]
Then
\[
h_S((1,g))
\ge \frac{2D(a^2+1+c^2)}9
=\frac{2T}{9}.
\]

## Proof

Fix \(L_h>0\), a nonnegative concave Lebesgue probability density
\[
h:[0,L_h]\to[0,\infty)
\]
of mean \(1\), and extend it by zero only for purposes of defining
\[
G_h(w)=\int_w^\infty h(y)\,dy.
\]
Fix also \(w_0,w_2\in\mathbb R\) satisfying
\[
aw_0+cw_2=2.
\]
All parameter, support, probability, mean, and tail hypotheses of accepted fact `52e60b02804280c7` hold. Therefore
\[
G_h(1)+a^2G_h(w_0)+c^2G_h(w_2)
\ge \frac{2(1+a^2+c^2)}9.
\tag{1}
\]
Since \(L_h\), \(h\), \(w_0\), and \(w_2\) were arbitrary, (1) is exactly the universal anchored-tail hypothesis required by accepted fact `95f17bf1e5ee59f0`.

The bodies \(K,A_0,A_1,A_2\), the point \(p\), the parameters \(a,c\), and the three-slice set \(S=C\cap(\mathbb Z\times\mathbb R^2)\) satisfy all geometric hypotheses of `95f17bf1e5ee59f0`. That accepted fact also establishes that \(g\in K\), handles projection endpoints and thresholds outside the projected support, proves the required monotonicity in the projected point \(p\), and checks both horizontal halfspaces including their full middle-slice boundary mass. Applying it with (1) gives
\[
h_S((1,g))
\ge \frac{2T}{9}
=\frac{2D(a^2+1+c^2)}9.
\]
This proves the theorem.
