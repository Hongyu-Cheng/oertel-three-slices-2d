## Proof

The set \(D_\tau\) is convex by (4), and
\[
C_\tau\subseteq D_\tau\subseteq A_{2,\tau}.
\tag{29}
\]
The exact support partitions \(D_\tau\), up to null cutting lines,
into \(Q_1,Q_2,Q_{12}\).  The definition (7) similarly partitions
\(C_\tau\) into \(Q_1,Q_2\), and its retained part of \(Q_{12}\).
After multiplying \(\tau\)-areas by \(s^2\),
\[
|D_r|=q_1+q_2+q_{12},
\qquad
|C_r|=q_1+q_2+\gamma_C,
\tag{30}
\]
so
\[
|D_r|-|C_r|=b_{12}.
\tag{31}
\]

If \(x\in P\) and \(\tau\in D_\tau\), then the corresponding point
\[
y=\frac12(x+2\lambda-s\tau)\in H_D
\]
satisfies
\[
f_3(y)
=\Delta\left(
\frac{x_3+2\lambda_3-s\tau_3}{2}-\lambda_3
\right)
=\frac{\Delta}{2}(x_3-s\tau_3)\geq0,
\tag{32}
\]
because \(x_3\geq0\) and \(\tau_3\leq0\).
Compatibility gives \(H_D\subseteq K\).  A nonconstant affine
cutting line has planar area zero, hence
\[
|H_D|\leq|K\cap\{f_3>0\}|=\kappa-c_3.
\tag{33}
\]

It remains to compare \(H_D\) with \(H_{12}\).  For planar convex
bodies write mixed area as
\[
|E+F|=|E|+|F|+2V(E,F).
\]
Inclusion \(C_r\subseteq D_r\) gives
\[
V(P,C_r)\leq V(P,D_r).
\tag{34}
\]
For completeness, (34) follows without an external theorem: for
every \(t>0\),
\[
P+tC_r\subseteq P+tD_r.
\]
Subtract the two mixed-area polynomials, divide by \(2t\), and let
\(t\downarrow0\).

Using (31), (34), and quadratic area scaling,
\[
\begin{aligned}
|H_D|-|H_{12}|
&=\frac14\left(
b_{12}
+2V(P,D_r)-2V(P,C_r)
\right)\\
&\geq\frac{b_{12}}4.
\end{aligned}
\tag{35}
\]
Equations (33) and (35) prove (9).

The third crossing is
\[
u_3+q_{123}+c_3=\frac{2(M+m+\kappa)}9,
\tag{36}
\]
because \(Q_{123}\) is the only positive \(Q\)-cell whose index
contains \(3\).  Rearranging (36) gives the equality in (10).
Substituting \(\kappa<M+m\) gives its strict inequality.

Under (11), accepted fact `9374e5c5a811bd3d` gives
\[
|H_{12}\cap\{f_i\leq0\}|
\geq
\frac14(s\beta_i\ell_i+q_i+\gamma_C).
\tag{37}
\]
Condition (13) contradicts the strict row capacity
\[
|K\cap\{f_i\leq0\}|<U_i.
\]
If both instances of (13) fail, (37) gives (14).  Combining (9),
(14), and the Bonnesen lower bound from accepted fact
`3c17eb0b21eeb63c`,
\[
|H_{12}|\geq\mathcal B_L,
\tag{38}
\]
gives
\[
\kappa-c_3>
\mathcal B_L+\frac{q_{12}-B}{4}.
\tag{39}
\]
The strict upper bound in (10) and (39) prove (16), and its reverse
weak inequality is therefore an exclusion gate.

We finish by auditing the sharpness calibration.  The four points in
(20) have coordinate sum one.  Their successive oriented edge
determinants in the displayed cyclic order are
\[

\frac{2560451}{201000000},\qquad

the remaining unsupported sign chambers are separated from the
quadrilateral.  This proves (21).  Direct clipping by the three
coordinate halfplanes and the shoelace formula give (22), whose sum
