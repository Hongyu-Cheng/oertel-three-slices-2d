## Statement

Let \(N=\{1,2,3\}\).  Assign every unlisted cell mass zero and set, for distinct \(i,j\in N\),
\[
p_\varnothing=\frac{121}{10},
\qquad
p_{\{i\}}=\frac15,
\]
\[
q_N=\frac25,
\qquad
q_{N\setminus\{i\}}=\frac15,
\]
\[
r_{\{i\}}=\frac{19}{10},
\qquad
r_{\{i,j\}}=1,
\qquad
r_N=\frac1{10}.
\]
Then
\[
M=\frac{127}{10},
\qquad
m=1,
\qquad
k=\frac{44}{5}.
\]
This table has the all-double \(Q\)-support of fact 91cb5bfc71da49ac, satisfies the crossings, canonical tails, source and middle cap inequalities, overlap correction, middle support implication, defect identity, and both inequalities of fact e1361027c1dda6fa, and has \(W=0\).

Moreover, for every disjoint \(A,B,C,D\subseteq N\) for which the two source partial unions in fact b530304a83fe1e51 have positive area,
\[
\frac14\left(\sqrt{|P[A;B]|}+\sqrt{|Q[C;D]|}\right)^2
\le
\sum_{G\in\mathcal T(A,B,C,D)}r_G.
\]
Every one of these inequalities is strict.

## Proof

We first derive and exhaust the complete partial-union family.  Write
\[
x_i=q_{N\setminus\{i\}}.
\]
Under all-double support, the exact expansion in fact b530304a83fe1e51 gives
\[
|Q[C;D]|=
\begin{cases}
\displaystyle q_N+\sum_{i\notin C}x_i,&D=\varnothing,\\[6pt]
x_i,&D=\{i\},\\[2pt]
0,&|D|\ge2.
\end{cases}
\tag{1}
\]
Indeed, when \(D=\varnothing\), the central cell and precisely the doubleton cells \(N\setminus\{i\}\) with \(i\notin C\) occur.  When \(D=\{i\}\), the only supported cell disjoint from \(D\) is \(N\setminus\{i\}\), and it contains every permissible \(C\subseteq N\setminus\{i\}\).  Thus the positive \(Q\)-side of the complete family has exactly the eight pairs with \(D=\varnothing\) and the twelve pairs with \(D=\{i\}\).

For the present \(P\)-support, the same exact expansion gives
\[
|P[A;B]|=
\begin{cases}
\displaystyle \frac{121}{10}+\frac{3-|B|}{5},&A=\varnothing,\\[6pt]
\displaystyle \frac15,&A=\{i\},\\[2pt]
0,&|A|\ge2.
\end{cases}
\tag{2}
\]
Hence there are also twenty positive \(P\)-side pairs.  The complete positive-source family therefore has \(20\cdot20=400\) raw members.  Partial unions of area zero are not assigned an inequality, exactly as required in the registered statement.

For all four active combinations \(A=\varnothing\) or \(\{a\}\), and \(D=\varnothing\) or \(\{d\}\), the two plane-sum exclusions in the closed formula for \(\mathcal T\) never fire.  If \(A=\varnothing\), then \(N\setminus A\) has size three; if \(A=\{a\}\), it has size two.  Likewise, if \(D=\varnothing\), then \(N\setminus D\) has size three; if \(D=\{d\}\), it has size two.  The sets on the other sides of the two forbidden containments have size at most one in every active case.  Consequently
\[
\mathcal T(A,B,C,D)
=
\{G\ne\varnothing:A\cap C\subseteq G,\ G\cap(B\cap D)=\varnothing\}.
\tag{3}
\]

The target masses needed below are
\[
\sum_{G\ne\varnothing}r_G=\frac{44}{5},
\tag{4}
\]
\[
\sum_{\substack{G\ne\varnothing\\d\notin G}}r_G
=2\cdot\frac{19}{10}+1
=\frac{24}{5},
\tag{5}
\]
\[
\sum_{G\ni a}r_G
=\frac{19}{10}+2+\frac1{10}
=4,
\tag{6}
\]
and, for \(a\ne d\),
\[
\sum_{\substack{G\ni a\\d\notin G}}r_G
=\frac{19}{10}+1
=\frac{29}{10}.
\tag{7}
\]
Equations (1)--(7) give the following complete table.  A row is a possible positive value \(a=|P[A;B]|\), a column is a possible positive value \(b=|Q[C;D]|\), and the entry is the minimum of \(\sum_{G\in\mathcal T}r_G\) over all index choices producing that pair of source areas:
\[
\begin{array}{c|ccccc}
 &\frac15&\frac25&\frac35&\frac45&1\\ \hline
\frac15
 &\frac{29}{10}&4&4&4&\frac{44}{5}\\
\frac{121}{10}
 &\frac{24}{5}&\frac{44}{5}&\frac{44}{5}&\frac{44}{5}&\frac{44}{5}\\
\frac{123}{10}
 &\frac{24}{5}&\frac{44}{5}&\frac{44}{5}&\frac{44}{5}&\frac{44}{5}\\
\frac{25}{2}
 &\frac{24}{5}&\frac{44}{5}&\frac{44}{5}&\frac{44}{5}&\frac{44}{5}\\
\frac{127}{10}
 &\frac{44}{5}&\frac{44}{5}&\frac{44}{5}&\frac{44}{5}&\frac{44}{5}
\end{array}
\tag{8}
\]
For completeness, the four cases producing (8) are as follows.  If \(A=D=\varnothing\), equation (3) gives every nonempty target cell.  If \(A=\varnothing\) and \(D=\{d\}\), it gives every target cell when \(d\notin B\), and exactly the cells excluding \(d\) when \(d\in B\).  If \(A=\{a\}\) and \(D=\varnothing\), it gives every target cell when \(a\notin C\), and exactly the cells containing \(a\) when \(a\in C\).  Finally, if \(A=\{a\}\) and \(D=\{d\}\), equation (3) imposes independently the possible conditions \(a\in G\) and \(d\notin G\); the minimum is (7), attained when \(a\ne d\), \(a\in C\), and \(d\in B\).  This proves every entry of (8), including all duplicates and all zero-area cases.

Put
\[
\Phi(a,b)=\frac14(\sqrt a+\sqrt b)^2.
\]
This function is increasing in both arguments.  Coordinatewise domination reduces all twenty-five entries of (8) to the following four triples \((a,b,c)\):
\[
\left(\frac15,\frac15,\frac{29}{10}\right),
\quad
\left(\frac15,\frac45,4\right),
\quad
\left(\frac{25}{2},\frac15,\frac{24}{5}\right),
\quad
\left(\frac{127}{10},1,\frac{44}{5}\right).
\tag{9}
\]
To verify \(\Phi(a,b)\le c\) exactly, set
\[
\Lambda=4c-a-b.
\]
It is enough that \(\Lambda>0\) and \(\Lambda^2-4ab>0\).  For the four triples in (9), respectively, these two quantities are
\[
\begin{array}{c|c}
\Lambda&\Lambda^2-4ab\\ \hline
\frac{56}{5}&\frac{3132}{25}\\
15&\frac{5609}{25}\\
\frac{13}{2}&\frac{129}{4}\\
\frac{43}{2}&\frac{8229}{20}
\end{array}
\tag{10}
\]
and are all strictly positive.  Thus every one of the 400 positive-source partial-union inequalities holds strictly.  Cutting-line boundaries have area zero by fact b530304a83fe1e51, so the weak/strict exact-cell convention does not change this enumeration or any area in (1)--(10).

It remains to verify every other registered scalar condition.  The totals are
\[
\frac{121}{10}+3\cdot\frac15=\frac{127}{10},
\qquad
\frac25+3\cdot\frac15=1,
\]
\[
3\cdot\frac{19}{10}+3+\frac1{10}=\frac{44}{5}.
\tag{11}
\]
For every \(i\),
\[
u_i=\frac15,
\qquad
v_i=\frac45,
\qquad
c_i=\frac{19}{10}+1+1+\frac1{10}=4.
\tag{12}
\]
The common crossing level is
\[
h=\frac{2(M+m+k)}9
=\frac{2(127/10+1+44/5)}9
=5.
\tag{13}
\]
Hence all three crossings hold:
\[
u_i+v_i+c_i=\frac15+\frac45+4=5=h.
\tag{14}
\]
The canonical left tails hold at equality,
\[
c_i+m=4+1=h,
\tag{15}
\]
and the right tails are strict because \(c_i+M>h\).  Also
\[
M\ge m>0,
\qquad
0<u_i<M,
\qquad
0<v_i<m,
\qquad
0<h<M,
\tag{16}
\]
so the equality-tail state is the allowed proper-proper case of fact 624b129b68e0fdee.

The weighted excess is zero directly.  The \(P,Q,R\) contributions are, respectively,
\[
-2\cdot\frac{121}{10}+3\cdot\frac15=-\frac{118}{5},
\]
\[
3\cdot4\cdot\frac15+7\cdot\frac25=\frac{26}{5},
\]
\[
3\cdot\frac{19}{10}+3\cdot4+7\cdot\frac1{10}=\frac{92}{5}.
\]
Therefore
\[
W=-\frac{118}{5}+\frac{26}{5}+\frac{92}{5}=0.
\tag{17}
\]
Equivalently, with
\[
E_R=r_{12}+r_{13}+r_{23}+2r_N=\frac{16}{5},
\]
the all-double excess equality is
\[
\sum_i u_i+m+q_N+E_R
=\frac35+1+\frac25+\frac{16}{5}
=\frac{26}{5}
=\frac{2M-m-k}{3}.
\tag{18}
\]
The defect identity of fact 0819231d0af83197 also holds exactly:
\[
2M+2m-3\sum_i(u_i+v_i)-k
=\frac{48}{5}
=3E_R.
\tag{19}
\]

For the source three-cap inequalities of fact 8234d1112ec2f031,
\[
q_N^2m=\frac4{25}
>
\frac4{125}
=4\prod_i q_{N\setminus\{i\}},
\tag{20}
\]
and
\[
\sum_i\sqrt{\frac{q_{N\setminus\{i\}}}{m}}
=\frac3{\sqrt5}
<\frac32.
\tag{21}
\]

The overlap-corrected inequality of fact 2bb72698f0248a65 has left side
\[
\frac14\sum_i
\left(\sqrt{p_\varnothing}+\sqrt{q_{N\setminus\{i\}}}\right)^2
=
\frac34\left(\sqrt{\frac{121}{10}}+\sqrt{\frac15}\right)^2
=\frac{369+66\sqrt2}{40},
\tag{22}
\]
whereas its right side is
\[
k-r_N+\sum_i r_i
=\frac{44}{5}-\frac1{10}+\frac{57}{10}
=\frac{72}{5}.
\tag{23}
\]
The difference is
\[
\frac{207-66\sqrt2}{40}>0,
\tag{24}
\]
since \(\sqrt2<2\).

All three doubleton middle masses and the central mass are positive, so the support implication of fact 93f1cefaa4795a25 holds.  For the corrected middle quantities of fact ce343e749df349fe, \(t=r_N=1/10\) and
\[
e_i
=
\left(
\frac{19}{10}-\frac{1\cdot1}{1/10}
\right)_+
=0.
\tag{25}
\]
Thus both corrected middle three-cap inequalities hold, with the product inequality reducing to \(k^3>0\) and the square-root left side equal to zero.

Finally, all singleton masses are positive, so the strengthened inequality of fact e1361027c1dda6fa applies.  Its left side is
\[
\left(
\sum_i\sqrt{r_i+\frac{2r_N}{9}}
\right)^2
=
9\left(\frac{19}{10}+\frac1{45}\right)
=\frac{173}{10},
\tag{26}
\]
while
\[
2k=\frac{88}{5}=\frac{176}{10}.
\tag{27}
\]
Hence the strengthened inequality is strict.  The support-uniform strict inequality is also satisfied:
\[
\left(\sum_i\sqrt{r_i}\right)^2
=9\cdot\frac{19}{10}
=\frac{171}{10}
<
\frac{176}{10}
=2k.
\tag{28}
\]
Equations (1)--(28) verify the complete registered scalar system exactly at \(W=0\).
