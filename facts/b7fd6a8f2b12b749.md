## Statement

Let \(A_0,A_2,K\subset\mathbb R^2\) be nonempty two-dimensional convex
polytopes with
\[
\frac{A_0+A_2}{2}\subseteq K,
\qquad
M=|A_0|\ge m=|A_2|>0,
\qquad
k=|K|>0,
\qquad
t=\frac{2(M+m+k)}9<M.
\tag{1}
\]
Let \(f_1,f_2,f_3\) be nonconstant affine functions with pairwise
nonparallel linear parts and
\[
f_1+f_2+f_3=-1.
\tag{2}
\]
For \(j=1,2,3\), put
\[
c_j=|K\cap\{f_j\le0\}|,
\]
\[
\mu_j(a)
=c_j+|A_0\cap\{f_j\le a\}|
     +|A_2\cap\{f_j\le-a\}|,
\qquad
I_j=\{a:\mu_j(a)<t\}.
\tag{3}
\]
Assume
\[
c_j+m\ge t,\qquad
I_j\ne\varnothing,\qquad
L_j=\inf I_j
\quad(j=1,2,3).
\tag{4}
\]
Relabel so that \(\{i,\ell,q\}=\{1,2,3\}\), and suppose the \(q\)-row is
strictly empty on \(A_0\):
\[
L_q<\alpha_q:=\min_{A_0}f_q.
\tag{5}
\]
Let
\[
\Delta=\left|\det(\nabla f_i,\nabla f_\ell)\right|>0,
\qquad
\Phi(x)=
\bigl(f_i(x)-L_i,\ f_\ell(x)-L_\ell\bigr),
\tag{6}
\]
and assume that, for some \(D>0\) and \(x,y\ge0\),
\[
\Phi(A_0)
=\{(a,b):a\ge-x,\ b\ge-y,\ a+b\le D\}.
\tag{7}
\]
Put
\[
H=D+x+y,\qquad
X=\frac{x}{H},\qquad
Y=\frac{y}{H},\qquad
r=\frac mM,
\tag{8}
\]
and assume the complementary residual ratio band
\[
\frac12<r<\rho,
\qquad
\rho=\frac{27-2\sqrt{26}}{25},
\tag{9}
\]
where \(\rho\) is the residual upper endpoint in accepted fact
`15bb2c1680a0254e`.

Then
\[
L_1+L_2+L_3\ge-1.
\tag{10}
\]
Equivalently, no configuration satisfying (1)-(9) belongs to the strict-low
alternative \(L_1+L_2+L_3<-1\).  This is exactly the high-ratio complement
of the shifted-wedge analysis in accepted fact `05d9f57f66fde272`, with the
lower-crossing boundary conventions supplied by accepted fact
`624b129b68e0fdee`.

## Proof

Suppose for contradiction that
\[
S:=L_1+L_2+L_3<-1.
\tag{11}
\]
For \(j=1,2,3\), define the two endpoint-cap areas
\[
u_j=|A_0\cap\{f_j\le L_j\}|,
\qquad
v_j=|A_2\cap\{f_j\le-L_j\}|.
\tag{12}
\]
Accepted fact `624b129b68e0fdee`, applied to (3)-(4), gives the three exact
lower-crossing equalities
\[
c_j+u_j+v_j=t
\qquad(j=1,2,3).
\tag{13}
\]
This statement includes both proper-proper and empty-full endpoint states.
Condition (5) gives
\[
u_q=0.
\tag{14}
\]

The three closed central caps cover the common middle polygon:
\[
K\subseteq\bigcup_{j=1}^3\{f_j\le0\}.
\tag{15}
\]
Indeed, a point outside the union would satisfy \(f_j>0\) for all \(j\),
contrary to (2).  Therefore
\[
\sum_{j=1}^3c_j\ge k.
\tag{16}
\]

The three closed small-end caps also cover \(A_2\):
\[
A_2\subseteq\bigcup_{j=1}^3\{f_j\le-L_j\}.
\tag{17}
\]
If a point of \(A_2\) lay outside this union, then
\[
-1=\sum_{j=1}^3f_j>-\sum_{j=1}^3L_j=-S>1,
\]
which is impossible.  Hence
\[
\sum_{j=1}^3v_j\ge m.
\tag{18}
\]
Thus the actual \(q\)-crossing is essential: it supplies the \(q\)-term in
(13), while (15) uses the single common \(K\).

Summing (13), and then using (14), (16), and (18), gives
\[
\frac{2(M+m+k)}3
=3t
=\sum_jc_j+u_i+u_\ell+\sum_jv_j
\ge k+u_i+u_\ell+m.
\]
Consequently
\[
u_i+u_\ell\le\frac{2M-m-k}{3}.
\tag{19}
\]
After division by \(M\), and with \(s=k/M>0\), this is
\[
\frac{u_i+u_\ell}{M}
\le\frac{2-r-s}{3}
<\frac{2-r}{3}.
\tag{20}
\]

Under the strict-low assumption (11), accepted fact
`05d9f57f66fde272` applies to the shifted wedge (7).  Its exact cap formulas
and branch-wide chord product give
\[
\frac{u_i}{M}=2X-X^2,
\qquad
\frac{u_\ell}{M}=2Y-Y^2,
\qquad
(1-X)(1-Y)\le r.
\tag{21}
\]
Set
\[
A=1-X,\qquad B=1-Y.
\]
Since \(D>0\) and \(x,y\ge0\), one has \(0<A,B\le1\).  Therefore
\[
(1-A^2)(1-B^2)\ge0,
\]
so
\[
A^2+B^2\le1+A^2B^2\le1+r^2.
\tag{22}
\]
Using (21)-(22),
\[
\frac{u_i+u_\ell}{M}
=2-A^2-B^2
\ge1-r^2.
\tag{23}
\]
This inequality includes \(X=0\), \(Y=0\), and equality in the accepted
chord product.

It remains only to compare (20) and (23).  Since \(\sqrt{26}>4\),
\[
\rho=\frac{27-2\sqrt{26}}{25}<\frac{19}{25}.
\tag{24}
\]
The polynomial \(\phi(r)=1+r-3r^2\) is strictly decreasing for
\(r>1/2\), and
\[
\phi\left(\frac{19}{25}\right)=\frac{17}{625}>0.
\]
Thus (9) and (24) imply
\[
1+r-3r^2>0,
\]
or equivalently
\[
1-r^2>\frac{2-r}{3}.
\tag{25}
\]
Equations (20), (23), and (25) now give
\[
\frac{u_i+u_\ell}{M}
\ge1-r^2
>\frac{2-r}{3}
>\frac{2-r-s}{3}
\ge\frac{u_i+u_\ell}{M},
\]
a contradiction.  Hence (11) is impossible, proving (10).

All cover statements above use closed caps, so cutting-line and supporting-face
contacts create no loss.  Accepted fact `624b129b68e0fdee` supplies (13) in
both the proper-proper and empty-full cases, while accepted fact
`05d9f57f66fde272` supplies (21) in the corresponding interior-chord and
supporting-face cases.  Thus every cap-state and equality breakpoint required
in the subgoal is included.
