## Statement

Let \(N=\{j,k,\ell\}\), and use standard coordinates \((r,\omega)\) on \(\mathbb R^2\). Define
\[
\begin{array}{lll}
p_k=7-r,&p_\ell=7+\omega-r,&p_j=2r-\omega,\\
q_k=-r,&q_\ell=\omega-r,&q_j=2r-\omega-16,
\end{array}
\]
and put
\[
f_i=\frac{p_i+q_i}{2},\qquad L_i=\frac{q_i-p_i}{2}.
\]
Let
\[
A_0=
\left[\frac{203}{365},\frac{2352}{365}\right]
\times\left[-\frac12,\frac12\right],
\]
\[
A_2=[-1,9]\times
\left[-\frac1{200},\frac1{200}\right],
\]
and
\[
K=
\left[-\frac{81}{365},\frac{5637}{730}\right]
\times\left[-\frac{101}{400},\frac{101}{400}\right].
\]
Then
\[
K=\frac{A_0+A_2}{2}.
\]
With the exact sign cells of accepted fact `0e51a47a9add571f`, one has
\[
p_\varnothing=\frac{2149}{365},\qquad
q_{\{j\}}=\frac{799}{80000}>0,\qquad
q_{\{k,\ell\}}=\frac1{100}>0.
\]
If
\[
T=|A_0|+|A_2|+|K|,\qquad t=\frac{2T}{9},
\]
and
\[
u_i=\left|A_0\cap\{f_i\le L_i\}\right|,\quad
v_i=\left|A_2\cap\{f_i\le-L_i\}\right|,\quad
c_i=\left|K\cap\{f_i\le0\}\right|,
\]
then, for every \(i\in N\),
\[
u_i+v_i+c_i=t.
\]
Moreover, for
\[
\mu_i(a)=c_i+\left|A_0\cap\{f_i\le a\}\right|
+\left|A_2\cap\{f_i\le-a\}\right|,
\qquad
I_i=\{a:\mu_i(a)<t\},
\]
one has
\[
L_i=\inf I_i.
\]
Thus all three displayed equalities are genuine lower crossings in the sense calibrated by accepted fact `624b129b68e0fdee`.

Define
\[
E=\overline{Q_{\{j\}}},\qquad
F=\overline{Q_{\{k,\ell\}}},\qquad
H=\operatorname{conv}(E\cup F),
\]
\[
U=\frac{P_\varnothing+E}{2},\qquad
V=\frac{P_\varnothing+F}{2},
\]
\[
D=\frac{P_\varnothing+H}{2}\cap\{-1<f_j<0\},
\qquad s_j=|D|.
\]
Then \(s_j>0\), the sets \(U,V,D\) occupy the three required pairwise disjoint \(f_j\)-bands, and nevertheless
\[
q_{\{j\}}+4q_{\{k,\ell\}}+|U|
+\left|V\cap\left(R_{\{k\}}\cup R_{\{\ell\}}\right)\right|
+4|V\cap R_{\{k,\ell\}}|+3s_j
<2p_\varnothing.
\]

## Proof

The affine functions are
\[
f_k=\frac72-r,\qquad
f_\ell=\frac72+\omega-r,\qquad
f_j=2r-\omega-8,
\]
and
\[
L_k=L_\ell=-\frac72,\qquad L_j=-8.
\]
Their linear parts are pairwise nonparallel, and
\[
\sum_i p_i=14,\qquad
\sum_iq_i=-16,\qquad
\sum_i f_i=-1,\qquad
\sum_iL_i=-15<-1.
\tag{1}
\]
The midpoint of two axis-parallel rectangles is the rectangle obtained by averaging the corresponding coordinate intervals. Directly averaging the endpoints of \(A_0\) and \(A_2\) gives the displayed \(K\). Hence the required midpoint containment holds with equality.

For completeness, set
\[
C=\operatorname{conv}\bigl((\{0\}\times A_0)\cup
(\{2\}\times A_2)\bigr)\subset\mathbb R^3.
\]
The slice of \(C\) at height \(1\) is \(K=(A_0+A_2)/2\), while its endpoint slices are \(A_0,A_2\). Since the height projection of \(C\) is \([0,2]\), these are exactly its three integer-height slices. Thus this is an admissible compact three-dimensional polytope realization of the locked three-slice hypotheses.

The minimum values of the three \(p\)-coordinates on \(A_0\) are
\[
\min_{A_0}p_k=\frac{203}{365},\qquad
\min_{A_0}p_\ell=\frac{41}{730},\qquad
\min_{A_0}p_j=\frac{447}{730}.
\tag{2}
\]
They are all positive. Therefore \(P_\varnothing=A_0\),
\[
p_\varnothing=|A_0|=\frac{2149}{365},
\tag{3}
\]
and \(u_i=0\) for all \(i\).

On \(A_2\), the cell \(Q_{\{j\}}\) is given, up to cutting-line boundaries, by
\[
-1\le r<\min\{0,\omega\}.
\]
The inequality \(q_j\le0\) is automatic there. Hence
\[
q_{\{j\}}
=\int_{-1/200}^{0}(1+\omega)\,d\omega
+\int_0^{1/200}1\,d\omega
=\frac{799}{80000}>0.
\tag{4}
\]
Likewise, \(q_j>0\) dominates the two other lower bounds in the cell
\(Q_{\{k,\ell\}}\), so
\[
q_{\{k,\ell\}}
=\int_{-1/200}^{1/200}
\left(1-\frac{\omega}{2}\right)\,d\omega
=\frac1{100}>0.
\tag{5}
\]

The three polygon areas are
\[
M:=|A_0|=\frac{2149}{365},\qquad
m:=|A_2|=\frac1{10},\qquad
|K|=\frac{585699}{146000}.
\]
Consequently,
\[
T=\frac{1459899}{146000},
\qquad
t=\frac{162211}{73000}.
\tag{6}
\]
Direct integration of the three lower caps of \(A_2\) gives
\[
v_k=v_\ell=v_j=\frac9{100}.
\tag{7}
\]
Indeed, the relevant chord intervals are respectively
\[
r\ge0,\qquad r\ge\omega,\qquad r\le8+\frac{\omega}{2},
\]
and their average \(r\)-length is \(9\).

The relevant chord intervals in \(K\) are
\[
r\ge\frac72,\qquad
r\ge\frac72+\omega,\qquad
r\le4+\frac{\omega}{2}.
\]
Each has average \(r\)-length
\[
\frac{1541}{365}.
\]
Since the \(\omega\)-height of \(K\) is \(101/200\),
\[
c_k=c_\ell=c_j
=\frac{101}{200}\frac{1541}{365}
=\frac{155641}{73000}.
\tag{8}
\]
Equations (6)-(8) give, for every \(i\),
\[
u_i+v_i+c_i
=0+\frac9{100}+\frac{155641}{73000}
=\frac{162211}{73000}
=t.
\tag{9}
\]

It remains essential to check the orientation of these crossings. For
\(|\eta|<41/730\), (2) makes every \(A_0\)-cap at threshold
\(L_i+\eta\) have area zero. The active \(A_2\)-chords are fully transverse,
and exact integration gives
\[
\mu_k(L_k+\eta)=t-\frac{\eta}{100},
\qquad
\mu_\ell(L_\ell+\eta)=t-\frac{\eta}{100},
\qquad
\mu_j(L_j+\eta)=t-\frac{\eta}{200}.
\tag{10}
\]
These are genuine one-sided chord profiles, not scalar cell data. In
particular, \(\mu_i(L_i+\eta)>t\) for small \(\eta<0\) and
\(\mu_i(L_i+\eta)<t\) for small \(\eta>0\).

There is also no earlier component of \(I_i\). If \(a\le L_i\), then the
\(A_0\)-cap is empty by (2), while monotonicity of lower caps gives
\[
\left|A_2\cap\{f_i\le-a\}\right|\ge v_i.
\]
Thus \(\mu_i(a)\ge c_i+v_i=t\) for every \(a\le L_i\). Equation (10) supplies
points of \(I_i\) immediately to the right of \(L_i\), proving
\[
L_i=\inf I_i
\tag{11}
\]
for all three directions. The canonical tails are also strict:
\[
c_i+m=t+\frac1{100}>t,\qquad
c_i+M=t+\left(M-\frac9{100}\right)>t.
\tag{12}
\]
At each lower crossing the ordered endpoint-cap state is therefore
\((\mathrm{empty},\mathrm{proper})\), exactly one of the admissible strict-tail
states in accepted fact `624b129b68e0fdee`.

The hypotheses of accepted facts `c472ceb412ca0594` and
`9922bcace1cdc113` now hold by (3), (5), and the midpoint equality. They give
\[
s_j=|D|>0,
\tag{13}
\]
\[
U\subseteq R_{\{j\}},\qquad
V\subseteq R_{\{k\}}\mathbin{\dot\cup}R_{\{\ell\}}
\mathbin{\dot\cup}R_{\{k,\ell\}},
\tag{14}
\]
and
\[
D\subseteq R_{\{j,k\}}\mathbin{\dot\cup}R_{\{j,\ell\}}
\mathbin{\dot\cup}R_N.
\tag{15}
\]
More precisely, \(U,V,D\) lie respectively in the disjoint bands
\[
f_j<-1,\qquad f_j>0,\qquad -1<f_j<0.
\tag{16}
\]
The exact weak-indexed, strict-unindexed convention and the nullity of all
cutting-line boundaries are those of accepted fact `0e51a47a9add571f`.

Finally, let \(p_J,q_H,r_G\) denote all exact cell areas. Summing the three
crossing equalities (9) and using \(3t=2T/3\) yields
\[
\sum_J |J|p_J+\sum_H|H|q_H+\sum_G|G|r_G
=\frac{2T}{3}.
\]
Subtracting twice the three area partitions from three times this identity
gives
\[
\sum_J(3|J|-2)p_J+
\sum_H(3|H|-2)q_H+
\sum_G(3|G|-2)r_G=0.
\tag{17}
\]
Accepted fact `0e51a47a9add571f` gives
\[
P_N=Q_\varnothing=R_\varnothing=\varnothing.
\]
Thus the only negative term in (17) is \(-2p_\varnothing\). All other
nonzero cell coefficients are \(1,4,7\) on singleton, doubleton, and
tripleton cells. If \(C_+\) is the total positive weighted cell charge, then
\[
C_+=2p_\varnothing.
\tag{18}
\]

The two displayed \(Q\)-cells belong to the \(A_2\)-partition. Within the
\(K\)-partition, the three subsets in (14)-(16) are pairwise disjoint.
Therefore their contribution to \(C_+\), together with the two \(Q\)-cell
contributions, is at least
\[
q_{\{j\}}+4q_{\{k,\ell\}}+|U|
+\left|V\cap\left(R_{\{k\}}\cup R_{\{\ell\}}\right)\right|
+4|V\cap R_{\{k,\ell\}}|+4s_j.
\tag{19}
\]
Here (15) gives the last term because every doubleton part of \(D\) has
coefficient \(4\), while its \(R_N\)-part has coefficient \(7\). Every
omitted charge is nonnegative. Combining (13), (18), and (19) proves
\[
q_{\{j\}}+4q_{\{k,\ell\}}+|U|
+\left|V\cap\left(R_{\{k\}}\cup R_{\{\ell\}}\right)\right|
+4|V\cap R_{\{k,\ell\}}|+3s_j
\le2p_\varnothing-s_j
<2p_\varnothing.
\]
This is the strict reverse of the registered target inequality.

The endpoint ratio is
\[
\frac mM=\frac{73}{4298}<\frac12.
\]
Hence the realization also lies outside the already excluded ratio range of
accepted fact `c473a1805ca54ac2`. It refutes the isolated target-side charge,
not the locked three-slice theorem.
