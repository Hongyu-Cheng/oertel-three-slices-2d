## Statement

Let
\[
\mathcal L=\{(x,y):x\le-|y|\},\qquad
\mathcal R=\{(x,y):x\ge|y|\},\qquad
\mathcal U=\{(x,y):y\ge|x|\},
\]
and take
\[
A_0=\{(x,y):|y|\le1344,\ -2021\le x\le-1-|y|\},
\]
\[
A_2=\operatorname{conv}\{(0,0),(2087,-12),(2087,12)\},
\qquad
K=[-1043,1043]\times[-678,678].
\]
Put
\[
B=\frac{A_0+A_2}{2},\qquad
Y=K\cap\mathcal U\cap\{x\ge1\},\qquad
G=\operatorname{conv}(B\cup Y),
\]
and let
\[
D(x,y)=(u,v)=(x+y,x-y),\qquad F=D(G),\qquad s=u+v.
\]
This is the integral triple of accepted fact
\(85353495158713c3\).  It satisfies all retained hypotheses of the
support-contact diagonal-tail placement subgoal.  In particular,
\[
M=3\,623\,424,\quad m=25\,044,\quad k=2\,828\,616,\quad
t=1\,439\,352,\quad \pi=1\,414\,308,
\]
\[
C=1\,415\,664,\qquad \delta=2021,\qquad
\theta=\left|A_2\cap\{x\ge2023\}\right|
=\frac{3\,156\,480}{2087},
\]
\[
A_0\subseteq\mathcal L,\quad A_2\subseteq\mathcal R,\quad
B\subseteq K,\quad M>k>m>0,\quad 0<t<M,
\]
\[
\min_{A_0}x=-\delta,\qquad
\min_{A_2}(x+y)=\min_{A_2}(x-y)=0,
\]
\[
|K\cap\{x+y\ge0\}|=|K\cap\{x-y\ge0\}|=\pi.
\]
Both diagonal contacts are genuine lower crossings,
\[
C+m-t=1356>0,\qquad
t-C-v_1=\frac{326\,508}{2087}>0,
\]
where \(v_1=m-\theta\), the positive forced tail obeys
\[
\frac{A_0+(A_2\cap\{x\ge2023\})}{2}
\subseteq B\cap\{x\ge1\},
\]
and both hull-excess budgets are bounded by
\[
103\,248<\pi-\frac m4=1\,408\,047.
\]
The two contact points in \(Q=D(A_2)\) are
\[
q_u=(0,\sigma)=(0,0),\qquad q_v=(\tau,0)=(0,0).

With \(P=-D(A_0)\), the joint contact copy is \(E=-P/2\).
Moreover, \(\alpha=\beta=1721\), the contact-box conclusion
\(M\le2\alpha\beta\) holds, and
\[
7C+2\pi-2k-4\alpha\beta=-4\,766\,332<0.
\]

The two exact transformed cell areas are
\[
\left|F\cap\{s\le2\}\right|
=\frac{5\,699\,312\,934}{2087},
\qquad
\left|F\cap\{v\le0\}\right|
=\frac{5\,732\,444\,706}{2099}.

Consequently
\[
5\left|F\cap\{s\le2\}\right|
-4\left|F\cap\{v\le0\}\right|

and

For the exact residual decomposition, define, up to their null
boundary lines,

A_-=F\cap\{s\le2,\ v\le0\},\qquad
A_+=F\cap\{s\le2,\ v\ge0\},

## Proof

Accepted fact \(85353495158713c3\) verifies the displayed polygons,
all mass and crossing identities, the two strict absolute-level
inequalities, positive forced tail and midpoint containment, both
hull-excess budgets, the contact points, and the strict scalar sign.
Accepted fact \(e8c7d289faa42849\) supplies the contact-box
conclusion from these contacts and midpoint inclusion.  Accepted fact
\(81ddf2d6ab5b6a17\) supplies the joint-copy containment and exact
contact-tail Jacobian, while accepted fact \(d50fa08d9577c890\)
supplies the weighted-cell reduction used below.

The cyclic vertices of \(G\), already verified in accepted fact
\(85353495158713c3\), are
\[
\begin{aligned}
&\left(-\frac{2021}{2},-672\right),(33,-678),(371,-678),
(1043,-6),\\
&(1043,6),(678,678),(1,678),
\left(-\frac{2021}{2},672\right).
\end{aligned}
\]
Applying \(D\) gives the following cyclic vertices of \(F\):
\[
\begin{aligned}
V_0&=\left(-\frac{3365}{2},-\frac{677}{2}\right),&
V_1&=(-645,711),\\
V_2&=(-307,1049),&
V_3&=(1037,1049),\\
V_4&=(1049,1037),&
V_5&=(1356,0),\\
V_6&=(679,-677),&
V_7&=\left(-\frac{677}{2},-\frac{3365}{2}\right).
\end{aligned}
\]
The line \(s=2\) meets the edge \([V_0,V_1]\) at
\[
I=\left(-\frac{1\,412\,515}{2087},
\frac{1\,416\,689}{2087}\right),
\]
because the interpolation parameter from \(V_0\) to \(V_1\) is
\(2023/2087\).  Its other relevant contact with the clipped polygon
is \(V_6=(679,-677)\), which already has \(s=2\).  Therefore
\[
F\cap\{s\le2\}
=\operatorname{conv}\{V_0,I,V_6,V_7\}.
\]
The signed doubled shoelace sum in this cyclic order is
\[
-\frac{11\,398\,625\,868}{2087},
\]
so
\[
\left|F\cap\{s\le2\}\right|
=\frac{5\,699\,312\,934}{2087}.
\]

The line \(v=0\) meets \([V_0,V_1]\) at
\[
J=\left(-\frac{2\,829\,180}{2099},0\right),
\]
because the interpolation parameter is \(677/2099\).  Hence
\[
F\cap\{v\le0\}
=\operatorname{conv}\{V_0,J,V_5,V_6,V_7\}.
\]
Its signed doubled shoelace sum is
\[
-\frac{11\,464\,889\,412}{2099},
\]
which gives
\[
\left|F\cap\{v\le0\}\right|
=\frac{5\,732\,444\,706}{2099}.
\]
Putting the two areas over the common denominator
\(2087\cdot2099=4\,380\,613\) gives
\[
5|F\cap\{s\le2\}|
=\frac{59\,814\,289\,242\,330}{4\,380\,613},
\]
\[
4|F\cap\{v\le0\}|
=\frac{47\,854\,448\,405\,688}{4\,380\,613}.
\]
Their difference is the asserted positive transformed slack.

The determinant of \(D\) has absolute value \(2\).  Also,
\[
x\le1\Longleftrightarrow s\le2,
\qquad
\mathcal L\cup\mathcal U
=D^{-1}(\{v\le0\}).
\]
It follows directly from the definition
\[
\Phi(G)=5|G\cap\{x\le1\}|
-4|G\cap(\mathcal L\cup\mathcal U)|
\]
that \(\Phi(G)\) is one half of the transformed slack, proving its
displayed exact value and sign.

It remains to audit what the contact-copy bound missed.  Since
\(G\subseteq K\), \(Y\subseteq G\), and, for \(s\ge2\), the condition
\(v\le0\) is exactly \(x\ge1\) and \(y\ge x=|x|\), one has
\[
Y'=D(Y)=\operatorname{conv}\{(2,0),(679,-677),(1356,0)\}.
\]
Thus \(|Y'|=458\,329\).  The two remaining left cells have cyclic
descriptions
\[
A_-=\operatorname{conv}\{V_0,J,(2,0),V_6,V_7\},
\]
\[
A_+=\operatorname{conv}\{J,I,(2,0)\}.
\]
Subtracting \(Y'\) from \(F\cap\{v\le0\}\) gives
\[
|A_-|
=\frac{5\,732\,444\,706}{2099}-458\,329
=\frac{4\,770\,412\,135}{2099}.
\]
The second polygon is a triangle with horizontal base from \(J\) to
\((2,0)\) and height \(1\,416\,689/2087\).  Therefore
\[
|A_+|
=\frac12\left(2+\frac{2\,829\,180}{2099}\right)
\frac{1\,416\,689}{2087}
=\frac{2\,007\,007\,722\,721}{4\,380\,613}.
\]

Finally, accepted fact \(85353495158713c3\) gives
\[
P=\operatorname{conv}\{(1,1),(2689,1),(3365,677),
(677,3365),(1,2689)\},
\]
\[
|P|=7\,246\,848,\qquad \sigma=\tau=0,\qquad
T_\tau=|P|,\qquad U_\sigma=0.
\]
The two contact copies coincide with \(E=-P/2\).  Since every point
of \(P\) has both coordinates at least \(1\), every point of \(E\)
has \(s\le-1\) and \(v\le-1/2\).  Thus \(E\subseteq A_-\), and the
homothety gives
\[
|E|=\frac{|P|}{4}=1\,811\,712.
\]
Consequently
\[
|A_-\setminus E|
=\frac{4\,770\,412\,135}{2099}-1\,811\,712
=\frac{967\,628\,647}{2099}.
\]
The weighted-cell identity from accepted fact
\(d50fa08d9577c890\) is, in the present notation,
\[
5|F\cap\{s\le2\}|-4|F\cap\{v\le0\}|
=|A_-|+5|A_+|-4|Y'|.
\]
Splitting \(A_-=E\mathbin{\dot\cup}(A_-\setminus E)\) yields the
claimed exact residual decomposition.  In particular, the failed
contact-only inequality loses \(21\,604\), while the two omitted
regions contribute
\[
\frac{967\,628\,647}{2099}
+5\frac{2\,007\,007\,722\,721}{4\,380\,613},
\]
which proves the positive final slack.
