## Statement

Use the normalized systems of accepted fact `b046e3066136e781`, the word automaton of accepted fact `46afc7eb815ffc5b`, the surviving interior/interior row of accepted fact `2833a8e581bcc380`, and the previous \(P\)-word deletion of accepted fact `e59a94f8905b800f`.

Thus
\[
\ell_R<0<1<u_R,\qquad \sigma_R\in\mathcal D,
\]
and the current label vectors satisfy
\[
\sigma_P\in\mathcal A^\circ,\qquad
\sigma_Q\in\mathcal C,\qquad
\sigma_R\in\mathcal D,
\]
where
\[
\mathcal A^\circ
=\{\sigma_P\in\mathcal A:
\sigma_{P2}\ne\mathrm P,\ \sigma_{P4}\ne\mathrm P\}.
\]

For every \(\eta\in\{0,1,2\}\), the system
\(\mathcal S_{\eta,\mathrm I,\mathrm I,\sigma}\) is infeasible if
\[
\sigma_{R2}=\mathrm P
\quad\text{or}\quad
\sigma_{R4}=\mathrm P.
\]
For \(\eta\in\{0,1\}\), it is also infeasible if
\[
\sigma_{R1}=\mathrm N
\quad\text{or}\quad
\sigma_{R3}=\mathrm N.
\]
Among the mixed-label, lexicographically symmetry-reduced \(140070\) current systems, exactly \(33624\) satisfy this filter.

## Proof

First derive the required strict area comparisons from accepted fact `b046e3066136e781`. Since \(9t=2(1+m+k)\), the inequality \(t<k\) is equivalent to
\[
2(1+m)<7k. \tag{1}
\]

If \(\eta=0\), then \(m=q^2\le9/25\) and \(k>1-m\). Hence
\[
7k>7(1-m)>2(1+m),
\]
because \(5>9m\). If \(\eta=1\) or \(2\), then \(q>3/5\) and
\[
2k>3q^2+2q-1.
\]
Consequently,
\[
7k>\frac72(3q^2+2q-1)>2(1+q^2)=2(1+m),
\]
where the second strict inequality is equivalent to
\[
17q^2+14q-11>0,
\]
which already holds at \(q=3/5\) and is increasing for \(q>0\). Thus
\[
t<k \tag{2}
\]
in all three branches. Accepted fact `e59a94f8905b800f` similarly gives \(t<1\), which produced the current set \(\mathcal A^\circ\).

For the empty-cap implications, one also has
\[
t>m\qquad(\eta=0,1). \tag{3}
\]
Indeed,
\[
9(t-m)=2+2k-7m.
\]
For \(\eta=0\),
\[
2+2k-7m>4-9m\ge4-\frac{81}{25}>0.
\]
For \(\eta=1\),
\[
2+2k-7m>1+2q-4q^2.
\]
Since \(2q^2\le1\) and \(q>3/5\),
\[
1+2q-4q^2\ge2q-1>0.
\]
This proves (3).

Now use the individual endpoint pairs from accepted fact `b046e3066136e781`.

If \(\sigma_{R2}=\mathrm P\), then
\[
-\upsilon_{R0}\ge0,\qquad -\upsilon_{R1}\ge0.
\]
Since \(U_R\) is affine, \(U_R(x)\le0\) throughout the support of \(R\). Therefore
\[
R\subseteq\{y\le0\},\qquad \alpha_R=k.
\]
The crossing equality
\[
\alpha_P+\alpha_Q+\alpha_R=t
\]
and nonnegativity of all caps give \(t\ge k\), contradicting (2).

If \(\sigma_{R4}=\mathrm P\), the affine function \(L_R(x)+x\) is nonnegative at both endpoints and hence throughout the support. Thus
\[
R\subseteq\{x+y\ge0\},\qquad \beta_R=k.
\]
The second crossing equality gives \(t\ge k\), again contradicting (2).

If \(\sigma_{R1}=\mathrm N\), then
\[
-\lambda_{R0}\le0,\qquad -\lambda_{R1}\le0.
\]
Thus \(L_R(x)\ge0\) throughout the support and
\[
\alpha_R=0.
\]
The tail-cap condition of accepted fact `b046e3066136e781`,
\[
\alpha_R+m\ge t,
\]
then gives \(m\ge t\), contradicting (3) when \(\eta=0\) or \(1\).

Likewise, \(\sigma_{R3}=\mathrm N\) makes \(U_R(x)+x\le0\) throughout the support, so
\[
\beta_R=0.
\]
The condition \(\beta_R+m\ge t\) again contradicts (3). This proves every claimed infeasibility.

It remains to count only within the \(140070\) systems left by accepted fact `e59a94f8905b800f`. From the table of accepted fact `46afc7eb815ffc5b`, \(\mathcal A^\circ\) has
\[
48\text{ words},\qquad 4\text{ \(\{\mathrm N,\mathrm P\}\)-only words},
\]
while \(\mathcal C\) has
\[
54\text{ words},\qquad 6\text{ \(\{\mathrm N,\mathrm P\}\)-only words}.
\]

Write \(X=\mathrm C_+\) and \(Y=\mathrm C_-\). The words of \(\mathcal D\) satisfying
\[
\sigma_{R2}=\mathrm P
\quad\text{or}\quad
\sigma_{R4}=\mathrm P
\]
are exactly
\[
NNPP,\quad PPNN,\quad PPXN,\quad PPXX,\quad XNPP,\quad XXPP. \tag{4}
\]
This set has six words, two \(\{\mathrm N,\mathrm P\}\)-only words, and no \(\pi\)-fixed word. It is \(\pi\)-invariant. Thus for \(\eta=2\) the number of deleted mixed-label orbits is
\[
\frac{48\cdot54\cdot6-4\cdot6\cdot2}{2}
=\frac{15552-48}{2}
=7752. \tag{5}
\]

The words of \(\mathcal D\) satisfying
\[
\sigma_{R1}=\mathrm N
\quad\text{or}\quad
\sigma_{R3}=\mathrm N
\]
are exactly
\[
NNPP,\quad NNPX,\quad NNXX,\quad
PPNN,\quad PXNN,\quad XXNN. \tag{6}
\]
The union of (4) and (6) is
\[
\{NNPP,NNPX,NNXX,PPNN,PPXN,PPXX,
PXNN,XNPP,XXNN,XXPP\}. \tag{7}
\]
It has ten words, exactly two \(\{\mathrm N,\mathrm P\}\)-only words, and no \(\pi\)-fixed word. It is again \(\pi\)-invariant. Therefore, for each of \(\eta=0,1\), the number of deleted mixed-label orbits is
\[
\frac{48\cdot54\cdot10-4\cdot6\cdot2}{2}
=\frac{25920-48}{2}
=12936. \tag{8}
\]

Combining (5) and (8), the exact new deletion count is
\[
2\cdot12936+7752=33624.
\]
Hence the number of surviving interior/interior systems becomes
\[
140070-33624=106446.
\]
