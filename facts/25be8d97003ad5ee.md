## Statement

Let \(N=\{1,2,3\}\). Assign every unlisted cell mass zero and, for distinct \(i,j\in N\), set
\[
p_\varnothing=\frac{35}{4},\qquad p_{\{i\}}=\frac15,
\]
\[
q_N=\frac25,\qquad q_{N\setminus\{i\}}=\frac15,
\]
\[
r_{\{i\}}=\frac65,\qquad r_{\{i,j\}}=\frac12,\qquad r_N=\frac3{10}.
\]
Then
\[
M=\frac{187}{20},\qquad m=1,\qquad k=\frac{27}{5}.
\]
With the notation of accepted facts `91cb5bfc71da49ac`, `624b129b68e0fdee`, `8234d1112ec2f031`, `2bb72698f0248a65`, `93f1cefaa4795a25`, `ce343e749df349fe`, and `0819231d0af83197`, this nonnegative all-double scalar table satisfies every listed crossing, tail, product, square-root, overlap, support, and defect condition, including exact equality in the all-double excess. This is only a scalar calibration and is not asserted to have a common-plane geometric realization.

## Proof

The totals are
\[
\frac{35}{4}+3\cdot\frac15=\frac{187}{20},
\qquad
\frac25+3\cdot\frac15=1,
\]
and
\[
3\cdot\frac65+3\cdot\frac12+\frac3{10}
=\frac{27}{5}.
\]
All four prescribed \(Q\)-cells are positive, so this is exactly the all-double support of accepted fact `91cb5bfc71da49ac`.

For every \(i\),
\[
u_i=\frac15,\qquad
v_i=\frac25+2\cdot\frac15=\frac45,
\]
and
\[
c_i=\frac65+2\cdot\frac12+\frac3{10}=\frac52.
\]
Moreover,
\[
h=\frac{2(M+m+k)}9
=\frac{2}{9}\left(\frac{187}{20}+1+\frac{27}{5}\right)
=\frac72.
\]
Hence all three crossings hold:
\[
u_i+v_i+c_i
=\frac15+\frac45+\frac52
=\frac72=h.
\]
The canonical tails from accepted fact `624b129b68e0fdee` hold at equality:
\[
c_i+m=\frac52+1=\frac72=h.
\]
Also \(0<u_i<M\), \(0<v_i<m\), and \(h<M\), so this is the proper-proper equality-tail state.

Put
\[
U=\sum_i u_i=\frac35,\qquad
E_R=r_{12}+r_{13}+r_{23}+2r_N
=\frac32+\frac35=\frac{21}{10}.
\]
The defect identity of accepted fact `0819231d0af83197` holds exactly:
\[
2M+2m-3\sum_i(u_i+v_i)-k
=\frac{63}{10}
=3E_R.
\]
Its all-double specialization, which is the equality forced in accepted fact `91cb5bfc71da49ac`, is
\[
U+m+q_N+E_R
=\frac35+1+\frac25+\frac{21}{10}
=\frac{41}{10}
=\frac{2M-m-k}{3}.
\]

For the source \(Q\)-caps, put \(x_i=q_{N\setminus\{i\}}=1/5\). The two inequalities of accepted fact `8234d1112ec2f031` are strict:
\[
q_N^2m=\frac4{25}>\frac4{125}=4x_1x_2x_3,
\]
and
\[
\sum_i\sqrt{\frac{x_i}{m}}
=\frac3{\sqrt5}
<\frac32.
\]

For the overlap inequality of accepted fact `2bb72698f0248a65`,
\[
\frac14\sum_i(\sqrt{p_\varnothing}+\sqrt{x_i})^2
=\frac34\left(\frac{\sqrt{35}}2+\frac1{\sqrt5}\right)^2
=\frac{537+60\sqrt7}{80}.
\]
Its right-hand side is
\[
k-r_N+\sum_i r_i
=\frac{27}{5}-\frac3{10}+\frac{18}{5}
=\frac{87}{10}
=\frac{696}{80}.
\]
Since
\[
(60\sqrt7)^2=25200<25281=159^2,
\]
one has \(537+60\sqrt7<696\), proving the overlap inequality strictly.

All three pair masses equal \(1/2>0\), while
\[
t=r_N=\frac3{10}>0,
\]
so the middle-support implication of accepted fact `93f1cefaa4795a25` holds.

Finally, for every \(i\), the corrected quantity of accepted fact `ce343e749df349fe` is in its positive branch:
\[
e_i
=
\left(
\frac65-\frac{(1/2)(1/2)}{3/10}
\right)_+
=
\frac65-\frac56
=
\frac{11}{30}>0.
\]
Thus
\[
k-\sum_i e_i
=\frac{27}{5}-\frac{11}{10}
=\frac{43}{10},
\]
and the corrected product inequality is strict:
\[
\left(k-\sum_i e_i\right)^2k
=\frac{49923}{500}
>
\frac{1331}{6750}
=
4e_1e_2e_3.
\]
The corrected square-root inequality is also strict because
\[
\frac{11}{30}<\frac{27}{20},
\]
which is exactly the squared comparison giving
\[
3\sqrt{\frac{11}{30}}
<
\frac32\sqrt{\frac{27}{5}}.
\]
Therefore every required scalar equality and inequality is satisfied.
