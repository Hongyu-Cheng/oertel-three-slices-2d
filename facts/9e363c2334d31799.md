## Statement

Let \(s,a,b,\delta>0\), and let \(P\) be a convex polygon contained in
\(\{x+y\leq\delta\}\) whose boundary contains the support segment joining
\[
p_0=(-a,\delta+a)
\quad\hbox{and}\quad
p_1=(\delta+b,-b).
\]
If
\[
|P|=16s,\qquad
|P\cap\{x\leq0\}|=\frac{s}{8},\qquad
|P\cap\{y\leq0\}|=\frac{s}{5},
\tag{1}
\]
then
\[
\delta>a+b.
\tag{2}
\]
Consequently no strictly convex rational hexagon in the declared sign cell
satisfies (1) together with \(\delta<a+b\).

## Proof

Put
\[
L=\delta+a+b
\quad\hbox{and}\quad
h=\delta-x-y.
\tag{3}
\]
The affine map
\[
(x,y)\longmapsto(\xi,h)=(x+a,\delta-x-y)
\tag{4}
\]
has determinant \(-1\), so it preserves area.  It sends \(p_0p_1\) to
the segment joining \((0,0)\) and \((L,0)\).  Since
\(P\subseteq\{x+y\leq\delta\}\), every point of its image has \(h\geq0\).
For \(0\leq\xi\leq L\), the vertical section of the image is therefore
\[
\{(\xi,h):0\leq h\leq U(\xi)\}
\tag{5}
\]
for a nonnegative concave function \(U\).  Concavity follows from
convexity of the image, and the lower endpoint in (5) is supplied by the
support segment.

The portion in (5) with \(0\leq\xi\leq a\) is contained in the actual
cap \(P\cap\{x\leq0\}\).  Hence
\[
A_x:=\int_0^a U(t)\,dt\leq\frac{s}{8}.
\tag{6}
\]
Fix \(\tau\in[a,L]\).  For \(0\leq t\leq a\), concavity and
nonnegativity give
\[
U(t)\geq
\left(1-\frac{t}{\tau}\right)U(0)
+\frac{t}{\tau}U(\tau)
\geq\frac{t}{\tau}U(\tau).
\tag{7}
\]
Integrating (7) and using (6) yields
\[
U(\tau)\leq\frac{2A_x\tau}{a^2}
\leq\frac{s\tau}{4a^2}.
\tag{8}
\]

The same argument with the area-preserving coordinates
\[
(\eta,h)=(y+b,\delta-x-y)
\tag{9}
\]
uses the same support segment, now with \(0\leq\eta\leq L\).
If \(V(\eta)\) is the corresponding nonnegative concave upper profile,
the actual cap \(P\cap\{y\leq0\}\) gives
\[
\int_0^b V(t)\,dt\leq\frac{s}{5}.
\tag{10}
\]
Consequently, for every \(\eta\in[b,L]\),
\[
V(\eta)\leq\frac{2s\eta}{5b^2}.
\tag{11}
\]

Now let
\[
K=P\cap\{x\geq0,\ y\geq0\}.
\tag{12}
\]
Apart from cutting lines of area zero, every point of \(P\setminus K\)
lies in one of the two caps in (1).  Thus
\[
|K|
\geq16s-\frac{s}{8}-\frac{s}{5}
=\frac{627}{40}s.
\tag{13}
\]
Under the area-preserving map
\[
(x,y)\longmapsto(x,h)=(x,\delta-x-y),
\tag{14}
\]
the set \(K\) has vertical sections
\[
\{(x,h):0\leq h\leq F(x)\},
\qquad 0\leq x\leq\delta,
\tag{15}
\]
for a nonnegative concave function \(F\).  Indeed, \(x,y,h\geq0\)
implies \(0\leq x\leq\delta\), while the part of \(p_0p_1\) in the first
quadrant supplies \((x,0)\) for every \(x\in[0,\delta]\).

For a point \((x,h)\) in (15), equation (8), with
\(\tau=a+x\), gives
\[
h\leq\frac{s(a+x)}{4a^2}.
\tag{16}
\]
The same point has \(y=\delta-x-h\geq0\).  Equation (11), with
\(\eta=b+y\), therefore gives
\[
h\leq\frac{2s(b+\delta-x-h)}{5b^2}.
\tag{17}
\]
Solving (17) for \(h\), and then taking the top endpoint \(h=F(x)\) in
(16)-(17), yields
\[
F(x)\leq
\min\left\{
\alpha(x+a),\,
\beta(\delta+b-x)
\right\},
\tag{18}
\]
where
\[
\alpha=\frac{s}{4a^2},
\qquad
\beta=\frac{2s}{5b^2+2s}.
\tag{19}
\]

Both affine functions in (18) are nonnegative on
\([-a,\delta+b]\).  Enlarging the integration interval in (15) to this
full tent, using (13), and evaluating at the unique intersection of the
two lines gives
\[
\begin{aligned}
\frac{627}{40}s
&\leq |K|
=\int_0^\delta F(x)\,dx\\
&\leq
\int_{-a}^{\delta+b}
\min\{\alpha(x+a),\beta(\delta+b-x)\}\,dx\\
&=\frac{\alpha\beta L^2}{2(\alpha+\beta)}
=\frac{sL^2}{8a^2+5b^2+2s}.
\end{aligned}
\tag{20}
\]
After cancelling \(s>0\), equation (20) implies
\[
L^2\geq
\frac{627}{40}\left(8a^2+5b^2+2s\right).
\tag{21}
\]
The right side is strictly larger than \(4(a+b)^2\).  Indeed,
\[
\frac{627}{40}\left(8a^2+5b^2+2s\right)
>8a^2+8b^2
\geq4(a+b)^2.
\tag{22}
\]
Since \(L,a,b>0\), equations (21)-(22) give
\[
L>2(a+b).
\tag{23}
\]
Finally \(L=\delta+a+b\), so (23) is exactly
\(\delta>a+b\).  This proves (2) and contradicts the required strict
inequality \(\delta<a+b\).  No vertex order beyond the stated support
edge was used, so the proof covers the entire declared hexagon sign cell.
