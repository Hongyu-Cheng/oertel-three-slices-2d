## Statement

Use the notation of `18e8f69f5a782c16`.  There is an exact positive rational scalar datum satisfying the three scalar cell identities and all four full-chord perspective inequalities of `fa0e57b225178b3b`, the hypotheses and reverse \(R_{jk}\) inequality of `06afbd5c9e94d1a2`, and the reverse \(R_{k\ell}\) inequality of `ed46def84052c5bf`, on the branch
\[
\gamma=\tau=0,\qquad \beta<A<B.
\]
This is only a scalar feasible point; no endpoint-polygon realization is asserted.

## Proof

Set
\[
\Delta=\delta=1,\qquad
J=K=\frac14,\qquad H=\frac12,
\]
\[

\]
Thus
\[
A=\alpha+J=\frac34,\qquad
B=\beta+H=1,\qquad G=\gamma+K=\frac14,\qquad S=3.
\]
For the \(A_2\) chords set
\[
J_2=\frac14,\qquad H_2=1,\qquad K_2=\frac12,
\]
\[
u=1,\qquad v=3,\qquad w_0=1.
\]
Then
\[
\theta=S-u-H_2=1,\qquad
\psi=S-w_0-K_2=\frac32.
\]
All chord lengths, \(\Delta,\delta,A,B,G,S,\theta,\psi,u,v,w_0\) are positive, the boxes hold, and the literal incidences
\[
\gamma=0\Longleftrightarrow\tau=0,\qquad
\alpha=0\Longleftrightarrow\beta=0,

The \(A_0\) perspective rows are
\[

\le\frac38

and
\[

\]
Writing
\[

\]
the two \(A_2\) perspective rows are

\[

The literal piecewise reverse inequality from `06afbd5c9e94d1a2` is the union of
\[

\]
\[

\]
The present point lies strictly in the second branch.  It also has
\[

\]

Choose the twelve positive cell areas
