## Statement

Use the notation and constraints of accepted fact `eef70ce3abe7606e`.
Suppose at least two of the three cyclic endpoint pairs vanish:
\[
(x_{12},x_{21}),\qquad
(x_{23},x_{32}),\qquad
(x_{31},x_{13}).
\]
Then
\[
D\geq \frac{2(3-\sqrt5)}7.
\tag{1}
\]
In particular,
\[
D>\frac4{169},
\]
so no such stratum can satisfy the necessary scale condition
\(D\leq4/169\).

## Proof

If all three endpoint pairs vanish, formula (4) of accepted fact
`eef70ce3abe7606e` gives \(D=1\), so assume exactly two pairs vanish.
After a cyclic relabeling,
\[
x_{23}=x_{32}=x_{31}=x_{13}=0.
\tag{2}
\]
Put
\[
a=x_{12},\qquad b=x_{21},\qquad
\ell=1-a,\qquad m=1-b.
\tag{3}
\]
The remaining pair is positive by condition (2a) of the accepted normal
form, and condition (2) there gives
\[
0<\ell,m<1.
\]
Writing \(\rho=\rho_{12}\), formula (4) becomes
\[
D=1-(1-\rho)ab
\geq1-ab
=\ell+m-\ell m.
\tag{4}
\]
The three cap heights from formula (5) of the accepted normal form are
\[
h_1=\frac{D}{2\ell},\qquad
h_2=\frac{D}{2m},\qquad
h_3=\frac D2.
\tag{5}
\]

Write the three cap vertices in barycentric coordinates as
\[
u_1=(-h_1,A,B),\qquad
u_2=(C,-h_2,E),\qquad
u_3=(F,G,-h_3).
\tag{6}
\]
All six letters in (6) are positive, and
\[
A+B=1+h_1,\qquad
C+E=1+h_2,\qquad
F+G=1+h_3.
\tag{7}
\]

The coincidences in (2) say
\[
p_{23}=p_{32}=e_1,\qquad
p_{31}=p_{13}=e_2.
\tag{8}
\]
Thus \(e_1,e_2,u_1,u_2\) all belong to the reduced convex polygon \(K\).
Its coordinate area is \(5D/4\) by the accepted normal form.  The triangles
\(\operatorname{conv}\{e_1,e_2,u_1\}\) and
\(\operatorname{conv}\{e_1,e_2,u_2\}\) have areas \(B/2\) and \(E/2\),
respectively.  Convexity therefore gives
\[
B\leq\frac{5D}{2},\qquad
E\leq\frac{5D}{2}.
\tag{9}
\]

We next use only pairwise disjointness of the caps.  The segment
\([u_2,u_3]\) lies in \(K\).  Along this segment, the two coordinates
\(\alpha_2,\alpha_3\) have endpoint values
\[
(-h_2,E),\qquad (G,-h_3).
\]
If their zeroes occurred in the wrong order, an open subsegment would have
both coordinates negative, and a relative neighborhood in the
two-dimensional polygon would give a positive-area overlap of caps \(2\)
and \(3\).  Hence
\[
EG\geq h_2h_3.
\tag{10}
\]
The same argument on \([u_3,u_1]\) gives
\[
FB\geq h_3h_1.
\tag{11}
\]

Let \(U=\operatorname{conv}\{u_1,u_2,u_3\}\).  The determinant of the three
barycentric columns in (6) is twice the coordinate area of \(U\):
\[
\begin{aligned}
2|U|
={}&h_1GE+h_3AC+h_2FB+CGB+FAE-h_1h_2h_3.
\end{aligned}
\tag{12}
\]
Put \(H=h_1h_2h_3\).  Equations (10)-(11) give
\[
h_1GE\geq H,\qquad h_2FB\geq H.
\]
Moreover, AM--GM and (10)-(11) give
\[
CGB+FAE
\geq2\sqrt{AC\cdot EG\cdot FB}
\geq2h_3\sqrt{AC\,h_1h_2}.
\]
Substitution in (12) yields
\[
2|U|
\geq
h_3\left(\sqrt{AC}+\sqrt{h_1h_2}\right)^2.
\tag{13}
\]
Since \(U\subseteq K\), equations (13) and \(|K|=5D/4\), together with
\(h_3=D/2\), imply
\[
\sqrt{AC}+\sqrt{h_1h_2}\leq\sqrt5.
\tag{14}
\]

We now derive the incompatible lower bound.  If \(D\geq2/5\), then (1)
already holds, so assume \(D<2/5\) and put
\[
q=1-\frac{5D}{2}>0,\qquad
P=\sqrt{h_1h_2}.
\]
Equations (7) and (9) give
\[
A\geq q+h_1,\qquad C\geq q+h_2.
\]
Since \(h_1+h_2\geq2P\),
\[
AC
\geq(q+h_1)(q+h_2)
\geq(q+P)^2,
\]
and therefore
\[
\sqrt{AC}+P\geq q+2P.
\tag{15}
\]

It remains to bound \(P\).  Equation (4) gives
\[
(1-\ell)(1-m)\geq1-D.
\]
Using AM--GM,
\[
\ell m
=1-\bigl((1-\ell)+(1-m)\bigr)+(1-\ell)(1-m)
\leq
\left(1-\sqrt{(1-\ell)(1-m)}\right)^2
\leq
\left(1-\sqrt{1-D}\right)^2.
\]
Consequently
\[
P
=\frac{D}{2\sqrt{\ell m}}
\geq
\frac{D}{2(1-\sqrt{1-D})}
=\frac{1+\sqrt{1-D}}2.
\tag{16}
\]
Combining (15)-(16) and using
\(\sqrt{1-D}\geq1-D\) gives
\[
\sqrt{AC}+P
\geq
2+\sqrt{1-D}-\frac{5D}{2}
\geq
3-\frac{7D}{2}.
\tag{17}
\]
Equations (14) and (17) now imply
\[
\sqrt5\geq3-\frac{7D}{2},
\]
which is exactly (1).

Finally, \(\sqrt5<5/2\), so
\[
\frac{2(3-\sqrt5)}7>\frac17>\frac4{169}.
\]
This excludes all three cyclic exactly-two-pair strata.  Together with the
all-three-pair case \(D=1\), it exhausts every stratum with at least two
coincident endpoint pairs.  The next smallest unresolved stratum has
exactly one pair \(x_{ij}=x_{ji}=0\); no claim is made for it or for the
fully positive endpoint stratum.
