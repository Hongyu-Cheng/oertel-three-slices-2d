## Statement

Let \(N=\{1,2,3\}\). Assume the affine sign-plane hypotheses and exact-cell convention of accepted fact `0e51a47a9add571f`: the nonconstant affine functions \(f_i\) have pairwise nonparallel linear parts,
\[
f_1+f_2+f_3=-1,\qquad L_1+L_2+L_3<-1,
\]
and \(A_0,A_2,K\) are nonempty two-dimensional convex polytopes with
\[
(A_0+A_2)/2\subseteq K.
\]
For \(J,H,G\subseteq N\), let \(P_J,Q_H,R_G\) be the corresponding exact sign cells and write
\[
p_J=|P_J|,\qquad q_H=|Q_H|,\qquad r_G=|R_G|,
\]
\[
M=|A_0|,\qquad m=|A_2|,\qquad k=|K|>0,
\]
\[
u_i=\sum_{J\ni i}p_J,\qquad
v_i=\sum_{H\ni i}q_H,\qquad
c_i=\sum_{G\ni i}r_G.
\]
Assume
\[
u_i+v_i+c_i=\frac{2(M+m+k)}9
\qquad(i=1,2,3).
\]
Put
\[
E_R=r_{12}+r_{13}+r_{23}+2r_{123}.
\]
Then
\[
2M+2m-3\sum_{i=1}^3(u_i+v_i)-k=3E_R,
\tag{1}
\]
and hence the sharp cap-sum band is
\[
\frac{2M+2m-7k}{3}
\le
\sum_{i=1}^3(u_i+v_i)
\le
\frac{2M+2m-k}{3}.
\tag{2}
\]
Equality on the right holds exactly when
\[
r_{12}=r_{13}=r_{23}=r_{123}=0,
\tag{3}
\]
while equality on the left holds exactly when \(r_{123}=k\).

Since \(q_0=0\), define
\[
E_Q=q_{12}+q_{13}+q_{23}+2q_{123}.
\]
The exact \(P/Q/R\)-cell form of (1) is
\[
p_1+p_2+p_3+2(p_{12}+p_{13}+p_{23})
+q_{12}+q_{13}+q_{23}+2q_{123}
+r_{12}+r_{13}+r_{23}+2r_{123}
=
\frac{2M-m-k}{3}.
\tag{4}
\]

For the two canonical supports classified in accepted fact `91cb5bfc71da49ac`, (4) specializes as follows.

If
\[
\mathcal S_Q=\{123,j,k,jk\},
\]
then
\[
\frac{2M-m-7k}{3}
\le
\sum_J|J|p_J+q_{jk}+2q_{123}
\le
\frac{2M-m-k}{3},
\tag{5}
\]
with exact defect
\[
\frac{2M-m-k}{3}
-\left(\sum_J|J|p_J+q_{jk}+2q_{123}\right)
=E_R.
\tag{6}
\]
In particular,
\[
k\le
2M-m-3\sum_J|J|p_J-3q_{jk}-6q_{123}
<2M-m.
\tag{7}
\]

If
\[
\mathcal S_Q=\{123,12,13,23\},
\]
then
\[
\frac{2M-m-7k}{3}
\le
\sum_J|J|p_J+m+q_{123}
\le
\frac{2M-m-k}{3},
\tag{8}
\]
with exact defect
\[
\frac{2M-m-k}{3}
-\left(\sum_J|J|p_J+m+q_{123}\right)
=E_R.
\tag{9}
\]
Consequently,
\[
k\le
2M-4m-3\sum_J|J|p_J-3q_{123}
<2M-4m.
\tag{10}
\]
Thus the all-double branch is impossible whenever \(M\le2m\). The single-double branch receives the global necessary restriction (5)-(7), but sign multiplicity alone gives no support-wide contradiction beyond it.

## Proof

Because \(f_1+f_2+f_3=-1\), every point of \(K\) belongs to exactly one \(R_G\) with \(G\ne0\). Its multiplicity among the three lower caps is \(|G|\). Therefore
\[
\sum_{i=1}^3c_i
=
\sum_{G\ne0}|G|r_G
=
k+r_{12}+r_{13}+r_{23}+2r_{123}
=
k+E_R.
\tag{11}
\]
Summing the three crossing equalities and substituting (11) gives
\[
\sum_i(u_i+v_i)+k+E_R
=
\frac{2(M+m+k)}3,
\]
which is equivalent to (1).

Moreover,
\[
0\le E_R\le2k,
\]
because
\[
2k-E_R
=
2(r_1+r_2+r_3)+(r_{12}+r_{13}+r_{23})\ge0.
\]
Substitution in (1) proves (2). Nonnegativity of the cell masses gives the right equality condition (3). The displayed formula for \(2k-E_R\) shows that \(E_R=2k\) exactly when all singleton and doubleton masses vanish, hence exactly when \(r_{123}=k\). All multiplicities \(1,2,3\) occur in nonempty ambient chambers by accepted fact `0e51a47a9add571f`; thus both endpoints are sharp consequences of the sign equation alone.

The exact partitions give
\[
\sum_i u_i=\sum_J|J|p_J.
\]
Also \(q_0=0\), so
\[
\sum_i v_i
=
\sum_{H\ne0}|H|q_H
=
m+q_{12}+q_{13}+q_{23}+2q_{123}
=
m+E_Q.
\]
Substituting these identities into (1) yields
\[
\sum_J|J|p_J+E_Q+E_R=\frac{2M-m-k}{3}.
\]
Since \(p_{123}=0\), expanding this equality gives (4).

For the single-double support from accepted fact `91cb5bfc71da49ac`,
\[
E_Q=q_{jk}+2q_{123}.
\]
Thus (4), together with \(0\le E_R\le2k\), gives (5)-(6). Exact positive support gives \(q_{jk},q_{123}>0\), so rearranging the upper bound proves (7).

For the all-double support,
\[
q_{12}+q_{13}+q_{23}=m-q_{123},
\]
and hence
\[
E_Q=m+q_{123}.
\]
This proves (8)-(9). Since \(q_{123}>0\), rearranging the upper bound gives (10), which is incompatible with \(k>0\) when \(M\le2m\). No analogous support-wide contradiction follows for the single-double case from multiplicity alone.
