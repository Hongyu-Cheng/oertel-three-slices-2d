## Statement

Let \(N=\{1,2,3\}\).  Assume the common affine-plane and exact-cell setup used
in accepted facts `2bb72698f0248a65` and `91cb5bfc71da49ac`.  Explicitly, let
\(f_1,f_2,f_3\) be nonconstant affine functions on \(\mathbb R^2\) with
pairwise nonparallel linear parts and
\[
f_1+f_2+f_3=-1.
\]
Let \(L_1,L_2,L_3\in\mathbb R\) satisfy
\[
s:=L_1+L_2+L_3<-1,
\]
and let \(A_0,A_2,K\) be nonempty two-dimensional convex polytopes satisfying
\[
\frac{A_0+A_2}{2}\subseteq K.
\]
For \(J,H,G\subseteq N\), use the exact cells
\[
P_J=A_0\cap
\{f_i\le L_i\ (i\in J),\ f_i>L_i\ (i\notin J)\},
\]
\[
Q_H=A_2\cap
\{f_i\le-L_i\ (i\in H),\ f_i>-L_i\ (i\notin H)\},
\]
\[
R_G=K\cap
\{f_i\le0\ (i\in G),\ f_i>0\ (i\notin G)\}.
\]
Put
\[
p_J=|P_J|,\qquad q_H=|Q_H|,\qquad r_G=|R_G|,
\]
\[
M=\sum_Jp_J,\qquad m=\sum_Hq_H,\qquad k=\sum_Gr_G,
\]
and assume
\[
M\ge m>0.
\]

Assume the all-double positive-area support
\[
q_{123}>0,\qquad q_{N\setminus\{i\}}>0\quad(i=1,2,3),
\]
with every other \(q_H=0\), and assume the equal-quarter specialization
\[
q_{123}=q_{12}=q_{13}=q_{23}=:q>0.
\tag{1}
\]
Thus \(m=4q\).  Define
\[
x_i=q_{N\setminus\{i\}},\qquad
u_i=\sum_{J\ni i}p_J,\qquad
v_i=\sum_{H\ni i}q_H=m-x_i,
\]
\[
c_i=\sum_{G\ni i}r_G,\qquad
h=\frac{2(M+m+k)}9.
\]
Assume the three crossing equalities
\[
u_i+v_i+c_i=h\qquad(i=1,2,3)
\tag{2}
\]
and the canonical left-tail inequalities from accepted fact
`624b129b68e0fdee`,
\[
c_i+m\ge h\qquad(i=1,2,3).
\tag{3}
\]
Finally, assume
\[
r_1r_2r_3r_{123}>0.
\tag{4}
\]
Then no such realization exists.

All cells use the displayed weak/strict convention.  Each cutting set
\(f_i=L_i\), \(f_i=-L_i\), or \(f_i=0\) is a line and hence has planar area
zero.  Thus (1) concerns two-dimensional areas and allows arbitrary
coincidences of polygon vertices or edges with cutting lines.

## Proof

Write
\[
\ell_i=f_i+L_i.
\]
Then the \(Q\)-cutting line for index \(i\) is \(\ell_i=0\), and
\[
\ell_1+\ell_2+\ell_3=s-1<0.
\tag{5}
\]
For each \(i\), define the closed halfplane cap
\[
C_i=A_2\cap\{\ell_i\ge0\},
\]
and define the central part
\[
C_0=A_2\cap\{\ell_1\le0,\ell_2\le0,\ell_3\le0\}.
\]
The cutting lines have area zero.  Away from \(\ell_i=0\), the cap \(C_i\)
is the union of exact \(Q_H\)-cells whose indices omit \(i\).  Under the
all-double support, the only such positive-area cell is
\(Q_{N\setminus\{i\}}\).  Hence
\[
|C_i|=x_i=q.
\tag{6}
\]
Also \(C_0=Q_{123}\) exactly under the weak convention, so
\[
|C_0|=q.
\tag{7}
\]

The interiors of \(C_1,C_2,C_3\) are pairwise disjoint.  Indeed, if an open
set lay in the interiors of \(C_i\) and \(C_j\), then on that open set one
would have
\[
\ell_i>0,\qquad \ell_j>0.
\]
For the remaining index \(k\), equation (5) would give \(\ell_k<0\).
The open set would therefore lie in the exact singleton cell \(Q_{\{k\}}\),
contradicting that this unsupported cell has area zero.

Apply the sharp three-cap inequality and equality characterization of accepted
fact `8234d1112ec2f031` to the polygon \(A_2\), with
\[
H_i^+=\{\ell_i\ge0\},\qquad H_i^-=\{\ell_i\le0\}.
\]
By (1), (6), and \(m=4q\),
\[
\sum_{i=1}^3\sqrt{\frac{|C_i|}{|A_2|}}
=3\sqrt{\frac q{4q}}
=\frac32.
\tag{8}
\]
All three cap areas are positive.  Equality in (8) therefore forces \(A_2\)
to be a triangle, the three lines \(\ell_i=0\) to be its medial lines, and
the \(C_i\) to be its three corner triangles.

Let the vertices of \(A_2\) be \(a_1,a_2,a_3\), relabeled so that \(C_i\)
is the corner triangle at \(a_i\).  The line \(\ell_i=0\) then joins the
midpoints of the two edges incident to \(a_i\), while the halfplane
\(\ell_i\le0\) is the side of that line containing the opposite edge.
Consequently the global intersection of the three central halfplanes is
exactly the medial triangle:
\[
\mathcal T_Q
:=\{x:f_i(x)\le-L_i\text{ for }i=1,2,3\}
=
\operatorname{conv}\left\{
\frac{a_1+a_2}{2},
\frac{a_1+a_3}{2},
\frac{a_2+a_3}{2}
\right\}.
\tag{9}
\]
In particular, this global ambient triangle is contained in \(A_2\) and
equals \(C_0=Q_{123}\), including their common closed boundaries.  Equations
(7) and (9) give
\[
|\mathcal T_Q|=q.
\tag{10}
\]

We next use the crossings and tails.  From (2) and (3),
\[
u_i+v_i=h-c_i\le m.
\]
Since \(v_i=m-x_i\), it follows that
\[
0\le u_i\le x_i=q.
\tag{11}
\]
Thus, with \(U=u_1+u_2+u_3\),
\[
U\le3q.
\tag{12}
\]
This is the crossing-tail consequence used in accepted fact
`2bb72698f0248a65`.

The cell \(P_{123}\) is empty: otherwise a point in it would satisfy
\[
-1=f_1+f_2+f_3\le L_1+L_2+L_3=s<-1,
\]
a contradiction.  Therefore the exact source partition and nonnegativity of
all cell areas give
\[
\begin{aligned}
M-p_\varnothing
&=\sum_{\varnothing\ne J\subsetneq N}p_J\\
&\le\sum_{\varnothing\ne J\subsetneq N}|J|p_J\\
&=U.
\end{aligned}
\]
Using (12), \(M\ge m\), and \(m=4q\), we obtain
\[
p_\varnothing\ge M-U\ge m-3q=q>0.
\tag{13}
\]

The hypotheses of the common-plane triangle-scale bridge in accepted fact
`e40abbed940a40ab` now hold: the \(Q\)-support is all-double,
\(p_\varnothing>0\) by (13), and (4) gives positive middle singleton and
central areas.  Hence
\[
|\mathcal T_Q|
\ge
\left(\sqrt{p_\varnothing}+2\sqrt{r_{123}}\right)^2.
\tag{14}
\]
Because \(r_{123}>0\) and \(p_\varnothing\ge q>0\),
\[
\left(\sqrt{p_\varnothing}+2\sqrt{r_{123}}\right)^2
>
p_\varnothing
\ge q.
\tag{15}
\]
Equations (14) and (15) yield
\[
|\mathcal T_Q|>q,
\]
contradicting (10).  This proves that the equal-quarter all-double branch is
impossible.

The equality step (8) is unaffected by weak-versus-strict assignments because
the three cutting lines have area zero.  Positive \(q\) excludes every
zero-cap or degenerate-cap exception in the equality statement of
`8234d1112ec2f031`; collinear polygon vertices on a cutting line are likewise
covered by that accepted equality characterization.
