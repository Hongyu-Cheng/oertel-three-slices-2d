## Statement

Let
\[
B=\frac{A_0+A_2}{2},\qquad
M=\max\{|A_0|,|A_2|\},\qquad
m=\min\{|A_0|,|A_2|\},\qquad
b=|B|,
\]
and normalize
\[
r=\frac mM,\qquad s=\frac bM.
\]
Work on the exact residual domain from accepted fact `15bb2c1680a0254e`,
\[
\mathcal D=\{(r,s):0<r<\rho,\ L(r)<s<U(r)\},
\qquad
\rho=\frac{27-2\sqrt{26}}{25},
\]
where
\[
L(r)=
\begin{cases}
1-r,&0<r\le 9/25,\\
(3r+2\sqrt r-1)/2,&9/25<r<\rho,
\end{cases}
\]
and
\[
U(r)=
\begin{cases}
1+r,&0<r\le1/2,\\
2-r,&1/2<r<\rho.
\end{cases}
\]

For each \(r\), let \(t_r\in(1/3,1/2)\) be the unique root of
\[
rt^3-3rt^2+3t-1=0,
\]
and set
\[
\beta(r)=\frac{1+r+rt_r+t_r^{-1}}4.
\]
For \(r>1/5\), set
\[
\alpha(r)=\frac{(10+\sqrt{70r-10})^2}{180}.
\]
Let \(t_*\in(37/100,187/500)\) be the unique root of
\[
241t^6-1122t^5+4935t^4-11164t^3+3279t^2+30t+25=0,
\]
and define
\[
r_*=\frac{3t_*-1}{t_*^2(3-t_*)}.
\]
Finally, define
\[
r_0=\frac{56}{583},\qquad
t_c=\frac{299-22\sqrt{114}}{185},
\]
and
\[
r_c=\frac{3t_c-1}{t_c^2(3-t_c)}
=\frac{980347-89034\sqrt{114}}{239575}.
\]
These constants satisfy
\[
0<r_0<\frac1{10}<r_c<\frac18<\frac15<r_*<\frac13.
\]

After taking the union of all regions certified by accepted facts `e27aad4aa20bc698`, `d0e8181f1bc85874`, and `3f8a2e2984d22a99`, the exact uncovered subset of \(\mathcal D\) is
\[
\begin{aligned}
\mathcal G={}&
\left\{(r,s):0<r\le r_0,\quad 1-r<s<1-\frac r8\right\}\\
&\cup
\left\{(r,s):r_0<r\le r_c,\quad
1-r<s<\frac{64(1+r)}{71}\right\}\\
&\cup
\left\{(r,s):r_c<r\le\frac15,\quad
1-r<s<\beta(r)\right\}\\
&\cup
\left\{(r,s):\frac15<r<r_*,\quad
\alpha(r)<s<\beta(r)\right\}.
\end{aligned}
\]
There is no uncovered point for \(r_*\le r<\rho\).

Here “uncovered” means only that none of the cited certified lower-bound envelopes reaches \(2(M+m+b)/9\). It does not mean that the three-slice theorem fails at any point of \(\mathcal G\).

## Proof

Accepted fact `e27aad4aa20bc698` gives the exact certification sections of its combined area and support envelope. For \(0<r\le1/5\), it certifies exactly
\[
\beta(r)\le s<U(r).
\]
For \(1/5<r<r_*\), it certifies exactly
\[
L(r)<s\le\alpha(r)
\quad\text{or}\quad
\beta(r)\le s<U(r).
\]
For \(r_*\le r<\rho\), it certifies all of \((L(r),U(r))\). It also proves
\[
\alpha(r)<\beta(r)\quad(1/5<r<r_*),
\qquad
\alpha(r_*)=\beta(r_*),
\]
and \(1/5<r_*<1/3\).

We next determine exactly what accepted fact `d0e8181f1bc85874` adds. After division by \(M\), its new term is
\[
J(r,s)=
\min\left\{\frac{15s}{32},\frac{4s}{9}+\frac r4\right\}.
\]
Against the normalized target \(2(1+r+s)/9\),
\[
\frac{15s}{32}\ge\frac{2(1+r+s)}9
\quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad
71s\ge64(1+r),
\]
and
\[
\frac{4s}{9}+\frac r4\ge\frac{2(1+r+s)}9
\quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad
8s+r\ge8.
\]
Thus \(J\) reaches the target if and only if
\[
s\ge d(r):=
\max\left\{\frac{64(1+r)}{71},1-\frac r8\right\}.
\tag{1}
\]
The two entries in (1) are equal exactly when
\[
583r=56.
\]
Hence
\[
d(r)=
\begin{cases}
1-r/8,&0<r\le r_0,\\
64(1+r)/71,&r\ge r_0.
\end{cases}
\tag{2}
\]
The other nonhorizontal term in `d0e8181f1bc85874` is the area-only term already included in `e27aad4aa20bc698`, while the horizontal terms are above the target throughout \(\mathcal D\). Consequently (1) is the exact additional region supplied by `d0e8181f1bc85874`.

Now consider accepted fact `3f8a2e2984d22a99`. Its certified band for \(r<1/3\) is
\[
s\ge\gamma(r):=1+r-\frac{1152}{625}r\Theta(r)^4,
\]
where
\[
0<\Theta(r)<1-\frac1{\sqrt2}<\frac13.
\]
Put
\[
B(r):=1+r-\beta(r).
\]
The support-boundary identity in `e27aad4aa20bc698` gives
\[
B(r)=\frac{r(1+t_r)^2}{2(1+rt_r^2)}.
\tag{3}
\]
For \(r<r_*<1/3\) and \(1/3<t_r<1/2\),
\[
B(r)>
r\frac{(4/3)^2}{2(1+(1/3)(1/2)^2)}
=\frac{32}{39}r.
\tag{4}
\]
On the other hand,
\[
\frac{1152}{625}r\Theta(r)^4
<
\frac{1152}{625\cdot81}r
=\frac{128}{5625}r
<\frac{32}{39}r.
\tag{5}
\]
Equations (3) through (5) imply
\[
\gamma(r)>\beta(r).
\]
Therefore every point certified by `3f8a2e2984d22a99` is already certified by the support band of `e27aad4aa20bc698`. For \(r\ge r_*\), the latter already covers the whole residual section. Thus the adaptive-center fact adds no area-parameter point to the union.

It remains to compare the empty-cap threshold \(d(r)\) with \(\beta(r)\). In terms of the headroom \(\delta=1+r-s\), the empty-cap band \(s\ge d(r)\) is
\[
\delta\le E(r):=1+r-d(r)
=
\begin{cases}
9r/8,&0<r\le r_0,\\
7(1+r)/71,&r\ge r_0.
\end{cases}
\tag{6}
\]
The support band is \(\delta\le B(r)\). Since \(t_r<1/2\),
\[
\frac{B(r)}r
=\frac{(1+t_r)^2}{2(1+rt_r^2)}
<\frac{(3/2)^2}{2}
=\frac98.
\tag{7}
\]
Thus \(E(r)>B(r)\) throughout the first branch of (6), so the empty-cap band strictly extends the support band there.

For the second branch, solve the defining equation of \(t_r\) for \(r\):
\[
r=r(t):=\frac{3t-1}{t^2(3-t)}.
\tag{8}
\]
On \(1/3<t<1/2\),
\[
r'(t)=\frac{6(t-1)^2}{t^3(t-3)^2}>0.
\tag{9}
\]
Substituting (8) into (3) gives
\[
B(r(t))=\frac{(t+1)(3t-1)}{4t^2}.
\]
Direct subtraction yields
\[
B(r(t))-\frac{7(1+r(t))}{71}
=
\frac{(t+1)(185t^2-598t+185)}
{284t^2(t-3)}.
\tag{10}
\]
The quadratic
\[
P(t)=185t^2-598t+185
\]
is strictly decreasing on \((1/3,1/2)\), with
\[
P(1/3)=\frac{56}{9}>0,\qquad
P(1/2)=-\frac{271}{4}<0.
\]
Its unique root in that interval is \(t_c\). Since the denominator in (10) is negative, (9) and (10) show
\[
B(r)<E(r)\quad(r<r_c),\qquad
B(r_c)=E(r_c),\qquad
B(r)>E(r)\quad(r>r_c).
\tag{11}
\]

The displayed radical for \(r_c\) is obtained by substituting \(t_c\) into (8). It is the smaller root of
\[
239575r^2-1960694r+239575=0.
\]
This polynomial is strictly decreasing on \([0,1/8]\), and its values at \(1/10\) and \(1/8\) are respectively
\[
\frac{918027}{20}>0,\qquad
-\frac{113177}{64}<0.
\]
Therefore
\[
\frac1{10}<r_c<\frac18.
\]
Also \(r_0=56/583<1/10\), proving the stated ordering.

Equations (2), (6), (7), and (11) show that the union of all upper certified bands has the single threshold
\[
s\ge w(r),
\qquad
w(r)=
\begin{cases}
1-r/8,&0<r\le r_0,\\
64(1+r)/71,&r_0<r\le r_c,\\
\beta(r),&r_c<r<r_*.
\end{cases}
\tag{12}
\]
At \(r=r_0\), the first two formulas agree. At \(r=r_c\), the last two formulas agree. The first two uncovered intervals are nonempty because
\[
1-r<1-\frac r8<1+r
\]
and, for \(r>r_0\),
\[
\frac{64(1+r)}{71}-(1-r)=\frac{135r-7}{71}>0.
\]

Since \(r_*<1/3<9/25\), one has \(L(r)=1-r\) and \(U(r)=1+r\) throughout every section that can remain uncovered. For \(r\le1/5\), the area component of `e27aad4aa20bc698` certifies no point of the open strip, so subtracting the upper band (12) leaves exactly the first three pieces of \(\mathcal G\). At \(r=1/5\),
\[
\alpha(1/5)=\frac45=1-\frac15,
\]
so no additional endpoint is lost. For \(1/5<r<r_*\), the area component certifies \(s\le\alpha(r)\), while the support component certifies \(s\ge\beta(r)\); their exact complement is
\[
\alpha(r)<s<\beta(r).
\]
At \(r=r_*\) the two closed certified bands meet, and for larger \(r\) accepted fact `e27aad4aa20bc698` covers the whole residual section.

Every point outside \(\mathcal G\) is therefore covered by at least one cited envelope. At every point of \(\mathcal G\), the exact criterion of `e27aad4aa20bc698` fails, condition (1) for the new term of `d0e8181f1bc85874` fails, and the adaptive threshold from `3f8a2e2984d22a99` is stricter than the failed support threshold. Thus all cited envelopes are below the target there. These are envelope noncoverage statements only and do not imply theorem noncoverage.
