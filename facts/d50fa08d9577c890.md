## Statement

Let
\[
\mathcal L=\{(x,y):x\le-|y|\},\qquad
\mathcal R=\{(x,y):x\ge|y|\},\qquad
\mathcal U=\{(x,y):y\ge|x|\},
\]
and take the rational polygons
\[
A_0=
\left[-\frac{3055}{19},-20\right]\times[-19,19],
\]
\[
A_2=\operatorname{conv}\left\{
(0,0),\left(210,-\frac{10}{21}\right),
\left(210,\frac{10}{21}\right)\right\},
\]
\[
K=[-100,100]\times[-10,10].
\]
Put
\[
B=\frac{A_0+A_2}{2},\qquad
M=|A_0|,\quad m=|A_2|,\quad k=|K|,
\quad t=\frac{2(M+m+k)}9,
\]
\[
\delta=\frac{3055}{19},\qquad
C=|K\cap\{x\le1\}|,\qquad
v_1=|A_2\cap\{x\le\delta+2\}|,
\]
\[
\pi=t-m,\qquad
Y=K\cap\mathcal U\cap\{x\ge1\},\qquad
G=\operatorname{conv}(B\cup Y).
\]
Then this triple satisfies all the retained canonical hypotheses:
\[
A_0\subseteq\mathcal L,\quad A_2\subseteq\mathcal R,\quad
B\subseteq K,\quad M\ge m>0,\quad 0<t<M,
\]
\[
\min_{A_0}x=-\delta,\qquad
\min_{A_2}(x+y)=\min_{A_2}(x-y)=0,
\]
\[
|K\cap\{x+y\ge0\}|=|K\cap\{x-y\ge0\}|=\pi,
\]
both contacts are genuine lower crossings, the forced tail is
positive and has the required midpoint inclusion,
\[
C+m>t,\qquad C+v_1<t,
\]
both inherited hull-excess budgets hold, and, for
\[
D(x,y)=(u,v)=(x+y,x-y),\qquad
\alpha=-\min_{D(K)}u,\quad\beta=-\min_{D(K)}v,
\]
one has
\[
7C+2\pi-2k-4\alpha\beta<0.
\]

Choose the two contact points
\[
q_u=(0,\sigma)=(0,0),\qquad
q_v=(\tau,0)=(0,0)
\]
in \(Q=D(A_2)\), put \(P=-D(A_0)\), and define \(T_\tau,U_\sigma\),
\(Y'\), and \(R\) as in the registered subgoal.  Then
\[
T_\tau+5U_\sigma=10700
<
16|D(B)\cap Y'|+32\pi-8m
=\frac{62\,663\,671\,072}{972\,895},
\]
while simultaneously
\[
\left|
\bigl(\operatorname{conv}(D(B)\cup Y')\setminus D(B)\bigr)
\cap\{v\ge0\}
\right|
=\frac{38\,081\,077}{1\,667\,820}
<2\pi-\frac m2=3950.
\]
Thus both alternatives in the claimed dichotomy fail.

## Proof

We first verify the complete canonical setting.  Direct area
calculation gives
\[
M=38\left(\frac{3055}{19}-20\right)=5350,\qquad
m=\frac12\cdot210\cdot\frac{20}{21}=100,\qquad
k=200\cdot20=4000.
\]
Consequently
\[
t=\frac{2(5350+100+4000)}9=2100,\qquad
\pi=t-m=2000.
\]
In particular \(M\ge m>0\) and \(0<t<M\).

Every point of \(A_0\) has \(x\le-20\) and \(|y|\le19\), so
\(A_0\subset\mathcal L\), with a strict gap of at least \(1\) from
both diagonal boundary rays.  A vertical section of \(A_2\) at
\(0\le x\le210\) is
\[
\left[-\frac{x}{441},\frac{x}{441}\right],
\]
so \(A_2\subseteq\mathcal R\).  Its only point on either boundary ray
of \(\mathcal R\) is \((0,0)\).  Hence
\[
\min_{A_2}(x+y)=\min_{A_2}(x-y)=0
\]
and both diagonal contacts may be chosen at the common vertex
\((0,0)\), giving \(\sigma=\tau=0\).

The six cyclic vertices of \(B\) are
\[
\begin{aligned}
&\left(-\frac{3055}{38},-\frac{19}{2}\right),
\left(\frac{935}{38},-\frac{409}{42}\right),
\left(95,-\frac{409}{42}\right),\\
&\left(95,\frac{409}{42}\right),
\left(\frac{935}{38},\frac{409}{42}\right),
\left(-\frac{3055}{38},\frac{19}{2}\right).
\end{aligned}
\]
All have \(-100<x<100\) and \(|y|<10\).  Thus \(B\subseteq K\).
The rectangle \(K\) is centrally symmetric, so every line through its
center bisects its area.  Therefore
\[
|K\cap\{x+y\ge0\}|=|K\cap\{x-y\ge0\}|=\frac k2=2000=\pi.
\]

The contacts are genuine in the required one-sided sense.  If
\(0<a<1\), then
\[
A_0\cap\{x+y\ge-a\}
=A_0\cap\{x-y\ge-a\}=\varnothing.
\]
For either nonnegative affine coordinate \(x+y\) or \(x-y\) on
\(A_2\), the strip where that coordinate is \(<a\) has positive area.
Thus
\[
|A_2\cap\{x+y\ge a\}|<m,\qquad
|A_2\cap\{x-y\ge a\}|<m,
\]
and hence both required strict crossing inequalities are
\(\pi+0+\text{(an area \(<m\)} )<\pi+m=t\).

The absolute-level quantities are
\[
C=101\cdot20=2020,\qquad
\delta+2=\frac{3093}{19}.
\]
Similarity of the sections of the triangle \(A_2\) gives
\[
v_1
=100\left(\frac{(3093/19)}{210}\right)^2
=100\left(\frac{1031}{1330}\right)^2
=\frac{1\,062\,961}{17\,689}.
\]
Therefore
\[
C+m=2120>2100=t
\]
and
\[
t-C-v_1
=80-\frac{1\,062\,961}{17\,689}
=\frac{352\,159}{17\,689}>0.
\]
Equivalently, with
\[
\theta=m-v_1=\frac{705\,939}{17\,689},
\]
one has
\[
\theta-(C-\pi)
=\theta-20
=\frac{352\,159}{17\,689}>0.
\]
The tail \(A_2^+=A_2\cap\{x\ge\delta+2\}\) is therefore
two-dimensional and positive-area.  For \(a_0\in A_0\) and
\(a_2\in A_2^+\),
\[
x\left(\frac{a_0+a_2}{2}\right)
\ge\frac{-\delta+(\delta+2)}2=1,
\]
which proves the required forced-tail inclusion
\[
\frac{A_0+A_2^+}{2}\subseteq B\cap\{x\ge1\}.
\]

Here
\[
Y=\operatorname{conv}\{(1,1),(1,10),(10,10)\}.
\]
Exact convex-hull enumeration gives the cyclic vertices
\[
\begin{aligned}
G=\operatorname{conv}\biggl\{&
\left(-\frac{3055}{38},-\frac{19}{2}\right),
\left(\frac{935}{38},-\frac{409}{42}\right),
\left(95,-\frac{409}{42}\right),\\
&\left(95,\frac{409}{42}\right),(10,10),(1,10),
\left(-\frac{3055}{38},\frac{19}{2}\right)
\biggr\}.
\end{aligned}
\]
The shoelace formula on the displayed cyclic lists gives
\[
|B|=\frac{2\,706\,035}{798},\qquad
|G|=\frac{3\,636\,729}{1064},
\]
and hence
\[
|G\setminus B|
=|G|-|B|
=\frac{86\,047}{3192}<27.
\]
It follows for both
\[
H_u=\{x+y\ge0\},\qquad H_v=\{x-y\ge0\}
\]
that
\[
|(G\setminus B)\cap H_u|,
\ |(G\setminus B)\cap H_v|
<27<1975=\pi-\frac m4.
\]
Thus both inherited hull-excess budgets hold with large strict slack.

The four vertices of \(D(K)\) are
\[
(-110,-90),\quad(-90,-110),\quad(110,90),\quad(90,110).
\]
Thus \(\alpha=\beta=110\), and the additional scalar sign is
\[
7C+2\pi-2k-4\alpha\beta
=7(2020)+2(2000)-2(4000)-4(110)^2
=-38260<0.
\]

We next rederive the exact weighted-cell reduction and all constants.
Write \(S=D(B)\), \(F=D(G)\), and \(s=u+v\).  Up to null boundary
lines, put
\[
A=F\cap\{s\le2,\ v\le0\},\qquad
B_+=F\cap\{s\le2,\ v\ge0\}.
\]
Since \(G\subseteq K\), \(Y\subseteq G\), and
\(s\ge2,\ v\le0\) implies \(u=s-v\ge0\), one has exactly
\[
F\cap\{s\ge2,\ v\le0\}=Y'.
\]
Consequently
\[
5|F\cap\{s\le2\}|\ge4|F\cap\{v\le0\}|
\]
is equivalent to
\[
|A|+5|B_+|\ge4|Y'|.
\tag{1}
\]

For general contact levels, a point
\((q_v-p)/2\), where \(q_v=(\tau,0)\), satisfies
\[
s=\frac{\tau-p_u-p_v}{2}\le2
\quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad
p_u+p_v\ge\tau-4,
\]
and it has \(v=-p_v/2\le0\).  Since the affine map has area
factor \(1/4\), this copy contributes \(T_\tau/4\) to \(A\).
Likewise, for \(q_u=(0,\sigma)\),
\[
s=\frac{\sigma-p_u-p_v}{2}\le2
\quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad
p_u+p_v\ge\sigma-4,
\]
and
\[
v=\frac{\sigma-p_v}{2}\ge0
\quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad p_v\le\sigma.
\]
It contributes \(U_\sigma/4\) to \(B_+\).  Hence
\[
|A|+5|B_+|\ge\frac{T_\tau+5U_\sigma}{4}.
\tag{2}
\]
This rederives both actual thresholds and the factor \(1/4\).

The translated-copy overlap is also exact.  If
\[
d=q_v-q_u=(\tau,-\sigma),
\]
then \((q_u-p)/2\) belongs to the \(q_v\)-copy precisely when
\(p+d\in P\).  The two left-cut conditions then agree.  Thus, with
\[
I=\left|\{p\in P:p+d\in P,\ p_u+p_v\ge\sigma-4\}\right|,
\]
\[
\left|
\left(\frac{q_u-P}{2}\cup\frac{q_v-P}{2}\right)
\cap\{s\le2\}
\right|
=\frac{T_\sigma+T_\tau-I}{4}.
\tag{3}
\]

For the present triple, the \((s,v)=(u+v,v)\) sections of \(P\) are
\[
P=\left\{(s,v):
40\le s\le\frac{6110}{19},\
\frac s2-19\le v\le\frac s2+19\right\}.
\tag{4}
\]
In particular \(|P|=10700\), \(p_v\ge1\), and the actual
thresholds are
\[
\sigma-4=\tau-4=-4.
\]
It follows that
\[
T_\sigma=T_\tau=10700,\qquad U_\sigma=0.
\tag{5}
\]
Moreover \(d=0\), so the two copies coincide and
\[
I=10700.
\]
Thus (3) retains the maximal possible translated overlap rather than
treating the copies as disjoint.

For completeness, the corresponding sections of \(Q=D(A_2)\) are
\[
Q=\left\{(s,v):
0\le s\le420,\
\frac{220}{441}s\le v\le\frac{221}{441}s\right\}.
\tag{6}
\]
The forced tail is obtained by imposing
\[
s\ge2(\delta+2)=\frac{6186}{19}.
\]
Consequently the forced-tail midpoint body
\[
R=\frac{Q^+-P}{2}
\]
has the following cyclic vertices in \((s,v)\)-coordinates:
\[
\begin{aligned}
&\left(2,-\frac{24256}{2793}\right),
\left(\frac{935}{19},\frac{5932}{399}\right),
\left(190,\frac{3581}{42}\right),\\
&\left(190,\frac{4399}{42}\right),
\left(\frac{935}{19},\frac{13703}{399}\right),
\left(2,\frac{29842}{2793}\right).
\end{aligned}
\tag{7}
\]
This explicitly retains the horizontal \(s\)-placement and the
vertical split at \(v=0\).

On the other hand,
\[
Y'=\operatorname{conv}\{(2,-9),(2,0),(20,0)\},
\qquad |Y'|=81.
\tag{8}
\]
Clipping the displayed polygon \(B\) by \(Y\) gives
\[
B\cap Y
=\operatorname{conv}\left\{
(1,1),
\left(\frac{10141}{1045},\frac{10141}{1045}\right),
\left(1,\frac{27049}{2793}\right)
\right\}.
\]
After applying \(D\), this is the triangle
\[
S\cap Y'
=\operatorname{conv}\left\{
(2,0),
\left(\frac{20282}{1045},0\right),
\left(2,-\frac{24256}{2793}\right)
\right\}.
\tag{9}
\]
The same triangle is exactly \(R\cap\{v\le0\}\), as is immediate
from (7).  Thus the forced-tail body supplies the whole right-bad
part of \(D(B)\), not merely a marginal lower bound.  Its area is
\[
|S\cap Y'|
=\frac12\left(\frac{20282}{1045}-2\right)
\cdot\frac{24256}{2793}
=\frac{73\,544\,192}{972\,895}.
\tag{10}
\]

The convex hull of \(R\) and \(Y'\) still creates very little new
area above \(v=0\).  Its \(v\ge0\) part has cyclic vertices
\[
\begin{aligned}
&(2,0),(20,0),
\left(190,\frac{3581}{42}\right),
\left(190,\frac{4399}{42}\right),\\
&\left(\frac{935}{19},\frac{13703}{399}\right),
\left(2,\frac{29842}{2793}\right),
\end{aligned}
\]
whereas its intersection with \(S\cap\{v\ge0\}\) has the same list
with the lower chain \((2,0),(20,0)\) replaced by
\[
(2,0),\quad
\left(\frac{20282}{1045},0\right),\quad
\left(\frac{935}{19},\frac{5932}{399}\right).
\]
The shoelace formula gives
\[
\left|
\bigl(\operatorname{conv}(R\cup Y')\setminus S\bigr)
\cap\{v\ge0\}
\right|
=\frac{38\,081\,077}{1\,667\,820}.
\tag{11}
\]
Direct enumeration of \(\operatorname{conv}(S\cup Y')=D(G)\)
shows that its new \(v\ge0\) part is exactly the same region.  Hence
\[
\left|
\bigl(\operatorname{conv}(S\cup Y')\setminus S\bigr)
\cap\{v\ge0\}
\right|
=\frac{38\,081\,077}{1\,667\,820}
<23<3950=2\pi-\frac m2.
\tag{12}
\]
This both verifies the second inherited budget in \(D\)-coordinates
and proves that the second alternative of the registered dichotomy
is false.

Finally, (5) and (10) give
\[
T_\tau+5U_\sigma=10700,
\]
whereas
\[
\begin{aligned}
16|S\cap Y'|+32\pi-8m
&=
16\frac{73\,544\,192}{972\,895}
+32(2000)-8(100)\\
&=\frac{62\,663\,671\,072}{972\,895}.
\end{aligned}
\]
Their strict difference is
\[
\frac{52\,253\,694\,572}{972\,895}>0,
\]
so the first alternative is false as well.

The source of the failure is now exact.  From (8)--(10),
\[
|Y'\setminus S|
=81-\frac{73\,544\,192}{972\,895}
=\frac{5\,260\,303}{972\,895}.
\tag{13}
\]
The inherited quantity \(2\pi-m/2=3950\) is only an upper bound for
this actual missing right-bad area; here it exceeds (13) by orders of
magnitude.  Equations (1)--(2) show that the exact sufficient payment
is
\[
T_\tau+5U_\sigma
\ge16|Y'|
=16|S\cap Y'|+16|Y'\setminus S|.
\tag{14}
\]
Indeed, this counterexample satisfies the corrected threshold
\(10700\ge16\cdot81=1296\).  Replacing the last term in (14) by the
full unused upper budget is not a valid necessary alternative, and
(11)--(12) show that the unused budget need not reappear as transverse
hull excess.
