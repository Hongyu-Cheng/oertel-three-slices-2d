## Statement

Use
\[
u=x-y,\qquad v=x+y,\qquad
x=\frac{u+v}{2},\qquad y=\frac{v-u}{2},
\]
so that \(dx\,dy=\frac12\,du\,dv\), and fix
\[
K=\left\{(u,v):
-100\leq u\leq100,\quad
\frac{101}{100}\leq v\leq\frac{1271}{1250}
\right\}.
\]
There are no real, and hence no rational, parameters
\[
A=v_P^-,\quad B=v_P^+,\quad C=u_P^-,
\qquad
D=u_Q^-,\quad E=u_Q^+,\quad
F=v_Q^-,\quad G=v_Q^+
\]
defining two-dimensional polygons
\[
P=\{A\leq v\leq B,\ C\leq u\leq-v\},
\qquad
Q=[D,E]\times[F,G]
\]
which simultaneously satisfy
\[
|P|=1,\qquad |Q|=\frac13,\qquad P+Q\subseteq2K,
\]
\[
|P\cap\{x\geq0\}|+|Q\cap\{x\geq0\}|=\frac1{10},
\]
\[
|P\cap\{y\geq0\}|+|Q\cap\{y\geq0\}|=\frac1{10},
\]
and for which \(b=0\) is a global minimizer of both profiles
\[
\Phi_x(b)=|P\cap\{x\geq-b\}|+|Q\cap\{x\geq b\}|,
\]
\[
\Phi_y(b)=|P\cap\{y\geq-b\}|+|Q\cap\{y\geq b\}|.
\]
This remains impossible after imposing all the further requirements
\[
\max_Px=0,\qquad
0<|Q\cap\{x\geq0\}|<\frac13,
\]
\[
L_{Q,x}(0+)\leq\mathcal H_1(P\cap\{x=0\}),
\]
and
\[
\frac{(d_P+d_Q)^2}{8}\geq\frac43,\qquad
d_R=\max_R(x+y)-\min_Rx-\min_Ry.
\]

## Proof

First reduce the endpoint system exactly.  Put
\[
\widehat B=\min\{B,-C\}.
\]
Because \(P\) is two-dimensional, \(A<\widehat B\).  Put
\[
\widehat h=\widehat B-A>0,\qquad q=G-F>0.
\]
The actual minimum and maximum \(v\)-coordinates of \(P+Q\) are
\(A+F\) and \(\widehat B+G\).  Since
\[
2K=\left\{(u,v):
-200\leq u\leq200,\quad
\frac{101}{50}\leq v\leq\frac{1271}{625}
\right\},
\]
containment gives
\[
A+F\geq\frac{101}{50},\qquad
\widehat B+G\leq\frac{1271}{625},
\]
and hence
\[
\widehat h+q\leq
\frac{1271}{625}-\frac{101}{50}
=\frac{17}{1250}<2.
\tag{1}
\]
Write \(w=-C-\widehat B\geq0\).  Exact integration of \(P\), including
the possible triangular endpoint \(\widehat B=-C\), gives
\[
1=|P|
=\frac12\int_A^{\widehat B}(-v-C)\,dv
=\frac{\widehat h\,w}{2}+\frac{\widehat h^2}{4}.
\]
Thus
\[
w=\frac2{\widehat h}-\frac{\widehat h}{2}>0
\tag{2}
\]
by (1).  In particular \(\widehat B<-C\), so the minimum defining
\(\widehat B\) cannot be attained by \(-C\).  Therefore
\[
\widehat B=B,\qquad C<-B.
\tag{3}
\]
Set
\[
h=B-A,\qquad r=E-D.
\]
The area equations and (1)-(3) are exactly
\[
-C-\frac{A+B}{2}=\frac2h,\qquad
rq=\frac23,\qquad
0<h,\quad0<q,\quad h+q\leq\frac{17}{1250}.
\tag{4}
\]
In particular
\[
w_B:=-C-B=\frac2h-\frac h2>0,\qquad
w_A:=-C-A=\frac2h+\frac h2,
\tag{5}
\]
and
\[
r=\frac2{3q}>q.
\tag{6}
\]
The last inequality follows already from \(q<17/1250<1/2\).
After (3), the four coordinate extrema show that containment
\(P+Q\subseteq2K\) is exactly the conjunction
\[
A+F\geq\frac{101}{50},\qquad
B+G\leq\frac{1271}{625},\qquad
C+D\geq-200,\qquad
-A+E\leq200.
\tag{7}
\]
Also
\[
|K|=\frac12\cdot200\cdot\frac{17}{2500}
=\frac{17}{25},
\]
so all three prescribed areas and every equality endpoint have been
retained in (4) and (7).

For completeness, the two global profiles admit the following exact
cap-breakpoint exhaustion.  Define the rectangle upper-tail function
\[
\Psi_{r,q}(z)=
\begin{cases}
rq,&z\leq0,\\[2pt]
rq-\dfrac{z^2}{2},&0\leq z\leq q,\\[6pt]
q(r-z)+\dfrac{q^2}{2},&q\leq z\leq r,\\[6pt]
\dfrac{(r+q-z)^2}{2},&r\leq z\leq r+q,\\[6pt]
0,&z\geq r+q.
\end{cases}
\tag{8}
\]
It is the \(uv\)-area of
\[
\{(\xi,\eta)\in[0,r]\times[0,q]:\xi+\eta\geq z\}.
\]
The ordering in (8) is exhaustive because (6) gives \(0<q<r\).
In the coordinates
\[
\eta=v-A,\qquad z_P=-u-v,
\]
the polygon \(P\) is
\[
0\leq\eta\leq h,\qquad
0\leq z_P\leq w_A-\eta.
\]
Define its two relevant \(uv\)-cap functions
\[
\Theta_h(z)=
\begin{cases}
0,&z\leq0,\\[2pt]
hz,&0\leq z\leq w_B,\\[2pt]
2-\dfrac{(w_A-z)^2}{2},&w_B\leq z\leq w_A,\\[6pt]
2,&z\geq w_A,
\end{cases}
\tag{9}
\]
and
\[
\Xi_h(z)=
\begin{cases}
2,&z\leq0,\\[2pt]
2-\dfrac{z^2}{4},&0\leq z\leq2h,\\[6pt]
2-hz+h^2,&2h\leq z\leq w_A,\\[2pt]
\dfrac{(w_A+h-z)^2}{2},&w_A\leq z\leq w_A+h,\\[6pt]
0,&z\geq w_A+h.
\end{cases}
\tag{10}
\]
Here \(\Theta_h(z)\) is the \(uv\)-area with \(z_P\leq z\), while
\(\Xi_h(z)\) is the \(uv\)-area with \(2\eta+z_P\geq z\).
The order \(0<2h<w_A<w_A+h\) in (10) follows from
\(h<17/1250\) and (5).  Direct slicing gives (8)-(10), and consecutive
formulas agree at every displayed breakpoint.

Since \(x=-z_P/2\) and
\(2y=2A+2\eta+z_P\), while the corresponding rectangle coordinates
give
\[
2x=D+F+\xi+\eta,\qquad
2y=F-E+\xi+\eta,
\]
the two true profiles are exactly
\[
\Phi_x(b)
=\frac12\Theta_h(2b)
+\frac12\Psi_{r,q}(2b-D-F),
\tag{11}
\]
\[
\Phi_y(b)
=\frac12\Xi_h(-2b-2A)
+\frac12\Psi_{r,q}(2b-F+E).
\tag{12}
\]
Thus all \(x\)-profile breakpoint cells are obtained by interleaving the
two internally ordered chains
\[
0<\frac{w_B}{2}<\frac{w_A}{2}
\tag{13}
\]
and
\[
\frac{D+F}{2}<\frac{D+G}{2}
<\frac{E+F}{2}<\frac{E+G}{2}.
\tag{14}
\]
There are exactly
\(\binom73=35\) strict shuffle cells; every equality cell is a face
obtained by replacing one or more cross-chain strict inequalities by
equalities.  Similarly, all \(y\)-profile breakpoint cells are obtained
by interleaving
\[
\frac{C-B}{2}<\frac{C-A}{2}<-B<-A
\tag{15}
\]
and
\[
\frac{F-E}{2}<\frac{G-E}{2}
<\frac{F-D}{2}<\frac{G-D}{2}.
\tag{16}
\]
The middle inequality in (15) is strict because
\[
-B-\frac{C-A}{2}
=\frac1h-\frac{3h}{4}>0,
\]
and the middle inequality in (16) is strict by \(r>q\).
Hence there are exactly
\(\binom84=70\) strict shuffle cells, together with all their equality
faces.  Equations (8)-(12) select the correct polynomial on every open
interval in every one of these cells.  On such an interval each profile
is quadratic, affine, or constant, so its only possible interior minimum
is the unique zero of its affine derivative, or the whole interval if
that derivative vanishes identically.  Together with all listed
breakpoints and the two unbounded intervals, this exhausts every global
profile minimum.  In particular, the imposed profile conditions are
exactly
\[
\Phi_x(0)=\Phi_y(0)=\frac1{10},
\qquad
\Phi_x(b)\geq\frac1{10},\quad
\Phi_y(b)\geq\frac1{10}\quad(b\in\mathbb R),
\tag{17}
\]
checked by (8)-(16) in each shuffle cell.  No numerical or
generic-position assumption has been made.

The exposed-section and support conditions also have exact endpoint
forms on every chamber.  The exposed edge \(u=-v\) maps to
\(\{x=0,\ A\leq y\leq B\}\), so
\[
\max_Px=0,\qquad
\mathcal H_1(P\cap\{x=0\})=h.
\tag{18}
\]
Writing \([s]_+=\max\{s,0\}\), the right section of \(Q\) at \(x=0\) is
\[
L_{Q,x}(0+)
=\bigl[\min\{G,-D\}-\max\{F,-E\}\bigr]_+.
\tag{19}
\]
Moreover,
\[
d_P
=B-\frac{C+A}{2}-A
=\frac{2B-C-3A}{2},
\qquad
d_Q
=G-\frac{D+F}{2}-\frac{F-E}{2}
=q+\frac r2.
\tag{20}
\]
Consequently the section and support requirements are precisely (19)
\(\leq h\) and
\[
\frac18\left(
\frac{2B-C-3A}{2}+q+\frac r2
\right)^2\geq\frac43.
\tag{21}
\]
Thus (4), (7)-(21) simultaneously encode areas, containment, both
global profile minima, the exposed section, and the support statistic.

It remains to eliminate these cells.  This requires only the common
constraints at \(b=0\), and therefore eliminates every one of the
breakpoint cells above at once.  The defining inequality \(u\leq-v\)
gives
\[
x=\frac{u+v}{2}\leq0
\quad\hbox{on }P,
\]
and its equality set is the one-dimensional edge in (18).  Hence
\[
|P\cap\{x\geq0\}|=0,\qquad
|Q\cap\{x\geq0\}|=\frac1{10}.
\tag{22}
\]

There are two exhaustive endpoint cells.

If \(A\geq0\), then every point of \(P\) has \(v=x+y\geq0\) and
\(x\leq0\).  Therefore \(y=v-x\geq0\) throughout \(P\), so
\[
|P\cap\{y\geq0\}|=|P|=1.
\]
This contradicts the second profile value in (17), whose two
nonnegative summands have total \(1/10\).

If \(A<0\), the lower containment inequality in (7) gives
\[
F\geq\frac{101}{50}-A>0.
\tag{23}
\]
Thus every point of \(Q\) satisfies \(v=x+y>0\), and hence
\[
Q\subseteq\{x\geq0\}\cup\{y\geq0\}.
\]
By (22) and the second equality in (17),
\[
|Q\cap\{x\geq0\}|=\frac1{10},
\qquad
|Q\cap\{y\geq0\}|\leq\frac1{10}.
\]
Subadditivity for the displayed covering of \(Q\) now gives the exact
contradiction
\[
\frac13=|Q|
\leq |Q\cap\{x\geq0\}|
+|Q\cap\{y\geq0\}|
\leq\frac15.
\tag{24}
\]

The cases \(A\geq0\) and \(A<0\) exhaust real endpoint space.  Therefore
each of the \(35\) by \(70\) possible strict profile-shuffle
combinations, as well as every equality face, has empty intersection
with the simultaneous constraints.  The additional global-minimum,
section, and support inequalities cannot restore a point to this empty
set.  Hence the entire declared aligned family is infeasible.
