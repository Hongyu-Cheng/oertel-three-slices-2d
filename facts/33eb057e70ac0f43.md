## Statement

Use the area-preserving coordinates
\[
u=y,\qquad v=x+y,
\]
so that \((x,y)=(v-u,u)\).  Define
\[
\rho=\frac{27-2\sqrt{26}}{25},
\qquad
U(r)=
\begin{cases}
1+r,&0<r\le\frac12,\\
2-r,&\frac12<r<\rho.
\end{cases}
\]
Set
\[
\alpha=\frac{2000}{201},\qquad
\beta=\frac{404}{201},\qquad
\varepsilon=\gamma=\frac1{4040},\qquad
w=\frac{25}{101}.
\]
Let \(P,Q\) be the rational triangles whose \((u,v)\)-coordinate descriptions are
\[
P=\operatorname{conv}\left\{
(\varepsilon,\alpha),\
(\varepsilon,\alpha-100),\
(\varepsilon+1,\alpha-100)
\right\},
\]
\[
Q=\operatorname{conv}\left\{
(-\gamma,-\beta),\
(w-\gamma,-\beta),\
(-\gamma,202-\beta)
\right\}.
\]
These displayed \((u,v)\)-points denote the corresponding original points
\[
(x,y)=(v-u,u),
\]
so \(P,Q\) are compact two-dimensional convex polygons with rational vertices in the original coordinates.  Their areas and ratio are
\[
M:=|P|=50,\qquad
m:=|Q|=25,\qquad
r:=\frac mM=\frac12,
\]
and hence \(M\ge m>0\) and \(0<r<\rho\).  Define
\[
\delta=101+\varepsilon-\alpha
=\frac{73936241}{812040}>0.
\]
Then
\[
\min_Px=-\delta,
\qquad
P\subseteq\{y\ge0\}.
\]

The two cap parameters in the \(y\)-row and the far \(x\)-row are
\[
d_2:=|Q\cap\{y\le0\}|=\frac{1999}{40000},
\]
\[
b:=|Q\cap\{x\ge\delta+2\}|
=\frac{1283430625}{183393606}.
\]
They satisfy \(0<d_2<m\), and the complete empty-proper shifted profile is
\[
|P\cap\{y\le s\}|+|Q\cap\{y\le-s\}|\ge d_2
\qquad\text{for every }s<0,
\]
whereas
\[
|P\cap\{y\le s\}|+|Q\cap\{y\le-s\}|<d_2
\qquad\text{for every }0<s<\frac1{4040}.
\]

For the \(x+y\)-row, put
\[
p=\frac{20000}{40401}.
\]
The exact cap values are
\[
|P\cap\{x+y\ge0\}|=p,
\qquad
|Q\cap\{x+y\ge0\}|=m-p.
\]
Thus
\[
0<|P\cap\{x+y\ge0\}|<m,
\qquad
0<|Q\cap\{x+y\ge0\}|<m,
\]
\[
|P\cap\{x+y\ge0\}|+|Q\cap\{x+y\ge0\}|=m.
\]
The complete proper-proper shifted profile is
\[
|P\cap\{x+y\ge-s\}|+|Q\cap\{x+y\ge s\}|\ge m
\qquad\text{for every }s<0,
\]
whereas
\[
|P\cap\{x+y\ge-s\}|+|Q\cap\{x+y\ge s\}|<m
\qquad\text{for every }0<s\le1.
\]

Finally, set
\[
a=\frac{79}{4}.
\]
Then
\[
0<a<m,\qquad
a+d_2=\frac{791999}{40000}<m,
\]
\[
4\min\{a,d_2\}+5\max\{a,d_2\}
=\frac{989499}{10000}
<100=4m,
\]
and
\[
b-(m-a)
=\frac{641228387}{366787212}>0.
\]
Nevertheless,
\[
\left|\frac{P+Q}{2}\right|
=\frac{277}{4}
<75
=M\,U(r).
\]
Thus these rational data satisfy every displayed hypothesis and the strict reverse of the proposed midpoint-area conclusion.

## Proof

The map \((u,v)\mapsto(v-u,u)\) has determinant of absolute value \(1\), and
\[
y=u,\qquad x+y=v,\qquad x=v-u.
\]
The two triangles have areas
\[
M=\frac{1\cdot100}{2}=50,\qquad
m=\frac{w\cdot202}{2}=25.
\]
Hence \(r=1/2\).  Moreover \(r<\rho\): the equivalent inequality
\[
29>4\sqrt{26}
\]
follows by squaring, since \(841>416\).  Therefore
\[
U(r)=1+r=\frac32.
\]

All \(u\)-coordinates of \(P\) are at least \(\varepsilon>0\), so
\[
P\subseteq\{y\ge0\}.
\]
At the three vertices of \(P\), the values of \(x=v-u\) are
\[
\alpha-\varepsilon,\qquad
\alpha-100-\varepsilon,\qquad
\alpha-101-\varepsilon=-\delta.
\]
Thus \(\delta>0\) and \(\min_Px=-\delta\).

For the two \(v\)-profiles, the horizontal slice lengths of \(P\) and \(Q\) are
\[
\ell_P(v)=\frac{\alpha-v}{100}
\quad(\alpha-100\le v\le\alpha),
\]
\[
\ell_Q(v)=w\,\frac{202-\beta-v}{202}
\quad(-\beta\le v\le202-\beta).
\]
The positive \(v\)-cap of \(P\) has area
\[
p:=|P\cap\{v\ge0\}|
=\frac{\alpha^2}{200}
=\frac{20000}{40401}.
\]
The negative \(v\)-cap of \(Q\) has the same area:
\[
|Q\cap\{v\le0\}|
=w\beta-\frac{w\beta^2}{404}
=\frac{100}{201}-\frac{100}{201^2}
=\frac{20000}{40401}
=p.
\]
Consequently
\[
|Q\cap\{v\ge0\}|=m-p
\]
and
\[
|P\cap\{v\ge0\}|+|Q\cap\{v\ge0\}|=m.
\]
Since \(0<p<m\), both caps in this equality are proper and have area strictly between \(0\) and \(m\).

We now verify the full left side of this shifted profile.  At \(v=0\),
\[
\ell_Q(0)-\ell_P(0)
=\frac{4975}{20301}-\frac{20}{201}
=\frac{985}{6767}>0,
\]
and \(\beta<\alpha\).  Let \(s<0\) and put \(t=-s>0\).  If \(0<t<\beta\), the area gained by the \(Q\)-cap when its threshold moves from \(0\) to \(-t\) is
\[
\int_0^t\ell_Q(-\tau)\,d\tau,
\]
whereas the area lost by the \(P\)-cap when its threshold moves from \(0\) to \(t\) is
\[
\int_0^t\ell_P(\tau)\,d\tau.
\]
The first integrand increases with \(\tau\), the second decreases, and their difference at \(0\) is \(985/6767>0\).  Hence the gain is larger than the loss.  If \(t\ge\beta\), then \(Q\cap\{v\ge-t\}=Q\), so the desired inequality is immediate.  Therefore, for every \(s<0\),
\[
|P\cap\{v\ge-s\}|+|Q\cap\{v\ge s\}|\ge m.
\]

The right side is strictly below \(m\) on an explicit interval.  For \(0\le\tau\le1\), the affine function
\[
\ell_Q(\tau)-\ell_P(-\tau)
\]
is decreasing, and at \(\tau=1\) it equals
\[
\frac{9181283}{68346700}>0.
\]
Thus, for every \(0<s\le1\),
\[
\begin{aligned}
&|P\cap\{v\ge-s\}|+|Q\cap\{v\ge s\}|-m\\
&\qquad=
\int_0^s\ell_P(-\tau)\,d\tau
-\int_0^s\ell_Q(\tau)\,d\tau
<0.
\end{aligned}
\]
This proves the complete proper-proper lower-crossing profile, including strictness on a positive interval to the right of zero.

For the \(u\)-profile, direct integration of the vertical slices of \(Q\) gives
\[
d_2=|Q\cap\{u\le0\}|
=202\gamma-\frac{202\gamma^2}{2w}
=\frac{1999}{40000}.
\]
In particular \(0<d_2<m\).  If \(s<0\), then \(P\cap\{u\le s\}\) is empty and
\[
Q\cap\{u\le0\}\subseteq Q\cap\{u\le-s\},
\]
so
\[
|P\cap\{u\le s\}|+|Q\cap\{u\le-s\}|\ge d_2.
\]
If \(0<s<\gamma=\varepsilon\), the \(P\)-term is still zero, while \(-s\) lies strictly between the minimum \(u\)-coordinate \(-\gamma\) of \(Q\) and \(0\).  The intervening slices of \(Q\) have positive length, and hence
\[
|Q\cap\{u\le-s\}|<|Q\cap\{u\le0\}|=d_2.
\]
This proves the complete empty-proper lower-crossing profile, again with strictness on a positive interval.

It remains to verify the far \(x\)-cap.  Write a point of the untranslated right triangle underlying \(Q\) as \((U,V)\), where
\[
U\ge0,\qquad V\ge0,\qquad \frac Uw+\frac V{202}\le1.
\]
For the translated triangle,
\[
x=v-u=V-U+\gamma-\beta.
\]
Thus \(x\ge\delta+2\) is equivalent to
\[
V-U\ge L,\qquad
L:=\delta+2-\gamma+\beta
=103-\alpha+\beta
=\frac{6369}{67}.
\]
Since \(0<L<202\), the cap is the triangle cut off between \(V-U=L\), \(U=0\), and the hypotenuse of \(Q\).  Its exact area is
\[
\begin{aligned}
b
&=\frac{w(202-L)^2}{2(w+202)}\\
&=\frac{25\cdot7165^2}{2\cdot20427\cdot67^2}\\
&=\frac{1283430625}{183393606}.
\end{aligned}
\]
The scalar conditions hold because \(a>d_2\) and
\[
0<a<m,\qquad
a+d_2=\frac{791999}{40000}<25=m,
\]
\[
4\min\{a,d_2\}+5\max\{a,d_2\}
=4d_2+5a
=\frac{989499}{10000}
<100=4m.
\]
Also
\[
b-(m-a)
=b-\frac{21}{4}
=\frac{641228387}{366787212}>0.
\]
Hence \(b>m-a\), as required.

Finally, translations do not affect Minkowski-sum area.  Removing the translations, write
\[
P_0=\operatorname{conv}\{(0,0),(0,-100),(1,-100)\},
\]
\[
Q_0=\operatorname{conv}\left\{(0,0),\left(\frac{25}{101},0\right),(0,202)\right\}.
\]
Their Minkowski sum is the quadrilateral
\[
P_0+Q_0
=\operatorname{conv}\left\{
(0,-100),\
(0,202),\
(1,102),\
\left(\frac{126}{101},-100\right)
\right\}.
\]
The shoelace formula gives
\[
|P+Q|=|P_0+Q_0|=277.
\]
Therefore
\[
\left|\frac{P+Q}{2}\right|
=\frac{|P+Q|}{4}
=\frac{277}{4}
<75
=50\cdot\frac32
=M\,U(r).
\]
Thus all displayed hypotheses hold while the reverse weak inequality, in fact a strict reverse inequality, holds.
