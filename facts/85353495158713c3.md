## Statement

Let
\[
\mathcal L=\{(x,y):x\le-|y|\},\qquad
\mathcal R=\{(x,y):x\ge|y|\},\qquad
\mathcal U=\{(x,y):y\ge|x|\}.
\]
Take the integral polygons
\[
A_0=
\{(x,y): |y|\le1344,\ -2021\le x\le-1-|y|\},
\]
\[
A_2=\operatorname{conv}\{(0,0),(2087,-12),(2087,12)\},
\qquad
K=[-1043,1043]\times[-678,678].
\]
Put
\[
B=\frac{A_0+A_2}{2},\qquad
M=|A_0|,\quad m=|A_2|,\quad k=|K|,
\quad t=\frac{2(M+m+k)}9,
\]
\[
\delta=2021,\qquad
C=|K\cap\{x\le1\}|,\qquad
v_1=|A_2\cap\{x\le\delta+2\}|,\qquad
\pi=t-m,
\]
\[
Y=K\cap\mathcal U\cap\{x\ge1\},\qquad
G=\operatorname{conv}(B\cup Y).
\]
Then
\[
A_0\subseteq\mathcal L,\quad A_2\subseteq\mathcal R,\quad
B\subseteq K,\quad M>k>m>0,\quad 0<t<M,
\]
\[
\min_{A_0}x=-\delta,\qquad
\min_{A_2}(x+y)=\min_{A_2}(x-y)=0,
\]
\[
|K\cap\{x+y\ge0\}|=|K\cap\{x-y\ge0\}|=\pi.
\]
Both diagonal contacts are genuine lower crossings, the two strict
absolute-level inequalities
\[
C+m>t,\qquad C+v_1<t
\]
hold, the forced tail is positive and has the required midpoint
containment, and both inherited hull-excess budgets hold.  Moreover,
for
\[
D(x,y)=(u,v)=(x+y,x-y),\qquad
\alpha=-\min_{D(K)}u,\quad\beta=-\min_{D(K)}v,
\]
one has
\[
7C+2\pi-2k-4\alpha\beta=-4\,766\,332<0.
\]

Let
\[
P=-D(A_0),\qquad Q=D(A_2),\qquad S=D(B).
\]
The actual contact points are
\[
q_u=(0,\sigma)=(0,0),\qquad
q_v=(\tau,0)=(0,0).
\]
For
\[
T_\tau=
\left|P\cap\{p_u+p_v\ge\tau-4\}\right|,
\]
\[
U_\sigma=
\left|P\cap
\{p_u+p_v\ge\sigma-4,\ p_v\le\sigma\}\right|,
\]
and
\[
Y'=D(K)\cap\{u\ge0,\ v\le0,\ u+v\ge2\},
\]
one has
\[
T_\tau+5U_\sigma=7\,246\,848
<7\,333\,264=16|Y'|.
\]
Thus the registered localized inequality is false.

For the local-gap rearrangement, quantify as follows.  For every pair
of Lebesgue-measurable sets \(S_0,Y_0\subseteq\mathbb R^2\) of finite
area and every \(T,U\in[0,\infty)\), define
\[
Y_{\mathrm{in}}=S_0\cap Y_0,\qquad
Y_{\mathrm{miss}}=Y_0\setminus S_0.
\]
Then
\[
T+5U\ge16|Y_0|
\quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad
|Y_{\mathrm{miss}}|
\le
\frac{T+5U}{16}-|Y_{\mathrm{in}}|.
\tag{LG}
\]
In the canonical notation above, take
\[
S_0=D(B),\qquad Y_0=Y',\qquad
T=T_\tau,\qquad U=U_\sigma.
\]
Thus \((\mathrm{LG})\) is exactly the localized inequality after
retaining the term \(|D(B)\cap Y'|\).

## Proof

The displayed description of \(A_0\) is the convex pentagon with
cyclic vertices
\[
(-2021,-1344),\quad(-1345,-1344),\quad(-1,0),\quad
(-1345,1344),\quad(-2021,1344).
\]
Every point of \(A_0\) satisfies \(x\le-1-|y|<-|y|\), so
\(A_0\subseteq\mathcal L\).  A vertical section of \(A_2\) at
\(0\le x\le2087\) is
\[
\left[-\frac{12x}{2087},\frac{12x}{2087}\right],
\]
so \(A_2\subseteq\mathcal R\).

The coordinate ranges in the Minkowski sum give
\[
-2021\le x(A_0)+x(A_2)\le-1+2087=2086
\]
and
\[
-1356\le y(A_0)+y(A_2)\le1356.
\]
Therefore
\[
B\subseteq[-1043,1043]\times[-678,678]=K.
\]
Direct integration and triangle and rectangle area formulas give
\[
M=2(1344)(2020)-1344^2=3\,623\,424,
\]
\[
m=(2087)(12)=25\,044,\qquad
k=(2086)(1356)=2\,828\,616.
\]
Set
\[
\pi=1\,414\,308,\qquad t=m+\pi=1\,439\,352.
\]
Then
\[
2(M+m+k)=12\,954\,168=9t,
\]
so this is exactly the canonical value
\(t=2(M+m+k)/9\).  The displayed values also give
\(M>k>m>0\) and \(0<t<M\).

The rectangle \(K\) is centrally symmetric and both diagonal lines
pass through its center.  Hence
\[
|K\cap\{x+y\ge0\}|
=|K\cap\{x-y\ge0\}|=\frac k2=\pi.
\]
On \(A_0\), both \(x+y\) and \(x-y\) are at most \(-1\).
For every \(0<a<1\), therefore,
\[
A_0\cap\{x+y\ge-a\}
=A_0\cap\{x-y\ge-a\}=\varnothing.
\]
The origin is the unique point of \(A_2\) on either diagonal boundary,
and every strip
\[
A_2\cap\{0\le x+y<a\},\qquad
A_2\cap\{0\le x-y<a\}
\]
has positive area.  Consequently
\[
\pi+|A_0\cap\{x+y\ge-a\}|
+|A_2\cap\{x+y\ge a\}|<\pi+m=t
\]
and the identical strict inequality holds with \(x-y\).  Thus both
contacts are genuine lower crossings, including their one-sided
strictness.

Since
\[
C=2(678)(1044)=1\,415\,664,
\]
one has
\[
C-\pi=1356,\qquad C+m-t=1356>0.
\]
The forced tail is
\[
A_2^+=A_2\cap\{x\ge\delta+2\}
=A_2\cap\{x\ge2023\}.
\]
Similarity of the removed triangle gives
\[
\theta:=|A_2^+|
=12\left(2(64)-\frac{64^2}{2087}\right)
=\frac{3\,156\,480}{2087}.
\]
Thus \(v_1=m-\theta\), and
\[
t-C-v_1
=\theta-(C-\pi)
=\frac{326\,508}{2087}>0.
\]
This proves both strict absolute-level inequalities and
\(\theta>C-\pi>0\).  For \(a_0\in A_0\) and \(a_2\in A_2^+\),
\[
x\left(\frac{a_0+a_2}{2}\right)
\ge\frac{-2021+2023}{2}=1.
\]
Hence the positive forced tail has the required containment
\[
\frac{A_0+A_2^+}{2}\subseteq B\cap\{x\ge1\}.
\]

For completeness, the cyclic vertices of \(B\) are
\[
\begin{aligned}
&\left(-\frac{2021}{2},-672\right),(33,-678),(371,-678),
(1043,-6),\\
&(1043,6),(371,678),(33,678),
\left(-\frac{2021}{2},672\right).
\end{aligned}
\]
Since
\[
Y=\operatorname{conv}\{(1,1),(1,678),(678,678)\},
\]
the cyclic vertices of \(G=\operatorname{conv}(B\cup Y)\) are
\[
\begin{aligned}
&\left(-\frac{2021}{2},-672\right),(33,-678),(371,-678),
(1043,-6),\\
&(1043,6),(678,678),(1,678),
\left(-\frac{2021}{2},672\right).
\end{aligned}
\]
The shoelace formula gives
\[
|B|=2\,326\,701,\qquad |G|=2\,429\,949,
\qquad |G\setminus B|=103\,248.
\]
On the other hand,
\[
\pi-\frac m4=1\,408\,047.
\]
For each of
\[
H_u=\{x+y\ge0\},\qquad H_v=\{x-y\ge0\},
\]
the relevant excess is bounded by the total excess:
\[
|(G\setminus B)\cap H_u|,
\ |(G\setminus B)\cap H_v|
\le103\,248<1\,408\,047=\pi-\frac m4.
\]
Thus both inherited hull-excess constraints hold.

The four vertices of \(D(K)\) show
\[
\alpha=\beta=1043+678=1721.
\]
Substitution gives
\[
7C+2\pi-2k-4\alpha\beta
=-4\,766\,332<0,
\]
so the additional canonical scalar sign is retained as well.

The exact polygon \(P=-D(A_0)\) has cyclic vertices
\[
(1,1),\quad(2689,1),\quad(3365,677),\quad
(677,3365),\quad(1,2689),
\]
and
\[
|P|=2M=7\,246\,848.
\]
Also
\[
Q=\operatorname{conv}\{(0,0),(2075,2099),(2099,2075)\}.
\]
Its two actual contact faces meet only at the origin, so
\(\sigma=\tau=0\).  Since \(p_u,p_v\ge1\) throughout \(P\),
\[
T_\tau=|P|=7\,246\,848,\qquad U_\sigma=0.
\]
The translation vector between the two contact copies is
\[
d=q_v-q_u=(0,0).
\]
Thus the two copies coincide,
\[
\frac{q_u-P}{2}=\frac{q_v-P}{2}=-\frac P2,
\]
and their exact clipped-overlap quantity is maximal:
\[
\left|\{p\in P:p+d\in P,\ p_u+p_v\ge-4\}\right|
=|P|=7\,246\,848.
\]

Applying \(D\) to \(Y\) gives
\[
Y'=\operatorname{conv}\{(2,0),(679,-677),(1356,0)\},
\qquad |Y'|=458\,329.
\]
Therefore
\[
16|Y'|=7\,333\,264
>7\,246\,848=T_\tau+5U_\sigma,
\]
with exact deficit \(86\,416\).

It remains to retain the forced midpoint body and locate the failure.
Put
\[
Q^+=D(A_2^+),\qquad
R=\frac{Q^+-P}{2}.
\]
The preceding midpoint argument gives
\[
R\subseteq S\cap\{u+v\ge2\}.
\]
Exact Minkowski addition gives the following cyclic vertices of \(R\):
\[
\begin{aligned}
&\left(-\frac{1\,412\,515}{2087},
\frac{1\,416\,689}{2087}\right),(-645,711),(-307,1049),
(1037,1049),\\
&(1049,1037),(1049,-307),(711,-645),
\left(\frac{1\,416\,689}{2087},
-\frac{1\,412\,515}{2087}\right).
\end{aligned}
\]
Clipping either \(R\) or \(S=D(B)\) by \(Y'\) gives the same cyclic
polygon
\[
\begin{aligned}
\operatorname{conv}\biggl\{&(2,0),
\left(\frac{1\,416\,689}{2087},
-\frac{1\,412\,515}{2087}\right),
(711,-645),\\
&(1049,-307),(1049,0)\biggr\}.
\end{aligned}
\]
Thus the forced midpoint body is retained exactly:
\[
R\cap Y'=S\cap Y'.
\]
The shoelace formula gives
\[
|S\cap Y'|=\frac{1\,716\,343\,007}{4174},
\qquad
|Y'\setminus S|=\frac{196\,722\,239}{4174}.
\]
The right side of the local-gap rearrangement is
\[
\frac{T_\tau+5U_\sigma}{16}-|S\cap Y'|
=\frac{174\,178\,465}{4174}.
\]
Hence this counterexample violates \((\mathrm{LG})\) by
\[
\frac{196\,722\,239-174\,178\,465}{4174}=5401.
\]
Multiplication by \(16\) recovers the exact localized deficit
\(16(5401)=86\,416\).

It remains only to prove the quantified rearrangement.  Let
\(S_0,Y_0\subseteq\mathbb R^2\) be Lebesgue-measurable sets of finite
area and let \(T,U\in[0,\infty)\).  By definition,
\[
Y_0=(S_0\cap Y_0)\mathbin{\dot\cup}(Y_0\setminus S_0)
=Y_{\mathrm{in}}\mathbin{\dot\cup}Y_{\mathrm{miss}}.
\]
Finite additivity gives
\[
|Y_0|=|Y_{\mathrm{in}}|+|Y_{\mathrm{miss}}|.
\]
Therefore
\[
\begin{aligned}
T+5U\ge16|Y_0|
&\Longleftrightarrow
\frac{T+5U}{16}
\ge |Y_{\mathrm{in}}|+|Y_{\mathrm{miss}}|\\
&\Longleftrightarrow
|Y_{\mathrm{miss}}|
\le\frac{T+5U}{16}-|Y_{\mathrm{in}}|.
\end{aligned}
\]
This proves \((\mathrm{LG})\), and substituting
\(S_0=D(B)\), \(Y_0=Y'\), \(T=T_\tau\), and \(U=U_\sigma\)
gives the stated local-gap form of the localized inequality.
