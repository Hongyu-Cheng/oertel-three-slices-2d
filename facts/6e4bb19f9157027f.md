## Statement

Assume the complete canonical finite-interior common-plane hypotheses, exact-cell convention, \(M\geq m\), the three crossing equalities, the canonical tails, the all-double positive \(Q\)-support, \(r_1r_2r_3r_{123}>0\), and \(W=0\).  Put
\[
q=q_{123}>0,\qquad
q_{N\setminus\{i\}}=\frac{5q}{21}\quad(i=1,2,3).
\]
Write
\[
p_0=p_\varnothing,\qquad
s_P=\sum_i p_{\{i\}},\qquad
d_P=\sum_{i<j}p_{\{i,j\}},
\]
\[
s_R=\sum_i r_{\{i\}},\qquad
d_R=\sum_{i<j}r_{\{i,j\}},\qquad
r=r_{123},
\]
\[
w_P=s_P+4d_P,\qquad
\eta=w_P+3d_R+6r.
\]
Let
\[
P=\overline{P_\varnothing},\qquad
C_i=\overline{Q_{N\setminus\{i\}}},\qquad
B_i=\frac{P+C_i}{2}.
\]
All closures are taken in the common affine plane; they do not change planar areas.  Define
\[
\mathcal V(P,C_i)
=\frac{|P+C_i|-|P|-|C_i|}{2},
\tag{1}
\]
\[
\mathcal U=B_1\cup B_2\cup B_3,
\qquad
\mathcal I=
\sum_{1\leq i<j\leq3}|B_i\cap B_j|,
\]
and the full-body slack
\[
\Xi=
\bigl(s_R+d_R-|\mathcal U|\bigr)
+
\bigl(s_R-\mathcal I\bigr).
\tag{2}
\]
Then \(\Xi\geq0\), and the following exact identities hold:
\[
2s_R+d_R
=
\frac{3p_0}{4}
+\frac{5q}{28}
+\frac12\sum_{i=1}^3\mathcal V(P,C_i)
+\Xi,
\tag{3}
\]
\[
7\eta
=
\frac{39p_0}{4}
+w_P
-\frac{1671q}{28}
-\frac32\sum_{i=1}^3\mathcal V(P,C_i)
-3\Xi.
\tag{4}
\]
Accepted fact `0009896ebff12f31` gives \(\eta>6q/7\) at these ratios.  Therefore every \(W=0\) realization necessarily satisfies the strict shape inequality
\[
\frac32\sum_{i=1}^3\mathcal V(P,C_i)+3\Xi
<
\frac{39p_0}{4}+w_P-\frac{1839q}{28}.
\tag{5}
\]
Consequently the registered sharp-ratio subgoal is closed by the single body-level estimate
\[
\boxed{\quad
\frac32\sum_{i=1}^3\mathcal V(P,C_i)+3\Xi
\geq
\frac{39p_0}{4}+w_P-\frac{1839q}{28}.
\quad}
\tag{6}
\]
This reduction includes every endpoint-coincidence, collinear-label, redundant-vertex, and exact-cell cutting-line boundary allowed by the hypotheses.

## Proof

The compatibility condition
\[
\frac{A_0+A_2}{2}\subseteq K
\]
is the exact slice-convexity condition of accepted fact `8ad0785e5b4ae9ef`.  Hence \(B_i\subseteq K\).

We next use the exact incidence statement of accepted fact `0e51a47a9add571f`.  If \(z\in B_i\), write \(z=(x+y)/2\) with \(x\in P\) and \(y\in C_i\).  Since \(P\) is the closure of the empty-index \(P\)-cell,
\[
f_i(x)-L_i\geq0.
\]
Since \(i\notin N\setminus\{i\}\) and \(C_i\) is the closure of that \(Q\)-cell,
\[
f_i(y)+L_i\geq0.
\]
Affineness gives
\[
2f_i(z)=\bigl(f_i(x)-L_i\bigr)+\bigl(f_i(y)+L_i\bigr)\geq0.
\tag{7}
\]
Thus every \(B_i\) lies in the closed halfplane \(f_i\geq0\).

There can be no triple intersection: a point of \(B_1\cap B_2\cap B_3\) would satisfy \(f_i\geq0\) for all \(i\), contradicting \(f_1+f_2+f_3=-1\).  Up to the null cutting lines, \(B_i\cap B_j\) lies in the remaining exact singleton cell \(R_{\{k\}}\), where \(\{i,j,k\}=N\).  The three pairwise intersections are mutually disjoint because their common intersection is empty.  Likewise, \(\mathcal U\) avoids the exact central cell \(R_{123}\) up to cutting lines, because every point of \(\mathcal U\) has some \(f_i\geq0\).  It follows that
\[
|\mathcal U|\leq s_R+d_R,\qquad
\mathcal I\leq s_R.
\]
This proves \(\Xi\geq0\).

Inclusion-exclusion and the empty triple intersection give
\[
\sum_i|B_i|=|\mathcal U|+\mathcal I.
\]
Using (2),
\[
2s_R+d_R=\sum_i|B_i|+\Xi.
\]
By the definition (1), \(|P|=p_0\), \(|C_i|=5q/21\), and planar area scaling under division by two,
\[
|B_i|
=\frac14|P+C_i|
=\frac{p_0}{4}+\frac{5q}{84}
+\frac12\mathcal V(P,C_i).
\]
Summing proves (3).

It remains to combine the body ledger with \(W=0\).  At the fixed ratios, the \(Q\)-contribution to \(W\) is
\[
4\sum_i q_{N\setminus\{i\}}+7q
=4\left(3\frac{5q}{21}\right)+7q
=\frac{69q}{7}.
\]
The \(P\)- and \(R\)-contributions are
\[
-2p_0+w_P,\qquad s_R+4d_R+7r.
\]
Since
\[
k=s_R+d_R+r,\qquad
\eta=w_P+3d_R+6r,
\]
the equation \(W=0\) is exactly
\[
2p_0=\frac{69q}{7}+k+\eta.
\]
Also
\[
d_R+2r=\frac{\eta-w_P}{3},
\qquad
2s_R+d_R=2k-(d_R+2r).
\]
Eliminating \(k,d_R,r\) gives
\[
2s_R+d_R
=4p_0-\frac{138q}{7}-\frac{7\eta}{3}+\frac{w_P}{3}.
\]
Equating this formula with (3), multiplying by \(3\), and collecting terms proves (4).

At \(\lambda_i=5/21\), accepted fact `0009896ebff12f31` computes the exact positive-middle defect \(\epsilon=6/7\) and proves
\[
\eta>\frac{6q}{7}.
\]
Substitution in (4) gives (5).  Conversely, (6) and (4) imply \(7\eta\leq6q\), contradicting that accepted strict defect.  This proves the stated reduction.

Finally, accepted fact `c49613ac26c0c4a8` reduces every positive cap \(C_i\), without changing its chord endpoints or area, to the explicit triangle
\[
\operatorname{conv}\{p_{i,i^-},u_i,p_{i,i^+}\}.
\]
Its eight endpoint-coincidence strata, and all collinear or redundant central labels, are retained.  Formulae (1)-(6) use the actual convex bodies and planar areas, make no division by an endpoint or side-gap parameter, and are unchanged on null cutting lines.  Thus the boxed target is valid on every registered boundary stratum.  It is the first missing convex-hull estimate: one must lower-bound the mixed-area term together with the exact coverage/overlap slack \(\Xi\); neither term is present in the earlier scalar tables.
