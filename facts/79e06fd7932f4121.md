## Statement

Use the normalized \(A_2\) coordinates and cell convention of accepted fact `fa0e57b225178b3b`. For the rational survivor of accepted fact `d1a8755c0cc50d14`, put
\[
S=3,\qquad T=S-U+V,\qquad \Delta=1.
\]
There is a compact two-dimensional rational convex polygon \(\mathcal P\) whose three full cutting-line chords are
\[
\mathcal P\cap\{U=0\}
=\left\{(0,V):\frac{561}{100000}\le V\le\frac{1061}{100000}\right\},
\]
\[
\mathcal P\cap\{V=0\}
=\left\{(U,0):\frac9{10}\le U\le\frac{171}{100}\right\},
\]
\[
\mathcal P\cap\{T=0\}
=\left\{(3-t,-t):\frac{23}{2000}\le t\le\frac{63}{5000}\right\}.
\]
Thus their offsets and lengths are exactly
\[
v=\frac{561}{100000},\quad J_2=\frac1{200},\qquad
u=\frac9{10},\quad H_2=\frac{81}{100},\qquad
w_0=\frac{23}{2000},\quad K_2=\frac{11}{10000}.
\]
The only positive-area strict cells of \(\mathcal P\) are
\[
\begin{array}{c|ccc|c}
 &U&V&T&\text{area}\\ \hline
Q_j&<0&>0&>0&1/5000\\
Q_{jk}&>0&>0&>0&7/1000\\
Q_N&>0&<0&>0&31/5000\\
Q_{k\ell}&>0&<0&<0&3/20000.
\end{array}
\]
Every unsupported strict cell is empty.

## Proof

In counterclockwise cyclic order, take the following ten vertices:
\[
\begin{array}{c|cc}
i&U_i&V_i\\ \hline
0&14937/5000&-63/5000\\
1&179219/55000&-71/5000\\
2&5977/2000&-23/2000\\
3&10733/4600&-1/200\\
4&171/100&0\\
5&17/20&2229/380000\\
6&0&1061/100000\\
7&-2/25&811/100000\\
8&0&561/100000\\
9&9/10&0.
\end{array}
\tag{1}
\]
Write these points as \(v_0,\ldots,v_9\), with indices modulo \(10\).

We first give a complete supporting-edge audit. For every oriented edge \(v_iv_{i+1}\), define
\[
D_i(r)=\det(v_{i+1}-v_i,v_r-v_i).
\]
Exact rational substitution gives \(D_i(i)=D_i(i+1)=0\). The exact minimum over every nonincident listed vertex is
\[
\begin{array}{c|c|c}
\text{edge}&\text{one vertex attaining the minimum}
&\min_{r\ne i,i+1}D_i(r)\\ \hline
v_0v_1&v_9&21/275000\\
v_1v_2&v_3&1767/126500000\\
v_2v_3&v_1&1767/126500000\\
v_3v_4&v_5&1125857/1748000000\\
v_4v_5&v_3&1125857/1748000000\\
v_5v_6&v_4&9059/10000000\\
v_6v_7&v_8&1/2500\\
v_7v_8&v_6&1/2500\\
v_8v_9&v_0&185157/500000000\\
v_9v_0&v_1&21/275000.
\end{array}
\tag{2}
\]
Every entry is positive. Hence every displayed edge strictly supports all nonincident vertices on its left. Therefore (1) is the complete boundary cycle of a compact two-dimensional convex rational decagon.

The three cutting forms at the vertices are
\[
\begin{array}{c|c|c|c}
i&U_i&V_i&T_i=3-U_i+V_i\\ \hline
0&14937/5000&-63/5000&0\\
1&179219/55000&-71/5000&-3/11\\
2&5977/2000&-23/2000&0\\
3&10733/4600&-1/200&761/1150\\
4&171/100&0&129/100\\
5&17/20&2229/380000&819229/380000\\
6&0&1061/100000&301061/100000\\
7&-2/25&811/100000&308811/100000\\
8&0&561/100000&300561/100000\\
9&9/10&0&21/10.
\end{array}
\tag{3}
\]
Thus \(U\) is negative only at \(v_7\), with neighboring zero vertices \(v_6,v_8\); \(V\) changes sign only through the zero vertices \(v_4,v_9\); and \(T\) is negative only at \(v_1\), with neighboring zero vertices \(v_0,v_2\). Convexity and (3) therefore give the full, rather than merely contained, sections
\[
\mathcal P\cap\{U=0\}=[v_8,v_6],\qquad
\mathcal P\cap\{V=0\}=[v_9,v_4],\qquad
\mathcal P\cap\{T=0\}=[v_0,v_2].
\tag{4}
\]
The first two segments in (4) are exactly the first two asserted intervals. Moreover
\[
v_2=\left(3-\frac{23}{2000},-\frac{23}{2000}\right),\qquad
v_0=\left(3-\frac{63}{5000},-\frac{63}{5000}\right),
\]
so the last segment is the asserted \(t\)-interval. The three chord lengths obtained by endpoint subtraction are \(1/200\), \(81/100\), and \(11/10000\).

Clipping the decagon by the three lines in (4) gives exactly
\[
\begin{aligned}
\overline{Q_{k\ell}}\cap\mathcal P
 &=\operatorname{conv}(v_0,v_1,v_2),\\
\overline{Q_N}\cap\mathcal P
 &=\operatorname{conv}(v_2,v_3,v_4,v_9,v_0),\\
\overline{Q_{jk}}\cap\mathcal P
 &=\operatorname{conv}(v_4,v_5,v_6,v_8,v_9),\\
\overline{Q_j}\cap\mathcal P
 &=\operatorname{conv}(v_6,v_7,v_8).
\end{aligned}
\tag{5}
\]
The sign table (3) places the relative interior of each polygon in (5) in its claimed strict chamber. These four polygons cover \(\mathcal P\) and meet only along cutting-line segments. Hence every unsupported strict chamber is empty.

It remains to verify all four areas. Exact shoelace evaluation gives
\[
\left|\operatorname{conv}(v_0,v_1,v_2)\right|
=\frac3{20000},
\qquad
\left|\operatorname{conv}(v_6,v_7,v_8)\right|
=\frac1{5000}.
\tag{6}
\]
For the \(Q_N\) cell, split off the boundary bulge triangle:
\[
\left|\operatorname{conv}(v_2,v_4,v_9,v_0)\right|
=\frac{93}{16000},
\qquad
\left|\operatorname{conv}(v_2,v_3,v_4)\right|
=\frac{31}{80000}.
\]
Their interiors are disjoint and their common part is their base edge, so
\[
|Q_N|=\frac{93}{16000}+\frac{31}{80000}
=\frac{31}{5000}.
\tag{7}
\]
Likewise,
\[
\left|\operatorname{conv}(v_4,v_6,v_8,v_9)\right|
=\frac{130941}{20000000},
\qquad
\left|\operatorname{conv}(v_4,v_5,v_6)\right|
=\frac{9059}{20000000},
\]
and therefore
\[
|Q_{jk}|=\frac{130941+9059}{20000000}
=\frac7{1000}.
\tag{8}
\]
The four values in (6)-(8) sum to
\[
\frac1{5000}+\frac7{1000}+\frac{31}{5000}
+\frac3{20000}
=\frac{271}{20000},
\]
which is also the direct shoelace area of the full decagon. Since \(\Delta=1\), these coordinate areas are the required physical cell areas. Equations (2), (4), (5), and (6)-(8) complete the supporting-edge, full-chord, clipping, unsupported-cell, and area audit.
