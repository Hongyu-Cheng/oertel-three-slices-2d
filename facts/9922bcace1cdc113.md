## Statement

Let \(N=\{1,2,3\}\). Let \(f_1,f_2,f_3\) be nonconstant affine functions on \(\mathbb R^2\), with pairwise nonparallel linear parts, such that
\[
f_1+f_2+f_3=-1.
\]
Let \(L_1+L_2+L_3<-1\), and let \(A_0,A_2,K\) be nonempty two-dimensional convex polytopes satisfying
\[
(A_0+A_2)/2\subseteq K.
\]
Fix \(j\in N\) and write \(N\setminus\{j\}=\{k,\ell\}\). Define
\[
P_0=A_0\cap\{f_i>L_i\text{ for every }i\in N\},
\]
\[
Q_{\{j\}}
=
A_2\cap
\{f_j\le-L_j,\ f_k>-L_k,\ f_\ell>-L_\ell\},
\]
\[
Q_{\{k,\ell\}}
=
A_2\cap
\{f_k\le-L_k,\ f_\ell\le-L_\ell,\ f_j>-L_j\},
\]
and assume that all three sets have positive planar area. Put
\[
E=\overline{Q_{\{j\}}},
\qquad
F=\overline{Q_{\{k,\ell\}}},
\qquad
H=\operatorname{conv}(E\cup F),
\]
where closures are taken in \(\mathbb R^2\), and put
\[
p_0=|P_0|,
\qquad
q_j=|Q_{\{j\}}|,
\qquad
q_{k\ell}=|Q_{\{k,\ell\}}|,
\]
\[
s_j=
\left|
\frac{P_0+H}{2}\cap\{-1<f_j<0\}
\right|.
\]
Then
\[
\left|\frac{P_0+H}{2}\right|
\ge
\frac14(\sqrt{p_0}+\sqrt{q_j})^2
+
\frac14(\sqrt{p_0}+\sqrt{q_{k\ell}})^2
+s_j,
\]
and
\[
|K|>
\frac14(\sqrt{p_0}+\sqrt{q_j})^2
+
\frac14(\sqrt{p_0}+\sqrt{q_{k\ell}})^2.
\]

## Proof

The exact sign-cell convention of accepted fact `0e51a47a9add571f` uses weak inequalities on indexed coordinates and strict inequalities on the others. Since \(A_2\) is closed and the \(f_i\) are continuous,
\[
E\subseteq
A_2\cap
\{f_j\le-L_j,\ f_k\ge-L_k,\ f_\ell\ge-L_\ell\}.
\]
Consequently,
\[
E\setminus Q_{\{j\}}
\subseteq
\{f_k=-L_k\}\cup\{f_\ell=-L_\ell\}.
\]
Each set on the right is an affine line because the corresponding \(f_i\) is nonconstant, and hence has planar area zero. Since \(Q_{\{j\}}\subseteq E\),
\[
|E|=|Q_{\{j\}}|=q_j.
\tag{1}
\]
Similarly,
\[
F\subseteq
A_2\cap
\{f_k\le-L_k,\ f_\ell\le-L_\ell,\ f_j\ge-L_j\},
\]
so
\[
F\setminus Q_{\{k,\ell\}}
\subseteq
\{f_j=-L_j\},
\]
and therefore
\[
|F|=|Q_{\{k,\ell\}}|=q_{k\ell}.
\tag{2}
\]

The sets \(P_0,E,F\) are bounded measurable convex sets of positive area. The planar Brunn--Minkowski inequality applied to \(P_0\) and \(E\), followed by the area scaling under multiplication by \(1/2\), gives
\[
\left|\frac{P_0+E}{2}\right|
=
\frac14|P_0+E|
\ge
\frac14\bigl(\sqrt{|P_0|}+\sqrt{|E|}\bigr)^2
=
\frac14(\sqrt{p_0}+\sqrt{q_j})^2.
\tag{3}
\]
The same argument with \(F\) gives
\[
\left|\frac{P_0+F}{2}\right|
\ge
\frac14(\sqrt{p_0}+\sqrt{q_{k\ell}})^2.
\tag{4}
\]

Set
\[
U=\frac{P_0+E}{2},
\qquad
V=\frac{P_0+F}{2},
\qquad
D=\frac{P_0+H}{2}\cap\{-1<f_j<0\}.
\]
Accepted fact `c472ceb412ca0594` proves the strict endpoint locations
\[
U\subseteq\{f_j<-1\},
\qquad
V\subseteq\{f_j>0\},
\tag{5}
\]
and proves \(|D|=s_j>0\). By definition,
\[
D\subseteq\{-1<f_j<0\}.
\tag{6}
\]
Thus \(U,V,D\) are pairwise disjoint as sets. Moreover, \(E,F\subseteq H\), so
\[
U\mathbin{\dot\cup}V\mathbin{\dot\cup}D
\subseteq
\frac{P_0+H}{2}.
\tag{7}
\]
The two excluded band boundaries \(\{f_j=-1\}\) and \(\{f_j=0\}\) are affine lines and hence have planar area zero. In particular, the strict band convention loses no positive boundary area, while the disjointness in (7) is exact rather than merely up to null sets. Area additivity in (7), followed by (3) and (4), yields
\[
\left|\frac{P_0+H}{2}\right|
\ge
|U|+|V|+|D|
\ge
\frac14(\sqrt{p_0}+\sqrt{q_j})^2
+
\frac14(\sqrt{p_0}+\sqrt{q_{k\ell}})^2
+s_j.
\tag{8}
\]

Finally, \(E,F\subseteq A_2\), and convexity of \(A_2\) gives \(H\subseteq A_2\). Since \(P_0\subseteq A_0\),
\[
\frac{P_0+H}{2}
\subseteq
\frac{A_0+A_2}{2}
\subseteq K.
\]
Combining this inclusion with (8) and \(s_j>0\) gives
\[
|K|
>
\frac14(\sqrt{p_0}+\sqrt{q_j})^2
+
\frac14(\sqrt{p_0}+\sqrt{q_{k\ell}})^2.
\]
Only the two endpoint images and \(D\) were charged; no assertion was made about any other portion of \((P_0+H)/2\).
