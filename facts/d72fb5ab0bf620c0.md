## Statement

Let \(N=\{1,2,3\}\).  Let \(A_0,A_2,K\subset\mathbb R^2\) be nonempty
two-dimensional convex polytopes with
\[
\frac{A_0+A_2}{2}\subseteq K.
\]
Let \(f_1,f_2,f_3\) be nonconstant affine functions with pairwise nonparallel
linear parts, let
\[
f_1+f_2+f_3=-1,
\]
and let the real thresholds \(\Lambda_i\) satisfy
\[
s:=\Lambda_1+\Lambda_2+\Lambda_3<-1.
\]
Use the common-plane exact-cell convention
\[
P_J=A_0\cap
\{f_i\leq\Lambda_i\ (i\in J),\ f_i>\Lambda_i\ (i\notin J)\},
\]
\[
Q_H=A_2\cap
\{f_i\leq-\Lambda_i\ (i\in H),\ f_i>-\Lambda_i\ (i\notin H)\},
\]
\[
R_G=K\cap
\{f_i\leq0\ (i\in G),\ f_i>0\ (i\notin G)\},
\]
and write their areas as \(p_J,q_H,r_G\).  Put
\[
M=\sum_Jp_J,\qquad m=\sum_Hq_H,\qquad k=\sum_Gr_G,
\]
\[
u_i=\sum_{J\ni i}p_J,\qquad
v_i=\sum_{H\ni i}q_H,\qquad
c_i=\sum_{G\ni i}r_G.
\]

Assume the complete canonical finite-interior branch and the exact
partial-union, overlap, source-cap, and middle-cap inequalities required in
accepted fact `084bf2f472bf29c7`.  In particular, assume
\[
M\geq m,\qquad p_\varnothing>0,\qquad p_N=0,\qquad r_\varnothing=0,
\]
\[
r_N>0,\qquad r_{\{1\}},r_{\{2\}},r_{\{3\}}>0,
\]
put
\[
h=\frac{2(M+m+k)}9,
\]
and assume the three crossings and the two canonical tails
\[
u_i+v_i+c_i=h,
\qquad
c_i+m\geq h,
\qquad
c_i+M\geq h
\quad(i=1,2,3).
\]
Assume also
\[
W:=
\sum_J(3|J|-2)p_J+
\sum_H(3|H|-2)q_H+
\sum_G(3|G|-2)r_G=0.
\]

Suppose the positive \(Q\)-support is exactly
\[
\{N,\{1,2\},\{1,3\},\{2,3\}\}.
\]
Write
\[
q=q_N>0,\qquad x_i=q_{N\setminus\{i\}}>0,
\qquad m=q+x_1+x_2+x_3,
\]
and suppose
\[
\frac9{20}\leq\frac{x_i}{q}\leq\frac{11}{20}
\qquad(i=1,2,3).
\tag{1}
\]
No endpoint genericity is assumed.  In the normal form of accepted fact
`c49613ac26c0c4a8`, all eight endpoint-pair strata
\[
(a_{ij},a_{ji})=(0,0)
\quad\hbox{or}\quad
a_{ij}>0,\ a_{ji}>0
\]
for \((i,j)=(1,2),(2,3),(3,1)\) are admitted, as are collinear and redundant
boundary labels.

Then no such full common-plane realization exists.  By accepted fact
`91cb5bfc71da49ac`, this excludes the \(W=0\) all-double positive-middle
branch and supplies the required strict all-double excess on this region.

## Proof

Define the ambient \(Q\)-triangle
\[
\mathcal T_Q=\{z\in\mathbb R^2:
f_i(z)\leq-\Lambda_i\ \text{for }i=1,2,3\}.
\]
Accepted fact `e40abbed940a40ab` shows that \(\mathcal T_Q\) is a
nondegenerate compact triangle.  Accepted fact `c49613ac26c0c4a8` gives the
endpoint-preserving normal form on every one of the eight endpoint strata.
In its barycentric coordinates,
\[
|\widehat Q_N|=\frac D2,\qquad
|\widehat Q_{N\setminus\{i\}}|
=\frac{Dx_i}{2q},
\qquad
|\mathcal T_Q|=\frac qD,
\tag{2}
\]
where
\[
D=\frac q{|\mathcal T_Q|}>0.
\tag{3}
\]

Apply accepted fact `6aa12d1b76699246` with its polygon equal to \(A_2\),
its central triangle equal to \(\mathcal T_Q\), its central cell equal to
\(Q_N\), and its \(i\)-th cap equal, up to null cutting-line pieces, to
\(Q_{N\setminus\{i\}}\).  This application is exact: the assumed \(Q\)-support
says that every other \(Q\)-cell has area zero, and an interior overlap of
two caps would have positive area in a forbidden \(Q\)-cell.  Thus the cap
interiors are pairwise disjoint and their area ratios are exactly
\[
\frac{x_i}{q}.
\]
The closed box (1), including all six ratio boundary faces and their
intersections, therefore gives
\[
D\geq\frac25
\tag{4}
\]
on every endpoint-coincidence stratum.  Combining (2)--(4) and \(q>0\)
gives
\[
|\mathcal T_Q|=\frac qD\leq\frac{5q}{2}.
\tag{5}
\]

Set
\[
U(q,x_1,x_2,x_3)=\frac{5q}{2}.
\]
Equation (5) is exactly the upper-bound hypothesis
\(|\mathcal T_Q|\leq U\) in accepted fact `084bf2f472bf29c7`.  All its other
hypotheses were included above.  Because \(r_N>0\), that accepted fact
retains strictness and implies from \(W=0\) that
\[
U>
\Phi(m,q):=
\left(
\frac{\sqrt m+\sqrt{120m+84q}}7
\right)^2.
\tag{6}
\]

We now compare the two sides exactly.  The closed ratio bounds (1) give
\[
\frac{47}{20}q
=q+3\frac9{20}q
\leq m
\leq q+3\frac{11}{20}q
=\frac{53}{20}q.
\tag{7}
\]
Since \(m,q>0\),
\[
\begin{aligned}
\Phi(m,q)
&=\frac{121m+84q+2\sqrt{m(120m+84q)}}{49}\\
&>\frac{120m+84q}{49}\\
&\geq
\frac{120(47q/20)+84q}{49}
=\frac{366}{49}q\\
&=\frac52q+\frac{487}{98}q
>\frac52q
=U.
\end{aligned}
\tag{8}
\]
Every comparison in (7) is non-strict, so all ratio-box boundary points are
retained.  The strict comparison in (8) comes from the positive terms
\(m+2\sqrt{m(120m+84q)}\), not from discarding a ratio endpoint.
Equations (6) and (8) are contradictory.

Finally,
\[
\frac{12}{25}-\frac9{20}=\frac3{100},
\qquad
\frac{11}{20}-\frac{13}{25}=\frac3{100}.
\]
Hence the originally requested closed box
\([12/25,13/25]^3\) is contained in the proved closed box
\([9/20,11/20]^3\).  Its fully noncoincident stratum is one of the eight
endpoint strata already covered.  Accepted fact `91cb5bfc71da49ac`
identifies exclusion of this \(W=0\) branch with the required all-double
strict-excess closure.
