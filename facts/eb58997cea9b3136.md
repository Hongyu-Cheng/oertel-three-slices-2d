## Statement

Use the exact rational realization of accepted fact `ac12ad62775a1d5f`, the variable order and full canonical equality matrix of accepted fact `b56843bf58782166`, and the first-event convention of accepted fact `42e17c270e11ad3e`. In the final cyclic list \((k_0,\ldots,k_{13})\) of \(K\), \(k_1\) is the first ear apex and \(k_0,k_2\) are its base endpoints.

Define the rational velocity \(\xi\) by
\[
\dot k_{1x}=2,\qquad \dot k_{1y}=-149,
\]
and set every other \(a_i\)-, \(\theta_i\)-, and \(k_j\)-coordinate velocity equal to zero. Then
\[
E_{\rm can}\xi=0.
\]
Moreover, the strict turn event
\[
\tau_0=[k_0-k_{13},\,k_1-k_0]
\]
satisfies
\[
D\tau_0[\xi]
=\frac{16208345651254381711}{100000000000000000}>0.
\]

## Proof

In the realization of accepted fact `ac12ad62775a1d5f`, write \(\lambda=50003/50000\). The first ear has
\[
k_0=k_0^0,\qquad k_2=k_1^0,
\]
and the definitions of \(k_j^0\) and \(b_j\) give
\[
k_2-k_0
=\lambda(b_1-b_0)
=\frac{\lambda}{2}(\pi_1-\pi_0)
=\frac{50003}{100000}(2,-149).
\tag{1}
\]
Thus the only moving vertex has velocity parallel to its ear base.

The block decomposition in accepted fact `b56843bf58782166` is
\[
E_{\rm can}=\operatorname{diag}(E_P,E_Q,E_R).
\]
Since all \(a_i\)- and \(\theta_i\)-velocities vanish,
\[
E_P\xi=(0,0,0,0)^{\mathsf T},
\qquad
E_Q\xi=(0,0,0,0)^{\mathsf T}.
\tag{2}
\]
The first ear triangle and both of its incident edges lie strictly in the \(R_{\{1,2\}}\) chamber. Hence the six \(E_R\)-rows indexed by
\[
\{1\},\{2\},\{3\},\{1,3\},\{2,3\},\{1,2,3\}
\]
do not depend on \(k_1\) on this fixed clipping code, and their pairings with \(\xi\) are zero. For the remaining row, only the two shoelace terms incident to \(k_1\) vary. With \(v=(2,-149)\),
\[
\begin{aligned}
D\mathcal A_{-1}^{\{1,2\}}(\mathcal K)[\xi]
&=\frac12\bigl([k_0,v]+[v,k_2]\bigr)\\
&=\frac12[k_0-k_2,v]
=0
\end{aligned}
\tag{3}
\]
by (1). Therefore, in the row order
\[
\{1\},\{2\},\{3\},\{1,2\},\{1,3\},\{2,3\},\{1,2,3\},
\]
one has
\[
E_R\xi=(0,0,0,0,0,0,0)^{\mathsf T}.
\tag{4}
\]
Equations (2)-(4) verify every one of the fifteen nonzero canonical equality rows exactly; every retained formal zero row also annihilates \(\xi\). Hence \(E_{\rm can}\xi=0\).

It remains to calculate a strict event differential. Polygon turn determinants belong to the first-event list in accepted fact `42e17c270e11ad3e`, and the strict audit in accepted fact `ac12ad62775a1d5f` gives \(\tau_0>1/1000\), so \(\tau_0\) is not an active equality. Since \(k_0\) and \(k_{13}\) are stationary,
\[
D\tau_0[\xi]=[k_0-k_{13},v].
\tag{5}
\]
Here \(k_{13}=k_6^0\), and
\[
k_0-k_{13}
=\lambda(b_0-b_6)
=\frac{\lambda}{2}(\theta_2-\theta_1).
\tag{6}
\]
The displayed rational vertices of the realization give
\[
\theta_2-\theta_1
=\left(
-\frac{133350172677}{10^{12}},
-\frac{152139144224182}{10^{12}}
\right)
\]
and therefore
\[
[\theta_2-\theta_1,(2,-149)]
=\frac{324147464177237}{10^{12}}.
\tag{7}
\]
The required integer product is, exactly,
\[
\begin{aligned}
324147464177237\cdot50003
&=324147464177237\cdot50000
  +324147464177237\cdot3\\
&=16207373208861850000+972442392531711\\
&=16208345651254381711.
\end{aligned}
\tag{8}
\]
Substituting (6)-(8) into (5) yields
\[
D\tau_0[\xi]
=\frac{50003}{100000}
 \frac{324147464177237}{10^{12}}
=\frac{16208345651254381711}{100000000000000000}>0.
\]
Thus this strict turn differential is nonzero on \(\ker E_{\rm can}\), as required.
