## Statement

Let \(A_0,A_2\subset\mathbb R^2\) be nonempty two-dimensional convex polytopes. Let

\[
B=\frac{A_0+A_2}{2},\qquad
a_i=|A_i|\ (i=0,2),\qquad b=|B|,\qquad
T_B=a_0+b+a_2.
\]

Let \(g_0,g_2\) be the centroids of \(A_0,A_2\), and set

\[
z=\frac{g_0+g_2}{2},\qquad
p=(\sqrt{a_0}+\sqrt{a_2})^2,\qquad
m=\min\{a_0,a_2\}.
\]

Assume

\[
a_0,b,a_2<\frac{T_B}{2},
\qquad
p+4m\ge 2T_B.
\tag{1}
\]

For every \(\rho\ge1\), define

\[
A_1^{(\rho)}=z+\rho(B-z),\qquad
E_\rho=A_1^{(\rho)}\setminus B.
\]

Then \(B\subseteq A_1^{(\rho)}\), the resulting triple is compatible, and

\[
h_{(\{0\}\times A_0)\cup(\{1\}\times A_1^{(\rho)})
\cup(\{2\}\times A_2)}((1,z))
\ge
\frac{2(a_0+|A_1^{(\rho)}|+a_2)}9.
\tag{2}
\]

Moreover, if

\[
\delta=h_{(\{0\}\times A_0)\cup(\{1\}\times B)
\cup(\{2\}\times A_2)}((1,z))-\frac{2T_B}{9},
\]

then (2) remains true for every \(\rho\) satisfying

\[
|E_\rho|=(\rho^2-1)b>\frac92\delta.
\]

Thus these cases are not certified by scalar slack.

## Proof

Because \(g_i\in A_i\), one has \(z\in B\). Accepted facts `8ad0785e5b4ae9ef` and `9fa81a67fd2eaafc`, applied to the minimal triple \((A_0,B,A_2)\), give

\[
h_{S_B}((1,z))\ge\frac{2T_B}{9}.
\tag{3}
\]

For every planar halfplane \(K\) whose boundary contains \(z\), the centroid-cap and Brunn-Minkowski argument inside accepted fact `9fa81a67fd2eaafc` gives

\[
|B\cap K|\ge\frac{p}{9}.
\tag{4}
\]

Condition (1) also implies

\[
p-2b
\ge 2(a_0+a_2)-4m
=2|a_0-a_2|
\ge0.
\tag{5}
\]

Consequently,

\[
|B\cap K|\ge\frac{2b}{9}
\tag{6}
\]

for every such \(K\).

Since \(z\in B\) and \(\rho\ge1\), convexity gives \(B\subseteq A_1^{(\rho)}\). Positive dilation about \(z\) preserves every halfplane through \(z\), so

\[
A_1^{(\rho)}\cap K
=
z+\rho((B\cap K)-z).
\]

Therefore

\[
|E_\rho|=(\rho^2-1)b
\]

and, exactly,

\[
|E_\rho\cap K|
=(\rho^2-1)|B\cap K|
\ge\frac{2|E_\rho|}{9}.
\tag{7}
\]

Let \(H\) be a closed halfspace whose boundary contains \((1,z)\). If \(H\) is nonhorizontal, its middle section \(K_H\) is a halfplane through \(z\), so (7) applies. If \(H\) is horizontal, \(K_H=\mathbb R^2\), and \(|E_\rho\cap K_H|=|E_\rho|\). In either case,

\[
|E_\rho\cap K_H|
\ge\frac{2|E_\rho|}{9}
\ge
\left(\frac{2|E_\rho|}{9}-\sigma(H)\right)_+,
\]

because (3) gives \(\sigma(H)\ge0\). Accepted fact `1c0264823debcab3` now yields (2). Compatibility follows from \(B\subseteq A_1^{(\rho)}\) and accepted fact `8ad0785e5b4ae9ef`.

Finally, \(\delta\) is fixed and finite while \(|E_\rho|=(\rho^2-1)b\) is unbounded. Choosing

\[
\rho^2-1>\frac{9\delta}{2b}
\]

violates the scalar condition while the geometric argument above still proves transfer.

A concrete noncentrally symmetric instance is

\[
A_0=\operatorname{conv}\{(0,0),(1,0),(0,1)\},
\qquad
A_2=\operatorname{conv}\{(0,0),(1,0),(1,1)\}.
\]

Here

\[
B=\operatorname{conv}\left\{
(0,0),(1,0),\left(1,\frac12\right),
\left(\frac12,1\right),\left(0,\frac12\right)
\right\},
\]

\[
(a_0,b,a_2)=\left(\frac12,\frac34,\frac12\right),
\qquad
z=\left(\frac12,\frac13\right).
\]

For \(\rho=3\), the added area is \(6\). A horizontal halfspace shows \(\delta\le31/36\), hence \((9/2)\delta\le31/8<6\), while (2) gives depth at least \(31/18\) at the same explicit point.
