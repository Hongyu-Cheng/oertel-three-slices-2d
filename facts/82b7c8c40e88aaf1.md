## Statement

Assume all hypotheses and the canonical notation of accepted fact `f8e871d10dac1791` for the surviving transformed triangular \(N=3\) system.  In particular, assume
\[
I_0=\{1,2\},\qquad I_1=\{3\},\qquad t\leq\frac12,
\]
write the middle triangle's vertex matrix as \(L=(\ell_{ij})_{i,j=1}^3\), and put
\[
J_i=\{j:\ell_{ij}>0\}.
\]
Suppose that all nine entries \(\ell_{ij}\) are nonzero and that the three strict counterexample inequalities
\[
A u_i+c_i+B v_i<t\qquad(i=1,2,3)
\]
hold in that notation.  Then, up to only the two interchanges allowed in accepted fact `f8e871d10dac1791`, namely interchange of rows \(1,2\) and interchange of columns \(2,3\), one has \(J_3=\{1\}\) and
\[
(J_1,J_2)\in
\left\{
(\{2\},\{2,3\}),
(\{1,2\},\{2,3\}),
(\{2,3\},\{2,3\})
\right\}.
\]
No conclusion is asserted on any feasible stratum on which at least one middle vertex entry \(\ell_{ij}\) is zero.

## Proof

Let \(s\) denote the allowed interchange of rows \(1,2\), and let \(\tau=(2\ 3)\) denote the allowed interchange of columns \(2,3\).  On an ordered support pair they act exactly as
\[
s(X,Y)=(Y,X),\qquad
\tau(X,Y)=(\tau X,\tau Y).
\]
The two involutions commute.  Accepted fact `f8e871d10dac1791` says that every open chamber under the present hypotheses is represented, modulo the group \(\{1,s,\tau,s\tau\}\), by exactly one of
\[
(\{2\},\{3\}),
(\{2\},\{1,3\}),
(\{2\},\{2,3\}),
(\{1,2\},\{2,3\}),
(\{2,3\},\{2,3\}),
\tag{1}
\]
and that \(J_3=\{1\}\).  To check that no relabeling is being added or omitted, the five orbits under precisely these two interchanges are
\[
\begin{aligned}
\mathcal O_1&=\{(\{2\},\{3\}),(\{3\},\{2\})\},\\
\mathcal O_2&=\{(\{2\},\{1,3\}),(\{1,3\},\{2\}),
                    (\{3\},\{1,2\}),(\{1,2\},\{3\})\},\\
\mathcal O_3&=\{(\{2\},\{2,3\}),(\{2,3\},\{2\}),
                    (\{3\},\{2,3\}),(\{2,3\},\{3\})\},\\
\mathcal O_4&=\{(\{1,2\},\{2,3\}),(\{2,3\},\{1,2\}),
                    (\{1,3\},\{2,3\}),(\{2,3\},\{1,3\})\},\\
\mathcal O_5&=\{(\{2,3\},\{2,3\})\}.
\end{aligned}
\tag{2}
\]
These orbits are distinct: their unordered support cardinalities are respectively \((1,1)\), \((1,2)\), \((1,2)\), \((2,2)\), \((2,2)\); in the two \((1,2)\) cases the singleton is respectively disjoint from or contained in the two-element support, and in the two \((2,2)\) cases the supports are respectively unequal or equal.

We first eliminate \(\mathcal O_1\).  For its representative, the three positive supports are
\[
J_1=\{2\},\qquad J_2=\{3\},\qquad J_3=\{1\}.
\]
Because every entry is nonzero, these are three one-positive rows with their positive entries in three distinct columns.  Let \(P\) be the permutation matrix whose columns are \(e_2,e_3,e_1\), and take \(R=I\).  Then \(LP\) has a strictly positive diagonal and strictly negative off-diagonal entries.  Dividing each row by its positive diagonal entry puts \(LP\) exactly in the positive-parameter form required by accepted fact `b894a3da1107beaf`.  Its lossless relabeling is exactly
\[
L\mapsto LP,\qquad U\mapsto P^\top U,\qquad
V\mapsto P^\top V,qquad \sigma\mapsto\sigma.
\]
Hence accepted fact `b894a3da1107beaf` rules out the three simultaneous strict inequalities.  This three-cycle of columns is used only to satisfy the existential normal-form hypothesis of that accepted fact; it is not used to identify any additional chambers in the quotient (1).  The representative, and therefore its entire allowed orbit \(\mathcal O_1\), is excluded.

For the representative of \(\mathcal O_2\), one has exactly
\[
J_1=\{2\},\qquad J_2=\{1,3\},\qquad J_3=\{1\}.
\]
Accepted fact `76bff99d5eaaa46f` excludes the three simultaneous strict inequalities in precisely this open chamber.  Every other member of \(\mathcal O_2\) is carried back to this representative by \(s\), \(\tau\), or \(s\tau\).  The row interchange preserves the sets \(I_0=\{1,2\}\) and \(I_1=\{3\}\); the column interchange only relabels the three vertices and fixes \(J_3=\{1\}\).  Both are exactly the lossless interchanges already allowed by accepted fact `f8e871d10dac1791`.  Thus the whole orbit \(\mathcal O_2\) is excluded.

Removing \(\mathcal O_1\) and \(\mathcal O_2\) from the exhaustive and disjoint list (2) leaves exactly \(\mathcal O_3,\mathcal O_4,\mathcal O_5\), represented by the three pairs in the statement.  No further chamber is removed.

Finally, this argument has used the open-sign assumption \(\ell_{ij}\ne0\) for all nine pairs \((i,j)\).  Accepted fact `f8e871d10dac1791` explicitly retains the zero-entry faces as boundary strata; accepted fact `b894a3da1107beaf` requires all of its positive parameters to be strictly positive, and accepted fact `76bff99d5eaaa46f` assumes its displayed open chamber.  Therefore, for every nonempty set \(Z\subseteq\{1,2,3\}\times\{1,2,3\}\), every feasible boundary stratum satisfying \(\ell_{ij}=0\) for all \((i,j)\in Z\), including strata with further zero entries, remains untouched and unresolved by this composition.
