## Statement

Let \(B\subset\mathbb R^2\) be a compact two-dimensional convex body
contained in \(\{y\ge h\}\), where \(h>0\), and suppose that
\(B\cap\{y=h\}\) contains a horizontal segment of length \(\ell>0\).
For any compact convex body \(K\supseteq B\), put
\[
E=\left|K\cap\{y\le0\}\right|,
\qquad
G=\left|(K\setminus B)\cap\{y>0\}\right|.
\]
If \(E>0\) and
\[
\frac{h\ell}{2}\le G<h\ell,
\]
then
\[
\boxed{\quad
E\le
\frac{(2G-h\ell)^2}{4(h\ell-G)}.
\quad}
\tag{1}
\]
The inequality is sharp given only the displayed horizontal-segment
datum.

Apply (1) to
\[
f_1(x,y)=x,\qquad f_2(x,y)=y,\qquad
f_3(x,y)=-1-x-y,\qquad L_1=L_2=L_3=-100,
\tag{2}
\]
\[
A_0=\operatorname{conv}\left\{
\left(-\frac{999}{10},-\frac{981}{10}\right),
\left(197,-\frac{981}{10}\right),
\left(\frac{49}{2},-50\right)
\right\},
\tag{3}
\]
and
\[
A_2=
\left\{
\left(-\frac{99}{2},\frac{991}{10}\right)
+s\left(302,\frac{19}{10}\right)
+t\left(-\frac1{10},\frac1{20}\right):
-\frac12\le s,t\le\frac12
\right\}.
\tag{4}
\]
Let \(B=(A_0+A_2)/2\).  There is no compact convex body \(K\supseteq B\)
such that
\[
|K\setminus B|=3600
\tag{5}
\]
and all three crossing equalities
\[
\left|A_0\cap\{f_i\le L_i\}\right|
+\left|A_2\cap\{f_i\le-L_i\}\right|
+\left|K\cap\{f_i\le0\}\right|
=\frac{2(|A_0|+|A_2|+|K|)}9
\tag{6}
\]
hold.  Thus this exact positive-opposite-pair scalar survivor has no
compatible convex middle polygon at \(A=3600\).

## Proof

First prove (1).  Let
\[
w(t)=\mathcal H_1\bigl(K\cap\{y=t\}\bigr)
\]
on the vertical projection interval of \(K\).  Horizontal sections are
intervals, and convexity gives
\[
\lambda\bigl(K\cap\{y=t_1\}\bigr)
+(1-\lambda)\bigl(K\cap\{y=t_2\}\bigr)
\subseteq
K\cap\{y=\lambda t_1+(1-\lambda)t_2\}.
\]
Taking one-dimensional lengths shows that \(w\) is concave.

Write
\[
s=w(0),\qquad W=w(h).
\]
Since \(E>0\), \(K\) has positive-area sections below \(0\); since it
also contains \(B\) above \(h\), one has \(s>0\).  The given segment
implies \(W\ge\ell\).  Because \(B\subseteq\{y\ge h\}\), the whole part
of \(K\) in \(0<y<h\) belongs to \(K\setminus B\).  Concavity on
\([0,h]\) therefore gives
\[
G\ge\int_0^h w(t)\,dt
\ge\frac h2(s+W)
\ge\frac h2(s+\ell).
\tag{7}
\]
Consequently
\[
0<s\le s_*:=\frac{2G-h\ell}{h}<\ell.
\tag{8}
\]

For any \(t<0\) in the vertical projection interval, monotonicity of
secant slopes of a concave function gives
\[
\frac{s-w(t)}{-t}
\ge
\frac{W-s}{h}
\ge
\frac{\ell-s}{h}.
\tag{9}
\]
Put \(p=(\ell-s)/h>0\).  Equation (9) says
\[
w(t)\le s+pt.
\tag{10}
\]
Thus a positive-length section below \(0\) can occur only for
\(-s/p<t<0\), and integration yields
\[
E\le\int_{-s/p}^0(s+pt)\,dt
=\frac{s^2}{2p}
=\frac{hs^2}{2(\ell-s)}.
\tag{11}
\]
The last expression is strictly increasing for \(0<s<\ell\).  Using
(8) in (11), followed by direct simplification, gives
\[
E
\le\frac{h s_*^2}{2(\ell-s_*)}
=\frac{(2G-h\ell)^2}{4(h\ell-G)},
\]
which is (1).  Given only the segment datum, equality is realized below
\(h\) by the triangular width profile
\[
w(t)=s+\frac{\ell-s}{h}t
\quad
\left(-\frac{hs}{\ell-s}\le t\le h\right).
\]
Choose centered horizontal sections, continue the profile affinely from
width \(\ell\) at \(h\) to width \(0\) at any level above \(h\), and
take that upper triangular cap as \(B\).  This gives two-dimensional
compact convex \(B\subset K\) and equality in (7) and (11).  Hence the
coupled bound itself is sharp; any further prescribed geometry of
\(B\) can only strengthen it.

We now audit (2)--(4) exactly.  The three affine functions have
pairwise nonparallel linear parts and sum to \(-1\).  The four vertices
of \(A_2\) are
\[
\left(-\frac{4011}{20},\frac{3927}{40}\right),\quad
\left(-\frac{4009}{20},\frac{785}{8}\right),\quad
\left(\frac{2029}{20},\frac{4003}{40}\right),\quad
\left(\frac{2031}{20},\frac{4001}{40}\right).
\tag{12}
\]
The determinant and triangle-area formulas give
\[
M:=|A_0|=\frac{1428089}{200},
\qquad
m:=|A_2|=\frac{1529}{100},
\qquad
\frac mM=\frac{3058}{1428089}<\frac38.
\tag{13}
\]
The exact midpoint polygon has area
\[
k_0:=|B|=\frac{2202453}{400}
\tag{14}
\]
and cyclic vertices
\[
\begin{aligned}
&\left(-\frac{6009}{40},\frac3{80}\right),
\left(-\frac{6007}{40},\frac1{80}\right),
\left(-\frac{69}{40},\frac1{80}\right),
\left(\frac{5971}{40},\frac{77}{80}\right),\\
&\left(\frac{5969}{40},\frac{79}{80}\right),
\left(\frac{2519}{40},\frac{2003}{80}\right),
\left(-\frac{3521}{40},\frac{1927}{80}\right).
\end{aligned}
\tag{15}
\]
In particular,
\[
B\subseteq\left\{y\ge\frac1{80}\right\},
\qquad
B\cap\left\{y=\frac1{80}\right\}
=
\left[-\frac{6007}{40},-\frac{69}{40}\right]
\times\left\{\frac1{80}\right\}.
\tag{16}
\]
Thus the parameters in (1) are
\[
h=\frac1{80},
\qquad
\ell=\frac{2969}{20}.
\tag{17}
\]

For completeness, put
\[
u_i=\left|A_0\cap\{f_i\le L_i\}\right|,
\quad
v_i=\left|A_2\cap\{f_i\le-L_i\}\right|,
\quad
b_i=\left|B\cap\{f_i\le0\}\right|.
\]
Exact rational halfplane clipping and shoelace evaluation give
\[
\begin{array}{c|ccc}
i&u_i&v_i&b_i\\ \hline
1&0&\dfrac{918929}{60400}
&\dfrac{698967161}{241600}\\[2mm]
2&0&\dfrac{56573}{3800}&0\\[2mm]
3&0&\dfrac{3083993}{202600}
&\dfrac{14291658397}{4862400}.
\end{array}
\tag{18}
\]
Here
\[
h_0=\frac{2(M+m+k_0)}9=\frac{1688249}{600}.
\]
Therefore the three base defects in accepted fact
`6f6869df3d151764` are exactly
\[
(F_1,F_2,F_3)=
\left(
\frac{68523839}{724800},
-\frac{7976753}{2850},
\frac{684104333}{4862400}
\right).
\tag{19}
\]

This is genuinely an opposite-pair endpoint datum.  With
\(q_i=f_i+L_i\), define
\[
Q_J=A_2\cap
\{q_j\le0\ (j\in J),\ q_j>0\ (j\notin J)\}.
\]
The cutting lines have area zero, and exact clipping gives
\[
\left|Q_{\{3\}}\right|=\frac{4587}{60400}>0,
\qquad
\left|Q_{\{1,2\}}\right|=\frac{13761}{202600}>0.
\tag{20}
\]
Also \(p_i=f_i-L_i=f_i+100\) is strictly positive throughout \(A_0\),
so \(P_\varnothing=A_0\).  The large endpoint is a triangle and hence
is not centrally symmetric.

The scalar-completion interval from accepted fact
`6f6869df3d151764` is
\[
\frac{23930259}{6650}
\le A\le
\frac{17881582275919}{2325037600}.
\tag{21}
\]
The proposed value \(A=3600\) lies strictly inside it, since
\[
3600-\frac{23930259}{6650}=\frac{9741}{6650}>0
\]
and
\[
\frac{17881582275919}{2325037600}-3600
=\frac{9511446915919}{2325037600}>0.
\]
At \(A=3600\), the completion identity
\[
e_i:=\left|(K\setminus B)\cap\{f_i\le0\}\right|
=\frac{2A}{9}-F_i
\]
from that accepted fact requires
\[
(e_1,e_2,e_3)=
\left(
\frac{511316161}{724800},
\frac{10256753}{2850},
\frac{3205815667}{4862400}
\right).
\tag{22}
\]

For the second row, (16) makes
\[
E=e_2=\frac{10256753}{2850},
\qquad
G=A-e_2=\frac{3247}{2850}.
\tag{23}
\]
The individual support-clearance value from accepted fact
`cf1a8ff16a0da82a`, for the halfplane \(\{y\ge0\}\), is
\[
\Gamma\bigl(B,\{y\ge0\}\bigr)
=\frac{h\ell}{2}
=\frac{2969}{3200}.
\tag{24}
\]
Indeed, the triangle with base equal to the segment in (16) gives the
lower bound for every boundary point, and the visible-edge formula at
\((-100,0)\) attains it.  The proposed budget passes this individual
test strictly:
\[
G-\Gamma
=\frac{1543}{7296}>0.
\tag{25}
\]
It also satisfies
\[
h\ell-G
=\frac{65329}{91200}>0,
\tag{26}
\]
so the coupled inequality (1) applies.

Substitution of (17) and (23) into (1) gives the exact upper bound
\[
E\le
\frac{(2G-h\ell)^2}{4(h\ell-G)}
=\frac{59521225}{953280768}
<1.
\tag{27}
\]
But the crossing equality requires
\[
E=\frac{10256753}{2850}>1,
\tag{28}
\]
a contradiction.  Hence no compact convex \(K\), polygonal or
otherwise, satisfies (5)--(6).  The failure occurs before genuine
lower-crossing verification and before any finite strict-low cover can
be formed.
