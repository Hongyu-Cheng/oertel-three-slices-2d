## Statement

Let \(N=\{1,2,3\}\). Assume the full actual dominant rank-two all-double common-plane hypotheses and exact-cell convention of accepted facts `174b54e9843ef8fc`, `29dfdfc94477e532`, `2f637c4da2df28f1`, and `42870b25955cb8db`. Thus \(A_0,A_2,K\) are compact two-dimensional convex polytopes,
\[
\frac{A_0+A_2}{2}\subseteq K,\qquad
f_1+f_2+f_3=-1,
\]
the linear parts of the affine functions \(f_i\) span \(\mathbb R^2\), and
\[
L_1+L_2+L_3<-1.
\]
For \(J,H,G\subseteq N\), use the exact cells
\[
P_J=A_0\cap
\{f_j\leq L_j\ (j\in J),\ f_j>L_j\ (j\notin J)\},
\]
\[
Q_H=A_2\cap
\{f_j\leq-L_j\ (j\in H),\ f_j>-L_j\ (j\notin H)\},
\]
\[
R_G=K\cap
\{f_j\leq0\ (j\in G),\ f_j>0\ (j\notin G)\}.
\]
Write
\[
M=|A_0|,\qquad m=|A_2|,\qquad k=|K|,
\]
\[
p_J=|P_J|,\qquad q_H=|Q_H|,\qquad r_G=|R_G|,
\]
\[
u_i=\sum_{J\ni i}p_J,\qquad
v_i=\sum_{H\ni i}q_H,\qquad
c_i=\sum_{G\ni i}r_G,\qquad
U=u_1+u_2+u_3.
\]
The positive \(Q\)-support is
\[
\{123,12,13,23\},
\]
with dominant index \(1\), so
\[
q=q_{123}>0,\qquad
x_i=q_{N\setminus\{i\}}>0,\qquad
m=q+x_1+x_2+x_3,
\]
\[
x_1-u_1>\frac{5m}{9}.
\]
Put
\[
E_R=r_{12}+r_{13}+r_{23}+2r_{123}.
\]
Put
\[
h=\frac{2(M+m+k)}9.
\]
Assume \(M\geq m\), the three crossings, the canonical tails,
\[
u_i+v_i+c_i=h,\qquad
c_i+m\geq h,\qquad c_i+M\geq h,
\]
the all-double equality
\[
2M=4m+k+3(U+q+E_R),
\]
the actual no-half inequality
\[
k<M+m,
\]
and
\[
r_1r_2r_3r_{123}>0.
\]

Let
\[
B_0=\frac{A_0+A_2}{2}.
\]
For cyclic \((i,j,\ell)\), let \(O_i\) be the point where
\(f_j=f_\ell=0\), define
\[
h_X(g)=\max_{z\in X}g(z),
\]
put
\[
I_i=\{t>0:h_{B_0}(f_\ell+t f_j)\leq0\},
\]
and assume total corner collapse:
\[
\operatorname{int}_{\mathbb R}I_i=\varnothing
\qquad(i=1,2,3).
\]
Use the dominant bodies
\[
P=\overline{P_\varnothing},\qquad
C=C_1^\sharp,\qquad
p=|P|,\qquad d=|C|,\qquad
S_1=\frac{P+C}{2}.
\]
Let
\[
a=\min_{S_1}f_1,\qquad b=\max_{S_1}f_1,
\qquad 0<a<b.
\]
Put
\[
\mathcal T=\operatorname{conv}\{O_1,O_2,O_3\},
\qquad E=[O_2,O_3],
\]
choose \(x\in S_1\) with \(f_1(x)=b\), and define
\[
Z_1=\operatorname{conv}(E\cup\{x\})
\cap\{0\leq f_1\leq a\},
\]
\[
\Theta=|Z_1|
=r_{123}\left(2a-\frac{a^2}{b}\right).
\]
Finally, let
\[
A_1=\left|K\cap\{f_1\geq0\}\right|,
\]
\[
\rho_1=
\left|
\bigl(K\cap\{f_1\geq0\}\bigr)\setminus(S_1\cup Z_1)
\right|.
\]
Then
\[
\boxed{\rho_1<r_1+r_{12}+r_{13}.}
\]
Equivalently, with the notation of accepted fact `42870b25955cb8db`,
\[
\mathcal V(P,C)+\frac83(p_{12}+p_{13}+p_{23})
>
\mathscr B-2\Theta+2\lambda_1
-2(r_1+r_{12}+r_{13}).
\]
Both strict inequalities hold in every one of the eight empty/singleton collapse strata, including every allowed endpoint coincidence and proper-cell boundary.

## Proof

Accepted fact `42870b25955cb8db` proves, uniformly over the eight total-collapse strata, that \(S_1\) and \(Z_1\) lie in \(K\cap\{f_1\geq0\}\), are area-disjoint, and satisfy the exact decomposition
\[
A_1=|S_1|+\Theta+\rho_1.
\tag{1}
\]

We first prove the strict scale estimate
\[
|S_1|>\frac{A_1}{5}.
\tag{2}
\]
Accepted fact `29dfdfc94477e532` gives
\[
|S_1|=\frac{p+d+2\mathcal V(P,C)}4,
\qquad
\mathcal V(P,C)\geq\sqrt{pd},
\qquad d\geq\frac{4x_1}{9}>0.
\tag{3}
\]
It also writes \(D_0=M-p\) and proves \(D_0\leq U\). Hence
\[
p\geq M-U,
\qquad
|S_1|>\frac p4\geq\frac{M-U}{4}.
\tag{4}
\]

Put \(\Xi=U+q+E_R\). The all-double equality and \(k<M+m\), both recorded for the actual branch in accepted fact `174b54e9843ef8fc`, give
\[
2M=4m+k+3\Xi
<M+5m+3\Xi,
\]
and therefore
\[
M<5m+3\Xi.
\tag{5}
\]
Accepted fact `29dfdfc94477e532` gives the exact dominant-cap formula
\[
A_1
=\frac{4M-7m-7\Xi}{3}+u_1-x_1.
\tag{6}
\]
Using (5) in the exact difference obtained from (6), we get
\[
\begin{aligned}
12\left(\frac{5(M-U)}4-A_1\right)
&=-M+13U+28m+28q+28E_R+12(x_1-u_1)\\
&>23m+10U+25q+25E_R+12(x_1-u_1)\\
&>0.
\end{aligned}
\tag{7}
\]
The last strict inequality uses nonnegativity of \(U,E_R\), positivity of \(m,q\), and \(x_1-u_1>5m/9\). Equations (4) and (7) imply
\[
5|S_1|>\frac{5p}{4}
\geq\frac{5(M-U)}4
>A_1,
\]
which proves (2).

It remains to charge the rest of the positive cap. For \(i\in N\), set
\[
K_i=K\cap\{f_i\geq0\}.
\]
The positivity \(r_1r_2r_3>0\) makes each \(K_i\) a two-dimensional convex body, and \(f_1+f_2+f_3=-1\) makes their threefold intersection empty. Accepted fact `2f637c4da2df28f1` proves that the dominant index \(1\) is the unique minimizer of \(|K_i|\), so
\[
|K_1|=A_1=\min_i|K_i|.
\tag{8}
\]
The Scott union estimate already verified there therefore gives
\[
\left|K_1\cup K_2\cup K_3\right|
\geq\frac95A_1.
\tag{9}
\]
Up to the three null cutting lines, the union consists of \(K_1\) together with exactly the cells \(R_1,R_{12},R_{13}\). Equivalently,
\[
\left|K_1\cup K_2\cup K_3\right|
=A_1+r_1+r_{12}+r_{13}
=k-r_{123}.
\tag{10}
\]
Combining (9) and (10) gives the direct common-plane charge
\[
r_1+r_{12}+r_{13}\geq\frac45A_1.
\tag{11}
\]

Finally, \(\Theta>0\) because \(r_{123}>0\) and \(0<a<b\). From (1), (2), and (11),
\[
\rho_1
=A_1-|S_1|-\Theta
<A_1-\frac{A_1}{5}
=\frac45A_1
\leq r_1+r_{12}+r_{13}.
\]
This proves the claimed comparison. Substituting it into the exact ledger identity of accepted fact `42870b25955cb8db` proves the equivalent mixed-area inequality in the statement. The only collapse-dependent input was (1), which that accepted fact proves uniformly in all eight strata. Every remaining boundary is contained in one of the affine cutting lines and has area zero, so endpoint coincidences and proper-cell boundaries do not change any equality or inequality above.
