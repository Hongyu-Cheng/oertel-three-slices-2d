## Statement

Assume the complete canonical finite-interior hypotheses, exact-cell convention, \(M\ge m\), the three crossings, the canonical tails, \(W=0\), the all-double positive \(Q\)-support
\[
\{123,12,13,23\},
\]
and \(r_1r_2r_3r_{123}>0\).  Write
\[
p=p_\varnothing,\qquad
\eta=s_P+4d_P+3d_R+6r_{123}.
\]
Use the constants and halfspace modulus of accepted fact `9b267b01d2864206`:
\[
L=\frac{20\sqrt{30}-27}{289},
\qquad
s_0=\frac{L}{12},
\qquad
\omega_0=\omega(s_0).
\]
Define
\[
c_{\mathrm{rel}}
=
\min\left\{
\frac L8,\,
\frac{3\omega_0}{4}
\right\}.
\tag{1}
\]
Then \(c_{\mathrm{rel}}>0\), and every full realization satisfies the scale-invariant relative gap
\[
\boxed{\quad
\frac{\eta}{p}\ge c_{\mathrm{rel}}.
\quad}
\tag{2}
\]

Now assume the sharp ratio
\[
\frac{x_1}{q}=\frac{x_2}{q}=\frac{x_3}{q}=\frac5{21},
\]
and normalize \(m=1\).  Then \(q=7/12\).  Put
\[
P_{\mathrm{rel}}
=
\left(
\frac{
1+\sqrt{169-96c_{\mathrm{rel}}}
}{
7-4c_{\mathrm{rel}}
}
\right)^2.
\tag{3}
\]
Every full \(W=0\) realization satisfies
\[
p\ge P_{\mathrm{rel}}>4,
\tag{4}
\]
\[
\frac{\eta}{q}
\ge
\frac{12}{7}c_{\mathrm{rel}}P_{\mathrm{rel}},
\tag{5}
\]
and, with \(D=q/|\mathcal T_Q|\),
\[
D
<
\frac{49}{
\left(
\sqrt{12/7}
+
\sqrt{
2028/7+48c_{\mathrm{rel}}P_{\mathrm{rel}}
}
\right)^2}
=
\frac{7}{12P_{\mathrm{rel}}}
<
\frac7{48}.
\tag{6}
\]
More strongly, the exact common-plane scale restriction retains the actual \(p\) and \(r_{123}\):
\[
D
\le
\frac{7/12}{
\left(\sqrt p+2\sqrt{r_{123}}\right)^2}
<
\frac{7}{12p},
\qquad
Dp<\frac7{12}.
\tag{7}
\]

## Proof

The positivity of \(L\) and \(\omega_0\) is part of accepted fact `9b267b01d2864206`, so \(c_{\mathrm{rel}}>0\).  Scale a full realization so that \(q=1\), and use the normalization from that accepted fact:
\[
e=\frac{\eta}{p},\qquad
t=\frac{k}{p},\qquad
y_i=\frac{r_i}{p}.
\]
It proves
\[
y_i\ge\frac L3-\frac{4e}{3},
\qquad
\frac14\le t\le2.
\tag{8}
\]

If \(e>L/8\), then immediately
\[
e\ge c_{\mathrm{rel}}.
\]
Suppose instead that
\[
e\le\frac L8.
\tag{9}
\]
This branch includes equality in (9).  Equation (8) gives, non-strictly,
\[
y_i\ge\frac L6,
\qquad
\frac{r_i}{k}=\frac{y_i}{t}\ge\frac{L}{12}=s_0
\quad(i=1,2,3).
\tag{10}
\]
Apply the halfspace modulus in accepted fact `9b267b01d2864206` to the middle body and the three halfspaces \(f_i\le0\).  The membership-count-zero cell is empty, the count-two cells have total area \(d_R\), and the count-three cell has area \(r_{123}\).  Hence
\[
d_R+r_{123}\ge\omega_0k.
\tag{11}
\]
Using the definition of \(\eta\), (11), and \(t=k/p\ge1/4\), one obtains
\[
\begin{aligned}
e
=\frac{\eta}{p}
&\ge
\frac{3(d_R+r_{123})}{p}\\
&\ge3\omega_0\frac{k}{p}\\
&=3\omega_0t
\ge\frac{3\omega_0}{4}.
\end{aligned}
\tag{12}
\]
Thus either \(e>L/8\), or (12) holds.  Both branches imply (2), and the equality boundary \(e=L/8\) is covered by the second branch.  Since \(\eta/p\) is unchanged when all areas are scaled together, undoing \(q=1\) preserves (2).

At the sharp ratio, \(m=1\) gives
\[
1=q+3\left(\frac5{21}q\right)=\frac{12}{7}q,
\]
so \(q=7/12\).  Put \(z=\sqrt p\) and abbreviate \(c=c_{\mathrm{rel}}\).  The exact \(W=0\) and \(\mathrm{F0}\) calculation in accepted fact `084bf2f472bf29c7` gives
\[
7z^2-2z-24-4\eta\ge0.
\tag{13}
\]
By (2), \(\eta\ge cp=cz^2\).  Therefore
\[
(7-4c)z^2-2z-24\ge0.
\tag{14}
\]
Since \(c\le L/8<1/8\), the leading coefficient in (14) is positive and \(169-96c>0\).  The negative root of its quadratic is negative, while the positive root is
\[
z_{\mathrm{rel}}
=
\frac{1+\sqrt{169-96c}}{7-4c}.
\]
Hence \(z\ge z_{\mathrm{rel}}\), proving \(p\ge P_{\mathrm{rel}}\).  Moreover, the left side of (14) evaluated at \(z=2\) equals
\[
4(7-4c)-4-24=-16c<0.
\]
Thus its positive root is strictly larger than \(2\), so \(P_{\mathrm{rel}}>4\).  Equation (2), \(p\ge P_{\mathrm{rel}}\), and \(q=7/12\) give (5).

Apply the exact scalar bridge in accepted fact `084bf2f472bf29c7` with \(|\mathcal T_Q|=q/D\).  Dividing by \(q\), using \(m/q=12/7\), and retaining the strict positive term \(2\sqrt{r_{123}/q}\) gives
\[
\frac1D
>
\frac1{49}
\left(
\sqrt{\frac{12}{7}}
+
\sqrt{
\frac{2028}{7}
+28\frac{\eta}{q}
}
\right)^2.
\tag{15}
\]
Substitution of (5) proves the strict inequality in (6).

It remains to verify its displayed closed form.  By the definition of \(P_{\mathrm{rel}}\), its positive square root is the positive root of (14), so
\[
(7-4c)P_{\mathrm{rel}}
-2\sqrt{P_{\mathrm{rel}}}
-24
=0.
\]
Equivalently,
\[
1+\sqrt{169+28cP_{\mathrm{rel}}}
=7\sqrt{P_{\mathrm{rel}}}.
\tag{16}
\]
Since \(q=7/12\),
\[
\begin{aligned}
&
\left(
\sqrt{\frac{12}{7}}
+
\sqrt{
\frac{2028}{7}
+48cP_{\mathrm{rel}}
}
\right)^2\\
&\qquad
=
\frac{12}{7}
\left(
1+\sqrt{169+28cP_{\mathrm{rel}}}
\right)^2
=84P_{\mathrm{rel}},
\end{aligned}
\]
where (16) is used in the last equality.  Thus the right side of (6) is \(49/(84P_{\mathrm{rel}})=7/(12P_{\mathrm{rel}})\).  Since \(P_{\mathrm{rel}}>4\), the last strict inequality in (6) follows.

Finally, accepted fact `e40abbed940a40ab` gives
\[
D
\le
\frac q{
\left(\sqrt p+2\sqrt{r_{123}}\right)^2}.
\]
Here \(r_{123}>0\), so after substituting \(q=7/12\) this is exactly (7).  It records the strongest coupled \(D,p_\varnothing,r_{123}\) restriction supplied by the common-plane scale geometry; (6) is its uniform consequence after the new relative gap forces \(p\ge P_{\mathrm{rel}}\).
