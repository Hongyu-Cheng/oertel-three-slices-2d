## Statement

Use the common-plane exact-cell convention of accepted fact `0e51a47a9add571f`, the three row coordinate maps of accepted fact `b431dbefa8a4aec7`, and the chamber orientation of accepted fact `decc0d9db44fe9e5`.  In the exact rational calibration let
\[
L_j=L_k=L_\ell=-\frac23,
\qquad
u=-q_j,\quad v=-q_\ell,\quad w=-q_k.
\]
Then
\[
u+v+w=3,
\]
and the relevant strict chambers are
\[
Q_N=\{u>0,\ v>0,\ w>0\},
\qquad
Q_{jk}=\{u>0,\ v<0,\ w>0\}.
\]
The retained \(A_0\)-trace lengths are
\[
J=\lambda_{0j}=\frac{21411}{2748500},\qquad
L=\lambda_{0\ell}=\frac{238468789}{198502762500},\qquad
K=\lambda_{0k}=\frac{5121}{2440010000}.
\]
The three chosen separator data \((a_{rs},d_{rs};\chi_{rs})\) are
\[
(1,1;1),\qquad
\left(1,\frac32;\frac{253}{250}\right),\qquad
\left(1,75;\frac{43}{5000}\right)
\]
for \(rs=jk,j\ell,k\ell\), respectively.  Their row-wise values are
\[
\Phi_{jk}=\frac{52257238824231}{13412734970000000},
\]
\[
\Phi_{j\ell}
=\frac{14245343933040883}{3273509056387500000},
\qquad
\Phi_{k\ell}
=\frac{11502606067977683}{145304617658287500000000}.
\]
The scalar calibration has
\[
b=\frac{57971357865588299}{545584842731250000},\qquad
c=\frac{89209920906187}{774957960844200000000},\qquad
d=\frac{5121}{4{,}880{,}020{,}000{,}000{,}000},
\]
and hence residual budget
\[
\mathcal R:=2b-5(c+d)
=\frac{10600448155527496649}{49882042764000000000}>0.
\]

Let \(A_2\) be a two-dimensional convex polygon for which \(Q_N\) and
\(Q_{jk}\) have positive area.  Say that \(A_2\) realizes the three
row-wise retained-axis models if its indicated cell closures contain the
following six intervals:
\[
\begin{array}{ll}
I_{jk}^{u}
=\{(0,3-s,s):1\le s\le1+J\}\subseteq\overline{Q_N},
&
I_{jk}^{w}
=\{(s,3-s,0):1\le s\le1+K\}\subseteq\overline{Q_N},
\\[1mm]
I_{j\ell}^{u}
=\{(0,s,3-s):\frac{253}{375}\le s\le\frac{253}{375}+J\}
\subseteq\overline{Q_N},
&
I_{j\ell}^{v}
=\{(s,0,3-s):\frac{253}{250}\le s\le\frac{253}{250}+L\}
\subseteq\overline{Q_N},
\\[1mm]
I_{k\ell}^{w}
=\{(3+s,-s,0):\frac{43}{375000}\le s
\le\frac{43}{375000}+K\}\subseteq\overline{Q_{jk}},
&
I_{k\ell}^{v}
=\{(3-s,0,s):\frac{43}{5000}\le s
\le\frac{43}{5000}+L\}\subseteq\overline{Q_{jk}}.
\end{array}
\]
These are exactly the two axis intervals in each set \(H_{rs}\) from
the calibrated row-wise construction after embedding its row coordinates
\((u,w)\), \((u,v)\), and \((w,-v)\) in the common plane.

Suppose also that the chosen separators hold in their actual chambers:
\[
u+w\ge1,\qquad
u+\frac32v\ge\frac{253}{250}
\quad\hbox{on }\overline{Q_N},
\]
\[
w-75v\ge\frac{43}{5000}
\quad\hbox{on }\overline{Q_{jk}}.
\]
Then no such \(A_2\) exists.  Consequently the feasible set of the
calibrated convex program minimizing
\[
7|Q_N|+5|Q_{jk}|
\]
is empty.  With the standard extended-real convention its infimum is
\(+\infty>\mathcal R\); equivalently, there is no exact rational \(A_2\)
realizing all three row-wise models within the residual budget.

## Proof

Accepted fact `b431dbefa8a4aec7` identifies the three \(D\)-row
coordinates as
\[
(u,w)\ \hbox{on }\overline{Q_N},\qquad
(u,v)\ \hbox{on }\overline{Q_N},\qquad
(w,-v)\ \hbox{on }\overline{Q_{jk}}.
\]
Accepted fact `decc0d9db44fe9e5`, under the exact-cell convention of
accepted fact `0e51a47a9add571f`, gives the displayed chamber
orientations.  Substituting the three coefficient pairs, the three
\(\chi\)-values, and \(J,L,K\) in the two-axis definition of
\(H_{rs}\) gives exactly the six intervals in the statement.

Only two of those intervals are needed for the contradiction.  Their
near endpoints are
\[
x=(1,2,0)\in I_{jk}^{w}\subseteq A_2
\]
and
\[
y=\left(3+\frac{43}{375000},
-\frac{43}{375000},0\right)
\in I_{k\ell}^{w}\subseteq A_2.
\]
Both lie on the same affine line \(w=0,\ u+v=3\).  By convexity,
\([x,y]\subseteq A_2\).  Put
\[
\varepsilon=\frac{43}{750000}
\]
and
\[
z=(3+\varepsilon,-\varepsilon,0).
\]
Since \(0<\varepsilon<43/375000\), one has
\[
z=(1-\theta)x+\theta y,\qquad
\theta=\frac{2+\varepsilon}{2+43/375000}\in(0,1),
\]
so \(z\in A_2\).

The point \(z\) has \(u>0,v<0,w=0\).  Because \(Q_{jk}\) has positive
area, it contains a point of \(\operatorname{int}A_2\) with
\(u>0,v<0,w>0\).  Convex combinations of that point with \(z\)
approach \(z\) through the strict \(Q_{jk}\) chamber.  Hence
\[
z\in\overline{Q_{jk}}.
\]
The chosen \(k\ell\) separator must therefore hold at \(z\).  But
\[
w(z)-75v(z)
=75\varepsilon
=\frac{43}{10000}
<\frac{43}{5000},
\]
a contradiction.

Thus no convex \(A_2\) realizes the six fixed intervals together with
the three separators.  The exact value of \(\mathcal R\) follows by
putting the displayed \(b,c,d\) over a common denominator.  Since the
joint feasible set is empty, no polygon can have objective at most
\(\mathcal R\).  This proves only the stated failure of the calibrated
simultaneous row model; it makes no assertion about arbitrary retained
interval placements or about the whole opposite branch.
