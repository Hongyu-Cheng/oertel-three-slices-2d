## Statement

Use all hypotheses and notation of accepted fact
`3c17eb0b21eeb63c`.  In particular,
\[
p=|P_\varnothing|>0,\qquad
q_1,q_2,q_{12},q_{123}>0,
\]
\[
M=|A_0|,\qquad m=|A_2|,\qquad
\kappa=|K|<M+m,
\]
and
\[
U_i=\frac{4(M+m)}9-u_i-v_i
\qquad(i=1,2).
\tag{1}
\]
Assume in addition that the endpoint levels \(L_i\) are the genuine
lower crossings in rows \(i=1,2\).

Put
\[
\Delta=-1-(L_1+L_2+L_3)>2,
\qquad
s=\frac{\Delta+2}{\Delta}=1+\frac2\Delta,
\tag{2}
\]
and use the normalized affine coordinates
\[
r_i=\frac{f_i-L_i}{\Delta},\qquad
\tau_i=-\frac{f_i+L_i}{\Delta+2},\qquad
\lambda_i=-\frac{L_i}{\Delta}.
\tag{3}
\]
Then
\[
\sum_i r_i=\sum_i\tau_i=1,\qquad
r_i=2\lambda_i-s\tau_i.
\tag{4}
\]
Use one common affine area normalization in which
\((r_1,r_2)\)-coordinate area is the area below.  An area computed in
the \(\tau\)-plane is therefore multiplied by \(s^2\).

Let
\[
P=\overline{P_\varnothing}
\]
in the \(r\)-plane, let \(A_{2,\tau}\) be the \(\tau\)-image of
\(A_2\), and put
\[
C_\tau=
\operatorname{conv}\bigl(
\overline{Q_1}^{\,\tau}\cup
\overline{Q_2}^{\,\tau}\bigr),
\qquad
H_{12}=\frac12(P+2\lambda-sC_\tau).
\tag{5}
\]
For \(i=1,2\), choose either other barycentric coordinate as a
transverse area coordinate.  Write the nonempty sections as
\[
P_{i,x}=[A_i^P(x),B_i^P(x)]
\quad(x\in X_i),
\]
\[
(C_\tau)_{i,t}=[L_i^C(t),R_i^C(t)]
\quad(t\in T_i),
\tag{6}
\]
and put
\[
w_i^P(x)=B_i^P(x)-A_i^P(x),\qquad
w_i^C(t)=R_i^C(t)-L_i^C(t),
\qquad
\beta_i=\max T_i.
\tag{7}
\]
The positive singleton \(Q_i\) gives
\[
\beta_i>1.
\tag{8}
\]
The opposite singleton \(Q_{3-i}\) contains a point with
\(\tau_i<0\), while \(T_i\) is an interval.  Hence
\[
[0,\beta_i]\subseteq T_i.
\tag{9}
\]
Also \(P\) lies in the standard closed \(r\)-simplex, so
\[
X_i\subseteq[0,1].
\tag{10}
\]

For every \(x\in X_i\), define
\[
\mathcal D_i(x)=
(s\beta_i-x)w_i^P(x)
+s^2\int_{x/s}^{\beta_i}w_i^C(t)\,dt.
\tag{11}
\]
Then
\[
\boxed{\quad
\left|H_{12}\cap\{f_i\leq0\}\right|
\geq\frac14\sup_{x\in X_i}\mathcal D_i(x)
\geq
\frac14\bigl((s\beta_i-1)p+q_i\bigr)
>
\frac14\left(q_i+\frac{2p}{\Delta}\right).
\quad}
\tag{12}
\]
Consequently the sufficient condition
\[
\boxed{\quad
q_i+\frac{2p}{\Delta}\geq4U_i
\quad}
\tag{13}
\]
excludes the canonical branch.  Every survivor satisfies
\[
4U_i>q_i+\frac{2p}{\Delta}
\qquad(i=1,2).
\tag{14}
\]

There is a sharper retained-\(Q_{12}\) conclusion when the active
face of \(P\) is present.  Define
\[
\gamma_C=
s^2\left|
C_\tau\cap
\{\tau_1\geq0,\ \tau_2\geq0,\ \tau_3\leq0\}
\right|_\tau.
\tag{15}
\]
Then \(0\leq\gamma_C\leq q_{12}\).  Whenever \(0\in X_i\), put
\[
\ell_i=w_i^P(0).
\]
Then
\[
\boxed{\quad
\left|H_{12}\cap\{f_i\leq0\}\right|
\geq
\frac14\bigl(s\beta_i\ell_i+q_i+\gamma_C\bigr).
\quad}
\tag{16}
\]
If \(0\in X_1\cap X_2\), every survivor obeys
\[
\gamma_C<
B:=\min_{i=1,2}
\{4U_i-s\beta_i\ell_i-q_i\},
\tag{17}
\]
and therefore
\[
\boxed{\quad
s^2\left|Q_{12}^{\tau}\setminus C_\tau\right|_\tau
=q_{12}-\gamma_C
>q_{12}-B.
\quad}
\tag{18}
\]
In particular, \(B<q_{12}\) forces a strictly positive quantified
unretained doubleton bulge.

The two complete active \(A_2\)-traces are retained in the precise
relative form
\[
\boxed{\quad
A_{2,\tau}\cap\{\tau_1=0\}
\subseteq\overline{Q_2}^{\,\tau}\subseteq C_\tau,
\qquad
A_{2,\tau}\cap\{\tau_2=0\}
\subseteq\overline{Q_1}^{\,\tau}\subseteq C_\tau.
\quad}
\tag{19}
\]
Since \(C_\tau\subseteq A_{2,\tau}\), the two coordinate-line
sections in (19) equal the corresponding sections of \(C_\tau\).

Let \(\ell_{0i}\) be the full \(r\)-coordinate chord length of
\(A_0\cap\{r_i=0\}\), and put
\[
\ell_{2i}=w_i^C(0).
\]
Whenever \(0\in X_i\),
\[
\boxed{\quad
\ell_i\leq\ell_{0i}\leq s\ell_{2i}.
\quad}
\tag{20}
\]
Concavity of the \(C_\tau\)-section profile further gives
\[
q_i+\gamma_C
\geq\frac{s^2\beta_i}{2}\ell_{2i}
\geq\frac{s\beta_i}{2}\ell_i,
\tag{21}
\]
and hence
\[
\left|H_{12}\cap\{f_i\leq0\}\right|
\geq\frac{3s\beta_i\ell_i}{8}.
\tag{22}
\]

## Proof

Fix \(i\in\{1,2\}\) and \(x\in X_i\).  Equations (2), (8), and
(10) give
\[
0\leq\frac{x}{s}<1<\beta_i.
\tag{23}
\]
Equation (9) therefore shows that the whole interval
\[
\left[\frac{x}{s},\beta_i\right]
\]
belongs to \(T_i\).  For
\[
\frac{x}{s}\leq t\leq\beta_i,
\qquad
u=x-st,
\tag{24}
\]
one has \(u\leq0\), \(t\in T_i\), and \(u+st=x\in X_i\).
In the notation of the exact two-rail identity in accepted fact
`cb3cba490b10885c`, choose
\[
t_-(u)=t_+(u)=\frac{x-u}{s}.
\tag{25}
\]
The corresponding integrand is
\[
\Phi_i(u;t_-(u),t_+(u))
=w_i^P(x)+s\,w_i^C\!\left(\frac{x-u}{s}\right)
\geq0.
\tag{26}
\]
Integrating (26) on \(x-s\beta_i\leq u\leq0\), using
\(du=-s\,dt\), and applying accepted fact
`cb3cba490b10885c` gives
\[
\begin{aligned}
\left|H_{12}\cap\{f_i\leq0\}\right|
&\geq\frac14
\left[
(s\beta_i-x)w_i^P(x)
+s^2\int_{x/s}^{\beta_i}w_i^C(t)\,dt
\right]\\
&=\frac14\mathcal D_i(x).
\end{aligned}
\tag{27}
\]
Taking the supremum proves the first inequality in (12).

Every strict point of \(Q_i\) has
\[
\tau_i>1,
\tag{28}
\]
because its other two \(\tau\)-coordinates are negative and the
coordinates sum to one.  Thus the whole cell \(Q_i\) lies in the
tail \(\{\tau_i\geq x/s\}\), and
\[
s^2\int_{x/s}^{\beta_i}w_i^C(t)\,dt\geq q_i.
\tag{29}
\]
Also
\[
p=\int_{X_i}w_i^P(x)\,dx,\qquad |X_i|\leq1.
\tag{30}
\]
The nonnegative function
\[
g_i(x)=(s\beta_i-x)w_i^P(x)
\]
satisfies
\[
\begin{aligned}
\max_{x\in X_i}g_i(x)
&\geq\frac1{|X_i|}\int_{X_i}g_i(x)\,dx\\
&\geq\int_{X_i}g_i(x)\,dx\\
&\geq(s\beta_i-1)p.
\end{aligned}
\tag{31}
\]
Choose a maximizer in (31) and use (29) at the same section.  This
proves the second inequality in (12).  Since \(\beta_i>1\) and
\(s-1=2/\Delta\), the last inequality in (12) is strict.

Accepted fact `3c17eb0b21eeb63c` gives
\[
H_{12}\subseteq K,\qquad
\left|K\cap\{f_i\leq0\}\right|<U_i.
\tag{32}
\]
Equations (12)-(13) contradict (32), proving the exclusion gate and
the necessary reverse (14).

For (16), set \(x=0\) in (27).  Both
\(\overline{Q_1}^{\,\tau}\) and
\(\overline{Q_2}^{\,\tau}\) lie in \(\{\tau_3\leq0\}\), hence so
does \(C_\tau\).  Convexity gives
\[
C_\tau\subseteq A_{2,\tau}.
\tag{33}
\]
Up to null coordinate lines,
\[
C_\tau\cap\{\tau_1\geq0\}
=Q_1^\tau\mathbin{\dot\cup}
\left(C_\tau\cap Q_{12}^\tau\right),
\tag{34}
\]
and symmetrically in row \(2\).  Therefore
\[
s^2\int_0^{\beta_i}w_i^C(t)\,dt=q_i+\gamma_C.
\tag{35}
\]
Equations (27) and (35) prove (16).  Combining (16) with (32) in
both rows gives (17).  Inclusion (33) shows that the retained part of
\(Q_{12}\) has area \(\gamma_C\), so subtraction proves (18).

We now prove (19).  Suppose
\[
z\in A_{2,\tau}\cap\{\tau_1=0\}.
\]
Choose an interior point \(y\) of the positive-area cell \(Q_2\), so
\[
\tau_1(y)<0,\qquad\tau_2(y)>0,\qquad\tau_3(y)<0.
\tag{36}
\]
If \(\tau_2(z)<0\), then for sufficiently small
\(\varepsilon>0\),
\[
z_\varepsilon=(1-\varepsilon)z+\varepsilon y
\]
has strict signs
\[
\tau_1(z_\varepsilon)<0,\qquad
\tau_2(z_\varepsilon)<0,\qquad
\tau_3(z_\varepsilon)>0.
\]
If \(0\leq\tau_2(z)<1\), the same point instead has strict signs
\[
\tau_1(z_\varepsilon)<0,\qquad
\tau_2(z_\varepsilon)>0,\qquad
\tau_3(z_\varepsilon)>0.
\]
In either case, \(z_\varepsilon\) is an interior point of
\(A_{2,\tau}\): a strict convex combination of a point of a convex
body with one of its interior points is interior.  The first sign
pattern would give the unsupported cell \(Q_3\) positive area, and
the second would give the unsupported cell \(Q_{23}\) positive area.
Both contradict the exact support.  Hence
\[
\tau_2(z)\geq1,\qquad\tau_3(z)\leq0.
\tag{37}
\]

For any \(0<\varepsilon<1\), the same convex combination
\[
z_\varepsilon=(1-\varepsilon)z+\varepsilon y
\]
now satisfies
\[
\tau_1(z_\varepsilon)<0,\qquad
\tau_2(z_\varepsilon)>0,\qquad
\tau_3(z_\varepsilon)<0,
\]
where strict negativity of \(\tau_3\) follows from (36) and
\(\tau_3(z)\leq0\).  Thus \(z_\varepsilon\in Q_2^\tau\) and
\(z_\varepsilon\to z\).  Therefore
\[
z\in\overline{Q_2}^{\,\tau}.
\]
This proves the first inclusion in (19).  Interchanging indices
\(1,2\) and perturbing toward an interior point of \(Q_1\) proves the
second.  Combining (19) with (33) proves equality of the coordinate
line sections.

It remains to prove (20)-(22).  Increase the row-\(i\) endpoint level
from \(L_i\) to \(L_i+\eta\).  In the coordinates (3), the
\(A_0\)-cap gains the strip
\[
0<r_i\leq\frac{\eta}{\Delta},
\]
whereas the \(A_2\)-cap loses the strip
\[
0\leq\tau_i<\frac{\eta}{\Delta+2}.
\]
The finite-interior conditions make \(r_i=0\) cross
\(\operatorname{int}A_0\).  The positive cells \(Q_i,Q_{3-i}\) lie
on opposite sides of \(\tau_i=0\), so that line crosses
\(\operatorname{int}A_{2,\tau}\).  Polygonal chord continuity and
Cavalieri integration give the right first-order change
\[
\frac{\eta}{\Delta}
\left(\ell_{0i}-s\ell_{2i}\right)+o(\eta).
\tag{38}
\]
At a genuine lower crossing there are values arbitrarily close on
the right where the endpoint-cap sum is smaller than its crossing
value.  Equation (38) forces
\[
\ell_{0i}\leq s\ell_{2i}.
\tag{39}
\]
The \(P\)-section is contained in the full \(A_0\)-section, so
\(\ell_i\leq\ell_{0i}\), proving (20).

Finally, \(w_i^C\) is concave on \(T_i\).  Since
\([0,\beta_i]\subseteq T_i\), for \(0\leq t\leq\beta_i\),
\[
w_i^C(t)\geq
\left(1-\frac{t}{\beta_i}\right)w_i^C(0).
\tag{40}
\]
Integrating (40), using (35), and then using (20), gives
\[
q_i+\gamma_C
\geq\frac{s^2\beta_i}{2}\ell_{2i}
\geq\frac{s\beta_i}{2}\ell_i.
\]
This proves (21), and substituting it in (16) proves (22).
