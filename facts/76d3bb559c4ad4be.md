## Statement

In the realization of accepted fact `4b7d14106fb321c7`, write the ambient coordinates as \((z,r,\omega)\), so the three slices are \(\{0\}\times A_0\), \(\{1\}\times K\), and \(\{2\}\times A_2\), where
\[
A_0=\left[\frac{203}{365},\frac{2352}{365}\right]\times
\left[-\frac12,\frac12\right],
\quad
K=\left[-\frac{81}{365},\frac{5637}{730}\right]\times
\left[-\frac{101}{400},\frac{101}{400}\right],
\quad
A_2=[-1,9]\times\left[-\frac1{200},\frac1{200}\right].
\]
Let
\[
y^\star=\left(0,\frac72,0\right).
\]
Then
\[
h_S(y^\star)=\frac{2149}{730}
>\frac{2T}{9}
=\frac{162211}{73000}.
\]

## Proof

The \(r\)-endpoints of \(A_0\) sum to
\[
\frac{203}{365}+\frac{2352}{365}=7,
\]
and its \(\omega\)-endpoints sum to \(0\). Hence \((7/2,0)\) is the center of \(A_0\), so \(y^\star\in S\). Also
\[
|A_0|=\left(\frac{2352-203}{365}\right)\left(\frac12-\left(-\frac12\right)\right)
=\frac{2149}{365}.
\]

Let \(H\) be any closed halfspace containing \(y^\star\). It can be written
\[
H=\left\{(z,r,\omega):
\alpha z+\beta\left(r-\frac72\right)+\gamma\omega\le\delta\right\},
\]
where \((\alpha,\beta,\gamma)\ne(0,0,0)\) and \(\delta\ge0\). The halfspace
\[
H_0=\left\{(z,r,\omega):
\alpha z+\beta\left(r-\frac72\right)+\gamma\omega\le0\right\}
\]
contains \(y^\star\) and is contained in \(H\). It therefore suffices to bound the measure of \(S\cap H_0\).

All normal-direction chambers and their boundaries reduce as follows. If
\((\beta,\gamma)\ne(0,0)\), the trace of \(H_0\) on the zeroth slice is
\[
A_0\cap\left\{(r,\omega):
\beta\left(r-\frac72\right)+\gamma\omega\le0\right\}.
\]
The four open sign chambers for \((\beta,\gamma)\), namely the four choices
\(\beta\gtrless0\), \(\gamma\gtrless0\), and the four boundary rays on which
exactly one of \(\beta,\gamma\) vanishes exhaust this nonzero stratum.
For every one of these chambers and boundary rays, the central reflection
\[
(r,\omega)\longmapsto(7-r,-\omega)
\]
preserves \(A_0\), preserves planar area, and exchanges the two strict sides
of the cutting line. The line itself has planar area zero. Consequently, in
every nonzero in-plane normal direction, including all chamber boundaries,
\[
\left|A_0\cap H_0\right|=\frac{|A_0|}{2}
=\frac{2149}{730}.
\]
The coefficient \(\alpha\) is arbitrary and does not affect this zeroth-slice
trace. On the remaining normal-direction stratum
\((\beta,\gamma)=(0,0)\), one has \(\alpha\ne0\), the whole zeroth slice lies
on the boundary of \(H_0\), and therefore
\[
\left|A_0\cap H_0\right|=|A_0|=\frac{2149}{365}.
\]
These cases exhaust every nonzero normal, while the contributions from the
first and second slices are always nonnegative. Hence every closed halfspace
containing \(y^\star\) has
\[
\mathcal H_2(S\cap H)\ge\frac{2149}{730}.
\]

For the reverse inequality, consider the closed halfspace
\[
H^\star=\left\{(z,r,\omega):r+4z\le\frac72\right\}.
\]
It contains \(y^\star\). On the zeroth slice it cuts \(A_0\) through its
center and contributes exactly \(2149/730\). On the first slice its trace is
\(r\le-1/2\), which misses \(K\) because
\[
-\frac{81}{365}>-\frac12.
\]
On the second slice its trace is \(r\le-9/2\), which misses \(A_2\) because
\(-1>-9/2\). Thus
\[
\mathcal H_2(S\cap H^\star)=\frac{2149}{730},
\]
which proves the exact depth formula.

Finally, accepted fact `4b7d14106fb321c7` gives
\[
\frac{2T}{9}=\frac{162211}{73000}.
\]
Therefore
\[
h_S(y^\star)-\frac{2T}{9}
=\frac{214900-162211}{73000}
=\frac{52689}{73000}>0.
\]
