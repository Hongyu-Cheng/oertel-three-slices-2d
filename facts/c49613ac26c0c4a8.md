## Statement

Let \(N=\{1,2,3\}\), and assume the affine sign-plane hypotheses and exact-cell convention of accepted fact `0e51a47a9add571f`.  To avoid a collision with normalized side lengths below, write \(\Lambda_i\) for the offsets denoted by \(L_i\) in that fact.  Thus
\[
f_1+f_2+f_3=-1,\qquad
s:=\Lambda_1+\Lambda_2+\Lambda_3<-1.
\]
Suppose that the only positive-area \(Q\)-cells of the two-dimensional convex polygon \(A_2\) are
\[
Q_{123},\quad Q_{12},\quad Q_{13},\quad Q_{23},
\]
and prescribe
\[
q:=|Q_{123}|>0,\qquad
x_i:=|Q_{N\setminus\{i\}}|>0\quad(i=1,2,3).
\]
Apply the endpoint-preserving finite boundary reduction of accepted fact
`b7cb6541c1d790bc`.  Put
\[
\gamma=1-s>2,\qquad
\alpha_i=-\frac{f_i+\Lambda_i}{\gamma}.
\]
Then \(\sum_i\alpha_i=1\), and the global \(Q\)-triangle becomes
\[
\Delta=\{\alpha_1,\alpha_2,\alpha_3\geq0,\ \alpha_1+\alpha_2+\alpha_3=1\}.
\]
Use \((\alpha_1,\alpha_2)\) as planar coordinates, and denote the barycentric
vertices by \(e_1,e_2,e_3\).

Every reduced polygon is represented by, and every tuple in the system below
reconstructs, an \(A_2\)-only geometry with the prescribed four areas.  The
variables are
\[
a_{ij}\quad(i\ne j),\qquad
\rho_{12},\rho_{23},\rho_{31},\qquad
\theta_{12},\theta_{23},\theta_{31},\qquad
\tau_1,\tau_2,\tau_3.
\]
With cyclic indices \(i^-=i-1\) and \(i^+=i+1\), define
\[
L_i=1-a_{i,i^-}-a_{i,i^+}.
\]
Their domains and endpoint-pair strata are
\[
a_{ij}\geq0,\qquad L_i>0,
\]
\[
(a_{ij},a_{ji})=(0,0)
\quad\hbox{or}\quad
a_{ij}>0,\ a_{ji}>0
\qquad ((i,j)=(1,2),(2,3),(3,1)),
\]
\[
0\leq\rho_{ij}\leq1,\qquad
0\leq\theta_{ij}\leq1
\quad ((i,j)=(1,2),(2,3),(3,1)),
\qquad
0<\tau_i<1.
\]
Thus the endpoint condition is the explicit union of the eight strata
obtained by independently choosing the first or second alternative for the
three cyclic pairs.

Define the polynomial
\[
D
=1-(1-\rho_{12})a_{12}a_{21}
  -(1-\rho_{23})a_{23}a_{32}
  -(1-\rho_{31})a_{31}a_{13},
\qquad D>0,
\]
and, with no additional free height variables, put
\[
h_i=\frac{D x_i}{qL_i}>0.
\]
For distinct \(i,j\), let \(k\) be the unique element of
\(N\setminus\{i,j\}\) and set
\[
p_{ij}=(1-a_{ij})e_k+a_{ij}e_j.
\]
For \((i,j)=(1,2),(2,3),(3,1)\), with the same missing index \(k\), set
\[
z_{ij}
=\rho_{ij}e_k
 +(1-\rho_{ij})
  \bigl((1-\theta_{ij})p_{ij}+\theta_{ij}p_{ji}\bigr),
\]
and set
\[
u_i
=-h_i e_i
 +(1+h_i)\bigl((1-\tau_i)e_{i^-}+\tau_i e_{i^+}\bigr).
\]
Form the cyclic labeled list
\[
(V_1,\ldots,V_{12})
=
(p_{13},u_1,p_{12},z_{12},p_{21},u_2,
  p_{23},z_{23},p_{32},u_3,p_{31},z_{31}),
\qquad V_{13}=V_1.
\]
For planar vectors let \([v,w]=v_1w_2-v_2w_1\).  After substituting all the
preceding definitions, define the explicit polynomial
\[
\Phi_{r,s}
=q^2L_1L_2L_3
 [V_{r+1}-V_r,V_s-V_r]
\qquad(1\leq r,s\leq12),
\]
where the right side is expanded after the substitution.  It is a polynomial
in the fifteen variables and in the positive parameters \(q,x_1,x_2,x_3\).
The remaining constraints are
\[
\Phi_{r,s}\geq0\qquad(1\leq r,s\leq12).
\]
The labeled list may have coincident consecutive entries, collinear entries,
or entries redundant in the geometric vertex list; no strict convexity is
imposed.

The coordinate cell areas of every feasible tuple are exactly
\[
|\widehat Q_{123}|=\frac D2,\qquad
|\widehat Q_{N\setminus\{i\}}|
=\frac{D x_i}{2q}\quad(i=1,2,3),
\]
and every other coordinate \(Q\)-cell has area zero.  An affine map whose
area multiplier is
\[
J=\frac{2q}{D}
\]
therefore reconstructs the prescribed physical areas \(q,x_1,x_2,x_3\), and
the physical global triangle has the exact area
\[
|\mathcal T_Q|=\frac J2=\frac qD.
\]

If the same cutting lines occur in a full common-plane realization with
\(p_\varnothing>0\), \(r_{123}>0\), and all three middle singleton cells of
positive area, then necessarily
\[
D\leq
\frac{q}{\bigl(\sqrt{p_\varnothing}
       +2\sqrt{r_{123}}\bigr)^2}.
\]
Equivalently, because \(D,p_\varnothing,r_{123}>0\), this last condition is
the polynomial semialgebraic pair
\[
q-D(p_\varnothing+4r_{123})\geq0,
\]
\[
\bigl(q-D(p_\varnothing+4r_{123})\bigr)^2
\geq16D^2p_\varnothing r_{123}.
\]

For \(q=2/5\) and \(x_1=x_2=x_3=1/5\), relabeling \(a_{ij}\) as \(x_{ij}\)
gives exactly the endpoint-stratum convention and the normal form of accepted
fact `eef70ce3abe7606e`: \(h_i=D/(2L_i)\), the cap area is \(D/4\), the
central area is \(D/2\), the coordinate total is \(5D/4\), and
\(|\mathcal T_Q|=2/(5D)\).  With the strict rational common-plane values in
accepted fact `b1eaab0ae37367d1`, the scale inequality specializes to
\(D\leq4/169\).

## Proof

The positive central cell and the three positive caps lie on the two sides
of each cutting line, so every line \(\alpha_i=0\) crosses the polygon
interior in a nondegenerate chord.  The interiors of two different outer
caps are disjoint: a positive-area intersection with
\(\alpha_i<0,\alpha_j<0\) would be a positive forbidden \(Q\)-cell.  Thus
accepted fact `b7cb6541c1d790bc` applies and preserves all six geometric
chord endpoints, all four prescribed areas, and the closed sign chamber of
every labeled boundary arc, while leaving at most one unmarked vertex on
each of the six arcs.  We work with that reduced polygon.

Fix \(i\).  Each endpoint of its chord lies on the corresponding side of
\(\Delta\), not on an extension of that side.  Indeed, if such an endpoint
had \(\alpha_j<0\) for \(j\ne i\), then a small relative neighborhood inside
the polygon on the side \(\alpha_i<0\) would have both coordinates negative
and would give a positive overlap of two cap interiors.  Hence the two
endpoints on \(\alpha_i=0\) have unique expressions \(p_{i,i^-}\) and
\(p_{i,i^+}\) of the displayed form.  Their nondegeneracy and boundary order
say exactly
\[
1-a_{i,i^-}-a_{i,i^+}=L_i>0.
\]
Endpoints belonging to different cutting lines can coincide only at their
common barycentric vertex.  If \(p_{ij}=e_k\), then \(e_k\) belongs to the
chord on \(\alpha_j=0\); since that chord is contained in the side of
\(\Delta\), its endpoint nearest \(e_k\) is also \(e_k\), so \(p_{ji}=e_k\).
The converse is immediate.  Thus
\[
a_{ij}=0\quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad a_{ji}=0
\]
for each cyclic pair, including all eight coincidence strata.

Consider the reduced central arc from \(p_{ij}\) to \(p_{ji}\), where \(k\)
is the missing index.  The chord joining these two points lies in the convex
central cell.  The complementary boundary arc passes through the other four
chord labels and is on the side of this chord opposite \(e_k\).  Therefore
the short central arc is contained in
\[
\operatorname{conv}\{e_k,p_{ij},p_{ji}\}.
\]
Its possible single unmarked vertex consequently has exactly the stated
form \(z_{ij}\) with \(0\leq\rho_{ij},\theta_{ij}\leq1\).  This remains
valid on every boundary stratum: \(\rho_{ij}=0\) puts the label on the
endpoint chord, \(\rho_{ij}=1\) puts it at \(e_k\), and a zero
\(a_{ij}a_{ji}\) collapses the corresponding corner triangle.

The positive cap on side \(i\) forces its reduced outer arc to have exactly
one outer vertex \(u_i\).  Write \(-h_i<0\) for its \(i\)-th barycentric
coordinate.  Its other two barycentric coordinates are strictly positive.
They cannot be negative because that would give a positive overlap with
another cap, and they cannot vanish because \(u_i\) would then be a chord
endpoint of another cutting line lying on an extension of the corresponding
side of \(\Delta\), which was just excluded.  Since their sum is \(1+h_i\),
there is a unique \(0<\tau_i<1\) giving the stated expression for \(u_i\).
Following the six outer and central arcs in their actual order gives the
displayed twelve-label cyclic list.  A counterclockwise convex boundary list
has every listed point in the closed left halfplane of every directed
consecutive edge, so all determinant inequalities are necessary.  Equality
allows precisely the asserted collinear, coincident, and redundant labels.

It remains to calculate the areas and thereby eliminate the heights.  The
six chord marks alone bound the central hexagon
\[
(p_{12},p_{21},p_{23},p_{32},p_{31},p_{13}).
\]
It is obtained from the coordinate triangle of area \(1/2\) by deleting the
three corner triangles of areas
\[
\frac{a_{12}a_{21}}2,\qquad
\frac{a_{23}a_{32}}2,\qquad
\frac{a_{31}a_{13}}2.
\]
Replacing the corner chord \(p_{ij}p_{ji}\) by the two edges through
\(z_{ij}\) restores
\[
\frac{\rho_{ij}a_{ij}a_{ji}}2.
\]
This expression is independent of \(\theta_{ij}\), since \(\rho_{ij}\) is
the affine height fraction from the endpoint chord to \(e_k\).  Hence the
coordinate central-cell area is exactly
\[
\frac12\left(1-\sum_{(i,j)\in\{(1,2),(2,3),(3,1)\}}
 (1-\rho_{ij})a_{ij}a_{ji}\right)=\frac D2.
\]
The cap on side \(i\) is a triangle whose base has side fraction \(L_i\) and
whose barycentric height is \(h_i\), so its coordinate area is
\[
\frac{L_i h_i}{2}.
\]
Let \(J>0\) be the area multiplier from normalized coordinates to the
original physical plane.  The prescribed areas give
\[
q=J\frac D2,\qquad
x_i=J\frac{L_i h_i}{2}.
\]
Since \(q,L_i>0\), division is legitimate, and these equations are
equivalent to
\[
h_i=\frac{D x_i}{qL_i}.
\]
They also give \(J=2q/D\) and
\[
|\mathcal T_Q|=J|\Delta|=\frac qD.
\]
In particular, a geometry with \(q>0\) necessarily has \(D>0\).

Only the three \(u_i\) contain denominators, and \(u_i\) has denominator
\(qL_i\).  An oriented three-point area
\([V_{r+1}-V_r,V_s-V_r]\) is a sum of pairwise determinants, so it contains
at most two distinct such denominators and never \(L_i^2\).  The already
proved signs
\[
q>0,\qquad L_1L_2L_3>0
\]
show that multiplication by \(q^2L_1L_2L_3\) both clears every denominator
and preserves every inequality.  Thus the \(\Phi_{r,s}\) are the claimed
explicit polynomial convex-order constraints.

Conversely, take any tuple satisfying the system.  Remove no labels, but
interpret repeated or collinear labels as redundant occurrences.  For every
nonzero directed consecutive edge, the determinant constraints put all
listed points in its closed left halfplane.  Thus the cyclic list is the
counterclockwise boundary list of its convex hull, with redundant boundary
labels allowed.  The calculation above gives a central subpolygon of area
\(D/2>0\), so the hull is two-dimensional and the list cannot collapse to a
line.

For a fixed \(i\), all listed vertices other than \(u_i\) have
\(\alpha_i\geq0\); the only strictly negative vertex \(u_i\) is flanked by
the two points \(p_{i,i^-},p_{i,i^+}\) on \(\alpha_i=0\).  Clipping this
convex boundary by \(\alpha_i\leq0\) therefore gives exactly
\[
\operatorname{conv}\{p_{i,i^-},u_i,p_{i,i^+}\}.
\]
Every point of its relative interior with \(\alpha_i<0\) has positive
\(\alpha_j\) for both \(j\ne i\), because its convex coefficient at \(u_i\)
is positive and both of those coordinates of \(u_i\) are positive.  Hence
the three cap interiors are pairwise disjoint and no forbidden sign cell has
positive area.  Clipping by all three central halfplanes leaves the central
hexagon with the three \(z\)-arcs.  The preceding shoelace calculation,
valid unchanged for zero corner triangles and redundant labels, gives
\[
|\widehat Q_{123}|=\frac D2,\qquad
|\widehat Q_{N\setminus\{i\}}|=\frac{D x_i}{2q}.
\]

Choose an orientation-preserving affine map with area multiplier
\(J=2q/D\).  It produces physical areas \(q,x_1,x_2,x_3\) and global-triangle
area \(q/D\).  This is also a reconstruction inside the original sign-plane
class, not merely an abstract labeled polygon.  Indeed, choose any
\(\gamma_0>2\), choose offsets \(\Lambda_i^0\) with
\(\sum_i\Lambda_i^0=1-\gamma_0<-1\), transport the barycentric functions by
the affine map, and define
\[
f_i^0=-\gamma_0\alpha_i-\Lambda_i^0.
\]
Then \(\sum_i f_i^0=-1\), their linear parts are pairwise nonparallel, and
\[
f_i^0\leq-\Lambda_i^0
\quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad
\alpha_i\geq0.
\]
Thus the reconstructed polygon has exactly the prescribed \(Q\)-cells under
the exact convention of accepted fact `0e51a47a9add571f`.  Cutting-line
pieces have zero planar area, so all weak-boundary assignments and all
coincident-label strata are covered.  No reconstruction of \(A_0\) or \(K\)
is asserted by this \(A_2\)-only converse.

Finally suppose a full common-plane realization exists.  Put
\[
\delta=-1-s>0,\qquad \gamma=1-s=\delta+2.
\]
Because all three middle singleton cells have positive area, accepted fact
`ce343e749df349fe` gives
\[
\mathcal T_1:=\{f_i\leq0\ (i=1,2,3)\}\subseteq K,\qquad
R_{123}=\mathcal T_1
\]
up to cutting lines.  Therefore
\[
|\mathcal T_1|=r_{123}.
\]
The affine sign-plane map from accepted fact `0e51a47a9add571f` shows that
\[
\mathcal T_P:=\{f_i\geq\Lambda_i\ (i=1,2,3)\}
\]
is a translated reflected dilation of \(\mathcal T_1\) by factor \(\delta\),
whereas \(\mathcal T_Q\) is a translated dilation by factor
\(\gamma=\delta+2\).  Hence
\[
|\mathcal T_P|=\delta^2r_{123},\qquad
|\mathcal T_Q|=(\delta+2)^2r_{123}.
\]
Since \(P_\varnothing\) is contained in the interior of
\(\mathcal T_P\) up to zero-area cutting lines,
\[
p_\varnothing\leq\delta^2r_{123}.
\]
The quantities \(\delta,r_{123}\) are positive, so
\[
\delta\geq\sqrt{\frac{p_\varnothing}{r_{123}}}.
\]
Combining this with \(|\mathcal T_Q|=q/D\) gives
\[
\frac qD
\geq
\left(\sqrt{\frac{p_\varnothing}{r_{123}}}+2\right)^2r_{123}
=\bigl(\sqrt{p_\varnothing}+2\sqrt{r_{123}}\bigr)^2,
\]
which is the asserted scale inequality.  Moving the radical term to one
side and squaring is reversible because that side is required nonnegative;
this gives exactly the displayed polynomial pair.

For the equal-cap specialization,
\[
\frac{D(1/5)}{(2/5)L_i}=\frac{D}{2L_i},
\]
and all coordinate-area and affine-scaling formulas reduce term by term to
accepted fact `eef70ce3abe7606e`, with the same eight endpoint strata.  The
strict rational values recorded in accepted fact `b1eaab0ae37367d1` are
\(p_\varnothing=121/10\) and \(r_{123}=1/10\), so
\[
\bigl(\sqrt{p_\varnothing}+2\sqrt{r_{123}}\bigr)^2
=\frac{169}{10}.
\]
With \(q=2/5\), the general scale inequality becomes \(D\leq4/169\), as
claimed.
