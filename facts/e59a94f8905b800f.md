## Statement

Use the normalized systems of accepted fact `b046e3066136e781`, the reduced word automaton of accepted fact `46afc7eb815ffc5b`, and the surviving interior/interior row of accepted fact `2833a8e581bcc380`. For every \(\eta\in\{0,1,2\}\) and every surviving label vector
\[
\sigma_P\in\mathcal A,\qquad
\sigma_Q\in\mathcal C,\qquad
\sigma_R\in\mathcal D,
\]
the system \(\mathcal S_{\eta,\mathrm I,\mathrm I,\sigma}\) is infeasible if
\[
\sigma_{P2}=\mathrm P
\quad\text{or}\quad
\sigma_{P4}=\mathrm P.
\]
Among the mixed-label, lexicographically symmetry-reduced surviving systems, exactly \(17442\) satisfy this condition.

## Proof

Fix a surviving system. Accepted fact `b046e3066136e781` normalizes \(|P|/\Lambda=1\) and identifies
\[
\alpha_P=\frac{|P\cap\{y\le0\}|}{\Lambda},
\qquad
\beta_P=\frac{|P\cap\{x+y\ge0\}|}{\Lambda},
\]
with the crossing equalities
\[
\alpha_P+\alpha_Q+\alpha_R=t,
\qquad
\beta_P+\beta_Q+\beta_R=t.
\]

If \(\sigma_{P2}=\mathrm P\), the position-2 endpoint pair
\[
(-\upsilon_{P0},-\upsilon_{P1})
\]
has both entries nonnegative. Since \(U_P\) is affine, \(U_P(x)\le0\) throughout the support of \(P\). Hence \(P\subseteq\{y\le0\}\), so
\[
\alpha_P=1.
\]
Because all caps are nonnegative, the first crossing equality gives \(t\ge1\).

If instead \(\sigma_{P4}=\mathrm P\), the position-4 endpoint pair
\[
(\lambda_{P0}+x_{P0},\lambda_{P1}+x_{P1})
\]
has both entries nonnegative. The affine function \(L_P(x)+x\) is therefore nonnegative throughout the support of \(P\). Every \((x,y)\in P\) satisfies
\[
x+y\ge x+L_P(x)\ge0,
\]
so \(\beta_P=1\). The second crossing equality again gives \(t\ge1\).

It remains to contradict this bound in every residual branch. Put \(s=m+k\). In branch \(\eta=0\),
\[
m=q^2,\qquad q\le\frac35,\qquad k<1+q^2,
\]
and hence
\[
s<1+2q^2\le\frac{43}{25}<2.
\]
In branch \(\eta=1\),
\[
2q^2\le1,\qquad k<1+q^2,
\]
so
\[
s<1+2q^2\le2.
\]
In branch \(\eta=2\),
\[
k<2-q^2=2-m,
\]
so again \(s<2\). The normalization equation of accepted fact `b046e3066136e781` now gives
\[
t=\frac{2(1+s)}9<\frac23<1,
\]
contradicting \(t\ge1\). Thus every system satisfying the stated word condition is infeasible.

For the exact count, abbreviate \(X=\mathrm C_+\) and \(Y=\mathrm C_-\). Reading the \(\mathcal A\)-table of accepted fact `46afc7eb815ffc5b`, the words satisfying
\[
\sigma_{P2}=\mathrm P
\quad\text{or}\quad
\sigma_{P4}=\mathrm P
\]
are exactly
\[
NNPP,\quad PPNN,\quad PPXN,\quad PPXX,\quad XNPP,\quad XXPP.
\]
Thus there are six such \(P\)-words, exactly two of them are \(\{\mathrm N,\mathrm P\}\)-only, and none is fixed by
\[
\pi(\sigma_1,\sigma_2,\sigma_3,\sigma_4)
=(\sigma_3,\sigma_4,\sigma_1,\sigma_2).
\]
This six-word set is \(\pi\)-invariant.

Accepted fact `2833a8e581bcc380` leaves precisely the word triples
\[
\mathcal A\times\mathcal C\times\mathcal D.
\]
Using the counts from accepted fact `46afc7eb815ffc5b`, for each fixed \(\eta\) the filtered family contains
\[
6\cdot54\cdot36=11664
\]
word triples before imposing the mixed-label condition. Exactly
\[
2\cdot6\cdot3=36
\]
are \(\{\mathrm N,\mathrm P\}\)-only. Hence there are \(11628\) mixed triples. No filtered triple is fixed by \(\pi\), because its \(P\)-word is not fixed. Burnside's formula, equivalently the lexicographic orbit selection, therefore gives
\[
\frac{11628}{2}=5814
\]
surviving indexed systems removed for each \(\eta\). Multiplying by the three residual indices gives
\[
3\cdot5814=17442.
\]
Consequently this filter leaves
\[
157512-17442=140070
\]
interior/interior systems.
