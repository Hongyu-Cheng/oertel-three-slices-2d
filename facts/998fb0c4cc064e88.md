## Statement

Work in the five \(t\leq1/2\) sign chambers recorded in accepted fact
`86e9bf31e8f5df7a`.  The union of the endpoint-independent sufficient
regions in accepted facts `37652924248e8019`,
`7a3fb456fc217c52`, `86e9bf31e8f5df7a`, and
`b91db809305bf041` does not cover the admissible nondegenerate middle
and scalar domain.

An exact rational survivor is
\[
A=\frac1{10},\qquad
B=\frac{10999}{10000},\qquad
t=\frac{2(1+A+B)}9=\frac{7333}{15000},
\tag{1}
\]
\[
L=
\begin{pmatrix}
-584&1167&-584\\
-415&-416&830\\
1000&-750&-245
\end{pmatrix}.
\tag{2}
\]
Its middle caps are
\[
c_1=\frac{1361889}{3066001},\qquad
c_2=\frac{830}{1869},\qquad
c_3=\frac{800}{1743}.
\tag{3}
\]
The rational scalar endpoint fractions
\[
(u_1,u_2,u_3)=
\left(\frac49,\frac49,\frac19\right),
\qquad
(v_1,v_2,v_3)=(0,0,0)
\tag{4}
\]
satisfy every strict scalar row
\[
Au_i+c_i+Bv_i<t
\qquad(i=1,2,3).
\tag{5}
\]
The data satisfy the strict residual strip and all necessary middle
cap, total-budget, and scalar restrictions inherited by the
five-chamber domain.  They deliberately fail only sufficient gate
hypotheses, as audited below.

Nevertheless:

1. the cap-only region of accepted fact `7a3fb456fc217c52` does not
   apply because \(t>19/40\);
2. the criterion of accepted fact `37652924248e8019` does not apply
   because all three allowances \(\alpha_i=(t-c_i)/A\) exceed \(1/4\);
3. the monotone threshold-sum consequence of accepted fact
   `b91db809305bf041` does not apply because every \(\alpha_i\) exceeds
   its lower two-cap level \(4222159/14284500\);
4. the radical sufficient condition of accepted fact
   `86e9bf31e8f5df7a` fails strictly for the common one-positive row.

All inequalities are strict, so (1)-(4) lie in an open uncovered
semialgebraic cell, not only at an isolated boundary point.

## Proof

We first describe the exact chamber audit.  Up to the accepted row
and column symmetries, the five open support pairs are
\[
\begin{array}{c|ccccc}
\text{chamber}&1&2&3&4&5\\ \hline

\{2\}&\{2\}&\{2\}&\{1,2\}&\{2,3\}\\
J_2&
\{3\}&\{1,3\}&\{2,3\}&\{2,3\}&\{2,3\}.
\end{array}
\tag{6}
\]
For the rational slice (1), fix the common third row
\[
g=(1000,-750,-245).
\tag{7}
\]
Write the first row as \((x,y,z)\).  The column-sum identities force
the second row to be
\[
(-999-x,\ 751-y,\ 246-z).
\tag{8}

For each column of (6), impose its literal strict sign conditions,
both negative row-mean conditions
\[

\tag{9}

the appropriate one- or two-positive exact cap formulas, nonzero
determinant, and
\[

\kappa:=\frac{2(1+B-A)}9=\frac{19999}{45000},

A+c_1+c_2+c_3<3t,
\tag{11}

\[
A+3(c_1+c_2+c_3)<2+2B,\qquad
c_1+c_2+c_3<\frac{11}{8},
\tag{12}

\[

These substitutions give five rational semialgebraic predicates
\(\mathscr E_1,\ldots,\mathscr E_5\), one for every chamber in (6).
Their disjunction is the exact uncovered-cell query on this slice.
In chamber \(1\), the two cap formulas supplied to the query are

Exact real cylindrical algebraic decomposition evaluated
