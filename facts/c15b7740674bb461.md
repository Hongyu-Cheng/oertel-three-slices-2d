## Statement

Set
\[
f_k(x,y)=x,\qquad f_\ell(x,y)=y,\qquad
f_j(x,y)=-1-x-y
\]
and
\[
p_j=\frac{1566}{25}-x-y,\quad p_k=x,\quad p_\ell=y,
\qquad
q_j=-\frac{1616}{25}-x-y,\quad q_k=x,\quad q_\ell=y.
\]
Let
\[
\begin{aligned}
A_0=\operatorname{conv}\bigg\{&
\left(-\frac1{10},-\frac{49}{100}\right),
\left(\frac{81}{100},-\frac{133}{100}\right),
\left(\frac{6267}{100},\frac3{100}\right),\\
&\left(\frac{1059}{50},\frac{3361}{100}\right),
\left(\frac1{100},-\frac3{25}\right)\bigg\}
\end{aligned}
\]
and
\[
\begin{aligned}
A_2=\operatorname{conv}\bigg\{&
\left(-\frac{1379}{20},-\frac{13}{50}\right),
\left(-\frac{3037}{100},-\frac{41}{50}\right),
\left(-\frac2{25},-\frac1{100}\right),\\
&\left(\frac{29}{20},\frac{14}{25}\right),
\left(0,\frac{133}{100}\right),
\left(-\frac{1323}{20},-\frac1{10}\right)\bigg\}.
\end{aligned}
\]
Put
\[
M=|A_0|,\qquad m=|A_2|,\qquad S=\frac{A_0+A_2}{2},
\]
and, for \(i=j,k,\ell\), put
\[
s_i=|A_0\cap\{p_i\leq0\}|+|A_2\cap\{q_i\leq0\}|.
\]
There do not exist a rational number \(\kappa\) and a compact two-dimensional rational convex polygon \(K\) such that
\[
M-m<\kappa<M+m,\qquad |K|=\kappa,\qquad S\subseteq K,
\]
and, with
\[
t=\frac{2(M+m+\kappa)}9,
\]
\[
|K\cap\{f_i\leq0\}|=t-s_i
\qquad(i=j,k,\ell).
\]
In fact, no two-dimensional compact convex body \(K\supseteq S\), of any area and without any rationality assumption, satisfies these three crossing equalities.

\[
0\leq w_j^{\mathsf T}\mathsf A z
=w_j^{\mathsf T}(-9\rho)

\tag{11}
\]

Hence no compact convex common middle exists at any area.  In particular, none exists in the required radical-free interval \(M-m<\kappa<M+m\), and the additional proper-cap conditions cannot be met.
