## Statement

Use the \(A_0\) coordinate and exact-cell convention of accepted fact `989c9b585d2b42cc`.  For the strict rational data of accepted fact `6090beea12ef2770`, there is no compact two-dimensional convex polygon \(A\subset\mathbb R^2\) with coordinate-area factor \(\Delta=1\), affine chamber coordinates
\[
p_j=X,\qquad p_\ell=Y,\qquad p_k=100-X-Y,
\]
full cutting-line chords
\[
A\cap\{X=0\}
=\{(0,y):249/5\le y\le499/10\},
\]
\[
A\cap\{Y=0\}
=\{(x,0):1995999/20000\le x\le1999999/20000\},
\]
\[
A\cap\{X+Y=100\}
=\{(100+t,-t):1/1000\le t\le101/1000\},
\]
and only the four positive-area strict cells
\[
\begin{array}{c|c|c}
P_j&X<0,\ Y>0,\ X+Y<100&1/10000\\
P_0&X>0,\ Y>0,\ X+Y<100&565001/4000\\
P_\ell&X>0,\ Y<0,\ X+Y<100&1/5\\
P_{k\ell}&X>0,\ Y<0,\ X+Y>100&1/10000.
\end{array}
\]

## Proof

Suppose that such a polygon \(A\) exists.  Put
\[
\alpha=\frac{249}{5},\qquad
\beta=\frac{1995999}{20000}.
\]
The point \((0,\alpha)\) belongs to \(A\) because it is an endpoint of the full \(X=0\) chord.  Take any \((x,y)\) in the strict \(P_\ell\)-cell.  Thus \(y<0\) and \(x+y<100\).  By convexity, the segment joining \((0,\alpha)\) to \((x,y)\) lies in \(A\).  It meets \(Y=0\) at
\[
\left(\frac{\alpha x}{\alpha-y},0\right),
\]
with the intersection parameter strictly between zero and one.  Since the full \(Y=0\) chord starts at \((\beta,0)\), necessarily
\[
\frac{\alpha x}{\alpha-y}\ge\beta.
\]
Because \(\alpha-y>0\), this is equivalent to
\[
\alpha x+\beta y\ge\alpha\beta.
\]
Consequently the entire strict \(P_\ell\)-cell is contained in
\[
\mathcal T=
\{(x,y):y\le0,\ x+y\le100,\ \alpha x+\beta y\ge\alpha\beta\}.
\]

Here \(\beta>\alpha>0\) and \(\beta<100\), so \(\mathcal T\) is the triangle with horizontal base from \((\beta,0)\) to \((100,0)\) and third vertex
\[
\left(100+h,-h\right),
\qquad
h=\frac{\alpha(100-\beta)}{\beta-\alpha}.
\]
At the accepted rational values,
\[
100-\beta=\frac{4001}{20000},\qquad
\beta-\alpha=\frac{999999}{20000},\qquad
h=\frac{332083}{1666665}.
\]
Its exact coordinate area is therefore
\[
|\mathcal T|
=\frac12\frac{4001}{20000}\frac{332083}{1666665}
=\frac{1328664083}{66666600000}.
\]
Since
\[
\frac15-|\mathcal T|
=\frac{12004655917}{66666600000}>0,
\]
the coordinate area of \(P_\ell\) is strictly less than the prescribed value \(1/5\).  Coordinate area equals physical area because \(\Delta=1\), and cutting-line boundaries have area zero.  This contradicts the prescribed \(P_\ell\)-area.  Hence no polygon realizing the full \(A_0\) data exists.
