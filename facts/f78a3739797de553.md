## Statement

Assume the complete Alternative 1 hypotheses of accepted facts `bc026780453747b8` and `92a1b537d7e7876c`.  Normalize the full positive coordinate-axis traces of \(P\) as in accepted fact `8b69ff3587679629`:
\[
[a,A]\times\{0\},\qquad \{0\}\times[b,B],
\qquad A=a+1,\quad B=b+1,
\]
and let \(D>0\) be the product of the two physical trace lengths.  Put
\[
p=\frac{97}{100},\qquad c=\frac3{200}.
\]
Thus the normalized positive-cell area is \(p/D\), and each normalized mixed-cap area is \(c/D\).

Choose a rightmost point \(R=(W,Y)\in P\) and a topmost point
\(T=(X,H)\in P\), with \(R\ne T\), and assume the mixed order
\[
0\leq X\leq A\leq W,\qquad 0\leq B\leq Y\leq H.
\tag{1}
\]
Then
\[
K=\operatorname{conv}\{(a,0),(A,0),R,T,(0,B),(0,b)\}
\subseteq P
\tag{2}
\]
has area
\[
T_K=\frac{AY+WH-XY+XB-ab}{2}.
\tag{3}
\]
The exact maximum horizontal chord length \(M_x=m_x(K)\) is obtained as follows.  Set
\[
G=A+\frac{(W-A)B}{Y}.
\tag{4}
\]
If \(H>B\), set
\[
J_x=W-\frac{X(Y-B)}{H-B};
\tag{5}
\]
then
\[
M_x=\max\{G,J_x\}.
\tag{6}
\]
If \(H=B\), necessarily \(Y=H=B\), and
\[
M_x=W=G.
\tag{7}
\]
The exact maximum vertical chord length \(M_y=m_y(K)\) is
\[
M_y=H\qquad(a\leq X).
\tag{8}
\]
If \(X<a\), then \(a>0\) and \(W>X\); setting
\[
G_y=H-\frac{b(a-X)}a,\qquad
J_y=H-\frac{(H-Y)(a-X)}{W-X},
\tag{9}
\]
one has
\[
M_y=\max\{G_y,J_y\}.
\tag{10}
\]
All equalities in (1), both alternatives \(a,b>0\) and \(a=b=0\), all collinear support faces, and the ties in (6) and (10) are included in (2)-(10).

For completeness, let
\[
\widehat\alpha
=\sup_{\substack{(u,v)\in P\\v>0}}\frac{u-A}{v},
\qquad
\widehat\beta
=\sup_{\substack{(u,v)\in P\\u>0}}\frac{v-B}{u}.
\tag{11}
\]
For positive inner supporting coefficients \(\sigma,\rho\) supplied by accepted fact `8b69ff3587679629`, put
\[
q_x=\widehat\alpha+\frac1\sigma,\qquad
q_y=\widehat\beta+\frac1\rho.
\tag{12}
\]
If \(q_x>0\), respectively \(q_y>0\), the corresponding normalized mixed cap is contained in a triangle of area \(1/(2q_x)\), respectively \(1/(2q_y)\).  If either quantity is zero, the two relevant support lines form a constant-width unbounded strip; if it is negative, they form an expanding wedge.  Thus (11)-(12) also cover nonattainment, zero outer coefficients, parallel supports, and every denominator degeneration without interpreting a zero denominator as a finite triangle.

Every actual \(P\) satisfying (1) obeys
\[
\frac{W}{m_x(P)}+\frac{H}{m_y(P)}
\leq\frac{5000}{291}.
\tag{13}
\]
The same conclusion holds for the coordinate-exchanged mixed order
\[
0\leq A\leq X\leq W,\qquad 0\leq Y\leq B\leq H.
\tag{14}
\]

## Proof

Accepted fact `8b69ff3587679629`, under the hypotheses of accepted facts `bc026780453747b8` and `92a1b537d7e7876c`, proves that an actual normalized trace has either \(a,b>0\) or \(a=b=0\).  It also supplies finite positive inner support coefficients satisfying
\[
y+\sigma(x-a)\geq0,\qquad x+\rho(y-b)\geq0
\tag{15}
\]
on the full normalized body.  When \(a,b>0\),
\[
b\geq\sigma a,\qquad a\geq\rho b,\qquad \sigma\rho\leq1.
\tag{16}
\]
When \(a=b=0\), choose one supporting line \(y+\sigma x=0\) at the origin and use the same line in the second form in (15), so that \(\rho=1/\sigma\).  Hence in every case relevant below the supports may be chosen with
\[
\sigma\rho\leq1.
\tag{17}
\]

We first justify the support polygon and all chord formulas.  The six successive cross products of the displayed vertex list in (2) are
\[
Y,\quad
(W-A)(H-Y)+Y(W-X),\quad
(W-X)(H-B)+X(H-Y),\quad
X,\quad a,\quad b.
\tag{18}
\]
They are nonnegative under (1).  The two boundary chains are coordinate-monotone, so after deleting coincident generators the list is simple; (18) therefore gives the boundary order of the convex polygon \(K\).  Zero values in (18) are exactly allowed collinear faces, including the coincident southwest generator when \(a=b=0\).  Shoelace gives (3).

Horizontal section length is affine between consecutive levels in
\(\{0,b,B,Y,H\}\).  At level \(B\), the segment from \((A,0)\) to \(R\) gives the chord of length \(G\).  If \(H>B\), the chord at level \(Y\) has left endpoint on the segment from \((0,B)\) to \(T\), hence has length \(J_x\).  The chord at level \(b\) has length
\[
A+\frac{(W-A)b}{Y}\leq G,
\]
the level-zero chord has length one, and the remaining vertex levels give no larger length.  This proves (4)-(6), including \(Y=B\), when \(G=J_x=W\), and \(Y=H\), when \(J_x=W-X\) is the possibly nontrivial top-face length.  If \(H=B\), (1) forces \(Y=H=B\), and the level-\(B\) chord has length \(W\), proving (7).  Thus no division by \(H-B\) is made on its zero stratum.

If \(a\leq X\), then \((X,0)\) belongs to the full horizontal trace and the segment from \((X,0)\) to \(T\) is a vertical chord of length \(H\).  No vertical chord can be longer, proving (8), including \(X=a\) and \(X=A\).  If \(X<a\), vertical section length is affine between consecutive levels in \(\{0,X,a,A,W\}\).  At abscissa \(X\) its length is \(G_y\), and at abscissa \(a\) its length is \(J_y\).  The level-zero vertical trace has length one; moreover \(G_y\geq1\).  Indeed, if \(X=0\), the full-trace implication in accepted fact `8b69ff3587679629` gives \(H=B\), so \(G_y=B-b=1\); if \(X>0\), then
\[
G_y\geq B-\frac{b(a-X)}a=1+\frac{bX}{a}\geq1.
\]
The section at \(A\) is no longer than the one at \(a\), and the rightmost section contributes no larger value.  This proves (9)-(10), including their maximum tie.  The branch \(X<a\) itself guarantees \(a>0\) and \(W-X>0\), so every denominator in (9) is positive.  Since \(K\subseteq P\) and \(K,P\) have the same coordinate widths,
\[
m_x(P)\geq M_x,\qquad m_y(P)\geq M_y.
\tag{19}
\]

The cap alternatives in (11)-(12) are the division-free alternatives proved in accepted fact `8b69ff3587679629`; they can also be seen directly by writing a lower-cap level as \(y=-t\).  Equations (15) and (11) confine its horizontal section to
\[
a+\frac t\sigma\leq x\leq A-\widehat\alpha t,
\]
whose available length is \(1-q_xt\).  If \(q_x>0\), integration up to \(1/q_x\) gives \(1/(2q_x)\); \(q_x=0\) and \(q_x<0\) give respectively the strip and wedge.  The vertical-section argument for the left cap is identical.  This verifies all cap and denominator assertions without assuming that either supremum in (11) is attained.

We now prove (13).  If \(Y=H\), then \(R\) simultaneously realizes both coordinate maxima.  If \(X=W\), then \(T\) does so.  In either case accepted fact `72d4135e14ce0663` gives (13), with no uniqueness or exposedness assumption.  These common-apex strata include \(H=B\), because then \(Y=H=B\).
Indeed, the normalized axis traces and \(P\subseteq\{x,y\geq0\}\) show that the coordinate widths occurring in that accepted fact are exactly \(W\) and \(H\).

It remains to assume
\[
Y<H,\qquad X<W.
\tag{20}
\]
Then \(H>B\).  The full-trace implication
\[
X=0\Longrightarrow H=B
\]
from accepted fact `8b69ff3587679629` shows that \(X>0\).  Define the finite endpoint coefficients
\[
\alpha=\frac{W-A}{Y}\geq0,\qquad
\beta=\frac{H-B}{X}>0,
\tag{21}
\]
and
\[
G=A+\alpha B,\qquad F=B+\beta A,\qquad q=\frac GW.
\tag{22}
\]
The endpoint choices in (21) occur among the ratios in (11), so
\[
\widehat\alpha\geq\alpha,\qquad
\widehat\beta\geq\beta.
\tag{23}
\]
In particular both cap coefficients in (12) are positive.  Since each normalized mixed-cap area is \(c/D\), their triangular capacities give
\[
D\geq2c\left(\alpha+\frac1\sigma\right),\qquad
D\geq2c\left(\beta+\frac1\rho\right).
\tag{24}
\]
Adding (24), and using (17), yields
\[
D\geq c\left(\alpha+\beta+\frac1\sigma+\frac1\rho\right)
\geq c(2+\alpha+\beta).
\tag{25}
\]

Because \(K\subseteq P\), equations (2)-(3) give
\[
DT_K\leq p.
\tag{26}
\]
Put
\[
L=T_K(2+\alpha+\beta).
\tag{27}
\]
Equations (25)-(26) imply the exact upper bound
\[
L\leq\frac pc=\frac{194}{3}.
\tag{28}
\]

We next extract a lower bound for \(L\) from the mixed support order.  Equations (21)-(22) give
\[
W=A+\alpha Y,\qquad H=B+\beta X.
\tag{29}
\]
Substitution into (3), using \(AB-ab=A+B-1\), gives the exact identity
\[
2T_K
=A+B-1+GY+FX-(1-\alpha\beta)XY.
\tag{30}
\]
Moreover,
\[
F-(1-\alpha\beta)Y
=(H-Y)+\beta(W-X)\geq0.
\tag{31}
\]
Thus
\[
2T_K\geq A+B-1+GY.
\tag{32}
\]

Since \(B\leq Y\), one has \(0<q\leq1\).  If \(q=1\), then
\[
\frac W{M_x}\leq\frac WG=1,
\tag{33}
\]
so the required horizontal estimate below is immediate.  Suppose \(q<1\), and set
\[
s=\sqrt{1-q}\in(0,1).
\tag{34}
\]
Then \(\alpha>0\), and
\[
Y=B+\frac{(1-q)G}{\alpha q}.
\tag{35}
\]
Using \(A,B\geq1\) and \(G=A+\alpha B\geq1+\alpha\), equations (32) and (35) give
\[
2T_K
\geq
2+\alpha+\frac{(1+\alpha)^2s^2}{\alpha(1-s^2)}.
\tag{36}
\]
The arithmetic-geometric mean inequality gives
\[
\alpha+\frac{(1+\alpha)^2s^2}{\alpha(1-s^2)}
\geq\frac{2s}{1-s},
\]
and therefore
\[
T_K\geq\frac1{1-s}.
\tag{37}
\]

On the other hand,
\[
1-q=\frac{\alpha(Y-B)}W.
\tag{38}
\]
Since \(H-B\geq Y-B\) and \(0<X\leq W\), equations (21) and (38) give
\[
\alpha\beta\geq1-q=s^2.
\tag{39}
\]
Hence
\[
2+\alpha+\beta\geq2(1+s).
\tag{40}
\]
Combining (27), (28), (37), and (40),
\[
\frac{2(1+s)}{1-s}\leq\frac{194}{3}.
\]
Thus \(s\leq47/50\), and
\[
q=1-s^2\geq\frac{291}{2500}.
\tag{41}
\]
Equations (4), (6), (19), and (41), together with the already treated case \(q=1\), prove
\[
\frac{W}{m_x(P)}
\leq\frac W{M_x}
\leq\frac WG
\leq\frac{2500}{291}.
\tag{42}
\]

For the vertical ratio, if \(a\leq X\), equations (8) and (19) give
\[
\frac{H}{m_y(P)}\leq1.
\tag{43}
\]
If \(X<a\), put
\[
d=\frac{b(a-X)}a.
\]
Then \(0\leq d\leq b<Y\), while (9) gives \(G_y=H-d\) and \(J_y\geq Y\).  If \(H\leq2Y\), then \(J_y\geq H/2\).  If \(H>2Y\), then
\[
G_y=H-d>H-Y>\frac H2.
\]
Consequently (10) and (19) give
\[
\frac{H}{m_y(P)}\leq2.
\tag{44}
\]
Equations (42)-(44) yield the stronger bound
\[
\frac{W}{m_x(P)}+\frac{H}{m_y(P)}
\leq\frac{2500}{291}+2
=\frac{3082}{291}
<\frac{5000}{291},
\tag{45}
\]
which proves (13) on every non-common-apex stratum.

Finally consider (14).  Under the coordinate exchange
\((x,y)\mapsto(y,x)\), define
\[
a'=b,\quad A'=B,\quad b'=a,\quad B'=A,
\]
\[
R'=(W',Y')=(H,X),\qquad
T'=(X',H')=(Y,W).
\]
Then (14) becomes
\[
0\leq X'\leq A'\leq W',\qquad
0\leq B'\leq Y'\leq H',
\]
which is exactly (1).  Distinct support points, zero offsets, endpoint equalities, common-apex incidences, collinear faces, maximum-chord ties, and denominator-degenerate strata are merely interchanged.  Moreover
\[
\frac{W'}{m_x(P')}+\frac{H'}{m_y(P')}
=\frac{H}{m_y(P)}+\frac{W}{m_x(P)}.
\]
Thus the already proved boundary-complete result applies to (14), completing the coordinate-exchanged case.
