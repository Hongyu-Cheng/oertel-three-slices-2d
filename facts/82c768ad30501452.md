## Statement

Let
\[
\Delta=\operatorname{conv}\{(0,0),(1,0),(0,1)\},
\qquad
g=(1/3,1/3),
\]
and set
\[
u=\frac{201}{200},\qquad
v=\frac{101}{1000},\qquad
k=\frac{u+v}{2}=\frac{553}{1000},
\qquad
p=kg=\left(\frac{553}{3000},\frac{553}{3000}\right).
\]
Define the rational triangles
\[
A_1=\Delta,\qquad
A_0=p+u(\Delta-g),\qquad
A_2=p+v(\Delta-g).
\]
Let \(a_i=|A_i|\), \(T=a_0+a_1+a_2\), let \(X_0,X_2\) be independent and uniform on \(A_0,A_2\), and let \(\nu\) be the law of \((X_0+X_2)/2\). Since \(m=\min\{a_0,a_2\}=a_2\), define
\[
\eta(E)=|A_1\cap E|+m\nu(E).
\]

For
\[
s_1=\frac{3377}{10000},
\qquad
s_2=\frac{3311}{10000},
\]
write
\[
\ell_1(x,y)=1-x-y,\qquad \ell_2(x,y)=x,\qquad \ell_3(x,y)=y
\]
and define
\[
Q_1=\{\ell_1\ge s_1\},\qquad
Q_2=\{\ell_2\ge s_2\},\qquad
Q_3=\{\ell_3\ge s_2\}.
\]
Then the triple is compatible and satisfies
\[
a_i<T/2\quad(i=0,1,2),
\qquad
2\max\{a_0,a_2\}>a_1+\min\{a_0,a_2\}.
\]
Moreover,
\[
A_1\subseteq
\operatorname{int}Q_1\cup\operatorname{int}Q_2\cup\operatorname{int}Q_3
\]
and
\[
\eta(Q_i)<\frac{2T}{9}\qquad(i=1,2,3).
\]
Consequently no point of \(A_1\) has planar halfspace depth \(2T/9\) with respect to \(\eta\).

## Proof

Because \(\Delta-g\) is convex,
\[
\frac{A_0+A_2}{2}
=p+\frac{u+v}{2}(\Delta-g)
=kg+k(\Delta-g)
=k\Delta
\subseteq\Delta=A_1.
\]
Accepted fact `8ad0785e5b4ae9ef` therefore proves compatibility.

Since \(|\Delta|=1/2\),
\[
a_0=\frac{40401}{80000},\qquad
a_1=\frac12,\qquad
a_2=\frac{10201}{2000000},
\qquad
T=\frac{1010113}{1000000}.
\]
The three balance margins are
\[
\frac T2-a_0=\frac{11}{250000}>0,
\qquad
\frac T2-a_1=\frac{10113}{2000000}>0,
\qquad
\frac T2-a_2=\frac{124989}{250000}>0.
\]
Also \(M=a_0\), \(m=a_2\), and
\[
2M-(a_1+m)=\frac{1009849}{2000000}>0,
\]
so this is the strict residual regime.

For every \((x,y)\),
\[
\ell_1(x,y)+\ell_2(x,y)+\ell_3(x,y)=1,
\]
whereas
\[
s_1+2s_2=\frac{9999}{10000}<1.
\]
Thus at least one of \(\ell_1>s_1,\ell_2>s_2,\ell_3>s_2\) holds, proving the asserted interior cover.

Let \(U,V\) be independent and uniform on \(\Delta\). Then
\[
X_0=p+u(U-g),\qquad X_2=p+v(V-g),
\]
and hence
\[
\frac{X_0+X_2}{2}
=p+\frac{u(U-g)+v(V-g)}2
=\frac{uU+vV}{2}.
\]
Its support is \(k\Delta\). On \(k\Delta\),
\[
\ell_1\ge1-k=\frac{447}{1000}>s_1,
\]
so \(\nu(Q_1)=1\).

For each barycentric corner halfplane,
\[
|\Delta\cap\{\ell_i\ge s\}|=\frac12(1-s)^2.
\]
Since \(m=a_2=v^2/2\), it follows that
\[
\eta(Q_1)
=\frac12\bigl((1-s_1)^2+v^2\bigr)
=\frac{44884229}{200000000}.
\]
As
\[
\frac{2T}{9}=\frac{1010113}{4500000},
\]
their exact difference is
\[
\frac{2T}{9}-\eta(Q_1)
=\frac{87139}{1800000000}>0.
\]

For \(Q_2\), let \(R\) and \(S\) be the first coordinates of \(U\) and \(V\). They are independent with density \(2(1-r)\) on \([0,1]\). Put \(c=2s_2\). Since
\[
0<c-v<c<u,
\]
conditioning on \(S\) gives
\[
\begin{aligned}
\nu(Q_2)
&=\int_0^1 2(1-s)
 \left(1-\frac{c-vs}{u}\right)^2\,ds\\
&=\frac{(u-c)^2+\frac23v(u-c)+\frac16v^2}{u^2}
=\frac{21344081}{151503750}.
\end{aligned}
\]
The same value holds for \(Q_3\). Therefore
\[
\eta(Q_2)=\eta(Q_3)
=\frac12\left((1-s_2)^2+v^2\frac{21344081}{151503750}\right)
=\frac{136009262274637}{606015000000000}.
\]
Finally,
\[
\frac{2T}{9}-\eta(Q_2)
=\frac{2T}{9}-\eta(Q_3)
=\frac{22655435363}{606015000000000}>0.
\]
Thus all three halfplanes have the required strict mass bound. Every point of \(A_1\) lies in the interior of one of them, so every point has \(\eta\)-halfspace depth strictly below \(2T/9\).
