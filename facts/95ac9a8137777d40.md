## Statement

Assume the actual dominant all-double common-plane hypotheses and exact-cell convention of accepted facts 174b54e9843ef8fc and 29dfdfc94477e532, together with the rank-two common-core hypotheses of accepted fact bd8c4d0245cddf92.  Thus \(A_0,A_2,K\subset\mathbb R^2\) are compact two-dimensional convex polytopes,
\[
B_0=\frac{A_0+A_2}{2}\subseteq K,
\qquad f_1+f_2+f_3=-1,
\]
and, for the centroids \(g_0,g_2\) and the three outer-plane offsets \(L_\ell\),
\[
f_\ell(g_0)>L_\ell\quad(\ell=1,2,3),\qquad
f_1(g_2)>-L_1,
\]
while
\[
\left|A_2\cap\{f_\ell>-L_\ell\}\right|=x_\ell>0
\quad(\ell=2,3).
\]
For cyclic \((i,j,k)\), let
\[
\Psi_i(r)=h_{B_0}(f_k+r f_j),\qquad
I_i=\{r>0:\Psi_i(r)\leq0\},
\]
and let \(D=\operatorname{conv}(B_0\cup\mathcal T)\) be the common augmented core from accepted fact bd8c4d0245cddf92.  Then
\[
h_{B_0}(f_\ell)>0\qquad(\ell=1,2,3).
\]
Consequently, for every cyclic \((i,j,k)\), \(O_i\) is admissible for \(D\), equivalently for \(\operatorname{conv}(B_0\cup\{O_i\})\), if and only if
\[
\operatorname{int}_{\mathbb R}I_i\neq\varnothing.
\]
In particular, within the actual dominant all-double branch the only possible corner escape in accepted fact bd8c4d0245cddf92 is
\[
\operatorname{int}_{\mathbb R}I_i=\varnothing.
\]

## Proof

Accepted fact 174b54e9843ef8fc gives
\[
f_\ell(g_0)>L_\ell\qquad(\ell=1,2,3).
\]
Since \(g_0\in A_0\), this implies
\[
h_{A_0}(f_\ell)>L_\ell\qquad(\ell=1,2,3).
\tag{1}
\]
For \(\ell=1\), the same accepted fact gives \(g_2\in A_2\) and
\[
f_1(g_2)>-L_1,
\]
so
\[
h_{A_2}(f_1)>-L_1.
\tag{2}
\]
For each \(\ell=2,3\), accepted facts 174b54e9843ef8fc and 29dfdfc94477e532 identify the exact strict positive-side cap
\[
A_2\cap\{f_\ell>-L_\ell\}
\]
as having area \(x_\ell>0\).  It is therefore nonempty, and any point in it gives
\[
h_{A_2}(f_\ell)>-L_\ell.
\tag{3}
\]
The full-midpoint support identity in accepted fact bd8c4d0245cddf92 is
\[
h_{B_0}(g)=\frac{h_{A_0}(g)+h_{A_2}(g)}2
\]
for every affine \(g\).  Combining this identity with (1)--(3) yields
\[
h_{B_0}(f_\ell)
=\frac{h_{A_0}(f_\ell)+h_{A_2}(f_\ell)}2
>\frac{L_\ell-L_\ell}{2}=0
\qquad(\ell=1,2,3).
\tag{4}
\]

Accepted fact bd8c4d0245cddf92 states, without assuming admissibility, that at a cyclic corner \(i\) admissibility for either of the two stated cores is equivalent to
\[
h_{B_0}(f_j)>0,\qquad
h_{B_0}(f_k)>0,\qquad
\operatorname{int}_{\mathbb R}I_i\neq\varnothing.
\]
The first two conditions are automatic by (4), proving the claimed equivalence and eliminating both positive-reach failure modes.  This contradiction occurs already at the exact endpoint-support level; the crossing, no-half, and mixed-area-window inequalities are therefore not needed to exclude these modes and remain relevant only to the residual support-slope interval collapse.
