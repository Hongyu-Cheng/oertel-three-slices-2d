## Statement

Under Alternative 2 of accepted fact `6e68b44550cac3c8`, the two root-tail inequalities are impossible.

## Proof

Assume Alternative 2. Retain the indices \(i,j,\ell\) after the interchange of \(i,\ell\) allowed in accepted fact `6e68b44550cac3c8`, and write
\[
c_h=\left|K\cap\{f_h\le0\}\right|,
\qquad
t=\frac{2(1+r+s)}9.
\]
That fact gives
\[
0<r<\frac13,\qquad s>1-r,\qquad
c_i+r<t,\qquad c_j+r<t,
\]
\[
f_i(g)>0,\qquad f_j(g)>0,\qquad c_\ell\ge\frac{4s}{9}.
\tag{1}
\]
Its strict low-mass inequality for the actual \(\ell\)-shift has the form
\[
c_\ell+u_\ell+v_\ell<t
\]
with \(u_\ell,v_\ell\ge0\), and hence
\[
c_\ell<t.
\tag{2}
\]
Also \(s>1-r>0\). Since \(r<1/3\), one has \(5>9r\), so
\[
7s>7(1-r)>2(1+r).
\]
Therefore \(2(1+r+s)<9s\), namely \(t<s\). Combining this with (1)-(2) gives the required common monotonicity domain
\[
\frac{4s}{9}\le c_\ell<t<s.
\tag{3}
\]

Apply accepted theorem `0688f679297c8baa` with its dummy labels
\[
(f_1,f_2,f_3)=(f_i,f_j,f_\ell).
\]
This is only a relabeling in that theorem: the functions remain nonconstant, their linear parts remain pairwise nonparallel, their sum is \(-1\), and the first two centroid signs are strict by (1). Thus
\[
\max\{c_i,c_j\}\ge 2\sqrt{s\,c_\ell}-2c_\ell.
\tag{4}
\]

For fixed \(s>0\), put \(\Phi(u)=2\sqrt{su}-2u\). On \([4s/9,s]\),
\[
\Phi'(u)=\sqrt{\frac{s}{u}}-2
\le\frac32-2=-\frac12<0.
\]
Hence \(\Phi\) is strictly decreasing there. By (3),
\[
\Phi(c_\ell)>\Phi(t).
\tag{5}
\]

It remains to check the strict Regime A comparison. Since
\[
3t-r=\frac{2+2s-r}{3}>0,
\]
we may compare squares. Direct substitution of \(t=2(1+r+s)/9\) gives
\[
4st-(3t-r)^2
=
\frac{4(s-1+r)(s+1+2r)+r(8-9r)}9
>0,
\tag{6}
\]
because \(s-1+r>0\), \(s+1+2r>0\), \(r>0\), and \(8-9r>0\). Thus
\[
2\sqrt{st}>3t-r,
\qquad\text{equivalently}\qquad
\Phi(t)=2\sqrt{st}-2t>t-r.
\tag{7}
\]

Finally, the two strict root tails in (1) give
\[
\max\{c_i,c_j\}<t-r.
\tag{8}
\]
Equations (4), (5), (7), and (8) yield the impossible strict chain
\[
\max\{c_i,c_j\}
\ge\Phi(c_\ell)
>\Phi(t)
>t-r
>\max\{c_i,c_j\}.
\]
Therefore Alternative 2 cannot occur anywhere in Regime A.
