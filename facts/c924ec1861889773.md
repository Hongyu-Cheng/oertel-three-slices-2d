## Statement

Put
\[
\ell=\frac{5488420}{5488550199},\qquad
M=2,\qquad
m=\frac{5872739599}{5488550199},\qquad
a=\frac1{100},
\]
\[
d_2=\frac{75686278552276995409}{120495872477237952816}.
\]
Let \(P,Q\) be the exact rational endpoint polygons of accepted fact
`5e723405fce2d727`, and put \(B=(P+Q)/2\).  Equivalently, in its
\((u,v)\)-coordinates, \(B\) is the convex polygon with cyclic vertex list
\[
\begin{aligned}
&\left(-52,-\frac{195999}{4000}\right),\quad
\left(-52+\frac{\ell}{2},-\frac{195999}{4000}\right),\quad
\left(-3+\frac{\ell}{2},-\frac{3951}{196000}\right),\\
&\left(67,\frac{13716049}{196000}\right),\quad
\left(67,\frac{1960207}{28000}\right),\quad
\left(18,\frac{588207}{28000}\right).
\end{aligned}
\]
Here \(x=v-u\).  Define
\[
r=\frac mM,\qquad
L(r)=\frac{3r+2\sqrt r-1}{2},\qquad
U(r)=2-r.
\]
There do not exist a rational number \(k\) and a compact
two-dimensional rational convex polygon \(R\) such that
\[
M L(r)<k<M U(r),\qquad |R|=k,\qquad B\subseteq R,
\]
and, on putting
\[
t=\frac{2(M+m+k)}9,\qquad d=a+k-t,
\]
all three equations
\[
|R\cap\{u\leq0\}|=t-d_2,\qquad
|R\cap\{v\geq0\}|=t-m,\qquad
|R\cap\{x\geq1\}|=d
\]
hold.

## Proof

Accepted fact `5e723405fce2d727` gives the displayed midpoint polygon
\(B=(P+Q)/2\) and the exact values of \(M,m,a,d_2\).

Define three affine functions
\[
f_1(u,v)=u,\qquad f_2(u,v)=-v,\qquad
f_3(u,v)=v-u-1=x-1.
\]
They satisfy
\[
f_1+f_2+f_3=-1.
\]
For a measurable set \(X\), write
\[
c_i(X)=|X\cap\{f_i\leq0\}|.
\]
Thus
\[
c_1(X)=|X\cap\{u\leq0\}|,\qquad
c_2(X)=|X\cap\{v\geq0\}|,\qquad
c_3(X)=|X\cap\{x\leq1\}|.
\]
If \(R\) were a polygon as in the statement, its cutting line
\(\{x=1\}\) would have area zero, and the third required equation would
give
\[
c_3(R)=k-|R\cap\{x\geq1\}|=k-d=t-a.
\]
Consequently all three required equations are exactly
\[
s_i+c_i(R)=\frac{2(M+m+|R|)}9\qquad(i=1,2,3),
\]
where
\[
(s_1,s_2,s_3)=(d_2,m,a).
\]

We now compute the exact extension residual at \(B\).  The shoelace
formula applied to its displayed cyclic vertices gives
\[
|B|=\frac{987605419723}{384198513930}.
\]
Clipping those vertices by \(u\leq0\) gives the cyclic list
\[
\begin{aligned}
&\left(-52,-\frac{195999}{4000}\right),\quad
\left(-\frac{285401866138}{5488550199},-\frac{195999}{4000}\right),\\
&\left(-\frac{16462906387}{5488550199},-\frac{3951}{196000}\right),\quad
\left(0,\frac{801261136226701}{268937038804000}\right),\quad
\left(0,\frac{589089}{196000}\right),
\end{aligned}
\]
while clipping by \(v\geq0\) gives
\[
\begin{aligned}
&\left(-\frac{801261136226701}{268938959751000},0\right),\quad
\left(67,\frac{13716049}{196000}\right),\quad
\left(67,\frac{1960207}{28000}\right),\\
&\left(18,\frac{588207}{28000}\right),\quad
\left(-\frac{196363}{65340},0\right).
\end{aligned}
\]
A second application of the shoelace formula yields
\[
c_1(B)=
\frac{53518074898775632575527}{73803721892308246099800},
\qquad
c_2(B)=
\frac{202769660155801322201}{105424072222392000000}.
\]
At the six displayed vertices of \(B\), the minimum of \(x=v-u\) is
\[
\frac{584049}{196000}-\frac{\ell}{2}.
\]
Since \(0<\ell<1/1000\), this minimum is greater than \(1\).
Therefore \(B\subset\{x>1\}\) and
\[
c_3(B)=0.
\]

Set
\[
\tau=\frac{2(M+m+|B|)}9
=\frac{2167094219513}{1728893312685}
\]
and define the three crossing residuals at \(B\) by
\[
\rho_i=s_i+c_i(B)-\tau.
\]
Direct reduction of the preceding exact rational quantities gives
\[
\rho_1=
\frac{132589705773695903650991}
{1328466994061548429796400},
\]
\[
\rho_2=
\frac{150077988517436863619}
{86256059091048000000},
\qquad
\rho_3=
-\frac{42996105727723}{34577866253700}.
\]
In particular, with \(\Gamma=\rho_1+\rho_2+\rho_3\),
\[
\Gamma=
\frac{258757153391143863479284466647}
{433965884726772487066824000000}>0.
\]

Accepted extension-cone fact `89563f2f27529ab0`, applied with
\(A_0=P\), \(A_2=Q\), \(K=R\), the three functions \(f_i\), and fixed
endpoint sums \(s_i\), proves that every actual extension
\(R\supseteq B\) satisfying the three crossing equalities must obey
\[
\widehat z_i=-\rho_i-\frac23\Gamma\geq0
\qquad(i=1,2,3).
\]
But the first exact gate is
\[
\widehat z_1
=-\frac{107908703073418285422756685549}
{216982942363386243533412000000}<0.
\]
This contradicts a necessary condition for every measurable actual
extension, hence also for every convex extension.  The contradiction
does not depend on \(k\), so it excludes all \(k\) in the residual strip
and proves the statement.
