## Statement

Let \(A_0,A_2\) be any actual endpoint completion of the fixed chords in
accepted fact `84d4961270542ec6`, and let \(K_{\mathrm{mid}}\) be its
compatible middle body, of physical area \(\kappa\).  Then the full,
un-clipped endpoint hull satisfies
\[
\frac{|D_{\mathrm{ep}}|}{4\Delta}\leq\kappa. \tag{1}
\]
For the endpoint datum in accepted facts `61d2f5e1d716401c` and
`8d1cb8030731422b`,
\[
|D_{\mathrm{ep}}|
=\frac{79{,}306{,}849}{400{,}000}. \tag{2}
\]
At the scalar survivor in `8d1cb8030731422b`,
\[
\kappa=\frac{8{,}500{,}013}{1{,}000{,}000},
\qquad
\frac{|D_{\mathrm{ep}}|}{4\Delta}-\kappa
=\frac{328{,}534{,}141}{8{,}000{,}000}>0. \tag{3}
\]
Hence the survivor violates (1).

## Proof

Take arbitrary \(p\in A_0\) and \(q\in A_2\).  The lifted points
\((0,p)\) and \((2,q)\) belong to the convex body \(C\).  Convexity at
weight \(1/2\) gives
\[
\left(1,\frac{p+q}{2}\right)
=\frac12(0,p)+\frac12(2,q)\in C.
\]
Therefore
\[
\frac{A_0+A_2}{2}\subseteq K_{\mathrm{mid}}. \tag{4}
\]
Accepted fact `84d4961270542ec6` proves, without a sign-cell clip,
\[
D_{\mathrm{ep}}
\subseteq
\Xi\left(\frac{A_0+A_2}{2}\right). \tag{5}
\]
Combining (4) and (5) yields
\[
D_{\mathrm{ep}}\subseteq\Xi(K_{\mathrm{mid}}). \tag{6}
\]
The physical middle-plane coordinates have Jacobian magnitude
\(\Delta\) in the \((r_k,r_\ell)\)-coordinates, while
\(\Xi(r)=(-2r_k,-2r_\ell)\) has determinant \(4\).  Consequently
\[
|\Xi(K_{\mathrm{mid}})|=4\Delta|K_{\mathrm{mid}}|
=4\Delta\kappa. \tag{7}
\]
Area monotonicity in (6), followed by division by \(4\Delta>0\), proves
(1).  Notice that no intersection with \(T_N\), or with any individual
middle sign cell, occurs in this argument.

It remains to audit (2).  The endpoint parameters in
