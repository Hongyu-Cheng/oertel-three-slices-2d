## Statement

Use the notation and constraints of accepted fact `eef70ce3abe7606e`. Suppose
\[
x_{ij}>0\qquad(i\ne j).
\tag{1}
\]
Then
\[
D>\frac4{169}.
\tag{2}
\]
Consequently the fully noncoincident endpoint stratum cannot satisfy the
necessary scale condition \(D\le4/169\).

## Proof

Assume for contradiction that
\[
0<D\le\delta:=\frac4{169}.
\tag{3}
\]
Write
\[
a=x_{12},\quad b=x_{21},\quad
c=x_{23},\quad d=x_{32},\quad
e=x_{31},\quad f=x_{13}.
\tag{4}
\]
The three side gaps in the accepted normal form are
\[
L_1=1-a-f,\qquad
L_2=1-b-c,\qquad
L_3=1-d-e,
\tag{5}
\]
and all six variables in (4) and all three gaps in (5) are positive.

Write the three cap vertices in barycentric coordinates as
\[
u_1=(-h_1,A,B),\qquad
u_2=(C,-h_2,E),\qquad
u_3=(F,G,-h_3),
\tag{6}
\]
where
\[
A+B=1+h_1,\qquad
C+E=1+h_2,\qquad
F+G=1+h_3,
\qquad
h_i=\frac{D}{2L_i}.
\tag{7}
\]
All six capital letters in (6) are positive.
Let \(K\) denote the convex polygon defined by the cyclic list
\(\mathcal V\) in the accepted normal form.

We first record six consequences of the convex-order constraints in the
accepted normal form. Evaluating the supporting determinant of each of the two
edges adjacent to a cap vertex at the adjacent chord endpoint gives
\[
b(A-a)\ge ah_1,\qquad
e(B-f)\ge fh_1,
\tag{8}
\]
\[
a(C-b)\ge bh_2,\qquad
d(E-c)\ge ch_2,
\tag{9}
\]
\[
c(G-d)\ge dh_3,\qquad
f(F-e)\ge eh_3.
\tag{10}
\]
For example, in the \((\alpha_1,\alpha_2)\)-coordinates of the accepted fact,
the determinant of the directed edge \(u_1p_{12}\) against \(p_{21}\) is
\[
[p_{12}-u_1,p_{21}-u_1]
=b(A-a)-ah_1,
\]
which is nonnegative by its convex-order condition. The other five formulas
are its cyclic counterparts, using the other edge at each cap vertex.

Adding the two inequalities belonging to each cap and using (5)--(7) yields
\[
\frac ab+\frac fe
\le 1+\frac{2L_1^2}{D},
\tag{11}
\]
\[
\frac ba+\frac cd
\le 1+\frac{2L_2^2}{D},
\tag{12}
\]
\[
\frac dc+\frac ef
\le 1+\frac{2L_3^2}{D}.
\tag{13}
\]
Indeed, for example,
\[
(A-a)+(B-f)=L_1+h_1,
\]
while (8) bounds the two summands below by \(ah_1/b\) and
\(fh_1/e\). Summing (11)--(13) and applying
\(r+r^{-1}\ge2\) to the three positive ratios gives
\[
6
\le
3+\frac{2(L_1^2+L_2^2+L_3^2)}D.
\tag{14}
\]
If every \(L_i\le D\), then (14) gives \(6\le3+6D\), hence
\(D\ge1/2\), contrary to (3). After a cyclic relabeling, therefore, we may
assume
\[
L_3>D.
\tag{15}
\]

Put
\[
S=ab+cd+ef,
\qquad
s_i=1-L_i.
\tag{16}
\]
Formula (4) for \(D\) in the accepted fact and
\(0\le\rho_{ij}\le1\) imply
\[
D\ge1-S,
\qquad\text{so}\qquad
S\ge1-D.
\tag{17}
\]
We also need the elementary bound
\[
S\le\max\{s_1s_2,s_2s_3,s_3s_1\}.
\tag{18}
\]
To see this, fix the three row sums
\[
a+f=s_1,\qquad b+c=s_2,\qquad d+e=s_3.
\]
After substituting \(f=s_1-a\), \(c=s_2-b\), and \(e=s_3-d\),
the expression \(S\) is affine in each of \(a,b,d\) separately. Its maximum
over the corresponding rectangular box is therefore attained at a vertex.
At the eight vertices its value is either zero or one of
\(s_1s_2,s_2s_3,s_3s_1\), proving (18).

By (15),
\[
s_1s_3<1-L_3<1-D\le S,
\qquad
s_2s_3<1-L_3<1-D\le S.
\]
Equations (17)--(18) therefore force
\[
(1-L_1)(1-L_2)=s_1s_2\ge S\ge1-D.
\tag{19}
\]
In particular,
\[
L_1+L_2-L_1L_2\le D,
\qquad
L_1\le D,\qquad L_2\le D.
\tag{20}
\]
Moreover, \(c<1\) and \(f<1\), so
\[
cd+ef<d+e=1-L_3.
\]
Together with (17), this gives the strict lower bound
\[
ab=S-cd-ef>L_3-D.
\tag{21}
\]

We now use the area of the triangle formed by the cap vertices. Pairwise
interior-disjointness of the three caps gives
\[
AC\ge h_1h_2,\qquad
EG\ge h_2h_3,\qquad
FB\ge h_3h_1.
\tag{22}
\]
For instance, along the segment \([u_2,u_3]\subseteq K\), the zero of
\(\alpha_2\) must occur no later than the zero of \(\alpha_3\); otherwise an
open subsegment, and hence a positive-area relative neighborhood in the
two-dimensional polygon, would have both coordinates negative. This is
exactly \(EG\ge h_2h_3\). The other two inequalities are cyclic.

Let \(U=\operatorname{conv}\{u_1,u_2,u_3\}\). Direct expansion of the
barycentric determinant gives
\[
\begin{aligned}
2|U|
={}&h_1GE+h_3AC+h_2FB+CGB+FAE-h_1h_2h_3.
\end{aligned}
\tag{23}
\]
The last two positive terms satisfy
\[
CGB+FAE
\ge2\sqrt{AC\cdot EG\cdot FB}
\ge2h_3\sqrt{AC\,h_1h_2}.
\]
Using the last two inequalities in (22) also in the first and third terms of
(23), we obtain
\[
2|U|
\ge
h_3\left(\sqrt{AC}+\sqrt{h_1h_2}\right)^2.
\tag{24}
\]
The accepted normal form gives \(|K|=5D/4\), and \(U\subseteq K\). Since
\(h_3=D/(2L_3)\), equation (24) implies
\[
\sqrt{AC}+\sqrt{h_1h_2}\le\sqrt{5L_3}.
\tag{25}
\]

The first inequalities in (8) and (9) sharpen the first term in (25):
\[
A\ge\frac{a(b+h_1)}b,
\qquad
C\ge\frac{b(a+h_2)}a.
\]
Consequently
\[
AC\ge(a+h_2)(b+h_1)
\ge
\left(\sqrt{ab}+\sqrt{h_1h_2}\right)^2,
\tag{26}
\]
where the second inequality is the identity
\[
(a+h_2)(b+h_1)
-\left(\sqrt{ab}+\sqrt{h_1h_2}\right)^2
=\left(\sqrt{ah_1}-\sqrt{bh_2}\right)^2.
\]
Putting
\[
P=\sqrt{h_1h_2}
=\frac{D}{2\sqrt{L_1L_2}},
\tag{27}
\]
equations (25)--(26) give
\[
\sqrt{ab}+2P\le\sqrt{5L_3}.
\tag{28}
\]

Equation (19) and the Cauchy--Schwarz inequality give
\[
\sqrt{L_1L_2}
\le
1-\sqrt{(1-L_1)(1-L_2)}
\le
1-\sqrt{1-D}.
\tag{29}
\]
Thus (27) and rationalization imply
\[
2P
\ge
\frac{D}{1-\sqrt{1-D}}
=1+\sqrt{1-D}.
\tag{30}
\]
Combining (21), (28), and (30), we obtain
\[
\sqrt{L_3-D}+1+\sqrt{1-D}
<
\sqrt{5L_3}.
\tag{31}
\]

It remains only to check that (31) is impossible on the stated range. For
fixed \(D\), define on \(D\le L\le1\)
\[
\Phi_D(L)
=
\sqrt{L-D}+1+\sqrt{1-D}-\sqrt{5L}.
\tag{32}
\]
Its derivative is nonnegative for \(L\le5D/4\) and nonpositive for
\(L\ge5D/4\). Hence its minimum on the interval is attained at an endpoint.
At the left endpoint,
\[
\Phi_D(D)=1+\sqrt{1-D}-\sqrt{5D}>0,
\tag{33}
\]
because \(5D\le20/169<1\). At the right endpoint,
\[
\Phi_D(1)=1+2\sqrt{1-D}-\sqrt5>0.
\tag{34}
\]
Indeed, \(D\le4/169<5/9\) gives \(\sqrt{1-D}>2/3\), while
\(7/3>\sqrt5\). Thus \(\Phi_D(L)>0\) throughout the interval, contradicting
(31) with \(L=L_3\).

The contradiction proves \(D>4/169\) whenever all six endpoint parameters
are positive.
