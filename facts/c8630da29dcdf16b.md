## Statement

Use the area-preserving coordinates
\[
u=y,\qquad v=x+y,\qquad x=v-u.
\]
Let
\[
\varepsilon=\gamma=\frac1{4040},\qquad
\alpha=\frac{2000}{201},\qquad
\beta=\frac{404}{201},\qquad
w=\frac{25}{101},
\]
and define
\[
P=\operatorname{conv}\left\{
(\varepsilon,\alpha),\
(\varepsilon,\alpha-100),\
(\varepsilon+1,\alpha-100)
\right\},
\]
\[
Q=\operatorname{conv}\left\{
(-\gamma,-\beta),\
(w-\gamma,-\beta),\
(-\gamma,202-\beta)
\right\}.
\]
Put
\[
B=\frac{P+Q}{2},\qquad
M=50,\qquad m=25,\qquad
a=\frac{79}{4},\qquad
d_2=\frac{1999}{40000}.
\]
There do not exist a rational number \(k\) and a compact two-dimensional rational convex polygon \(R\) such that
\[
50\left(\frac14+\frac{\sqrt2}{2}\right)<k<75,
\qquad |R|=k,
\qquad B\subseteq R,
\]
and, on defining
\[
t=\frac{2(M+m+k)}9=\frac{2(75+k)}9,
\qquad d=a+k-t,
\]
all three equations
\[
|R\cap\{u\le0\}|=t-d_2,
\qquad
|R\cap\{v\ge0\}|=t-m,
\qquad
|R\cap\{v-u\ge1\}|=d
\]
hold.

## Proof

Accepted fact `33eb057e70ac0f43` certifies for these exact triangles that
\[
|B|=\left|\frac{P+Q}{2}\right|=\frac{277}{4}.
\]
Every point of \(P\) has \(u\ge\varepsilon\), and every point of \(Q\) has \(u\ge-\gamma\). Since \(\varepsilon=\gamma\), every midpoint of a point of \(P\) and a point of \(Q\) has \(u\ge0\). Hence
\[
B\subseteq\{u\ge0\}.
\]

Suppose that \(k\) and \(R\) satisfy the stated area and containment conditions. Since \(B\subseteq R\),
\[
k=|R|\ge |B|=\frac{277}{4}.
\]
The line \(\{u=0\}\) has planar area zero. Therefore \(B\subseteq\{u\ge0\}\) and \(B\subseteq R\) imply
\[
\begin{aligned}
|R\cap\{u\le0\}|
&=|R\cap\{u<0\}|\\
&\le |R\setminus B|\\
&=k-\frac{277}{4}\\
&<75-\frac{277}{4}
=\frac{23}{4}.
\end{aligned}
\]
On the other hand, \(k\ge277/4\) gives
\[
\begin{aligned}
t-d_2
&=\frac{2(75+k)}9-\frac{1999}{40000}\\
&\ge
\frac{2(75+277/4)}9-\frac{1999}{40000}\\
&=\frac{11522009}{360000}\\
&=\frac{23}{4}+\frac{9452009}{360000}
>\frac{23}{4}.
\end{aligned}
\]
Thus \(|R\cap\{u\le0\}|<23/4<t-d_2\), contradicting the first required cap equation. Consequently no such \(k\) and \(R\) exist. The argument applies, more strongly, to every measurable planar set \(R\) containing \(B\) with \(|R|=k<75\).
