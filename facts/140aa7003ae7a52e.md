## Statement

Assume the locked problem, let \(A_0,A_1,A_2\) be compatible nondegenerate
triangles, and assume their areas lie in the full strict residual strip of
accepted fact `15bb2c1680a0254e`.  Write
\[
a_i=|A_i|,\qquad T=a_0+a_1+a_2,\qquad
M=\max\{a_0,a_2\},\qquad m=\min\{a_0,a_2\}.
\]
After possibly exchanging the endpoint labels, assume
\[
a_0=m,\qquad a_2=M.
\tag{1}
\]
Let \(g_i\) be the centroid of \(A_i\), and suppose
\[
x:=2g_1-g_0\in A_2.
\tag{2}
\]
Write the barycentric coordinates of \(x\) in \(A_2\), in increasing order,
as
\[
0\leq\beta\leq\gamma\leq1-\beta-\gamma.
\]
If
\[
\boxed{18\beta\gamma M\geq M+m-a_1,}
\tag{3}
\]
then the endpoint \(s=1\) of the registered centroid segment satisfies
\[
h_S((1,g_1))\geq\frac{2T}{9}.
\tag{4}
\]
Equivalently, every directionwise feasible-\(s\) interval contains the
common parameter \(s=1\).

The criterion contains a new open residual class.  Explicitly, put
\[
\Delta=\operatorname{conv}\{(0,0),(1,0),(0,1)\},\qquad
g=\left(\frac13,\frac13\right),\qquad
c=\left(\frac3{10},\frac{47}{100}\right),
\tag{5}
\]
and define
\[
A_1=\Delta,\qquad
A_0=c+\frac8{21}(\Delta-g),\qquad
A_2=c+\frac{20}{21}(\Delta-g).
\tag{6}
\]
This is a strictly compatible rational triple in the full strict residual
strip.  It satisfies (2)-(3) strictly, while the sufficient conditions of
accepted facts `1e612c16bc508e32`, `a6808da3e0b737ca`, and
`fa47d2b2236c29fd` all fail strictly.  All these strict properties persist
on a full Euclidean neighborhood of the nine vertices in (6).

## Proof

We first prove (4).  The raw strict inequalities in accepted fact
`15bb2c1680a0254e`, with the labeling (1), include
\[
M-m<a_1<M+m
\tag{7}
\]
and \(a_i<T/2\) for all three slices.  Put
\[
d=\frac{2(M+m-a_1)}9.
\tag{8}
\]
Equation (7) gives
\[
0<d<\frac{4m}{9}.
\tag{9}
\]
Indeed, the second inequality in (9) is equivalent to
\(M+m-a_1<2m\), or \(a_1>M-m\).  This is the essential use of the
residual strip.

Fix a nonhorizontal closed halfspace containing \((1,g_1)\).  Shrinking it
parallel to itself until its boundary contains \((1,g_1)\) can only decrease
its mass.  After orienting its nonzero planar normal \(q\), its three slice
thresholds have the form
\[
c_0=q\cdot g_1+\lambda,\qquad
c_1=q\cdot g_1,\qquad
c_2=q\cdot g_1-\lambda,
\tag{10}
\]
so in particular
\[
c_0+c_2=2q\cdot g_1.
\tag{11}
\]

The exact triangle-depth formula in accepted fact
`fa47d2b2236c29fd` says that a point whose two smallest barycentric
coordinates are \(u,v\) has planar halfspace depth \(4uv\) times the
triangle area.  At a triangle centroid this is \(4/9\) of the area.
Consequently the middle cap in (10) has area at least
\[
\frac{4a_1}{9}.
\tag{12}
\]

It remains to show that the two endpoint caps in (10) have total area at
least \(d\), for every \(q\) and every finite \(\lambda\).  There are two
cases.  If
\[
c_0\geq q\cdot g_0,
\]
then the \(A_0\)-cap contains its centroid, so its area is at least
\[
\frac{4m}{9}>d
\tag{13}
\]
by (9).  If instead \(c_0<q\cdot g_0\), equation (11) gives
\[
c_2=2q\cdot g_1-c_0
>q\cdot(2g_1-g_0)=q\cdot x.
\tag{14}
\]
Thus the \(A_2\)-cap contains \(x\).  Its area is at least
\[
4\beta\gamma M
\geq\frac{2(M+m-a_1)}9=d,
\tag{15}
\]
where the inequality is exactly (3).  The unused endpoint cap is
nonnegative.  Equations (13)-(15) cover every allocation, including the
closed equality \(c_0=q\cdot g_0\) in the first case.

Combining (12) with the endpoint bound gives
\[
\frac{4a_1}{9}+d
=\frac{4a_1+2M+2m-2a_1}{9}
=\frac{2T}{9}.
\tag{16}
\]
The two horizontal halfspaces containing height \(1\) have masses
\[
a_0+a_1=T-M,\qquad a_1+a_2=T-m.
\]
Since every residual slice area is strictly less than \(T/2\), both masses
are strictly larger than \(T/2>2T/9\).  Hence every closed halfspace
containing \((1,g_1)\) has mass at least \(2T/9\), proving (4).  In the
registered segment notation, \(z_1=g_1\), so all directionwise feasible
intervals contain \(s=1\), including directions orthogonal to the segment.

We now verify the rational calibration.  The barycentric coordinates of
\(c\) in \(\Delta\) are
\[
\mu=\left(\frac{23}{100},\frac3{10},\frac{47}{100}\right).
\tag{17}
\]
Since the two endpoint triangles in (6) are positive homothetic copies of
the same triangle,
\[
\frac{A_0+A_2}{2}
=c+\frac23(\Delta-g).
\tag{18}
\]
Containment of the right side in the interior of \(\Delta\) is equivalent
to every coordinate in (17) being strictly larger than \(2/9\).  The three
slacks are
\[
\frac7{900},\qquad \frac7{90},\qquad \frac{223}{900},
\]
so compatibility is strict.  As in the standard convex-hull construction,
(18) also ensures that
\[
C=\operatorname{conv}\bigl(
(\{0\}\times A_0)\cup(\{1\}\times A_1)\cup(\{2\}\times A_2)
\bigr)
\]
has exactly the three prescribed integer slices.

The areas are
\[
m=a_0=\frac{32}{441},\qquad
a_1=\frac12,\qquad
M=a_2=\frac{200}{441}.
\tag{19}
\]
Thus the normalized coordinates of accepted fact `15bb2c1680a0254e` are
\[
r=\frac mM=\frac4{25},\qquad
s=\frac{a_1}{M}=\frac{441}{400}.
\]
Here \(r<9/25\), and the exact residual slacks are
\[
s-(1-r)=\frac{21}{80}>0,\qquad
(1+r)-s=\frac{23}{400}>0.
\tag{20}
\]
Accepted fact `15bb2c1680a0254e` therefore places (19) strictly in its full
residual strip.

For this calibration, \(g_0=g_2=c\) and \(g_1=g\), so
\[
x=2g-c=\left(\frac{11}{30},\frac{59}{300}\right).
\tag{21}
\]
If \(\nu\) denotes the barycentric vector of \(x\) in \(A_2\), the affine
homothety in (6) and (17) give
\[
\nu_i
=\frac13+\frac{21}{10}\left(\frac13-\mu_i\right).
\]
Therefore
\[
\nu=
\left(\frac{1651}{3000},\frac{121}{300},\frac{139}{3000}\right).
\tag{22}
\]
All entries are positive, so \(x\in\operatorname{int}A_2\), and the two
smallest are
\[
\beta=\frac{139}{3000},\qquad
\gamma=\frac{121}{300}.
\]
The strict margin in (3) is
\[
18\beta\gamma M-(M+m-a_1)
=\frac{16819}{110250}-\frac{23}{882}
=\frac{13944}{110250}>0.
\tag{23}
\]

It remains to verify that this branch is not already contained in the three
accepted triangle classes named in the statement.  At
\((g_0+g_2)/2=c\), the two smallest \(A_1\)-barycentric coordinates are
\[
\frac{23}{100},\qquad\frac3{10}.
\]
The criterion of accepted fact `1e612c16bc508e32` fails because
\[
\left(18\left(\frac{23}{100}\right)^2-1\right)a_1
=-\frac{239}{10000}
<\frac8{21}=M-m.
\tag{24}
\]
The stronger product criterion of accepted fact `fa47d2b2236c29fd` also
fails:
\[
\left(18\frac{23}{100}\frac3{10}-1\right)a_1
=\frac{121}{1000}
<\frac8{21}=M-m.
\tag{25}
\]

Finally, the normalized area condition of accepted fact
`a6808da3e0b737ca` fails strictly.  For the values in (19),
\[
r-\frac{2(1+r-s)}9=\frac{53}{360},
\qquad
\frac{20s}{9}=\frac{49}{20},
\]
but
\[
\left(1+\sqrt{\frac{53}{360}}\right)^2<\frac{49}{20}.
\tag{26}
\]
After subtracting \(1+53/360\), both sides are positive, and squaring
reduces (26) to the exact integer inequality
\[
76320<219961.
\]

Nondegeneracy, the strict compatibility slacks, the residual slacks (20),
the strict reflected-depth margin (23), the strict failures (24)-(26), and
the ordering \(M>m\) all persist under sufficiently small independent
perturbations of the nine vertices.  The reflected point and its
barycentric coordinates vary continuously there.  Hence a full Euclidean
neighborhood of (6) remains in this new theorem class and remains outside
all three previously accepted triangle classes.
