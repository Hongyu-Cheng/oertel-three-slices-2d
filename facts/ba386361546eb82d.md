## Statement

Let
\[
\Delta=\operatorname{conv}\{e_1,e_2,e_3\},\qquad |\Delta|=1,
\]
and define
\[
\mathcal C(z;\alpha)
=\left|\{\lambda\in\Delta:z\cdot\lambda\geq\alpha\}\right|.
\]
Assume the exact \(N=3\) barycentric system of accepted fact
`64760c76cadff481` in the surviving orientation of accepted fact
`09393c6edf1fc4e2`.  Thus
\[
A=|\det U|<B=|\det V|,\qquad
q=\sum_{i=1}^3\sigma_i>1,\qquad
1+A<2B,
\]
\[
\mathbf1^\top L=\mathbf1^\top,\qquad \det L\neq0,
\qquad
t=\frac{2(1+A+B)}9,
\]
and the literal row masses are
\[
M_i=
\mathcal C(L_{i*};0)
+A\mathcal C((LU)_{i*};-\sigma_i)
+B\mathcal C((LV)_{i*};\sigma_i).
\tag{1}
\]
Accepted fact `291c24bb45bd4946` permits restriction of every
counterexample search to
\[
r:=\frac AB<\frac38.
\tag{2}
\]

Suppose the middle rows lie in the following exact sign chamber:
\[
L=
\begin{pmatrix}
p&-a&-b\\
-d&u&-e\\
-g&-h&k
\end{pmatrix},
\qquad
p,a,b,d,u,e,g,h,k>0,
\tag{3}
\]
and suppose their centroid incidences are
\[
p-a-b>0,\qquad u-d-e>0,\qquad k-g-h<0.
\tag{4}
\]
Suppose also that the small-endpoint centroid incidences are disjoint from
the middle ones:
\[
\frac13\sum_{j=1}^3(LU)_{1j}+\sigma_1<0,\qquad
\frac13\sum_{j=1}^3(LU)_{2j}+\sigma_2<0,
\tag{5}
\]
\[
\frac13\sum_{j=1}^3(LU)_{3j}+\sigma_3>0.
\tag{6}
\]

Put
\[
c_i=\mathcal C(L_{i*};0).
\tag{7}
\]
If
\[
M_1<t,\qquad M_2<t,\qquad M_3<t,
\qquad t\leq\frac12,
\tag{8}
\]
then necessarily
\[
\boxed{\quad c_3>(1-t)^2\quad}
\tag{9}
\]
and
\[
\boxed{\quad
A\mathcal C((LU)_{3*};-\sigma_3)
+B\mathcal C((LV)_{3*};\sigma_3)
<t-(1-t)^2.
\quad}
\tag{10}
\]

Consequently, assume in addition that
\[
A\geq\frac14,\qquad A+B\leq\frac54,
\tag{11}
\]
and that the third small-endpoint cap is full:
\[
\min_j(LU)_{3j}\geq-\sigma_3.
\tag{12}
\]
Then the three strict inequalities in (8) are impossible.

Equivalently, writing \(S=1/B\), conditions (11) are
\[
S\leq4r,\qquad S\geq\frac{4(1+r)}5.
\tag{13}
\]
The conclusion in particular excludes the full-small-cap chamber
(3)--(6), (12) throughout
\[
\frac3{10}\leq r<\frac38,\qquad
\frac{4(1+r)}5\leq S\leq4r,
\tag{14}
\]
wherever these inequalities meet the exact residual strip of accepted
fact `64760c76cadff481`.  Row permutations and a simultaneous relabeling
of the three barycentric vertices give the identical conclusion for every
orbit of the displayed chamber.

## Proof

All three column sums of \(L\) equal one by accepted fact
`64760c76cadff481`.  In the notation of (3), this says
\[
p-d-g=1,\qquad -a+u-h=1,\qquad -b-e+k=1.
\tag{15}
\]
Define the first two positive row sums
\[
\alpha=p-a-b>0,\qquad
\beta=u-d-e>0.
\tag{16}
\]
Summing all nine entries of \(L\) by columns and by rows gives
\[
\alpha+\beta+k-g-h=3.
\]
Therefore, with
\[
s=g+h,
\]
the negative third-centroid condition in (4) and the preceding identity
give
\[
s=k+\alpha+\beta-3>0.
\tag{17}
\]

The complete exact cap formula in accepted fact
`64760c76cadff481`, applied on the strict sign chamber (3), gives
\[
c_1=\frac{p^2}{(p+a)(p+b)},\qquad
c_2=\frac{u^2}{(u+d)(u+e)},\qquad
c_3=\frac{k^2}{(k+g)(k+h)}.
\tag{18}
\]
All endpoint contributions in (1) are nonnegative.  Thus (8) implies
\[
c_1<t,\qquad c_2<t.
\tag{19}
\]
Set
\[
\rho=\frac{1-2t}{t}\geq0.
\tag{20}
\]
Using \(p=a+b+\alpha\), the first denominator in (18) is
\[
(p+a)(p+b)=2p^2-p\alpha+ab.
\]
The first strict inequality in (19) is therefore equivalent to
\[
(1-2t)p^2+tp\alpha<tab,
\]
and hence
\[
\alpha<\frac{ab}{p}-\rho p<b-\rho p.
\tag{21}
\]
The final strict inequality uses \(a<p\), which follows from
\(p>a+b\).  Identically, \(u=d+e+\beta\), \(d<u\), and the second
inequality in (19) give
\[
\beta<\frac{de}{u}-\rho u<e-\rho u.
\tag{22}
\]

The third column identity in (15) says
\[
k=1+b+e.
\tag{23}
\]
Combining (17), (21)--(23) yields
\[
s<2k-4-\rho(p+u).
\tag{24}
\]
The first two column identities in (15) also give
\[
p=1+d+g,\qquad u=1+a+h,
\]
so
\[
p+u=s+2+a+d>s+2.
\tag{25}
\]
Equations (20), (24), and (25) imply
\[
(1+\rho)s<2k-4-2\rho.
\]
Since
\[
1+\rho=\frac{1-t}{t},
\]
this is exactly
\[
s<\frac{2kt-2}{1-t}.
\tag{26}
\]
The left side is positive, so the right side is positive as well.

The exact identity
\[
4(k+g)(k+h)=(2k+s)^2-(g-h)^2
\tag{27}
\]
and (26) now give
\[
(k+g)(k+h)
\leq\frac{(2k+s)^2}{4}
<\frac{(k-1)^2}{(1-t)^2}.
\tag{28}
\]
Here \(k=1+b+e>1\) by (23).  Substituting (28) into the last formula
in (18) proves
\[
c_3>
\left(\frac{k}{k-1}\right)^2(1-t)^2
>(1-t)^2,
\]
which is (9).  Subtracting \(c_3\) from the strict third-row inequality
in (8), and again using nonnegativity of the two endpoint terms, proves
(10).

It remains to prove the exclusion under (11)--(12).  Condition (12)
puts all three vertices, and hence all of \(U\Delta\), in the third
small-endpoint cap.  Therefore
\[
\mathcal C((LU)_{3*};-\sigma_3)=1.
\tag{29}
\]
Equations (1), (9), (11), and (29) give
\[
M_3>(1-t)^2+\frac14.
\tag{30}
\]
For \(0<t\leq1/2\),
\[
(1-t)^2+\frac14-t
=\frac{(1-2t)(5-2t)}4\geq0.
\tag{31}
\]
Thus (30) gives \(M_3>t\), contradicting (8).

Finally,
\[
A=\frac rS,\qquad B=\frac1S.
\]
Hence \(A\geq1/4\) is \(S\leq4r\), and
\(A+B\leq5/4\) is \(S\geq4(1+r)/5\), proving (13).
This proves the parameter claim (14).  The proof used the literal column
sums, strict determinant-compatible sign chamber,
centroid incidences, complete cap formulas, and all three literal row
masses.  No numerical optimization output enters the conclusion.
