## Statement

Use the common-plane hypotheses and exact-cell convention of accepted
fact `0e51a47a9add571f`.  Thus \(f_1,f_2,f_3\) are nonconstant affine
functions on \(\mathbb R^2\), with pairwise nonparallel linear parts,
\[
f_1+f_2+f_3=-1,
\]
\[
L_1+L_2+L_3<-1,
\]
and \(A_0,A_2,K\) are nonempty two-dimensional compact convex
polytopes satisfying
\[
\frac{A_0+A_2}{2}\subseteq K.
\]
Write
\[
p_J=|P_J|,\qquad q_J=|Q_J|,\qquad r_J=|R_J|
\]
for their exact cell areas, and put
\[
W=
\sum_{J\subseteq\{1,2,3\}}(3|J|-2)p_J+
\sum_{J\subseteq\{1,2,3\}}(3|J|-2)q_J+
\sum_{J\subseteq\{1,2,3\}}(3|J|-2)r_J.
\]
If
\[
W=0,
\]
then
\[
\boxed{\quad
q_{\{1\}}q_{\{2\}}q_{\{3\}}=0.
\quad}
\tag{1}
\]
Consequently, if \(\{j,k,\ell\}=\{1,2,3\}\) and
\[
q_{\{j\}}>0,\qquad q_{\{k,\ell\}}>0,
\]
then
\[
q_{\{k\}}q_{\{\ell\}}=0.
\tag{2}
\]

## Proof

Put
\[
\delta=-1-(L_1+L_2+L_3)>0,\qquad
\sigma=\delta+2,
\]
\[
\pi_i=f_i-L_i,\qquad \theta_i=f_i+L_i.
\]
Then
\[
\pi_1+\pi_2+\pi_3=\delta,
\qquad
\theta_1+\theta_2+\theta_3=-\sigma.
\tag{3}
\]
The pairwise nonparallel linear parts make each of the affine maps
\[
x\longmapsto(\pi_1(x),\pi_2(x),\pi_3(x)),
\qquad
x\longmapsto(\theta_1(x),\theta_2(x),\theta_3(x))
\]
an affine isomorphism from \(\mathbb R^2\) onto its constant-sum
plane.

Assume for contradiction that
\[
q_{\{1\}},q_{\{2\}},q_{\{3\}}>0.
\tag{4}
\]
For each \(i\), positive area in \(Q_{\{i\}}\) supplies a point
\(y^{(i)}\in A_2\) away from all three cutting lines.  Define
\[
x^{(i)}
=-\frac1{\sigma}
\bigl(\theta_1(y^{(i)}),
\theta_2(y^{(i)}),
\theta_3(y^{(i)})\bigr).
\]
By (3), every \(x^{(i)}\) belongs to the affine plane
\[
\mathcal A=\{x\in\mathbb R^3:x_1+x_2+x_3=1\}.
\]
The strict sign pattern of \(Q_{\{i\}}\) gives
\[
x_i^{(i)}>0,\qquad x_h^{(i)}<0\quad(h\ne i).
\]
Since the coordinates sum to \(1\), in fact
\[
x_i^{(i)}>1.
\tag{5}
\]

We use the following elementary barycentric observation.  Let \(X\)
be the \(3\times3\) matrix whose \(i\)-th column is \(x^{(i)}\), and
let
\[
D=\operatorname{diag}
\bigl(x_1^{(1)},x_2^{(2)},x_3^{(3)}\bigr),
\qquad
C=D-X,
\qquad
B=CD^{-1}.
\]
By (5), \(C\) and \(B\) are entrywise nonnegative.  The sum of column
\(i\) of \(B\) is
\[
\frac{-\sum_{h\ne i}x_h^{(i)}}{x_i^{(i)}}
=\frac{x_i^{(i)}-1}{x_i^{(i)}}<1.
\]
Thus \(\|B\|_1<1\), and
\[
X=(I-B)D
\]
is invertible with
\[
X^{-1}=D^{-1}\sum_{n=0}^{\infty}B^n
\]
entrywise nonnegative.  Every column of \(X\) sums to \(1\), so
\[
\mathbf 1^{\mathsf T}X=\mathbf 1^{\mathsf T},
\qquad
\mathbf 1^{\mathsf T}X^{-1}=\mathbf 1^{\mathsf T}.
\]
For every standard basis vector \(e_i\), the vector
\(X^{-1}e_i\) is therefore nonnegative and has coordinate sum \(1\).
Consequently
\[
e_i\in
\operatorname{conv}\{x^{(1)},x^{(2)},x^{(3)}\}.
\]
Convexity gives
\[
\operatorname{conv}\{e_1,e_2,e_3\}
\subseteq
-\frac1{\sigma}
\bigl(\theta_1,\theta_2,\theta_3\bigr)(A_2).
\tag{6}
\]

Pulling (6) back to \(\mathbb R^2\) shows that \(A_2\) contains the
closed bounded triangle
\[
\mathcal T_Q=\{\theta_1\leq0,\ \theta_2\leq0,\ \theta_3\leq0\}.
\]
The ambient \(Q_{123}\)-chamber is exactly \(\mathcal T_Q\).
Therefore
\[
q_{123}=|\mathcal T_Q|.
\tag{7}
\]
On the \(P\)-side,
\[
P_0\subseteq
\mathcal T_P
:=\{\pi_1\geq0,\ \pi_2\geq0,\ \pi_3\geq0\},
\]
and hence
\[
p_0\leq|\mathcal T_P|.
\tag{8}
\]

Use \((\pi_1,\pi_2)\) as affine coordinates, and let
\(\gamma>0\) be the constant area Jacobian back to the original
plane.  By (3),
\[
|\mathcal T_P|=\frac{\gamma\delta^2}{2}.
\tag{9}
\]
The functions \(\theta_i\) and \(\pi_i\) have identical linear parts,
so the same Jacobian applies to \((\theta_1,\theta_2)\).  Again by
(3),
\[
|\mathcal T_Q|
=\frac{\gamma\sigma^2}{2}
=\frac{\gamma(\delta+2)^2}{2}
>|\mathcal T_P|.
\tag{10}
\]
Equations (7)--(10) give
\[
q_{123}>p_0.
\tag{11}
\]

Accepted fact `0e51a47a9add571f` gives the forced empty cells
\[
P_{123}=Q_0=R_0=\varnothing.
\]
Thus the only negative coefficient occurring in \(W\) is the
coefficient \(-2\) of \(p_0\).  Every other nonempty cell has
coefficient at least \(1\), while \(q_{123}\) has coefficient \(7\).
It follows from (11) that
\[
W
\geq -2p_0+7q_{123}
>5p_0
\geq0,
\]
contradicting \(W=0\).  This proves (1).

For (2), the assumed positive singleton \(q_{\{j\}}\) cannot be the
zero factor in (1).  Hence at least one of
\(q_{\{k\}},q_{\{\ell\}}\) is zero.  No connected-support conclusion
is needed.
