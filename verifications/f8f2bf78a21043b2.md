MATH_PROVER_VERDICT: REJECTED

SUMMARY

The proof invokes Grünbaum's inequality for \(A_k\), but the statement never
assumes that any \(A_i\) is a convex body. The claim is false for general
measurable planar sets.

CRITICAL_ERRORS

Without convexity, a set's centroid need not belong to the set, so
\(y_k=(k,g_k)\in S\) is unjustified. More importantly, a nonconvex \(A_k\)
can place arbitrarily little area in a halfplane containing its centroid,
invalidating the claimed \(4/9\) bound and hence the \(2T/9\) conclusion.

GAPS

The statement also does not specify measurability, finite positive area, or the
meaning of \(|\cdot|\). These conditions are needed for \(a_k\), \(T\),
\(a_k/T\), and \(g_k\) to be well-defined.

REPAIR_HINTS

The statement would need explicit hypotheses ensuring that the relevant
\(A_i\), in particular \(A_k\), are planar convex bodies of finite positive
Lebesgue measure.
