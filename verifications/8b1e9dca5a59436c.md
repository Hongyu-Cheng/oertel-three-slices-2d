MATH_PROVER_VERDICT: REJECTED

SUMMARY

The proof incorrectly infers positive area from nonemptiness. A nonempty convex
polytope in \(\mathbb R^2\) may be a point or line segment and therefore have
zero planar area.

CRITICAL_ERRORS

The candidate statement is false under the supplied hypotheses. Set
\[
A_0=A_1=A_2=\{(0,0)\}.
\]
Then \((A_0+A_2)/2\subseteq A_1\), so accepted fact
`8ad0785e5b4ae9ef` supplies a compact polytope whose only nonempty
integer-height sections occur at \(0,1,2\) and equal these sets. Their areas
are \((0,0,0)\), contradicting the claimed necessary condition
\(a_0,a_1,a_2>0\).

GAPS

The assertion that all three sections are planar convex bodies is unsupported.
The statement and accepted fact assume only nonempty convex polytopes, not
full-dimensional ones. Consequently, the quoted Brunn-Minkowski formulation
for convex bodies is also not shown to apply to every permitted section.

REPAIR_HINTS

Establishing positivity would require an additional full-dimensionality or
positive-area hypothesis. No such hypothesis is present, so the stated claim
cannot be proved as written.
